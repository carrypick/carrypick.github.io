Watch size chart: which dial size suits your wrist?

A free tool. Updated 3 Nov 2026

Quick answer

  • Wrap a strip of paper or a string around your wrist bone, mark it, and measure it with a ruler in cm.
  • Most men's wrists are about 16–18 cm and suit a 38–42 mm dial. Slimmer wrists look better with 34–38 mm.
  • Bigger isn't always better: the lugs (the ends that hold the strap) shouldn't stick out past your wrist.

Wrist size to dial size chart

WristWrist sizeDial size that suits it
Small wristUnder 15 cm28–36 mm

Average wrist16.5–18 cm38–42 mm
Wide wrist18–19.5 cm40–44 mm
Very wide wristOver 19.5 cm42–46 mm

A rule of thumb, not a strict rule. A thin case and a leather strap look smaller; a thick steel watch looks bigger. Try one on if you can.

Questions

How do I measure my wrist for a watch?

Wrap a strip of paper or a string around your wrist just above the wrist bone, mark where it meets, and measure that length with a ruler in cm.

What watch size is best for a 17 cm wrist?

A 17 cm wrist is average. A dial of about 38–42 mm usually suits it.

Is a 42 mm watch too big?

Not for an average or wide wrist (about 16.5 cm and up). On a slim wrist under 16 cm, 36–38 mm usually looks better.

What size watch suits women?

Most women's watches are 28–36 mm. On a slim wrist, 28–34 mm is a safe choice.
