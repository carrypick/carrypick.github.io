Is my power bank allowed on the flight? mAh to Wh checker

A free tool. Rules checked on 20 Nov 2026

Quick answer

  • Power banks go in your cabin bag only, never in check-in. Every Indian airline here says so.
  • Up to 100 Wh (about 27,000 mAh) is fine on all three airlines. IndiGo takes up to 160 Wh; Air India and Akasa Air stop at 100 Wh.
  • You can't use your power bank or charge it during the flight, and it can't go in the overhead bin.

The mAh and Wh are printed on the back of the power bank. Most use 3.7 V cells; if the label gives another cell voltage, use that.

On the plane

  • Don't use your power bank to charge a phone during the flight, and don't charge the power bank itself. Akasa Air's conditions of carriage say this. News reports from January 2026 say DGCA's dangerous goods advisory circular bans it on all Indian flights, but we couldn't find the circular on DGCA's website, so treat it as the rule on every airline.
  • Keep it in the seat pocket, under the seat in front or on you, not in the overhead bin. Air India and Akasa Air both say this.
  • The MOBIKINS MK-PB-04 power bank is banned on every flight to, from and within India, in any bag. Air India's and Akasa Air's pages say DGCA banned it.
  • Keep each power bank in its box or a separate plastic bag, so the ends can't touch metal. Air India and Akasa Air ask for this.

What "needs airline approval" means

A power bank over 100 Wh and up to 160 Wh is allowed on IndiGo's list, but Air India and Akasa Air's pages allow only up to 100 Wh. Ask your airline before you fly, or take a smaller one. Over 160 Wh, no airline here takes it.

Not confirmed: we couldn't find DGCA's circular on in-flight use on DGCA's own website, so that rule is from Akasa Air's page and news reports. Everything else above is from the airlines' own pages, not from BCAS or DGCA circulars.

Questions

Is a 20000 mAh power bank allowed in flight in India?

Yes, in your cabin bag. At 3.7 V it is about 74 Wh, under the 100 Wh limit of Air India and Akasa Air and the 160 Wh limit of IndiGo.

How do I convert mAh to Wh?

Multiply the mAh by the voltage and divide by 1,000. For a 10,000 mAh power bank with 3.7 V cells: 10,000 x 3.7 ÷ 1,000 = 37 Wh.

Can I keep a power bank in check-in baggage?

No. IndiGo, Air India and Akasa Air all ban power banks in check-in bags. Akasa Air says a check-in bag with a power bank in it won't be loaded.

Can I use a power bank during the flight?

No. Akasa Air's rules ban using or charging power banks during the flight, and news reports say DGCA has banned it on all Indian flights. Charge your phone before you board.

What is the biggest power bank allowed on IndiGo?

IndiGo's list allows power banks up to 160 Wh (about 43,200 mAh at 3.7 V), with the power rating printed on it.

How many power banks can I carry?

Air India and Akasa Air allow 2 spare batteries per person, and a power bank counts as one. IndiGo's list doesn't give a number, so keep to two.
