Shoe size chart for India: India/UK to US, EU and cm

A free tool. Updated 20 Nov 2026

Quick answer

  • India and UK sizes are the same number. An India 8 is a UK 8.
  • On Bata India's chart, a men's UK 8 is a US 8.5 and an EU 42, for a foot of 25.8–26.5 cm.
  • Your foot length in cm is the surest way to pick a size. Sizes vary by brand, so check the brand's own chart too.

Step 1: measure your foot

  1. Put a sheet of paper on the floor, against a wall.
  2. Stand on it with your heel touching the wall, wearing the socks you'll wear with the shoes.
  3. Ask someone to mark the tip of your longest toe. (It isn't always the big toe.)
  4. Measure from the wall to the mark with a ruler, in cm. Do both feet and use the longer one.
  5. Measure in the evening, when feet are at their largest.

Questions

Is Indian shoe size the same as UK size?

Yes. Shoes in India are sold in UK sizes, so an India 8 is the same as a UK 8.

What is UK 8 in US and EU size?

On Bata India's chart, a men's UK 8 is a US 8.5 and an EU 42, for a foot of 25.8–26.5 cm. Other brands can give a different US size, so check the brand's own chart.

What is size 40 in India for women?

On Bata India's women's chart, EU 40 is an India/UK 7 and a US 7.5, for a foot of 25–25.7 cm.

What shoe size is a 26 cm foot?

For men, about an India/UK 8 (EU 42) on Bata India's chart, which covers feet of 25.8–26.5 cm.

How do I measure my foot size in cm at home?

Stand on a sheet of paper with your heel against a wall, mark the tip of your longest toe, and measure from the wall to the mark in cm. Measure both feet in the evening and use the longer one.

Are men's and women's shoe sizes the same?

On Bata India's chart, the same UK number fits the same foot length for men and women, but the EU number is different: a men's UK 6 is an EU 40, a women's UK 6 is an EU 39.

What shoe size does a 5 year old wear in India?

Often about a UK 13 kids' size on Campus's chart (ages 4.5–5.5, foot 19.3–20.1 cm). Children grow fast, so measure the foot rather than going by age.
