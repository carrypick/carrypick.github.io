Excess baggage calculator: allowance and overweight fee by airline

A free tool. 33 airlines. Updated 21 Nov 2026

Quick answer

  • Weigh each bag at home with a luggage scale, then type the real weights below. The tool compares them with your airline's allowance for your fare.
  • On flights within India, IndiGo, Air India, Air India Express, Akasa Air and SpiceJet charge about ₹775 to ₹850 per kg over the allowance at the airport. Pre-booking extra kg online is usually cheaper.
  • Every allowance and fee here comes from the airline's own website, with the date we checked it. Your ticket and the airline's site are final.

How to avoid excess baggage fees

  • Weigh every bag at home. A luggage scale is cheaper than one extra kilo at most airport counters.
  • Move weight to where you have room. If your checked bag is over but your cabin bag is under its limit, move heavy things like shoes or books into the cabin bag, within its size and weight limit.
  • Wear the heaviest clothes. Put on your jacket and heaviest shoes for the flight instead of packing them.
  • Pre-book extra kg online. On flights within India, the airlines' own fee lists show pre-booked kg cost less than paying at the counter. Buy the smallest pack that covers the extra.
  • Check your fare. Lite and Basic fares often include no checked bag at all. See our extra baggage charges and international baggage rules.

Questions

How is excess baggage calculated?

Airlines compare what your bags weigh at check-in with the free allowance on your ticket. On flights within India, IndiGo, Air India, Akasa Air, SpiceJet and Air India Express charge a rate per kg over the allowance at the airport (about ₹775 to ₹850 per kg in their fee lists), or sell cheaper kg packs online before you fly.

How much is excess baggage per kg on IndiGo?

IndiGo's fees page lists ₹800 per kg at the airport for domestic flights. Pre-booked online, 3 kg costs ₹2,250 and 5 kg ₹3,750. See the table above for the other slabs and the date we checked.

Is it cheaper to pre-book extra baggage?

Usually, yes. On flights within India the airlines' own fee lists show pre-booked kg at about ₹695 to ₹750 per kg against ₹775 to ₹850 at the counter. Buy the smallest pack that covers your extra weight, as unused kg aren't refunded.

What if my cabin bag is over 7 kg?

Most airlines will check it in, and its weight then counts towards your checked allowance. Air India, Air India Express and SpiceJet sell extra cabin kg on domestic flights; the calculator shows the charge when your airline has one.

Does my jacket count in my baggage weight?

Clothes you wear aren't weighed with your bags, so wearing your heaviest jacket and shoes leaves more room in your allowance.
