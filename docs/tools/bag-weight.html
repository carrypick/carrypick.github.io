How heavy is my bag? Packed bag weight calculator

A free tool. Updated 3 Nov 2026

Quick answer

  • Most Indian airlines allow a 7 kg cabin bag and 15 kg of check-in baggage on domestic flights.
  • The bag counts too: a hard cabin trolley is about 2.5–3 kg empty, which leaves only about 4–4.5 kg for your things.
  • This tool uses typical weights. To be sure, weigh the packed bag at home with a luggage scale.

Questions

How much can I pack in a 7 kg cabin bag?

A hard cabin trolley weighs about 2.5–3 kg empty, so you have about 4–4.5 kg left: roughly clothes for 2–3 days, a toiletry kit and a charger. A light backpack leaves more room.

How heavy is a laptop?

Most laptops weigh about 1.2–2 kg, plus about 0.3 kg for the charger.

How can I check my bag's weight at home?

Use a small hand-held luggage scale: hook the bag on and lift it. Or weigh yourself on a bathroom scale with and without the bag and subtract.
