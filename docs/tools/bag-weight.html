Excess baggage calculator: allowance and overweight fee by airline

A free tool. 35 airlines. Updated 26 Nov 2026

Quick answer

  • Excess baggage charge = kg over your allowance × the airline's rate per kg. With a 15 kg allowance and a 19 kg bag, you are 4 kg over: on IndiGo that is 4 × ₹800 = ₹3,200 at the airport.
  • On flights within India the airport rate is ₹775 (SpiceJet) to ₹850 (Air India Express, "up to") per kg. Pre-booked packs work out at about ₹695 to ₹750 per kg, but only in fixed sizes: if you are 4 kg over, a 5 kg pack can cost more than paying at the counter.
  • Weigh each bag at home with a luggage scale, then type the real weights below. The tool compares them with your airline's allowance for your fare. Every allowance and fee comes from the airline's own website, with the date we checked it; the price shown when you pay is final.

What each bag weighs (kg)

Allowances checked on each airline's site between 16 Nov 2026 and 28 Nov 2026; domestic excess fees checked 15 Nov 2026. Fees change often, and the price shown when you pay is final.

How to calculate excess baggage

  1. Find your free allowance. It is on your ticket or in Manage Booking, for example "15 kg, 1 piece". Cheap fares such as IndiGo Lite and Air India Express Xpress Lite include no checked bag at all.
  2. Weigh each packed bag. Use a luggage scale, or stand on a bathroom scale with and without the bag and take the difference.
  3. Subtract. Total checked weight minus the allowance = kg over. Your cabin bag has its own limit (usually 7 kg) and is weighed separately.
  4. Multiply by the airline's rate. On flights within India that is the per-kg rate at the airport, or the price of the smallest pre-booked pack that covers your extra kg.
  5. Add any piece fee. A second checked bag usually costs a handling fee (₹950 to ₹1,000 on IndiGo, Akasa Air, SpiceJet and Air India Express) on top of its weight.

How to avoid excess baggage fees

  • Weigh every bag at home. A luggage scale is cheaper than one extra kilo at most airport counters.
  • Move weight to where you have room. If your checked bag is over but your cabin bag is under its limit, move heavy things like shoes or books into the cabin bag, within its size and weight limit.
  • Wear the heaviest clothes. Put on your jacket and heaviest shoes for the flight instead of packing them.
  • Pre-book extra kg online. On flights within India, the airlines' own fee lists show pre-booked kg cost less than paying at the counter. Buy the smallest pack that covers the extra.
  • Check your fare. Lite and Basic fares often include no checked bag at all. See our extra baggage charges and international baggage rules.

Questions

How to calculate excess baggage?

Add up what your checked bags weigh, subtract the free allowance on your ticket, and multiply the kg over by the airline's rate. Example: 19 kg on a 15 kg IndiGo fare is 4 kg over, so 4 × ₹800 = ₹3,200 at the airport. Pre-booked online, 3 kg costs ₹2,250 and 5 kg ₹3,750.

How much is excess baggage per kg in India?

At the airport, on flights within India: IndiGo and Akasa Air ₹800 per kg, Air India ₹800 per kg plus tax, Air India Express up to ₹850 per kg and SpiceJet ₹775 per kg (their own fee pages, read on 26 Nov 2026).

What are IndiGo's excess baggage charges on domestic flights?

IndiGo's fees page lists ₹800 per kg at the airport. Pre-booked: 3 kg ₹2,250, 5 kg ₹3,750, 10 kg ₹7,500, 15 kg ₹11,250, 20 kg ₹15,000, 25 kg ₹18,750, 30 kg ₹22,500 and 40 kg ₹30,000.

What are Air India's excess baggage charges per kg?

Air India's excess baggage page lists INR 800 per kg plus taxes for travel within India, and says paying ahead saves up to 20%. The prepaid price is shown in Manage Booking, not on the page.

Is it cheaper to pre-book extra baggage?

Per kg, yes: the packs work out at about ₹695 to ₹750 per kg against ₹775 to ₹850 at the counter. But packs come in fixed sizes (3, 5, 10 kg and up) and unused kilos aren't refunded, so if you are 4 kg over, a 5 kg pack (₹3,750 on IndiGo) costs more than 4 kg at the airport (₹3,200).

What are extra baggage charges per kg on international flights?

Much higher than within India. Akasa Air lists ₹1,500 per kg at the airport from India to Abu Dhabi, Doha and Riyadh, and Air India Express ₹1,500 to ₹1,650 per kg to the Gulf and ₹2,000 to Singapore and Thailand (read 26 Nov 2026). Pre-booking 5 kg costs about ₹5,500 to ₹7,700 on these routes.

What if my cabin bag is over 7 kg?

Most airlines will check it in, and its weight then counts towards your checked allowance. Air India, Air India Express and SpiceJet sell extra cabin kg on domestic flights; the calculator shows the charge when your airline has one.

Does my jacket count in my baggage weight?

Clothes you wear aren't weighed with your bags, so wearing your heaviest jacket and shoes leaves more room in your allowance.

What is the most one checked bag can weigh?

32 kg on the airlines here, even if your allowance is higher or you pay for more. Air India's excess baggage page says each bag can't weigh more than 32 kg, and Air India Express's fee list says the same. Split heavy luggage into two bags.
