Quick answer

  • Dress in layers. Days and nights can differ by 10–17 °C in winter, in the hills and in Rajasthan alike.
  • Below 5 °C at night, pack thermals, a puffer jacket, a cap and gloves. Above 25 °C by day, pack light cotton, sunglasses and sunscreen.
  • Going where it snows or rains? Add waterproof shoes, or a poncho and a phone pouch.

How we pick the clothes

Day clothes follow the warmest day in the month, and evening clothes the coldest night. Where a guide gives only a range, as for Manali in December and January, day clothes follow the middle of it. Below 0 °C at night, a wet month's rain may fall as snow. Rain of 60 mm or more in a month adds a poncho, a phone pouch and quick-dry clothes, and a chance of snow adds waterproof shoes, gloves and sunglasses.

Questions

What to wear in Manali in December?

Manali in December has about −3 °C at night to 17 °C by day. Wear by day: full sleeves, a sweater or fleece; evenings and nights: thermals, a puffer jacket, cap and gloves, woollen socks; shoes: waterproof shoes; also pack: sunglasses. Snow is possible but not certain, so take waterproof shoes.

What to wear in Kashmir in December?

Kashmir in December has Srinagar days about 10 °C, nights about −1 °C; Gulmarg days about 4 °C, nights about −5 °C. Wear by day: full sleeves, a sweater or fleece; evenings and nights: thermals, a puffer jacket, cap and gloves, woollen socks; shoes: waterproof shoes; also pack: sunglasses. Gulmarg often gets snow, and Chillai Kalan starts on 21 December.

What to wear in Manali in October?

Manali in October has days about 21.6 °C and nights about 6.3 °C. Wear by day: T-shirts, a light jacket or shawl; evenings and nights: a sweater or fleece, a warm jacket; shoes: walking shoes. October is dry and sunny, but nights are cold, so pack a warm jacket.

What to wear in Kashmir in October?

Kashmir in October has Srinagar days about 23 °C, nights about 6.2 °C; Gulmarg days about 14.2 °C, nights about 3.8 °C. Wear by day: T-shirts, a light jacket or shawl; evenings and nights: thermals, a puffer jacket, cap and gloves, woollen socks; shoes: warm boots. Gulmarg is far colder than Srinagar, so take your warmest layers for a day trip there.

What to wear in Dubai in December for ladies?

Dubai in December has days about 26 °C and nights about 15 °C. Wear by day: light cotton: kurtas, loose tops, palazzos, cotton dresses and a stole for shade and temples, a cap or hat; evenings and nights: a shawl or stole, or a light cardigan; shoes: sandals, walking shoes; also pack: sunglasses, sunscreen. Cover your shoulders and midriff in public places, and keep swimwear for the beach and pool.

What to wear in Goa for men?

Goa in December has days about 32.9 °C and nights about 20.9 °C. Wear by day: light cotton or linen: shirts, T-shirts, chinos and shorts, a cap or hat; evenings and nights: a light jacket, sweatshirt or overshirt; shoes: sandals, walking shoes; also pack: sunglasses, sunscreen. Put a shirt or kaftan over swimwear when you go into town.

What to wear in Shimla in December?

Shimla in December has days about 12 °C and nights about 4 °C. Wear by day: full sleeves, a sweater or fleece; evenings and nights: thermals, a puffer jacket, cap and gloves, woollen socks; shoes: waterproof shoes; also pack: sunglasses. Snow is more likely in January and February.

What to wear in Thailand?

Thailand in December has Bangkok days about 31 °C, nights about 21 °C; Pattaya days about 30 °C, nights about 22 °C. Wear by day: light cotton, a cap or hat; evenings and nights: a light jacket or shawl; shoes: sandals, walking shoes; also pack: sunglasses, sunscreen, a poncho, a phone pouch, quick-dry clothes. Cover your shoulders for temples and the Grand Palace.

What to wear in Varanasi in winter?

Varanasi in January has days about 22 °C and nights about 9 °C. Wear by day: T-shirts, a light jacket or shawl; evenings and nights: a sweater or fleece, a warm jacket; shoes: walking shoes. Early mornings on the ghats are the coldest part of the day.

Why can I only pick some months?

We only show months we have official climate numbers for (IMD for India, DHM for Nepal, the UAE's met office for Dubai), mostly October to March. We never guess the weather for other months. More months are coming.
