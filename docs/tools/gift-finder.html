    Questions

    What is a good Christmas gift for men?

    A watch he'll wear every day, like the Timex Classics or the Titan Classique, a leather wallet gift set from WildHorn, or a sling bag. For a man who travels, a weekender duffle or a toiletry bag.

    What are good gift ideas for women?

    A dressy watch such as the Titan Raga, a sling or tote bag from Zouk or Lavie, or a zip-around wallet. For a woman who travels, a light crossbody bag or packing cubes.

    What is a good Secret Santa gift for coworkers?

    Something anyone can use at work: a Milton steel bottle, a cable and charger organiser, a wallet and pen gift set for men, or a zip-around wallet for women. Skip jokes and anything too personal.

    What should I give a colleague as a farewell gift?

    A wallet gift set, a passport holder for someone moving abroad, or a laptop backpack for a new job. For a boss or senior colleague, a team gift such as a Titan watch.

    What gift can I give my parents?

    A pair watch set like Titan Bandhan or Sonata Bandhan, warm thermal sets for winter, or comfortable walking shoes for dad.

    What gift should I give for a marriage?

    Something the couple will use together: a couple watch set, a set of 3 trolley bags for the new home, or a cabin trolley and weekender bag for the honeymoon. Check the couple's list first if they have one.

    What do small token, nice gift and big gift mean?

    They say how much of a gift you want, not a price. A small token is a desk gift or Secret Santa, a nice gift suits a friend or family, and a big gift is for a partner, parents or a gift from the whole team. We don't show prices: check them on Amazon.in.
