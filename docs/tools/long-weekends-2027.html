Long weekends 2027 in India: holiday list and leave planner

A free planner from the official list. Checked 21 Nov 2026

Quick answer

  • 4 long weekends need no leave in 2027: Good Friday (26–28 Mar), Mahavir Jayanti (17–19 Apr), Id-ul-Zuha (15–17 May), Diwali (29–31 Oct).
  • Best deal: Ram Navami and Mahavir Jayanti. Take Fri 16 Apr off and get 5 days (Thu 15 Apr to Mon 19 Apr).
  • Longest break: 9 days for 3 days of leave, around Holi and Good Friday (20–28 Mar) or Id-ul-Zuha and Buddha Purnima (15–23 May).

Whose holidays? These dates are from DoPT O.M. F. No. 12/2/2023-JCA dated 16 July 2026, the Government of India's list for central government offices in Delhi / New Delhi. States, banks and private companies follow their own holiday lists, so check yours. Weekends here are Saturday and Sunday.

Tips for booking a long weekend

  • Book trains early. Reservations open about 60 days before you travel, and long weekends sell out first.
  • Keep moon-based trips flexible. Id-ul-Fitr, Id-ul-Zuha, Muharram and Id-e-Milad can move by a day after the moon is sighted.
  • Pick one place. Three or four days is enough for one city or hill town; moving between places eats a day. For winter trips, see where to go in December and January. Going away for New Year? See our New Year trips 2027.

Questions

How many long weekends are there in 2027?

With no leave at all, central government offices in Delhi get 4 long weekends of 3 days in 2027: Good Friday (26–28 Mar), Mahavir Jayanti (17–19 Apr), Id-ul-Zuha (15–17 May), Diwali (29–31 Oct). Take 1 day of leave and you get 4 more: Republic Day (23–26 Jan): take Mon 25 Jan off for 4 days, Holi (20–23 Mar): take Mon 22 Mar off for 4 days, Ram Navami and Mahavir Jayanti (15–19 Apr): take Fri 16 Apr off for 5 days, Buddha Purnima (20–23 May): take Fri 21 May off for 4 days.

Is there a long weekend in January 2027?

Yes, with 1 day of leave. Republic Day, 26 January 2027, is a Tuesday. Take Monday 25 January off and you get Sat 23 Jan to Tue 26 Jan, 4 days. If you can use your restricted holidays, New Year's Day gives Fri 1 Jan to Sun 3 Jan (3 days); Makar Sankranti / Magha Bihu and Pongal, Guru Gobind Singh's Birthday gives Thu 14 Jan to Sun 17 Jan (4 days).

Which is the longest holiday break in 2027?

Take 3 days of leave and you get 9 days off: Holi and Good Friday (20–28 Mar) (take Mon 22 Mar, Wed 24 Mar, Thu 25 Mar off) or Id-ul-Zuha and Buddha Purnima (15–23 May) (take Tue 18 May, Wed 19 May, Fri 21 May off). With just 1 day of leave, Ram Navami and Mahavir Jayanti gives 5 days (Thu 15 Apr to Mon 19 Apr).

What is the 2027 holiday list for central government offices?

DoPT O.M. F. No. 12/2/2023-JCA dated 16 July 2026 lists 17 gazetted holidays for central government offices in Delhi / New Delhi in 2027, and 32 restricted holidays, of which each employee may take 2. The full list is in the table above, with the day of the week.

Which 2027 holidays fall on a weekend?

Independence Day on Sunday 15 Aug; Milad-un-Nabi or Id-e-Milad on Sunday 15 Aug; Mahatma Gandhi's Birthday on Saturday 2 Oct; Dussehra on Saturday 9 Oct; Guru Nanak's Birthday on Sunday 14 Nov; Christmas Day on Saturday 25 Dec. No extra day off is given for these (para 3.2 of the O.M.).

When is Diwali 2027?

Diwali (Deepavali) is on Friday, 29 October 2027, in the central government list, so Fri 29 Oct to Sun 31 Oct is a 3-day weekend with no leave. Naraka Chaturdasi, the day before, is a restricted holiday.

Do states, banks and private companies follow this list?

No. This list is for central government administrative offices in Delhi / New Delhi. Central offices in other cities pick 3 of their holidays locally, states publish their own lists, banks follow their own rules, and private companies set their own holidays. Check your office's list before you book.

Can moon-based holiday dates change?

Yes. For Id-ul-Fitr, Id-ul-Zuha, Muharram and Id-e-Milad, DoPT may change the date after the moon is sighted, sometimes at short notice. Keep those trips flexible.

Can I add these long weekends to my calendar?

Yes. Download the calendar file on this page and open it with Google Calendar, Outlook or your phone's calendar. It has every gazetted holiday and the long weekends that need 1 day of leave or none.
