Questions

How do I make a packing list for a trip?

Choose the kind of trip, how many days, the month, who is going and how you travel. The maker builds a list from about 92 common items, with counts for your number of days, and you tick things off as you pack.

How many clothes should I pack for a 7-day trip?

About one top a day plus one spare (8), one pair of trousers or jeans for every 3 days (3), one set of underwear a day plus one (8) and 7 pairs of socks. For longer trips, pack for about a week and wash clothes there.

What should I pack for a baby or kids?

For a baby: about 6 diapers a day, wipes, one set of clothes a day plus 2 spare, food or formula, a blanket and their medicines. For kids: clothes for each day plus 2 spare, their ID, medicines, a bottle, snacks and something to keep them busy.

Can I take a power bank on a flight in India?

Yes, but only in your cabin bag, never in check-in. Keep it with you, not in the overhead bin, and don't use it during the flight. Many airlines allow up to 2 power banks of 100 Wh or less.

Are my ticks saved?

Yes, on this device only, in your browser. Nothing is sent to us. Clear them with the Clear ticks button.

Can I print the list or send it to my family?

Yes. Use Print for a paper list, Copy list to paste it anywhere, or Share on WhatsApp to send the list as text. It has only the items, not your name or details.
