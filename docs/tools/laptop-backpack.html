Which backpack fits my laptop? Laptop backpack size guide

A free tool. Updated 3 Nov 2026

Quick answer

  • Laptop sizes are the screen measured corner to corner, in inches. A "15.6 inch" laptop is about 36 cm wide.
  • A bag "for 15.6 inch laptops" fits most 15.6 inch and smaller laptops. For 16 or 17 inch laptops, look for a bag that says 17 inch.
  • Gaming laptops are often thicker and wider, so check your laptop's width and the bag's sleeve size.

Questions

Will a 15.6 inch laptop fit in a 15 inch bag?

Sometimes, but it can be tight. A bag made for 15.6 inch laptops is the safer choice.

What size backpack do I need for a 17 inch laptop?

One that says it fits 17 inch laptops. These bags usually hold 28 litres or more.

How do I know my laptop's size?

The size is the screen, measured corner to corner. It's usually in the laptop's name or on the box, or measure the width of the laptop itself in cm.
