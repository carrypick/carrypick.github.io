Trolley bag set of 3: sizes, total weight and what to check

Updated 23 Nov 2026 · 6 min read

A trolley bag set of 3 gives you a cabin, a medium and a large bag in one box, usually about 55, 66–69 and 76–79 cm tall. It's handy for families and for anyone setting up their luggage for the first time. Before you buy one, check two things the box doesn't make obvious: the cabin bag's width and depth, and the large bag's total size.

Quick answer

  • Lightest set of 3 we read: 8.03 kg for all three, the Kamiliant Rock-Lite set.
  • Set of 3 with a TSA lock: the American Tourister Liftoff+ set, 8.6 kg for all three, with a 3-year international warranty.
  • Polycarbonate set: the Safari Thorium Neo set, 10.5 kg for all three.
  • Check the large bag. Two of these sets list a large bag of 164–167 cm in total, over the 158 cm that most Indian airlines allow for a checked bag.
  • Only need two? A set of 2 (cabin and medium) suits couples and short trips.
  • Going abroad to study? See our study abroad packing list, with student baggage allowances and lists for the USA, the UK, Canada, Australia and Germany.

The large bag: check the total

Most Indian airlines allow a checked bag up to 158 cm in total (length + width + height). Two sets list the large bag's full size:

SetLarge bag, listed sizeTotal
Kamiliant Rock-Lite79 x 55 x 33 cm167 cm
Safari Thorium Neo77 x 56 x 31 cm164 cm

Both are over 158 cm on paper. Airlines don't measure every bag, but a bag over the limit can be charged as oversize. If you fly with 23 kg pieces abroad, also see trolley bag size for international travel and the luggage size chart.

The cabin bag: check width and depth

The small bag in a set is sold as "55 cm", but that's only the height. IndiGo wants 55 x 35 x 25 cm and Air India 55 x 40 x 20 cm, so a 55 cm bag can still be too wide or too deep. Most set listings don't give each bag's full size, so measure the cabin bag when it arrives and test it in our cabin bag checker. Limits for every airline are in carry-on size by airline.

If you mostly fly one airline, it can make sense to buy a cabin bag that fits it, and a single medium or large bag, instead of a set. See how to pick a cabin trolley.

Set or single bags?

A set suits you if...Single bags suit you if...
You need all three sizes nowYou need one or two sizes
The whole family travels togetherYou want a cabin bag that fits your airline exactly
You want matching bags that are easy to spot on the beltYou want the lightest bag in each size
You have room at home: smaller bags can often be stored inside the large oneYou want different materials, such as a soft cabin bag

How much can you pack?

On a 15 kg domestic fare, a medium bag of about 3 kg leaves about 12 kg for your things. On a 23 kg fare abroad, a large bag of about 3.5–4.5 kg leaves 18.5–19.5 kg. The excess baggage calculator does the maths for your airline. Not sure which size holds what? See trolley bag capacity in kg and litres.

Questions

What sizes are in a trolley bag set of 3?

Usually a cabin bag of about 55 cm, a medium bag of 66–69 cm and a large bag of 76–79 cm. In inches that's roughly 22, 26 and 30.

Is a set of 3 trolley bags worth it?

It is if you need all three sizes. If you only need one or two, or you want a cabin bag that fits your airline exactly, single bags are often the better choice.

How much does a set of 3 trolley bags weigh?

The sets we read list 8.03 to 10.6 kg for all three bags together. Each bag's weight counts against its own baggage allowance.

Can the small bag in a set go in the cabin?

Often, but check its full size. A 55 cm bag can still be too wide for IndiGo (35 cm) or too deep for Air India (20 cm).

Do the bags fit inside each other?

Smaller bags can often be stored inside the large one, which saves space at home. Check the listing or the box, because not every set is made to nest.
