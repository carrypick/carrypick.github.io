How to choose an anniversary gift

  • Choose something that lasts. An anniversary is about years together, so a good watch or a leather wallet fits better than something that's used up in a week.
  • Plan time together. A short trip, a dinner or a day out is often the real gift. A bag or packing cubes for that trip turns it into something they can hold.
  • For parents, think "pair". A couple watch set or two matching things say "for both of you". It's hard to go wrong with a pair watch set.
  • Check the colour and the strap. One Amazon page can show several colours. Gold-tone, silver or rose gold should match what they already wear.
  • Add a handwritten card. The words often matter more than the gift. See the card ideas below.
  • Order a few days early. Anniversaries have a fixed date, so check the delivery date before you pay.

What to write in an anniversary card

Keep it short and true. Pick a line and add your own memory:

  • To your husband: "Happy anniversary. Another year of you stealing the blanket, and I'd still choose you every time."
  • To your wife: "Happy anniversary to the woman who makes our house a home. Thank you for every year so far."
  • To your parents: "Happy anniversary, Mummy and Papa. You showed us what love looks like. Thank you for everything."
  • For a 25th or 50th: "25 years (or 50!) of standing by each other. You're our favourite love story. Happy anniversary."
  • To a couple: "Happy anniversary to two people who make each other better. Wishing you many more happy years."

Then add one line about a moment you shared, like your first trip or the day you met. That's what makes the card worth keeping.

Questions

What is a good anniversary gift for a husband?

A classic watch like the Titan Classique, a leather wallet gift set, or a perfume gift set. If you're planning a trip, a weekender bag makes the gift part of the trip.

What can I gift my wife on our anniversary?

An elegant watch like the Titan Karishma or the Titan Raga, a perfume gift set with four small bottles, or a work tote she'll carry every day. A trip together with a handwritten card is a gift too.

What is the best anniversary gift for parents?

A pair watch set like Titan Bandhan or Sonata Bandhan, which says "for both of you". For a 25th or 50th anniversary, a Timex couple set or a cabin trolley for a family trip or pilgrimage.

What gift can I give a couple, like my sister and brother-in-law?

Something they'll use together: a set of trolley bags for their trips, a perfume set with scents for him and her, or a steel bottle that keeps tea hot. Our wedding gift guide has more couple gifts.

What should I write in an anniversary card?

Wish them a happy anniversary, say one thing you love about them as a couple, and add a shared memory. See our sample lines for a husband, a wife, parents and couples above.
