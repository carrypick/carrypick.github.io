Safari vs Skybags: trolley bags compared by the facts

Updated 24 Nov 2026 · 7 min read

Safari and Skybags are both popular for young travellers and first trolleys in India. Skybags belongs to VIP Industries, and Safari to Safari Industries. We compared what their Amazon.in listings say and what each brand's own website says about the warranty. We haven't tested these bags, so nothing here tells you how long they last.

Quick answer

Safari or Skybags, which is better? It depends on the size you need:

  • Lightest large bag: Skybags. Its Swirl 75 cm is 3.6 kg and 152 cm in total. Safari's Astra Neo 76 cm is 4.3 kg and 155 cm. Both are within the 158 cm check-in limit.
  • Polycarbonate shells: Safari. Most of the Safari bags we picked are polycarbonate. The Skybags bags we picked are ABS or polypropylene.
  • Cabin bag that fits IndiGo: Safari. Its 55 x 35 x 25 cm bag fits IndiGo, Akasa Air and SpiceJet. Neither Skybags cabin bag fits the five airlines we checked by listed size.
  • Warranty: check the listing and the card. Our Skybags cabin picks list 5 years and the Swirl 3 years. Safari's 66 cm Pentagon Pro and Benson list 5 years, its other picks 3.

Cabin bags: check the full size

By listed size, against the cabin limits of IndiGo, Air India, Air India Express, Akasa Air and SpiceJet:

  • Safari 55 cm with laptop pocket (55 x 35 x 25 cm): fits IndiGo, Akasa Air and SpiceJet.
  • Safari Astra Neo 56 (56 x 36 x 23 cm): fits Air India Express.
  • Skybags Splash (37 x 23 x 55 cm): 1 cm too wide for Air India Express and 2 cm too wide for IndiGo.
  • Skybags Trooper (38 x 21 x 55 cm): too wide for IndiGo and 1 cm too deep for Air India.

Being over doesn't mean you'll be stopped every time, but it's your risk. Test any bag in our cabin bag checker. The Trooper has 4 wheels rather than 8: see 4 wheel vs 8 wheel trolleys.

Questions

Safari or Skybags: which is better?

Neither wins on everything. In the bags we compared, Skybags had the lighter large bag, and Safari had more polycarbonate shells and cabin sizes that fit IndiGo or Air India Express. We haven't tested them, so we can't say which lasts longer.

Is Skybags a VIP brand?

Yes. Skybags belongs to VIP Industries Ltd, and VIP's warranty terms name it. Skybags bags are sold on VIP's own website.

Are Safari and Skybags Indian brands?

Yes. Safari belongs to Safari Industries (India) Ltd and Skybags to VIP Industries Ltd. Both companies are based in Mumbai.

What is the warranty on Skybags trolley bags?

On VIP's own website, Skybags luggage has 3 years from the invoice date, for manufacturing defects. On Amazon.in, the Skybags cabin bags we picked list 5 years and the Swirl 3 years. Check the card on the bag you receive.

Safari or Skybags for a backpack?

This guide compares trolleys only. Note that VIP's terms give Skybags backpacks 6 months of warranty. See our backpack guides.
