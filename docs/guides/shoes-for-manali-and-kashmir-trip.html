Shoes for a Manali, Kashmir or Gulmarg trip in the snow

Updated 23 Nov 2026 · 6 min read

In Manali, Kashmir and Gulmarg in winter, your shoes matter more than your jacket. Snow melts into slush by midday, paths freeze again at night, and ordinary sports shoes get wet in minutes. You need one pair that keeps water out and grips on ice. Here's how to choose, and the snow shoes we picked by their listed specs.

Quick answer

  • For snow points like Solang, Gulmarg and Sonamarg: waterproof boots or waterproof hiking shoes with a deep-grip rubber sole.
  • For one snow day only: you can rent gumboots there, and wear your walking shoes in town.
  • Avoid mesh sports shoes. Mesh lets snow water in.
  • Buy half a size up only if you'll wear two pairs of thick socks. Check with our shoe size converter.

  • The snow points are colder than the towns. Solang, the Atal Tunnel and the top of the Gulmarg Gondola are much higher, and windy.
  • Snow turns to slush in the sun. By afternoon you walk through cold water, not dry powder.
  • Mornings are icy. Paths and hotel steps freeze overnight, so grip matters as much as warmth.

For the weather, roads and snow chances, read Manali in winter, Kashmir in December and Gulmarg in December.

  • Gumboots are fine for one snow day at Solang or in Gulmarg. Shops in Manali, near Solang, in Tangmarg and in Gulmarg rent them by the day with snow suits. Check the size and that they're dry inside before you pay.
  • Hiking shoes are the most useful buy if you trek or travel in the monsoon too.
  • Winter boots suit people who feel the cold, and anyone staying several days in the snow.

What "waterproof" means on a listing

  • Waterproof shoes are made to keep water out when you stand in slush.
  • Water resistant shoes handle splashes and light snow only.
  • Listings don't always agree with themselves. Our Columbia men's pick says "waterproof" in its title and "water resistant" in its specs, so we show both.
  • Water comes in over the top. Even waterproof shoes fill up in deep snow. Tuck waterproof trousers over the top, or rent gumboots for the deep snow.

Socks, size and grip

  • Two pairs of woollen socks: one to wear and one dry pair in your day bag. Wet socks make your feet cold fast.
  • Try new shoes with the socks you'll take. Thick socks can take up half a size. Most sizes in India are UK sizes; find yours in US, EU and cm with our shoe size converter, then check the brand's chart on the Amazon page.
  • Look at the sole. Deep lugs grip snow; a flat sole slips on ice.
  • Break them in at home for a few days. Blisters at −5 °C are miserable.
  • Wear the boots on the flight or bus. They're the heaviest thing you pack, and that keeps them out of your bag's weight.

Questions

Which shoes are good for a Manali trip in winter?

Waterproof boots or waterproof hiking shoes with a deep-grip rubber sole. In town, on dry days, comfortable walking shoes are fine, but take the waterproof pair for Solang and the Atal Tunnel.

Can I wear sports shoes in Kashmir snow?

Mesh sports shoes get wet within minutes in snow and slush. Wear waterproof shoes, or rent gumboots for the snow and keep your sports shoes for the car and the hotel.

Can we rent snow boots in Gulmarg or Manali?

Yes. Shops in Tangmarg, Gulmarg, Manali and near Solang rent gumboots and snow suits by the day. They're fine for one snow day, but they're not warm and not comfortable for long walks.

Do I need snow boots for Kashmir in December?

You need shoes that keep water out and grip on ice. Winter boots are the warmest. Waterproof hiking shoes with woollen socks also work for most tourists.

Should I buy a bigger size for snow?

Only if your thick socks make the shoes tight. Try them on with the socks you'll wear. Use our shoe size converter to compare UK, US and EU sizes.

What shoes should kids wear in the snow?

The same idea: waterproof shoes or boots with grip, two pairs of woollen socks, and rented gumboots for the deep snow. Kids' feet get cold faster, so carry dry socks.
