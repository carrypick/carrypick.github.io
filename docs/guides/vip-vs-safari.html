VIP vs Safari: trolley bags compared by the facts

Updated 24 Nov 2026 · 7 min read

VIP and Safari are both Indian luggage companies from Mumbai, and both sell hard polycarbonate trolleys in the same sizes. So the choice comes down to the bag. We compared their Amazon.in listings and read each brand's own warranty page. We haven't tested these bags, so nothing here tells you how long they last.

Quick answer

VIP or Safari, which is better? Neither wins on everything:

  • Medium polycarbonate with a TSA lock: VIP. VIP's Vero 65 cm is 3.1 kg and has a TSA lock. Safari's Astra Neo 66 cm is 3.3 kg with a combination lock.
  • Cabin bag that fits IndiGo: Safari. Safari's 55 x 35 x 25 cm cabin bag fits IndiGo, Akasa Air and SpiceJet by listed size. VIP's Vero cabin is over all five airlines we checked.
  • Longest listed warranty: Safari. Its Pentagon Pro and Benson medium bags list 5 years. Our VIP picks list 3 years, the same as VIP's own site gives for luggage.
  • More choice on Amazon.in: Safari. In our November searches we found 18 Safari medium and large listings, against 3 from VIP.

Questions

VIP or Safari: which is better?

Neither wins on everything. In the bags we compared, VIP's Vero medium was a little lighter and had a TSA lock, while Safari had a cabin bag that fits IndiGo and medium bags listed with 5 years of warranty. We haven't tested them, so we can't say which lasts longer.

Are VIP and Safari Indian companies?

Yes. VIP Industries Ltd and Safari Industries (India) Ltd are both Indian companies based in Mumbai.

Is Safari owned by VIP?

No. They're separate companies. VIP Industries owns VIP, Skybags and Aristocrat. Safari Industries owns Safari and Urban Jungle by Safari.

Does the Safari warranty work abroad?

Safari's warranty page says it applies in more than 200 countries, but you pay to get the bag to them. Keep the warranty card and the invoice.

Does the VIP or Safari warranty cover airline damage?

No. Both exclude damage in transit. Report it to the airline at the airport, with photos.
