Travel adapter by country: which plug you need, from India

Updated 23 Nov 2026 · 11 min read

India uses round-pin plugs (types C, D and M) at 230 V. Most countries don't. This page lists the plug types and voltage for 45 countries, says whether your Indian charger will fit, and tells you which adapter to pack.

Quick answer

  • One universal adapter covers every country on this page. It's the simplest choice if you travel more than once a year.
  • Your thin two-pin Indian charger (type C) fits in much of Europe, Bali, South Korea, Thailand, Vietnam and Turkey. The thick three-pin Indian plug (type D or M) fits almost nowhere outside Nepal, Bhutan and Sri Lanka.
  • Dubai, the UK, Singapore, Malaysia, Hong Kong, Saudi Arabia and Oman use type G (three square pins). You need an adapter.
  • The USA, Canada and Japan use flat pins and only 100 to 120 V. You need an adapter, and your charger must say 100-240V on the label.

The USA and Canada run at 120 V, about half of India's. A phone or laptop charger marked 100-240V is fine with a plain adapter. A 230 V-only hair dryer or kettle from India won't work properly there. See the USA packing list for students, the Canada packing list and the Kenya safari packing list.

Will my Indian charger work abroad?

Look at the small print on the charger or the brick:

  • "Input: 100-240V": works in every country on this page. You only need a plug adapter. Almost all phone, laptop, camera and trimmer chargers say this.
  • "220-240V" or "230V" only: works in India-voltage countries (most of Asia, Europe, the Gulf, the UK, Australia). Don't use it in the USA, Canada or Japan.
  • A travel adapter does not change voltage. All three of our picks say so on their listings. For a 230 V-only appliance in the USA, buy a dual-voltage one instead.

Which adapter should I buy?

  • Going to one country only? A cheap single-country adapter is fine, but you'll need a new one for the next trip.
  • Travelling a few times a year? Buy one universal adapter with pull-out plugs. It covers types A, C, G and I, which between them work in every country above.
  • Taking a phone, earbuds and a watch? Get one with USB-C ports, so you need one wall socket and no Indian charger.
  • Taking a laptop? Check the USB-C output in watts. 60 W to 100 W charges most laptops.
  • Taking a hair dryer or straightener? Use an adapter with no USB ports and a high listed load, and check the voltage first.

Visiting India? Plugs for travellers from the USA, UK and Europe

India uses types C, D and M at 230 V and 50 Hz. Bring a universal adapter:

  • From the USA or Canada: your flat-pin plugs won't fit. Check your chargers say 100-240V; a 120 V-only hair dryer will be damaged by 230 V.
  • From the UK: type G plugs don't fit Indian sockets. Voltage is the same.
  • From Europe: a thin type C plug often fits Indian sockets, but it can be loose, and a thick type E or F plug won't fit. Wikipedia's mains table notes this combination is unsafe, so an adapter is better.

Before you buy

  • We haven't tested these adapters. Plugs, ports and loads come from each Amazon.in listing.
  • Hotels differ. The plug types above are the national lists. A new hotel may have universal sockets; an old guest house may not. A universal adapter covers both.
  • Pack the adapter in your cabin bag with your phone and power bank, so you can charge at the airport.

Questions

Which travel adapter do I need for Thailand from India?

Thailand uses types A, B, C and O at 230 V. Most sockets take a thin two-pin Indian charger (type C), but not a three-pin Indian plug. A universal adapter covers every socket you'll find.

Which adapter do I need for Europe?

Most of Europe uses types C, E and F at 230 V. A thin two-pin Indian charger often fits. A three-pin Indian plug never does, and Switzerland (J) and Italy (L) have their own types. Take a universal adapter or a European type C adapter.

What plug type is used in Dubai and the UAE?

Type G, three square pins, at 230 V, the same plug as the UK. Indian plugs don't fit, so take a UK or universal adapter.

Do I need an adapter for the USA from India?

Yes. The USA uses flat-pin types A and B at 120 V. Your charger must say 100-240V on the label. A 230 V-only hair dryer or straightener from India won't work properly there.

What plug type is used in Singapore and Malaysia?

Mostly type G (three square pins) at 230 V. Type C is on the official lists, but sockets for it are rare. Take a UK or universal adapter.

Which plug is used in Japan?

Type A (two flat pins), and some type B, at 100 V. Indian plugs don't fit. Phone and laptop chargers marked 100-240V work with an adapter.

Does Bali need a travel adapter?

Bali (Indonesia) uses types C and F at 230 V, so a thin two-pin Indian charger usually fits. A three-pin Indian plug won't, so a small adapter is still useful.

Does a travel adapter change the voltage?

No. It only changes the shape of the plug. Phone and laptop chargers marked 100-240V work anywhere. For a 230 V-only appliance in the USA, Canada or Japan you need a voltage converter or a dual-voltage appliance.

What plug type is used in India?

Types C, D and M at 230 V and 50 Hz. Visitors from the USA, UK and most of Europe need an adapter.
