Singapore packing list: what to pack and wear for a trip from India

Updated 21 Nov 2026 · 12 min read

Singapore is hot and humid all year, and it rains on about half the days. From November to January it rains the most. So pack light clothes, an umbrella, comfortable walking shoes and one layer for the cold air conditioning in malls, trains and theatres. Singapore also has strict rules on chewing gum, vapes and cigarettes. This list tells you what to pack and why, for a trip from India.

Singapore weather by month

Long-term averages (1991–2020) at Changi from the Meteorological Service Singapore:

MonthRainRain days
NovemberAbout 252 mmAbout 19
DecemberAbout 332 mm, the wettest monthAbout 19
JanuaryAbout 222 mmAbout 13
FebruaryAbout 105 mm, the driest monthAbout 9
  • It's warm every day. Days usually reach 31–33 °C and nights rarely drop below 23–25 °C. December averages about 30.5 °C by day and 24 °C at night.
  • It's humid. December's average humidity is about 85%. Cotton and linen feel best.
  • November to January is the wettest time. The Northeast Monsoon runs from December to early March. Showers are often heavy but short, so an umbrella or poncho is enough.
  • Malls, the MRT and cinemas are cold. Carry one light layer.

The short list

If you pack only these, you'll be fine:

  1. Passport with your Singapore visa, printed tickets and hotel bookings
  2. Your SG Arrival Card, submitted online in the 3 days before you land
  3. Light cotton or linen clothes, one outfit for each day
  4. One light jacket, shirt or stole for cold AC and flights
  5. Comfortable walking shoes, plus flip-flops or sandals
  6. A small umbrella or rain poncho
  7. Sunscreen, sunglasses and a cap
  8. Swimwear, if your hotel has a pool or you're going to Sentosa
  9. A universal travel adapter
  10. Phone charger and a power bank, in your cabin bag

Documents and money

  • Visa. Indian passport holders need a visa to enter Singapore. ICA says to apply through an authorised visa agent appointed by Singapore's missions in India, or at a Singapore mission. You can't get a visa at the immigration counter, and a visa doesn't guarantee entry.
  • Passport. Keep it on you, not in your check-in bag, and make sure it's valid well beyond your return date. ICA's Entry Requirements Checker on its website shows the rules for your trip. Carry a photocopy and a photo of it on your phone.
  • SG Arrival Card. Every visitor must submit it online within 3 days before arrival, counting the day you land. It's free on ICA's website or the MyICA app. ICA warns that it doesn't endorse sites that charge a fee to submit it for you.
  • Print or save your bookings: return ticket, hotel bookings and travel insurance.
  • Cash. RBI lets a bank or money changer sell you up to US$3,000 in foreign currency notes per trip, and you can take up to ₹25,000 in Indian notes. Cards work almost everywhere in Singapore, so a forex card keeps the cash small. See how much cash you can carry abroad.
  • Carrying more than S$20,000 in cash? Singapore asks you to declare it online on Form NP 727 before you arrive or leave.

What to wear in Singapore

There's no dress code on the streets. Shorts, T-shirts, dresses and skirts are normal everywhere: Orchard Road, Sentosa, Universal Studios and the hawker centres.

  • Women: cotton dresses, skirts, shorts with light tops, or loose trousers and kurtas. A light stole for AC malls, flights and temples.
  • Men: shorts or chinos with T-shirts, polos or cotton shirts. One pair of long trousers for dinners and the flight.
  • Temples and mosques: cover your shoulders and knees, and be ready to take your shoes off. A stole or a long skirt or trousers in your day bag is enough.
  • One nicer outfit for a rooftop bar, a show or a good dinner. Some bars and restaurants don't allow slippers or shorts in the evening.
  • Universal Studios, the zoo and Sentosa: light clothes, a cap and shoes you can walk in all day. Some rides get you wet, so a quick-dry T-shirt helps.
  • Buying clothes online for the trip? Check your size first with our clothing size converter, then the brand's own size chart.

What to wear in Singapore in December

December is the wettest month, but it's still about 30 °C by day. Pack the same summer clothes, plus:

  • A small umbrella or a poncho, in your day bag every day
  • Shoes that dry fast, or sandals with straps for rainy days
  • One light jacket for cold malls and the flight home
  • A plastic or zip bag for a wet umbrella or clothes

What not to pack

  • Chewing gum. Singapore Customs bans it, except dental and medicinal gum approved by the Health Sciences Authority.
  • Vapes and e-cigarettes. Singapore prohibits e-cigarettes and vaporisers.
  • Cigarettes, if you don't want to pay tax. Singapore has no duty-free allowance for cigarettes or tobacco. Declare them at the Red Channel and pay the tax.
  • More alcohol than the allowance. After at least 2 days away (and not arriving from Malaysia), adults can bring 2 litres in one of these mixes: 1 litre of spirits with 1 litre of wine or beer, 1 litre each of wine and beer, or 2 litres of wine or beer.
  • More than S$500 of new goods. That's the GST relief for travellers away 2 days or more. Above it, declare and pay GST.
  • A power bank in your check-in bag. It goes only in your cabin bag.
  • Big bottles in your cabin bag. Each liquid must be 100 ml or less, in one clear 1-litre bag. Bigger bottles go in check-in.

Singapore Customs says not declaring goods at the Red Channel can lead to fines or prosecution. Not sure about an item on the flight? Search our can I carry it on a flight? list.

Questions

What should I pack for a Singapore trip from India?

Light cotton clothes, one layer for cold AC, walking shoes and flip-flops, a small umbrella or poncho, sunscreen and sunglasses, swimwear for the pool or Sentosa, a universal adapter, your medicines with the prescription and a power bank in your cabin bag. Don't forget your visa and the SG Arrival Card.

What should women wear in Singapore?

Dresses, skirts, shorts and light tops are normal everywhere. Carry a light stole for cold malls and flights, and to cover your shoulders at temples and mosques. Wear shoes you can walk in all day.

What should men wear in Singapore?

Shorts, T-shirts, polos and cotton shirts for most days. Take one pair of long trousers for nicer dinners and temples. Some bars don't allow slippers or shorts in the evening.

What should I pack for Singapore in December?

Summer clothes, because it's about 30 °C by day, plus an umbrella or poncho: December is Singapore's wettest month, with about 332 mm of rain on about 19 days. Add shoes that dry fast and one light jacket for AC.

Do Indians need a visa for Singapore?

Yes. ICA lists India among the countries that need a visa. Apply through an authorised visa agent appointed by Singapore's missions in India, or at a Singapore mission. You can't get one on arrival.

What is the SG Arrival Card?

An online form every visitor submits within 3 days before arriving in Singapore. It's free on ICA's website and the MyICA app. Don't pay sites that offer to file it for you.

Can I take chewing gum to Singapore?

No. Chewing gum is a prohibited import. Only dental and medicinal gum approved by the Health Sciences Authority is allowed.

How much baggage can I take from India to Singapore?

On IndiGo, 30 kg on regular fares (35 kg on UpFront). Singapore Airlines gives 25 kg on Lite and Value and 30 kg on Standard and Flexi, and Air India Express 30 kg on Value fares. All allow a 7 kg cabin bag.
