School bag size chart by class and age

Updated 21 Nov 2026 · 6 min read

A school bag that's too big gets filled with too much, and a heavy bag hurts a child's back. Here's what size suits each class, and how heavy it should be, using India's School Bag Policy 2020.

School bag weight: the 10% rule (School Bag Policy 2020)

India's Ministry of Education shared the School Bag Policy 2020 with the states in late 2020. It gives a class-wise guide for school bag weight at about 10% of body weight, as ThePrint reported on 8 Dec 2020. Two examples from it:

  • Class 1 and 2: children of about 16–22 kg, so a bag of 1.6–2.2 kg.
  • Class 11 and 12: students of about 35–50 kg, so a bag of 3.5–5 kg.

The policy also says schools should spread textbook weight fairly across the days of the timetable, gives no homework up to class 2, and suggests schools keep a weighing machine to check bags.

To work out your own child's limit, divide their weight by 10:

Weigh the packed bag on a bathroom scale: weigh yourself holding it, then without it, and take the difference.

The empty bag counts too

A bag's own weight is part of the 10%. A 560 g bag is already a quarter of a 2 kg limit for a class 1 child. So for small children, pick a light bag and don't buy bigger than needed: extra space only gets filled.

How to check the size fits your child

  • Height: the top of the bag should sit a little below the shoulders, and the bottom shouldn't hang far below the waist.
  • Width: the bag shouldn't be wider than the child's back.
  • Straps: two wide, padded straps, both worn, tightened so the bag sits close to the back.
  • Heavy things close to the back: put the big books in the compartment nearest the back.

A bag size in cm is usually written as height x width x depth. To turn cm into litres, multiply the three and divide by 1,000. Our backpack size chart in litres explains more.

School bag size for class 1

About 12–18 litres and 35–38 cm tall, for a 6 year old. At 16–22 kg, the whole bag should weigh about 1.6–2.2 kg, so choose a light bag with a bottle pocket. A 23 L bag like the uppercase Sprout is fine if it isn't overfilled.

School bag size for class 5 to 8

20–30 litres suits most 9 to 13 year olds. Two main compartments help keep books apart from the lunch box and water bottle.

School bag size for class 10

About 30–37 litres and 46–50 cm tall. Older students carry more books and often a geometry box and a laptop or tablet. If they need a laptop, check the sleeve size with our laptop backpack tool.

Questions

What size school bag for a 7 year old?

About 12–18 litres and 35–38 cm tall. Most 7 year olds are in class 1 or 2.

What size school bag for an 8 or 9 year old?

About 18–25 litres and 38–43 cm tall, for class 3 to 4.

What is the school bag weight limit in India?

The School Bag Policy 2020 suggests about 10% of the child's body weight. For class 1 and 2 that's about 1.6–2.2 kg, and for class 11 and 12 about 3.5–5 kg.

What is a school bag size in inches?

Bags are usually sold in cm and litres. A 38 cm bag is about 15 inches tall and a 46 cm bag about 18 inches.

Is a 30 litre bag too big for school?

For class 8 and up, 30 L is fine. For a young child, it's usually too big: it reaches below the waist and tends to get overfilled.

Should I buy a school bag with wheels?

Wheels add weight and are hard on stairs. A light bag with two padded straps is usually the better choice.
