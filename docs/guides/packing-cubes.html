Packing cubes: are they worth it, and which set to buy?

Updated 23 Nov 2026 · 5 min read

Packing cubes are zip bags that sort your clothes inside a suitcase or backpack: shirts in one, underwear in another, shoes in their own bag. You find things without unpacking everything, and a bag opened at security or customs closes again quickly. They don't make clothes lighter, though. Here's how to choose, and the sets we picked by their listed specs.

Quick answer

  • Are they worth it? Yes, if you live out of a bag for a week or more, share a bag, or move hotels a lot.
  • Regular cubes sort your things. Compression cubes have an extra zip that squeezes clothes flatter.
  • They don't save weight. A full cube weighs the same as the clothes, plus the cube.
  • A set of 4 to 6 covers a week. Pick sizes that fit your bag.

Watch out forBuying sizes too big for your bagA squeezed bag that goes over the weight limit

Compression is most useful for bulky things: sweaters, thermals and jackets on a winter trip. For a beach week of cotton clothes, regular cubes are enough.

They save space, not weight

This is the mistake people make with compression cubes. You fit more in, so the bag gets heavier. On a 7 kg cabin fare or a 15 kg check-in fare, that can mean extra baggage fees at the counter.

  • Weigh the packed bag at home. A luggage scale does it in a second.
  • Or check your packed weight against your fare with our bag weight calculator.
  • The empty cubes count too: our sets weigh 220 to 323 g.

How to pack cubes

  • Roll soft clothes like T-shirts and leggings. Fold shirts and trousers flat.
  • Big cube at the bottom of the bag, by the wheels. Smaller ones on top.
  • One cube per outfit type, or one per person when you share a bag.
  • Keep one empty bag for dirty laundry on the way home.
  • Don't overfill a compression cube. If the zip strains, take something out, or the zip may split.

Questions

Are packing cubes worth it?

For a trip of a week or more, a shared bag or several hotel moves, yes. They keep clothes sorted and make a bag easy to repack. For a weekend with one small backpack, you can do without.

Do packing cubes save space?

Regular cubes save a little, because clothes are packed tightly and neatly. Compression cubes save more, with an extra zip that presses clothes flatter.

Do compression packing cubes reduce weight?

No. They make clothes take less space, not weigh less. A fuller bag is a heavier bag, so weigh it before you go.

Which packing cubes are good for a cabin bag?

Small and medium ones. Check the sizes in the listing against the inside of your bag. Our Mokobara set lists 33 x 27 x 3.5 cm for its pieces, and the Tressential cubes list 23 x 3 x 34 cm.

How many packing cubes do I need for a week?

Three or four cubes and a shoe bag are enough for most people: one for tops, one for bottoms, one for underwear and socks, and one spare for laundry.

Can airport security open packing cubes?

Yes, security can open anything in your bag. Cubes make it quick to put things back. Liquids in your cabin bag still go in one clear bag; see can I carry liquids on a flight?
