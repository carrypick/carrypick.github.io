Honeymoon packing list: what to pack for the bride, the groom and the trip

Updated 21 Nov 2026 · 11 min read

Most Indian couples leave for the honeymoon a few days after the wedding, when everyone is tired and the house is full of guests. That's when things get forgotten. This list keeps it simple: what you both need, what the bride and groom should pack, and what changes for a beach honeymoon like the Maldives, Bali, Goa or Kerala, or a hill honeymoon like Manali or Kashmir.

The short list

If you pack only these, you'll be fine:

  1. ID for both of you, tickets, hotel bookings, and passports and visas if you're going abroad
  2. Two or three outfits each for photos, and one special-dinner outfit each
  3. Clothes for the weather: summer clothes for a beach, warm layers for the hills
  4. Comfortable shoes, plus flip-flops or snow boots
  5. Sunscreen, sunglasses and lip balm
  6. Your regular medicines, with the prescription
  7. A small pouch for the few jewellery pieces you take
  8. Phone chargers, one power bank and an adapter if you're going abroad
  9. A small day bag for outings
  10. Some cash and a card each

Documents and money

  • ID for both of you. Hotels in India ask every guest for photo ID, so carry Aadhaar, a driving licence or a passport for each of you.
  • Abroad: passports and visas. The name on your ticket must match the name in the passport exactly. If the bride's passport still has her old name, book the ticket in that name.
  • Print or save your bookings: tickets, hotel vouchers, transfers and travel insurance. Keep a copy on both phones.
  • Split your cash and cards between the two of you, so one lost wallet doesn't stop the trip.
  • Going abroad? RBI lets a bank or money changer sell you up to US$3,000 in foreign currency notes per trip. See how much cash you can carry abroad.

Outfits for honeymoon photos

  • Two or three outfits each for photos. Bright colours and white stand out against blue water, green hills and snow.
  • Matching or colour-coordinated outfits, if you like couple photos: the same colour, or one colour and white.
  • One special-dinner outfit each, for a candlelight dinner, a beach dinner or a nice restaurant.
  • One Indian outfit each, like a light saree or a kurta set, if you want a few traditional photos. Choose a fabric that doesn't crush.
  • Pack outfits on hangers inside a garment cover, or roll them in a packing cube, so they don't come out creased.

Honeymoon packing list for the bride

  • Three or four day outfits: dresses, tops with skirts or trousers, or kurtas
  • Two or three photo outfits and one dinner dress or saree
  • Swimwear and a kaftan or sarong for a beach honeymoon
  • A light stole: for flights, AC rooms, temples and cool evenings
  • Nightwear for each night, and comfortable loungewear
  • Flat sandals or flip-flops, and one pair of dressier sandals. Heels are hard on sand, jetties and hill paths.
  • Your skincare and makeup in small bottles, makeup remover wipes and a hair brush
  • A few bangles or light jewellery, not your heavy wedding sets
  • A crossbody bag that zips shut

Beach honeymoon: Maldives, Bali, Goa and Kerala

Pack summer clothes, swimwear and lots of sun care.

  • Swimwear: two sets each, so one can dry, and a kaftan, sarong or shirt to wear over it.
  • Sunscreen, SPF 30 or higher, and an SPF lip balm. Put sunscreen on again every 2 hours and after swimming. See our sunscreen guide.
  • Sunglasses and a hat or cap.
  • A waterproof pouch for phones on boats and snorkelling trips.
  • Mosquito repellent for evenings.

Things to know for each place:

  • Maldives: resorts are relaxed, but Malé and local islands expect modest clothes. Alcohol bought on the way is confiscated on arrival, and idols are banned. Seaplanes have low bag limits. See our Maldives packing list.
  • Bali: hot all year, about 33 °C by day, and very rainy from December to February. Temples need a sarong. See our Bali packing list.
  • Goa: days about 32–33 °C and nights about 20–21 °C in December and January, with almost no rain. See our Goa packing list and Goa trip guide.
  • Kerala: the coast is about 21–34 °C in December and January, but Munnar nights fall to about 2–9 °C. Pack a warm layer if you're going to Munnar. See our Kerala trip guide.

Hill honeymoon: Manali and Kashmir in winter

Pack warm layers. Nights are below freezing in winter.

  • Thermals: two sets each, worn under your clothes.
  • A warm jacket each, with a hood.
  • Sweaters or fleece, a cap, gloves and a muffler.
  • Shoes with a good grip, waterproof if you'll walk in snow, and woollen socks.
  • Lip balm and a moisturiser. Cold wind dries your skin and lips.
  • Sunglasses for snow, which is very bright.

Things to know for each place:

  • Kashmir: in December, Srinagar averages about 10 °C by day and −1 °C at night, and Gulmarg about 4 °C and −5 °C. Prepaid SIMs from other states usually don't work there, so carry a postpaid SIM. See Kashmir in December.
  • Manali: Himachal Tourism says nights can fall below −5 °C in December and January, and snow is possible but not certain. See our Manali trip guide and what to wear in Manali in December.

Jewellery and valuables

  • Leave heavy wedding jewellery at home. Take a few light pieces, or good imitation jewellery, for photos.
  • Keep the jewellery you take on you or in your cabin bag, never in a check-in bag.
  • Use the hotel room safe for jewellery, passports and extra cash.
  • Going abroad with gold? Read our Indian customs rules before you fly back.

Which bags to take

  • About 5 nights at a beach: one cabin trolley each, or one cabin bag each plus one shared medium check-in bag. Summer clothes are light.
  • A week or more, or the hills: one shared medium or large check-in bag for warm clothes, plus a cabin bag each.
  • Seaplane or small plane? Pack light. Seaplane limits in the Maldives are 15–20 kg of check-in luggage per person.
  • Check your fare's baggage before you pay. Compare airlines on our domestic baggage rules and international baggage rules pages, or measure your bag with the cabin bag checker.

What not to pack

  • Heavy wedding jewellery and expensive lehengas or sherwanis. They're hard to carry and worry about.
  • A power bank in your check-in bag. It goes only in your cabin bag.
  • Big bottles in your cabin bag. Each liquid must be 100 ml or less, in one clear 1-litre bag. Bigger bottles go in check-in.
  • Perfume in your cabin bag on IndiGo. Put it in your check-in bag.
  • Alcohol and idols to the Maldives. Both are confiscated or banned on arrival.
  • Too many outfits. Two or three photo outfits each are enough. Leave space for shopping.

Questions

What should I pack for my honeymoon?

ID or passports, tickets and bookings, two or three photo outfits and one dinner outfit each, clothes for the weather, comfortable shoes, sunscreen and sunglasses, your medicines, a few light jewellery pieces, chargers and one power bank in your cabin bag.

What should a bride pack for her honeymoon?

Three or four day outfits, two or three photo outfits and one dinner dress or saree, swimwear and a kaftan for a beach, nightwear, flat sandals, a light stole, skincare and makeup in small bottles, and a few light jewellery pieces instead of heavy wedding sets.

What should the groom pack for the honeymoon?

Shorts and T-shirts or warm layers, two or three shirts for photos, one dinner outfit, swim shorts, walking shoes and sandals, sunglasses, a cap and a shaving kit.

What should we pack for a beach honeymoon?

Two swimsuits each, a kaftan or shirt to wear over them, light clothes, flip-flops, sunscreen, an SPF lip balm, sunglasses, a hat, mosquito repellent and a waterproof pouch for your phones.

What should we pack for a honeymoon in Kashmir or Manali in winter?

Two sets of thermals each, a warm hooded jacket, sweaters, a cap, gloves, woollen socks, shoes with a good grip, lip balm and sunglasses for the snow. Gulmarg averages about −5 °C at night in December.

Should I take my wedding jewellery on the honeymoon?

It's better not to. Take a few light pieces or imitation jewellery for photos, keep them in your cabin bag or on you, and use the hotel safe.

How many bags should a couple take on a honeymoon?

For about 5 nights at a beach, a cabin bag each is often enough. For a week or the hills, share one medium check-in bag and take a cabin bag each. Pack light if you're taking a seaplane.
