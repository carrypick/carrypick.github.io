Polycarbonate vs polypropylene trolley bags: which is better?

Updated 26 Nov 2026 · 8 min read

Polycarbonate or polypropylene: which is better? Neither, as a rule. Luggage sellers usually call polycarbonate (PC) tougher and polypropylene (PP) lighter and cheaper. But they don't agree on which resists cracks better. In the listings we read, PP was only about 0.2 kg lighter. So choose by the bag: its own weight, its full size and its warranty.

We haven't tested these materials, and we didn't find independent drop tests. What follows is what sellers say, re-read on 26 Nov 2026, and what real listings weigh.

Quick answer

  • Is polypropylene luggage good? Yes, for most trips. It's the usual low-cost hard shell, and many check-in bags use it, including American Tourister's Liftoff+.
  • Is polycarbonate stronger? Travelpro calls it the sturdier choice. Nasher Miles' own summary says PP is the most crack-resistant. Sellers disagree, so don't pay extra for "strength" alone.
  • Is PP lighter? A little. Typical PP medium and large bags were 0.2 to 0.25 kg lighter. In cabin bags, PC was lighter in our small sample.
  • What protects you more: the warranty. Nasher Miles, for example, gives 3 years on its PP shells and 2 years on its PC shells. Most brands exclude airline damage either way.

Where they disagree: Travelpro says PC is "the sturdier choice". Nasher Miles' summary says PP is "the most crack-resistant", though its own table rates PC's longevity higher. Both are sellers, so treat words like "unbreakable" or "scratch-proof" as marketing.

Is polycarbonate luggage durable, breakable or waterproof?

  • Durable: sellers describe it as tough and flexible, so it bends rather than cracks under a hit. No independent test we found says how many trips it lasts.
  • Breakable: any hard shell can crack. Safari's warranty excludes cracks and dents, and American Tourister's, Kamiliant's and VIP's exclude airline damage. So a crack from a baggage belt is usually the airline's claim, not the brand's.
  • Scratch-resistant: some PC bags have a coating; VIP, for example, calls its Vero, Pod NXT and Barclay shells "scratch-resistant polycarbonate". Glossy shells still show scuffs, and scratches are excluded from the warranties we read.
  • Waterproof: a hard shell sheds rain better than fabric, and Travelpro calls PC water resistant. The zip is still a gap, so pack electronics in a pouch. Safari's and American Tourister's warranties exclude water damage.
  • Heavy: not really. Typical PC medium bags we read were 3.5 kg, against 3.3 kg for PP.

Is polypropylene luggage durable?

Sellers say it's crack-resistant and flexible, but less rigid than PC. Two cautions come from the same sellers: Travelpro says the colour can fade in UV light, and Nasher Miles says high heat may warp it. Don't leave a PP bag in a hot car boot or on a sunny terrace for days.

Questions

Is polypropylene luggage good?

Yes, for most trips. It's the usual low-cost hard shell, it's light, and sellers call it crack-resistant. Its colour can fade in strong sun, and high heat may warp it, according to two sellers' guides.

Is polypropylene luggage better than polycarbonate?

Not clearly. Travelpro calls PC the sturdier choice. Nasher Miles' summary calls PP the most crack-resistant, and gives PP shells a longer warranty (3 years against 2). In the listings we read, PP was only about 0.2 kg lighter.

Polycarbonate vs polypropylene trolley: which is better?

Choose by the bag, not the label. Compare the bag's empty weight, its full size in cm and the warranty length. A 1 kg difference in weight matters on a 15 kg fare; a 0.2 kg difference doesn't.

Is polycarbonate luggage durable?

Sellers describe it as tough, flexible and highly resistant to cracks. We didn't find independent tests, and we haven't tested it ourselves. A long warranty is better evidence than a material name.

Is polycarbonate luggage breakable?

It can crack or dent under a hard enough hit, like any hard shell. Safari's warranty excludes cracks and dents, and most brands exclude airline damage. If an airline cracks your bag, report it at the airport.

Is polycarbonate luggage waterproof?

Not fully. The shell sheds rain, and Travelpro calls PC water resistant, but water can get in through the zip. The warranties we read from Safari and American Tourister exclude water damage.

Is polypropylene lighter than polycarbonate?

Usually, but not by much: 0.2 to 0.25 kg lighter in the medium and large bags we read. In cabin bags, polycarbonate was slightly lighter, from a small sample.

Polycarbonate vs polypropylene vs ABS luggage: which is best?

Of the three, ABS gets the most cautious notes from sellers. Travelpro says it can be damaged by high heat or rough handling and suggests it for infrequent travel. Only 2 of the listings we read named ABS.

Is polypropylene good for check-in luggage?

Yes. Many check-in bags we read were polypropylene, including American Tourister's Liftoff+. Look at the bag's weight, its full size against the 158 cm limit, and its warranty.
