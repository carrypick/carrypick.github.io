How to choose a Christmas gift for a couple

  • Choose something for both, not for one. Pair watches, a luggage set or a perfume pack with scents for each of them feels fair.
  • Think about their plans. A couple who travels a lot will use bags and organisers. A couple who stays home in winter will use warm wear.
  • For couples who have everything, give a trip. A guidebook or a travel journal for a place they want to see is a gift they'll remember.
  • For a married couple, a bigger shared gift works. A set of trolley bags is useful for years, and it's an easy gift to share with siblings.
  • Choose a known brand. Titan, Sonata, Timex, Safari and Aristocrat have service centres in India, so warranty claims are easier. Keep the invoice.
  • Order by mid-December. Deliveries can take longer in Christmas week, so check the delivery date before you pay.

Questions

What is a good Christmas gift for a couple?

Pair watches like the Titan Bandhan, a perfume pack with scents for both, or a luggage set if they travel. Choose something they'll both use.

What do you give a couple who has everything?

A trip, more than a thing: a guidebook or a travel journal for a place they want to see, or a perfume pack with scents they can try together.

What is a good Christmas gift for a couple who travels?

A set of trolley bags, a weekender duffle for short trips, packing cubes they can share, or a passport holder each.

What is a good Christmas gift for married couple friends?

Something for both, not too personal: a perfume pack with scents for men and women, packing cubes, or pair watches if you're close.

When should I order a Christmas gift?

By mid-December. Check the delivery date shown on Amazon before you pay, because deliveries can take longer in Christmas week.
