Umrah packing list: what to carry for Umrah from India, for ladies, men and kids

Updated 25 Nov 2026 · 18 min read

Umrah means many hours of walking in crowds, in clean clothes, without perfume while you are in ihram. Good packing makes it easier. This list covers what to carry for Umrah from India: for men, for ladies and for kids, plus the documents, medicines, bags and the Zamzam water rules on the flight home. We are a luggage and travel gear site, not a religious authority: for the rites and any rule of ihram, follow your scholar or your Umrah group.

  • Nights are mild, about 18–20 °C, but the mosques, hotels and buses are air-conditioned. Carry a light shawl or sweater.

The short list

If you pack only these, you'll be fine:

  1. Passport with your Umrah visa, flight tickets, hotel bookings and your Umrah permit in the Nusuk app
  2. Your vaccine certificate
  3. For men: two or three sets of ihram, an ihram belt and safety pins
  4. For ladies: loose, modest clothes that cover the body, and two or three scarves
  5. Comfortable flip-flops or slippers, plus a pouch to carry them in the mosque
  6. Unscented soap, shampoo and deodorant for the days in ihram
  7. Your medicines, in their original packs, with the prescription
  8. A small zip bag for your passport and phone
  9. A type G (UK-style) adapter, your phone charger and a power bank, in your cabin bag
  10. A refillable water bottle and a light prayer mat

Documents and money

  • Passport with your Umrah visa, valid for your whole trip. Keep it on you, not in your check-in bag. Carry a photocopy and a photo on your phone.
  • Your Umrah permit. Saudi Arabia's Ministry of Hajj and Umrah says the Umrah permit is issued through the Nusuk app, and permits are checked through the Nusuk and Tawakkalna apps. Install Nusuk before you fly and keep your phone charged.
  • Rawdah visit in Madinah: the Ministry says you need a booking to pray in the Rawdah, for ages 5 and up, with 30 days between visits. Book it in Nusuk.
  • Your vaccine certificate for meningitis (see Medicines and health).
  • Flight tickets, hotel bookings and your group's contact numbers, printed and on your phone.
  • Cash. RBI lets a bank or money changer sell you up to US$3,000 in foreign currency notes per trip, and you can take up to ₹25,000 in Indian notes. Put the rest on a forex card. See how much cash you can carry abroad.
  • Saudi customs: you must declare cash of SAR 60,000 or more, and the same for gold, jewellery or precious stones, new or worn (ZATCA).
  • Ladies: the Ministry's FAQ says a woman may perform Umrah without a mahram. Check your group's and your airline's own rules.

Ihram for men

Saudi Arabia's Ministry of Hajj and Umrah explains ihram on its website (updated 15 Nov 2026). What matters for packing:

  • Two pieces of cloth: an izaar for the lower body and a ridaa' for the upper body. White is the custom. Take two or three sets, so you always have a clean one.
  • No fitted clothes: no shirt, trousers, vest, socks or gloves, and nothing on the head.
  • Slippers that don't cover the whole foot. Shoes that cover the whole foot and heel are not worn in ihram.
  • Allowed: a waist belt or clip for your phone and money, a watch, rings, bandages, and an umbrella that doesn't touch your head.
  • No perfume, and no clothes with perfume on them. This is for men and women.
  • Ask your group where you'll change into ihram. If you fly straight to Jeddah, you pass the miqat before landing, so keep one set in your cabin bag.

For the rules themselves, ask your scholar or your group. This page covers only what to pack.

What to pack for ladies

The Ministry of Hajj and Umrah says women in ihram may wear clothes "of all colors and shapes" if they are loose, cover the body and show no adornment. In ihram, women don't wear a niqab or gloves. Glasses, rings and watches are allowed.

  • Abayas or loose, long dresses in cotton or other breathable cloth: two or three for the stay
  • Two or three scarves (hijab) and under-scarf caps, so one can dry while you wear the other
  • Light cotton clothes to wear under the abaya, and nightwear
  • Socks for cold marble floors and the AC
  • A light shawl for winter evenings and cold mosques
  • Flip-flops or soft slippers and one pair of walking shoes
  • A small crossbody bag that zips shut, for your phone, passport copy and money
  • Unscented toiletries for the days in ihram, and sanitary pads or your usual products
  • A small mirror, safety pins and hair clips

What to pack for men

  • Two or three sets of ihram, so you have a clean set if one gets dirty
  • An ihram belt with zip pockets for your money, phone and hotel card
  • Safety pins to hold the top cloth
  • Kurta-pajama or other loose, clean clothes for the days after Umrah
  • A cap for prayers outside ihram
  • Flip-flops for ihram, and walking shoes or sandals for Madinah and the days after
  • A light shawl for cold mosque floors and AC buses in winter

What to pack for kids

  • Light, loose clothes, and one warm top for winter evenings and the AC
  • Ihram for boys, if your family plans it: ask your group what size to buy
  • Comfortable slippers that stay on, and socks
  • Your child's medicines, and ORS sachets for hot days
  • A card in their pocket with your name, phone number and hotel. The crowds are huge
  • Snacks and a refillable bottle for the journey

Slippers and shoes

  • Flip-flops or slippers for the days in ihram. Choose a pair that is easy to take off at the mosque door, with a sole that grips on wet marble.
  • A small pouch or cloth bag for your slippers. At the big mosques you take them off at the door, and carrying them saves time looking for them later.
  • Walking shoes or soft sandals for Madinah and the days after Umrah. You may walk several kilometres a day.
  • Socks for ladies and kids, and for cold marble floors in winter.

Toiletries: unscented for ihram

The Ministry of Hajj and Umrah says perfume is not allowed in ihram, for men and for women.

  • Pack unscented soap, shampoo and deodorant for the days in ihram.
  • Nail cutter, comb and a small mirror. A nail cutter is fine in your check-in bag; see can I carry a nail cutter?
  • Toothbrush, toothpaste and miswak, if you use one.
  • Lip balm and moisturiser. The air in Makkah and Madinah is dry, and the AC dries your skin too. Check that they are unscented before you use them in ihram.
  • Sunscreen, for the walk between your hotel and the mosque in the day. Choose an unscented one for ihram.
  • Tissue packs, wet wipes (unscented) and a small towel.
  • Liquids in your cabin bag must be 100 ml or less each, in one clear 1-litre bag. Bigger bottles go in check-in. See can I carry liquids?

Medicines and health

Saudi Arabia's Ministry of Health publishes health rules for Umrah pilgrims (the 1447H, 2026 version, read 25 Nov 2026):

  • Meningitis vaccine is required for every pilgrim. It must be ACYW or ACYWX conjugate vaccine within the last 5 years, or ACYW polysaccharide vaccine within the last 3 years, and at least 10 days before you arrive. If your certificate doesn't name the vaccine type, it counts for 3 years only. Ask your doctor or a government vaccination centre in good time.
  • India is not on the polio or yellow fever lists, so Indian pilgrims don't need those certificates under these rules.
  • COVID-19 vaccine proof is required for people over 65, pregnant women and people with certain long-term illnesses.
  • The flu vaccine is advised.

For medicines, Saudi Arabia's Food and Drug Authority (SFDA) rules for pilgrims say:

  • Bring only enough for your stay, up to one month.
  • Carry a prescription or medical report less than 6 months old, with your diagnosis, the dose and how long you take it.
  • Keep medicines in their original packs. Loose tablets and expired medicines are not allowed.
  • Narcotic or psychotropic medicines (some painkillers, sleeping pills and anxiety tablets) need SFDA approval before you travel, through its CDS system.
  • Keep medicines in your cabin bag, in case your check-in bag is late. See can I carry medicines on a flight?

A small first-aid kit is worth packing too: plasters for blisters, pain relief, ORS sachets, antacids, cold and cough medicine, and anything your doctor suggests.

Phone, plugs and power bank

  • Saudi Arabia uses the type G plug, the same three square pins as the UK and Dubai. Indian plugs won't fit, so take an adapter. See our travel adapter for Dubai and the Gulf page.
  • Indian phone and laptop chargers work if the label says 100–240 V. Saudi Arabia runs at 60 Hz, which they don't mind.
  • Power bank in your cabin bag only, never in check-in. Saudia allows up to two, and you can't charge them on board. See can I carry a power bank?
  • Save your hotel's location and your group leader's number on your phone, and carry them on paper too.

Small things that help

  • A small zip bag or sling worn on the front, for your passport, phone, money and hotel card. Pockets are few in ihram, and the crowds are thick.
  • A refillable water bottle. Zamzam water is available in the mosques.
  • A light, foldable prayer mat for prayers outside, in the hotel or on the journey.
  • A small Quran, dua book or your dua list if you prefer paper to your phone.
  • A small umbrella for the sun on the walk to the mosque. The Ministry of Hajj and Umrah allows men in ihram an umbrella that doesn't touch the head.
  • Earplugs and an eye mask for the bus and the flight.
  • A few spare plastic bags or a foldable bag for dirty clothes and for gifts on the way back.

Which bag to take

  • One medium check-in bag per person is enough for 10 to 15 days: about 66 cm, or 24 inches.
  • A small cabin trolley or backpack for your medicines, documents, one change of clothes and an ihram set, in case your check-in bag is late.
  • Leave space for dates, prayer mats, gifts and Zamzam. Zamzam goes as a separate piece on most airlines (see below), but everything else must fit your allowance.
  • Use a proper suitcase or a well-taped box. Air India warns that some Gulf airports refuse or charge extra for round bags and bundles tied with rope or wrapped in a blanket.

Zamzam water on the flight home

Zamzam water is the most asked question about Umrah luggage. You can't put it inside your suitcase, and you can't carry a 5-litre can in the cabin. Most airlines take one sealed 5-litre package per passenger, bought at the airport or from approved outlets, as a separate checked item. Some carry it free, others count it in your allowance:

AirlineZamzam rule (from our airline pages)
SaudiaOne package up to 5 litres, as a separate checked item, never inside your suitcase. Free from Madinah; from other Saudi airports it counts in your allowance.
IndiGoFrom Jeddah and Madinah to India, it must fit within your 30 kg allowance.
Air IndiaNot inside your checked bag. Only Hajj and Umrah visa holders may carry one official 5-litre bottle.
EmiratesUp to 5 litres in a sealed container, on top of your allowance, for Hajj and Umrah pilgrims whose trip includes Madinah or Jeddah.
Qatar AirwaysUp to 5 litres in approved bottles, only for pilgrims flying from Jeddah, Madinah or Taif.
EtihadOne package up to 5 litres free, sealed in a plastic container in a cardboard box sold at the airport. From Jeddah you need an Umrah or Hajj visa.
flydubaiFrom Jeddah, Madinah, Taif or Yanbu, up to 5 litres free as checked baggage, in approved packaging.
  • In your cabin bag, Zamzam follows the normal liquid rule: 100 ml or less per bottle, in your clear liquids bag.
  • Rules change. Read your airline's page, or ask at check-in, before you buy.

  • Indian Customs lets you bring goods worth ₹75,000 per person duty free. Dates, prayer mats and gifts count. See our Indian customs rules.
  • Gold has its own limits: see how much gold you can bring.

  • Dates and dry fruits are fine in your bags. See can I carry dry fruits?
  • Weigh your bags before you leave the hotel. Gifts and dates add up fast.

What not to pack

  • Medicines without their original pack or prescription, or narcotic and psychotropic medicines without SFDA approval.
  • Undeclared cash, gold or jewellery of SAR 60,000 or more.
  • A power bank in your check-in bag. It goes only in your cabin bag.
  • Big bottles in your cabin bag. Each liquid must be 100 ml or less, in one clear 1-litre bag.
  • Perfume and scented products for the days in ihram. Keep them for after Umrah, or leave them at home.
  • Bundles tied with rope, round bags or blankets. Pack in a proper bag or a taped box.
  • Too much. You'll walk a lot, and your hotel room may be small. Take fewer clothes and wash them.

Questions

What should I pack for Umrah?

Your passport and visa, the Nusuk app with your permit, your meningitis vaccine certificate, ihram (men) or loose modest clothes and scarves (ladies), flip-flops, unscented toiletries, your medicines with the prescription, a small zip bag, a type G adapter and a power bank.

What should ladies wear for Umrah?

Loose clothes that cover the body and show no adornment, in any colour, such as an abaya with a hijab. In ihram, women don't wear a niqab or gloves, according to Saudi Arabia's Ministry of Hajj and Umrah.

How many sets of ihram should I take?

Take two or three sets, so you always have a clean one. Keep one set in your cabin bag if you plan to change on the way.

What slippers are allowed in ihram?

The Ministry of Hajj and Umrah says men may wear suitable slippers that don't cover the whole foot. Pick a pair that is easy to take off at the mosque door and grips on wet floors.

Can I carry Zamzam water in my luggage?

Not inside your suitcase. Most airlines allow one sealed 5-litre package per passenger, bought at the airport, as a separate checked item. Some carry it free and some count it in your allowance. Only 100 ml fits the cabin liquid rule. Check your airline's rule before you fly.

Which vaccine is required for Umrah from India?

The meningitis vaccine (ACYW), at least 10 days before you arrive in Saudi Arabia. Indian pilgrims don't need polio or yellow fever certificates under the Ministry of Health's 2026 rules. COVID-19 proof is needed for people over 65, pregnant women and some patients.

How much baggage is allowed for Umrah from India?

It depends on your airline and fare: IndiGo gives 30 kg on regular fares, Air India 25–35 kg, and Saudia shows it when you book, with each bag up to 23 kg. All allow a 7 kg cabin bag. Group packages often fix the allowance, so check your ticket.

What is the weather in Makkah in December and January?

Warm days of about 31–32 °C and mild nights of about 19–20 °C. Madinah is slightly cooler. Pack light cotton clothes and a shawl for the air-conditioning.
