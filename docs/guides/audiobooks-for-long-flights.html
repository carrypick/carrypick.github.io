Audiobooks for long flights and train journeys

Updated 22 Nov 2026 · 5 min read

A 10-hour flight to London, a 16-hour train to Mumbai, a long drive to the hills: the hours go faster with a good story in your ears. Audiobooks weigh nothing, work with your eyes closed, and don't need a reading light on a night flight. Here's how to get them ready before you leave, keep your phone charged, and try Audible free.

Try Audible free

Try Audible free for 30 days

Audible's page on Amazon.in says the trial gives you 1 credit for any audiobook (2 credits if you're an Amazon Prime member new to Audible) and the Plus catalogue to listen to as much as you like. It renews every month unless you cancel, and you can cancel at any time. Audible on Amazon.in is for accounts in India.

Don't want to sign up? Read on: the offline and battery tips work for any audiobook app, and our travel books page has printed books for the trip.

Why audiobooks help on long trips

  • No screen, no seat-back TV needed. Most low-cost flights in India have no seat-back screens. A downloaded audiobook works anywhere.
  • Eyes closed is fine. You can listen with an eye mask on, in a dark cabin or a moving train where reading makes some people feel sick.
  • Nothing to carry. One phone holds many books. A paper guidebook can weigh close to 1 kg.
  • Good for road trips. Play it through the car speakers so everyone hears the same story. Pick something the whole family likes.
  • Kids stay calm. A children's audiobook on headphones helps on long train journeys, without a bright screen at bedtime.

Download before you leave

Planes, tunnels, ghats and mountain roads have weak or no signal. Stream nothing: download everything at home on Wi-Fi.

  1. Install the Audible app (Android or iPhone) and sign in at home, not at the airport.
  2. Open your library and tap Download on each title you want. Download one more than you think you need.
  3. Turn on airplane mode and play a minute of each book. If it plays, it's on your phone.
  4. Check free space on your phone. Long books take up space, so delete old downloads you have finished.
  5. Turn off automatic updates for the trip, so the app doesn't need to update when you're offline.

Battery and headphones

A long flight and a long book can empty a phone. Plan for the whole day, including the taxi at the other end.

  • Carry a power bank in your cabin bag. Power banks are never allowed in check-in. A 10,000 mAh power bank is about 37 Wh, under the 100 Wh limit. Read power bank rules on flights.
  • Lower the screen and close other apps. Audio alone uses little battery when the screen is off.
  • Wired earphones never run out of charge and work with the plane's audio jack too (some planes need a two-pin adapter).
  • Bluetooth earphones are fine on most airlines once the crew allows phones in airplane mode. Charge the case fully before you leave.
  • Noise: a plane cabin is loud. Earbuds that seal well let you keep the volume lower, which is better for your ears.

Questions

Is there an Audible free trial in India?

Yes. Audible's page on Amazon.in offers a 30-day free trial with 1 credit, or 2 credits for Amazon Prime members who are new to Audible. It renews monthly unless you cancel.

Do I get Audible free with Amazon Prime?

No, Prime doesn't include Audible. When we checked, Prime members new to Audible got 2 credits in the free trial instead of 1.

Can I listen to audiobooks on a plane without internet?

Yes, if you download them first. Download at home on Wi-Fi, then test in airplane mode before you leave.

What are good audiobooks for long drives?

Pick something everyone in the car likes: a gripping story, a funny memoir or a children's adventure if kids are with you. Short chapters help when you stop often.

Can I use Bluetooth earphones on a flight?

On most airlines, yes, once the crew says phones in airplane mode are allowed. Some ask you to stop during take-off and landing. Wired earphones always work.

Will an audiobook drain my battery?

Not much, if the screen stays off. Carry a power bank in your cabin bag for long days. Power banks are not allowed in check-in.
