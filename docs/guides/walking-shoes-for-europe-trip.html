Walking shoes for a Europe trip, for men and women

Updated 23 Nov 2026 · 5 min read

On a Europe trip you walk far more than at home: old town lanes, museum halls, station stairs and the walk back to the hotel at night. Most city days run to 10,000 to 20,000 steps. The right shoes make the difference between enjoying day five and sitting it out. Here's how to choose, and the walking shoes we picked by their listed specs.

Quick answer

  • Summer (May to September): light, cushioned walking shoes. Slip-ons save time at airport security.
  • Winter (November to March): waterproof shoes with a grippy sole. Rain, wet leaves and snow make cobbles slippery.
  • Pack two pairs at most: one to walk in, and one spare pair in case the first gets wet.
  • Break them in with a few long walks at home before you fly.

In December, Zurich averages about 3 °C by day, Paris about 8 °C and Rome about 13 °C, says the World Meteorological Organization. See the full table in our Europe packing list.

What makes a good walking shoe for Europe

  • Cushioning. Long days on stone and concrete are hard on your feet. Look for a foam midsole or a cushioned insole.
  • A grippy rubber sole. Cobblestones are uneven, and smooth stone gets slippery when wet.
  • Light weight. Lighter shoes tire you less, and they're easier to pack.
  • Slip-on or easy laces. You take shoes off at some airport security checks, and slip-ons are quicker.
  • Mesh breathes, but lets water in. Great for summer, poor for winter rain.
  • Comfort over looks. Clean, plain sneakers in black, navy or grey go with jeans and smart trousers, so one pair is enough.

How many pairs to pack

  • Wear your heaviest pair on the flight. It doesn't count towards your bag's weight.
  • Carry one spare pair. If one pair gets wet in the rain, you'll want dry shoes the next morning.
  • Flip-flops or slides for hotel bathrooms and the beach in the south.
  • Smart shoes only if you need them for a wedding or a business dinner.

Check your bag size and weight in Europe cabin bag rules before you pack a third pair.

Fit and size

  • Most Indian sizes are UK sizes. Europe uses EU sizes, and US brands use US sizes. Convert yours with our shoe size converter, then check the brand's chart on the Amazon page.
  • Feet swell on long walking days and long flights. Shoes that are tight at home will hurt by day three.
  • Try them with the socks you'll take: thin cotton for summer, thicker wool for winter.

Questions

Which shoes are good for walking all day in Europe?

Light, cushioned walking shoes with a grippy rubber sole, already broken in. In winter, choose waterproof shoes instead of mesh.

Are sneakers fine in Europe?

Yes. Plain sneakers are normal in European cities, in restaurants and in most museums. Choose comfortable ones with a good sole rather than flat fashion sneakers.

Do I need waterproof shoes for Europe in winter?

It helps a lot. Winter days are often wet, and the Alps have snow. Wet mesh shoes stay cold all day. Take one waterproof pair with a grippy sole.

Is a slip-on shoe good for travel?

Slip-ons are quick at airport security and on long flights. Make sure they hold your heel firmly, so you can walk far in them.

What is my shoe size in EU sizes?

In Bata India's size chart, men's UK 7 is EU 41, but brands differ. Use our shoe size converter for UK, US, EU and cm, and check the brand's chart.

How many pairs of shoes should I take to Europe?

Two: one walking pair you wear, and one spare. Add flip-flops for the hotel, and smart shoes only if you have an event.
