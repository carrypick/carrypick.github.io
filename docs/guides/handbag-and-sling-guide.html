Handbag, tote or sling: how to choose for work and travel

Updated 4 Nov 2026 · 2 min read

A good everyday bag holds what you carry, sits comfortably, and keeps your phone and wallet safe in a crowd. Start with what you need to carry, then choose the shape.

Which type of bag?

TypeBest forWatch out for
ToteOffice, laptop, daily useHeavy on one shoulder; some have no zip
SatchelWork, smart occasionsHolds less than a tote
Crossbody slingSightseeing, markets, airportsSmall; rarely fits a tablet

Three things to check

  1. How it closes. In crowded markets and trains, a zip is safer than a magnet or an open top. Wear a sling in front of you.
  2. Its weight when empty. Listed weights on this page go from about 140 g to 800 g, and a heavy bag gets tiring by the end of a long day.
  3. Your laptop's size. If you carry one, check for a padded sleeve and the size it fits.

Your bag on a flight

Most Indian airlines limit the second bag you carry on board, and the rules differ:

AirlinePersonal bag rule
IndiGoOne personal item up to 3 kg, like a ladies' purse or a small laptop bag
Air IndiaOne small bag up to 3 kg and 40 x 30 x 20 cm, under the seat
Akasa AirOne personal item up to 3 kg, under the seat
Air India ExpressA laptop bag up to 30 x 40 x 10 cm, but it shares your 7 kg
SpiceJetA laptop bag or ladies' purse, but it counts inside your 7 kg

These rules were checked on 3 Nov 2026. Fares and rules change, so see our airline baggage rules before you fly.
