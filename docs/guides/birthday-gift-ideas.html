How to choose a birthday gift

  • Think about their normal day. Office, college, home or always travelling? A gift that fits that day gets used, and they remember who gave it.
  • Look at what they already carry. A worn-out wallet, an old watch with a cracked strap or a bag with a broken zip tells you the gift.
  • Pick the colour on the Amazon page with care. One page can show several colours. Check that the one you click is the one they'd wear.
  • Clothes and shoes need the right size. If you have to guess, choose a watch, a wallet, sunglasses or a bag instead.
  • Perfume is easier as a set. Four small bottles let them find a favourite, so you don't have to guess one scent.
  • Check the delivery date before you pay. Birthdays don't move. Order a few days early, or have it delivered to your address and wrap it yourself.

What to write in a birthday card

A short, real line means more than a long poem. Pick one and make it yours:

  • Husband: "Happy birthday to my best friend and my favourite person. Here's to many more years of chai, long drives and you."
  • Wife: "Happy birthday! Thank you for making every ordinary day better. This year, let me spoil you a little."
  • Brother: "Happy birthday, bro. Still annoying, still my favourite. Have a great year."
  • Sister: "Happy birthday to the one who knows all my secrets. Thank you for always being there."
  • Father: "Happy birthday, Papa. Everything good in me, I learned from you."
  • Mother: "Happy birthday, Maa. Thank you for every packed tiffin and every prayer. Love you always."
  • Friend: "Happy birthday! Thank you for the laughs, the late-night calls and the help when I needed it."

Add one memory from the past year, like a trip or a funny moment. That's the line they'll read twice.

Questions

What is the best birthday gift for a husband?

A watch he can wear to work and on weekends, like the Timex Classics, a slim leather wallet, or a perfume gift set with four small bottles. If he travels for work, a leather sling or a weekender bag.

What can I gift my wife on her birthday?

A watch like the Timex with a rose gold band or the Titan Karishma, a tote that fits what she carries every day, or a perfume gift set. A zip-around wallet is a good smaller gift or add-on.

What should I gift my brother or sister?

For a brother: sunglasses, a pocket power bank or a black Fastrack watch. For a sister: a Fastrack watch with a pink dial, cat-eye sunglasses or a light crossbody bag for college.

What is a good birthday gift for father or mother?

For your father: a Casio watch with a date window, a leather wallet or walking shoes. For your mother: an elegant Titan watch, a light satchel or an attar set.

What is a good birthday gift for a best friend?

Round sunglasses, a wallet and pen gift set, or a perfume set with scents for men and women. For a friend who travels a lot, packing cubes or a passport holder.

What should I write in a birthday card?

Keep it short and personal: wish them a happy birthday, say one thing you love about them, and add one memory from the past year. See our sample lines for each person above.
