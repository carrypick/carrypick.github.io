Visa-free countries for Indians 2026: visa on arrival and e-visa list

Checked 21 Nov 2026 on each country's official site · Indian passport, tourist trips

Quick answer

  • Visa-free (8): Thailand, Malaysia, Nepal, Bhutan, Mauritius, Seychelles, Hong Kong and Kazakhstan.
  • Visa on arrival (2): Indonesia and Maldives.
  • e-Visa (8): Vietnam, Sri Lanka, Japan, Georgia, Azerbaijan, Oman, Egypt and Kenya.
  • Visa needed (9): UAE, Singapore, South Korea, Turkey, United Kingdom, Schengen countries, United States, Canada and Australia.
  • Not confirmed (2): Qatar and New Zealand. We could not confirm the rule on an official page, so check with the embassy.

Visa rules change often, sometimes with only a few days' notice. We read each rule on the country's own official website and show the date we checked it. Before you book, confirm with the country's embassy or high commission in India and with your airline: airlines can refuse to board you if your papers are not right.

Visa-free, visa on arrival and e-visa: what it means

  • Visa-free: no visa. You show your passport at immigration, and the officer can still ask for a return ticket, hotel booking or money for your stay.
  • Visa on arrival: you get the visa at the airport when you land, sometimes for a fee. Some countries also let you apply online before you fly.
  • e-Visa: you apply online before you travel and carry the approval, printed or on your phone. Apply only on the official website.
  • Visa needed: you apply before you travel, usually with biometrics at a visa centre in India. Apply weeks or months ahead.

Countries Indians can visit with a US, UK or Schengen visa

Some countries make it easier if you already hold a valid visa or residence permit from the US, the UK or a Schengen country. The exact list of accepted visas differs, so read the official page in the row.

  • UAE: GDRFA Dubai gives a 14-day visa on arrival (extendable once by 14 days) to Indians with a US green card or a UK or EU residence visa, applied for online first. The page does not name a US visit visa, so check before you fly.
  • Singapore: Changing planes? Indians may qualify for 96-hour visa-free transit if they hold a valid visa or long-term pass from one of the countries Singapore lists, plus an onward ticket.
  • Turkey: With a valid US, UK, Schengen or Irish visa or residence permit, Indians can get a single-entry e-visa, valid for one month, for tourism or business.
  • Georgia: With a valid visa or residence permit of the US, UK, an EU or Schengen country or another listed country, Indians can stay up to 90 days in any 180 without a Georgian visa.
  • Oman: With a GCC residence permit, or a valid visa for the US, Canada, Australia, the UK, a Schengen country or Japan, Indians can stay 14 days without a visa (not extendable).

Before you fly

  • Passport: most countries want it valid for some months after you arrive or leave, often 6 months, and with blank pages. Check the row.
  • Return ticket and hotel: carry printouts or screenshots. Immigration often asks for both, even where no visa is needed.
  • Money: some countries ask you to show enough money for your stay. Carry a card and some cash.
  • Arrival forms: several countries now ask for an online arrival card or registration a few days before you land. The row says where we found one.

Questions

Which countries are visa free for Indians?

Of the 29 popular countries in our table, these let Indian passport holders in without a visa for a tourist trip: Thailand, Malaysia, Nepal, Bhutan, Mauritius, Seychelles, Hong Kong and Kazakhstan. Rules change, so confirm with the embassy before you book.

Which countries give visa on arrival to Indian passport holders?

In our table: Indonesia and Maldives. Carry a return ticket, a hotel booking and enough money, as immigration can ask for them.

Which countries have an e-visa for Indians?

In our table: Vietnam, Sri Lanka, Japan, Georgia, Azerbaijan, Oman, Egypt and Kenya. Apply only on the official website linked in each row.

Is Thailand visa free for Indians?

Yes, for tourism: since 15 September 2026 Indian passport holders can stay up to 30 days without a visa (it was 60 days before), says the Royal Thai Embassy in New Delhi. Fill in the online Thailand Digital Arrival Card (TDAC) before you land. For longer stays, apply for an e-visa.

Do Indians need a visa for Dubai?

Most Indians do. The UAE's visa on arrival for Indians is only for those holding a US green card or a UK or EU residence visa (14 days, extendable once), says GDRFA Dubai. Everyone else needs a UAE visit visa before the flight.

Is Malaysia visa free for Indians?

Yes, until 31 December 2026: Malaysia's immigration department exempts Indian nationals from the visa for social visits, for up to 30 days. For trips from 1 January 2027, check Malaysia's official visa site before you book, because the exemption has an end date.

Is Vietnam visa free for Indians?

No. Ordinary Indian passport holders need a Vietnam e-visa, valid for up to 90 days with single or multiple entry, applied for on Vietnam's official e-visa portal. Only Indian diplomatic and official passports are visa-exempt.

Do Indians need a visa for Singapore?

Yes. Indian passport holders need a visa for Singapore, applied for through an authorised visa agent in India. The visa fee is ₹2,100 per application, says Singapore's High Commission in New Delhi, and your passport needs 6 months' validity.

Do Indians need a visa for Sri Lanka?

You need an ETA (electronic travel authorisation), but for Indians it is free: 30 days with double entry, says Sri Lanka's official ETA site. Apply online before you fly.

Which countries can I visit with a US visa?

In our table, UAE, Singapore, Turkey, Georgia and Oman make entry easier for Indians who hold certain valid visas or residence permits, often from the US, UK or Schengen countries. Each official page lists which visas count.

Are any European countries visa free for Indians?

No Schengen country is: Indians need a Schengen visa for France, Germany, Italy, Switzerland and the other Schengen countries. Indians need a Schengen visa for France, Germany, Italy, Switzerland and the other Schengen countries: up to 90 days in any 180, €90 for adults (EU home affairs), with fingerprints at a visa centre.

Can I travel to Nepal and Bhutan without a passport?

Indians need no visa for Nepal. When flying, carry a valid Indian passport or your original Voter ID card: the Embassy of India in Kathmandu says these are the only two documents accepted, so Aadhaar or PAN won't do. Indians need no visa for Bhutan, but they need an entry permit and pay a Sustainable Development Fee of ₹1,200 per night. Carry a passport valid for 6 months or your original Voter ID, says the Embassy of India in Thimphu.
