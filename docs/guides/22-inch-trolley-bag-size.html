22 inch trolley bag: size in cm, and is it allowed as a cabin bag?

Updated 15 Nov 2026 · 6 min read

A 22 inch trolley bag is 55.9 cm tall. It's a small, cabin-size bag, not a medium one. But it's a little over the 55 cm cabin limit of most Indian airlines, so the exact size in cm matters more than the inch number.

Quick answer

  • 22 inches is 55.9 cm. A 55 cm cabin bag is 21.7 inches, and a 56 cm one is 22 inches.
  • It's a small (cabin) size. Medium trolleys start at about 65 cm (26 inches).
  • On paper, it's 0.9 cm too tall for IndiGo, Air India, Akasa Air and SpiceJet, which allow 55 cm. Air India Express allows 56 cm.
  • Listings don't agree on what "22 inch" means. We found "22 inch" bags 55–58 cm tall, and many 55 cm bags sold as "20 inch". Check the size in cm.
  • Weight counts too. Cabin bags are usually limited to 7 kg, and these bags weigh 2.2–2.8 kg empty.

How much does a 22 inch trolley hold?

  • Litres: the bags we read listed 30–55 litres. Bags of the same size can list very different numbers.
  • In the cabin: a 7 kg limit includes the bag. A 2.3 kg bag leaves 4.7 kg for your things, and a 2.8 kg bag only 4.2 kg.
  • As a check-in bag: it can hold roughly 6–12 kg of clothes, by our estimate. That's less than a 15 kg fare allows, so a small check-in bag only makes sense for short trips. See trolley bag capacity in kg and litres.

What is a 22 inch trolley bag in cm?

It's a cabin size, but it's 0.9 cm taller than the 55 cm limit of IndiGo, Air India, Akasa Air and SpiceJet. Air India Express allows 56 cm. For those four airlines, pick a 55 cm bag.

Is a 22 inch trolley small or medium?
