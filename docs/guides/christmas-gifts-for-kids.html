How to choose a Christmas gift for a child

  • Choose something for every day. A watch, a school bag or a bottle gets used on every school day, not just on Christmas morning.
  • Think about their age and size. The Zoop watches below have a 40 mm case, so they suit school-age kids better than toddlers. A 37 litre bag is a lot for a small child.
  • Let them pick the colour. The same Zoop watch comes in blue and pink, and many school bags come in several prints. One Amazon page can show several colours, so check the one you've clicked before you pay.
  • Know what "30 m water resistant" means. It's splash-proof: fine for washing hands and rain, but not for swimming or a bath.
  • Keep the box and the invoice. You'll need them if something breaks and you claim the warranty.
  • Order by mid-December. Deliveries can take longer in Christmas week, so check the delivery date before you pay.

Return gifts for a Christmas party

For a class or a party, choose one small, useful thing and give the same to every child, so nobody feels left out. A sipper bottle like the Milton above suits boys and girls. Ask the school first, because some schools don't allow gifts.

Questions

What is a good Christmas gift for kids?

Something they'll use every day: a first watch, a new school bag or a steel bottle for school. For a family trip, packing cubes or waterproof phone pouches get used straight away.

What is a good Christmas gift for a boy?

A watch like the Zoop with a blue strap, a school bag like the uppercase Sprout for younger boys or the Safari Rove for older boys, or a sling for a teenager.

What is a good Christmas gift for a girl?

The Zoop watch with a pink strap, a school bag in her favourite colour, or a sipper bottle for school. Let her choose the colour if you can.

Is a watch a good gift for a child?

Yes, for school-age kids. A watch with hands helps them practise telling the time. The Zoop watches above are water resistant to 30 m, so they're fine for washing hands but not for swimming.

What are good Christmas return gifts for kids?

Give every child the same small, useful thing, like a sipper bottle. Check the school's rules first.

When should I order Christmas gifts for kids?

By mid-December. Check the delivery date shown on Amazon before you pay, because deliveries can take longer in Christmas week.
