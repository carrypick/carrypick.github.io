24 inch trolley bag: size in cm, weight and how much you can pack

Updated 6 Nov 2026 · 4 min read

A 24 inch trolley bag is 61 cm tall. It's a check-in bag, not a cabin bag. In India, brands usually sell this size as "medium" and list it at 65–69 cm, so a bag called "24 inch" may be taller than you expect.

Quick answer

  • 24 inches is 61 cm. A 65 cm bag is really 25.6 inches.
  • It's too tall for the cabin. Indian airlines limit cabin bags to about 55 cm.
  • It suits a trip of about a week, or a shorter trip with heavy clothes.
  • The bag's own weight counts. On a 15 kg fare, a typical medium bag leaves you about 11.6 kg.

On a train

Indian Railways allows suitcases up to 100 x 60 x 25 cm in most classes, and only 55 x 45 x 22.5 cm in AC 3-tier and AC Chair Car. Most medium trolleys are deeper than these limits on paper. See our train luggage rules.

Questions

What is a 24 inch trolley bag in cm?

24 inches is 61 cm. Many bags sold as "24 inch" in India are listed at 65 cm or more, so check the size in cm.

Can I take a 24 inch trolley in the cabin?

No. It's too tall for the 55–56 cm cabin limits of Indian airlines. Use it as a check-in bag.

How many days is a 24 inch trolley good for?

About a week. It can be less with bulky winter clothes, or if your fare allows only 15 kg.

How much weight can I pack in a 24 inch trolley?

It depends on your fare, not the bag. On a 15 kg fare, a typical 3.4 kg medium bag leaves about 11.6 kg for your things.

Is a 65 cm trolley the same as 24 inch?

Not exactly. 65 cm is 25.6 inches, and 24 inches is 61 cm. Some brands still sell 65 cm bags as "24 inch".
