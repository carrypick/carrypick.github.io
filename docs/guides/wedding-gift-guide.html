Wedding gift ideas: watches and travel bags couples actually use

Updated 2 Nov 2026 · 2 min read

Wedding season in India brings a long list of functions and gifts. Couples often receive many decorative items they never use. A good watch or a travel bag is different: they use it every day or on every trip, and they remember who gave it.

Here are gift ideas that work, grouped by who you're buying for.

How to choose a wedding gift that gets used

  • Pick a known brand. Titan, Sonata, Timex and American Tourister have service centres across India, so warranty claims are easy.
  • Check the warranty. Look for at least 1 year. Some Titan watches come with 2 years.
  • For a couple, pair watches are the safest choice. Matching designs make a lovely gift, and you don't need to guess one person's taste.
  • For the honeymoon, think travel. A weekender bag or a good cabin trolley gets used right away.
  • Order early. Delivery can take longer during wedding and festive season.
  • Keep the invoice. The couple will need it for warranty claims.

Quick gift checklist

  1. Known brand with service centres in India.
  2. At least a 1-year warranty.
  3. Order at least a week before the function.
  4. Look for a gift-wrap or gift-message option at checkout.
  5. Keep the invoice for the couple.
