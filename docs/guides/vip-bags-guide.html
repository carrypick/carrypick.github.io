VIP trolley bags: every range, sizes, warranty and returns

Which VIP line suits you?

  • Want a hard shell? VIP's pages name polycarbonate for Vero, Pod NXT and Barclay, and polypropylene for Maxxplus and Classic. Not sure which is better? See polycarbonate or polypropylene.
  • Carry a laptop on a cabin-only trip? Flex, Aer Plus Pro and Sleek each list a cabin size that takes a laptop.
  • Overpack on the way back? Maxxplus, Flex, Arrive and Grande are expandable. An expanded bag is deeper, though, so it may no longer fit the cabin limit.
  • Need something for road and train trips? The duffles on wheels have a shoulder strap for stairs and platforms. See our train luggage rules.

Reading VIP's sizes

Alumina, Zorro, Dual Tone NXP, Stargaze and Arlington put the height in cm in their names. "55" is a cabin size by height, "65–69" is medium and "79–80" is large. The other lines just say cabin, medium or large. Not sure which you need? See what size luggage you need.

Comparing VIP with other brands? See VIP vs American Tourister, VIP vs Safari and Safari vs Skybags.

What to check before you order

  • The full size. The VIP product pages we read gave no width or depth. Airlines check all three sides, so ask VIP for the full size if your trip depends on it. Most Indian airlines allow only 55 x 35 x 25 cm or 55 x 40 x 20 cm in the cabin. Test any size with our cabin bag checker.
  • Large bags. "79" or "80" is tall. Most Indian airlines limit a checked bag to 158 cm in total, and most large bags we checked from other listings went over that. Measure it when it arrives, inside the 3-day return period.
  • Capacity. Some VIP pages we read showed capacity figures that can't be right, such as "2 Litres" for a cabin trolley. Don't choose by that number.
  • Weight. We didn't find the bag's own weight on these pages. Weigh it when it arrives, because it counts towards your airline allowance.
  • Warranty. We only read the warranty on a few models. Check it on the bag you pick, and keep your invoice.
  • Returns. You have only 3 days from delivery. Unpack and measure the bag quickly, and keep all tags and packaging until you're sure.

Questions

Is there a coupon code for VIP Bags?

Code CK15 took 15% off in VIP's cart when we last checked, on 25 Nov 2026. Coupons can end at any time, so check the discount before you pay. It may not apply to every order. See our VIP coupon page.

Does VIP sell directly on its own website?

Yes. VIP Industries sells VIP bags on vipbags.com, along with its other brands such as Skybags and Aristocrat.

What is the warranty on VIP trolley bags?

The VIP models we read on 6 Nov 2026 showed a 3-year warranty covering the frame, wheels, zips and shell. Check the page for the bag you choose, as it may differ.

Can I return a VIP bag bought from its website?

VIP's policy allows return requests within 3 days of delivery. The bag must be unused, in its original condition, with all tags, packaging and accessories, and it must pass VIP's inspection.

Which VIP trolley bags are polycarbonate?

On VIP's website on 16 Nov 2026, the Vero, Pod NXT and Barclay pages said polycarbonate. Maxxplus and Classic said polypropylene. Some lines' pages didn't name a material.

Which VIP trolley bags are expandable?

Maxxplus, Flex, Arrive and Grande, going by VIP's own pages on 16 Nov 2026. Expanding makes the bag deeper, so a cabin bag may not fit the airline's limit when it's expanded.

What do the numbers in VIP bag names mean?

They usually show the height in cm. A "55" is a cabin size by height, but check its width and depth too before you fly.
