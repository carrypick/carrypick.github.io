Return gift ideas for kids' birthday parties (and for grown-up guests)

Updated 24 Nov 2026 · 7 min read

A good return gift is small, useful and the same for every child, so nobody compares. Art kits, sketch pens, puzzles and story books work best: kids use them at home and at school, and parents are happy to see them come home. Below are return gift ideas by age, for a 1st birthday, for a class birthday at school and for grown-up guests.

How to choose return gifts

  • Give every child the same thing. Kids compare. One gift for everyone, in a colour that suits boys and girls, saves tears at the door.
  • Check the ages on the guest list. A puzzle for 3 year olds bores a 9 year old, and crayons and paint cakes aren't safe for babies who put things in their mouths. If the ages are very mixed, buy two kinds: one for under-5s and one for older kids.
  • Choose something they'll use. Art kits and sketch pens go straight into the school bag. Plastic toys often break by the next day.
  • Buy 3 or 4 extra. Siblings come along, and some guests bring a cousin. Spare art kits never go to waste: keep them for the next party you're invited to.
  • Read the age on the listing. Every pick below says the age range its Amazon.in listing gives. Follow it, especially for small parts.
  • Order a week early. For 20 or 30 of the same item, check that Amazon shows enough stock and a delivery date before your party.
  • Wrap them simply. A paper bag with the child's name, or a sticker that says "Thank you for coming", is enough.

Return gifts for a class birthday at school

Many schools in India let a child share sweets or small gifts with the class on their birthday, but the rules differ. Ask the class teacher first. Some schools allow only stationery, and some don't allow any gifts at all.

  • Choose stationery: sketch pens or a small colouring kit fit in a school bag and are useful in class.
  • Buy one for every child in the class, plus 2 or 3 for the teachers or anyone absent.
  • Skip anything with small parts for nursery and KG classes.
  • Write the birthday child's name on each pack, so the class knows who it's from.

Questions

What are good return gifts for a kids' birthday party?

Art kits, sketch pens, jigsaw puzzles and story books. Give every child the same thing. The DOMS Junior Art Kit and the Apsara colouring kit suit most kids from 4 to 8, and a box of animal puzzles suits 3 to 5 year olds.

What return gift can I give for a 1st birthday?

For babies and toddlers in the family, a baby toy set like the Funskool Giggles stacking rings, teether and rattle (6 months and up). For guests of 3 and up, animal puzzles or a story book. Follow the age on each listing, because small parts aren't safe for babies.

What is a good small return gift for kids?

A pack of sketch pens or a small colouring kit is a nice small token that every child uses. If you want something bigger, give an art kit with crayons, colour pencils and paints.

Should boys and girls get different return gifts?

There's no need. Art kits, sketch pens, puzzles and story books suit both, and giving everyone the same thing avoids comparisons. If you pick a bottle or a bag, choose a colour that works for everyone.

What return gifts can I give in school?

Stationery, like sketch pens or a small colouring kit, after asking the class teacher. Buy one for every child, plus a few spares.

What are good return gift ideas for adults?

Small, useful things anyone can use: a steel flask bottle, a pocket perfume, a passport holder or a travel neck pillow set.

How many return gifts should I buy?

One for every child on the guest list, plus 3 or 4 extra for siblings and surprise guests.
