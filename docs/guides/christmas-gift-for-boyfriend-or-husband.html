How to choose a Christmas gift for him

  • Upgrade something he uses every day. An old wallet, a worn-out bag or a watch with a broken strap are easy clues.
  • Choose a watch size that suits his wrist. 38 to 40 mm suits most men. 41 to 45 mm is large and looks best on a wider wrist.
  • Leather or steel? A leather strap looks classic but doesn't like water. A steel strap is better for rain and sweat.
  • Choose a known brand. Titan, Timex and Casio have service centres across India, so warranty claims are easier. Keep the invoice.
  • Plan around a trip. Going away for Christmas or New Year? A duffle, a toiletry bag or a warm jacket gets used straight away.
  • Order by mid-December. Deliveries can take longer in Christmas week, so check the delivery date before you pay.

Questions

What is a good Christmas gift for a boyfriend?

A watch like the Timex Classics, polarized sunglasses, or a small leather sling. If you're going away together, a toiletry bag or a weekender duffle gets used straight away.

What is a good Christmas gift for a husband?

A classic watch like the Titan Classique, a wallet gift set, or a laptop backpack for work. If he travels a lot, a gadget organiser or a toiletry bag is a useful smaller gift.

What is a good Christmas gift for dad?

A watch that's easy to read, a thermal set for the winter, or comfortable walking shoes. Pair watches are a good gift for mom and dad together.

What size watch should I buy for a man?

38 to 40 mm suits most men, so choose that if you're not sure. 41 to 45 mm is large and looks best on a wider wrist.

When should I order a Christmas gift?

By mid-December. Check the delivery date shown on Amazon before you pay, because deliveries can take longer in Christmas week.
