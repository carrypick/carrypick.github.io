Which shoes to take on a trip: snow, sightseeing and the beach

Updated 4 Nov 2026 · 2 min read

Shoes take up the most room in a bag, and they're the most painful thing to get wrong. For most trips, you need only two pairs: one to walk in, and one light pair for the hotel or the beach.

Which shoes for which trip

TripWhat to takeWhy
Snow, such as ManaliWaterproof shoes or boots with a grippy soleMelting snow soaks ordinary shoes, and roads get icy
Forts and cities, such as JaipurCushioned walking shoesLong days with slopes and steps
Beach, such as GoaSandals or flip-flopsSand and water get into closed shoes
Rainy places, such as KeralaSandals that can get wetThey dry faster than closed shoes
Flights and trainsSlip-on shoesQuick to take off at security and on the berth

Waterproof or water resistant?

These don't mean the same thing:

  • Waterproof shoes are made to keep water out, even when you step in a puddle.
  • Water resistant shoes handle light rain and splashes only.
  • Mesh shoes let air in, and water too. Don't wear them in snow.

Buying and packing tips

  • Most sizes in India are UK sizes. Check the size chart on the Amazon page, because brands differ.
  • For snow, allow for thick socks. Try them on with the socks you'll wear.
  • Never take new shoes on a trip without breaking them in. Wear them around home for a few days first, and return them if they hurt.
  • Wear your heaviest pair on the flight. They don't count towards your bag's weight.
  • Put shoes in a bag or a packing cube so they don't dirty your clothes.
