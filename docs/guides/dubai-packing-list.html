Dubai packing list: what to pack and wear for a trip from India

Updated 16 Nov 2026 · 13 min read

Dubai is warm and dry from November to March, and very hot from May to September. In winter you need light clothes for the day, one warm layer for the evening and the desert, and a few modest outfits for malls, the souks and mosques. This list tells you what to pack and why, for a trip from India.

Dubai weather by month

Long-term averages from the UAE's National Center of Meteorology, published by the World Meteorological Organization:

MonthDayNightRain
NovemberAbout 31 °CAbout 18 °CAbout 2 mm
DecemberAbout 26 °CAbout 15 °CAbout 14 mm
JanuaryAbout 24 °CAbout 14 °CAbout 11 mm
FebruaryAbout 25 °CAbout 15 °CAbout 36 mm
MarchAbout 28 °CAbout 17 °CAbout 22 mm
JulyAbout 41 °CAbout 29 °CAlmost none
  • November to March is warm, not hot. Pack light clothes for the day.
  • Winter nights are cool. December and January nights are about 14–15 °C. Take a light jacket, sweater or shawl for the evening, the desert safari and the dhow cruise.
  • Malls and the metro are cold all year because of the AC.
  • Rain is rare. Even in winter it rains on fewer than 4 days a month, so you don't need rain gear.
  • May to September is very hot: about 37–41 °C in the day. Plan outdoor trips for the morning and evening.

The short list

If you pack only these, you'll be fine:

  1. Passport, printed tickets, hotel bookings and travel insurance
  2. Light cotton clothes for the day, one outfit for each day
  3. A few outfits that cover shoulders and knees, for malls, souks and mosques
  4. One light jacket, sweater or shawl for winter evenings and the desert
  5. Swimwear, for the beach or the hotel pool
  6. Comfortable walking shoes, plus sandals or flip-flops
  7. Sunscreen, sunglasses and a cap or hat
  8. Your medicines, in their original packs, with the prescription
  9. A universal travel adapter
  10. Phone charger and a power bank, in your cabin bag

Documents and money

  • Passport. Keep it on you, not in your check-in bag. Carry a photocopy and a photo of it on your phone.
  • Print or save your bookings: return ticket, hotel bookings and travel insurance. Check the entry rules for Indian passport holders on the official UAE or embassy websites before you fly.
  • Cash. RBI lets a bank or money changer sell you up to US$3,000 in foreign currency notes per trip, and you can take up to ₹25,000 in Indian notes. Put the rest on a forex card. See how much cash you can carry abroad. The UAE has its own cash declaration rule: see UAE customs rules.

What to wear in Dubai

What should I wear? Try the what-to-wear helper for day and night temperatures and an outfit list by month.

Dubai's official tourism site, Visit Dubai, says most clothes are fine in the city, with a few rules:

  • Swimwear only at the beach and the pool. It isn't right for malls or business areas.
  • Cover your shoulders and midriff in public places. Visit Dubai's own packing list includes clothes like these.
  • Heritage areas and cultural sites: cover your shoulders and knees, or wear loose clothes. Trousers, or skirts and shorts that reach past the knee, work well.
  • Mosques: men and women should cover their arms and legs, with loose clothes down to the ankles. Women may need a headscarf, so carry one.
  • Evenings in the desert: Visit Dubai suggests a thicker jacket and warmer clothes.

Clothes

  • Cotton and linen for the day. They're light and cool.
  • Long dresses, maxi skirts, loose trousers and shirts are cool and work almost everywhere: malls, the souks, the Burj Khalifa and dinners.
  • Shorts and T-shirts are fine at the beach, theme parks and your hotel.
  • Swimwear for the beach and the pool only. Put something over it when you leave.
  • One warm layer for winter evenings, the desert and cold malls.
  • One nicer outfit for a dinner cruise, a rooftop or a good restaurant.
  • Buying clothes online for the trip? Check your size first with our clothing size converter, then the brand's own size chart.

Shoes

  • Comfortable walking shoes. Malls, the souks and sightseeing mean a lot of walking.
  • Sandals or flip-flops for the beach, the pool and the hotel.
  • For the desert safari, wear sandals or shoes you don't mind filling with sand. Sand gets into everything.
  • Two or three pairs are enough. See our travel shoes guide.

Sun and health

  • Sunscreen, SPF 30 or higher, even in winter. Read our sunscreen guide.
  • A cap or hat and a refillable water bottle.
  • Lip balm and moisturiser. Hours in AC dry your skin.
  • Your medicines, in their original packs, with the prescription. Some common medicines, such as tramadol, pregabalin and codeine, are controlled in the UAE: you can carry only enough for your stay, up to 3 months, with a prescription or medical report. See the UAE medicine rules.

Phone, plugs and power bank

  • A universal travel adapter. Socket types can differ from India's, and from one hotel to another, so one adapter that takes many plug types is the simple answer.
  • Check your charger's label. Phone and laptop chargers marked 100–240 V work abroad with just an adapter. Check hair dryers and straighteners before you pack them.
  • Power bank: in your cabin bag only, never in check-in. Emirates doesn't let you use or charge a power bank on board since 1 October 2025, and flydubai allows only one, up to 100 Wh. See can I carry a power bank?

Which bag to take

  • About 4–5 nights: a cabin trolley or a 30–40 litre backpack is enough.
  • About a week, or if you'll shop: a medium or large trolley. Leave space, or carry a foldable bag for the way back.
  • Your fare decides more than your bag. Some cheap fares include no check-in bag at all.

What not to pack

  • Poppy seeds (khus khus), paan and betel leaves. They're banned in the UAE. Check your masala box.
  • Controlled medicines without a prescription, or more than 3 months' supply. See the UAE medicine rules.
  • More than 200 cigarettes or 4 litres of alcohol per adult. Extra alcohol is confiscated, and under-18s can bring in neither.
  • A lot of home-cooked food. Food in commercial amounts isn't allowed, and cooked meals are the riskiest. Small sealed packs for yourself are usually fine.
  • Drones, unless you have UAE approval.
  • A power bank in your check-in bag. It goes only in your cabin bag.
  • Big bottles in your cabin bag. Each liquid must be 100 ml or less, in one clear 1-litre bag. Bigger bottles go in check-in.
  • Bundles tied with rope. Emirates and flydubai ask you to pack them in a box.

Questions

What should I pack for Dubai in December?

Light cotton clothes for days of about 26 °C, a light jacket or shawl for nights of about 15 °C, a warmer jacket for the desert, a few outfits that cover shoulders and knees, walking shoes, sandals, sunscreen and sunglasses.

What should women wear in Dubai?

Most clothes are fine. Long dresses, maxi skirts, loose trousers and tops with sleeves work almost everywhere. Wear swimwear only at the beach or pool, cover your shoulders and knees at heritage sites, and carry a headscarf for mosques.

What should men wear in Dubai?

Cotton shirts, T-shirts and long trousers or chinos for the city. Shorts are fine at the beach, theme parks and the hotel. At a mosque, cover your arms and legs.

Is Dubai cold in December?

Not in the day: December days average about 26 °C. Nights average about 15 °C, so take a light jacket or sweater, and a warmer one for the desert.

What should I wear for a desert safari in Dubai?

Loose, light clothes that cover your shoulders and knees, sandals or shoes you don't mind filling with sand, sunglasses and a scarf. In winter, take a warm jacket for the evening.

What should I not carry to Dubai?

Poppy seeds (khus khus), paan and betel leaves, drugs and controlled medicines without a prescription. Don't take more than 200 cigarettes or 4 litres of alcohol, and declare more than AED 60,000 in cash or gold.

How much baggage can I take from India to Dubai?

It depends on the airline and fare. Emirates gives 20–35 kg, IndiGo 30 kg on regular fares, Air India 25–35 kg, and flydubai 20 or 30 kg (none on Lite). All of them allow a 7 kg cabin bag.
