Passport holder: one passport or the whole family's?

Updated 23 Nov 2026 · 5 min read

A passport holder keeps your passport, boarding pass, cards and a little cash together, so you aren't digging through a bag at check-in, immigration and the gate. The first choice is simple: one passport or several. A family holder keeps everyone's passports in one zip case. Here's how to choose, and the holders we picked by their listed specs.

Quick answer

  • Travelling alone or as a couple with one each: a single passport holder.
  • Family trip: a family holder that takes 4 passports, carried by one adult.
  • RFID blocking is a nice extra for cards. It isn't a must.
  • Check it fits. A passport is 125 x 88 mm, so the holder must be bigger than that.

At immigration, families with children often go up to the counter together, so one adult holding all the passports is handy. Each adult still shows their own passport.

What RFID blocking does

RFID blocking stops contactless cards from being read through the holder. It's a nice extra for crowded airports and markets, but your cards already need to be very close to a reader. Both of our picks list it. We explain it in more detail in our wallet and belt guide.

How to choose a passport holder

  • Size. A passport booklet is 125 x 88 mm (the ICAO standard size). A single holder should be at least about 14 x 10 cm.
  • Closure. A zip or a lock stops things from sliding out when you pull the holder from a bag.
  • Card and SIM slots. Handy if you carry a forex card and buy a local SIM when you land.
  • A boarding pass pocket. Keeps the pass with your passport through security and boarding.
  • A pen loop. Some countries still give you a paper arrival card to fill in.

Keep your passport safe on a trip

  • Keep a photo of your passport's photo and address pages on your phone, and a printed copy in a different bag.
  • Carry it on you at the airport, in a sling or a zipped pocket. See our anti-theft backpack guide and handbag and sling guide.
  • Check the expiry date. Many countries want your passport valid for at least 6 months after you arrive. See our visa guide for Indian passport holders for the entry rules by country.

Questions

Is a passport holder necessary?

Not necessary, but useful. It keeps your passport, boarding pass and cards together, and protects the passport cover from bending and getting wet.

Which passport holder is good for a family?

One with a slot for each passport and a zip all round. Our HAMELIN pick takes 4 passports, 8 cards and boarding passes, at 24.5 x 13 x 2.8 cm.

Do I need an RFID passport holder?

It isn't a must. RFID blocking stops contactless cards from being read through the holder, which some people like for travel abroad. Both of our picks list it.

Will a passport holder fit in my pocket?

A single one usually will: our Spigen pick is 14.1 x 10.4 cm. A family holder is bigger and suits a handbag, sling or backpack pocket.

Can I keep my passport in a holder at immigration?

Take it out when you reach the counter. Officers usually want the passport on its own, and they may need to scan or stamp it.
