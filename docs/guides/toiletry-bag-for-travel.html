Toiletry bag for travel: hanging or flat, for men and women

Updated 23 Nov 2026 · 5 min read

A toiletry bag keeps your toothbrush, razor, creams and medicines in one place, and keeps a leaking shampoo away from your clothes. The big choice is hanging or flat: a hanging bag hooks onto a door in a small hotel bathroom, and a flat one packs smaller. Here's how to choose, and three bags we picked by their listed specs, for men and women alike.

Quick answer

  • Small hotel bathrooms and long trips: a hanging toiletry bag with a hook.
  • Short trips and a small bag: a flat, light one.
  • Flying with liquids in your cabin bag? They still go in one clear bag at security. See can I carry liquids on a flight?
  • Men's and women's bags are the same thing. Choose by pockets and size.

How to choose a toiletry bag

  • Size. 2 to 4 litres is enough for one person for a week. Our Mokobara pick lists 3.8 litres.
  • Water-resistant inside. A wipe-clean lining stops a leak from soaking the bag. All three of our picks are listed as water-resistant.
  • Weight. Our picks range from 140 g to 435 g. On a 7 kg cabin fare, a light one leaves more room for your things.
  • Pockets and loops. Elastic loops hold bottles upright; mesh pockets let you see what's inside.
  • A handle. Handy to carry it to a shared bathroom on a train or in a hostel.

Toiletry bag and the 100 ml rule

Your toiletry bag and the airport liquids bag are two different things:

  • At security, liquids, gels and creams in your cabin bag go in one clear, resealable bag of up to 1 litre, each bottle 100 ml or less. Our liquids page has the full rule, with links to Air India, IndiGo and airport security.
  • Flying in or from the USA? The TSA's liquids rule is on its own site.
  • A toiletry bag that isn't clear can go in your check-in bag with bigger bottles, or in your cabin bag with things that aren't liquids.
  • Mokobara's listing calls its detachable pouch "airport-friendly", but it doesn't say it's clear, so check it against the rule above before you rely on it.

Razors and trimmers have their own rules: see can I carry a razor on a flight? and can I carry a trimmer?

What to pack in a toiletry bag

  • Toothbrush, toothpaste and floss
  • Soap or body wash, shampoo, and a small towel if you're staying in hostels
  • Razor or trimmer, deodorant, comb
  • Face wash, moisturiser and sunscreen in 100 ml or less
  • Medicines you take every day, in their strip with the label, and a few plasters
  • Sanitary pads or a menstrual cup, and a lip balm

Questions

Which toiletry bag is good for men?

Any of these. Toiletry bags aren't really made for men or women: choose by size and pockets. Our Lifelong pick is light and flat for a shaving kit; the Travel Blue and Mokobara bags hang up.

What is a hanging toiletry bag?

A toiletry bag with a hook, so you can hang it on a bathroom door, a towel rail or a shower rod and see everything inside. It helps in small hotel bathrooms with no counter space.

Can I take a toiletry bag in my cabin bag?

Yes. Liquids, gels and creams in it must follow the cabin rules: each 100 ml or less, all in one clear 1-litre bag that you show at security. See our liquids page.

What size toiletry bag do I need?

About 2 litres for a weekend and 3 to 4 litres for a week or for two people sharing. Our picks list sizes from 15 x 20 x 18 cm to 26 x 10 x 20 cm.

Is a toiletry bag a good gift?

Yes, for someone who travels for work or stays in hotels. See more ideas in gifts for travellers.
