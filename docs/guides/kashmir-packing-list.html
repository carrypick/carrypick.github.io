Kashmir packing list for winter: what to pack for Srinagar, Gulmarg and Pahalgam

Updated 25 Nov 2026 · 10 min read

Kashmir in winter is properly cold. Chillai Kalan, the 40 coldest days, runs from 21 December to the end of January, and IMD's averages put Gulmarg at about −8 °C at night in January. Srinagar is milder but still below freezing at night. This list covers what to wear for the snow, what to carry for the phone and the cold, what to wear at shrines, and which bag to take, for a 5- to 7-day trip to Srinagar, Gulmarg, Pahalgam and Sonamarg.

Kashmir weather in winter

IMD's long-term averages (1981–2010), as in our Kashmir and Gulmarg guides (read 25 Nov 2026):

PlaceDecember, day / nightJanuary, day / night
SrinagarAbout 10 °C / −1 °CAbout 7 °C / −2 °C
PahalgamColder than SrinagarAbout 4 °C / −7 °C
GulmargAbout 4 °C / −5 °CAbout 0.5 °C / −8 °C
  • Gulmarg and Pahalgam are much colder than Srinagar, and get two to three times its snow. Pack for Gulmarg, even if you sleep in Srinagar.
  • The top of the Gulmarg Gondola is colder still. Phase 2 goes up to about 3,980 m on Apharwat, with strong wind.
  • Records are far lower. IMD's coldest January nights are −14.4 °C in Srinagar, −18.6 °C in Pahalgam and −19.8 °C in Gulmarg.
  • January is the coldest and snowiest month. Heavy snow can shut Srinagar airport and the Jammu–Srinagar highway for a day or two, so pack a spare day's clothes and medicines.
  • Check the forecast on IMD Srinagar in the week before you go.

The short list

If you pack only these, you'll be fine:

  1. Two or three thermal sets (top and bottom)
  2. Two sweaters or fleeces, one of them thick
  3. A really warm jacket with a hood: a down or heavy puffer jacket for Gulmarg
  4. Warm trousers, and waterproof trousers for the snow (or rent a snow coat)
  5. Waterproof gloves, a woollen cap that covers your ears, and a neck warmer
  6. Woollen socks, more pairs than days
  7. Waterproof boots or shoes with a deep grip
  8. Sunglasses, sunscreen and lip balm
  9. A rich moisturiser and cold cream
  10. A postpaid SIM, a power bank and a small torch
  11. Your photo ID, hotel bookings, gondola tickets and some cash

How to layer for Chillai Kalan

  1. Next to your skin: thermals, top and bottom. Take a spare set.
  2. Middle: a thick sweater or fleece, or two thin ones.
  3. Outside: a warm, hooded jacket that covers your hips. For Gulmarg and Pahalgam, a down jacket or a heavy puffer over a fleece.
  4. Extras: a woollen cap, neck warmer, waterproof gloves and woollen socks.
  5. The pheran: Kashmiris wear this long, loose woollen cloak over their clothes in winter. It's warm, and a good thing to buy in Srinagar.

Take a layer off in a heated room or a warm car, and put it back on before you step out. For more, read what to wear on a winter hill trip and our winter jacket for travel guide.

For the snow at Gulmarg, Pahalgam and Sonamarg

  • Waterproof gloves. Knit gloves are fine in Srinagar, but not for snow play or a sledge ride.
  • Waterproof trousers, or rent a snow coat and gumboots. Shops in Tangmarg, Gulmarg and Pahalgam rent them by the day. Jeans soak up snow and stay cold.
  • Woollen socks, and a dry pair in your day bag.
  • Sunglasses. Snow glare at the gondola top is very strong.
  • A cap that covers your ears, and a neck warmer. The wind on Apharwat is the coldest part of most trips.
  • Skiing? Read skiing in Gulmarg. Ski gear is rented in Gulmarg, but your own thermals, socks and gloves matter.

Shoes and boots

  • Waterproof, with a deep, grippy sole. Roads in Srinagar and Gulmarg are icy in the morning.
  • Avoid mesh sports shoes and smooth soles.
  • Room for thick socks. Go half a size up if you'll wear woollen socks.
  • Slippers for the hotel and the houseboat. Floors are cold.

Phone, money and power

  • Prepaid SIMs from other states usually don't work in Jammu and Kashmir. Carry a postpaid SIM, or buy a local prepaid SIM there with your ID.
  • Cold drains phone batteries fast. Keep your phone in an inside pocket, and carry a power bank.
  • Power cuts happen in winter. A small torch helps, and ask your hotel or houseboat about heating and backup power before you book.
  • Carry some cash for taxis, ponies, sledges and small shops.
  • Save your bookings offline, and book Gulmarg Gondola tickets on the official J&K Cable Car Corporation site only.

Skin, health and medicines

  • Lip balm with SPF, a rich moisturiser and cold cream. The cold, dry air and the heaters crack skin fast.
  • Sunscreen for the snow, even on cold days.
  • Your own medicines, with the prescription, plus cold, headache and stomach tablets. Pack a few extra days' worth in case snow stops flights.
  • Breathing problems or heart trouble? Talk to your doctor before Gulmarg's gondola top and the cold.

At mosques and shrines

At Hazratbal, Jamia Masjid and other shrines, dress modestly, with arms and legs covered. Women should carry a scarf or stole to cover their head. Shoes come off at the door, so wear socks you don't mind showing, and shoes that are quick to take off.

What to pack for women

  • Two or three thermal sets, and leggings to wear under trousers or kurtas
  • Two sweaters or fleeces, and a long, warm jacket or down jacket with a hood
  • Warm trousers, and waterproof trousers or a rented snow coat
  • A stole or scarf for shrines, and a woollen cap
  • Waterproof gloves, a neck warmer and woollen socks
  • Waterproof boots, and slippers for the hotel
  • A crossbody bag that zips shut

What to pack for men

  • Two or three thermal sets
  • Three full-sleeve T-shirts or shirts, and two sweaters or fleeces
  • A down or heavy puffer jacket with a hood
  • Two pairs of warm trousers, and waterproof trousers for the snow
  • A cap, neck warmer, waterproof gloves and woollen socks
  • Winter boots or waterproof hiking shoes, and slippers

What to pack for kids

  • Thermals, two sweaters, a warm hooded jacket, and a cap that covers their ears
  • Two pairs of waterproof gloves: one will get wet
  • More socks than days, and waterproof shoes
  • Cold cream, lip balm and their own medicines
  • Snacks and a water bottle for the drives to Gulmarg and Pahalgam

Kashmir packing list for 7 days

  • 3 thermal sets
  • 2 sweaters or fleeces (one thick)
  • 1 down or heavy puffer jacket
  • 2 pairs of warm trousers, plus 1 waterproof pair or a rented snow coat
  • 4 T-shirts or tops
  • 9 pairs of socks, at least 4 of them woollen
  • 1 cap, 1 neck warmer, 1 pair of waterproof gloves (and knit gloves for town)
  • 1 pair of waterproof boots, 1 pair of slippers
  • A stole or scarf, sunglasses, sunscreen, lip balm and moisturiser
  • Postpaid SIM, power bank, torch, charger, ID, cash and your medicines

Which bag to take

  • 5 to 7 days: a medium trolley or a big duffle, plus a day backpack. Winter clothes are bulky.
  • Wear your jacket and boots on the flight or train. They don't count towards your bag's weight.
  • Packing cubes keep thermals, sweaters and socks apart.
  • Flying to Srinagar? Check your fare's checked baggage and cabin bag size on our domestic baggage rules page. Taking the Vande Bharat from Jammu or Katra? Keep the bag you can lift yourself.

Questions

What should I pack for Kashmir in December?

Two or three thermal sets, two sweaters or fleeces, a warm hooded jacket (a down or heavy puffer jacket for Gulmarg), warm trousers, waterproof gloves, a woollen cap, a neck warmer, woollen socks and waterproof boots. Add sunglasses, sunscreen, lip balm, a postpaid SIM and a power bank. IMD's December averages are about 10 °C by day and −1 °C at night in Srinagar, and 4 °C and −5 °C in Gulmarg.

What should I wear in Kashmir in January?

Your warmest layers: thermals, a thick sweater or fleece and a down or heavy puffer jacket, plus a cap, neck warmer, waterproof gloves and boots. January is the heart of Chillai Kalan, about −2 °C at night in Srinagar and −8 °C in Gulmarg on IMD's averages.

Can I rent snow clothes in Kashmir?

Yes. Shops in Tangmarg, Gulmarg and Pahalgam rent snow coats and gumboots by the day. Carry your own thermals, socks, gloves and jacket, because you need them in Srinagar and at night too.

Will my prepaid SIM work in Kashmir?

Usually not. Prepaid SIMs from other states don't usually work in Jammu and Kashmir. Carry a postpaid SIM, or buy a local prepaid SIM there with your ID.

What should women wear in Kashmir in winter?

Warm layers, as for anyone, with a stole or scarf to cover your head at mosques and shrines. Leggings under kurtas or trousers, a long warm jacket and waterproof boots work well. A pheran, bought in Srinagar, is warm and comfortable.

Do I need a down jacket for Kashmir?

For Gulmarg, Pahalgam and Sonamarg in December and January, a down or heavy puffer jacket over a fleece is the best choice. For Srinagar alone, a padded jacket over a thick sweater can be enough. See our winter jacket for travel guide.

How many clothes should I pack for a 7-day Kashmir trip?

Three thermal sets, two sweaters, one warm jacket, two pairs of warm trousers and one waterproof pair, four tops and nine pairs of socks. See the 7-day list above.
