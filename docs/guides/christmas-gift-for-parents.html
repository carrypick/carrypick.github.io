How to choose a Christmas gift for parents

  • Choose something they'll use every day. A watch, a warm thermal or a steel bottle gets used far more than a showpiece.
  • Make it easy to use. For older parents, choose a watch with a large, clear dial and shoes that slip on without laces.
  • One gift for both works well. Pair watches or a luggage set are a gift from you to both of them, so nobody feels left out.
  • For parents-in-law, keep it safe. Choose a known brand and something useful, like a watch, an attar set or a thermal set. Skip clothes in a size you'd have to guess.
  • Choose a known brand. Titan, Sonata, Timex and Casio have service centres across India, so warranty claims are easier. Keep the invoice.
  • Order by mid-December. Deliveries can take longer in Christmas week, so check the delivery date before you pay.

Questions

What is a good Christmas gift for parents?

Pair watches like the Titan Bandhan are a gift for both of them. A thermal set for winter, comfortable walking shoes or a steel bottle are useful gifts they'll use every day.

What should I give my parents who have everything?

Something they use up or wear out: warm winter wear, thermals, walking shoes, or a guidebook for a trip they want to take. Parents rarely replace these for themselves.

What is a good Christmas gift for parents-in-law?

A known-brand gift that's useful and not too personal: pair watches, an attar gift set they can share, or a steel bottle. Skip clothes unless you know their size.

What is a good Christmas gift for mom and dad together?

Pair watches, a luggage set if they travel, or matching thermal sets for winter. One gift for both feels thoughtful and is easier to choose.

When should I order a Christmas gift?

By mid-December. Check the delivery date shown on Amazon before you pay, because deliveries can take longer in Christmas week.
