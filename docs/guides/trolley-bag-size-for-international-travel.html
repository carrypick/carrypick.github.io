Trolley bag size for international travel: which bag fits 23 kg and 158 cm?

Updated 15 Nov 2026 · 7 min read

For a flight abroad, the right check-in trolley is usually a large bag of 75–79 cm that's within 158 cm in total (length + width + height). On flights to the USA, Canada and Europe, each bag can often weigh only 23 kg, so a light bag leaves more for your things.

Quick answer

  • Size: up to 158 cm in total. Most airlines flying from India use this limit for one checked bag. Emirates and flydubai allow 203 cm, and Qatar Airways 300 cm.
  • Weight: 23 kg per bag on piece fares. Air India gives one or two bags of 23 kg each to the USA, Canada and Europe. Gulf and Asia routes usually give a total in kg instead, with no bag over 32 kg.
  • For a 23 kg bag, pick a large trolley within 158 cm. A medium one may run out of room before 23 kg unless you pack heavy things.
  • The bag's weight counts. A 3.5 kg bag leaves 19.5 kg for your things, and a 4.5 kg bag only 18.5 kg.

Two bags? Check their total size

Air India's page says each Economy bag on these routes can be up to 158 cm, and both bags together up to 271 cm (another line on the same page says 272 cm). So two large bags of 158 cm each, 316 cm together, would be over. A large bag of about 152 cm and a smaller bag of about 115 cm, like a cabin-size trolley, fit within it.

Airline staff don't measure every bag, but they can. Weigh each bag at home with a luggage scale, because 23 kg is a hard limit for one piece.

Lock and cabin bag

  • A TSA lock lets security staff in the USA open your bag without breaking the lock. It's useful if you fly to or through the USA.
  • Cabin bags are usually 7 kg abroad too, but sizes vary. Qatar Airways allows only 50 x 37 x 25 cm, smaller than most Indian 55 cm trolleys. See every airline's cabin limit in our size chart and how to pick one in our cabin bag guide.

Questions

What size trolley bag is allowed on international flights?

On most airlines flying from India, one checked bag can be up to 158 cm in total (length + width + height). Emirates and flydubai allow 203 cm, and Qatar Airways 300 cm. Check your airline before you buy a very large bag.

What size suitcase do I need for 23 kg?

Usually a large trolley of 75–79 cm that's listed within 158 cm. A medium 65–69 cm bag can work if you pack heavy things like books or shoes.

What is a 23 kg bag size in cm?

23 kg is a weight limit, not a size. Airlines that use it usually limit the same bag to 158 cm in total, such as 79 x 49 x 30 cm.

Is 23 kg including the bag?

Yes. The bag's own weight counts, so a 4 kg trolley leaves 19 kg for your things.

How many bags can I take to the USA on Air India?

In Economy, one bag of up to 23 kg on a Value fare and two bags of up to 23 kg each on Classic and Flex fares, as listed on 15 Nov 2026. Check your ticket.

Is a 30 kg trolley bag allowed?

On many Gulf and Asia routes, yes, if your fare allows 30 kg: no single bag can be over 32 kg. On routes with 23 kg pieces, like Air India to the USA, a 30 kg bag is over the limit.
