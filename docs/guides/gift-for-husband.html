How to choose a gift for your husband

  • Think about his normal day. Office, site visits, work trips or work from home? A gift that fits that day gets used.
  • Look at what he's been putting up with. A wallet that's falling apart, a trimmer that won't charge or a bag with a broken strap tells you the gift.
  • Check the dial size. Around 39–42 mm suits most men's wrists. Our watch gift for husband guide explains more.
  • Perfume is easier as a set. Four small bottles let him find a favourite. If he already has one, buy a full bottle of that.
  • Make it about the two of you. A weekender bag for a trip together says more than another shirt.
  • Order a few days early. Check the delivery date, or have it delivered to a friend's address if you want it to be a surprise.

Questions

What is the best gift for a husband?

A watch he'll wear to work and on weekends, a slim leather wallet, a Philips all-in-one trimmer or a perfume gift set. If he travels for work, a leather sling or a weekender bag.

What can I gift my husband for no reason?

Something small he uses every day: a slim wallet, a pocket perfume, polarized sunglasses or a new belt. Add a short note.

What is a good gift for a husband who has everything?

Something for both of you, like a weekender bag for a trip or a couple watch set, or something he uses up, like perfume or grooming gear.
