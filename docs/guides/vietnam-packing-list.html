Vietnam packing list: what to pack and wear for a trip from India

Updated 18 Nov 2026 · 17 min read

Vietnam is long, so the weather changes a lot from north to south. In December and January, Hanoi and the north are cool, Da Nang and Hoi An are often rainy, and Ho Chi Minh City is hot and dry. So you may need a jacket, a rain poncho and summer clothes on the same trip. This list tells you what to pack and why, for a trip from India.

Vietnam weather from November to February

Long-term averages (1991–2020) from Viet Nam's Meteorological and Hydrological Administration, published by the World Meteorological Organization:

PlaceDecember dayDecember nightDecember rain
HanoiAbout 22 °CAbout 15 °CAbout 24 mm
Da NangAbout 25 °CAbout 21 °CAbout 272 mm, on about 21 days
Ho Chi Minh CityAbout 32 °CAbout 24 °CAbout 41 mm
  • Hanoi and the north are cool in winter. January is the coldest month in Hanoi, at about 20 °C in the day and 15 °C at night, and Vietnam's tourism authority says cold spells from late December to March bring lows of about 10 °C. Pack a light jacket or sweater and long trousers.
  • Sa Pa is much colder. The tourism authority says Sa Pa is much cooler than the rest of the country from late December to March, with occasional snowfall in January. Take a warm jacket if you go.
  • Da Nang and Hoi An are wet from September to December. October is the wettest month in Da Nang, at about 658 mm, and November and December still bring rain on about 21 days. February to April is much drier.
  • Ho Chi Minh City is hot all year. December to April is the dry season, with only about 1–5 rainy days a month. May to October is the rainy season.
  • Hanoi is hot and wet in summer. From June to August, days are about 33 °C, with about 230–335 mm of rain a month.

The short list

If you pack only these, you'll be fine:

  1. Passport, printed tickets and hotel bookings, and a copy of your passport
  2. Light cotton clothes for the south and the beaches
  3. A light jacket or sweater, and long trousers, for Hanoi and the north in winter
  4. A rain poncho or small umbrella, for Da Nang, Hoi An and the rainy season
  5. One outfit that covers shoulders and knees, for temples, pagodas and the Ho Chi Minh Mausoleum
  6. Comfortable walking shoes, plus sandals or flip-flops
  7. Sunscreen, sunglasses, a cap and mosquito repellent
  8. Your medicines, in their original packs, with the prescription
  9. A universal travel adapter
  10. Phone charger and a power bank, in your cabin bag

Documents and money

  • Passport. Keep it on you, not in your check-in bag. Carry a photocopy and a photo of it on your phone, kept apart from the passport.
  • Print or save your bookings: return ticket, hotel bookings and travel insurance. Check the entry rules for Indian passport holders on Vietnam's official e-visa portal or the Vietnamese embassy's website before you fly.
  • Cash. RBI lets a bank or money changer sell you up to US$3,000 in foreign currency notes per trip, and you can take up to ₹25,000 in Indian notes. Put the rest on a forex card. Vietnam's tourism authority says hotels, restaurants and spas generally accept international cards. See how much cash you can carry abroad.
  • Pre-arrival form. Vietjet says foreign passport holders landing in Ho Chi Minh City, Hanoi, Phu Quoc or Cam Ranh (Nha Trang) must fill in a pre-arrival declaration. Vietnam's Ministry of Public Security called it an optional trial when it started in 2026. It's free on the official site, prearrival.immigration.gov.vn, within 3 days before you arrive, and gives you a QR code to save. Use only that site: the ministry warns of fake ones.
  • Declare large amounts of cash and gold. Vietnam asks you to declare cash over US$5,000 (or the same value in rupees or other money) or over 15 million dong, and gold jewellery of 300 g or more.
  • Keep cash, cards and your passport in a bag that zips shut. Vietnam's tourism authority warns of bag snatching by people on motorbikes in crowded areas, so carry your bag on the side away from the road.
  • Riding a motorbike? The tourism authority says to wear a helmet and carry an International Driving Permit.

What to wear in Vietnam

  • In the north from December to March: long trousers, full-sleeved tops and a light jacket or sweater. Hanoi nights are about 15 °C, and Ha Long Bay can be very cold in January, says the tourism authority, so bring warm layers.
  • In Sa Pa: a warmer jacket, a cap and gloves. The tourism authority says Sa Pa is cool all year, with cold winters, occasional frost around Fansipan and occasional snow in January.
  • In Da Nang and Hoi An from September to December: quick-dry clothes and a rain jacket or poncho. This is the late rainy season, with storms and floods some years.
  • In Ho Chi Minh City and the south: light cotton clothes, shorts and dresses. It's about 32 °C in the day.
  • One outfit that covers shoulders and knees, for pagodas, temples and the Ho Chi Minh Mausoleum. See the dress rules below.
  • One nicer outfit for a rooftop bar, a water puppet show or a good dinner.

What to wear to temples and the Ho Chi Minh Mausoleum

Vietnam's tourism authority says to dress respectfully at pagodas, temples, religious sites and official landmarks such as the Ho Chi Minh Mausoleum:

  • Cover your shoulders and knees. Long trousers or a long skirt, and a top with sleeves.
  • Take off your hat and shoes before you go into a sacred shrine, when you're asked to.
  • At the Ho Chi Minh Mausoleum, the tourism authority says to dress formally and keep quiet inside, and photos are strictly forbidden inside.

A light scarf or stole in your bag covers your shoulders when you need it.

What to wear in Vietnam in December

What should I wear? Try the what-to-wear helper for day and night temperatures and an outfit list by month.

December needs clothes for three kinds of weather:

  • Hanoi, Ha Long and Ninh Binh: cool, misty and mostly dry. Hanoi is about 22 °C in the day and 15 °C at night. Pack jeans or trousers, full-sleeved tops and a light jacket.
  • Sa Pa: colder than Hanoi, with cold winters. Add a warm jacket, a cap and socks.
  • Da Nang and Hoi An: mild, about 25 °C, but with rain on about 21 days. Pack a poncho, quick-dry clothes and sandals that can get wet.
  • Ho Chi Minh City and the south: hot and dry, about 32 °C. Pack summer clothes, and swimwear for the beaches.

Sun, rain and health

  • Mosquito repellent with DEET. Vietnam's tourism authority says dengue is more common across the country than malaria. It advises repellent and light long-sleeved clothes in the evening.
  • A rain poncho or small umbrella, for Da Nang and Hoi An from September to December, and anywhere from May to October.
  • Sunscreen, SPF 30 or higher, sunglasses and a cap for the south and the beaches. Read our sunscreen guide.
  • Your regular medicines, with your prescription. Vietnam's rules let you bring medicines for yourself without a permit up to 30 days' supply on your prescription, or up to US$200 in value. Narcotic medicines are limited to 7 days and psychotropic ones, such as some sleeping pills, to 10 days, as per the prescription. If you have a medical condition, the tourism authority advises carrying a doctor's letter that describes it and its treatment.
  • A small medical kit: the tourism authority suggests repellent, paracetamol, anti-itch cream, sunscreen and antihistamines. Add ORS and something for an upset stomach.
  • Drink bottled or filtered water, as the tourism authority advises, and carry a refillable bottle.

Which bag to take

  • About 5 nights: a cabin trolley or a 30–40 litre backpack is enough. Leave space for a jacket if you go to the north.
  • About a week or more: a medium trolley. Leave space for shopping.
  • Moving between Hanoi, Ha Long, Da Nang and Ho Chi Minh City? Internal flights and overnight trains are easier with one light bag each.

What not to pack

  • Vapes, e-cigarettes and heated tobacco. Vietnam's National Assembly banned them from 2025: the law prohibits their trade, import, storage, transport and use. Using one can be fined 3–5 million dong. Vietjet doesn't accept e-cigarettes or heated tobacco devices in any bag, not even in check-in.
  • More than Vietnam's duty-free limits. Each adult can bring 1.5 litres of spirits (or 2 litres of wine under 20%, or 3 litres of beer), and 200 cigarettes (or 20 cigars or 250 g of tobacco). Under-18s get no alcohol or tobacco allowance. Other goods are duty free up to 10 million dong in total.
  • A power bank in your check-in bag. It goes only in your cabin bag.
  • Big bottles in your cabin bag. Each liquid must be 100 ml or less, in one clear 1-litre bag. Bigger bottles go in check-in. See can I carry liquids?
  • Cash, jewellery, cameras, laptops and phones in your check-in bag. Vietjet asks you to keep them in your cabin bag.
  • Too many summer clothes in winter. In the north from December to March, you'll want trousers and a jacket more than shorts.

Questions

What should I pack for Vietnam from India?

Light cotton clothes for the south, a light jacket and long trousers for Hanoi and the north in winter, a rain poncho for Da Nang and Hoi An, one outfit that covers shoulders and knees for temples, walking shoes and sandals, mosquito repellent with DEET, sunscreen, your medicines with the prescription, a universal adapter and a power bank in your cabin bag.

What should I wear in Vietnam in December?

It depends on where you go. Hanoi is cool, about 22 °C in the day and 15 °C at night, so wear trousers, full-sleeved tops and a light jacket. Sa Pa is colder. Da Nang and Hoi An are mild but rainy, so take a poncho. Ho Chi Minh City is hot, about 32 °C, so pack summer clothes.

Is Vietnam cold in December?

The north is. Hanoi averages about 15 °C at night in December and January, and Vietnam's tourism authority calls December to March cool to cold in Hanoi and Ha Long Bay, with cold winters in Sa Pa. The south stays hot, at about 32 °C in Ho Chi Minh City.

What should women wear in Vietnam?

Dresses, skirts, shorts and tops are fine in the cities and at the beaches. Pack jeans and a jacket for the north in winter. For pagodas, temples and the Ho Chi Minh Mausoleum, cover your shoulders and knees, and take off your hat and shoes in shrines when asked.

What should men wear in Vietnam?

Shorts, T-shirts and cotton shirts for the south, and jeans or chinos with full-sleeved tops and a light jacket for the north in winter. Wear long trousers and a shirt with sleeves to temples and the Ho Chi Minh Mausoleum.

Does it rain in Da Nang and Hoi An in November and December?

Yes, often. Da Nang averages about 486 mm of rain on about 21 days in November, and about 272 mm on about 21 days in December. Vietnam's tourism authority says central Vietnam gets storms and floods from September to November. Pack a poncho and quick-dry clothes.

Can I take a vape to Vietnam?

No. Vietnam banned e-cigarettes and heated tobacco from 2025, including their import, transport and use. Vietjet doesn't accept them in any bag.

Do I need to fill in a form before landing in Vietnam?

Vietjet says foreign passport holders landing in Ho Chi Minh City, Hanoi, Phu Quoc or Cam Ranh must fill in a pre-arrival declaration, though Vietnam's Ministry of Public Security called it optional when the trial began. It's free on prearrival.immigration.gov.vn, within 3 days before you arrive. You don't need a customs form if your goods are within the duty-free limits and your cash is under US$5,000.

How much baggage can I take from India to Vietnam?

IndiGo gives 30 kg to Vietnam on regular fares, and Air India 25–35 kg by fare. Vietjet's Eco fares have no free check-in bag, Deluxe gives 20 kg and SkyBoss 30 kg. All allow 7 kg in the cabin; on Vietjet that 7 kg includes your small item.
