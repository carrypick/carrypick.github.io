Before the sale: choose your size

Sale days are busy, and stocks can change quickly. Decide this first:

  • Which bag? Cabin, medium or large? See what size luggage you need.
  • Does it fit your airline? Test the size in our cabin bag checker.
  • How big is it really? Inch sizes can be confusing. See our guides to 20 inch, 24 inch and 28 inch trolleys.
  • Which watch size? Measure your wrist with our watch size tool.

What to check on any listing

  1. The exact size and colour you've selected. One Amazon page can show many sizes, and each has its own details. We've seen one set of listings where a colour showed a size that couldn't be right.
  2. All three sides, not just the height. Add them up. A checked bag over 158 cm breaks the limit of most Indian airlines.
  3. The bag's own weight. A lighter bag leaves more of your allowance for your things.
  4. The warranty. Look for how long it is and whether it's international. Some listings don't say.
  5. Who sells it, and the return period. They can differ for the same product.
  6. Bank offers. They depend on your card and can change, so check them at checkout.
