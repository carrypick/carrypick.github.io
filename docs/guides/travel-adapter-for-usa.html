Which travel adapter do I need for the USA from India?

Updated 23 Nov 2026 · 4 min read

The USA uses flat-pin type A and B plugs at 120 V and 60 Hz. That's two problems for Indian travellers: the plug doesn't fit, and the voltage is about half of India's 230 V. A plug adapter fixes the first. For the second, check your charger's label.

Quick answer

  • Plug types: A (two flat pins) and B (two flat pins and a round earth pin). Canada and Mexico use the same.
  • Voltage: 120 V and 60 Hz.
  • Phone, laptop, camera and trimmer chargers almost always say "Input: 100-240V". They work with just a plug adapter.
  • Hair dryers, straighteners, kettles and irons from India are often 230 V only. They won't work properly. Buy one in the USA or take a dual-voltage one.
  • A travel adapter does not change voltage. Both of our picks say so on their listings.

How to read your charger's label

Look for the small print that starts with "Input":

Dual-voltage hair dryers and straighteners have a small switch (115 V / 230 V). Set it to 115 V before you plug it in.

Going to the USA to study?

Take one adapter per charger you'll use every day, or one universal adapter with USB ports for your phone. Many students buy a US extension board after arriving and plug their Indian chargers into it with one adapter. Leave the Indian hair straightener and kettle at home. Questions

Which plug is used in the USA?

Type A (two flat pins) and type B (two flat pins and a round earth pin), at 120 V and 60 Hz.

Do I need a converter for the USA from India?

Not for phone, laptop or camera chargers marked 100-240V. You do for a 230 V-only appliance, but it's usually simpler and safer to buy that appliance in the USA.

Will my Indian hair dryer work in the USA?

Only if it's dual voltage. A 230 V-only hair dryer runs weak or not at all on 120 V, and a cheap converter can overheat with it.

Is the USA plug the same as Canada?

Yes. Canada also uses types A and B at 120 V. Mexico uses the same plug types.

Does the USA plug work in Japan?

Japan uses the same flat pins, but most sockets only take type A (two pins). A three-pin type B plug may not fit there. See travel adapter for Japan.

Which plug do I need for India from the USA?

India uses types C, D and M at 230 V. See plugs in India for visitors.
