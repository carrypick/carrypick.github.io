Plugs and voltage in India for visitors from the USA, UK and Europe

Updated 23 Nov 2026 · 4 min read

India uses round-pin sockets, types C, D and M, at 230 V and 50 Hz. Plugs from the USA, Canada, the UK and Australia don't fit. Most phone and laptop chargers work, because they accept 100-240V. A 120 V-only appliance from the USA will be damaged.

Quick answer

  • Plug types: C (two thin round pins), D (three round pins, the usual socket) and M (three big round pins, for heavy appliances).
  • Voltage: 230 V and 50 Hz.
  • From the USA or Canada: bring an adapter. Check every charger says 100-240V. Leave 120 V-only hair dryers and straighteners at home.
  • From the UK: bring an adapter. The voltage is the same as the UK's.
  • From Europe: a thin type C plug often fits Indian sockets, but loosely. Thick type E and F plugs don't. An adapter is safer.
  • From Australia: bring an adapter. Voltage is the same.

Which socket will I see? Most Indian hotel rooms have type D sockets, often a combined socket that takes thin two-pin (C) and three-pin (D) plugs. The bigger type M socket is for air conditioners, geysers and other heavy appliances. Newer hotels may have "universal" sockets that take UK and US plugs, but don't count on them.

Phones, laptops, cameras and most electric toothbrush and shaver chargers say 100-240V. Hair dryers and straighteners from the USA often don't. If you need one, ask your hotel whether it has a hair dryer.

Power cuts and phone charging

  • Short power cuts can happen, more often in smaller towns. Charge your phone when you can.
  • Carry a power bank for train journeys and long day trips. Power banks go in your cabin bag, never in checked bags. See can I carry a power bank on a flight.
  • Many Indian sockets have a switch next to them. Turn it on, or nothing charges.

Questions

What plug type is used in India?

Types C, D and M, all round pins, at 230 V and 50 Hz. Type D (three round pins) is the most common wall socket.

Do I need an adapter for India from the USA?

Yes. US flat-pin plugs don't fit. Also check your chargers say 100-240V, because India runs at 230 V, not 120 V.

Do I need a converter for India from the USA?

Not for chargers marked 100-240V. For a 120 V-only appliance, it's safer to buy one in India than to use a converter.

Do I need an adapter for India from the UK?

Yes. UK type G plugs don't fit Indian sockets. The voltage is the same, so no converter.

Do European plugs work in India?

A thin two-pin type C plug often fits, but it can be loose. Thick type E and F plugs don't fit. An adapter is safer.

Is the Indian plug the same as the UK?

No. The UK uses type G (rectangular pins). India's type D is the old British round-pin plug (BS 546), which the UK no longer uses for normal sockets.
