Business trip packing list: what to pack for a work trip

Updated 24 Nov 2026 · 8 min read

A work trip goes best when you carry one cabin bag, one laptop bag and nothing you'll have to wait for at the baggage belt. This list covers what to pack for a business trip of one to five days, in India or abroad: documents, the right bags, chargers, grooming and clothes that look sharp after a flight.

Want to tick things off as you pack? Use our business trip checklist, which changes the quantities with the number of days. Need a list for your exact trip, month and travel mode? Try the packing list maker.

The short list

If you pack only these, you'll be fine:

  1. Photo ID, tickets, hotel booking, office ID and visiting cards
  2. Laptop, laptop charger, phone charger and a power bank
  3. A laptop backpack or office tote that fits under the seat
  4. A cabin trolley, so you skip check-in
  5. Formal shirts or tops, one per day, plus one spare
  6. One blazer and two trousers or skirts that match everything
  7. Formal shoes and one pair of comfortable shoes
  8. A small grooming kit and a toiletry bag
  9. A travel adapter if you're going abroad
  10. Your regular medicines

Documents

  • Photo ID (Aadhaar, driving licence or passport) and your boarding pass or train ticket on your phone.
  • Hotel booking and the office address, saved offline.
  • Office ID card and visiting cards.
  • Company travel and expense rules, and a folder or zip pouch for receipts. Snap each bill as you go.
  • Going abroad? Passport, visa, return ticket, travel insurance and a forex card. See our visa guide for Indians if you still need one.

Bags: what to carry on a work trip

Most business trips need only two bags: a cabin trolley for clothes and a laptop backpack or tote as your personal item.

  • Cabin bag rules. IndiGo and Akasa Air allow one cabin bag up to 7 kg and 55 x 35 x 25 cm. Air India allows 7 kg and 55 x 40 x 20 cm. Each also allows one small personal bag, up to 3 kg. Check your bag with the cabin bag checker, or compare all airlines in our carry-on size guide.
  • Keep formals neat. Fold shirts with tissue or a plastic dry-cleaning bag between them, pack them flat on top and roll T-shirts and nightwear. A packing cube for shirts stops them sliding about.
  • A trolley with a laptop pocket lets you take the laptop out at security without opening the whole bag.
  • Over 7 kg? Weigh your bag at home, and if you need to check it in, see what the extra kilos cost with our excess baggage calculator.

Laptop backpack or office bag

  • Choose by laptop size. Most office laptops are 14 or 15.6 inch. Check the sleeve size in the listing.
  • Slim and smart beats big. An office backpack of about 20–30 litres looks good with formals and fits under the seat.
  • A trolley sleeve lets the bag ride on your cabin trolley's handle.
  • For women, a laptop tote with a zip works as both office bag and personal item.

Chargers, power bank and adapter

  • Laptop charger and phone charger. A single charger with USB-C ports can charge both if your laptop supports it.
  • A power bank in your cabin bag. Power banks are not allowed in checked bags, and many airlines don't let you use them on board. Read our power bank rules on flights, or check yours with the power bank flight checker.
  • A travel adapter for trips abroad. Plugs differ by country. See which travel adapter you need.
  • A small extension board. Hotel rooms often have few sockets near the desk.
  • A cable pouch keeps chargers, earphones, a pen drive and a clicker together.

Grooming and toiletries

  • Travel-size toiletries. On flights, each liquid in your cabin bag must be 100 ml or less, all in one clear 1-litre bag.
  • A trimmer or razor. A USB-charging trimmer saves packing a separate charger.
  • Deodorant, a comb, face wash and moisturiser.
  • A lint roller or a small clothes brush for your blazer.
  • For women: a small make-up kit, hair ties and pins, and a compact mirror.

Clothes and shoes

  • One formal shirt or top per meeting day, plus one spare for spills.
  • One blazer or jacket in navy, grey or black that goes with everything. Wear it on the flight so it doesn't crease in the bag.
  • Two trousers or skirts in neutral colours.
  • One smart-casual outfit for a team dinner.
  • Nightwear, innerwear and socks for each day.
  • A light sweater. Offices, flights and trains are cold.
  • Formal shoes and one comfortable pair. Wear the bulkier pair on the journey.
  • A belt and a watch to finish the look.

Overnight business trip

Going for one night? Leave the trolley at home:

  • A 30–40 litre laptop backpack takes one change of clothes, toiletries, chargers and the laptop
  • Wear your formal shoes and blazer; pack nightwear and one shirt
  • Travel-size toiletries in a clear pouch

See our 40 litre travel backpack guide for bags that fit as cabin baggage.

International business trip

Add these to the list:

  • Passport, visa, return ticket and travel insurance
  • A forex card and some local cash
  • A travel adapter for that country's plugs
  • A local SIM or eSIM plan, set up before you land
  • A printed copy of the hotel booking and the invitation letter, if your visa needed one
  • Check the airline's cabin and checked baggage rules in our international baggage rules

Questions

What should I pack for a 3-day business trip?

Three formal shirts or tops plus a spare, one blazer, two trousers or skirts, one smart-casual outfit, formal shoes and a comfortable pair, nightwear, innerwear, a toiletry kit, your laptop, chargers, a power bank, photo ID, office ID and visiting cards. It all fits in one cabin trolley and a laptop bag.

Can I take only a cabin bag on a business trip?

Yes, for most trips of up to 5 days. IndiGo, Akasa Air and Air India allow one cabin bag up to 7 kg plus one small personal bag up to 3 kg, such as a laptop bag. Check your bag's size with the cabin bag checker.

Which bag is best for a business trip?

A cabin trolley with a laptop compartment for clothes, and a slim laptop backpack or office tote as your personal item. Pick a backpack with a trolley sleeve so it rides on the trolley handle.

How do I pack formal clothes so they don't crease?

Fold shirts flat with tissue or a dry-cleaning bag between them, and put them on top of everything else. Wear your blazer on the journey, and hang your clothes in the hotel bathroom while you shower: the steam takes out light creases.

Can I carry a power bank on a business trip flight?

Yes, in your cabin bag only, never in the checked bag. Most airlines allow power banks up to 100 Wh, and many don't let you use them on board. Our power bank flight checker tells you if yours is allowed.

What should women pack for a business trip?

Formal tops or kurtas, two trousers or skirts, a blazer, one dinner outfit, formal flats or low heels and a comfortable pair, a laptop tote, a small make-up kit and a stole for cold offices and flights.
