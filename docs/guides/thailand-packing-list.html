Thailand packing list: what to pack and wear for a trip from India

Updated 16 Nov 2026 · 14 min read

Thailand is hot all year. From December to February it's also mostly dry in Bangkok, Pattaya and Chiang Mai, and the north gets cool at night. So pack light clothes, good sun care, one outfit for temples and one light jacket. This list tells you what to pack and why, for a trip from India.

Thailand weather from November to February

Long-term averages from the Thai Meteorological Department, published by the World Meteorological Organization:

PlaceDecember dayDecember nightDecember rain
BangkokAbout 31 °CAbout 21 °CAbout 10 mm
PattayaAbout 30 °CAbout 22 °CAbout 6 mm
PhuketAbout 32 °CAbout 24 °CAbout 62 mm, on about 8 days
Chiang MaiAbout 28 °CAbout 15 °CAbout 18 mm
  • It's hot everywhere. Days are 28–33 °C from November to February. Pack for the sun.
  • Phuket still gets showers in November and December. November averages about 174 mm of rain on about 16 days, December about 62 mm. January and February are drier.
  • Chiang Mai is cool at night. January nights average about 14 °C, so take a light jacket or sweater for the north.
  • May to October is the rainy season. Bangkok's wettest month is September, at about 344 mm, and Phuket's is September too, at about 387 mm. Pack a rain poncho or umbrella if you travel then.

The short list

If you pack only these, you'll be fine:

  1. Passport, printed tickets and hotel bookings, and a copy of your passport
  2. Light cotton or linen clothes, one outfit for each day
  3. One outfit that covers shoulders and knees, for temples and the Grand Palace
  4. Swimwear, and a shirt or kaftan to wear over it
  5. One light jacket or sweater for flights, AC malls and the north
  6. Flip-flops, plus sandals with straps or walking shoes
  7. Sunscreen, sunglasses, a cap or hat and mosquito repellent
  8. A rain poncho or small umbrella
  9. A waterproof pouch for your phone on boat trips
  10. Phone charger and a power bank, in your cabin bag

Documents and money

  • Passport. Keep it on you, not in your check-in bag. Carry a photocopy and a photo of it on your phone, kept apart from the passport.
  • Print or save your bookings: return ticket, hotel bookings and travel insurance. Check the entry rules for Indian passport holders on the Royal Thai Embassy's website before you fly.
  • Cash. RBI lets a bank or money changer sell you up to US$3,000 in foreign currency notes per trip, and you can take up to ₹25,000 in Indian notes. Put the rest on a forex card. See how much cash you can carry abroad.
  • Keep cash, cards and your passport in one place you always carry, like a passport holder or a sling that zips shut.

Clothes

What should I wear? Try the what-to-wear helper for day and night temperatures and an outfit list by month.

  • Cotton and linen. They dry fast and stay cool in the heat and humidity.
  • Shorts, T-shirts, dresses and skirts are normal in the cities, at the beaches and on the islands.
  • One temple outfit. Long trousers or a long skirt and a top with sleeves. See the temple rules below.
  • Swimwear for the beach and the pool. Put a shirt or kaftan over it when you walk to shops and restaurants.
  • One light layer. Malls, buses, trains and flights are cold, and Chiang Mai nights are about 14–15 °C in December and January.
  • One nicer outfit for a rooftop bar, a show or a good dinner.
  • Buying clothes online for the trip? Check your size first with our clothing size converter, then the brand's own size chart.

What to wear to temples and the Grand Palace

The Grand Palace in Bangkok says visitors must dress properly. Its list of clothes that aren't allowed includes:

  • sleeveless shirts, vests, short tops and see-through tops
  • shorts, hot pants and mini skirts
  • torn, tight or cycling trousers, and sleepwear

For other temples, the Tourism Authority of Thailand says:

  • Men: a shirt with sleeves, and long trousers or shorts that cover the knees.
  • Women: a top with sleeves, and a skirt longer than the knee.
  • Shoes off: you enter a temple building barefoot. If you wear sandals, choose ones with a strap at the back.

A light scarf or stole in your bag covers your shoulders when you need it.

Shoes

  • Flip-flops or slides for the beach and the hotel.
  • Sandals with straps or walking shoes for markets, temples and long sightseeing days. You'll walk a lot in Bangkok.
  • Shoes you can slip off quickly. You take your shoes off before you enter a temple building.
  • Two pairs are enough for most trips. See our travel shoes guide.

Sun, rain and health

  • Sunscreen, SPF 30 or higher. Put it on again every 2 hours and after swimming. Read our sunscreen guide.
  • Sunglasses and a cap or hat.
  • Mosquito repellent, especially in the evening.
  • A rain poncho for Phuket and the islands in November, or anywhere from May to October.
  • Your regular medicines in their original packs, with your prescription, plus something for an upset stomach, ORS and plasters. Thailand allows up to 30 days' supply for personal use.
  • Controlled medicines need papers. Psychotropic medicines need a medical certificate and are allowed for 30 days. Narcotic medicines need approval from Thailand's FDA before you travel. Anything with cannabis or hemp is banned.
  • A refillable water bottle. Drink more water than usual in the heat.

Guidebook, phrasebook and something to listen to

  • Guidebook: a printed guide is handy where your phone has no signal and it never runs out of battery. Keep it in your day bag, not in check-in. See our travel books.
  • Phrasebook: in Thailand, a few words of the local language (hello, thank you, how much) go a long way. A phrasebook app works offline if you download the language first.

Which bag to take

  • About 5 nights: a cabin trolley or a 30–40 litre backpack is enough. Summer clothes are light.
  • About a week or more: a medium trolley. Leave space for shopping.
  • Island hopping by ferry? A backpack or a light trolley is easier on boats and piers.

What not to pack

  • Vapes and e-cigarettes. They're illegal in Thailand. The Tourism Authority of Thailand warns of jail or a fine of several times the item's value, and says not to bring the liquid either.
  • More than 200 cigarettes or 1 litre of alcohol. That's Thai Customs' duty-free limit (or 250 g of tobacco). Extra must go in the Customs box at the airport, or you can be prosecuted.
  • Medicines with cannabis or hemp, and narcotic medicines without Thai FDA approval.
  • A power bank in your check-in bag. It goes only in your cabin bag.
  • Big bottles in your cabin bag. Each liquid must be 100 ml or less, in one clear 1-litre bag. Bigger bottles go in check-in.
  • Expensive jewellery. You won't need it on the beach.

Thai Customs also lets your personal things through the green channel if they're worth no more than 20,000 baht in total. The Bank of Thailand asks you to declare foreign currency notes over US$15,000 when you arrive. Not sure about an item on the flight? Search our can I carry it on a flight? list.

Questions

What should I pack for Thailand from India?

Light cotton clothes, one outfit that covers shoulders and knees for temples, swimwear, a light jacket for flights and the north, flip-flops and walking shoes or strap sandals, sunscreen, a rain poncho, your medicines with the prescription, a passport holder and a power bank in your cabin bag.

What should women wear in Thailand?

Dresses, shorts, skirts and tops are normal in the cities and at the beaches. For temples, wear a top with sleeves and a skirt longer than the knee. The Grand Palace doesn't allow sleeveless or see-through tops, short tops, shorts or mini skirts.

What should men wear in Thailand?

Shorts, T-shirts and cotton shirts for most days. For temples, wear a shirt with sleeves and long trousers or shorts that cover the knees. The Grand Palace doesn't allow shorts or sleeveless shirts.

Is Thailand cold in December?

No. Bangkok averages about 31 °C in the day and 21 °C at night in December. Chiang Mai nights are cooler, about 15 °C in December and 14 °C in January, so take a light jacket for the north.

Does it rain in Phuket in November and December?

Yes, sometimes. Phuket averages about 174 mm of rain on about 16 days in November, and about 62 mm on about 8 days in December. Pack a poncho or small umbrella.

Can I take a vape to Thailand?

No. E-cigarettes are illegal in Thailand, and the Tourism Authority of Thailand warns of jail or a heavy fine. Don't bring the liquid either.

How much baggage can I take from India to Bangkok?

On IndiGo, 20 kg on regular fares (25 kg on UpFront). Thai Airways gives one 23 kg bag on Saver and Standard fares, and Air India Express 20 kg on Value fares to Thailand. All allow a 7 kg cabin bag.
