Andaman packing list: what to pack and wear for Andaman

Updated 18 Nov 2026 · 12 min read

Andaman is warm all year, at about 30 °C in the day, and humid. December can still bring showers, and you'll spend hours on ferries and boats. So pack light clothes, good sun care, something for the rain and a waterproof pouch. This list tells you what to pack and why.

Andaman weather from December to February

Monthly averages for Sri Vijaya Puram (Port Blair), from IMD's Port Blair weather station:

MonthDayNightUsual rain
DecemberAbout 30–31 °CAbout 24–26 °CAbout 113 mm
JanuaryAbout 30–31 °CAbout 24–25 °CAbout 46 mm
FebruaryAbout 30–31 °CAbout 24 °CAbout 15 mm
  • It never gets cold. Nights stay at about 24 °C, so you don't need woollens. One light layer for the flight and AC rooms is enough.
  • December can be wet. Usual rain is about 113 mm, but it changes a lot from year to year. Pack a poncho or a small umbrella, and clothes that dry fast.
  • January is drier, and February is the driest. You can pack less rain gear then.
  • The sun is strong on boats and beaches, even on cloudy days. Pack sunscreen, sunglasses and a hat.

The short list

If you pack only these, you'll be fine:

  1. A government photo ID, and the same ID you used to book your ferry tickets
  2. Light cotton or linen clothes, one outfit for each day
  3. Swimwear, and a shirt, kaftan or sarong to wear over it
  4. One long-sleeved top, for the sun and mosquitoes
  5. Floaters or sandals that can get wet, plus flip-flops
  6. Sunscreen, sunglasses, a cap or hat and lip balm with SPF
  7. A rain poncho or small umbrella, for December showers
  8. A waterproof pouch for your phone, cash and ID
  9. A towel, a steel water bottle and mosquito repellent
  10. Your medicines, and pills for seasickness if boats upset you

Documents, permits and money

  • Carry your photo ID everywhere. You need it for ferry tickets and hotel check-in. On government ferries, you must show the same ID in original that you entered when you booked.
  • Save your tickets. Keep your flight, ferry and hotel bookings on your phone and on paper, in case the network is weak.
  • Report early for ferries. Government ferries ask you to report 2 hours before sailing, and the gates close 30 minutes before.
  • Permits for some places. Indians don't need a permit for the main islands, but Jolly Buoy and the Baratang mud volcano need Forest Department permits. Book Jolly Buoy early: only 280 visitors are allowed each day.
  • Keep some cash. Carry UPI and a debit card too, and keep cash and cards in a bag that zips shut.

What to wear in Andaman

  • Cotton and linen. They stay cool in the humid heat and dry fast after a shower or a boat ride.
  • Shorts, T-shirts, dresses and kurtas are fine on the beaches and around the hotels.
  • Swimwear for the beach and the pool, with a shirt, kaftan or sarong to put on when you walk to shops and restaurants.
  • One long-sleeved top and light trousers. They protect you from the sun on boats and from mosquitoes in the evening and near forests.
  • Clothes that dry fast. December showers are common, and wet clothes stay wet in the humidity.
  • One nicer outfit for a dinner or the Cellular Jail light and sound show in the evening.

What to wear in Andaman in December

December is warm, with days at about 30–31 °C, but it rains more than in January or February. On top of the usual summer clothes, add:

  • a light rain jacket or poncho, or a small umbrella
  • quick-dry clothes, and one spare set in a zip bag
  • sandals or floaters, not canvas shoes, which stay wet
  • a zip bag or dry bag for your phone, ID and tickets on the ferry

Andaman trip dresses for women

  • Three or four cotton dresses: midi or maxi dresses are cool and look good in beach photos
  • Shorts or skirts with light tops, and one pair of loose trousers
  • Swimwear, and a kaftan or sarong to wear over it
  • A light scarf or stole for the sun, the flight and AC rooms
  • A wide hat or cap, and sunglasses
  • Flip-flops and one pair of sandals or floaters
  • A crossbody bag that zips shut

What to wear in Andaman for a honeymoon

  • Two or three outfits for photos. Radhanagar Beach on Swaraj Dweep is known for its sunsets, and Andaman Tourism says to arrive by 4 PM for the best light. Bright colours stand out against white sand and blue water.
  • One nice outfit each for a candlelight dinner at your resort.
  • Comfortable clothes for the boats. Long ferry rides and early starts are part of most Andaman trips, so keep the photo outfits for the beach.

Shoes

  • Floaters or sandals that can get wet. You'll step off boats into shallow water at many beaches.
  • Flip-flops for the beach and the hotel.
  • Walking shoes if you plan the Baratang limestone caves, a forest walk or a long day in Sri Vijaya Puram.

Sun, rain and health

  • Sunscreen, SPF 30 or higher. Put it on again every 2 hours and after swimming. Read our sunscreen guide.
  • Sunglasses and a hat. The glare from the sea is strong on boats.
  • Lip balm with SPF, and after-sun or aloe gel.
  • A rain poncho or small umbrella, for December.
  • Mosquito repellent, especially in the evening and near forests.
  • Pills for seasickness if boats upset you. Ferries to Swaraj Dweep take about 1.5 to 3 hours.
  • Your regular medicines, plus something for an upset stomach, ORS and plasters.
  • Going scuba diving? Tell the dive centre about any health problems before you dive.

Snorkelling and swimming

  • Leave shiny jewellery at the hotel. Andaman Tourism advises removing shiny jewellery and bright-coloured clothes before you swim, so they don't attract predatory fish.
  • Swim only where it's allowed, inside the nets where there are nets, and never at Wandoor Beach. Ask the lifeguard about currents and tides.
  • Don't touch or stand on coral. Andaman Tourism says touching, stepping on or collecting coral and seashells is a punishable offence.
  • A quick-dry T-shirt keeps the sun off your back while you snorkel.

Phone, camera and power bank

  • A power bank in your cabin bag only, never in check-in. See can I carry a power bank?
  • A charger with two ports for both phones, and a long cable.
  • Drones need an official permit in Andaman, so leave yours at home unless you have one.
  • Never photograph or film tribal people. It's a crime in Andaman.

Which bag to take

  • About 5 nights: a cabin trolley or a 30–40 litre backpack is enough. Beach clothes are light.
  • About a week, or a couple: one medium trolley, or a cabin bag each.
  • Island hopping? A backpack or a light trolley is easier on jetties and ferry steps.

On ferries: government ferries between the islands allow free baggage of up to 25 kg in Deck class, 30 kg in Bunk class and 40 kg in Cabin class. Heavy luggage and goods aren't allowed on board.

On flights: Andaman Tourism names Air India, IndiGo and SpiceJet among the airlines that fly to Sri Vijaya Puram. Economy rules, from our airline pages:

What not to pack

  • Single-use plastic. Carry a steel bottle and a cloth bag instead.
  • Expensive jewellery. You won't need it on the beach, and you should take it off before you swim.
  • A drone without a permit.
  • Lighters and matches. They're banned in both cabin and check-in bags on flights.
  • Big bottles in your cabin bag. Each liquid must be 100 ml or less, in one clear 1-litre bag. Bigger bottles go in check-in. See can I carry liquids?
  • Too many shoes and heavy jeans. They take space and stay wet in the humidity.

Flying home from Andaman

  • Don't take coral or shells. Collecting coral and seashells is a punishable offence in Andaman.
  • Dry coconut (copra) is banned in both cabin and check-in bags, because it can catch fire. See can I carry coconut?
  • Spice powders can't go in your cabin bag. Pack them in check-in. See can I carry spices?
  • Weigh your bag before you leave the hotel. Most domestic fares give only 15 kg.

Questions

What should I pack for an Andaman trip?

Light cotton clothes, swimwear and a cover-up, one long-sleeved top, floaters or sandals that can get wet, flip-flops, sunscreen, sunglasses, a hat, a rain poncho for December, a waterproof pouch, a towel, a steel bottle, mosquito repellent and your photo ID.

What should I wear in Andaman in December?

Light cotton or linen clothes that dry fast, swimwear, sandals or floaters, a hat and sunglasses. Add a light rain jacket, poncho or umbrella, because December gets about 113 mm of rain in Sri Vijaya Puram. Days are about 30–31 °C, so you don't need woollens.

What dresses should women wear in Andaman?

Cotton midi or maxi dresses, shorts or skirts with light tops, swimwear with a kaftan or sarong over it, and a light stole for the sun. Pack one long-sleeved top and loose trousers for boats and mosquito-prone evenings.

What should I wear in Andaman for a honeymoon?

Two or three outfits for beach photos, especially for the sunset at Radhanagar Beach, one nice outfit each for dinner, and comfortable quick-dry clothes for the ferries. Bright colours look good against the white sand, but take off shiny jewellery before you swim.

Which shoes are best for Andaman?

Floaters or strap sandals that can get wet, because you'll step off boats into shallow water, plus flip-flops for the beach. Take walking shoes only if you plan the Baratang caves or forest walks.

How much luggage can I take on Andaman ferries?

Government ferries between the islands allow up to 25 kg in Deck class, 30 kg in Bunk class and 40 kg in Cabin class. On flights to Sri Vijaya Puram, IndiGo and SpiceJet give 15 kg of check-in baggage on most fares, plus a 7 kg cabin bag.

Can I bring shells or coral back from Andaman?

No. Andaman Tourism says touching, stepping on or collecting coral and seashells is a punishable offence. Dry coconut is also banned on flights, in any bag.

Do I need warm clothes for Andaman?

No. Nights stay at about 24–26 °C in December, according to IMD. One light layer for the flight and AC rooms is enough.
