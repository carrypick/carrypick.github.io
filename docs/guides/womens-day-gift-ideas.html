How to choose a Women's Day gift

  • For the office, keep it small and the same for everyone. One kind of gift for every woman in the team avoids any "why did she get more" talk.
  • Useful beats showy. A lip balm, a lotion, a wallet or a bottle gets used. A trophy or a showpiece goes in a drawer.
  • Skip anything with a size. Clothes and shoes are hard to get right for a colleague. Keep shoes for your mom or wife, when you can check an old pair.
  • For family, upgrade something old. A watch with a faded strap, a bag with a broken zip or a worn-out wallet is your clue.
  • It's a Monday. Order by the middle of the week before, and check the delivery date shown on Amazon before you pay. For a team, order a few extra.
  • Add a line in your own words. "Thank you for what you do" means more than a printed quote.

Questions

When is Women's Day 2027?

International Women's Day is on Monday 8 March 2027. It is the same date every year, all over the world. It is not a public holiday in India, so offices usually mark it that morning.

What is a good Women's Day gift for female colleagues?

A small, useful gift that's the same for everyone: an SPF lip balm, a small hand and face lotion, a steel bottle or a zip-around wallet. For a colleague you know well, or a gift from the whole team, an office tote with a laptop pocket.

What can I give for Women's Day in the office if I'm buying for many people?

Choose one small token, like a lip balm or a small lotion, and buy enough for the whole team plus a few extra. Add a ribbon and a handwritten line for each person. We don't list prices: check the current price on Amazon.in.

What is a good Women's Day gift for a wife?

An elegant watch like the Titan Karishma, a small tote with enough pockets, or a perfume gift set. Add a note that says what you're thankful for.

What should I gift my mother on Women's Day?

Something she'll use every day: a Timex watch with a clear dial, slip-on walking shoes for her walk, or an attar gift set. The same ideas work for a mother-in-law.

What can I give my sister or a friend on Women's Day?

A Fastrack watch, a vegan leather sling, cat-eye sunglasses or a perfume gift set. For a friend who reads, Eat, Pray, Love is a gift about travel and starting again.
