Are Titan and Fastrack the same company?

Yes. Titan Company lists Titan, Fastrack, Sonata and Fastrack Smart as its own brands on titancompany.in. Fastrack is described there as "India's quintessential youth brand". So the warranty and service work the same way through Titan, whichever one you buy.

Titan vs Fastrack vs Sonata

  • Titan: the main brand. Classic dials, thin cases, steel and leather straps. Good for office and gifting.
  • Fastrack: the youth brand. Bold colours, sporty cases, silicone straps. Good for college and casual wear.
  • Sonata: a third brand from the same company, with simple everyday watches. See our Sonata couple watch set.

Water resistance: what 30 m and 50 m mean

The number is not a diving depth. 30 m means splash-proof: rain and hand washing are fine, but not a bath or a swim. 50 m is a little more, but still not for swimming. For swimming, look for 100 m or more. Most Titan and Fastrack analog watches are 30 m or 50 m, so take them off before a shower.

Warranty

On the brands' own sites, a Titan analog watch (for example this Titan model) and a Fastrack analog watch (for example this Fastrack model) both say: 24 months on the movement and 12 months on the battery. Titan's warranty policy gives 12 months for Fastrack Smart smartwatches and 6 months for their straps.

A seller on Amazon may list a different period, as our Fastrack Minimal Black does (6 months). Check the listing and keep the invoice. Titan suggests registering for its e-warranty after you buy.

Titan vs Fastrack for women

Both make women's watches. Titan's are usually more formal, like the mother-of-pearl Titan Raga Charm or the Titan Karishma for women. Fastrack's are lighter and more playful, like the pink-dial Fastrack. For a gift for her, see our Christmas gifts for her.

Titan vs Fastrack smartwatch

Titan Company sells smartwatches under both names. We list only analog watches for now. Remember a smartwatch needs charging every few days, and the Fastrack Smart warranty is 12 months, shorter than the analog one.

Questions

Which is better, Titan or Fastrack?

Neither is better overall, as one company makes both. Titan suits office wear and gifts, and Fastrack suits college and casual wear.

Are Titan and Fastrack the same company?

Yes. Both, and Sonata too, are brands of Titan Company Limited.

Is Fastrack a good brand for men?

Yes, for casual and sporty wear. For a formal office watch, a Titan with a steel or leather strap usually looks smarter.

Is Titan or Fastrack better for a gift?

For a parent, boss or a wedding gift, Titan. For a student or a young friend, Fastrack.

Can I wear a Titan or Fastrack watch while bathing?

Not if it's 30 m or 50 m. These are splash-proof only. Take the watch off before a bath or swim.

What is the warranty on Fastrack watches?

Fastrack's own site lists 24 months on the movement and 12 months on the battery for its analog watches. Fastrack Smart smartwatches have 12 months. Some Amazon listings show a shorter period, so check before you buy.
