Rishikesh packing list: what to pack and wear for rafting, camps and the Ganga aarti

Updated 25 Nov 2026 · 10 min read

Rishikesh in winter is sunny and pleasant at noon and cold in the morning and at night, and the Ganga is icy all season because it comes straight down from the Himalayas. So you pack light layers for the day, a warm jacket for the aarti and the camp, quick-dry clothes for rafting, and modest clothes for temples and ashrams. This list covers a 2- to 5-day trip from October to March.

Rishikesh weather in winter

IMD has no climate table for Rishikesh itself. These are its long-term averages (1991–2020) for Dehradun, the nearest IMD station, about 40 km away, as in our Rishikesh guide (read 25 Nov 2026). Rishikesh is lower, in the river valley, so its days are often a little warmer:

MonthDayNightRain
DecemberAbout 22 °CAbout 8 °CAbout 13 mm
JanuaryAbout 20 °CAbout 7 °CAbout 44 mm
FebruaryAbout 23 °CAbout 9 °CAbout 58 mm
  • The coldest nights at Dehradun are usually 1–6 °C in December and 0–5 °C in January, IMD says. Riverside camps feel colder.
  • January is the wettest winter month, with about 3 rainy days. Pack a light rain layer.
  • It doesn't snow in Rishikesh. The town is only about 350 m above sea level.
  • The Ganga is very cold in winter. Plan for it on the raft and at the ghats.
  • Check the forecast on IMD Dehradun in the week before you go.

The short list

If you pack only these, you'll be fine:

  1. T-shirts or tops for sunny afternoons, some full-sleeved
  2. A sweater or fleece and a warm jacket for mornings, the aarti and camps
  3. Quick-dry clothes for rafting, and a full change of warm clothes for after
  4. Clothes that cover shoulders and knees, and a scarf or stole, for temples and ashrams
  5. Walking shoes, and sandals or flip-flops that come off fast
  6. A waterproof phone pouch
  7. Sunglasses, sunscreen and lip balm
  8. A small towel, warm sleepwear and socks for a camp night
  9. A power bank, a small torch, a refillable bottle and a cloth bag
  10. Your photo ID, bookings and some cash

What to wear for rafting

  • Quick-dry clothes, such as a synthetic T-shirt and track pants or shorts. Not cotton, and never jeans: they soak up cold water and stay wet.
  • A full change of warm clothes and a towel in a dry bag in the car, for straight after.
  • Shoes that stay on: sports sandals with a back strap, or old trainers. Flip-flops come off in the water.
  • The operator gives you the life jacket and helmet. Book only a licensed operator, and wear both the whole time.
  • Sunglasses with a strap, or leave them behind. Many people lose them in the first rapid.
  • Phone: in a waterproof pouch, or leave it with the operator.
  • Raft later in the morning in December and January. Early mornings on the river are cold.

For a camp night by the river

  • Stay only in a licensed camp. Camps are allowed only at approved sites, at least 100 metres from the river.
  • Warm sleepwear, socks and a cap. Nights in camps can drop close to 5 °C in December and January. Check that the camp gives you warm bedding.
  • A small torch for the walk to the bathroom.
  • A power bank. Not every tent has a socket.
  • Never sleep with a heater or charcoal burning in a closed tent or room.

Clothes for the day and the aarti

  • Layers: a T-shirt or shirt for sunny afternoons, a sweater or fleece, and a warm jacket for the evening aarti at Triveni Ghat or Parmarth Niketan. It gets cold fast after sunset, around 5:30 PM in winter.
  • A morning safari in Rajaji or a sunrise at Kunjapuri means your warmest jacket and a cap: it's much colder in an open jeep or up on the hill.
  • A light rain jacket or poncho for January showers.

At temples, ashrams and the ghats

  • Cover your shoulders and knees at temples and ashrams. Since 2023, the Neelkanth Mahadev temple near Rishikesh has asked visitors not to come in shorts, torn jeans or short skirts, as our Rishikesh guide notes.
  • A scarf or stole is useful for women at temples and the aarti.
  • Shoes come off at temples and many ghats. Slip-ons or flip-flops save time.
  • Bathe only at proper ghats, and hold the chains. The current is strong even where the river looks calm.
  • Rishikesh is a vegetarian town with no liquor shops. Meat, fish and eggs aren't sold within municipal limits.

  • Three or four tops or kurtas, some full-sleeved, and leggings or loose trousers

  • Quick-dry clothes for rafting, and a full change for after
  • A stole or scarf for temples and the aarti

What to pack for men

  • Three or four T-shirts or shirts, some full-sleeved
  • Two pairs of trousers (shorts are fine for rafting, not for temples)
  • A sweater or fleece and a warm jacket
  • Quick-dry clothes for rafting
  • Walking shoes, and sports sandals or old trainers for rafting

What to pack for kids

  • Sunscreen, a sun hat and their own medicines
  • Snacks and a water bottle for the drive

Rishikesh packing list for 3 days

  • 3 tops, 2 pairs of trousers
  • 1 quick-dry set for rafting, plus a full change of warm clothes
  • 1 sweater or fleece, 1 warm jacket, 1 cap
  • 1 thermal set or warm sleepwear for a camp night
  • 4 pairs of socks and underwear
  • 1 scarf or stole

Staying 5 days or doing a yoga course? Add two tops, comfortable yoga clothes and a pair of trousers, and wash clothes there.

Questions

What should I pack for Rishikesh in winter?

Layers for sunny afternoons, a sweater or fleece and a warm jacket for mornings, nights and the aarti, quick-dry clothes and a warm change for rafting, modest clothes and a scarf for temples, walking shoes, sandals, sunscreen, sunglasses and a waterproof phone pouch. IMD's averages for Dehradun, the nearest station, are about 22 °C by day and 8 °C at night in December.

What should I wear for rafting in Rishikesh?

Quick-dry clothes, not cotton or jeans, and sports sandals with a back strap or old trainers. The operator gives you a life jacket and helmet. Keep a full change of warm clothes and a towel for straight after, because the Ganga is very cold in winter.

What should I wear in Rishikesh in December?

Light layers for the day, and a sweater or fleece and a warm jacket for mornings, evenings and camps. Cover your shoulders and knees at temples and ashrams.

What should women wear in Rishikesh?

Loose tops or kurtas with leggings or trousers, a stole or scarf, and warm layers for the evenings. At the Neelkanth Mahadev temple, visitors are asked not to wear shorts, torn jeans or short skirts.

Is it cold in a Rishikesh camp at night?

Yes, in December and January. Nights can drop close to 5 °C by the river. Take warm sleepwear, a cap and socks, and check that the camp gives you warm bedding.

Do I need a heavy jacket for Rishikesh?

Not a snow jacket, but a warm one for the evening aarti, early starts and camps. A puffer or quilted jacket over a sweater is enough for most people.
