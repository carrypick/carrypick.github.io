Gadget organiser: hard case or soft pouch?

Updated 23 Nov 2026 · 5 min read

A gadget organiser is a small zip case for the things that get tangled at the bottom of a bag: chargers, cables, earphones, a power bank, pen drives and a travel adapter. At airport security or in a hotel room you lift one case out instead of fishing for each cable. The big choice is hard case or soft pouch. Here's how to choose, and five organisers we picked by their listed specs.

Quick answer

  • Just a phone charger, a cable and earbuds: a small soft pouch.
  • Laptop charger, hard drive or camera bits: a bigger case with dividers or two layers.
  • Packed under heavy clothes in a suitcase: a hard-shell case keeps its shape.
  • Men's and women's organisers are the same thing. Choose by size and pockets.

A hard shell helps most when the case sits under clothes in a suitcase. In a day bag you carry yourself, a soft pouch is lighter and bends to fit.

How to choose a gadget organiser

  • Size. Lay out what you'll carry. A laptop charger brick needs a deeper case than a phone charger; our picks are 5 to 11 cm deep.
  • Elastic loops hold coiled cables in place. Mesh pockets let you see the small bits, like SIM pins and pen drives.
  • Dividers stop a heavy charger from crushing earbuds. Our Amazon Basics pick has 3 you can move.
  • A zip all round that opens flat makes it easy to use on a hotel desk or a train seat.
  • Water resistance. Handy in the monsoon. Four of our five picks list a water-resistant or waterproof outside; the double-layer UGREEN listing doesn't mention it.
  • Warranty. Ours range from 30 days to 24 months, as each listing states.

What to pack in a gadget organiser

  • Phone charger and cable, and a laptop charger if you carry a laptop
  • A spare USB-C cable, and the one your earbuds or watch uses
  • Earphones or earbuds
  • A travel adapter if you're going abroad
  • Pen drive, SD cards and a card reader
  • A SIM pin and a small notebook of important numbers

Power banks go in your cabin bag, never in check-in. If your organiser holds one, keep the organiser with you. See power bank rules on flights.

Questions

What is a gadget organiser?

A small zip case with loops, pockets and sometimes dividers, for chargers, cables, earphones, pen drives and other small electronics. It keeps them together and stops cables from tangling.

Is a hard case gadget organiser better?

Only if it gets pressed, for example under clothes in a suitcase, or if you carry a hard drive. A soft pouch is lighter and squeezes into a full backpack. Our AirCase pick is the hard one, at 259 g.

Which gadget organiser is good for men?

Any of these. Gadget organisers aren't made for men or women: choose by what you carry. The small UGREEN pouch suits a phone kit; the double-layer UGREEN and Amazon Basics cases fit a laptop charger too.

Can I carry a gadget organiser in my cabin bag?

Yes. Keep it near the top, as security staff may want to see what's inside. A power bank in it must stay in your cabin bag: see power bank rules on flights.

Will a laptop charger fit in a gadget organiser?

In a deeper one, usually. Check the depth: our double-layer UGREEN is 5 cm deep, the DailyObjects Vault 8.5 cm and the Amazon Basics case 11 cm.

Is a gadget organiser a good gift?

Yes, for someone who travels for work or carries a laptop every day. See more ideas in gifts for travellers.
