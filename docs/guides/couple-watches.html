Couple watches: Titan, Sonata, Fastrack and Timex pair sets compared

Updated 23 Nov 2026 · 4 min read

A couple watch set is two matching watches in one box, his and hers. It's one of the most common wedding and anniversary gifts in India, because it says "for both of you". Here are four sets from Titan, Sonata, Fastrack and Timex, and how to choose between them.

Titan, Sonata and Fastrack are one company

Titan, Sonata and Fastrack are all brands of Titan Company, so service and warranty work the same way through Titan's stores. Titan is the classic brand, Sonata the simpler value brand and Fastrack the young, casual one. Our Titan vs Fastrack guide explains the difference. Timex is a separate American brand.

How to choose a couple watch set

  1. Strap. Steel suits daily wear and rain. Leather looks softer but doesn't like water or sweat.
  2. Dial size. Couple sets pair a big men's watch with a small women's one. The Titan Bandhan's men's dial is large at 47.3 mm, so it suits a wider wrist. Check your partner's wrist in our watch size tool.
  3. Matching or "mixmatched". Titan and Sonata Bandhan sets match closely. Fastrack's Mixmatched sets use two different dial colours that go together.
  4. Water resistance. 30 m and 50 m are both splash-proof only. Take them off before a bath or a swim.
  5. Warranty. Titan Bandhan lists 2 years, Sonata and Timex 1 year. Keep the invoice for both watches.

Questions

Which brand is best for couple watches?

There's no single best. Titan is the classic choice for weddings and parents, Sonata is simpler, Fastrack suits young couples and Timex is a classic international brand.

What is a couple watch set?

Two watches sold together in one box, one for a man and one for a woman, in a matching or complementary design.

Is a couple watch a good wedding gift?

Yes. It's practical, it lasts for years, and it's for both people. Choose steel straps for a gift, as they last longer than leather.

Can I wear a couple watch in the shower?

No. These sets are rated 30 m or 50 m, which means splash-proof only. Take them off before a bath or a swim.

Do Titan couple watches have a warranty?

Our Titan Bandhan pick lists a 2-year warranty. Titan's own site lists 24 months on the movement for its analog watches. Keep the invoice.
