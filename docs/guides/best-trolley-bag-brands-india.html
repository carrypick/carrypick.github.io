Best trolley bag brands in India: 7 brands compared

Updated 6 Nov 2026 · 6 min read

There's no single best trolley brand. The right one depends on your airline, your fare and how you travel. So we read 146 trolley listings on Amazon.in and compared seven brands by what they list.

We haven't tested these bags, so nothing here tells you how long they last.

Quick answer

  • Lightest: Kamiliant, then American Tourister. In the large size, Kamiliant's Rock-Lite was the lightest at 3.48 kg.
  • Most listed litres in medium: Safari, though litres are often unreliable.
  • Closest to the 158 cm check-in limit: L'Attitude and Safari had the most large bags within it.
  • Most soft-case choices: Aristocrat. Skybags and VIP also have some.
  • Warranty: most models we picked list 3 years. We didn't find a warranty on the Kamiliant listings we picked.

The brands, in short

  • American Tourister belongs to the Samsonite group. Its bags were among the lightest, and the Liftoff+ range is listed with a TSA lock. See American Tourister vs Safari.
  • Safari is an Indian company based in Mumbai. It listed plenty of polycarbonate bags, and its medium bags listed the most litres of the bigger groups here.
  • Kamiliant is sold as "Kamiliant by American Tourister". It had the lightest typical bags, all hard shells. We didn't find a warranty on the two we picked, so check before you buy.
  • Aristocrat has many soft-case bags as well as hard ones. It's one of the brands sold on VIP's own store.
  • Skybags is also sold on VIP's store. Its range mixes hard bags with the soft Rubik.
  • VIP showed few bags in our Amazon searches, so we can't say much from these numbers. Prefer to buy from VIP directly? See VIP's sizes, warranty and returns.
  • L'Attitude lists its bags by both cm and inches. Its Meridian large bag was among the heaviest here, at 5.16 kg.

Questions

Which is the best trolley bag brand in India?

There's no single best brand. In the listings we read, Kamiliant and American Tourister were the lightest, and Safari listed the most litres among the bigger groups. Choose by your airline's limits and your fare.

Which trolley bag brand is the lightest?

Kamiliant had the lowest typical weights we read: 2.97 kg in medium and 3.96 kg in large. American Tourister was close behind in large, at 4 kg.

VIP or Safari: which is better?

We read too few VIP listings on Amazon.in to compare them fairly: 3 medium and large bags, against 18 from Safari. Two of those VIP bags were listed as soft cases. Compare the size, weight and warranty of the two bags you're choosing between.

VIP or American Tourister: which is better?

We can't judge from our small VIP sample. The VIP bags we read weighed more than American Tourister's typical bags. But two of the three were listed as soft cases, so it isn't a like-for-like test.

Skybags or American Tourister: which is better?

In our listings, American Tourister's typical bags were lighter: 3.3 kg against 3.57 kg in medium, and 4 kg against 4.6 kg in large. Skybags' Swirl large was smaller in total, at 152 cm.

Are Aristocrat and Skybags part of VIP?

VIP's own store sells VIP, Skybags, Aristocrat, Caprese, Alfa and Savoy Paris. So buying from these brands is often buying from the same group. Models and sizes still differ.
