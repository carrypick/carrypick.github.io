Anti-theft backpack: what it really means, and which one to buy

Updated 23 Nov 2026 · 6 min read

"Anti-theft" is not one feature. On Amazon it can mean a hidden zip, a lock, or just one secret pocket. These help in a crowded train, metro or market, where most theft is a quick hand in an open pocket. Here's what each one does, and the bags we picked for each.

What does "anti-theft backpack" mean?

An anti-theft backpack makes it hard to open the bag without you noticing. Sellers use the word for four different things:

  • Hidden zip. The main zip is on the back panel, against your body. To open it, someone has to take the bag off your back. This is the "true" anti-theft design.
  • Lock. A number lock or zip loops that a small padlock fits through. It stops someone opening the bag when it's on a train rack, a bus floor or a hostel bed.
  • Anti-theft pocket. A small hidden pocket on the back panel. Your main compartment still opens normally, but your phone, wallet or passport stay safe against your back.
  • Slash-proof fabric and RFID blocking. Fabric with wire mesh, or a pocket that blocks card readers. These are rare in India. Believe them only when the listing clearly names them.

What anti-theft bags don't do

  • They don't stop someone taking the whole bag. Keep a hand or a foot on it, and loop a strap round your leg when you sit.
  • A number lock can be forced. It slows a thief down; it doesn't make the bag a safe.
  • A USB charging port is not an anti-theft feature. It's just a cable inside the bag for your power bank.
  • They aren't waterproof by default. Check the water resistance row before the monsoon.

  • Long train journeys, hostels, college: the number-lock Fur Jaden. Its details table says it isn't water resistant, so carry a rain cover in the monsoon.

Simple habits that stop most theft

  1. Wear the bag on your front in a packed metro or train.
  2. Keep your phone and wallet in the back pocket or the hidden zip, never in an outer pocket.
  3. On an overnight train, lock the zips together and chain the bag to the berth.
  4. At airports, a small TSA lock on the zips is fine for a cabin bag.

Questions

What is an anti-theft backpack?

A backpack that is hard to open while you're wearing it: usually the main zip faces your back, or the bag has a lock or a hidden back pocket for your valuables.

Do anti-theft backpacks really work?

They stop the most common theft, a quick hand in an open pocket in a crowd. They don't stop someone taking the whole bag, so keep it close.

Are anti-theft backpacks waterproof?

Not always. The number-lock Fur Jaden's details table says it isn't water resistant. Check each listing, or pick one with a rain cover like the Safari Forge.

Is a USB port an anti-theft feature?

No. It's a cable that connects your own power bank inside the bag to your phone outside it. The power bank isn't included.

Which anti-theft backpack is good for a 15.6 inch laptop?

Both Fur Jaden bags, the Swiss Military 30L and the Gear Classic 4 list a 15.6 inch laptop fit. Not sure of your size? Use our laptop backpack size tool.

Can I take an anti-theft backpack as cabin baggage?

Yes, if it fits your airline's cabin size, like 55 x 35 x 25 cm on IndiGo. All five bags compared here are listed within that size. Check yours with our cabin bag checker.
