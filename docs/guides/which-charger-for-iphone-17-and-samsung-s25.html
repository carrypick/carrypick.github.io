Which charger for iPhone 17, Samsung S25 or Pixel 10?

Updated 24 Nov 2026 · 9 min read

New phones from Apple, Samsung and Google come without a charger in the box. So the first question is which one to buy, and how many watts. The short answer: a USB-C charger with USB PD, about 45 W for a Samsung Ultra or Pixel 10 Pro XL, and 20 W or more for an iPhone. A 65 W GaN charger covers all of them and a laptop too, which is handy on a trip.

Quick answer

  • iPhone 17, 17 Pro, 17 Pro Max: Apple says 50% in about 20 minutes with a 40 W adapter or higher. A 20 W charger also works, just slower.
  • iPhone Air, 17e, 16, 15: a 20 W USB-C PD charger or higher gives 50% in about 30 minutes (Apple).
  • Galaxy S25 Ultra and S25+: 45 W Super Fast Charging 2.0 needs a 45 W charger with PPS and a 5 A cable. The base S25 takes 25 W.
  • Pixel 10 and 10 Pro: 30 W PPS charger. Pixel 10 Pro XL and 10a: 45 W PPS.
  • Is a bigger charger safe? Yes. The phone takes only what it needs. A 65 W USB PD charger on an iPhone is fine.

Which charger for iPhone 17?

Apple's figure for the iPhone 17, 17 Pro and 17 Pro Max is 50% in about 20 minutes with a 40 W adapter or higher. On its support page Apple names its own 40W Dynamic Power Adapter with 60W Max, or a third-party charger that is 60 W-capable, supports USB PD 3.2 and AVS.

Many chargers in India are USB PD 3.0 and don't mention AVS. Our two picks are like that. They still charge an iPhone 17 safely and fast, but Apple only promises the 20-minute figure with the charger type above. If you want that exact speed, check the box for "PD 3.2" or "AVS".

A 20 W USB-C charger still works with every iPhone 17. Apple gives 50% in about 30 minutes with 20 W for the Air and 17e, and older iPhones.

Can I use a 65W charger for iPhone 17?

Yes. USB PD chargers and the phone agree on the power first. The iPhone asks for what it can take, and the charger gives no more than that. The same is true for the iPhone 16 and 15.

Which charger for Samsung S25 Ultra?

The S25 Ultra and S25+ reach 45 W with Samsung's Super Fast Charging 2.0. Samsung's own 45 W adapter page says it needs a USB PD 3.0 device with PPS, and its box has a 5 A cable. Samsung UK's help page says 45 W is only possible with a 45 W charger and a 5 A cable.

So you need two things:

  • A charger that says PPS and gives 45 W on one port.
  • A 5 A USB-C to USB-C cable. The 3 A cable in the phone's box caps it at 25 W.

The base Galaxy S25 takes 25 W, so any 25 W PPS charger is enough. The A36 also does 45 W Super Fast Charge, by Samsung's page.

Of our picks, the Spigen listing states 45 W Super Fast Charging for Samsung when one port is used alone. The Belkin lists PD 3.0 and PPS and 45 W on a port, but doesn't name Samsung's 45 W mode.

Pixel, Nothing and OnePlus

  • Pixel 10 and 10 Pro: Google says fast charging needs a 30 W USB-C PPS charger or higher. The 10 Pro XL and 10a need 45 W PPS. Both our picks list PPS.
  • Nothing Phone (3): 65 W, and Nothing lists PPS and PD 3.0, so a 65 W PPS charger gets close.
  • OnePlus 13 and 15: their top speed (100 W and 120 W) is SUPERVOOC, which needs the OnePlus charger in the box. OnePlus doesn't state the speed on other USB PD chargers, so take the box charger on trips.

GaN charger vs normal charger

GaN (gallium nitride) is a material inside the charger. It wastes less power as heat, so a GaN charger is smaller and lighter for the same watts. A 65 W GaN charger is often about the size of an old 20 W phone charger.

It doesn't charge your phone faster by itself. Speed comes from the watts and the standard (USB PD, PPS) that both the phone and charger support.

Is a GaN charger safe? As safe as any charger from a known brand. In India, chargers must carry the BIS mark. Both our picks list BIS.

One charger for phone and laptop on a trip

On a trip, one 65 W charger with two or three ports can replace a phone charger, an earbuds charger and often the laptop brick:

  • 65 W runs most USB-C laptops, like a MacBook Air. A big 14 or 16-inch laptop may want more.
  • Ports share the power. With two devices plugged in, each gets less. The Belkin splits 45 W + 20 W.
  • Abroad you still need the right plug. Both picks have Indian pins. Use a travel adapter for the UK, Europe or the USA.
  • Voltage: our picks' listings don't say "100-240 V". Most countries use 220-240 V, like India. For the USA, Canada or Japan, read the small print on the charger itself before you go.
  • Chargers are fine in both bags. Power banks are not: they go in the cabin only. See power bank rules on flights.

Keep the charger and cables together in a gadget organiser.

Questions

Which charger is best for iPhone 17?

Any USB-C charger with USB PD works. Apple says 40 W or higher gives 50% in about 20 minutes, and names its 40W Dynamic Power Adapter or a 60 W-capable charger with USB PD 3.2 and AVS. A 20 W charger is slower but fine.

How many watts does iPhone 17 support?

Apple doesn't give one top number. It says 50% in about 20 minutes with a 40 W adapter or higher. For the iPhone Air and 17e it says 20 W or higher.

Does iPhone 17 come with a charger?

No. Apple's box has the phone, a USB-C cable and papers. The charger is sold separately.

Which charger for Samsung S25 Ultra?

A 45 W charger with PPS and a 5 A USB-C cable, for Super Fast Charging 2.0. The S25 Ultra box has no charger.

Can I use a Samsung charger for an iPhone?

Yes, if it's USB-C with USB PD, like Samsung's 25 W or 45 W adapters. The iPhone takes what it needs.

Is a GaN charger better than a normal charger?

It's smaller and runs cooler for the same watts. It isn't faster on its own: watts and the charging standard decide speed.

Can I carry a charger in a flight?

Yes, a wall charger is fine in cabin and check-in bags. Power banks are the ones that must go in the cabin.
