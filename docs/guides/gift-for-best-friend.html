How to choose a gift for your best friend

  • Think of the plans you make together. Trips, concerts, chai at midnight? A gift for the next plan says "let's do it again".
  • Unisex picks are safe. Round sunglasses, a black watch or a shared perfume box suit a girl or a boy.
  • Notice what they keep borrowing. If they always take your power bank or sunglasses, that's the gift.
  • Group gift? Pool money with friends for something bigger, like a watch or a cabin trolley.
  • A note is half the gift. Write about one memory only the two of you know.
