Backpack size chart in litres: which size do you need?

Updated 6 Nov 2026 · 5 min read

Backpacks are sold by litres. A bigger number means more space, but also more weight on your back. Here's what each size suits, and what fits in the cabin on Indian flights.

Quick answer

LitresGood forCabin bag on a flight?
15–20 LSchool, college, a light daily bagYes, usually
20–30 LOffice with a laptop, a day tripYes, usually
30–40 L2–4 day trips, a weekend awayOften, so check the full size
40–50 L3–5 day trips, light treksRarely, so check the full size
50–70 LTreks of several daysNo, check it in
70 L and upLong treks with a lot of gearNo, check it in

These are rough guides. Bulky winter clothes need more room, and light packers need less. For most people who fly, 30–40 L is the largest useful size.

Why 40 L is about the limit in the cabin

An airline's size limit is a box. Work out how many litres fit in that box:

Airline limitBox sizeSpace in that box
IndiGo, Akasa Air, SpiceJet55 x 35 x 25 cm48 L
Air India Express56 x 36 x 23 cm46 L
Air India55 x 40 x 20 cm44 L

A backpack isn't a neat box, and a full one bulges. So a 45 L or 50 L bag rarely fits once it's packed. Most Indian airlines also allow only 7 kg in the cabin, including the bag. See our baggage rules.

Don't trust the litres alone

Litres aren't measured the same way by every brand. REI, a big outdoor store, points out the same thing about hiking packs. Some of our own listings show it:

  • Wesley Milestone 2.0 lists 30 L, but 45.7 x 31.8 x 15.5 cm works out to about 22.5 L.
  • Mokobara Transit lists 30 L, but 48 x 31 x 16 cm works out to about 24 L.
  • American Tourister Valex lists 28 L, and 47.5 x 32.5 x 20 cm works out to about 31 L.

So a "30 L" bag can be much smaller than another "30 L" bag. To check, multiply length x width x depth in cm, then divide by 1,000.

Choosing for your use

  • Office and college: 20–30 L is plenty. Check the laptop size with our laptop backpack tool, and read our laptop backpack guide.
  • Weekend trips: 30–40 L. See the weekend packing list.
  • Winter hill trips: add 10 L or so for jackets, or wear the bulky layers. See what to wear on a winter hill trip.
  • Treks with a tent: 50 L and up, usually with a hip belt and a frame. We don't list trekking packs yet, so check how one fits your back before you buy.

Questions

What size backpack do I need for a 3 day trip?

About 30–40 L, if you pack light. Bulky winter clothes may need more room, or wear the heaviest layers while you travel.

Is a 40 litre backpack allowed as cabin baggage?

Often, yes, but it depends on its full size and weight. For example, a 50 x 30 x 25 cm bag fits IndiGo's limit by size, but goes over Air India's 20 cm depth. Most Indian airlines allow 7 kg in the cabin, including the bag.

Is a 50 litre backpack allowed on a flight?

Usually only as a check-in bag. IndiGo's whole cabin limit works out to 48 L, so a packed 50 L backpack is likely to go over it.

Is a 30 litre backpack big enough for daily use?

Yes, and for many people it's more than enough. 20–30 L usually holds a laptop, a lunch box and a jacket. Smaller is lighter on your back.

How much weight can a 40 litre backpack hold?

It depends on what you pack, not just the size. The real limit on a flight is usually 7 kg in the cabin. For carrying on your back, a heavy bag gets tiring quickly, so pack only what you need.

What does 40 L mean on a backpack?

It means the seller says the bag holds about 40 litres. Brands measure this differently, so compare the listed size in cm too.
