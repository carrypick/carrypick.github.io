USA packing list for Indian students: what to carry from India

Updated 23 Nov 2026 · 12 min read

Flying to the USA for your studies, maybe for the January intake? This list covers what to carry from India, in the order that matters: the documents you must keep with you, how much baggage students get, which bags fit the airline limits, what US customs says about Indian food and medicines, and the clothes, plugs and small things that make the first weeks easier.

The short list

If you pack only these, you'll be fine:

  1. Your passport, with your F-1 (or J-1) visa in it
  2. Your Form I-20, signed, and the other papers your university asked for, in your hand bag
  3. Two sets of photocopies of these documents
  4. Your medicines in their original packs, with the prescription
  5. A warm jacket, thermals and shoes for cold, wet days if your city has a winter
  6. A laptop and its charger, a travel adapter for US sockets, and a power bank in your cabin bag
  7. A debit or forex card that works abroad, and some US dollars for the first days
  8. A check-in trolley within 158 cm and 23 kg, and a luggage scale

Documents to carry in your hand bag

Study in the States, the US Department of Homeland Security's site for students, says to carry your original documents with you at all times and not to put them in your checked baggage (page last updated 25 May 2023, read 23 Nov 2026). Its list of the important immigration documents is:

  • Your passport
  • Your visa (the F-1 or M-1 student visa in your passport)
  • Form I-20, the "Certificate of Eligibility for Nonimmigrant Student Status" from your school

It also suggests two sets of copies: one to leave with your family in India, and one for your school's officials.

  • If you arrive without all the papers, the CBP officer may send you to secondary inspection, an interview area, or give you a Form I-515A, says the same page.
  • Timing: you can enter the USA up to 30 days before the program start date on your I-20, and you must report to your school by that date.
  • Your I-94 record is created electronically when you arrive by air. You can get it later at the CBP I-94 website.
  • Keep any other papers your university or the US consulate told you to bring, such as your admission letter and proof of funds, in the same folder. Only your school and the official visa pages can tell you what you need.

Keep the folder in your backpack, so it's with you at immigration.

Baggage allowance for students

Students on these airlines can get more checked baggage than a normal ticket, as each airline's own page says (read 23 Nov 2026):

  • Air India student fare: on routes where bags are counted by piece, such as the USA, Economy and Premium Economy get 2 bags of 23 kg each. Choose "Student" as the concession type when you book. International student fares are for full-time students aged 12 to 30, and you must show a student ID, student visa or acceptance letter at the airport if asked.
  • Virgin Atlantic student offer: from Delhi, Mumbai or Bengaluru to the USA (and to London and Canada), Economy Classic and Delight and Premium get 3 bags of 23 kg, 69 kg in all. It isn't valid on Economy Light, it's for the outbound flight only, and you show your student visa at check-in. Virgin says to book it through its India contact centre.

On a normal fare, check what your ticket includes. From our airline data: Air India gives 1 bag of 23 kg on Value and 2 on Classic and Flex to the USA, Lufthansa gives 1 bag of 23 kg (none on Economy Light), and British Airways 1 bag of 23 kg (none on Basic). Emirates and Qatar Airways depend on your fare. See our international baggage rules and, if you'll pay for extra bags, the excess baggage calculator.

  • Each bag can usually be up to 158 cm in total (length + width + height). Air India's page also limits both Economy bags together to 271 cm, so two large bags of 158 cm each are too big. See trolley bag size for international travel.
  • Cabin bag: Air India allows 7 kg and 55 x 40 x 20 cm in Economy. Check yours with the cabin bag checker.

Which bags to take

Most students take one large check-in trolley, one medium one, and a cabin bag or laptop backpack.

  • Large bag: pick one of 75–79 cm that's listed within 158 cm. Lighter bags leave more of the 23 kg for your things.
  • Second bag: a medium bag of 65–69 cm keeps you within Air India's 271 cm for two bags when the large bag is about 152 cm or less. Read our 28 inch trolley guide for the sizes.
  • Buying a set? The large bag in many sets of 3 is over 158 cm. Check the listed total in our trolley bag set of 3 guide.
  • Don't buy a 32 inch bag: almost all are over 158 cm. See the 32 inch trolley guide.

What to wear in the USA

The weather depends on your city. New York averages about 4 °C by day and −3 °C at night in January, and about 6 °C and 0 °C in December. Los Angeles is about 20 °C and 9 °C in January (US National Weather Service averages on the WMO site, as in our USA trip guide). Many university towns in the north and midwest have colder winters with snow.

  • For the flight and the first weeks: thermals, two sweaters or fleeces, and a warm jacket with a hood. Wear the bulky jacket on the plane.
  • For a hard winter: a jacket from India is fine for autumn and for arriving, but many students buy a heavier winter coat and boots after they land, once they know their city.
  • Cap, gloves and a neck warmer: cold ears and hands are what you feel first.
  • Waterproof shoes with a good grip, worn in before you fly.
  • One formal outfit for presentations, interviews and events.
  • Check the forecast for your city in the week before you fly.

Indian food: what US customs allows

US Customs and Border Protection (CBP) says "All agriculture items must be declared" when you arrive (page last modified 30 Dec 2025, read 23 Nov 2026). Undeclared prohibited items are taken away and can bring a civil penalty. If you declare something that isn't allowed, you can leave it at the airport.

From CBP's prohibited and restricted items page (last modified 14 Mar 2025):

  • Allowed: condiments, vinegars, oils, packaged spices, honey, coffee and tea, bakery items and certain cheeses.
  • Not allowed: almost anything containing meat, including soup mixes and bouillon.
  • Rice: CBP says it's best to avoid bringing it.
  • Fresh fruit, vegetables, plants and seeds may be prohibited or restricted.
  • Not sure? Declare it. It costs nothing to declare, and a lot not to.

Pack spices in sealed shop packs, and keep them together in one bag so they're easy to show.

Medicines

  • Carry them in their original containers, with the label, says CBP. If you can't, bring a copy of the prescription or a letter from your doctor.
  • For non-US citizens, CBP asks for a valid prescription or doctor's note in English.
  • Bring only what you need for personal use. CBP's rule of thumb is a 90-day supply.
  • Keep them in your cabin bag, with the prescription, in case your check-in bag is late.
  • See can I carry medicines on a flight?

Laptop, phone, plugs and power bank

  • The USA uses 120 V and type A and B plugs (flat pins), so Indian plugs don't fit. See our power bank and travel adapter guide or our travel adapter for the USA page.
  • Check your chargers. Laptop and phone chargers marked 100–240 V work with just an adapter. Indian hair dryers and straighteners are often 230 V only, so they won't work properly on 120 V.
  • An adapter changes only the plug, not the voltage.
  • Power bank: in your cabin bag only, never in check-in. See can I carry a power bank?
  • Take two adapters if you can: one for your room and one for the library.

What not to pack

  • Meat products and homemade food with meat (see the customs rules above)
  • Fresh fruit and vegetables
  • A 32 inch bag that's over 158 cm
  • Too many clothes. Bags fill up fast, and you can buy most everyday things in the USA.
  • Power banks in check-in. They must go in your cabin bag.

Money

  • Over US$10,000? Report it. CBP says you must report currency and monetary instruments over US$10,000 when you enter or leave the USA (page last modified 24 Nov 2025). For a family travelling together, it's the total.
  • Cash from India: see how much cash you can carry abroad.
  • Carry a card that works abroad and some dollars for the first days, for a taxi, food and a SIM.

Weigh your bags before you leave home

Weigh each bag at home, because 23 kg is per bag and a few hundred grams over means repacking at the counter.

Questions

What should Indian students pack for the USA?

Your passport with the F-1 visa, Form I-20 and other university papers in your hand bag, your medicines with the prescription, winter clothes if your city has a winter, a laptop with a travel adapter for US sockets, a power bank in your cabin bag, and a card that works abroad. Pack one large trolley within 158 cm and 23 kg, a medium bag and a backpack.

What documents should I carry to the USA as a student?

Study in the States lists your passport, your visa and your Form I-20, and says to keep the originals with you, not in checked baggage. Make two sets of copies: one for your family and one for your school.

How much baggage can students carry to the USA on Air India?

Air India's student fare gives 2 bags of 23 kg each in Economy and Premium Economy on piece-based routes, such as the USA, as its student offer page said on 23 Nov 2026. You choose "Student" when booking and may have to show a student ID, visa or acceptance letter.

Can I take Indian food to the USA?

Yes, some. You must declare all food. CBP allows packaged spices, condiments, oils, honey, tea, coffee and bakery items, but not meat products. It says it's best to avoid rice. Fresh fruit and vegetables may not be allowed.

Can I take medicines to the USA from India?

Yes, for personal use. CBP asks for the original containers and a valid prescription or doctor's note in English, and gives a 90-day supply as a rule of thumb.

What size suitcase should I take to the USA?

A large trolley of 75–79 cm that's listed within 158 cm in total, for a 23 kg bag. If you have two bags on Air India, keep both together within 271 cm.

When can I travel to the USA on an F-1 visa?

Study in the States says you can enter the USA up to 30 days before the program start date shown on your Form I-20.

Do Indian plugs work in the USA?

No. The USA uses 120 V and flat-pin type A and B plugs. Take a travel adapter, and check that your chargers say 100–240 V.
