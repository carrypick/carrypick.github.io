How to choose a laptop backpack for college or office

Updated 26 Nov 2026 · 8 min read

The short answer: buy a backpack whose laptop sleeve fits your laptop's screen size, in 20–30 litres for daily use, with padded straps and a rain cover. If you fly often, check the bag's listed size too: a 30-litre backpack can usually be your one 7 kg cabin bag, but it's too big to be the small "personal item" that some airlines allow on top.

1. Match the sleeve to your laptop

Find your laptop's screen size in its product details, or measure the screen from corner to corner.

Your laptopLook for a sleeve made forNotes
13–14 inch14 or 15.6 inchA 15.6 inch sleeve fits, but the laptop may slide about
15.6 inch15.6 inch or biggerThe most common size in India. See our 15.6 inch backpack size guide
16 inch16 or 17 inchSome "15.6–16 inch" bags take both
17 inch (many gaming laptops)17 inchMany "15.6 inch" bags won't close over it

Some listings only say "padded sleeve" with no size. Two of our picks below are like that, so check the sleeve's inside size on the listing or ask the seller before you buy for a big laptop. Our laptop backpack size finder does the matching for you.

2. Choose the size in litres

SizeGood for
15–21 litresA slim office bag: laptop, charger, a few small things
22–30 litresCollege: laptop, books, lunch box and a bottle
30–35 litresLong days, gym clothes, or a 1–2 night trip
36–40 litresA week of light travel in one bag. See our 40 litre carry-on backpack guide

Not sure? Our backpack size chart shows what fits in each size.

3. Comfort, pockets and rain

  • Padding where it matters: thick, S-shaped shoulder straps and a padded back panel. Wear both straps.
  • A sleeve that stops above the bottom of the bag, so the laptop doesn't take the bump when you put the bag down.
  • Two or three compartments, so the laptop stays away from your lunch box and water bottle, plus side pockets for a bottle or umbrella.
  • A rain cover (a stretchy cover kept in a bottom pocket) protects a laptop in the monsoon far better than "water-resistant" fabric alone. Only two of our five picks list one.
  • Extras: a trolley sleeve to slide over your suitcase handle, a hidden back pocket for your phone and wallet (see our anti-theft backpack guide), and reflective strips for walking at night.
  • Warranty: branded bags usually give 1 year on zips and stitching, the parts that fail first. Two of our picks don't list a warranty.

4. Taking your laptop backpack on a flight

Indian airlines handle laptop bags in two different ways. Some let you carry a small personal bag on top of your 7 kg cabin bag. Others count everything in the same 7 kg.

AirlineLaptop bag on top of your cabin bag?
IndiGoYes: one personal article up to 3 kg, "such as ladies' purse or a small bag containing laptop". No size is given
Air IndiaYes: one personal item up to 3 kg that fits under the seat. A laptop bag can be up to 40 x 30 x 20 cm
Akasa AirYes: one personal item up to 3 kg that fits under the seat in front of you
Air India ExpressNo: on domestic flights, the 7 kg includes your laptop and personal items
SpiceJetNo: a laptop bag or ladies' purse counts inside the same 7 kg

We read these on the airlines' own pages on 26 Nov 2026. On international flights the rules vary more: Emirates lists one cabin piece only for Economy from India, Etihad doesn't confirm a separate personal item, and Qatar Airways allows a small purse or briefcase but not a separate laptop bag (as we read them on 3 Nov 2026). See all airline baggage rules.

What this means for a backpack: a 25–35 litre backpack is too big for Air India's 40 x 30 x 20 cm personal item. So on most flights it will be your cabin bag, and it must fit the cabin bag size and weigh 7 kg or less with everything in it. If you want to carry a trolley too, you need a small laptop sleeve or tote as your personal item instead.

Quick checklist before you buy

  1. Does the sleeve fit my laptop's screen size?
  2. Is it 20–30 litres for daily use?
  3. Padded straps and a padded back?
  4. A rain cover, or will I carry an umbrella?
  5. If I fly: does its listed size fit my airline's cabin bag?
  6. Is a warranty listed?

Questions

Is a laptop bag counted as hand luggage?

It depends on the airline. IndiGo, Air India and Akasa Air allow one small personal bag of up to 3 kg, such as a laptop bag, on top of your 7 kg cabin bag. On SpiceJet and Air India Express, a laptop bag counts inside the same 7 kg.

Can I carry a laptop bag and a cabin bag on IndiGo?

Yes. IndiGo allows one cabin bag up to 7 kg and 115 cm in total, plus one personal article up to 3 kg, such as a small bag with a laptop. A large laptop backpack is better treated as your cabin bag.

What is the laptop bag weight limit on a flight?

On IndiGo, Air India and Akasa Air the personal bag can weigh up to 3 kg, with the laptop and charger inside. On SpiceJet and Air India Express there's no separate limit: everything you carry on must be 7 kg in total.

Can a laptop backpack be my cabin bag?

Yes, if it's within your airline's cabin bag size and 7 kg. Most 28–30 litre laptop backpacks are listed at under 50 x 33 x 20 cm, which fits IndiGo and Air India. A 34 litre bag that is 22 cm deep is too deep for Air India's 20 cm limit.

How do I choose a laptop bag size?

Match the sleeve to your laptop's screen size first (a 15.6 inch sleeve for a 15.6 inch laptop), then choose litres for what else you carry: 15–21 litres for office, 22–30 litres for college.

Is a waterproof laptop backpack worth it in the monsoon?

A rain cover is the safer choice. Water-resistant fabric handles light rain, but water can still get in through zips and seams in a heavy shower. A cover goes over the whole bag.
