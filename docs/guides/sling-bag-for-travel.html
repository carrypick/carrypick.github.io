Sling bag for travel: which one for men and women

Updated 23 Nov 2026 · 6 min read

A sling bag keeps your passport, phone, wallet and boarding pass on your body, in front of you, where you can reach them without taking a bag off. That makes it the easiest bag for airports, crowded stations, markets and sightseeing days. Here's how to choose one, and the travel slings we picked by their listed specs.

Quick answer

  • Airports and sightseeing: a small zip sling for your passport, phone, cards and cash.
  • Crowded cities and long day trips: a chest sling with a lock, room for a power bank and a small tablet.
  • Dinner and evenings: a light crossbody bag.
  • Always choose a zip over the main pocket for travel, and wear the bag in front of you in crowds.

Anti-theft features that really help

  • Zips, not magnets or open tops. A magnetic flap opens with one pull. Of our picks, the Zouk and WildHorn bags close with magnets; the others use zips.
  • A lock or hidden zips. The BANGE sling lists a combination lock on its zips, and a hidden side pocket.
  • Pockets against your body. A back pocket that sits against you is the safest place for a passport and cash.
  • Wear it in front in crowds, metros and markets, and keep a hand on it.
  • RFID blocking stops cards from being read through the bag. It's a nice extra, not a must. See our passport holder guide.
  • Don't keep everything in one place. Carry a spare card and some cash in a different bag.

Your sling on a flight

A sling usually goes on board as your personal item, and most Indian airlines allow one small personal bag up to 3 kg alongside your cabin bag. On some fares and airlines it counts inside your 7 kg. The rules for each airline are in our handbag and sling guide and our airline baggage rules.

  • Power banks go in your cabin bag or sling, never in checked bags. See power bank rules on flights.
  • Keep it on you at security and put it in the tray last, so it reaches the other side with you.

Questions

Which sling bag is good for travel?

One with a zip over every pocket, a strap you can adjust, and room for your passport, phone, wallet and a power bank. For crowded cities, a lock or hidden pocket helps.

Are sling bags good for travel?

Yes, for the things you need often: passport, phone, cards and cash. They're easier to reach than a backpack, and you can wear them in front in crowds. They're too small for a day's clothes or a laptop.

Is a sling bag safe from pickpockets?

Safer than a back pocket or an open tote, if it has zips and you wear it in front. A lock or hidden pocket helps more. No bag is fully pickpocket-proof, so stay alert in crowds.

Can I carry a sling bag on a flight with a cabin bag?

On most Indian airlines, a small personal bag is allowed with your cabin bag, often up to 3 kg. Some airlines and fares count it inside your 7 kg, so check our airline baggage rules.

What's the difference between a sling bag and a crossbody bag?

People use the names for the same idea: a bag on one strap worn across the body. "Sling" often means a sportier chest bag, and "crossbody" a smaller bag worn at the hip.
