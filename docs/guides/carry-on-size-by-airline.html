Carry-on size by airline (worldwide): cabin bag limits chart

30 airlines. Updated 21 Nov 2026

Quick answer

  • The most common carry-on limit is about 55 x 35 x 25 cm or 56 x 36 x 23 cm (22 x 14 x 9 in), including wheels and handles.
  • US airlines (American, Delta, United) give no weight limit for the carry-on; most airlines in Asia, the Gulf and India allow 7 kg, Lufthansa 8 kg, and Air France and KLM 12 kg together with the small bag.
  • Low-cost airlines like Ryanair and easyJet include only a small under-seat bag for free; the full-size cabin bag costs extra.

How to measure a carry-on

  • Include wheels and handles. Most airlines measure the bag as it is, with wheels, handles and outside pockets.
  • Measure it packed. A soft bag stuffed full can be several cm thicker than when it's empty.
  • Know your inches. 22 x 14 x 9 inches is about 56 x 36 x 23 cm. See our luggage size chart for more.

Questions

What is the standard carry-on size?

There is no single world standard. The most common limits are 55 x 35 x 25 cm in Europe and Asia and 22 x 14 x 9 inches (56 x 36 x 23 cm) on American and United. Southwest allows a bigger 24 x 16 x 10 inch bag.

Do US airlines weigh carry-on bags?

American, Delta, United and Southwest give no weight limit for carry-on bags on the pages we read. Your bag must fit the size limit and the overhead bin.

Is a personal item free on Ryanair and easyJet?

Yes. Ryanair includes one small bag up to 40 x 30 x 20 cm, and easyJet one under-seat bag up to 45 x 36 x 20 cm and 15 kg. A larger cabin bag costs extra unless your fare includes it.

What carry-on size fits most airlines?

A bag of 55 x 35 x 20 cm weighing up to 7 kg packed fits most airlines in this chart, but not Qatar Airways (50 cm long) or Gulf Air (45 cm long). Test your own bag in our cabin bag checker.
