What to wear in Manali in December: layers, shoes and a packing list

Updated 15 Nov 2026 · 8 min read

Manali in December is cold, mostly dry and often sunny. Nights go below freezing, and snow is possible but not certain. The answer is simple: dress in layers that you can add and take off, and wear waterproof shoes with a good grip.

Planning the whole trip? Read our Manali winter guide for roads, the Atal Tunnel, New Year crowds and places to stay. Going in January? See Manali in January. For the basics of layering, see what to wear on a winter hill trip, and to choose a jacket, our winter jacket for travel guide.

Want a checklist to pack from? Use our Manali packing list for snow, or the snow trip packing list.

How cold is Manali in December?

Long-term December averages from the India Meteorological Department (IMD):

PlaceDayNight
ManaliAbout 14 °CAbout 0 °C
ShimlaAbout 12 °CAbout 4 °C
  • Nights go below freezing. Himachal Tourism says it can fall below −5 °C in December and January. IMD's lowest ever reading in Manali is −11.6 °C, in January 1996.
  • Days can feel mild in the sun, but it gets cold fast in the shade and after sunset.
  • The snow points are much colder. The Atal Tunnel is at about 3,060 m, about 1,000 m higher than Manali. Air usually gets about 6 °C colder for every 1,000 m you climb, and the wind there makes it feel colder still.

Will it snow?

Maybe. Some Decembers are snowy and some are dry:

  • December 2025 was very dry. There was no snow in Manali on New Year's Day, and the first big snowfall came only in late January 2026.
  • Christmas 2024 was snowy. Heavy snow left more than 1,000 vehicles stuck between Solang and the Atal Tunnel.
  • This season: on 13 November 2026, Rohtang Pass got about 5 inches of fresh snow, and police closed the high passes beyond it. Tourists went to the snow at the Atal Tunnel, Sissu and Gulaba instead.

So pack for cold in any case, and for snow if the forecast shows it. Even in a dry December, you can usually find snow higher up, at the Atal Tunnel or Sissu, when the road is open.

Dress in layers

LayerWhat to wearWhy
1. Next to your skinThermal top and bottomKeeps warm air close to your body
2. MiddleA sweater or fleeceHolds in most of the heat
3. OutsideA padded jacket with a hoodStops wind and snow
4. ExtrasCap, neck warmer, gloves and woollen socksEars, fingers and toes get cold first

Take a layer off before you start to sweat. Damp clothes make you cold as soon as you stop.

What to wear in town

For Mall Road, Old Manali, Hadimba Temple and Vashisht:

  • Thermals under jeans or warm trousers
  • A sweater or fleece, and your jacket
  • A cap for mornings and evenings
  • Walking shoes or boots with a good grip

On a sunny afternoon you may want only a sweater. Keep the jacket with you anyway: it gets cold quickly after 4 PM.

What to wear in the snow

For Solang, the Atal Tunnel, Sissu and Gulaba:

  • Something waterproof on top and bottom. Jeans soak up snow and stay cold and wet. Wear waterproof or quick-dry trousers over thermals, or rent a snow suit.
  • Waterproof gloves. Woollen gloves get wet as soon as you touch snow. Carry a spare pair.
  • Two pairs of woollen socks, one to wear and one dry pair in your bag.
  • Sunglasses. Snow reflects a lot of sunlight, and the glare hurts your eyes.
  • Sunscreen and lip balm with SPF. You can burn in the snow even on a cold day.

Renting: shops in Manali and near Solang rent snow suits and gumboots by the day. That's fine for one snow day. Check the size, and that they're dry, before you pay.

Which shoes to take

Shoes matter more than anything else in Manali in December. Mornings can be icy, and afternoons turn snow into slush.

  • Waterproof boots or hiking shoes with deep grip. This is the one thing worth buying before you go.
  • Not mesh sports shoes. They get wet in minutes in snow or slush.
  • Woollen socks, and a spare pair every day.
  • Slippers for the hotel.
  • Break in new boots at home for a few days, so they don't hurt on the trip.
  • Wear your boots on the bus or plane. They're heavy, and that saves space and weight in your bag.

Packing list for men

  • 2 thermal sets
  • 2–3 sweaters or fleeces
  • 1 padded jacket with a hood
  • 2 pairs of warm trousers or jeans, plus 1 waterproof or quick-dry pair for the snow
  • Woollen cap, neck warmer and 2 pairs of gloves
  • Woollen socks, one pair per day
  • Waterproof boots and slippers

Packing list for women

  • 2 thermal sets
  • 2–3 sweaters, cardigans or fleeces
  • 1 padded jacket with a hood
  • Warm trousers, jeans or thick leggings worn over thermals, plus 1 waterproof or quick-dry pair for the snow
  • A warm shawl or stole for evenings
  • Woollen cap, neck warmer and 2 pairs of gloves
  • Woollen socks, one pair per day
  • Waterproof boots and slippers

Packing list for kids

  • One more set of warm clothes than you think: kids get wet in the snow
  • Thermals, a warm jacket with a hood, a cap that covers the ears, and mittens
  • Waterproof boots
  • A small towel and a bag for wet clothes
  • Their medicines, and a moisturiser for dry skin

Kids feel cold faster than adults. Check their hands and feet often, and go indoors if they start shivering.

Skin care and health

The air in Manali is cold and dry. Pack:

  • A moisturiser and a lip balm with SPF
  • Sunscreen for your face
  • Your regular medicines, and something for a cold

Going to Shimla too?

Shimla is a little warmer than Manali at night. IMD's December averages are about 12 °C by day and 4 °C at night. But IMD's lowest ever reading in Shimla, −12.2 °C, was in December, on 13 December 1963. So pack the same layers and shoes for both places. Higher places near Shimla, such as Kufri and Narkanda, usually see snow more often than the town. For the weather, snow chances, the toy train and winter roads, read our Shimla in December guide.

Questions

What should I wear in Manali in December?

Thermals, a sweater or fleece, and a padded jacket with a hood, plus a woollen cap, neck warmer, gloves and woollen socks. Wear waterproof boots or hiking shoes with a good grip. For the snow, add waterproof trousers or rent a snow suit.

How cold is Manali in December?

IMD's long-term averages are about 14 °C in the day and about 0 °C at night. Himachal Tourism says it can fall below −5 °C. The Atal Tunnel and Sissu are much colder, because they're about 1,000 m higher.

Is one jacket enough for Manali in December?

Only with layers under it. Wear thermals and a sweater or fleece under a padded jacket. One thick jacket alone isn't enough at night, and it's too warm on a sunny afternoon.

Can I wear jeans in the snow in Manali?

You can, but jeans soak up snow and stay cold and wet. Wear thermals under them in town. For the snow at Solang or the Atal Tunnel, wear waterproof or quick-dry trousers, or rent a snow suit.

Which shoes are best for Manali in December?

Waterproof boots or hiking shoes with deep grip, and woollen socks. Avoid mesh sports shoes, which get wet quickly. Snow and slush are common, and mornings can be icy.

Will it snow in Manali in December?

It might, but it isn't certain. December 2025 was very dry, while Christmas 2024 had heavy snow. Check the forecast in the week before you go. Snow is more common higher up, at the Atal Tunnel and Sissu.

What should I wear in Shimla and Manali in December?

The same layers work for both: thermals, a sweater and a padded jacket, plus a cap, gloves and waterproof shoes. Shimla is a little warmer at night, about 4 °C against about 0 °C in Manali, on IMD's averages.
