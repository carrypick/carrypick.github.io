Lightest cabin trolley bags and lightweight luggage: empty weights compared

Updated 23 Nov 2026 · 7 min read

Airlines weigh the bag, not just your things. A hard cabin trolley usually weighs 2 to 3 kg empty, so on a 7 kg cabin allowance the bag can take more than a third of it. Here are our luggage picks sorted by the empty weight each Amazon.in listing states.

Quick answer

  • Lightest cabin trolley: 2 kg, the American Tourister Splash 55 cm. It leaves 5 kg of a 7 kg allowance.
  • Lightest that fits an airline by listed size: 2.2 kg, the American Tourister Liftoff+ Small, within Air India's 55 x 40 x 20 cm.
  • Lightest medium trolley: 2.52 kg, the Kamiliant Rock-Lite 68 cm.
  • Lightest large trolley: 3.3 kg, the soft it luggage Census 81 cm. The lightest hard one is 3.5 kg.
  • Light isn't everything. A light cabin bag that is too wide for your airline still gets sent to check-in.

What makes a bag light?

  • Material matters less than the model. The lightest cabin bag above is polycarbonate, and the lightest medium bag is polypropylene. See polycarbonate vs polypropylene.
  • Soft bags can be lighter in big sizes. Our lightest large bag is soft. See hard vs soft luggage.
  • Wheels and extras add weight, but in our picks 4-wheel bags weren't lighter than 8-wheel ones. See 4 wheel vs 8 wheel trolleys.
  • Litres aren't weight. A bigger bag tempts you to pack more, so a light large bag can still go over 15 kg.

Questions

Which is the lightest cabin trolley bag?

Of our picks, the American Tourister Splash 55 cm, listed at 2 kg. The Kamiliant Rock-Lite 55 cm is close at 2.03 kg. Neither fits IndiGo or Air India by its listed size.

How heavy should a cabin bag be when empty?

Aim for 2.5 kg or less. On a 7 kg allowance that leaves at least 4.5 kg for your things. Our light picks are 2 to 2.7 kg.

Is a lightweight trolley good for international travel?

Yes, if it has a lock you trust and a warranty that works abroad. Our light large pick, the American Tourister Liftoff+ 79 cm, lists a TSA lock and a 3-year international warranty. See trolley bag size for international travel.

Are soft bags lighter than hard ones?

Not always. Among our picks, the lightest large bag is soft, but the lightest cabin and medium bags are hard.

Does the airline count the bag's weight?

Yes. The cabin and check-in limits are for the bag and everything in it.
