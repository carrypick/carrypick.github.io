About CarryPick

CarryPick is a small team that helps shoppers in India pick good bags, luggage and watches on Amazon.in.

How we choose products

  • We read the full product details: size, capacity, material, weight and warranty.
  • We pick products that suit everyday Indian use, like college, office commutes and domestic flights.
  • We never accept money from brands to list a product.

We don't show prices because they change many times a day. The button on each pick takes you to Amazon.in, where you see the current price.
