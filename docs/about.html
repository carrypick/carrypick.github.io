About CarryPick

CarryPick is a small team that helps shoppers in India pick good bags, luggage, watches and travel essentials on Amazon.in.

How we choose products

  • We read the full product details: size, capacity, material, weight and warranty.
  • We pick products that suit everyday Indian use, like college, office commutes and domestic flights.
  • We never accept money from brands to list a product.

We don't show prices because they change many times a day. The button on each pick takes you to Amazon.in, where you see the current price.

Photos, drawings and diagrams

Each pick has a simple drawing that we make ourselves from its listed size, colour and details, drawn to scale where the listing gives a size. It is not a photo of the product, so check the real photos on Amazon.in before you buy. Our diagrams are drawn by us too.

The photos on our home, category and guide pages are free stock photos that set the scene. They are not photos of the products we pick.
