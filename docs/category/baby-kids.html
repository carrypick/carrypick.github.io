Baby & Kids Travel

Travel gear for families with a baby or young kids: a stroller that folds small, a baby carrier, a diaper bag, a fold-up changing mat, a milk powder container, a kids' cabin trolley, volume-limited headphones and a kids' travel pillow, plus art kits, puzzles, a board game and baby toys for birthdays and return gifts. We read the folded size, weight, age range and colour on each Amazon.in listing, and only repeat safety facts the listing itself states.

Before you buy

Indian airlines let a baby fly on your lap from a few days old until the day before the second birthday, with age proof such as a birth certificate. IndiGo and Akasa carry one stroller or pram per infant free, but give an infant no check-in allowance. For any question about your baby's health and flying, ask your paediatrician.
