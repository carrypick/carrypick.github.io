Packing tips

  • Cabin bag first. Keep medicines, documents, your power bank, laptop and one change of clothes in the bag that stays with you. See what you can carry on a flight.
  • Heavy things at the bottom. Put shoes and heavy items near the wheels so the trolley doesn't tip over.
  • Roll T-shirts, fold shirts. Rolling saves space; packing cubes keep everything in order.
  • Weigh it at home. Check your airline's limit on our baggage rules page, or the train luggage rules for rail trips.
  • Pick the right bag size. Not sure? Read what size luggage you need.
