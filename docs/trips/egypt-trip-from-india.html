Egypt trip from India: the e-visa, direct flights, a 7-day plan with a Nile cruise and the best time to go

Updated 22 Nov 2026 · 12 min read

Egypt at a glance

e-Visa feeUS$30 single entry, US$65 multiple entry
ApplyAt least 7 days before you leave

Egypt is the trip of a lifetime for many Indians: the pyramids and the Sphinx at Giza, the treasures of the Grand Egyptian Museum, a cruise on the Nile between Aswan and Luxor, and the temples and royal tombs of Luxor. It's about 4,400 km from Delhi, with direct flights to Cairo, and Indians can apply for an e-visa online. This guide covers the visa, flights, a 7-day plan with a Nile cruise, the weather by month, getting around and food for vegetarians.

Egypt visa for Indians

Indians can get an Egypt e-visa online: India is on the list of countries whose nationals "may be issued with an e-Visa upon application", says Egypt's official e-Visa portal (FAQ, read 22 Nov 2026).

  • Apply only on the official portal, visa2egypt.gov.eg. Many other websites look official and charge extra.
  • e-Visa fee: US$30 for a single-entry tourist visa, or US$65 for multiple entries, says the portal. You pay online by credit or debit card.
  • Apply at least 7 days before you leave, says the portal. A link to download your e-visa is emailed to you when it's processed. The portal doesn't give a fixed processing time.
  • Your passport must be valid for at least 6 months from the day you arrive.
  • Carry: a printout of the e-visa, your travel plan, and your hotel bookings, says the portal's list of requirements.
  • Entry is still the officer's decision. The portal says Egypt's port authorities can refuse entry to an e-visa holder without giving a reason.

Visa on arrival in Egypt for Indians with a US, UK or Schengen visa?

Many people search for this. We couldn't find an official page that confirms a visa on arrival for Indians, with or without a US, UK or Schengen visa, when we checked on 22 Nov 2026. The official portal lists only the e-visa. To be safe, get the e-visa before you fly, or check with the Embassy of Egypt in New Delhi.

We couldn't confirm the e-visa's validity or how many days you can stay on it on the official portal. Check them when you apply.

Flights from India to Egypt

  • There are direct flights from India to Cairo (CAI). EgyptAir flies from Delhi and Mumbai, says Cairo Airport's airline list (Wikipedia, read 22 Nov 2026).
  • From other Indian cities, fly one-stop via Delhi or Mumbai, or through the Gulf: Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Doha, Sharjah or Bahrain.
  • For Luxor and Aswan, take a domestic flight from Cairo, or the overnight train.
  • Air India doesn't fly to Egypt, so we don't show it below.
  • Time difference: India is 3 hours 30 minutes ahead of Egypt in winter, and 2 hours 30 minutes ahead from the last Friday of April to the last Thursday of October, when Egypt uses summer time.
  • Baggage: check the limits by airline in the international baggage rules and the carry-on size by airline guide.
  • Fares go up for Christmas, New Year and Indian school holidays. Book early.

A 7-day Egypt itinerary: Cairo, Aswan, a Nile cruise and Luxor

Most first trips from India spend a week: 2 or 3 days in Cairo and Giza, then 3 or 4 nights on the Nile between Aswan and Luxor. This plan uses one domestic flight each way.

DayPlanEvening
1Land in Cairo and restA walk by the Nile
2The pyramids of Giza and the Great Sphinx, then the Grand Egyptian Museum near the pyramidsThe pyramids' sound and light show
3Old Cairo: the Citadel, the mosque of Muhammad Ali and Islamic Cairo. Fly to Aswan in the eveningBoard the Nile cruise in Aswan, or a hotel
4Aswan: Philae Temple by boat, the High Dam and a felucca sail on the Nile. Optional early-morning trip to Abu SimbelOn board
5Sail north: the temples of Kom Ombo and EdfuOn board
6Luxor: the Valley of the Kings, the Temple of Hatshepsut, then Karnak TempleLuxor Temple lit up at night
7Fly back to Cairo for your flight home
  • Nile cruises usually run between Aswan and Luxor in either direction, for 3 or 4 nights, with guided visits to the temples on the way.
  • Ten days? Add a beach break on the Red Sea at Hurghada or Sharm El Sheikh, or Alexandria on the Mediterranean.
  • Start early. The big sites are busiest and hottest at midday, especially in Luxor.
  • Mosques: take off your shoes, cover your shoulders and knees, and women should cover their hair.

Egypt weather by month, and the best time to go

Long-term averages (1981–2010) from the Egyptian Meteorological Authority, published by the World Meteorological Organization:

  • October to April is best: warm, sunny days in Cairo and comfortable days in Luxor and Aswan. December and January are the busiest months.
  • It almost never rains. Cairo gets about 5 mm of rain a month in winter, and Luxor almost none.
  • Winter nights are cool, about 6 to 8 °C in Luxor in December and January, and colder in the desert. Carry a warm layer for early starts.
  • Summer is very hot, above 40 °C by day in Luxor from June to August. If you go then, plan sightseeing for early morning.

Getting around Egypt

  • Between Cairo and Aswan or Luxor, fly (about an hour) or take the overnight sleeper train.
  • Between Aswan and Luxor, take a Nile cruise, or a road transfer.
  • In Cairo, traffic is heavy. Use ride-hailing apps or taxis, and agree the fare first if there's no meter. The Cairo Metro is cheap and avoids the traffic.
  • At the sights, you'll be offered camel and horse rides and guides. Agree the price before you start.
  • Many sites charge extra for cameras or for some tombs. Check at the ticket office.
  • Learn a few words. Arabic is spoken everywhere; English is common in hotels and tourist areas. "Shukran" means thank you.

Food for Indian travellers: veg and Jain tips

Egyptian food has good vegetarian dishes, many of them based on beans, lentils and bread:

  • Koshari: Egypt's national dish of rice, lentils, pasta and chickpeas with a spicy tomato sauce and fried onions. Usually vegetarian.
  • Ful medames: slow-cooked fava beans, eaten at breakfast with bread.
  • Ta'meya: Egyptian falafel, made with fava beans.
  • Mezze: baba ghanoush, hummus, tahini and salads with flatbread.
  • Feteer: a flaky layered pastry, sweet or with cheese.
  • Ask about stock. Molokhia (a green soup) and some rice dishes are often cooked with chicken or meat stock.
  • Indian restaurants: Cairo has Indian restaurants, and many Nile cruises serve Indian dishes for Indian groups. Ask your tour company.
  • Jain travellers: most dishes use onion and garlic. Carry ready-to-eat food or khakhra, and check what you can take in our can I carry it on a flight? list.

Phone, SIM and eSIM in Egypt

You have three ways to stay online in Egypt:

  • Roaming on your own SIM: the easiest, but often the costliest. Check your mobile company's international pack before you fly.
  • A local SIM card: sold at Cairo airport and in phone shops. Shops ask for your passport to register it.
  • An eSIM: a data plan you buy online and install on your phone before you go, with no plastic card. Your phone must support eSIM and must not be locked to one network. On an iPhone, look in Settings > Mobile Service (or Cellular). On Android, look in Settings > SIM or Connections.

Install the eSIM at home over Wi-Fi and switch it on when you land. Most travel eSIMs give data only, with no local number, so keep your home SIM on for calls and bank OTP texts (roaming charges may apply).

In Cairo, stay near Giza for pyramid views and the Grand Egyptian Museum, or in Downtown and Zamalek by the Nile for the old city and restaurants. Money and rules to know

  • The currency is the Egyptian pound (E£, EGP). Cards work in hotels, malls and big restaurants. Carry cash for taxis, markets, tips and small shops.
  • Tipping (baksheesh) is expected for guides, drivers, hotel and cruise staff, and toilet attendants. Keep small notes ready.
  • Cash from India: RBI lets you buy up to US$3,000 in foreign currency notes per trip, and you can carry up to ₹25,000 in Indian notes. Put the rest on a forex card. See how much cash you can carry abroad.
  • Bargaining is normal in markets like Khan el-Khalili, not in shops with marked prices.
  • Carry your passport or a copy.
  • Photography: don't take photos of military sites, police or bridges, and ask before photographing people.

What to wear in Egypt

  • Dress modestly, especially outside resorts: cover your shoulders and knees. Women should carry a scarf for mosques.
  • Winter (December to February): light clothes for the day and a sweater or light jacket for evenings, early starts and the cruise deck.
  • Summer: loose, light cotton clothes, a sun hat, sunglasses and sunscreen.
  • Comfortable closed shoes for sand, stone steps and long walks around the temples.

Help in an emergency

  • Emergency numbers in Egypt: police 122, ambulance 123, tourist police 126.
  • Embassy of India, Cairo: contact details are on its website.
  • Keep a photo of your passport, your e-visa and your insurance papers on your phone, and save your hotel or cruise boat's details.

Questions

Do Indians need a visa for Egypt?

Yes. Indians can apply for an Egypt e-visa online on the official portal, visa2egypt.gov.eg, at least 7 days before they leave.

What is the Egypt visa fee for Indians?

US$30 for a single-entry tourist e-visa, or US$65 for multiple entries, says Egypt's official e-Visa portal. You pay online by card.

Can Indians get a visa on arrival in Egypt with a US, UK or Schengen visa?

We couldn't confirm it on an official page when we checked. Egypt's official portal lists only the e-visa for Indians. Get the e-visa before you fly, or check with the Embassy of Egypt in New Delhi.

How long does the Egypt e-visa take?

The portal doesn't give a fixed time. It says to apply at least 7 days before you leave, and emails you a link when the visa is processed.

Are there direct flights from India to Egypt?

Yes. EgyptAir flies direct from Delhi and Mumbai to Cairo. From other cities, fly one-stop via Delhi, Mumbai or the Gulf.

How many days are enough for Egypt?

Seven days is enough for a first trip: 2 or 3 days in Cairo and Giza and a 3 or 4 night Nile cruise between Aswan and Luxor. With 10 days, add the Red Sea or Alexandria.

What is the best time to visit Egypt from India?

October to April. Cairo is about 20 °C by day in December, and Luxor about 24 °C, on the Egyptian Meteorological Authority's averages. Summer is very hot in Luxor and Aswan.

Is Egypt good for vegetarians?

Yes. Koshari, ful medames, ta'meya, hummus and baba ghanoush are vegetarian, but ask whether soups and rice use meat stock. Cairo has Indian restaurants.

What is the time difference between India and Egypt?

India is 3 hours 30 minutes ahead of Egypt in winter, and 2 hours 30 minutes ahead during Egypt's summer time, from late April to late October.
