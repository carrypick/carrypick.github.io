Coorg in December and January: weather, a 2–3 day plan and what to wear

Updated 22 Nov 2026 · 10 min read

Coorg at a glance

Weather in DecemberAbout 25 °C by day and 13 °C at night (IMD averages for Madikeri)
JanuaryDrier: about 26 °C and 12 °C
SnowNo. Expect misty mornings and cool nights
HeightMadikeri is about 1,170 m
How long2 days for Madikeri and Kushalnagar, 3 with Talacauvery or Tadiandamol
Get thereNo airport or railway in Kodagu: fly to Mysuru (about 121 km), Kannur (about 120 km) or Mangaluru (about 137 km); Bangalore is about 257 km by road
Emergency112, police 100, ambulance 108

Coorg (Kodagu) in December is cool, dry and green. Days are pleasant, nights are cool, and the mornings are often misty over the coffee estates. It doesn't snow. This guide covers the weather, a simple 2-day and 3-day plan, the trip from Bangalore, December end and New Year, and what to wear.

Coorg weather in December and January

Long-term averages for Madikeri, Coorg's main town, from the India Meteorological Department (IMD, 1981–2010):

MonthDayNightRain
DecemberAbout 25 °CAbout 13 °CAbout 20 mm
JanuaryAbout 26 °CAbout 12 °CAbout 2 mm
FebruaryAbout 28 °CAbout 13 °CAbout 3 mm
  • December is almost dry. It rains on about one day in an average December, and January is drier still.
  • Nights are cool, not freezing. The lowest readings on record are 5.5 °C in December and 4.8 °C in January.
  • Mornings are misty. Karnataka Tourism calls Coorg the land of "coffee, clouds and calm". Mist often fills the valleys at dawn and clears by mid-morning.
  • Coorg is not very high. Madikeri is about 1,170 m above sea level, lower than Ooty or Munnar, so it is milder than both.

Does it snow in Coorg?

  • No, Coorg doesn't get snow. The coldest nights on record are about 5 °C, far above freezing.
  • What you get instead is mist and cool air, especially at dawn at Raja's Seat and on Mandalpatti.
  • Want frost? Ooty and Munnar are higher and colder, and see ground frost on the coldest mornings. See Ooty in December.
  • Want snow? Choose Kashmir, Manali or Shimla.

Coorg trip plan: 2 days

Coorg's sights are spread out, so hire a taxi for the day or drive yourself. Start early, when the mist is still in the valleys.

Day 1: Madikeri

  1. Abbey Falls, about 8 km from Madikeri, in the morning. Karnataka Tourism says the falls are best from July to December, so there's still water in December. Entering the water is not allowed.
  2. Omkareshwara Temple, about 1 km from the Madikeri bus stand. It was built in 1820 in an Indo-Saracenic style, with a dome and minarets.
  3. Madikeri Fort, in the town centre.
  4. Raja's Seat at sunset, about 1 km west of the bus stand, for the view over the hills and valleys.

Day 2: Kushalnagar side

  1. Dubare Elephant Camp, about 38 km from Madikeri by road, in the morning. It was once a training camp for the Mysore Dasara elephants and is run by Jungle Lodges & Resorts. Go early: elephant activities happen in the morning. Check timings with Jungle Lodges.
  2. Namdroling Monastery (the Golden Temple) at Bylakuppe, about 35 km from Madikeri and 5 km from Kushalnagar. Karnataka Tourism lists it as open from 9 AM to 6 PM.
  3. Drive back or carry on towards Mysuru and Bangalore.

Coorg trip plan: 3 days

Add one of these:

  • Talacauvery and Bhagamandala. Talacauvery, the source of the Kaveri river, is about 46 km from Madikeri. Bhagamandala, with its triveni sangama (meeting of three rivers), is on the way, about 39 km from Madikeri.
  • Mandalpatti, about 18 km from Madikeri, for views over the Pushpagiri hills. The forest department recommends 4×4 jeeps for the last stretch.
  • Tadiandamol, Coorg's highest peak at 1,748 m. Karnataka Tourism calls it an easy trek of about 7 km each way, starting at Nalaknad Palace, 43 km from Madikeri. Camping isn't allowed, and you must be back before sunset.
  • Iruppu Falls in the Brahmagiri hills, in south Coorg, which also fits with a trip to Nagarahole.

Driving from Bangalore? The road takes most of a morning each way, so a 2-day trip leaves only one full day in Coorg. Plan 3 days, or 2 nights, if you can.

Getting to Coorg

From the Kodagu district website:

  • By air: there's no airport in Kodagu. The nearest are Mysuru (about 121 km from Madikeri), Kannur (about 120 km) and Mangaluru (about 137 km). Bangalore airport is further, but has the most flights.
  • By train: there's no railway station in Kodagu either. The nearest railheads are Mysuru, Hassan, Mangaluru, Thalassery and Kannur.
  • From Bangalore: Kodagu is about 257 km by road, via Mysuru and Kushalnagar.
  • By bus: Karnataka's state buses (KSRTC) run from Bangalore and Mysuru to Madikeri, including overnight services. Book on the official site, ksrtc.in.

Coorg trip from Bangalore: by car, bus or train

  • By car: the usual road goes from Bangalore to Mysuru, then west through Hunsur or Periyapatna to Kushalnagar and up to Madikeri. Leave early to beat the city traffic, and stop at Bylakuppe (the Golden Temple) on the way.
  • By bus: KSRTC runs day and night buses from Bangalore to Madikeri, many via Mysuru. Book on ksrtc.in.
  • By train: no train goes to Coorg. Take a train from Bangalore to Mysuru, then a bus or taxi (Mysuru is about 121 km from Madikeri).
  • Coming back: don't drive the ghat roads after dark if you can avoid it, and watch for wild elephants near forest stretches.

Trekking rules

  • Forest department treks in Kodagu, such as Brahmagiri and Talakaveri–Nishanimotte, are booked on the official Aranya Vihaara site. Book at least one day and at most 30 days ahead, and carry the original ID you booked with.
  • Tadiandamol: Karnataka Tourism says camping isn't allowed and you must come back before sunset.
  • Forest helpline: 1926.

Things to do in winter

  • Abbey Falls, still flowing in December.
  • Raja's Seat at sunset and Madikeri Fort and Omkareshwara Temple in town.
  • Dubare Elephant Camp on the Kaveri, near Kushalnagar.
  • Namdroling Monastery (the Golden Temple) at Bylakuppe, one of the largest Tibetan settlements in India.
  • Talacauvery and Bhagamandala for the source of the Kaveri.
  • Mandalpatti and Tadiandamol for hill views and an easy trek.
  • Coffee estates: many homestays are on working coffee estates. Winter is the coffee-picking season, so ask your host if you can see the harvest.
  • Barapole river rafting, about 78 km from Madikeri. Check with the operator if it's running.

Coorg in December end: Christmas and New Year

  • Book early. Coorg is a favourite long weekend from Bangalore, Mysuru and Kerala.
  • This year Christmas is on a Friday, and so is New Year's Day (1 January 2027), so both are long weekends. Expect full homestays and traffic on the Mysuru road.
  • Huttari (Puthari), Kodagu's harvest festival, falls in late November or December. Its date is fixed each year by tradition, so check locally if you'd like to see it.
  • Start early for Abbey Falls, Raja's Seat and Dubare: they get crowded by late morning in the holidays.

What to wear in Coorg in December

What should I wear? Try the what-to-wear helper for day and night temperatures and an outfit list by month.

Coorg's days are warm in the sun, and nights and early mornings are cool.

  • Layers: cotton tops for the day, and a sweater, fleece or light jacket for evenings and early mornings.
  • For dawn at Raja's Seat, Mandalpatti or a trek: a warmer jacket and a cap.
  • For ladies: jeans or leggings with a kurta or top, and a cardigan or shawl for the evening. Cover your shoulders at temples and the monastery.
  • Shoes: comfortable walking shoes with a good grip for the falls, estates and treks.
  • Mosquito repellent for evenings on the estates, plus sunscreen and sunglasses.

Health and safety

  • Never sleep with a heater or coal fire in a closed room. It gives off carbon monoxide, which can kill without warning.
  • Stay out of the water at waterfalls. Rocks are slippery, and entering the water at Abbey Falls is not allowed.
  • Keep away from wild elephants on forest roads, especially near Dubare and Nagarahole.

For foreign visitors

  • Visa: apply for the e-Tourist Visa only at the official site, indianvisaonline.gov.in.

Emergency numbers and help

From the Kodagu district website:

  • Police: 100
  • Fire: 101
  • Ambulance: 108
  • Rescue and relief: 1070
  • Department of Tourism, Madikeri: 08272-200519
  • Forest helpline: 1926
  • Ministry of Tourism helpline, 24 hours, many languages: 1363 or 1800-11-1363

Questions

What is the weather in Coorg in December?

Cool and dry. IMD's long-term averages for Madikeri are about 25 °C by day and 13 °C at night, with rain on only about one day in the month. Mornings are often misty.

What is the temperature in Coorg in December?

About 25 °C in the day and 13 °C at night (IMD averages, 1981–2010). The coldest December night on record was 5.5 °C. January is a little cooler at night.

Does it snow in Coorg in December?

No. Coorg never gets snow: the coldest nights on record are about 5 °C. You'll see mist over the hills instead. For snow, go to Kashmir, Manali or Shimla.

Is Coorg good in December end?

Yes. The weather is cool and dry, and Abbey Falls still has water. It's also one of the busiest times, with Christmas and New Year long weekends, so book your stay early and start sightseeing early.

How many days are enough for Coorg?

Two days are enough for Madikeri (Abbey Falls, Raja's Seat, the fort and temple) and the Kushalnagar side (Dubare and the Golden Temple). Add a third for Talacauvery, Mandalpatti or the Tadiandamol trek.

How do I go from Bangalore to Coorg?

By road, about 257 km via Mysuru and Kushalnagar. KSRTC buses run day and night. There's no train to Coorg: take a train to Mysuru, then a bus or taxi.

Which is the nearest airport to Coorg?

There's no airport in Kodagu. Mysuru (about 121 km from Madikeri), Kannur (about 120 km) and Mangaluru (about 137 km) are the closest. Many people fly to Bangalore, which has more flights, and drive about 257 km.

What should I wear in Coorg in December?

Light cotton for the day, and a sweater or light jacket for the cool evenings and misty mornings. Wear good walking shoes for the falls and treks.

Coorg or Ooty in December?

Coorg is milder, with coffee estates, waterfalls and homestays. Ooty is higher and colder, with frosty mornings and the toy train. See Ooty in December.
