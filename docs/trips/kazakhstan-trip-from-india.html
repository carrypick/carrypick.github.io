Kazakhstan trip from India: the visa-free rule, a 5-day Almaty plan and Almaty in winter

Updated 21 Nov 2026 · 14 min read

Direct flightsFrom Delhi and Mumbai to Almaty

Kazakhstan has become a favourite short trip abroad for Indians: there are direct flights from Delhi and Mumbai to Almaty, and Indians don't need a visa for a stay of up to 14 days. Almaty sits right below snowy mountains, so in one trip you get a modern city, a ski resort in the mountains just above the city, lakes and canyons. This guide covers the visa rule, flights, a 5-day Almaty plan, Almaty in winter, snow and skiing, getting around and food for vegetarians.

Kazakhstan visa for Indians

Indians don't need a visa for a short trip to Kazakhstan, say the Embassy of Kazakhstan in New Delhi and the Embassy of India in Astana (both read 21 Nov 2026).

  • Up to 14 days at a time, without a visa. Indian citizens can stay up to 14 days from the day they enter, and no more than 42 days in any 180 days. It covers tourist, business and private trips. The rule started in July 2022 (Government Decree No. 464).
  • 14 days is short, so count your dates. The day you land counts. Book your return flight inside the 14 days.
  • Staying longer? Apply for a visa before you go. The Kazakh embassy in New Delhi says Indians can apply for some single-entry e-visas on Kazakhstan's official migration portal, and lists a consular fee of US$60 for a single-entry tourist visa (page of 21 May 2025). Many visa types need an invitation, and processing takes about 5 working days. Ask the embassy before you book.
  • Registration: your hotel tells the migration police you are staying there. If you stay with friends or in a private flat, your host must do this within 3 working days, says Kazakh Invest, the government's investment agency.
  • Entry is up to the border officer. Carry your return ticket and hotel bookings, printed or on your phone.
  • Your passport should have at least 6 months left and a couple of blank pages. Most airlines check this before you board.

Flights from India to Kazakhstan

  • IndiGo also started Delhi–Almaty in September 2023, says IndiGo, but the airport's list didn't show it when we checked. Search your dates to see what flies.
  • No direct flights to Astana from India were listed. Fly via Almaty, or via Dubai, Sharjah or Istanbul.
  • Flights can change at short notice. The Embassy of India in Astana says regional airspace restrictions have cancelled, delayed or rerouted some direct flights between India and Kazakhstan. If you get stuck past your 14 days, contact the embassy and the nearest immigration office: a delay beyond your control can be excused with proof.
  • India is 30 minutes ahead of Kazakhstan. The whole country has been on one time zone, UTC+5, since 1 March 2024. A flight that lands at 7 AM in Almaty lands at 7:30 AM India time.
  • Baggage: carrying ski clothes or a heavy jacket? Wear the jacket on the plane. See the limits by airline in the international baggage rules.
  • Fares go up for Christmas, New Year and Indian school holidays. Book early for December and May–June.

A 5-day Almaty itinerary

Most Indians spend 4 to 6 days in and around Almaty. This plan works all year; the winter notes are below the table.

DayPlanEvening
1Land in Almaty. Walk Panfilov Park and see the wooden Zenkov (Ascension) Cathedral, then the Green Bazaar for dried fruit and nutsArbat (Zhibek Zholy) street walk
2Medeu, then the gondola up to Shymbulak mountain resort: snow and skiing in winter, mountain walks in summerRest; the mountain air is cold
3Big Almaty Lake in the mountains (best from June to October) or, in winter, a day at Shymbulak's ski schoolA mall: Dostyk Plaza or Mega Alma-Ata
4A full-day trip by road to Charyn Canyon (add the Kolsai lakes on a two-day trip in summer)Back late; an easy dinner
5The cable car up Kok Tobe hill for city views, a last bit of shopping, then fly home
  • Big Almaty Lake is in a national park in the mountains. In winter the road can be icy or closed and the lake is frozen, so go with a tour and ask about access first.
  • The Medeu skating rink is closed for rebuilding. News reports quoting Almaty's city government say work began in autumn 2025 and should finish in late 2027. You can still pass Medeu on the way up to Shymbulak.
  • Charyn Canyon is a long drive east of Almaty, so leave early. In winter the days are short and the canyon floor can be icy.
  • Only 3 or 4 days? Skip Charyn Canyon. Almaty city, Shymbulak and Kok Tobe fit in 3 days.
  • Adding Astana? The capital is a short domestic flight from Almaty, with Bayterek tower, the Khan Shatyr tent mall and the Hazrat Sultan mosque. It is far colder than Almaty in winter.

  • December to February is the snow season. Almaty is below freezing most nights, and the mountains above it are colder still. Bring proper winter clothes: see what to wear below.
  • Astana is much colder than Almaty, often −20 °C at night in January, with strong wind. Most winter trips from India stay in Almaty.
  • June to September is best for lakes, canyons and hikes. July is hot in the city but cool in the mountains.
  • April and May are rainy, with fresh green hills and fewer tourists.
  • In the mountains, weather changes fast. Check the forecast before a mountain day.

Snow and skiing near Almaty

  • Shymbulak is Almaty's ski resort, high above the city. You reach it by road to Medeu and then the gondola, or by road. It has a ski school and gentle beginner slopes, and you can rent skis, boots and clothes there.
  • The 2026–27 season opened on 19 November 2026 with only beginner runs and the lower slopes open, said local news reports. The upper runs open once there is enough snow. We couldn't read Shymbulak's own website, so check it for the lift hours and opening dates before you go.
  • Never skied before? Book a lesson with an instructor. Wear sunglasses or goggles and sunscreen: the sun on the snow is strong.
  • Not skiing? Ride the gondola for the views, try snow tubing or just play in the snow.

Getting around Almaty

  • Taxis through ride-hailing apps are the easiest way around the city and to the airport, and the app shows the price first.
  • Almaty has one metro line and city buses. They work with the Onay transport card, which you top up at machines or in the Onay app.
  • For mountain trips, take a taxi, a tour or a city bus to Medeu, then the gondola to Shymbulak.
  • Charyn Canyon and the Kolsai lakes are far from the city, so book a tour or a car with a driver.
  • Learn a few words of Russian. Russian and Kazakh are spoken everywhere; English is less common outside hotels and big restaurants. A translation app helps.

Food for Indian travellers: veg and Jain tips

Kazakh food is built around meat: beshbarmak (meat with noodles), plov (rice with meat), shashlik kebabs and horse meat dishes. Vegetarians should plan ahead:

  • Indian restaurants are easy to find in Almaty, and many serve vegetarian thalis, paneer and dal. Book a hotel near the city centre to be close to them.
  • Safe choices elsewhere: salads, baursak (fried dough), bread, vegetable soups (ask that they're made without meat stock), Korean-style vegetable salads, cheese and fresh fruit.
  • Useful Russian words: "bez myasa" (without meat) and "ya vegetarianets" (I'm vegetarian).
  • The Green Bazaar sells dried fruit, nuts, honey, fresh fruit and bread, good for day trips.
  • Most food is halal, and pork is less common than in many countries, but beef, mutton and horse meat are everywhere.
  • Jain travellers should carry ready-to-eat food or khakhra. Check what you can take in our can I carry it on a flight? list.

Phone, SIM and eSIM in Kazakhstan

You have three ways to stay online in Kazakhstan:

  • Roaming on your own SIM: the easiest, but often the costliest. Check your mobile company's international pack before you fly.
  • A local SIM card: sold at the airport and in phone shops. Shops usually ask for your passport to register it.
  • An eSIM: a data plan you buy online and install on your phone before you go, with no plastic card. Your phone must support eSIM and must not be locked to one network. On an iPhone, look in Settings > Mobile Service (or Cellular). On Android, look in Settings > SIM or Connections.

Install the eSIM at home over Wi-Fi and switch it on when you land. Most travel eSIMs give data only, with no local number, so keep your home SIM on for calls and bank OTP texts (roaming charges may apply).

Money and rules to know

  • The currency is the Kazakh tenge (₸, KZT). Cards work in hotels, malls and most restaurants. Carry some cash for bazaars and small shops.
  • Cash from India: RBI lets you buy up to US$3,000 in foreign currency notes per trip, and you can carry up to ₹25,000 in Indian notes. Put the rest on a forex card. See how much cash you can carry abroad.
  • Declare cash in Kazakhstan in writing if you carry more than US$10,000 (or the same in other currencies), under Eurasian Economic Union customs rules, says Kazakhstan's eGov portal.
  • Medicines: carry them in the original packs with your prescription.
  • Keep your passport with you. Police can ask to see it.

What to wear in Almaty in winter

  • A warm padded jacket, thermal innerwear, a sweater or fleece, gloves, a woolly cap and a scarf. Nights are below freezing from November to March.
  • Waterproof boots with a good grip for icy pavements and snow.
  • Snow trousers or ski wear for Shymbulak. You can rent ski clothes at the resort.
  • Sunglasses and sunscreen for the mountains.
  • In summer, light clothes for the city and a warm layer for the mountains.

Help in an emergency

  • Embassy of India, Astana, and its Representative Office in Almaty: contact details are on the embassy's contact page.
  • Keep a photo of your passport and your travel insurance papers on your phone, and save your hotel's address in Russian.

Questions

Do Indians need a visa for Kazakhstan?

Not for a short trip. Indian citizens can stay in Kazakhstan without a visa for up to 14 days at a time, and no more than 42 days in any 180 days, say the Embassy of Kazakhstan in New Delhi and the Embassy of India in Astana. For a longer stay, apply for a visa before you go.

Is Kazakhstan visa free for Indians in 2026?

Yes, for up to 14 days at a time. The rule has been in force since July 2022, and both embassies' pages still said so on 21 November 2026. It covers tourist, business and private trips.

Can Indians get a Kazakhstan visa on arrival?

You don't need one for up to 14 days. For a longer stay, the Kazakh embassy in New Delhi says to apply before you travel, as an e-visa on the official migration portal or at the embassy.

How many days are enough for Kazakhstan?

Four to six days is enough for Almaty: a day in the city, a day at Shymbulak and Medeu, a day trip to Charyn Canyon or Big Almaty Lake, and Kok Tobe. Remember that a visa-free stay is 14 days at most.

Is Almaty good in winter?

Yes, if you want snow. Almaty averages about 0 °C by day and −9 °C at night in December, and the Shymbulak ski resort is in the mountains just above the city. Big Almaty Lake is frozen and harder to reach, and the Medeu skating rink is closed for rebuilding.

What is the weather in Almaty in December?

Cold: about 0 °C by day and −9 °C at night, with snow some days, on Kazhydromet averages. January is a little colder. Pack thermals, a padded jacket and boots with a good grip.

Which Indian cities have direct flights to Kazakhstan?

Delhi and Mumbai have direct flights to Almaty, with Air Astana and IndiGo. There were no direct flights from India to Astana when we checked. Routes change, so search your dates.

Is Kazakhstan good for vegetarians?

It takes some planning. Kazakh food is mostly meat, but Almaty has many Indian restaurants with vegetarian food, and you can find salads, bread, baursak and fruit everywhere. Jain travellers should carry some food.

What is the time difference between India and Kazakhstan?

India is 30 minutes ahead of Kazakhstan. Since 1 March 2024 all of Kazakhstan uses one time zone, UTC+5.
