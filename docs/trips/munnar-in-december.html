Munnar in December and January: weather, frost and a 2–3 day plan

Updated 22 Nov 2026 · 12 min read

SnowNo. On the coldest mornings you may see frost on the grass
HeightAbout 1,600 m
How long2 days for the sights, 3 with Top Station or Chinnar
EravikulamOpen in December and January, entry 8 AM to 2 PM; book online
Get thereFly to Kochi (COK), about 108 km away, then 3–4.5 hours by road
Emergency112

Munnar in December is sunny by day and cold at night. The monsoon is over, the tea hills are green, and on the coldest mornings at the end of December and in January, frost forms on the grass. It doesn't snow. This guide covers how cold it gets, a simple 2-day and 3-day plan, Eravikulam National Park, the road from Kochi, the plastic rules and what to wear.

Munnar weather in December and January

Munnar has no IMD weather station with published long-term averages, so here is what official and news reports say about recent winters:

DecemberJanuary
DaysMild and sunnyMild and sunny, about 23 °C (Jan 2026)
NightsCold; −1 °C on some estates in late Dec 2025Mostly 2–9 °C (Jan 2026)
RainA few showers early in the monthAlmost dry
  • The coldest spots are the tea estates outside town. Kerala Tourism reported that Munnar dipped to 0 °C in early 2025, with the lowest reading at Chundavurrai estate. In December 2025, news reports said Sevenmallay and Chenduvarai fell to −1 °C.
  • Late December and January are the coldest. That is when frost is most likely. Early December is a little warmer and can still bring a shower.
  • Days are pleasant, nights are cold. The sun is warm at midday, but it gets cold quickly after sunset. Dress in layers.
  • Munnar is high. The town is about 1,600 m above sea level, according to Kerala Tourism. Mattupetty and Top Station are about 1,700 m.

These estate readings are not IMD figures, and town nights are usually warmer than the estates. Check a forecast in the week you travel.

Does it snow in Munnar?

  • No, Munnar doesn't get snow. What you may see on the coldest mornings is ground frost: white ice on the grass and on low tea bushes. Some news reports call it "snowfall", but it is frost.
  • Frost comes on clear, still nights, mostly from late December to January. It melts soon after sunrise, so go out early.
  • The frost spots are tea estates such as Sevenmallay, Chenduvarai and Chundavurrai, outside the town.
  • Neelakurinji won't be in bloom. Kerala Tourism says the last big bloom was in 2018 and the next is expected in 2030, from July to October.

Want real snow? Choose Kashmir in December, Manali or Shimla.

Munnar trip plan: 2 days

Munnar's sights are spread along two main roads, so hire a taxi or a jeep for the day. Start early: Eravikulam's entry closes at 2 PM, and the roads get busy by late morning in the holidays.

Day 1: Eravikulam and the town

  1. Eravikulam National Park (Rajamala) in the morning, about 13 km from town. Book your ticket online first. Look for the Nilgiri tahr, a mountain goat found only in these hills.
  2. Tea Museum, about 2 km from town, run by the Kannan Devan tea company. See how tea is made. Check the opening hours before you go: it is usually closed on Mondays.
  3. Tea estate walks around town in the late afternoon. Stay on the paths and don't pick the leaves.

Day 2: Mattupetty, Echo Point, Kundala and Top Station

  1. Mattupetty dam and lake, about 13 km from Munnar, for the view and a boat ride.
  2. Echo Point, about 15 km from Munnar on the same road.
  3. Kundala lake and dam, further along.
  4. Top Station, about 32 km from Munnar, for the view over the Tamil Nadu side. Go before noon, as clouds often roll in after lunch. Drive back before dark.

Munnar trip plan: 3 days

Add one of these:

  • Chinnar Wildlife Sanctuary and Marayoor, on the dry rain-shadow side towards Tamil Nadu, with sandalwood forests on the way.
  • Kolukkumalai, a high tea estate on the Tamil Nadu border, famous for sunrise. It is reached by jeep only, on a rough track, so book with a licensed jeep operator.
  • Attukal waterfalls and a slower day in the tea estates.

Coming from Kochi? The drive takes 3–4.5 hours each way, so a 2-day trip leaves only one full day in Munnar. Plan 3 days, or 2 nights, if you can.

Eravikulam National Park in December

  • It's open in December and January. The park closes every year for the Nilgiri tahr calving season. Its booking site gives the closure as February to March (and elsewhere as 1 February to 1 April), so check the date if you go in late January.
  • Entry: 8 AM to 2 PM, from the park's booking site. Fees listed there are ₹200 for Indian adults and ₹500 for foreign visitors, plus camera fees. Fees can change, so check the site.
  • Book online at eravikulamnationalpark.in, with an ID proof. At Christmas and New Year, tickets for the day can run out.
  • Visitors can enter only the Rajamala tourism zone. The park's buses take you up the hill. Unauthorised trekking is punishable, so don't plan to climb Anamudi, South India's highest peak (over 2,700 m, per the Idukki district).
  • Rules: no plastic, food, alcohol or smoking inside, and no loud music. Don't feed the tahr.

Getting to Munnar

From the Idukki district website:

  • By air: Kochi (COK) is the nearest airport, about 108 km away. Madurai (about 153 km) and Coimbatore (about 160 km) are other choices from the Tamil Nadu side.
  • By train: Munnar has no railway. The nearest stations are Aluva (about 108 km) and Angamaly (about 109 km), on the main Kerala line. Ernakulam is a little further.
  • From Kochi by road: about 130 km, roughly 4.5 hours, via Kothamangalam, Neriamangalam and Adimali on NH 85. The last part climbs winding hill roads.
  • By bus: Kerala's state buses (KSRTC) connect Munnar with Kochi and other towns. Book on the official site, keralartc.com.
  • Roads close after landslides. On 20 October 2026, news reports said the Adimali–Munnar road was closed near Second Mile after a boulder fell, with traffic sent via Anachal. The Gap Road towards Devikulam has had night bans in the rains. Check the latest before you leave, and avoid driving the ghat roads at night.

Plastic rules in Munnar

  • Munnar has banned single-use plastic, news reports said in June 2026: carry bags, water bottles under 500 ml and straws, with big fines for shops and for littering. Green check posts were planned at the entry roads.
  • A tourist entry fee was proposed by the panchayat in 2026. We couldn't confirm it has started, so check locally.
  • Carry a steel bottle and a cloth bag, and take your rubbish back with you.

Things to do in winter

  • Eravikulam National Park for the Nilgiri tahr and the hills (open in December and January).
  • Tea Museum and walks in the tea estates.
  • Mattupetty, Echo Point and Kundala on the Top Station road.
  • Top Station for valley views over Tamil Nadu.
  • Frost at dawn on the tea estates on the coldest late-December and January mornings.
  • Chinnar and Marayoor for a drier, wilder side of the hills.
  • Kolukkumalai for sunrise over the hills.

Munnar in December end: Christmas and New Year

  • Book early. Munnar is one of Kerala's busiest places from Christmas to New Year, and cold weather brings even more visitors.
  • This year Christmas is on a Friday, and so is New Year's Day (1 January 2027), so both are long weekends. Expect full hotels and slow traffic on the Kochi road.
  • Start early. Roads to Mattupetty and Top Station have jammed in past New Year rushes.
  • 1 January is a dry day in Kerala, according to news reports, so bars and liquor shops are closed that day.
  • Munnar's flower show is in May, not December, so don't plan around it.

What to wear in Munnar in December

What should I wear? Try the what-to-wear helper for an outfit list by month (Munnar is listed under Kerala).

Munnar's days are mild, but nights and early mornings can be close to 0 °C on the estates.

  • Layers: a full-sleeve top or thermal, a sweater or fleece, and a warm jacket for evenings and early mornings.
  • For frost at dawn and Top Station: your warmest jacket, a woollen cap, gloves and a muffler.
  • For ladies: jeans or leggings with a kurta or top, a cardigan or jacket, and a stole.
  • For early-December showers: a light rain jacket or umbrella.
  • Shoes: walking shoes with a good grip. Estate paths are wet in the mornings.
  • Sunscreen and sunglasses: the sun is strong at this height.
  • Going on to the coast? Pack light cotton clothes too: Kochi and Alappuzha are about 30 °C by day.

Health and safety

  • Never sleep with a heater or coal fire in a closed room. It gives off carbon monoxide, which can kill without warning.
  • Drive slowly on the ghat roads, use low gear going down, and don't drive at night if you can avoid it.
  • Keep away from wild elephants and gaur on the roads around Munnar. Don't stop to feed or photograph them from close by.

For foreign visitors

  • Visa: apply for the e-Tourist Visa only at the official site, indianvisaonline.gov.in.

Emergency numbers and help

  • Emergency: 112 (connected to the police in Kerala)
  • Police: 100. Fire: 101. Ambulance: 108.
  • Kerala Tourism: 1-800-425-4747 (free, within India)
  • Idukki Collectorate: 04862-233103
  • Ministry of Tourism helpline, 24 hours, many languages: 1363 or 1800-11-1363

Questions

Does it snow in Munnar in December?

No. Munnar doesn't get snow. On the coldest mornings at the end of December and in January, frost can form on the grass and low tea bushes on the estates, and it melts soon after sunrise.

What is the temperature in Munnar in December?

Days are mild and sunny. Nights are cold, and on the tea estates they can fall to about 0 °C: in December 2025, news reports said Sevenmallay and Chenduvarai touched −1 °C. In January 2026 most nights were between 2 and 9 °C, with days around 23 °C. There are no IMD long-term averages for Munnar.

Is Munnar good in December end?

Yes, if you like cool weather: late December has the coldest, clearest mornings and the best chance of frost. It is also the busiest time of the year, so book your stay and your Eravikulam tickets early.

Is Eravikulam National Park open in December?

Yes. It closes only for the Nilgiri tahr calving season, around February and March. Entry is from 8 AM to 2 PM; book online on the park's site.

How many days are enough for Munnar?

Two days are enough for Eravikulam, the Tea Museum, Mattupetty, Echo Point and Top Station. Add a third for Chinnar and Marayoor, or Kolukkumalai at sunrise. From Kochi, plan 3 days, because the drive takes 3–4.5 hours each way.

How do I reach Munnar from Kochi?

By road: about 130 km, roughly 4.5 hours, via Kothamangalam and Adimali. Kochi airport is about 108 km from Munnar. There's no train to Munnar; the nearest stations are Aluva and Angamaly. KSRTC buses also run from Kochi.

What should I wear in Munnar in December?

Layers: a full-sleeve top, a sweater or fleece and a warm jacket, plus a woollen cap and gloves for early mornings. Carry light cotton clothes too if you're going on to Kochi or Alappuzha.

Munnar or Ooty in December?

Both are cool tea-hill towns with frosty mornings. Munnar fits well with Kochi and the Alappuzha backwaters. Ooty has the toy train and is easier from Bangalore and Coimbatore. See Ooty in December.
