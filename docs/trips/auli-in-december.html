Auli in December: snow, skiing, weather and what to wear

Updated 24 Nov 2026 · 11 min read

Auli at a glance

Weather in DecemberAbout 13 °C by day and 4 °C at night in Joshimath (IMD averages). Auli is higher and colder
SnowNot certain in December. Official pages name January to March as the snow and ski season
SkiingGMVN's 2025–26 ski courses ran from 8 January to 31 March, with no December batches
RopewayThe Joshimath–Auli ropeway has been shut since January 2023. Go up by road
HeightAbout 2,500 to 3,050 m
Get thereFly to Dehradun or take a train to Rishikesh, then a long drive via Joshimath (16 km below Auli)
Emergency112, disaster 1070, tourism helpline 1364

Auli is Uttarakhand's ski slope: open meadows and oak and deodar forest above Joshimath, with views of Nanda Devi. In December it's cold and quiet, but snow isn't certain: the official snow and ski season is January to March. This guide covers the weather, the chances of snow, skiing, the closed ropeway and the road up, a simple 3-day plan, getting there, and what to wear.

Auli weather in December and January

There's no IMD station at Auli. These are IMD's long-term averages for Joshimath, 16 km below Auli by road (1971–2000):

MonthDayNightRain or snow
DecemberAbout 13 °CAbout 4 °CAbout 27 mm
JanuaryAbout 11 °CAbout 2 °CAbout 57 mm
FebruaryAbout 12 °CAbout 3 °CAbout 94 mm
  • Auli is colder than these numbers. It's about 700 to 1,100 m higher than Joshimath, so expect nights below freezing.
  • December is the driest of the three months. Most of the winter snow falls in January and February.
  • The coldest night on record in Joshimath was −3.1 °C in December and −15.1 °C in January.
  • Sunny days feel warmer. Out of the wind, in the sun, the middle of the day can be pleasant. Mornings and evenings are very cold.

Will there be snow in Auli in December?

  • Not for sure. Uttarakhand Tourism and the Chamoli district site both name January to March as the season for snow and skiing.
  • Some Decembers have had none. News reports said Uttarakhand's mountains had almost no snow in December 2025, and Auli's slopes stayed bare into January. (A news report, not official data.)
  • Your best chance is late December to February. If snow matters most, go in January or February, or book late and watch the IMD forecast.
  • Want surer snow in December? Gulmarg is higher, and usually gets snow earlier.

Skiing in Auli

  • The slopes: Uttarakhand Tourism describes about 5 km of slopes. GMVN, the state tourism company, gives the ski area as about 2,915 to 3,049 m, with longer cross-country routes.
  • GMVN ski courses: GMVN's 2025–26 programme had 3-day and 7-day non-certificate courses and a 14-day certificate course, all between 8 January and 31 March. There were no December batches. The course includes stay, food, ski gear and lifts. Book only through GMVN's skiing page. The 2026–27 dates were not posted when we checked.
  • Gear on hire: GMVN rents skis and snowboards by the day, and shops in Auli rent snow boots and jackets.
  • The chair lift: GMVN runs a chair lift of about 800 m inside Auli. Whether it runs depends on the snow and the season, so ask at the GMVN counter (not confirmed for this winter).
  • Competitions: the National Skiing Championship and Winter Carnival began at Auli on 13 February 2026 (All India Radio).
  • Beginners: take a course or a certified instructor. Don't ski alone off the marked slope.

The Joshimath–Auli ropeway and the road up

  • The ropeway is closed. Uttarakhand Tourism's page still describes it as one of India's longest, about 4 km from Joshimath to Gorson near Auli. But it stopped in January 2023 because of the land sinking in Joshimath, and we found no official notice that it has reopened. News reports say a new ropeway is planned, with no opening date. (Not confirmed on an official page.)
  • Go up by road. Auli is 16 km by road from Joshimath. Hire a local taxi in Joshimath.
  • After snowfall, the road can close. In January 2026 police closed the Joshimath–Auli road for a while after vehicles skidded on snow, then reopened it. Tourists went up in 4×4s (news report).
  • Don't drive the hill road after dark, and leave the driving to a local driver if there's snow or ice.

Auli trip plan: 3 days

The drive from Rishikesh to Joshimath takes most of a day, so plan at least 3 days in the hills, plus travel days.

Day 1: reach Joshimath or Auli

  1. Leave Rishikesh early. The road runs along the Ganga and the Alaknanda past Devprayag, Srinagar, Rudraprayag, Karnaprayag and Chamoli.
  2. Stay in Joshimath or drive up to Auli before dark.

Day 2: Auli and Gorson Bugyal

  1. Morning on the slopes. Walk the meadows, or take a short ski lesson if there's snow.
  2. Gorson Bugyal, about 3 km from Auli, at about 3,056 m. Uttarakhand Tourism calls it a good trek for beginners, with views of Nanda Devi, Trishul and Dronagiri, and a "snow-covered magical land in winter".
  3. Chattrakund, a small lake about 1 km beyond Gorson Bugyal, if you're fit and the path is clear.

Day 3: views and back down

  1. Sunrise on Nanda Devi (7,816 m) and the other peaks from the slopes.
  2. Drive down to Joshimath and start back towards Rishikesh, or stay a night on the way at Rudraprayag or Srinagar.

Short on time? Auli is too far for a weekend from Delhi. For a 2-day trip, see weekend getaways from Delhi.

Things to do in winter

  • Skiing and snow play when there's snow, mostly from January.
  • Gorson Bugyal and Chattrakund for a short trek with big mountain views.
  • Nanda Devi views from the open slopes, best in the clear mornings.
  • Kuari Pass: Uttarakhand Tourism says the Gorson trail joins the Kuari Pass trek. GMVN sells its Kuari Pass trek only from April to October; winter treks are run by private operators and need snow gear and a guide.

Getting to Auli

From Uttarakhand Tourism, GMVN and the Chamoli district site:

  • By air: the nearest airport is Jolly Grant, Dehradun. Official pages give the distance to Auli as about 273 to 286 km.
  • By train: the nearest railheads are Rishikesh (about 250 km, says the Chamoli district site) and Haridwar.
  • By road: the route runs from Rishikesh through Devprayag, Srinagar, Rudraprayag, Karnaprayag and Chamoli to Joshimath, then 16 km up to Auli. From Delhi it's roughly 500 km in all (our estimate: no official figure).
  • By bus: buses run from Delhi to Rishikesh and on to Srinagar and Joshimath. Then take a taxi to Auli.

Auli in December end: Christmas and New Year

  • Book early, but book flexibly. Snow can come or not, and roads can close for a day after heavy snow. A refundable stay is safer.
  • This year Christmas is on a Friday, and so is New Year's Day (1 January 2027), so both are long weekends.
  • Allow a spare day on the way back, in case the road is closed by snow.
  • Joshimath: after the land sinking in 2023, some buildings were declared unsafe. Stay only in hotels that are open and approved, and ask your hotel about any local advisory.

What to wear in Auli in December

What should I wear? Try the what-to-wear helper for Joshimath temperatures and an outfit list by month.

Auli is high and windy, and nights are below freezing. Dress in warm layers:

LayerWhat to wear
1. Next to your skinThermal top and bottom, and a spare set
2. MiddleA thick fleece or sweater, or two thin ones
3. OutsideA warm, hooded winter jacket, windproof if possible
4. ExtrasWoollen cap, neck warmer, waterproof gloves and two pairs of woollen socks
  • For the snow: waterproof trousers over thermals. Jeans soak up snow and freeze.
  • Shoes: waterproof boots with deep grip. Paths are icy in the morning.
  • Sunglasses, sunscreen and lip balm. Sun on snow is strong at this height, and the air is dry.

Safety in the cold

  • Never sleep with a heater or coal fire in a closed room. It gives off carbon monoxide, which can kill without warning.
  • Watch for altitude sickness. Auli is up to about 3,050 m. Headache, nausea and breathlessness are warning signs: rest, drink water and go lower if they get worse.
  • Change out of wet clothes quickly. Shivering, confusion and clumsiness are signs of hypothermia.
  • Carry a power bank, and keep your phone in an inside pocket: the cold drains batteries fast.

For foreign visitors

  • Visa: apply for the e-Tourist Visa only at the official site, indianvisaonline.gov.in.
  • Permits: we found no permit rule for Auli itself. Areas beyond Joshimath towards the border need permits, so check before going further.

Emergency numbers and help

From the Uttarakhand State Disaster Management Authority and Uttarakhand Tourism:

  • Emergency (police, fire, ambulance, disaster): 112
  • State Emergency Operation Centre: 1070
  • District Emergency Operation Centre: 1077
  • Uttarakhand Tourism helpline: 1364 (from other states, +91-135-1364)
  • Ministry of Tourism helpline, 24 hours, many languages: 1363 or 1800-11-1363

Questions

Is there snow in Auli in December?

Sometimes, but not every year. Official pages name January to March as Auli's snow and ski season, and December 2025 had almost no snow. Your best chance in December is the last week. For surer snow, go in January or February.

What is the temperature in Auli in December?

IMD's averages for Joshimath, 16 km below Auli, are about 13 °C by day and 4 °C at night in December. Auli is about 700 to 1,100 m higher, so it's colder, and often below freezing at night.

Can I ski in Auli in December?

Only if there's enough snow. GMVN's 2025–26 ski courses started on 8 January, with no December batches. Late December can have snow in a good year, but plan a ski trip for January to March.

Is the Auli ropeway open?

It has been closed since January 2023 because of the land sinking in Joshimath, and we found no official notice that it has reopened. A new ropeway is planned. Go up the 16 km road from Joshimath by taxi.

How do I reach Auli from Delhi?

Go to Rishikesh or Haridwar by train or bus, or fly to Dehradun. Then drive about 250 km to Joshimath through Devprayag, Rudraprayag and Chamoli, and 16 km up to Auli. Allow a full day for the drive.

How many days are enough for Auli?

Plan 3 days in the hills, plus a day each way from Rishikesh. Add a day for skiing lessons or a spare day in case snow closes the road.

Auli or Gulmarg in December?

Gulmarg is higher, with a working gondola and surer December snow. Auli is quieter and closer to Delhi by road, but its snow and ski season really starts in January. See Gulmarg in December.

What should I wear in Auli in December?

Thermals, a thick fleece and a warm hooded jacket, plus a woollen cap, gloves and waterproof boots. Nights are below freezing.
