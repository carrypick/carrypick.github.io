Rishikesh in December and January: weather, rafting and what to wear

Updated 16 Nov 2026 · 13 min read

Rishikesh at a glance

JanuaryA little colder: about 20 °C and 7 °C
SnowNone in Rishikesh. The nearest snow is in the hills, such as Dhanaulti
RaftingOpen: the season runs from September to June
BridgesLakshman Jhula is closed and Ram Jhula is shut for repairs. Cross on Bajrang Setu or Janki Setu
Haridwar KumbhStarts with the Makar Sankranti bath on 14 January 2027
Emergency112

Rishikesh in December and January is cool and sunny by day and cold at night. The town sits low, by the Ganga, so there's no snow, and the rafting season is open. This winter, two things are different: the famous Ram Jhula and Lakshman Jhula bridges are both closed, and the Haridwar Ardh Kumbh starts on 14 January 2027. This guide covers the weather, rafting and camps, the Ganga aarti, the bridges, getting there and what to wear.

Weather in December and January

IMD has no climate table for Rishikesh itself. These are its long-term averages (1991–2020) for Dehradun, the nearest IMD station. Rishikesh is lower, in the river valley, so its days are often a little warmer:

DecemberAbout 22 °CAbout 8 °CAbout 13 mm
JanuaryAbout 20 °CAbout 7 °CAbout 44 mm
FebruaryAbout 23 °CAbout 9 °CAbout 58 mm
  • Days are pleasant, mornings and nights are cold. IMD says the coldest winter nights at Dehradun are usually 1–6 °C in December and 0–5 °C in January.
  • Fog is occasional. At Dehradun, IMD counts about one foggy day in December and two in January, mostly at night and early morning. Fog on the plains is more common, and it can delay early trains and flights from Delhi.
  • It does not snow in Rishikesh. The town is only about 350 m above sea level. For snow, you have to go up into the hills, such as Dhanaulti above Mussoorie.
  • The Ganga is very cold in winter, because it comes down from the Himalayas. Keep that in mind for rafting and for a dip at the ghats.

Is rafting open in December and January?

  • Yes. Uttarakhand Tourism says September to June are the best months for rafting in Rishikesh. Rafting stops only in the monsoon, roughly July to September.
  • This season started on 23 September 2026, with safety rules: a trained guide on every raft, certified life jackets and helmets, no rafting or kayaking without a helmet, and no overloading.
  • Book only with a licensed operator. Licences go to registered rafting firms, and since December 2025 guides must also have first-aid and CPR training. Ask to see the licence.
  • Pick a stretch that suits you. News reports give these rough lengths: Brahmpuri about 6 km, Golf Course about 8 km, Shivpuri about 18 km and Marine Drive about 24 km. The longest trips start further up, from Kaudiyala. Beginners and families usually take the short Brahmpuri or Shivpuri runs.
  • Children: there's no official minimum age. Operators set their own, often 12 to 14 years, so ask before you book.
  • Dress for cold water. Wear quick-dry clothes, not cotton or jeans, and carry a full change of warm clothes and a towel. Mornings on the river are cold, so many people raft late in the morning.

Camps by the river

The National Green Tribunal banned camping on the Ganga's beaches between Kaudiyala and Rishikesh in 2015. In 2017 it allowed camps again, but only at approved sites at least 100 metres from the river. So stay only in a licensed camp, and don't pitch a tent on the sand by the water. Nights in camps are cold in December and January, so check that the camp gives you warm bedding.

The Ganga aarti

  • Triveni Ghat is Rishikesh's main bathing ghat. Uttarakhand Tourism describes its evening aarti as a ceremony of lights, drums and chanting, with the whole ghat lit with lamps.
  • Parmarth Niketan, one of Rishikesh's largest ashrams, holds its Ganga aarti every day at sunset. Its own site says the time changes with the season, so check at reception.
  • In winter, the evening aarti is roughly from 5 PM to 6:30 PM. Sunset is early, so arrive 30 to 45 minutes before to get a place to sit, and carry a warm layer: it gets cold quickly after dark.

Which bridges are open this winter?

The bridges over the Ganga have changed a lot, so old maps and videos can mislead you:

  • Lakshman Jhula is closed. The old suspension bridge was shut in July 2019 after experts found it unsafe, and it is still closed.
  • Bajrang Setu is open. The new bridge beside Lakshman Jhula is 132 m long and has glass footpaths on both sides. According to news reports, the glass footpaths are open from 6 AM to 10 PM.
  • Ram Jhula is closed for repairs. The Public Works Department shut it completely, even to people on foot, on 25 July 2026, and said it would reopen by the end of December 2026. In mid-October, about 55% of the work was done, so it may open later than that. Check locally.
  • Janki Setu, downstream of Ram Jhula, is the other footbridge people are using to cross.

The Haridwar Kumbh and New Year crowds

  • The Ardh Kumbh in Haridwar runs from 14 January to 20 April 2027. The first big bath is on Makar Sankranti, 14 January, and the next are on 6, 11 and 20 February. Haridwar is only about 25 km from Rishikesh, and most trains and buses come through it, so expect very crowded roads, trains and ghats around these dates. If you can, avoid travelling through Haridwar on the bath days, and book early.
  • New Year and long weekends bring long traffic jams on the Haridwar and Dehradun roads, at Tapovan and near the bridges. No traffic plan for this New Year has been announced yet. Arrive early in the day, and walk once you're there.

Getting to Rishikesh

  • By air: Jolly Grant Airport, Dehradun (DED), is the nearest airport. Uttarakhand Tourism says it's about 21 km from Rishikesh, with daily flights from Delhi, and taxis are easy to find.
  • By train: the quickest way from Delhi is usually a fast train to Haridwar, such as the Vande Bharat, Shatabdi or Jan Shatabdi, then a bus or taxi for the last 25 km. Rishikesh's own station, Yog Nagari Rishikesh, has a few direct trains from Delhi, but they are slow, about 7 to 9 hours. Check times on the IRCTC site.
  • By road: Rishikesh is about 240 km from Delhi. The new Delhi–Dehradun Expressway opened on 14 April 2026 and cuts Delhi to Dehradun to about 2.5 hours. Rishikesh is about 45 km further on from Dehradun. Uttarakhand Tourism says ordinary and luxury buses run from Delhi's Kashmere Gate ISBT.

Getting around

  • Walk. The busy areas, Tapovan, Lakshman Jhula, Swargashram and Muni ki Reti, are best seen on foot. Uttarakhand Tourism calls the 3 km walk from Lakshman Jhula to Ram Jhula, past rows of small shops, an interesting one.
  • Plan your river crossings. With Ram Jhula and Lakshman Jhula closed, you can cross only on Bajrang Setu or Janki Setu, so the walk between the two banks can be longer than it looks on old maps.
  • Taxis and shared autos run from the railway station and bus stand to Muni ki Reti and Swargashram. Agree the fare before you get in.

Things to do in winter

  • The Beatles Ashram (Chaurasi Kutia). The Beatles came to Maharishi Mahesh Yogi's ashram in February 1968 to learn meditation. Today you can walk around its ruins and painted walls in the forest at Swargashram. It is part of the Rajaji Tiger Reserve, which says it's open all year, and the Ministry of Tourism lists it as open from 10 AM to 4 PM.
  • A jungle safari in Rajaji. The Chilla zone of Rajaji Tiger Reserve is open from 15 November to 15 June. Safaris run from 6 to 10 AM and 2 to 6 PM, so dress warmly for the open jeep in the morning.
  • Sunrise at Kunjapuri. The Kunjapuri Devi temple sits on a 1,676 m peak above Rishikesh, with views of Himalayan peaks at sunrise and sunset. Leave in the dark, and take a jacket: it's much colder up there.
  • Yoga and ashram stays. Many ashrams in Swargashram run yoga and meditation courses. Note that the International Yoga Festival is in March (9–15 March 2027), not in winter.
  • Adventure. Besides rafting, Mohanchatti has bungee jumping and a giant swing, both from a height of up to 83 m, according to Uttarakhand Tourism.
  • Day trips. Haridwar's Har Ki Pauri has a famous Ganga aarti at dusk every day, but avoid the Kumbh bath days. For a chance of snow, Dhanaulti (2,250 m) and Mussoorie are the nearest hill stations, but snow there isn't guaranteed.

Local rules

  • No meat, fish or eggs. Their sale is banned within Rishikesh's municipal limits, a rule the Supreme Court upheld in 2004.
  • No liquor shops. Rishikesh is treated as a dry town, according to news reports, and smoking and drinking are banned on rafts.
  • Single-use plastic items such as plastic plates, cups and straws have been banned across India since 1 July 2022. Carry a cloth bag and a refillable bottle.
  • Dress modestly at temples and ashrams. Cover your shoulders and knees. Since 2023, the Neelkanth Mahadev temple near Rishikesh has asked visitors not to come in shorts, torn jeans or short skirts.

What to wear in Rishikesh in December

What should I wear? Try the what-to-wear helper for day and night temperatures and an outfit list by month.

  • Layers: a T-shirt or shirt for sunny afternoons, plus a sweater or fleece and a light jacket for mornings, evenings and the aarti. Nights in riverside camps can drop close to 5 °C, so bring warm sleepwear.
  • For rafting: quick-dry clothes, not cotton or jeans, and a full change of warm clothes for afterwards. The operator gives you the life jacket and helmet.
  • Shoes: comfortable walking shoes for the slopes and steps, and sandals that are easy to take off at temples and ghats.
  • For temples: clothes that cover your shoulders and knees, and a scarf or shawl.
  • Sun care: sunglasses and sunscreen for days on the river.

Health and safety

  • Respect the Ganga. The current is strong even where the river looks calm, and the water is very cold in winter. Bathe only at proper ghats, hold the chains, and never go in at a quiet spot or after drinking. In May 2026, after a tourist drowned at Sai Ghat, the state disaster response force (SDRF) asked visitors to avoid unfamiliar or deep spots.
  • Never sleep with a heater or charcoal burning in a closed room or tent. They give off carbon monoxide, which can kill without warning.
  • Watch your things near the bridges and temples. Monkeys snatch food, phones and glasses, and the bridges get very crowded in the evenings.

For foreign visitors

  • Visa: apply for the e-Tourist Visa only at the official site, indianvisaonline.gov.in.
  • Getting there: fly to Delhi, then take a train to Haridwar or a flight to Dehradun. Many ashrams welcome foreign visitors on their yoga courses.

Emergency numbers and help

  • Emergency (police, fire, ambulance): 112
  • Uttarakhand disaster helpline: 1070
  • Uttarakhand tourist helpline: 1364 (0135-1364 from outside Uttarakhand)
  • Ministry of Tourism helpline, 24 hours, many languages: 1363 or 1800-11-1363

Questions

Is December a good time to visit Rishikesh?

Yes. Days are sunny and about 20–22 °C, rafting is open, and the evening aarti is beautiful. Mornings and nights are cold, about 7–8 °C on average, so carry warm layers. Note that two famous bridges, Ram Jhula and Lakshman Jhula, are closed this winter.

Is rafting open in Rishikesh in December and January?

Yes. The rafting season runs from September to June, and this season began on 23 September 2026. Book with a licensed operator, wear the helmet and life jacket, and dress in quick-dry clothes, because the water is very cold.

Does it snow in Rishikesh?

No. Rishikesh is only about 350 m above sea level. The nearest places that sometimes get snow are hill stations such as Dhanaulti and Mussoorie, and even there snow isn't certain.

Is Ram Jhula open?

Not right now. Ram Jhula was closed to everyone, including people on foot, on 25 July 2026 for repairs. The Public Works Department aims to reopen it by the end of December 2026, but check locally. Cross on Janki Setu or Bajrang Setu instead.

Is Lakshman Jhula open?

No. Lakshman Jhula has been closed since 2019 because it was found unsafe. Use the new Bajrang Setu right next to it, whose glass footpaths are open from 6 AM to 10 PM.

What time is the Ganga aarti in Rishikesh in winter?

At sunset, roughly from 5 PM to 6:30 PM in December and January, at Triveni Ghat and Parmarth Niketan. Arrive 30 to 45 minutes early to find a place to sit.

What should I wear in Rishikesh in December?

Layers: light clothes for sunny afternoons, plus a sweater or fleece and a light jacket for cold mornings, evenings and camps. Wear walking shoes, carry quick-dry clothes for rafting, and cover your shoulders and knees at temples.
