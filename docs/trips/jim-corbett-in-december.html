Jim Corbett in December: open safari zones, booking, weather and what to wear

Updated 24 Nov 2026 · 10 min read

Jim Corbett at a glance

Weather in DecemberAbout 23 °C by day and 6 °C at night (IMD averages for Pantnagar, nearby)
Zones open in DecemberDhikala, Bijrani, Jhirna, Dhela, Durga Devi, Pakhro, Sonanadi and Garjia
BookOnly on the official site, corbettgov.org, up to 45 days ahead
SafariMorning and afternoon, about 3–4 hours, with a registered guide
Get thereTrain to Ramnagar (12 km from the park) or fly to Pantnagar; Delhi is about 295 km by road
Emergency112; Corbett support 1800 309 6260

Jim Corbett in December is a fine time for a safari: all the main zones are open, the grass is lower than after the monsoon, and the mornings are misty and cold. This guide covers which zones are open, how to book the permit on the official site, the rules, the weather, a simple 2-day plan, getting there from Delhi, and what to wear on a cold open-jeep safari.

Which safari zones are open in December?

Corbett Tiger Reserve has eight tourism zones. From the reserve's official site:

ZoneSeasonSafari in December
Dhikala15 November to 15 JuneYes: canter (shared bus) safari and forest rest house stays only, no jeep
BijraniMid-November to JuneYes: jeep and canter
JhirnaAll year (weather permitting)Yes: jeep
DhelaAll yearYes: jeep
Durga Devi15 October to 15 JuneYes
Pakhro15 October to 15 JuneYes
Sonanadi15 October to 15 JuneYes
GarjiaNo dates on the official pageCheck when you book
  • Dhikala is the biggest and best-known zone, on the Ramganga, with the old forest rest house from 1890. In the day, you can only see it by canter. Project Tiger was launched here in 1973.
  • Bijrani, Jhirna and Dhela are the usual jeep zones from Ramnagar, about 15 to 20 km from town.
  • Phato, Sitabani and Pawalgarh are sold as "Corbett" safaris by some agents, but they aren't zones of Corbett Tiger Reserve and aren't on its site. We couldn't confirm their seasons or booking.

How to book the Corbett safari online

From the reserve's booking process page and terms:

  • Book only on the official site, corbettgov.org. The reserve's FAQ also names corbettonline.uk.gov.in, which now shows the same site. Many look-alike sites sell permits. The reserve says it can cancel permits bought through agents without a refund.
  • When: jeep and canter permits open up to 45 days ahead, a day at a time. Choose the zone, the date, and a morning or afternoon safari.
  • Group size: up to 6 people per jeep (Gypsy) permit, and one jeep per permit. The canter at Dhikala seats 16 and leaves from the Ramnagar Corbett office or Dhangari gate.
  • Children under 12 don't need a permit.
  • You need: each person's name, age, gender, country and ID number, and a WhatsApp number for the OTP. Since 22 September 2026, the group leader uploads the front and back of their ID, and shows everyone's original ID at the gate.
  • Guide and driver charges are paid separately; they're shown on the permit.
  • Tickets can't be refunded, changed or transferred.
  • Night stays in forest rest houses (Dhikala and others) open up to about 45 days ahead, about 90 days for foreigners. The site said the next window opens on 7 December 2026, for stays from 21 January to 19 February 2027, so late-December rooms are probably already gone.

Safari timings and rules

  • Timings: the zone pages give about 6:30 to 9:30 AM and 3 to 6 PM. Winter times can change, so go by the time printed on your permit.
  • Be at the gate at least 45 minutes early. Late entry isn't allowed.
  • A registered nature guide must go with you, and only the reserve's registered vehicles can go in.
  • Don't get out of the vehicle. Walking is allowed only on marked nature trails.
  • Not allowed: alcohol, non-vegetarian food, smoking, fires, pets, loud music and plastic. No food on the safari.
  • Mobile phones aren't allowed in the core tourism zones, by a Supreme Court order the reserve quotes.
  • Never feed, tease or chase animals. It's an offence under the Wildlife (Protection) Act.

Jim Corbett weather in December and January

There's no IMD table for Ramnagar. These are IMD's averages for Pantnagar, the nearest station, in the plains (1991–2020):

MonthDayNightRain
DecemberAbout 23 °CAbout 6 °CAbout 12 mm
JanuaryAbout 20 °CAbout 6 °CAbout 28 mm
FebruaryAbout 24 °CAbout 8 °CAbout 35 mm
  • December is almost dry, with rain on about one day in two Decembers.
  • Mornings are cold. The coldest night on record at Pantnagar was 0.2 °C in December and −2.2 °C in January. In an open jeep at 6:30 AM, it feels much colder.
  • Mist is common on winter mornings in the grasslands, as in our photo from Dhikala in December. (General experience: there's no official fog figure.)

Corbett trip plan: 2 days

Day 1: arrive and a first safari

  1. Reach Ramnagar by the overnight train or by road from Delhi.
  2. Afternoon jeep safari in Bijrani, Jhirna or Dhela.
  3. Garjia Devi temple on the way, built on a big rock in the middle of the Kosi river.

Day 2: morning safari and back

  1. Morning safari in a different zone, or the Dhikala canter for the grasslands and the Ramganga.
  2. Corbett Museum at Kaladhungi, Jim Corbett's old house, about 25 to 30 km from Ramnagar. The reserve lists it as open from 7 AM to 6 PM, closed only on Holi. Check locally.
  3. Corbett Falls, about 25 km from Ramnagar, then back to Delhi.

Have a third day? Add another safari zone, or go up to Nainital.

Getting to Jim Corbett

From Uttarakhand Tourism and the reserve's site:

  • By train: Ramnagar station is about 12 km from the park. The Corbett Park Link Express runs from Delhi with the Ranikhet Express. Check times on NTES or IRCTC.
  • By road: Delhi to Corbett is about 295 km, about 6 hours, says Uttarakhand Tourism.
  • By air: the nearest airport is Pantnagar. Uttarakhand Tourism says about 50 km; the reserve's zone pages say about 90 km. It depends on the gate.

Corbett in December end: Christmas and New Year

  • Book permits the day they open, 45 days ahead. Christmas and New Year permits go fast.
  • This year Christmas is on a Friday, and so is New Year's Day (1 January 2027), so both are long weekends.
  • Book your hotel in Ramnagar early. Many resorts sit along the Kosi river.
  • If the zone you want is full, try Jhirna or Dhela, which have more jeeps.

What to wear on a Corbett safari in December

What should I wear? Try the what-to-wear helper for day and night temperatures and an outfit list by month.

Afternoons are warm, but a 6:30 AM safari in an open jeep is very cold.

  • Layers for the morning safari: a thermal top, a fleece or sweater and a warm, windproof jacket. Take layers off as the sun comes up.
  • Extras: a woollen cap, gloves, a scarf or neck warmer, and warm socks. A small blanket helps in the jeep.
  • Colours: wear browns, greens and greys. Bright colours stand out in the forest.
  • Shoes: closed, comfortable shoes. You won't walk much, but mornings are damp.
  • Sunglasses, sunscreen and a cap for the afternoon sun and the dust on the tracks.
  • Binoculars if you have them, and a camera.

Health and safety

  • Stay in the vehicle and follow the guide. Never step out to take a photo.
  • Keep your voice low. Noise scares animals away, and loud music is banned.
  • Watch for elephants on the roads around Ramnagar, especially at dusk.
  • Never sleep with a heater or coal fire in a closed room. It gives off carbon monoxide, which can kill without warning.

For foreign visitors

  • Book on the official site and choose your nationality correctly. Foreigner-quota stays need at least one foreign guest present.
  • Visa: apply for the e-Tourist Visa only at the official site, indianvisaonline.gov.in.

Emergency numbers and help

  • Emergency (police, fire, ambulance): 112 (Uttarakhand Police)
  • Police: 100 (Nainital district)
  • Corbett Tiger Reserve support: 1800 309 6260
  • Human–wildlife conflict, toll-free: 1800 890 1975
  • Ministry of Tourism helpline, 24 hours, many languages: 1363 or 1800-11-1363

Questions

Is Jim Corbett open in December?

Yes. All eight tourism zones are open in December: Dhikala (from 15 November, canter and stays only), Bijrani, Jhirna, Dhela, Durga Devi, Pakhro, Sonanadi, and Garjia (check dates when you book).

Which zone is best in Jim Corbett in December?

Dhikala, if you can get a canter seat or a forest rest house stay: it's the largest zone, with grasslands on the Ramganga. For a jeep safari from Ramnagar, Bijrani is the most popular. Jhirna and Dhela are good if Bijrani is full.

How do I book a Jim Corbett safari?

On the official site, corbettgov.org, up to 45 days ahead. You need each person's ID details and a WhatsApp number for the OTP. Up to 6 people per jeep. The reserve warns against permits bought through agents.

What is the weather in Jim Corbett in December?

IMD's averages for nearby Pantnagar are about 23 °C by day and 6 °C at night. Mornings are cold and often misty, so an early safari in an open jeep feels freezing.

What are the safari timings in December?

The zone pages give about 6:30 to 9:30 AM and 3 to 6 PM. Go by the time on your permit, and be at the gate at least 45 minutes early.

How do I reach Jim Corbett from Delhi?

By train to Ramnagar, about 12 km from the park (the Corbett Park Link Express runs with the Ranikhet Express), or by road, about 295 km. The nearest airport is Pantnagar.

Can I see a tiger in Corbett in December?

Maybe. No safari can promise a tiger. Winter's lower grass and the morning light help, and Corbett also has elephants, deer, crocodiles and many birds.

What should I wear on a Corbett safari in December?

Warm layers for the morning: a thermal, a fleece, a windproof jacket, a cap and gloves. Wear muted colours, and take off layers as the day warms up.
