Kashmir in December: weather, snow and what to wear

Updated 15 Nov 2026 · 9 min read

Kashmir at a glance

Weather in DecemberSrinagar about 10 °C by day and −1 °C at night. Gulmarg about 4 °C and −5 °C (IMD averages)
SnowGulmarg often gets snow in December, but some early winters are dry
Coldest weeksChillai Kalan, from 21 December to the end of January
Fly toSrinagar (SXR)
TrainVande Bharat from Jammu Tawi or Katra to Srinagar
PhonesPrepaid SIMs from other states usually don't work. Carry a postpaid SIM
Emergency112

Kashmir in December is cold, quiet and often snowy. Srinagar's nights go below freezing, Gulmarg is colder still, and the hardest part of winter, Chillai Kalan, starts on 21 December. This guide covers the weather, the chances of snow, getting there in winter, and what to wear.

Weather in December

Long-term December averages from the India Meteorological Department (IMD):

PlaceDayNightRain and snow
SrinagarAbout 10 °CAbout −1 °CAbout 46 mm
GulmargAbout 4 °CAbout −5 °CAbout 104 mm
  • Gulmarg is much colder than Srinagar. Its lowest December reading, according to IMD, was −18 °C, on 4 December 1968. Srinagar's was −12.8 °C, on 13 December 1934.
  • Days are short and cold. Most December days are dry, and sunny days feel pleasant in the sun but cold in the shade.
  • Chillai Kalan is the name for the 40 coldest days of the Kashmir winter. It starts on 21 December every year and lasts until the end of January. Lakes and taps can freeze, and this is when heavy snow is most likely.

Will there be snow?

  • Gulmarg usually gets snow from December, and IMD's averages show even more rain and snow from January to March. So snow is more certain in January and February.
  • Some early winters are dry. Last winter, after a long dry spell, the first big snow in Gulmarg and Sonamarg came on 21 December 2025, the first day of Chillai Kalan.
  • This season: the first snow in the higher reaches closed the Srinagar–Leh road over Zojila on 13 and 14 November 2026.
  • Srinagar city gets snow less often than Gulmarg, Sonamarg or Pahalgam.

Check the forecast in the week before you go. If snow matters most, plan for late December to February, and keep a spare day.

The Gulmarg Gondola

The gondola goes up from Gulmarg in two stages: Phase 1 to Kongdori, and Phase 2 higher up, on Apharwat. It's the easiest way to reach deep snow.

  • Book only on the official site. The J&K Cable Car Corporation sells tickets online at etickets.jammukashmircablecar.com, and its own site asks tourists not to fall prey to touts. News reports say tickets bought any other way aren't valid, and police have caught tourists with fake tickets.
  • Phase 2 stops in bad weather. Services are often stopped when snow or strong wind comes in. Plan the gondola for your first clear morning, and keep a spare day.
  • It's much colder at the top. Wear every layer you have, plus gloves, a cap and sunglasses.

Skiing: Gulmarg is India's best-known ski area. The snow events of the Khelo India Winter Games were held there in February 2026, but the 2025 events had to be postponed because there wasn't enough snow. For skiing, January to March is safer than December.

Getting there in winter

  • By air: fly to Srinagar (SXR). Heavy snow can delay or cancel flights, so don't plan a tight connection on your way home.
  • By train: since 2 May 2026, Vande Bharat trains run directly from Jammu Tawi to Srinagar, via Katra and Banihal, in about 5 hours. From Delhi, take a train to Jammu Tawi or Katra and change there. Book early for the holidays.
  • By road: the Jammu–Srinagar highway (NH44) can close for a day or more after heavy snow. Mughal Road usually stays closed for much of the winter.
  • Gulmarg is about 50 km from Srinagar by road, through Tangmarg. After snow, the road may be open only to vehicles with snow chains or 4x4s. Ask your hotel or driver in the morning.
  • Sonamarg can now be reached in winter through the Sonamarg (Z-Morh) tunnel, opened in January 2025. The road beyond it, over Zojila to Ladakh, closes after snow.

Which places are open?

After the attack near Pahalgam in April 2025, many tourist spots in Jammu and Kashmir were closed. They reopened in stages: by February 2026, 42 places had reopened, including Yusmarg and Doodhpathri, and the Chief Minister said all the rest would reopen by May 2026.

Srinagar, Gulmarg, Sonamarg and Pahalgam town are the usual winter trips. Rules can change at short notice, so check with your hotel or the tourism office before you go to a smaller spot.

Phones, money and cold-weather tips

  • Prepaid SIMs from other states usually don't work in Jammu and Kashmir. Carry a postpaid SIM, or buy a local prepaid SIM there with your ID. Save your hotel address and tickets offline.
  • Carry a power bank and a small torch. Cold drains phone batteries fast. Keep your phone in an inside pocket.
  • Carry some cash for taxis, ponies and small shops.
  • Book a room with proper heating, and ask about hot water before you book.

What to wear in Kashmir in December

What should I wear? Try the what-to-wear helper for day and night temperatures and an outfit list by month.

Dress in layers, as for Manali, but plan for colder nights, especially in Gulmarg and Pahalgam:

LayerWhat to wear
1. Next to your skinThermal top and bottom, and a spare set
2. MiddleA thick sweater or fleece, or two thin ones
3. OutsideA warm jacket with a hood. For Gulmarg, a heavier coat or two layers under a padded jacket
4. ExtrasWoollen cap, neck warmer, waterproof gloves and woollen socks
  • For the snow: wear waterproof trousers over thermals. Jeans soak up snow and stay cold. Shops in Tangmarg and Gulmarg rent snow coats and gumboots by the day.
  • Shoes: waterproof boots or hiking shoes with deep grip. Roads and paths can be icy in the morning. Avoid mesh sports shoes.
  • The pheran: Kashmiris wear the pheran, a long, loose woollen cloak, over their clothes in winter. It's warm, and a good thing to buy in Srinagar.
  • At mosques and shrines, such as Hazratbal, dress modestly. Women should carry a scarf to cover their head.
  • Sunglasses, sunscreen and lip balm. The sun on snow is strong, and the air is dry.

Health and safety in the cold

  • Never sleep with a heater burning in a closed room. Gas heaters, charcoal and kangris give off carbon monoxide, which can kill without warning. At least 19 people in Jammu and Kashmir died this way between December 2024 and early January 2025, including a family of five in Srinagar. Keep a window slightly open, and switch heaters off before you sleep.
  • Dress for the cold, and change out of wet clothes quickly. Shivering, confusion and clumsiness are signs of hypothermia.
  • Moisturiser and lip balm help with dry, cracked skin.

For foreign visitors

  • Read your government's advice. The UK's travel advice (updated 7 October 2026) is against all travel to Jammu and Kashmir, including Srinagar, Gulmarg, Pahalgam, Sonamarg and the Jammu–Srinagar highway. The US advice says "Do Not Travel" to Jammu and Kashmir, except eastern Ladakh and Leh. Travel against this advice can make your travel insurance invalid.
  • Visa: apply for the e-Tourist Visa only at indianvisaonline.gov.in.

Emergency numbers

  • Emergency (police, fire, ambulance): 112
  • Ministry of Tourism helpline, 24 hours, many languages: 1363 or 1800-11-1363

Questions

How cold is Kashmir in December?

Very cold at night. IMD's long-term averages for Srinagar are about 10 °C in the day and about −1 °C at night. Gulmarg is colder, at about 4 °C in the day and −5 °C at night.

Does it snow in Gulmarg in December?

Often, but not every year. Some early winters are dry: last winter, the first big snow came on 21 December 2025. Snow is more certain from late December to February.

What should I wear in Kashmir in December?

Thermals, a thick sweater or fleece, and a warm jacket with a hood, plus a woollen cap, neck warmer, waterproof gloves and woollen socks. Wear waterproof boots with a good grip. For the snow, add waterproof trousers or rent a snow coat.

What is Chillai Kalan?

The 40 coldest days of the Kashmir winter. It starts on 21 December and lasts until the end of January. Heavy snow and frozen taps are common then.

Do prepaid SIM cards work in Kashmir?

Prepaid SIMs from other states usually don't work in Jammu and Kashmir. Carry a postpaid SIM, or buy a local prepaid SIM there with your ID.

How do I book the Gulmarg Gondola?

Only online, on the J&K Cable Car Corporation's official site, etickets.jammukashmircablecar.com. Don't buy from touts: tickets bought any other way aren't valid. Phase 2 often stops in bad weather, so keep a spare day.

Can I go to Srinagar by train in winter?

Yes. Vande Bharat trains run directly from Jammu Tawi to Srinagar, via Katra, in about 5 hours. From Delhi, take a train to Jammu Tawi or Katra and change.
