Where to go in India in December and January

Updated 6 Nov 2026 · 5 min read

December and January are the best months for most of India: the monsoon is long over, the skies are clear, and it's cool almost everywhere except the far south. They are also the busiest months, so it pays to choose well and book early. Here's how four of India's favourite winter trips compare.

Goa: for beaches and New Year parties

Goa is warm, dry and sunny in winter, and New Year's Eve is its biggest night. Fly into Mopa for North Goa's busy beaches, or Dabolim for central and South Goa. Remember the rules that bring big fines: no drinking or cooking on the beach, helmets on scooters, and loudspeakers only until midnight from 24 to 31 December.

Manali: for snow and mountains

Manali is the classic snow trip, but snow isn't guaranteed: December 2025 was almost completely dry, and the first big snowfall came only in late January 2026. Rohtang Pass is closed in winter, and police often stop cars beyond Solang after snow. Keep your dates flexible, and pack thermals.

Read the full Manali winter guide and the winter hill trip packing list.

Jaipur: for forts, palaces and markets

Jaipur has warm sunny afternoons and chilly mornings, perfect for walking around forts. Combine it with Delhi and Agra for the Golden Triangle. In January, the Jaipur Literature Festival (14–18 January 2027) and the kite festival around 14 January add more to see.

Read the full Jaipur trip guide.

Kerala: for backwaters, tea hills and beaches

Kerala is warm on the coast and cool in the Munnar hills. Spend a night on a houseboat in Alappuzha, walk the tea estates in Munnar, and end on the cliffs at Varkala. In Fort Kochi, New Year's Eve ends with the Pappanji burning on the beach at midnight. Note that 1 January is a dry day in Kerala.

How to choose

  • Want snow? Go to Manali, but plan for January or February for a better chance, and check the forecast.
  • Want to party on New Year's Eve? Goa, or the Cochin Carnival in Fort Kochi.
  • Travelling with parents or children? Jaipur and Kerala are calmer, with easy sightseeing and mild days.
  • Visiting India for the first time? Jaipur with Delhi and Agra, or Kerala, are easy first trips. Goa is popular with foreign visitors for a beach holiday.
  • Short on time? Pick one place. Moving between them takes most of a day.

Book early, travel light

  • Book hotels and transport early. Christmas to New Year is peak season in all four places, and prices and availability get worse every week.

Questions

Where should I go in India in December?

For beaches and parties, Goa. For snow, Manali, though snow isn't guaranteed in December. For forts and palaces, Jaipur. For backwaters, hills and quieter beaches, Kerala. All four are at their best in winter.

Which place in India has snow in December?

Manali often gets snow from December to February, but not every year: December 2025 was almost completely dry. For a better chance of snow, go in January or February and check the forecast.

Where can I celebrate New Year in India?

Goa is India's biggest New Year party, and Fort Kochi in Kerala ends the year with the Cochin Carnival and the Pappanji burning at midnight. Book early, because both get very crowded.

Is December a good time to visit Kerala?

Yes. The monsoon is over, the backwaters are calm, and Munnar is cool. A few showers are still possible on the coast in December.

Is it cold in Jaipur in December and January?

Mornings and nights are cold, around 8–9 °C on average and sometimes 2–3 °C, while afternoons are sunny and around 22–24 °C. Carry a warm jacket.
