Where to go in June: Ladakh, the hills and summer trips abroad

Updated 26 Nov 2026 · 16 min read

India and abroad in June at a glance

MonsoonIMD normal dates: Kerala 1 June, Mumbai 11 June, Delhi 27 June. Averages, not a forecast
HolidayMuharram, Wednesday 16 June 2027 on the DoPT list; it depends on the moon
For high mountainsLadakh and Spiti: both roads are usually open by June
For cool hillsKashmir, Manali, Shimla, Mussoorie
Char DhamBusy until the monsoon; register first
AbroadEurope, UK, Bali, Russia, Canada, Kazakhstan

June is the month the monsoon arrives. It usually reaches Kerala at the start of the month and spreads north over the next few weeks, so the south and west turn wet while the north is still hot. Schools in much of India are still on summer holiday, so the hills stay busy.

It's the best month to start a Ladakh or Spiti trip: both high roads are usually open, the passes are clearing, and these dry valleys get little of the monsoon rain. Kashmir and the Himachal hills are green and cool. The Char Dham yatra is busy until the rains set in, and Sikkim and Darjeeling get heavy rain.

Abroad, June is early summer in Europe, the UK, Russia and Canada, and the dry season in Bali.

This page sums up our trip guides that fit June, in India and abroad, with the dates we could confirm for 2027. Each place links to its full guide, where the official sources are listed.

Going earlier? See where to go in May and where to go in April. Only looking abroad? Compare all our international trips from India by flight time, best months and visa type.

June 2027 dates to know

  • Muharram: Wednesday 16 June 2027 on the DoPT holiday list for central government offices. It depends on the moon, so the date may move by a day.
  • No other gazetted holiday in June 2027 on the DoPT list. Rath Yatra, a restricted holiday, is just after, on Monday 5 July 2027.
  • School summer holidays: many states keep schools closed into June, but every state and board sets its own dates, so check your school's calendar.
  • Monsoon: IMD's normal date for Kerala is 1 June, for Mumbai 11 June and for Delhi 27 June (see below). These are long-term averages, not a forecast for 2027.
  • Ladakh and Spiti roads: by June, the Manali–Leh road and Kunzum La are usually open. In 2026, the Manali–Leh road opened to light vehicles on 15 May and Kunzum La around 20 May, news reports said. The 2027 dates are to be announced.
  • Char Dham: all four temples are open. The 2027 opening dates are to be announced, and registration is compulsory.
  • Amarnath Yatra: the 2027 dates are to be announced by the Shri Amarnathji Shrine Board. In 2026 the yatra ran from 3 July to 28 August, so it usually starts just after June.

Long weekends in June 2027

Muharram falls on a Wednesday, so there's no long weekend without leave. On the DoPT list for central government offices in Delhi:

  • Saturday 12 to Wednesday 16 June, 5 days with 2 days off (Monday 14 and Tuesday 15 June).
  • Wednesday 16 to Sunday 20 June, 5 days with 2 days off (Thursday 17 and Friday 18 June).
  • Saturday 12 to Sunday 20 June, 9 days with 4 days off.

The date may move with the moon, so keep bookings flexible. See every 2027 long weekend in our long weekends 2027 planner.

When does the monsoon arrive?

IMD publishes normal dates for the start of the south-west monsoon: the average date it arrives, from many years of records. These are not a forecast. Each year it can come a week or more early or late, and IMD announces the actual arrival in Kerala in its press releases.

Kerala (Thiruvananthapuram)1 June
Chennai and Guwahati4 June
Gangtok10 June
Mumbai and Kolkata11 June
Shimla24 June
Chandigarh26 June

The whole country8 July

IMD's normal dates were revised in 2020, from records for 1961–2019. What happened in 2026: the monsoon reached Kerala on 4 June, 3 days after the normal date, and covered the whole country on 9 July, IMD said.

  • Kerala, Goa and the west coast get heavy rain from June. Beaches are rough, many beach shacks close, and swimming is unsafe in many places. Kerala's backwaters and tea hills are green, but it rains most days.
  • Mumbai and the Western Ghats turn green after mid-June, and the waterfalls fill. Roads and trains can be hit by heavy rain.
  • Delhi and the north are hot and humid in June until the monsoon reaches them, normally near the end of the month.
  • Check IMD's warnings for your route on mausam.imd.gov.in or the Mausam app. In the hills, heavy rain brings landslides.

Ladakh and Spiti in June

June is when most people go. Ladakh and Spiti are high, dry valleys behind the Himalaya, so the monsoon brings them little rain, even when the plains are wet.

  • Ladakh: IMD's averages for Leh are about 24 °C by day and 11 °C at night in June, and Leh gets only about 35 mm of rain and snow in a whole year. Both roads, from Srinagar over Zoji La and from Manali over Baralacha La, are usually open by June. In 2026 the Manali–Leh road opened to light vehicles on 15 May, newspapers reported. The 2027 date is to be announced.
  • Rest for the first 48 hours in Leh, whether you fly or drive in. Altitude sickness is common if you rush. Use a postpaid SIM: Ladakh Police says only postpaid connections work there.
  • Spiti: June is the start of the full circuit, in through Kinnaur from Shimla and out over Kunzum La to Manali. In 2026, Kunzum La opened to light 4x4 vehicles around 20 May, news reports said. The 2027 date is to be announced. Foreigners need an Inner Line Permit for the border stretch between Kinnaur and Spiti.
  • Chandratal lake near Kunzum La is reached in the summer months, once the road is open. Stay in a camp near the lake, as nights are very cold.
  • The roads in, through Kinnaur and the Himachal hills, do get monsoon rain. Keep a spare day for landslides, especially late in June.

Read our full Ladakh trip guide for permits, the 48-hour rest rule and a 7-day plan, and our Spiti Valley trip guide for both roads and an 8-day circuit. Going in winter instead? See Spiti in winter.

Kashmir and the Himachal hills in June

  • Kashmir: Srinagar, Gulmarg, Pahalgam and Sonamarg are green and cool, the busiest time of year. The Vande Bharat train runs from Jammu Tawi and Katra to Srinagar. Pack with our Kashmir packing list.
  • Manali: busy with the school holidays. Vehicles going up to Rohtang Pass need a permit from the official site, rohtangpermits.hp.gov.in, with a set number allowed each day. Pack with our Manali packing list.
  • Shimla: the Ridge, Mall Road, Kufri and the toy train from Kalka. Book the train early.
  • Mussoorie and Nainital: their main season runs from March to June. They're close to Delhi, so weekends are crowded.
  • Once the monsoon reaches the hills, usually late in June, roads in Himachal and Uttarakhand can close after heavy rain. Travel by day and keep a spare day.

Sikkim and Darjeeling in June

June is monsoon time in the eastern Himalaya. IMD's averages for Gangtok give about 647 mm of rain in June, one of its wettest months.

  • Landslides close NH10, the main road from Siliguri to Gangtok, and roads in North Sikkim most years. See our Sikkim trip plan for the road and permit details.
  • Clouds hide Kanchenjunga most days. For mountain views, October to December is far better.
  • If you go, keep spare days, don't travel at night, and check with your driver or hotel about the road each morning.
  • Darjeeling is cool and green, with mist and rain. Check the toy train's running days before you book, as hill lines can be stopped after heavy rain.

Char Dham yatra in June

The Char Dham temples in Uttarakhand, Yamunotri, Gangotri, Kedarnath and Badrinath, are all open in June. The 2027 opening dates are to be announced. In 2026 they opened between 19 and 23 April.

  • May and June are the busiest months, with the school holidays. Book stays early.
  • Nights are cold, even in June. IMD recorded 4.7 °C at Kedarnath on 6 June 2026, says our Char Dham Yatra 2027 guide. Carry warm layers and a rain jacket.
  • Registration is compulsory on the Uttarakhand government's portal, registrationandtouristcare.uk.gov.in.
  • Watch for the monsoon. In 2025 the yatra was stopped for 24 hours on 29 June after an IMD red alert. Go early in June if you can, and keep a spare day.
  • Plan it step by step with our Char Dham Yatra 2027 guide, and pack with our Kedarnath packing list.

Europe and the UK in June

Europe is at its best: about 21 °C by day in Zurich, 22 °C in Paris and 27 °C in Rome, on the long-term averages in our guide. It's busy, so book early, and apply for the Schengen visa well ahead: the EU says you can apply up to 6 months before you travel. Pack with our Europe packing list, walking shoes for Europe and our adapter for Europe guide.

The UK is mild in June, with very long days and daylight until 9 PM or later. It's the busy season, so book flights and stays early. GOV.UK says you can apply for the Standard Visitor visa up to 3 months before you travel. See our adapter for the UK guide.

Bali and Mauritius

Bali is in its driest time, June to September, with rain on only a few days a month. It's also the busiest, so book early. Pack with our Bali packing list. Mauritius is in its cool, dry winter, with nights around 16 to 19 °C, and Indians don't need a visa.

Russia, Canada, Kazakhstan and Kenya

Russia has the white nights in June, when St Petersburg stays light almost all night. Canada's best season for Banff, the Rockies and Vancouver starts in June. Kazakhstan's mountain lakes near Almaty are best from June, and Indians don't need a visa for up to 14 days. Kenya's long dry season starts in late June, when the wildebeest migration usually reaches the Masai Mara, though its timing depends on the rains.

Where not to go in June

  • Japan: June to early July is the rainy season.
  • Dubai and the Gulf: very hot, about 37–41 °C by day from May to September.
  • Egypt: above 40 °C by day in Luxor from June to August.
  • Sri Lanka's west and south coasts get the south-west monsoon from May to September. Pick the east coast instead.

Questions

Which is the best place to visit in June in India?

Ladakh and Spiti, where both high roads are usually open by June and the monsoon brings little rain. Kashmir, Manali and Shimla are cool and green, and the Char Dham yatra is on. Avoid the beaches of Kerala and Goa, where the monsoon arrives.

When does the monsoon reach Kerala, Mumbai and Delhi?

IMD's normal dates are 1 June for Kerala, 11 June for Mumbai and 27 June for Delhi. These are long-term averages, not a forecast: each year the monsoon can come a week or more early or late. IMD announces the actual dates.

Is June a good time to visit Ladakh?

Yes. It's the start of the main season, with warm days in Leh (about 24 °C, IMD averages) and cold nights. Both roads are usually open by June, but the 2027 opening dates are to be announced. Rest for the first 48 hours after you arrive.

Is June a good time for the Char Dham Yatra?

Yes, until the monsoon arrives. June is busy, nights at the temples are cold, and heavy rain can stop the yatra late in the month. Register on the official portal before you go.

Where can I go outside India in June?

Europe, the UK, Russia and Canada are in early summer, Bali is in its dry season, and Kenya's dry safari season starts in late June. Avoid Japan's rainy season and the heat in Dubai and Egypt.

Is there a long weekend in June 2027?

Not without leave. Muharram is on Wednesday 16 June 2027 on the DoPT list, and it may move with the moon. Take Monday and Tuesday off for 12 to 16 June, or Thursday and Friday for 16 to 20 June. See our long weekends 2027 planner.

Can I go to Sikkim in June?

You can, but it's the monsoon: Gangtok gets about 647 mm of rain in June on IMD's averages, and landslides close roads most years. Keep spare days, or go in October to December for clear views.
