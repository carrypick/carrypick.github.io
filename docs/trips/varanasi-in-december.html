Varanasi in December and January: weather, a 2–3 day trip plan and what to wear

Updated 18 Nov 2026 · 12 min read

Varanasi at a glance

Weather in DecemberAbout 24 °C by day and 10 °C at night (IMD averages)
JanuaryColder: about 22 °C by day and 9 °C at night
RainAlmost none in December
FogDense fog is common from late December to January. It delays trains and flights
How long2 days for the ghats, the temple and Sarnath, 3 with a spare foggy morning
Evening aartiDashashwamedh Ghat, around 6 PM in winter
Kashi VishwanathOpens 2:30 AM, closes 11 PM (temple trust)
Get thereFly to Varanasi (VNS), or take a train to Varanasi Junction (BSB)
Emergency112

Varanasi in December and January is cool, dry and often foggy. Afternoons are sunny and pleasant for walking the ghats. Mornings and nights are cold, and the river breeze makes them feel colder. This guide covers the weather and fog, a simple 2-day and 3-day plan, the Ganga aarti, Kashi Vishwanath timings and New Year crowds, the new ropeway, how to get there and what to wear.

Varanasi weather in December and January

Long-term averages for Varanasi from the India Meteorological Department (IMD, 1991–2020):

MonthDayNightRain
DecemberAbout 24 °CAbout 10 °CAbout 4 mm
JanuaryAbout 22 °CAbout 9 °CAbout 16 mm
FebruaryAbout 27 °CAbout 12 °CAbout 22 mm
  • January is the coldest month. The lowest January reading on record is 1.0 °C. The lowest in December is 2.2 °C.
  • December is very dry. It rains on less than one day in an average December.
  • Afternoons are warm in the sun. Early mornings on the river and nights are cold. Dress in layers.
  • Late December is colder than early December. Cold waves on the plains usually come at the end of December and in January.

Fog in Varanasi

  • Dense fog is common in late December and January. It is worst at night and early in the morning.
  • Trains run late or get cancelled. Every winter, Indian Railways cancels or cuts some trains in North India because of fog. In winter 2025–26, dozens of trains were cancelled from 1 December to 28 February, including some from Varanasi. Check your train status on the day, and keep spare time before a connecting flight.
  • Flights can be delayed or diverted. Fog often delays flights into Varanasi in the morning. If you can, choose an afternoon flight and keep a free day before anything important.
  • Morning boat rides may see little. On foggy mornings you may not see the far bank. Sunrise views are better on clear days, so keep a spare morning if the boat ride matters to you.

Varanasi trip plan: 2 days

The old city runs along the river, and most sights are on the ghats or in the lanes behind them. Stay near the ghats and walk; the lanes are too narrow for cars.

Day 1: Kashi Vishwanath, the ghats and the evening aarti

  1. Kashi Vishwanath early in the morning. General darshan is from 4 AM to 11 AM, and again from 12 noon to 7 PM, says the temple trust. Leave your phone and bag at the hotel or in a locker.
  2. The old lanes around the temple for breakfast, Banarasi silk and brassware.
  3. A ghat walk in the afternoon sun, from Assi Ghat to Dashashwamedh. Manikarnika, the main cremation ghat, is a short walk further on. Don't take photos of cremations.
  4. The evening Ganga aarti at Dashashwamedh Ghat. It starts after sunset, so come about 45 minutes early for a place on the steps, or watch from a boat.

Day 2: a dawn boat ride and Sarnath

  1. A boat ride at sunrise along the ghats. Fix the fare and the time with the boatman first, and ask for a life jacket.
  2. Subah-e-Banaras at Assi Ghat, the morning aarti and music at dawn. Check the time locally, because it changes with the season.
  3. Sarnath, about 10 km away, where the Buddha gave his first sermon. The Sarnath Museum is open from 10 AM to 5 PM and is closed on Fridays, so don't plan Sarnath on a Friday.
  4. Back to the ghats in the evening, or an early night if your train leaves the next morning.

Varanasi trip plan: 3 days

Add a third day:

  • A spare morning for fog. Late December and January mornings are often foggy. If fog hid the river on your boat ride, try again.
  • Mangala Aarti at Kashi Vishwanath, from 3 AM to 4 AM, for ticket holders only. Book on the temple's official site, well ahead for the New Year week.
  • The aarti from the other side: if you watched from the steps on Day 1, watch from a boat this time, or the other way round.
  • Time for delays. Keep this day free before a flight or a long train, because fog delays trains and morning flights.

The Ganga aarti

  • Dashashwamedh Ghat has the main evening aarti. It follows sunset, so in winter it starts around 6 PM, about an hour earlier than in summer. Arrive 45 minutes early to get a place on the steps or a boat.
  • Watching from a boat is popular. Fares are agreed with the boatman before you get in, so fix the price and the time first. Ask for a life jacket.
  • Assi Ghat has a morning aarti and music programme at dawn, called Subah-e-Banaras. Check the time locally, because it changes with the season.
  • Carry a warm layer. The ghats get cold quickly after sunset, and you will sit for about an hour.

Kashi Vishwanath timings and New Year crowds

  • Christmas to New Year is the busiest time. From 24 December 2025 to 1 January 2026, more than 30 lakh devotees visited the temple, according to news reports. On 1 January 2026, about 3.5 lakh came by the afternoon.
  • Special darshan can stop on rush days. Last winter the temple stopped VIP, protocol and sparsh (touch) darshan from 24 December to 3 January.
  • Temple hours: the temple opens at 2:30 AM and closes at 11 PM. Mangala Aarti is from 3 AM to 4 AM. General darshan is from 4 AM to 11 AM and from 12 noon to 7 PM, with the midday Bhog Aarti from 11:30 AM to 12 noon. The Sapta Rishi Aarti is from 7 PM to 8:30 PM, the Shringar/Bhog Aarti starts at 9 PM, and the Shayan Aarti at 10:30 PM. These are the temple trust's times; they can change on festival days.
  • Phones are not allowed inside. Leave phones, bags and other gadgets in the lockers near the gates, or at your hotel.
  • Check timings and tickets on the official site, shrikashivishwanath.org. Book Mangala Aarti (very early morning) there too, well ahead for the New Year week.

Getting to Varanasi

  • By air: Lal Bahadur Shastri International Airport (VNS) is at Babatpur, about 26 km from the city. A cab takes about an hour.
  • By train: the main stations are Varanasi Junction (BSB) and Banaras (BSBS). Pt. Deen Dayal Upadhyaya Junction (DDU, once Mughalsarai) is across the river. Vande Bharat trains run between New Delhi and Varanasi in about 8 hours. Check running days on IRCTC.
  • Inside the old city: the lanes near the ghats are too narrow for cars. Walk, or take an e-rickshaw to Godowlia and walk from there.
  • The new Kashi ropeway will link Varanasi Cantt station with Godowlia Chowk, 3.75 km in about 16 minutes. Trial runs began in September 2026. News reports in November said it may open by the end of the month, with a fare of about ₹50. Check that it is open before you plan around it.

Things to do in winter

  • A boat ride along the ghats at sunrise or before the evening aarti.
  • Walk the ghats from Assi to Dashashwamedh in the afternoon sun. Manikarnika, the main cremation ghat, is a short walk further on. Don't take photos of cremations.
  • Sarnath, about 10 km away, where the Buddha gave his first sermon. The Sarnath Museum is open from 10 AM to 5 PM and is closed on Fridays.
  • The old lanes for food, Banarasi silk and brassware. Go in the afternoon, when it's warm.
  • Dev Deepawali is on 24 November 2026, when lakhs of lamps light the ghats. It falls before most December trips, so plan for late November if you want to see it.

What to wear in Varanasi in December

What should I wear? Try the what-to-wear helper for day and night temperatures and an outfit list by month.

Varanasi doesn't get freezing like the hills, but mornings and nights near 9–10 °C feel cold by the river.

  • Layers: a light thermal or full-sleeve top, a sweater or fleece, and a medium jacket or shawl for mornings, evenings and boat rides.
  • For ladies: a salwar suit, kurta with leggings, or a saree with a cardigan and a warm shawl works well. Covered shoulders and knees are best for temples.
  • For men: jeans or trousers, a full-sleeve shirt or T-shirt, a sweater and a jacket. A muffler and a woollen cap help on the dawn boat ride.
  • Shoes: comfortable shoes you can slip off quickly, because you remove them at temples. The ghat steps can be wet and slippery, so pick a good grip.
  • A small bag: a cross-body bag is easier in crowded lanes, and you can't take bags into Kashi Vishwanath.

Health and safety

  • Don't swim in the Ganga. The current is strong in places, and the water is very cold in winter.
  • Never sleep with a heater or coal fire in a closed room. It gives off carbon monoxide, which can kill without warning.
  • Watch your step on the ghats. The stone steps are steep and can be wet.
  • Use only registered guides, and agree on boat and rickshaw prices before you start.

For foreign visitors

  • Visa: apply for the e-Tourist Visa only at the official site, indianvisaonline.gov.in.
  • Temple entry rules can differ for foreign visitors. Check the official Kashi Vishwanath site before you go.

Emergency numbers and help

  • Emergency (police, fire, ambulance): 112
  • Ministry of Tourism helpline, 24 hours, many languages: 1363 or 1800-11-1363

Questions

What is the temperature in Varanasi in December?

IMD's long-term averages are about 24 °C in the day and 10 °C at night. The coldest December night on record was 2.2 °C. January is a little colder, at about 22 °C by day and 9 °C at night.

How many days are enough for Varanasi?

Two days are enough for Kashi Vishwanath, a ghat walk, the evening aarti, a sunrise boat ride and Sarnath. Add a third day in winter, as a spare morning in case fog hides the river, and as time for train or flight delays.

What are the Kashi Vishwanath darshan timings?

The temple trust says the temple opens at 2:30 AM and closes at 11 PM. General darshan is from 4 AM to 11 AM and from 12 noon to 7 PM. Mangala Aarti, from 3 AM to 4 AM, is for ticket holders. Timings can change on festival days and in the New Year rush.

Is December a good time to visit Varanasi?

Yes. December is dry, with sunny afternoons, and it's a comfortable time to walk the ghats. Late December brings fog and cold mornings, and Christmas to New Year is very crowded, so book early and keep spare time for train or flight delays.

What should I wear in Varanasi in December?

Layers: a full-sleeve top, a sweater or fleece, and a jacket or shawl for mornings, evenings and boat rides. Ladies can wear a salwar suit or kurta with a warm shawl. Wear shoes that are easy to take off at temples.

What should ladies wear in Varanasi in winter?

A salwar suit, a kurta with leggings, or a saree, with a cardigan and a warm shawl. Covered shoulders and knees are best for temples. Add a cap or scarf for the dawn boat ride.

What time is the Ganga aarti in Varanasi in winter?

The evening aarti at Dashashwamedh Ghat follows sunset, so in winter it starts around 6 PM. Arrive about 45 minutes early. The morning programme at Assi Ghat starts around dawn.

Is there fog in Varanasi in December?

Often, especially in the last two weeks of December and in January. Fog delays trains and morning flights, so check the status before you leave and keep spare time.

Is Varanasi crowded at New Year?

Very. More than 30 lakh devotees visited Kashi Vishwanath between 24 December 2025 and 1 January 2026, according to news reports. Book hotels early, and go to the temple very early in the morning.

Varanasi or Rishikesh in December?

Both have a famous Ganga aarti. Varanasi is about the ghats, the old city and its temples. Rishikesh has hills, yoga and rafting, and is closer to Delhi. See our Rishikesh in December guide.
