Ooty in December and January: weather, a 2–3 day trip plan and what to wear

Updated 22 Nov 2026 · 13 min read

Ooty at a glance

Weather in DecemberAbout 21 °C by day and 7 °C at night (IMD averages)
JanuaryDrier, with colder nights: about 21 °C and 6 °C
SnowNo. You may see ground frost on cold mornings
How long2 days for Ooty and Coonoor, 3 with Pykara or the toy train
Toy trainMettupalayam–Coonoor–Ooty, a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Stops after landslides, so check first
E-passNeeded for cars from outside the Nilgiris (epass.tnega.org)
Get thereFly to Coimbatore (CJB), about 105 km away, or drive from Bangalore (about 290 km)
Emergency100 police, 108 ambulance

Ooty in December and January is cool by day and cold at night. December still gets some rain from the north-east monsoon. January is dry and sunny, with the coldest mornings of the year. This guide covers the weather, the frost (Ooty doesn't get snow), a simple 2-day and 3-day plan, the trip from Bangalore, the toy train, the e-pass and plastic rules, and what to wear.

Ooty weather in December and January

Long-term averages for Ooty (Udhagamandalam) from the India Meteorological Department (IMD, 1991–2020):

MonthDayNightRain
DecemberAbout 21 °CAbout 7 °CAbout 50 mm
JanuaryAbout 21 °CAbout 6.5 °CAbout 5 mm
FebruaryAbout 22 °CAbout 7 °CAbout 5 mm
  • January has the coldest nights. The lowest January reading on record is −5.1 °C. The lowest in December is −1.1 °C.
  • December can still be wet. It rains on about 3 days in an average December. January and February are almost dry.
  • Days are pleasant, nights are cold. The sun is warm at midday, but it gets cold fast after 4 PM. Dress in layers.
  • Ooty is high. The town is about 2,240 m above sea level, a little higher than Darjeeling.

Does it snow in Ooty?

  • No, Ooty doesn't get snow. What you see on cold mornings is ground frost: ice on the grass, fields and car roofs.
  • Frost comes on clear, cold nights in December and January. In December 2025, news reports said Thalaikundha fell to −2 °C and the Botanical Garden to −1 °C, and nights stayed near 0 °C for more than ten days.
  • The best frost spots are open, low grounds. Reports name the racecourse area (Kuthirapanthaya), Kandal, Thalaikundha and Avalanche.
  • Go early. The frost lasts from about 6 AM to 8 AM and melts after sunrise.

So go to Ooty for cool weather, tea gardens and frosty mornings, not for snow. If snow matters most, choose Kashmir, Manali or Shimla.

Ooty trip plan: 2 days

Ooty's sights are spread over the hills, so hire a taxi or an auto for the day. Start early: the sun is warm by mid-morning, and it gets cold fast after 4 PM.

Day 1: Ooty town

  1. Government Botanical Garden in the morning. It was laid out in 1848 on the slopes of Doddabetta, and has a fossil tree and an Italian-style garden.
  2. Rose Garden, a short ride away. Check timings and fees at the gate of both gardens.
  3. Ooty Lake after lunch, for a boat ride from the TTDC boat house.

Day 2: Doddabetta, tea and Coonoor

  1. Frost at dawn, on a clear, cold morning in December or January: the racecourse area or the Avalanche road, from about 6 AM to 8 AM.
  2. Doddabetta, Tamil Nadu's highest peak at 2,623 m, about 10 km from Ooty. Go early for clear views.
  3. A tea factory and tea gardens along the Coonoor road.
  4. Coonoor: Sims Park, and Dolphin's Nose (about 12 km from Coonoor) for the valley view. Drive back before dark.

Ooty trip plan: 3 days

Add one of these:

  • The toy train. Ride the Ooty–Coonoor section, or the whole line down to Mettupalayam. Book on IRCTC well ahead, check the times there, and check the status the day before.
  • Avalanche Lake, about 28 km from Ooty, past Emerald Lake, in thick forest.
  • Pykara lake and falls. Pykara is on the Gudalur road, so it fits well on the day you drive back to Mysore or Bangalore.

Driving from Bangalore? The road takes a good part of a day each way, so a 2-day trip leaves little time in Ooty. Plan 3 days, or 2 nights, if you can.

The Ooty toy train

The Nilgiri Mountain Railway runs 46 km from Mettupalayam, through Coonoor, to Ooty. Between Kallar and Coonoor it climbs on a rack (a toothed rail), as steep as 1 in 12. It passes 16 tunnels and about 250 bridges. Since 2005 it has been a UNESCO World Heritage Site, as one of the Mountain Railways of India.

  • The morning train (56136) leaves Mettupalayam at 7:10 AM and reaches Ooty around noon. It waits for the overnight Nilgiri Express from Chennai. Check the latest timings on IRCTC.
  • Book on IRCTC, and book early. Seats are few, and Christmas and New Year sell out.
  • Landslides stop the train in the rainy months. This season it was suspended from 24 October to 7 November 2026, and Mettupalayam–Coonoor was cancelled again from 8 to 10 November. On 2 January 2026, rain stopped the Coonoor–Ooty section too. Check the status the day before you travel.
  • Short joy rides between Ooty and Ketti have run on some winter holidays, such as Christmas and New Year's Eve in 2023. Watch for announcements.

Getting to Ooty

  • By air: Coimbatore (CJB) is the nearest airport, about 105 km away. By road, Ooty is about 89 km from Coimbatore.
  • By train: take a train to Mettupalayam or Coimbatore, then the toy train or the road up via Coonoor.
  • From Bangalore: about 290 km by road, via Mysore. See Ooty trip from Bangalore below for the car, bus and train options.
  • The Kalhatti (Kallatty) ghat road via Masinagudi is steep, with many hairpin bends. In April 2026 the district said tourist vehicles may not use it, and a later court order allowed a small number by day only. Use the Gudalur or Mettupalayam roads unless you have checked the latest rule.
  • E-pass: cars, bikes and buses from outside the Nilgiris need an e-pass, from the official site epass.tnega.org. Vehicles are checked at entry points such as Kallar and Masinagudi. On busy days the number of vehicles may be limited, so apply before you leave.

Ooty trip from Bangalore: by car, bus or train

Ooty is about 290 km from Bangalore, and Mysore is 160 km from Ooty via Gudalur, according to the Nilgiris district.

  • By car: the usual road goes from Bangalore to Mysore, then through the Bandipur and Mudumalai forests to Gudalur, and up the ghat road to Ooty. The forest roads are closed to traffic at night, from 9 PM to 6 AM, so leave Bangalore early and cross in daylight. Apply for the e-pass before you leave, and don't take the Kalhatti ghat road via Masinagudi unless you have checked the latest rule.
  • By bus: the district says regular buses connect Ooty with Bangalore and Mysore. Book state buses on their official sites: KSRTC (Karnataka) or TNSTC (Tamil Nadu). Bus passengers don't need an e-pass.
  • By train: no train runs from Bangalore to Ooty. The only railway up the hill is the toy train from Mettupalayam. Take a train from Bangalore to Coimbatore, then go up by road (89 km), or carry on to Mettupalayam for the morning toy train. It leaves at 7:10 AM, so you need to be in Mettupalayam early. Check trains and times on IRCTC.
  • On the way back: stop at Pykara lake and falls on the Gudalur road, and watch for wild animals in the forests. Don't stop to feed or photograph them from close by.

Plastic rules in the Nilgiris

The Nilgiris has strict plastic rules, and vehicles are checked at the entry points:

  • 19 single-use items are banned, including plastic carry bags, plastic cups, straws and spoons, paper cups and plates, and thermocol.
  • Water and soft drinks in plastic bottles of 1 to 4 litres are banned, by order of the Madras High Court.
  • Carry a refillable steel bottle. Water ATMs are at the Boat House, Rose Garden, Botanical Garden, Doddabetta, Sims Park and other spots.

Things to do in winter

  • Government Botanical Garden and the Rose Garden, in town. Check timings and fees at the gate.
  • Ooty Lake for boating, run from the TTDC boat house.
  • Doddabetta, at 2,623 m the highest peak in Tamil Nadu, about 10 km from Ooty. Go early for clear views.
  • Avalanche Lake (about 28 km, past Emerald Lake) and Pykara lake and falls.
  • Tea gardens and tea factories along the Coonoor road.
  • Coonoor: Sims Park, and Dolphin's Nose (about 12 km from Coonoor) for valley views.
  • Frost at dawn at the racecourse or on the Avalanche road. Some dam areas are closed, so watch from the roadside.

Christmas and New Year in Ooty

  • Book early. Ooty is busy from Christmas to New Year, with visitors from Bangalore, Chennai and Kerala.
  • Expect traffic and queues at the entry checkposts and on the ghat roads. Leave early in the morning.
  • Have your e-pass ready before you reach the district border.

What to wear in Ooty in December

What should I wear? Try the what-to-wear helper for day and night temperatures and an outfit list by month.

Ooty's days are mild, but mornings and nights can fall to near 0 °C.

  • Layers: a thermal or full-sleeve top, a sweater or fleece, and a warm jacket for evenings and early mornings.
  • For frost at dawn and Doddabetta: your warmest jacket, a woollen cap, gloves and a muffler.
  • For ladies: jeans or leggings with a kurta or top, a warm cardigan or jacket, and a stole. Dresses work by day with tights and a jacket.
  • For rain in December: a light rain jacket or umbrella.
  • Shoes: comfortable walking shoes with a good grip. Paths are wet and slippery in the mornings.
  • Sunscreen and sunglasses: the sun is strong at this height.

Health and safety

  • Never sleep with a heater or coal fire in a closed room. It gives off carbon monoxide, which can kill without warning.
  • Drive slowly on the ghat roads, use low gear going down, and don't drive at night if you can avoid it.
  • Don't feed or go near wild animals, especially on the forest roads through Mudumalai and Bandipur.

For foreign visitors

  • Visa: apply for the e-Tourist Visa only at the official site, indianvisaonline.gov.in.

Emergency numbers and help

From the Nilgiris district website:

  • Police: 100
  • Ambulance: 108
  • Fire: 101
  • District disaster control room: 1077
  • Tourist Officer, Ooty: (0423) 2443977
  • Ministry of Tourism helpline, 24 hours, many languages: 1363 or 1800-11-1363

Questions

Does it snow in Ooty in December?

No. Ooty doesn't get snow. On clear, cold mornings in December and January you can see ground frost on the grass, which melts soon after sunrise. In December 2025, some spots fell to −2 °C.

What is the temperature in Ooty in December?

IMD's long-term averages are about 21 °C in the day and 7 °C at night. The coldest December night on record was −1.1 °C. January nights are a little colder, and the record is −5.1 °C.

What should I wear in Ooty in December?

Layers: a thermal or full-sleeve top, a sweater or fleece, and a warm jacket, plus a woollen cap and gloves for early mornings. Carry a light rain jacket, because December can still bring showers.

How many days are enough for Ooty?

Two days are enough for Ooty town, Doddabetta, a tea factory and Coonoor. Add a third day for the toy train, Avalanche Lake or Pykara. If you're driving from Bangalore, plan 3 days, because the road takes a good part of a day each way.

How do I go from Bangalore to Ooty?

By road, about 290 km via Mysore, Bandipur, Mudumalai and Gudalur. The forest roads are closed from 9 PM to 6 AM, so drive in daylight, and get the e-pass first. Regular buses also run from Bangalore. There's no train to Ooty from Bangalore: go to Coimbatore by train, then up by road or the toy train from Mettupalayam.

Is December a good time to visit Ooty?

Yes, if you like cool weather. Days are pleasant and the hills are green after the rains. Some rain is still possible, and the toy train can stop after landslides. January is drier, with the coldest, frostiest mornings.

Is the Ooty toy train running in December?

It usually runs, but it stops after landslides in the rainy months. This season it was suspended from 24 October to 7 November 2026, and again on part of the line from 8 to 10 November. Check the status on IRCTC the day before.

Do I need an e-pass for Ooty?

Yes, if your vehicle is from outside the Nilgiris district. Apply on the official site, epass.tnega.org, before you travel. Public buses and Nilgiris-registered vehicles don't need one.

Can I carry plastic water bottles to Ooty?

Not big ones. Plastic water and soft-drink bottles of 1 to 4 litres are banned in the Nilgiris, along with plastic bags and other single-use items. Carry a steel bottle and refill it at the water ATMs.

Ooty or Munnar in December?

Both are cool tea-hill towns with cold nights. Ooty has the toy train and is easier to reach from Bangalore and Coimbatore. Munnar is better if you're also seeing Kerala's backwaters. See our Munnar in December guide.

Ooty or Coorg in December?

Ooty is higher and colder, with frosty mornings and the toy train. Coorg is milder, about 25 °C by day and 13 °C at night, with coffee estates, waterfalls and homestays. See Coorg in December.
