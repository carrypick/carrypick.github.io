Azerbaijan trip from India: the e-visa, a 5-day Baku plan and Baku in December

Updated 21 Nov 2026 · 13 min read

Azerbaijan, on the Caspian Sea, has become a short trip abroad for Indians: the old walled city of Baku next to glass towers, the Flame Towers lit up at night, fire temples and burning hillsides, mud volcanoes and mountain villages. Indians apply for an e-visa online, and a standard one takes 3 working days. This guide covers the visa, flights, a 5-day plan, Baku's weather by month, adding Georgia, getting around and food for vegetarians.

Azerbaijan visa for Indians

Indians need a visa for Azerbaijan, and India is on the list of countries that can get an e-visa online. Facts below are from Azerbaijan's official e-visa portal (ASAN Visa), read on 21 Nov 2026.

  • Apply only on the official portal, evisa.gov.az. Other websites look official and charge extra.
  • Standard or urgent: a standard e-visa is issued within 3 working days, and an urgent one within 3 hours, regardless of holidays. Apply for a standard e-visa at least 3 working days before you arrive.
  • What you get: a single-entry e-visa, valid for 90 days, with permission to stay up to 30 days.
  • Your passport must be valid for at least 3 months after the e-visa expires.
  • The fee is paid online by Visa or Mastercard (the card doesn't have to be in your name). It's shown on the portal when you apply, and it isn't refunded if the visa is refused.
  • Print the e-visa and show it with your passport at the border. It isn't stuck in your passport.
  • A family application can cover 2 to 10 people.
  • With a US, UK or Schengen visa: the portal and the conditions page say nothing about a different rule for holders of these visas, so apply for the e-visa as usual.
  • You can't get the e-visa on arrival. Apply before you travel.

Staying more than 15 days: register

  • If you stay in Azerbaijan for more than 15 days, you must be registered with the State Migration Service, says the e-visa portal's conditions page. A hotel registers you; if you stay in an apartment, the owner must do it. Registration is free.
  • The portal's FAQ says 10 days, so if you're staying more than 10 days, ask your hotel or host to register you to be safe.
  • India's embassy in Baku also advises Indians to carry a copy of their medical insurance, and not to carry metal acupressure items, knuckle-dusters or knives (travel advisory, read 21 Nov 2026).

Flights from India to Azerbaijan

  • Direct flights to Baku (GYD) weren't confirmed when we checked. Baku airport's airline list (Wikipedia, read 21 Nov 2026) shows IndiGo from Delhi and Azerbaijan Airlines from Delhi and Mumbai, but IndiGo halted its Baku flights in February 2026 because of Iran tensions, said a news report cited there, and the Azerbaijan Airlines entries have no source. Check the airline's own website before you plan around a direct flight.
  • One-stop flights to Baku go through Dubai (Azerbaijan Airlines), Sharjah or Abu Dhabi (Air Arabia) or Istanbul (Turkish Airlines, Azerbaijan Airlines and Pegasus), among others.
  • Air India doesn't fly to Baku, so we don't show it below.
  • A layover in Dubai or Istanbul can be short, but check whether you need a transit visa if you change airports or stay overnight.
  • India is 1 hour 30 minutes ahead of Azerbaijan. A flight that lands at 4 AM in Baku lands at 5:30 AM India time.
  • Baggage: low-cost fares to the Gulf often include only a cabin bag. Check the limits by airline in the international baggage rules.
  • Fares go up for Christmas, New Year and Indian school holidays. Book early.

A 5-day Azerbaijan itinerary: Baku, Gobustan and Absheron

Most first trips from India spend 4 to 6 days, with Baku as the base. This plan keeps the day trips on separate days.

DayPlanEvening
1Land in Baku and rest. Walk the seaside Baku BoulevardThe Flame Towers lit up, seen from the boulevard
2The Old City (İçərişəhər): the Maiden Tower and the Palace of the Shirvanshahs, then Fountains Square and Nizami StreetHighland Park (Dagüstü Park) for the view over the bay
3Gobustan: the rock carvings and the mud volcanoes, south of BakuBack in Baku; the Heydar Aliyev Center at night
4The Absheron peninsula: the Ateshgah fire temple and Yanar Dag, the hillside where a fire burns all the timeBack in Baku
5Shopping and a last walk, then fly home
  • The Ateshgah of Baku is an old fire temple with inscriptions in Sanskrit and Punjabi, left by Indian traders who stayed there. It's a short drive from the city.
  • Six or seven days? Add a day trip to the mountains: Quba and the Shahdag area in the north, or Gabala, with cable cars and forests. Sheki, with its old palace, is farther and better with a night there.
  • The Old City is a UNESCO World Heritage Site, with lanes, caravanserais and carpet shops inside its walls.
  • Go with a driver or a tour for Gobustan and the mud volcanoes: the last part is a rough track.
  • Dress for mosques. Cover your shoulders and knees; women may be asked to cover their hair.

Baku with Georgia

Many trips from India combine Baku with Georgia's Tbilisi.

  • Fly between Baku and Tbilisi: Azerbaijan Airlines flies the route, says Baku airport's airline list.
  • We couldn't confirm on an official page whether Azerbaijan's land borders are open to tourists, so plan to fly unless your tour company confirms the road route.
  • You need both visas: an Azerbaijan e-visa and a Georgian e-visa, or no Georgian visa if you hold a valid US, UK, EU or Schengen visa or residence permit. See our Georgia trip guide.

  • Baku is dry, with little rain in any month, but it's known as a windy city. Winter wind makes it feel colder than the numbers.
  • December in Baku is cool, not snowy: about 10 °C by day. For snow, go up to the mountains around Shahdag or Gabala, but check the roads and the resorts' opening dates before you go.
  • July and August are hot, above 30 °C by day.

Getting around Azerbaijan

  • In Baku, use the BakuCard, a travel card for the metro and city buses, sold at machines in stations.
  • The Baku Metro links the city centre, the Old City (İçərişəhər station) and the railway station.
  • Taxis: use a ride-hailing app or official taxis, and check the price first.
  • Outside Baku, hire a car with a driver or take a tour; the sights are spread out.
  • Learn a few words. Azerbaijani is the main language; Russian is widely understood, and many young people speak English. A translation app helps.

Food for Indian travellers: veg and Jain tips

Azerbaijani food is meat-heavy, but there are good vegetarian dishes:

  • Qutab: a thin folded flatbread filled with greens (göyərti qutabı), pumpkin or cheese. Ask for the non-meat ones.
  • Salads and starters: tomato and cucumber salads, aubergine dishes, yoghurt, cheese and fresh bread.
  • Dolma: stuffed vine leaves or vegetables, usually with meat, but some places make a vegetable version. Ask first.
  • Plov: rice dishes, often with meat; ask for a version with dried fruit and chestnuts only.
  • Sweets: pakhlava and shekerbura, often with nuts, and tea with jam. Tea is part of daily life.
  • Indian restaurants: Baku has Indian restaurants serving vegetarian food.
  • Jain travellers: most dishes use garlic and onion. Carry ready-to-eat food or khakhra, and check what you can take in our can I carry it on a flight? list.

Phone, SIM and eSIM in Azerbaijan

You have three ways to stay online in Azerbaijan:

  • Roaming on your own SIM: the easiest, but often the costliest. Check your mobile company's international pack before you fly.
  • A local SIM card: sold at the airport and in phone shops. Shops ask for your passport to register it.
  • An eSIM: a data plan you buy online and install on your phone before you go, with no plastic card. Your phone must support eSIM and must not be locked to one network. On an iPhone, look in Settings > Mobile Service (or Cellular). On Android, look in Settings > SIM or Connections.

Install the eSIM at home over Wi-Fi and switch it on when you land. Most travel eSIMs give data only, with no local number, so keep your home SIM on for calls and bank OTP texts (roaming charges may apply).

Money and rules to know

  • The currency is the Azerbaijani manat (₼, AZN). Cards work in hotels, malls and most restaurants in Baku. Carry some cash for markets, taxis and villages.
  • Cash from India: RBI lets you buy up to US$3,000 in foreign currency notes per trip, and you can carry up to ₹25,000 in Indian notes. Put the rest on a forex card. See how much cash you can carry abroad.
  • Carry your passport or a copy; police can ask for ID.
  • Carry a copy of your medical insurance, as India's embassy in Baku advises.

What to wear in Azerbaijan

  • Winter (December to February): a warm, windproof jacket, a sweater or fleece, gloves and a cap for Baku's wind. Add thermal innerwear and snow boots for the mountains.
  • Spring and autumn: layers, a light jacket and comfortable walking shoes for the Old City's stone lanes.
  • Summer: light, breathable clothes, sunglasses and sunscreen.
  • For mosques: a scarf for women, and long trousers or a long skirt for everyone.

  • Embassy of India, Baku: contact details are on its website.
  • Keep a photo of your passport, your e-visa and your insurance papers on your phone, and save your hotel's address.

Questions

Do Indians need a visa for Azerbaijan?

Yes. Indians need a visa, but India is on the list of countries that can apply for an e-visa online on Azerbaijan's official portal, evisa.gov.az, before they travel.

How long does an Azerbaijan e-visa take for Indians?

A standard e-visa is issued within 3 working days, and an urgent e-visa within 3 hours, regardless of holidays, says the official portal. Apply for a standard e-visa at least 3 working days before you arrive.

How long can Indians stay in Azerbaijan on an e-visa?

Up to 30 days, says the official portal. The e-visa is valid for 90 days and is single entry. If you stay more than 15 days, you must be registered with the State Migration Service.

What is the Azerbaijan visa fee for Indians?

The fee is shown on evisa.gov.az when you apply and is paid by Visa or Mastercard. It isn't refunded if the visa is refused. We haven't listed an amount because we couldn't read it on the portal without starting an application.

Can Indians with a US visa enter Azerbaijan without a visa?

The official e-visa portal doesn't mention any such rule. Apply for the Azerbaijan e-visa as usual.

Are there direct flights from India to Baku?

We couldn't confirm it. Baku airport's list shows IndiGo from Delhi and Azerbaijan Airlines from Delhi and Mumbai, but IndiGo halted its Baku flights in February 2026, said a news report. Check the airlines' own websites, or fly one-stop via Dubai, Sharjah, Abu Dhabi or Istanbul.

How many days are enough for Azerbaijan?

Four to five days is enough for Baku, Gobustan and the Absheron fire sites. Add one or two days for the mountains (Quba, Shahdag or Gabala), or a few days in Georgia.

Does Baku have snow in December?

Rarely in the city: Baku averages about 10 °C by day and 5 °C at night in December, on the national weather service's averages. For snow, go up to the mountains around Shahdag or Gabala.

What is the time difference between India and Azerbaijan?

India is 1 hour 30 minutes ahead of Azerbaijan.
