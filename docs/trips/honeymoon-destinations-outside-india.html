Honeymoon destinations outside India: the best trips abroad for Indian couples

Updated 23 Nov 2026 · 12 min read

Most Indian weddings fall between November and February, and many couples want their honeymoon abroad. The good news: some of the most romantic trips are a short flight away, and several need no visa at all for an Indian passport. This page compares the best international honeymoon destinations from India by month, visa, flight time and style, with links to our full trip guide for each country.

Every fact here comes from our trip guides and our international trips hub, where the official source is listed with the date we checked it. Visa rules change often, so check our visa guide for Indian passport holders before you book. Prefer to stay in India? See our honeymoon destinations in India.

Packing for the trip? Start with our honeymoon packing list: documents, outfits for photos, what the bride and groom should pack, and what changes for a beach or a hill honeymoon.

Honeymoon destinations abroad by month

November

Thailand (November to February) and Dubai (November to March) start their best season. Mauritius is at the end of its best months (May to November), and Nepal and Bhutan have clear mountain views from October to December.

December

  • Beach: the Maldives has warm sea and short showers on about 12 days, at the start of the drier months. Thailand is in its cool, dry season. Mauritius is warm, about 31 °C by day, with rain on about 6 days in Port Louis. The Seychelles are warm, about 30 °C, with short showers on most days. Malaysia is good for Langkawi and Penang, but avoid the east coast islands, where the monsoon is at its peak.
  • City and desert: Dubai has warm days and cool evenings, the best time of year there; nights are about 14–15 °C.
  • Hills and beaches: Sri Lanka has some rain on the west coast in December, and is drier in January. Vietnam is dry and warm in the south and cool in Hanoi.
  • Cold and snowy: Europe has Christmas markets and snow in the Swiss Alps; Zurich averages about −2 °C at night. Nepal and Bhutan have clear views and sunny days but very cold nights. Turkey is in its low season: Istanbul about 11 °C and rainy, Cappadocia below freezing at night.
  • Less ideal: Bali is in its wet season, with rain on most days, and Singapore has its wettest month, with heavy but short showers.

Christmas to New Year is the busiest and costliest week everywhere, so book early. Fares also go up for the wedding season, so honeymooners should book flights as soon as the dates are fixed.

January

The Maldives and Sri Lanka both start their best months (January to April, and January to March on Sri Lanka's west and south coasts). Thailand, Dubai, Malaysia (Langkawi and Penang) and southern Vietnam are still in season. Mauritius is wetter in January than in December, and Bali is still in its wet season.

February

The Maldives and Sri Lanka are in their best months, and Singapore starts its best season (February to April). February is the last of the best months in Thailand and Langkawi in Malaysia, and Dubai is in season until March. In Vietnam, Hoi An's best months start in February.

Easiest visas for an Indian honeymoon

Right after a wedding there's little time for a visa office. These are the simplest for an Indian passport right now, from our visa guide:

  • No visa at all: Nepal, Bhutan (with an entry permit and a daily Sustainable Development Fee) Mauritius (carry a return ticket and a hotel booking, and fill in the free travel form online) and the Seychelles (a free visitor's permit on arrival, but a paid Travel Authorisation online before you fly).
  • No visa for a short tourist stay: Thailand (up to 30 days) and Malaysia (up to 30 days, until 31 December 2026).
  • A free visa online, or a visa on arrival: Sri Lanka (free ETA), the Maldives and Bali (visa on arrival; Bali also sells it online as an e-VOA).
  • Apply before you fly: Vietnam (e-visa online), Dubai (through a tourism company, airline or hotel), Singapore (through an agent in India), Turkey (a Turkish mission, or an e-visa if you hold a US, UK, Schengen or Irish visa) and Europe (a Schengen visa, applied for at least 15 days before you fly).

Name on the passport: the name on your ticket must match the name in the passport exactly. If the bride's passport still has her old name, book the ticket in that name (honeymoon packing list). Rules change, and the officer at the airport decides on entry, so check the trip guide before you book.

Shortest flights for a honeymoon abroad

After the wedding, a short flight means more days on the beach and less time in airports.

  • Under 3 hours: Nepal (about 1½ hours from Delhi), Bhutan (about 2 hours), Sri Lanka and the Maldives (about 2½ hours from Mumbai).
  • 3 to 5 hours: Dubai, Thailand, Vietnam, Malaysia and the Seychelles (about 4½ hours from Mumbai).
  • 5 to 7½ hours: Singapore, Turkey, Mauritius and Bali.
  • 8 hours or more: Europe, about 8 to 9 hours from Delhi to Zurich or Paris.

Honeymoon destinations abroad by style

Beach and islands

  • Maldives: India's favourite beach honeymoon abroad. Choose a private resort island with beach and overwater villas for privacy, or a guesthouse on a local island near Malé to save money. Check the meal plan, the transfer and the green tax before you pay, and tell the resort it's your honeymoon when you book.
  • Mauritius: blue lagoons, resorts on white beaches and a mountain backdrop, with Indian food everywhere and no visa. Le Morne in the south-west is quiet and good for honeymoons; Flic en Flac has sunsets. Many couples stay a week.
  • Seychelles: the boulder beaches of La Digue, the Vallée de Mai on Praslin and quiet resorts on Mahé, a direct flight from Mumbai with no visa (get the Travel Authorisation online first). Spend the week on two or three islands, linked by ferries.
  • Bali: pool villas and rice terraces in Ubud, and beach sunsets in Seminyak and Uluwatu. Split your stay between the two. Best from April to October; December to February is wet.
  • Thailand: islands and beaches in Phuket and Krabi, Bangkok's temples, and no visa for up to 30 days.
  • Malaysia: Langkawi's beaches, with direct IndiGo flights from Bengaluru to Langkawi.

Hills, nature and culture

  • Sri Lanka: tea hills in Nuwara Eliya and Ella, Sigiriya rock, Kandy and south coast beaches, on the same clock as India.
  • Vietnam: cruises in Ha Long Bay's limestone islands and the lantern-lit old town of Hoi An.
  • Bhutan: quiet, scenic and unhurried, with dzongs and mountain passes.
  • Nepal: Pokhara's lake and mountains, and Kathmandu's temples.
  • Turkey: Istanbul's mosques, bazaars and the Bosphorus, then hot air balloons (weather allowing) and cave hotels in Cappadocia.

Snow and mountains

  • Europe: the dream honeymoon for many Indian couples is about 10 days in Switzerland, Paris and Italy, by train, on one Schengen visa. In December there's snow in the Swiss Alps and Christmas markets in the towns.
  • Nepal and Bhutan: Himalayan views and very cold winter nights, a short flight from Delhi.

City breaks

  • Dubai: malls, the desert and a dhow dinner cruise on the creek or at the Marina, a short flight from most big Indian cities.
  • Singapore: gardens, Sentosa and the river cruise at Clarke Quay; many Indians combine it with Malaysia.

Questions

Which is the best honeymoon destination outside India?

The Maldives, Mauritius and Bali are India's favourite honeymoon trips abroad, and Switzerland is the dream for many couples. Thailand's islands and Sri Lanka's hills and beaches are also popular.

Which international honeymoon destinations are best in December?

The Maldives, Thailand, Dubai, Mauritius and Sri Lanka, with Langkawi in Malaysia and southern Vietnam. For snow and Christmas markets, Europe. Bali and Singapore are wetter in December.

What are the best honeymoon places abroad in January and February?

The Maldives and Sri Lanka are at their best from January, and Thailand, Dubai, Malaysia's Langkawi and southern Vietnam are all in season. Singapore's best months start in February.

Which honeymoon destinations near India need no visa?

Nepal and Bhutan need no visa (Bhutan has an entry permit and a daily fee), and Mauritius and the Seychelles need none (the Seychelles ask for a paid Travel Authorisation online first). Thailand needs no visa for up to 30 days, and Malaysia for up to 30 days until 31 December 2026. Sri Lanka has a free ETA online, and the Maldives a visa on arrival.

What is the nearest honeymoon destination abroad from India?

Nepal is the closest, about 1½ hours from Delhi, then Bhutan (about 2 hours), and Sri Lanka and the Maldives (about 2½ hours from Mumbai).

Which international honeymoon is good on a budget?

Short flights and easy visas cost the least: Nepal, Bhutan, Sri Lanka and Thailand. We don't give our own prices, because they change with your dates and hotel. Add your own amounts in our trip budget calculator to compare countries.

Is Bali or the Maldives better for a honeymoon?

The Maldives is a short flight with a visa on arrival, and best from January to April; it's about the beach, privacy and the sea. Bali is a longer flight, also with a visa on arrival, and best from April to October; it adds rice terraces, temples and villas, but December to February is its wet season.
