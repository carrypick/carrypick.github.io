Spiti Valley trip guide: best time, routes, permits and an 8-day plan

Updated 25 Nov 2026 · 12 min read

Spiti Valley at a glance

Best timeJune to September, when both roads are usually open
RoutesShimla–Kinnaur–Kaza (main road, also in winter) and Manali–Atal Tunnel–Kunzum La–Kaza (summer only)
Kunzum LaAbout 4,551 m. In 2026 it opened to light 4x4 vehicles around 20 May (news report)
HeightKibber is about 14,200 ft (4,330 m), and Key monastery about 13,500 ft
PermitsForeigners need a permit for Kinnaur and Spiti's border areas; we found none for Indians
Nearest airportsShimla and Kullu-Manali (small); Chandigarh has more flights
Emergency112

Spiti is a high, dry valley in Himachal Pradesh, between Kinnaur and Ladakh: mud-brick villages, monasteries on cliffs and some of the highest villages in India reached by road. There are two ways in. The road from Shimla through Kinnaur is the main one and the gentler climb, and the road from Manali over Kunzum La opens only in summer. Most people do a circuit, in through Kinnaur and out over Kunzum to Manali. This guide covers the best time, both roads, permits, altitude, an 8-day plan, Spiti in winter and what to pack.

Best time to visit Spiti Valley

  • June to September is the main season. Both roads are usually open, so you can do the full circuit. Days are warm in the sun, and nights are cold.
  • May: the Kinnaur road is open, but Kunzum La may still be shut. In 2026 it opened to light 4x4 vehicles only around 20 May, news reports said.
  • October: clear skies and few tourists, but very cold nights, and the first snow can close Kunzum La for good. Plan to go in and out through Kinnaur.
  • November to April: winter Spiti, by the Kinnaur road only (see below).
  • Weather numbers: we couldn't find IMD averages for Kaza, so we don't give a table. Spiti is far higher than Shimla or Manali, so expect it to be much colder than either, especially at night.

Best time for snowfall: winter, from about December, when only the Kinnaur road is open.

How to reach Spiti Valley

Via Shimla and Kinnaur (the main road)

  • The route: Shimla – Narkanda – Rampur – Reckong Peo (Kinnaur) – Pooh – Nako – Sumdo – Tabo – Kaza, on NH-5 and then NH-505.
  • It's open for most of the year, and it's the only way into Spiti in winter. Landslides and heavy snow can still close it for a day or more, especially in the monsoon and after winter storms.
  • It climbs slowly, over several days, which is much better for your body than going straight up from Manali.
  • Plan 2–3 days from Shimla to Kaza, with nights in Sangla, Kalpa or Nako.

Via Manali, the Atal Tunnel and Kunzum La

  • The route: Manali – Atal Tunnel – Gramphu – Chhatru – Batal – Kunzum La – Losar – Kaza. The Lahaul-Spiti district says Kunzum La is about 4,551 m, and about 60 km from Gramphu.
  • It's a summer road. The district says Kunzum is crossable on foot from about May or June to October or November, depending on the snow.
  • In 2026, BRO reopened the Gramphu–Kaza road over Kunzum to light 4x4 vehicles around 20 May, after about six months, with heavy vehicles allowed later, news reports said. The 2027 opening is to be announced.
  • The Gramphu–Batal stretch is rough, with stream crossings that rise in the afternoon. Cross early, and drive a car with good ground clearance.
  • It's a big jump in height in one day. Manali is at about 2,050 m, and Kaza at about 3,650 m. Leaving Spiti this way is easier than arriving this way.
  • Outside cars: Manali charges a green tax. See our Manali guide for the amount and the official site.

Spiti Valley trip from Delhi

There's no airport or railway in Spiti. From Delhi, take an overnight bus or drive to Shimla or Manali, or fly to Chandigarh and drive from there:

  • Chandigarh (IXC) has the most flights. It's the usual start for the Shimla road.
  • Shimla airport (SLV) is small, with Alliance Air flights from Delhi and Dharamshala on its Wikipedia list.
  • Kullu-Manali airport (KUU) at Bhuntar is also small, and Alliance Air is the only airline on its Wikipedia list. Flights are often cancelled in bad weather.

Spiti Valley permits

Indian citizens: the permit rules on the Kinnaur and Lahaul-Spiti district websites are for foreign nationals. We found no permit for Indians on the Shimla–Kinnaur–Kaza road or the Manali–Kunzum road. Carry your ID, as there are police checkposts. Vehicles going over Rohtang Pass need a separate permit, but the Atal Tunnel route to Spiti doesn't cross Rohtang.

Foreigners need an Inner Line Permit (a protected area permit) for the border stretch between Kinnaur and Spiti:

  • Which places: the Kinnaur district lists places in the Pooh block, such as Nako, Khab, Pooh, Leo and Kaa, and in Spiti, such as Tabo, Dhankar, Poh, Lari and Sumdo. The Lahaul-Spiti district lists Khab, Samdo, Dhankar, Tabo, Kaza, Morang and Dubling.
  • Without the permit, foreigners can visit Kaza and trek from Kaza via Kibber and Parang La, says the Kinnaur district. So coming from Manali to Kaza doesn't need one, but going on to Tabo and Kinnaur does.
  • Group size and validity differ by page. Kinnaur says groups of two or more, through a recognised Indian travel agency, for up to 30 days, at ₹200 a person (page updated July 2023). The Lahaul-Spiti page (updated 2018) says groups of four or more. Ask the issuing office before you plan.
  • Where to get it: the SDM offices at Reckong Peo (Kinnaur) or Kaza (Spiti, 01906-222302), or the District Magistrate at Keylong (01900-222501). Carry your passport, visa and photocopies.
  • Shipki La and Kaurik: Kinnaur says no access is permitted on the roads leading to these border areas from the highway.

Altitude and health in Spiti

  • Spiti is high. The Lahaul-Spiti district puts Key monastery at about 13,500 ft (4,115 m), Kibber at about 14,200 ft (4,330 m) and Losar at 4,085 m.
  • Go up slowly. The district's travel advice is to gain height slowly. Coming in from Shimla over 2–3 days is the gentle way.
  • Rest on your first day in Kaza, drink plenty of water, and avoid alcohol.
  • Warning signs of altitude sickness are headache, nausea, poor sleep and breathlessness. If you're breathless at rest or confused, go down and get medical help.
  • Ask your doctor before the trip about medicine for altitude, especially for children, older travellers and anyone with a heart or lung condition.
  • Carry cash and fuel. Fill up in Reckong Peo or Kaza, as pumps are few. Phone network is patchy in the valley.

Spiti Valley trip plan: 8 days

A circuit in from Shimla through Kinnaur and out over Kunzum La to Manali. Do it from June to September, when Kunzum is usually open. Before June or after the first snow, return the same way through Kinnaur.

Day 1: Shimla to Sangla or Chitkul

  1. Leave Shimla early for the drive along the Sutlej.
  2. Night in Sangla, or in Chitkul at the head of the Baspa valley.

Day 2: Sangla to Kalpa

  1. Short drive to Kalpa above Reckong Peo, with big mountain views.
  2. Foreigners: get your Inner Line Permit at Reckong Peo.

Day 3: Kalpa to Nako

  1. Through Pooh and up the hairpin bends to Nako village and its lake.
  2. Rest and walk slowly. You're now well above 3,000 m.

Day 4: Nako to Kaza, via Tabo and Dhankar

  1. Tabo monastery and its old mud-walled temples.
  2. Dhankar monastery on its cliff, then on to Kaza.

Day 5: rest day in Kaza

  1. Take it easy around Kaza market and the monastery.
  2. Short walks only.

Day 6: Key, Kibber, Langza and Hikkim

  1. Key monastery in the morning.
  2. Kibber, then the villages of Langza and Hikkim in the afternoon.

Day 7: Kaza to Chandratal

  1. Drive up to Losar and over Kunzum La.
  2. Chandratal lake, about 21 km from Kunzum. Stay in a camp near the lake, as nights are very cold.

Day 8: Chandratal to Manali

  1. Leave early to cross the Batal–Chhatru streams before they rise.
  2. Through the Atal Tunnel to Manali by evening.

Spare day: keep one for landslides or a closed road, especially in July and August.

Things to do in Spiti

  • Monasteries: Key, Tabo, Dhankar and Kaza. Dress modestly and ask before you take photos inside.
  • High villages: Kibber, Langza and Hikkim, all reached by road from Kaza.
  • Chandratal lake near Kunzum La, in the summer months only.
  • Stargazing: Spiti's dry, clear nights are good for it, away from village lights.
  • Short treks and homestays: stay with a family in a village, and go with a local guide on longer walks.

Spiti in winter

Going between December and February? Read our full Spiti in winter guide: the Kinnaur road, IMD winter temperatures, snow leopards, homestays and a 7-day winter route.

  • Only the Kinnaur road is open. Kunzum La is shut by snow, so you go in and out through Shimla and Kinnaur.
  • It's extremely cold, well below freezing at night. Water pipes freeze, and many hotels and camps close, so stay in homestays that stay open in winter.
  • Roads can close after snow for a day or more. Keep 2–3 spare days, and travel with a local driver who knows the road in winter.
  • Chandratal and Kunzum are out of reach in winter.
  • Pack for serious cold: see our snow trip packing list.

What to wear in Spiti

Spiti is warm in the sun, but cold in the shade, at night and on the passes, even in July. Pack layers:

LayerWhat to wear
1. Next to your skinQuick-dry T-shirts by day, and a thermal set for nights
2. MiddleA fleece or warm sweater
3. OutsideA windproof jacket; a light down jacket for Chandratal, Kunzum and October
4. ExtrasWoollen cap, gloves, warm socks, sunglasses with UV protection, sunscreen and lip balm
  • Sun protection: the sun is very strong at this height, so wear a cap, sunglasses and sunscreen every day.
  • Shoes: walking shoes with a good grip for the villages and monasteries.
  • In winter: dress for snow, with a heavy down jacket and insulated boots.

Emergency numbers

  • Emergency (police, fire, ambulance): 112
  • SDM Spiti, Kaza: 01906-222302
  • District Magistrate, Lahaul and Spiti, Keylong: 01900-222501
  • Ministry of Tourism helpline, 24 hours, many languages: 1363 or 1800-11-1363

Questions

What is the best time to visit Spiti Valley?

June to September, when both the Kinnaur road and the Manali–Kunzum La road are usually open, so you can do the full circuit. October is clear but very cold, and in winter only the Kinnaur road is open.

Is Spiti Valley open in winter?

Yes, by the Shimla–Kinnaur road only. Kunzum La is closed by snow, and roads can close for a day or more after snowfall, so keep spare days.

When does Kunzum Pass open?

It depends on the snow. In 2026, BRO reopened it to light 4x4 vehicles around 20 May, news reports said. The 2027 date is to be announced.

Do Indians need a permit for Spiti?

We found no permit for Indian citizens on either road. The district permit rules are for foreigners, who need an Inner Line Permit for the border stretch between Kinnaur and Spiti, including Nako and Tabo.

How many days are enough for Spiti Valley?

About 8 days for the full circuit from Shimla to Manali, plus a spare day for road closures. Going in and out through Kinnaur takes about the same.

Which route is better for Spiti, Shimla or Manali?

Go in from Shimla through Kinnaur: the road climbs slowly, which is better for altitude. Come out over Kunzum La to Manali, if it's open.
