Georgia trip from India: the e-visa, a 6-day plan and Georgia in December

Updated 21 Nov 2026 · 14 min read

Georgia, the small country between the Black Sea and the Caucasus mountains, has become a popular trip abroad for Indians: old churches on mountain tops, the old town of Tbilisi, wine villages, and snow and skiing in winter. Indians apply for a Georgian e-visa online, and those with a valid US, UK, EU or Schengen visa or residence permit don't need one at all. This guide covers the visa, flights, a 6-day plan, Georgia in December, skiing at Gudauri, getting around and food for vegetarians.

Georgia visa for Indians

Most Indians need a Georgian e-visa, applied for online before the trip. Facts below are from Georgia's official e-visa portal, read on 21 Nov 2026.

  • Apply only on the official portal, evisa.gov.ge. India is on its list. It is for short visits of less than 90 days.
  • The fee is US$20 plus a 2% service fee, paid by Visa or Mastercard. It isn't refunded if the visa is refused.
  • There are two steps, and both are mandatory. After the e-visa application, complete a "DuVerify" application within 24 hours. It costs US$15 more per person, and the link comes by email.
  • The decision takes at least 5 working days, so apply well before you fly. Print the e-visa and carry it.
  • Documents to upload: a photo, your passport, your hotel booking, a return flight ticket, travel insurance and proof that you have enough money for the trip.
  • Your passport must be valid for at least 3 months after the visa ends, have at least 2 blank pages and have been issued in the last 10 years.
  • Every traveller needs their own e-visa, children too.
  • Carry health or accident insurance for your whole stay. The portal warns that you can be refused entry if you can't show it at the border.
  • Total stay: no more than 90 days in any 180 days, says the portal's FAQ.

Visa-free with a US, UK, EU or Schengen visa

If you hold a valid visa or residence permit of certain countries, you don't need a Georgian visa, under Georgia's Government Ordinance No. 256 (as amended up to 17 April 2025).

  • You can stay up to 90 days in any 180 days without a Georgian visa.
  • The countries include the USA, the UK, all EU and Schengen countries, Switzerland, Norway, Canada, Australia, New Zealand, Japan, South Korea, Israel, the UAE, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Bahrain and Oman. The ordinance lists 50 in all.
  • Your visa or residence permit must be valid on the day you cross the border. Carry the passport that has it.
  • Not sure? Read the ordinance on Georgia's official legal site or ask the Embassy of Georgia in New Delhi before you book.

Flights from India to Georgia

  • There were no direct flights from India when we checked. IndiGo flew Delhi to Tbilisi daily from August 2024, but India's Ministry of External Affairs said in July 2026 that the flight is "currently suspended". No Indian airline was on Tbilisi airport's list of airlines (Wikipedia, read 21 Nov 2026).
  • One-stop flights to Tbilisi (TBS) go through the Gulf or Istanbul: flydubai via Dubai, Air Arabia via Sharjah or Abu Dhabi, Qatar Airways or Georgian Airways via Doha, Turkish Airlines via Istanbul, and Azerbaijan Airlines via Baku, among others.
  • Batumi and Kutaisi have their own airports. Batumi, on the Black Sea, has a seasonal flydubai flight from Dubai and flights from Istanbul.
  • A Dubai or Doha layover can be short, but check whether you need a transit visa if you change airports or stay overnight.
  • India is 1 hour 30 minutes ahead of Georgia. A flight that lands at 4 AM in Tbilisi lands at 5:30 AM India time.
  • Baggage: low-cost fares to the Gulf often include only a cabin bag. Wear your heavy jacket on the plane. See the limits by airline in the international baggage rules.
  • Fares go up for Christmas, New Year and Indian school holidays. Book early.

A 6-day Georgia itinerary

Most first trips from India spend 5 to 7 days: Tbilisi, the mountains of Kazbegi and a day in the wine region. This plan keeps the long drives on separate days.

DayPlanEvening
1Land in Tbilisi and rest. Walk the old town: the Bridge of Peace and Rike ParkDinner in the old town
2The cable car up to Narikala fortress, the sulphur bath district (Abanotubani), Mtatsminda hill for city viewsShota Rustaveli Avenue
3Mtskheta, the old capital: Jvari monastery and Svetitskhoveli cathedral, a short drive from TbilisiBack in Tbilisi
4The mountain road north: Ananuri fortress, the Gudauri viewpoint, then Stepantsminda (Kazbegi) and Gergeti Trinity Church below Mount KazbekStay in Kazbegi or drive back
5Kakheti, the wine region: the hill town of Sighnaghi and its old wallsBack in Tbilisi
6Shopping and a last walk, then fly home
  • The road to Kazbegi crosses a high mountain pass. In winter, Georgia's Roads Department often closes the Gudauri–Kobi section for avalanche risk, said reports by Georgia's public broadcaster. Check the road before you go, and keep a spare day.
  • Go with a driver or a tour for the mountains, especially in winter: the road is steep and icy.
  • Batumi on the Black Sea is a beach city for summer, far from Tbilisi in the west. Add it only on a longer trip.
  • Skiing? Swap days 4 and 5 for two days in Gudauri. See the ski section below.
  • Dress for churches. Women may be asked to cover their head and wear a long skirt; men shouldn't wear shorts. Many churches lend scarves and wraps at the door.

Georgia weather in December and the best time to go

Long-term averages for Tbilisi from Georgia's hydrometeorology department, published by the World Meteorological Organization:

MonthTbilisi average temperatureTbilisi rain or snow
NovemberAbout 8 °CAbout 30 mm
DecemberAbout 4 °CAbout 21 mm
JanuaryAbout 2 °C, the coldest monthAbout 19 mm
FebruaryAbout 3 °CAbout 26 mm
MarchAbout 7 °CAbout 30 mm
JulyAbout 24 °C, the hottest monthAbout 45 mm
AugustAbout 24 °CAbout 48 mm
  • These are averages for day and night together. Days are warmer and nights colder, often below freezing in winter.
  • Tbilisi's winter averages stay just above freezing, so for reliable snow go up to the mountains: Gudauri, Kazbegi or Bakuriani.
  • May and June are the wettest months in Tbilisi, about 76–78 mm each.
  • May to October is best for sightseeing, the Kazbegi road and the wine villages. September and October are the grape harvest season.
  • December is quiet in Tbilisi, with Christmas lights and the New Year. Georgians celebrate Orthodox Christmas on 7 January.

Snow and skiing in Gudauri and Bakuriani

  • The 2026–27 ski season "will officially open on December 20" in Gudauri, Bakuriani, Goderdzi and Mestia, says Georgia's Mountain Trails Agency, which runs the lifts (28 Oct 2026). It didn't give a closing date.
  • Gudauri is the big ski resort on the road to Kazbegi, a drive of a few hours from Tbilisi. It has ski schools and gear to rent.
  • Bakuriani is a family resort west of Tbilisi, with gentle slopes for beginners.
  • Never skied before? Book a lesson with an instructor. Wear goggles or sunglasses and sunscreen: the sun on the snow is strong.
  • Not skiing? Take the lifts for the views, try a snow scooter or paragliding with a licensed operator, or just play in the snow.

Getting around Georgia

  • In Tbilisi, use taxis through ride-hailing apps. The app shows the price first.
  • Tbilisi has a metro and city buses. Tbilisi Transport Company sells the Metromoney travel card and lists its fares on its website.
  • The old town is hilly, with cobbled lanes. Wear comfortable shoes.
  • Outside Tbilisi, hire a car with a driver or take a tour. Shared minibuses (marshrutkas) are cheap but slow and crowded.
  • Learn a few words. Georgian and Russian are spoken; many young people speak English. A translation app helps.

Food for Indian travellers: veg and Jain tips

Georgian food has many vegetarian dishes, thanks to beans, walnuts, vegetables and cheese:

  • Khachapuri: bread baked with cheese. Adjaruli khachapuri, shaped like a boat, has egg and butter on top, so ask for it without egg.
  • Lobio: a bean stew in a clay pot, often eaten with mchadi (corn bread).
  • Badrijani: fried aubergine rolls with walnut paste.
  • Pkhali: vegetable and walnut balls made with spinach, beetroot or beans.
  • Khinkali (dumplings) usually have meat, but many restaurants make them with mushroom, potato or cheese. Ask first.
  • Churchkhela: walnuts dipped in thick grape juice, sold everywhere as a sweet.
  • Indian restaurants: Tbilisi has a large Indian community, including many students, and many Indian restaurants serve vegetarian food.
  • Jain travellers: many Georgian dishes use garlic and onion. Carry ready-to-eat food or khakhra, and check what you can take in our can I carry it on a flight? list.

Phone, SIM and eSIM in Georgia

You have three ways to stay online in Georgia:

  • Roaming on your own SIM: the easiest, but often the costliest. Check your mobile company's international pack before you fly.
  • A local SIM card: sold at the airport and in phone shops. Shops usually ask for your passport to register it.
  • An eSIM: a data plan you buy online and install on your phone before you go, with no plastic card. Your phone must support eSIM and must not be locked to one network. On an iPhone, look in Settings > Mobile Service (or Cellular). On Android, look in Settings > SIM or Connections.

Install the eSIM at home over Wi-Fi and switch it on when you land. Most travel eSIMs give data only, with no local number, so keep your home SIM on for calls and bank OTP texts (roaming charges may apply).

Money and rules to know

  • The currency is the Georgian lari (₾, GEL). Cards work in hotels, malls and most restaurants. Carry some cash for markets, villages and small shops.
  • Cash from India: RBI lets you buy up to US$3,000 in foreign currency notes per trip, and you can carry up to ₹25,000 in Indian notes. Put the rest on a forex card. See how much cash you can carry abroad.
  • Strong medicines: you can bring medicines with narcotic or psychotropic substances for your own use, up to 31 days' supply, with a prescription or doctor's letter (your name, the medicine, what's in it, the dose and for how long) and a customs declaration, says the Embassy of Georgia in India.
  • Wine is part of Georgian life, and hosts may offer a toast. It's fine to say no politely.

What to wear in Georgia

  • Winter (December to February): a warm padded jacket, thermal innerwear, a sweater or fleece, gloves, a woolly cap and waterproof boots with a good grip. Ski wear for Gudauri can be rented.
  • Spring and autumn: layers, a light jacket and comfortable walking shoes for the hills of Tbilisi.
  • Summer: light clothes, plus a warm layer for Kazbegi, where it's cool even in July.
  • For churches: a scarf for women and long trousers or a long skirt.

Help in an emergency

  • Embassy of India, Tbilisi: India has had a resident embassy in Tbilisi since July 2024. Contact details are on its website.
  • Keep a photo of your passport, your e-visa and your insurance papers on your phone, and save your hotel's address.

Questions

Do Indians need a visa for Georgia?

Yes, unless you hold a valid visa or residence permit of a country on Georgia's list, such as the USA, the UK or a Schengen country. Most Indians apply for an e-visa on Georgia's official portal, evisa.gov.ge, before they travel.

Can Indians with a US, UK or Schengen visa enter Georgia without a visa?

Yes. Under Georgia's Government Ordinance No. 256, holders of a valid visa or residence permit of the USA, the UK, EU and Schengen countries and other listed countries can stay up to 90 days in any 180 days without a Georgian visa. It must be valid on the day you enter.

What is the Georgia visa fee for Indians?

The e-visa fee is US$20 plus a 2% service fee, says the official portal. After it, you must complete the DuVerify step within 24 hours, which costs US$15 more per person. The fee isn't refunded if the visa is refused.

How long does a Georgia e-visa take for Indians?

At least 5 working days, says the official portal. Apply a few weeks before your flight, and make sure you have travel insurance, a hotel booking and a return ticket.

Does Georgia have snow in December?

The mountains do. The ski season opens on 20 December 2026 in Gudauri and Bakuriani, says Georgia's Mountain Trails Agency. Tbilisi averages about 4 °C in December and snow there doesn't usually last, so go up to Gudauri or Kazbegi for snow.

How many days are enough for Georgia?

Five to seven days is enough for a first trip: two days in Tbilisi, a day in Mtskheta, a day or two in Kazbegi and a day in the wine region of Kakheti. Add two days for skiing in Gudauri in winter.

Are there direct flights from India to Georgia?

Not when we checked. IndiGo's Delhi–Tbilisi flight was suspended, says India's Ministry of External Affairs (July 2026). Fly one-stop via Dubai, Sharjah, Abu Dhabi, Doha or Istanbul.

Is Georgia good for vegetarians?

Yes. Khachapuri (cheese bread), lobio (bean stew), badrijani (aubergine with walnuts) and pkhali (vegetable and walnut balls) are vegetarian, and Tbilisi has many Indian restaurants.

What is the time difference between India and Georgia?

India is 1 hour 30 minutes ahead of Georgia.
