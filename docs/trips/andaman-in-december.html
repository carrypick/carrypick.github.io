Andaman in December and January: weather, ferries and what to pack

Updated 16 Nov 2026 · 10 min read

Andaman at a glance

Weather in DecemberWarm: days about 30–31 °C, nights about 24–26 °C
RainDecember can still be wet; January is drier; February is the driest
Fly toVeer Savarkar International Airport (IXZ), Sri Vijaya Puram (Port Blair)
IslandsFerries to Swaraj Dweep (Havelock) and Shaheed Dweep (Neil) from Phoenix Bay Jetty
PermitNone for Indians to visit the main islands. Carry a photo ID
FestivalIsland Tourism Festival, 27–31 December 2026
HelpPolice 100, ambulance 108, tourist helpline 1363

Andaman in December is warm and green, with calm seas on most days and some rain. It is the start of the best season, which runs to April. This guide covers the weather and rain, how to get there, ferries between the islands, permits, the rules on beaches and coral, the Island Tourism Festival and what to pack.

Andaman weather in December and January

Monthly averages for Sri Vijaya Puram (Port Blair), from IMD's Port Blair weather station:

MonthDayNightUsual rain
DecemberAbout 30–31 °CAbout 24–26 °CAbout 113 mm
JanuaryAbout 30–31 °CAbout 24–25 °CAbout 46 mm
FebruaryAbout 30–31 °CAbout 24 °CAbout 15 mm
  • It's warm all year. Days are about 30 °C and nights never get cold, so you won't need woollens.
  • December rain changes a lot from year to year. Port Blair had 494 mm in December 2016 but less than 2 mm in December 2019. Expect some showers and a few grey days.
  • January is drier, and February is the driest. If you want the calmest sea and clearest water, go from February to April.
  • Storms can come in November and December. Low-pressure systems and cyclones form over the Bay of Bengal in these months in most years. IMD's Port Blair centre issues the warnings for the islands.

Getting to Andaman

  • By air: fly to Veer Savarkar International Airport (IXZ) in Sri Vijaya Puram, the new name of Port Blair since September 2024. Andaman Tourism lists regular flights from Chennai, Kolkata, Hyderabad, Visakhapatnam, Bengaluru and Delhi. Most people fly.
  • By ship: government ships sail from Chennai, Kolkata and Visakhapatnam. The trip takes about 60 hours, and there are only a few sailings a month. You need a government photo ID and must report 2 hours before sailing.

Flying? Check the cabin bag rules for Indian airlines, extra baggage charges and what you can carry on a flight. Island hops often use small bags, so a cabin trolley that fits or a backpack is easiest.

Ferries to Havelock and Neil

Havelock and Neil were renamed Swaraj Dweep and Shaheed Dweep in December 2018. Ross Island is now Netaji Subhas Chandra Bose Dweep.

  • Government ferries leave Phoenix Bay Jetty every day and take about 2.5 to 3 hours. Book on the Directorate of Shipping Services (DSS) website.
  • Private fast boats (Makruzz, Nautika, Green Ocean) take about 1.5 hours.
  • A helicopter flies to Swaraj Dweep in about 20–25 minutes, with very few seats.
  • Carry a photo ID. You need it for ferry tickets and hotel check-in.
  • Bad weather can cancel boats. The administration can cancel or change sailings. In January 2019, during Cyclone Pabuk, boats to Havelock, Neil and Baratang were stopped. Keep a spare day before your flight home.

Permits and rules

  • Indians don't need a permit for Sri Vijaya Puram, Swaraj Dweep, Shaheed Dweep and the main tourist places. Carry a photo ID.
  • Baratang needs a forest permit. The mud volcano is open from 7 AM to 3 PM, and the road passes through tribal reserve land, so vehicles go in convoys.
  • Never photograph or film tribal people. Posting photos or videos of Andaman tribes is a crime. Don't stop, feed or talk to anyone on the road through the Jarawa reserve. North Sentinel Island is closed to all outsiders.
  • Don't touch or take coral or shells. Andaman Tourism says touching, stepping on or collecting coral and seashells is a punishable offence.
  • Single-use plastic is banned across the islands. Carry a steel bottle and a cloth bag.
  • Watch for crocodiles. Saltwater crocodiles live in creeks, mangroves and some sheltered beaches. Swim only where it's allowed, read the signs, and never swim at Wandoor Beach, where swimming is banned. Radhanagar has netted swimming areas.
  • Drones need an official permit.

Things to do in December and January

  • Cellular Jail National Memorial in Sri Vijaya Puram. The museum is open from 8:30 AM to 5 PM. The evening light and sound show runs at 5:50, 6:50 and 7:50 PM; the 6:50 PM show is in English on Monday, Wednesday and Friday, and in Hindi on other days. Book online on the Andaman Tourism website.
  • Radhanagar Beach on Swaraj Dweep, the islands' best-known beach, with netted swimming areas.
  • Jolly Buoy in Mahatma Gandhi Marine National Park, for clear water and coral. It is open from mid-November to mid-May, from 9 AM to 2 PM, and only 280 visitors are allowed each day. Boats leave Wandoor at about 8:30 to 9 AM, and you need a Forest Department permit. Red Skin opens instead from mid-May to mid-November.
  • Netaji Subhas Chandra Bose Dweep (Ross Island) is about 15–20 minutes by boat. It is open from 8 AM to 4 PM, and the last boat back leaves at 4:30 PM. North Bay is about 15–20 minutes by boat from Aberdeen Jetty.
  • Scuba diving on Swaraj and Shaheed Dweep. Introductory dives are open to anyone 10 or older, and no special permit is needed. Tell the dive centre about any health problems before you dive.
  • Island Tourism Festival: this year it runs from 27 to 31 December 2026 in Sri Vijaya Puram.

Christmas and New Year in Andaman

  • Book flights, ferries and hotels early. Late December is the busiest time, and the festival adds to the crowds.
  • Leave a spare day. Don't take the last ferry from Havelock on the day of your flight home, in case boats are cancelled.
  • Book Jolly Buoy early, because visitor numbers are limited each day.

What to wear and pack for Andaman

What should I wear? Try the what-to-wear helper for day and night temperatures and an outfit list by month.

Andaman is hot and humid, with sudden showers, so pack light. Our Andaman packing list has the full list, with outfits for women, men and couples.

  • Clothes: light cotton or linen, shorts, and a dress or kurta for the evenings. One long-sleeved shirt helps against sun and mosquitoes.
  • Swimwear and a quick-dry towel.
  • Rain gear: a light rain jacket or a small umbrella, for December showers.
  • Shoes: sandals or floaters that can get wet, plus comfortable walking shoes.
  • Sun: sunscreen, sunglasses and a hat. The sun is strong on the boats and beaches. See how to pick a travel sunscreen.
  • Bags: a dry bag or zip pouches keep your phone and ID dry on the ferries.
  • Medicines: pills for seasickness if boats upset you, plus your usual medicines.

Health and safety

  • Drink plenty of water. It's hot and humid, and you lose water fast in the sun.
  • Use mosquito repellent, especially in the evenings and near forests.
  • Swim only at safe beaches, inside the nets where there are nets, and never alone or after drinking.
  • Follow weather warnings. If IMD warns of a depression or cyclone, stay off the sea and follow local advice.

For foreign visitors

  • Visa: apply for the e-Tourist Visa only at the official site, indianvisaonline.gov.in. e-Visa holders can also enter at the Port Blair seaport.
  • Restricted Area Permit: a notice of 5 March 2026 says most foreign visitors don't need one for 29 listed islands, and 11 more islands are open for day visits until 31 December 2027. Citizens of Afghanistan, China and Pakistan still need one, with approval in advance. Tribal reserves and defence areas need separate permission. Check the notice before you go, and carry your passport for ferry tickets.

Questions

Is December a good time to visit Andaman?

Yes. December is the start of the best season, which runs to April. It's warm, with days about 30 °C, and the sea is calm on most days. Some years have a lot of rain in December, so keep your plans flexible and leave a spare day for cancelled ferries.

Does it rain in Andaman in December?

Often, yes, but how much changes a lot. Port Blair had 494 mm in December 2016 and under 2 mm in December 2019. Usual rain is about 113 mm in December, 46 mm in January and 15 mm in February.

What is the temperature in Andaman in December?

Days are about 30–31 °C and nights about 24–26 °C, according to IMD's Port Blair station. It never gets cold, so pack light cotton clothes.

What should I wear in Andaman?

Light cotton or linen clothes, swimwear, sandals that can get wet, a hat and sunglasses. Add a light rain jacket or umbrella in December, and one long-sleeved top for sun and mosquitoes.

Do Indians need a permit for Andaman?

No permit is needed for the main islands, including Sri Vijaya Puram, Swaraj Dweep (Havelock) and Shaheed Dweep (Neil). Carry a government photo ID for ferries and hotels. Baratang and Jolly Buoy need forest permits, and tribal reserves are closed.

How do I go from Port Blair to Havelock?

By government ferry from Phoenix Bay Jetty (about 2.5–3 hours, booked on the DSS website), by private fast boat such as Makruzz, Nautika or Green Ocean (about 1.5 hours), or by helicopter (about 20–25 minutes, very few seats).

Is Jolly Buoy open in December?

Yes. Jolly Buoy is open from mid-November to mid-May, from 9 AM to 2 PM, with a limit of 280 visitors a day. You need a Forest Department permit, and boats leave from Wandoor.

Andaman or Goa in December?

Goa is easier to reach, drier in December and has nightlife and New Year parties. Andaman has clearer water, coral and quieter beaches, but needs a flight and ferries, and December can be rainy. See our Goa trip guide.
