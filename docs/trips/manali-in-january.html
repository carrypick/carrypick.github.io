Manali in January: snow, weather, the Winter Carnival and roads

Updated 24 Nov 2026 · 11 min read

Manali in January at a glance

Weather in JanuaryAbout 10.5 °C by day and −1 °C at night (IMD averages, 1991–2020)
SnowJanuary is wetter than December, with about 90 mm of rain and snow. Last winter the first big snow came only on 23 January 2026
Winter CarnivalUsually in January. In 2025 and 2026 it ran from 20 to 24 January. 2027 dates not announced yet
SkiingSolang ski courses run from January to March, if there's enough snow
Atal TunnelOften closed to tourist cars after snow, or open to 4x4s only
Long weekendRepublic Day, Tuesday 26 January 2027
Emergency112

January is Manali's snow month. It's colder and snowier than December, the New Year crowds have gone home by the first weekend, and the ski slopes at Solang usually open. But snow still isn't certain: last winter, Manali waited until 23 January for its first proper snowfall. This guide covers January's weather, the snow, each week of the month, the Winter Carnival, skiing, the Atal Tunnel in snow, and a simple 3-day plan.

Manali weather in January

The India Meteorological Department's (IMD) long-term averages for Manali, 1991–2020:

MonthDayNightRain and snow
DecemberAbout 13.5 °CAbout 0.3 °CAbout 39 mm, on about 2 days
JanuaryAbout 10.5 °CAbout −1 °CAbout 90 mm, on about 6 days
FebruaryAbout 12 °CAbout 0.3 °CAbout 114 mm, on about 7 days
  • January is the coldest month, and the only one with an average night below freezing.
  • It's more than twice as wet as December. Most of that falls as snow in town and higher up. That's why January is a better bet than December for snow.
  • The coldest January night on record in Manali was −11.6 °C, says IMD's table.
  • Sunny days can feel mild in the middle of the day, then drop fast after about 4 PM. Carry your warm layers every day.

Before you go, check IMD's forecast for Himachal Pradesh for snow alerts and cold-wave warnings in the week of your trip.

Will there be snow in Manali in January?

  • More likely than in December, but not certain. IMD's averages show January bringing more than twice December's rain and snow.
  • Last winter was late. After a dry spell of about three months, Manali and the higher hills got the season's first major snowfall on 23 January 2026, with an IMD orange alert for Kullu and Lahaul-Spiti, says All India Radio. More snow fell on 27 January.
  • Solang and the road to the Atal Tunnel get snow more often than Manali town, because they're higher. Even in a dry spell, you may find old snow at Solang.
  • If snow matters most, go in the second half of January or in February, and keep your dates flexible. Watch the forecast a week ahead.

Manali in the first week of January

New Year's crowds leave by about 3 January, and the town gets quieter. It's very cold, but snow in town isn't certain this early. A good week for a quieter trip, Hadimba temple and Old Manali without queues.

Manali in the middle of January

Lohri is on Wednesday 13 January 2027 and Makar Sankranti on Thursday 14 January, both restricted holidays on the central government's 2027 list. The snow odds keep getting better, and the Solang ski season usually starts around now, if there's enough snow.

Manali in the last week of January

The busiest week of the month. Republic Day is on Tuesday 26 January 2027, so many people take Monday off for a 4-day trip, and Himachal also marks its Statehood Day on 25 January (the 2027 state holiday list wasn't published when we checked). If snow comes at the same time, expect full hotels and long jams: on the Republic Day weekend of 2026, news reports described a jam of about 8 km and nearly full hotels. Book early and arrive before Saturday.

Manali Winter Carnival

  • What it is: a five-day festival that Manali has held since 1977, with a cultural procession and tableaux, folk dances at Manu Rangshala, the Winter Queen contest, Mahanati (a women's folk-dance event), street plays, and craft and food stalls.
  • When: Himachal Tourism says it's held in the first week of January, and the government's events site lists it as 2–6 January. But the last two editions ran later: 20 to 24 January in both 2025 and 2026, say news reports. The Chief Minister opened the 2026 carnival on 20 January.
  • 2027 dates: not announced when we checked on 24 November 2026. Watch the Kullu district site and local news from December.
  • Winter sports at the carnival depend on snow, and have been postponed in years with too little.

Skiing at Solang in January

  • The season: the government mountaineering and skiing institute in Manali (ABVIMAS) runs its skiing courses from January to March, says Incredible India, on the slopes at Solang, about 13 km from Manali.
  • Snow first: an ABVIMAS notice from a past season said a January course would be postponed if the snow was poor. The 2027 course dates weren't on abvimas.org when we checked.
  • A day of skiing: at Solang you can hire skis and boots and take a short lesson with a local instructor. Ask your hotel for a registered one, and agree what's included first.
  • The Solang ropeway stops in strong wind or heavy snow. Go in the morning.

The Atal Tunnel and Sissu in January

  • Rohtang Pass is closed all winter. The road beyond Gulaba usually closes from mid-November.
  • The Atal Tunnel road is the one that matters in January. After snow, the Kullu administration usually stops tourist cars at Solang Nala, or lets only 4x4s through. On 27 January 2026 the road beyond Solang was closed after heavy snow, and it reopened to tourists only on 9 February 2026, for 4x4 vehicles only, after the Border Roads Organisation cleared it (news reports).
  • Sissu and the Lahaul side are frozen and beautiful in January, but treat them as a bonus, not the plan. Check the day's rule with your hotel or driver in the morning.
  • Don't drive yourself in snow unless you're used to it. Take a local taxi from an authorised stand, and never go past a police barrier.

Manali trip plan for January: 3 days

Day 1: arrive and walk the town

  1. Arrive by overnight bus from Delhi or by road from Chandigarh, and rest for the morning.
  2. Hadimba Devi Temple in its cedar forest, then Old Manali and the Manu Temple.
  3. Mall Road in the evening for woollens and hot food.

Day 2: Solang and the snow

  1. Leave early for Solang Nala, 13 km away, before the traffic.
  2. Snow play, the ropeway or a ski lesson, depending on the snow and the wind.
  3. If police allow it, go on through the Atal Tunnel to Sissu in a 4x4 taxi. If not, come back by mid-afternoon.

Day 3: hot springs and Naggar

  1. Vashisht hot springs in the morning, about 3 km from town.
  2. Naggar Castle and the Roerich Art Gallery, about 22 km down the valley.
  3. Leave on an evening bus, or stay another night if the roads are slow after snow.

Getting to Manali in January

  • By road from Chandigarh: about 300 km. Most people fly or take a train to Chandigarh and drive.
  • By bus from Delhi: Himachal Tourism (HPTDC) and HRTC run overnight Volvo buses, about 10–12 hours. In heavy snow, buses may stop short of Manali or run late.
  • By air: Kullu-Manali Airport at Bhuntar is 50 km away. It's small, with few winter flights, and they're often cancelled in bad weather. Chandigarh (IXC) is the surer airport.
  • Fog: January fog on the plains can delay trains and flights from Delhi and Chandigarh in the early morning. Leave a spare half-day.

What to wear in Manali in January

What should I wear? Try the what-to-wear helper for Manali temperatures by month.

January nights are below freezing, so dress in layers:

LayerWhat to wear
1. Next to your skinThermal top and bottom, and a spare set
2. MiddleA thick fleece or sweater
3. OutsideA warm, hooded, windproof jacket
4. ExtrasWoollen cap, neck warmer, waterproof gloves and woollen socks
  • For the snow at Solang: waterproof trousers, or rent a snow suit and gumboots there.
  • Shoes: waterproof boots with a deep grip. Paths ice over in the morning.
  • Sunglasses and sunscreen for the glare off the snow.

Safety in the cold

  • Never sleep with a heater, coal or wood fire burning in a closed room. They give off carbon monoxide, which can kill without warning.
  • Change out of wet clothes quickly. Shivering, confusion and clumsiness are signs of hypothermia.
  • The cold drains phone batteries fast. Carry a power bank and keep your phone in an inside pocket.
  • Respect police barriers at Solang and on the tunnel road. They're there because of ice and avalanche risk.

For foreign visitors

  • No permit for Manali itself. Some border areas of Kinnaur and Spiti need an Inner Line Permit, but in January those roads are mostly snowbound anyway.
  • Visa: apply for the e-Tourist Visa only at indianvisaonline.gov.in.

Emergency numbers

  • Emergency (police, fire, ambulance): 112
  • Ambulance: 108
  • Ministry of Tourism helpline, 24 hours, many languages: 1363 or 1800-11-1363

Questions

Is January a good time to visit Manali?

Yes, if you want cold and the best chance of snow. IMD's averages are about 10.5 °C by day and −1 °C at night, with more than twice December's rain and snow. It's quieter than late December, except on the Republic Day weekend.

Will there be snow in Manali in January 2027?

Likely, but not certain. Last winter the first big snowfall came only on 23 January 2026. Solang, 13 km higher up the valley, gets snow more often than the town. Check IMD's forecast a week before you go.

Which week of January is best for Manali?

The second half of January has the best odds of snow. The first week is quiet after New Year. The last week has the Republic Day long weekend, so it's the busiest.

When is the Manali Winter Carnival 2027?

Not announced yet. Official pages say the first week of January, but the 2025 and 2026 carnivals both ran from 20 to 24 January.

Is the Atal Tunnel open in January?

The tunnel itself is kept open, but after snow the administration often stops tourist cars at Solang Nala, or lets only 4x4s through. In 2026 the road was closed from late January and reopened to 4x4s on 9 February.

Can I ski in Manali in January?

Yes, at Solang, if there's enough snow. The government institute ABVIMAS runs ski courses from January to March. Its 2027 dates weren't published when we checked.

How cold is Manali in January?

About 10.5 °C by day and −1 °C at night on IMD's averages, and the record is −11.6 °C. Solang and the Atal Tunnel are colder.
