Gulmarg in December: weather, snow, the gondola and what to wear

Updated 22 Nov 2026 · 11 min read

Gulmarg at a glance

Weather in DecemberAbout 4 °C by day and −5 °C at night (IMD averages)
JanuaryColder: about 0.5 °C by day and −8 °C at night
SnowOften in December, but some early winters are dry. Surest from late December to February
HeightAbout 2,650 m
GondolaTwo phases, up to about 3,980 m on Apharwat. Book on the official site only
Get thereFly or take the Vande Bharat train to Srinagar, then about 52 km by road via Tangmarg
Emergency112

Gulmarg is Kashmir's snow meadow: a wide bowl of pine forest and slopes about 52 km from Srinagar, with India's best-known ski area and a gondola that climbs high up Apharwat. In December it's very cold, and snow is likely but not certain. This guide covers the weather, the first week versus the last week of December, the gondola, skiing, the road up in snow, and what to wear.

Gulmarg weather in December

Long-term averages for Gulmarg from the India Meteorological Department (IMD), 1981–2010:

MonthDayNightRain and snow
DecemberAbout 4 °CAbout −5 °CAbout 104 mm, on about 5 days
JanuaryAbout 0.5 °CAbout −8 °CAbout 158 mm, on about 8 days
  • Below freezing every night. Even the December days barely get above freezing in the shade, and the top of the gondola is far colder than the meadow.
  • The coldest nights on record: −18 °C on 4 December 1968 and −19.8 °C on 4 January 1991, says IMD. In recent years, the coldest December night was −12.5 °C, on 24 December 2015.
  • January is colder and snowier. IMD's averages show about half as much rain and snow again in January as in December.
  • Chillai Kalan, the 40 coldest days of the Kashmir winter, starts on 21 December and lasts until the end of January.

Gulmarg weather right now: on 22 November 2026, IMD's Gulmarg forecast showed mainly clear skies until 28 November, with days of about 9–11 °C and nights of about −1 to −3 °C. Check IMD's Gulmarg forecast in the week before you go.

Will there be snow in December?

  • Often, but not every year. Last winter, after a long dry spell, the first big snow in Gulmarg came only on 21 December 2025, the first day of Chillai Kalan.
  • This season so far: news reports say Gulmarg had its first snow on 13 October 2026, and fresh snow of a few inches on about 12 November.
  • For deep snow, late December to February is safest. December snow can melt on sunny days in the lower meadow, while the slopes higher up keep it.

Gulmarg in the first week of December

Cold and usually quiet, with fewer tourists before Christmas. You may see snow on the slopes and the higher gondola stations, but a white meadow isn't certain this early. It's a good week for clear views and the gondola without long queues, if you don't mind patchy snow.

Gulmarg in the second and third weeks

The odds of snow get better as the month goes on. Hotels fill up from about 20 December, so book early if you want the last two weeks.

Gulmarg in the last week of December

The busiest week of the winter, around Christmas and New Year, and the start of Chillai Kalan. Snow is more likely than in early December, but not guaranteed. Book your stay, car and gondola tickets well ahead, and expect queues and traffic on the Tangmarg road after fresh snow.

The Gulmarg Gondola

The gondola, run by the J&K Cable Car Corporation, is a two-stage ropeway and the easiest way to reach deep snow.

  • Phase 1 goes from Gulmarg up to Kongdori, at about 3,100 m (10,500 ft), says the state tourism corporation, JKTDC.
  • Phase 2 goes on from Kongdori to about 3,980 m on Apharwat. It's the higher, snowier ride, and the first to stop in bad weather.
  • Book on the official site only. Tickets are sold online at etickets.jammukashmircablecar.com, and the corporation's own site asks tourists to book online and not to fall prey to touts. News reports say police have caught tourists with fake tickets. We don't show ticket prices: the official site has the current fares.
  • Phase 2 often stops when snow or strong wind comes in. Go on your first clear morning, and keep a spare day.
  • It's much colder at the top. Wear every layer, plus gloves, a cap and sunglasses.
  • The Kongdori chair lift is listed on the corporation's site, but its page says "coming soon", so we can't confirm whether it runs this winter.

Skiing in Gulmarg

  • Season: Gulmarg's ski slopes are usually best from January to March. December snow can be thin.
  • Ski courses: the government's Indian Institute of Skiing and Mountaineering (IISM) in Gulmarg runs 14-day snow skiing and snowboarding courses for ages 10 to 40, in groups of 25. Its 2026–27 schedule isn't published yet: the institute says dates come "as and when sanctioned by the ministry". Check iismgulmarg.in before you plan around a course.
  • Khelo India Winter Games: the snow events of the 2026 Games were held in Gulmarg from 23 to 26 February 2026. The 2027 dates haven't been announced yet.
  • Beginners can hire skis, boots and instructors in Gulmarg for a day. Ask your hotel to suggest a registered instructor.

Things to do in Gulmarg in winter

  • The gondola to Kongdori and Apharwat, for snow and the views.
  • Sledging and snow walks on the meadow. Sledge pullers wait near the gondola base; agree the fare and the route first.
  • Khilanmarg, about 4 km from Gulmarg, a higher meadow with views of Nanga Parbat on a clear day.
  • The golf course: JKTDC calls it the highest green golf course in the world, at about 2,650 m. It opened to golfers in 1904. In winter it's under snow.
  • St Mary's Church, a small old stone church in the meadow, and the Maharani temple on its little hill.
  • Drung waterfall, near Tangmarg, often freezes in winter.

Getting to Gulmarg in winter

  • By air: fly to Srinagar (SXR). Gulmarg is about 52 km from Srinagar city and about 57 km from the airport, says JKTDC, through Tangmarg. Heavy snow can delay or cancel flights, so keep a spare day before your flight home.
  • By train: since 2 May 2026, Vande Bharat trains run from Jammu Tawi to Srinagar, via Katra and Banihal, in about 5 hours. Check times and running days on the IRCTC or NTES site, then take a taxi from Srinagar.
  • The road from Tangmarg: after snow, the authorities usually allow only 4x4 vehicles or cars with anti-skid chains on the Tangmarg–Gulmarg road, and chains are fitted only by registered vendors at set spots. Ask your hotel or driver about the day's rule in the morning.
  • Day trip or stay? A day trip from Srinagar is possible, but the drive, the chains and the gondola queue make it a long day. One or two nights in Gulmarg give you a clear morning for Phase 2.

What to wear in Gulmarg in December

What should I wear? Try the what-to-wear helper for Gulmarg and Srinagar temperatures and an outfit list by month.

Gulmarg is colder than Srinagar, and the gondola top is colder still, so dress in warm layers:

LayerWhat to wear
1. Next to your skinThermal top and bottom, and a spare set
2. MiddleA thick fleece or sweater, or two thin ones
3. OutsideA heavy, hooded winter jacket, or two layers under a padded jacket
4. ExtrasWoollen cap, neck warmer, waterproof gloves and two pairs of woollen socks
  • For the snow: waterproof trousers over thermals. Jeans soak up snow. Shops in Tangmarg and Gulmarg rent snow coats and gumboots by the day.
  • Shoes: waterproof boots with deep grip. Paths are icy in the morning.
  • Sunglasses, sunscreen and lip balm. Sun on snow is strong at this height, and the air is dry.

Safety in the cold

  • Never sleep with a heater burning in a closed room. Gas heaters, charcoal and kangris give off carbon monoxide, which can kill. Keep a window slightly open, and switch heaters off before you sleep.
  • Change out of wet clothes quickly. Shivering, confusion and clumsiness are signs of hypothermia.
  • Don't go off the marked slopes at Apharwat without a guide. Snow there can slide.
  • Carry a power bank, and keep your phone in an inside pocket: the cold drains batteries fast. Prepaid SIMs from other states usually don't work in Jammu and Kashmir, so carry a postpaid SIM.

For foreign visitors

  • Read your government's advice. The UK's travel advice (updated 7 October 2026, still current on 22 November) is against all travel to Jammu and Kashmir, naming Gulmarg, Srinagar, Pahalgam, Sonamarg and the Jammu–Srinagar highway. The US advice says "Do Not Travel" to Jammu and Kashmir, except eastern Ladakh and Leh. Travel against this advice can make your travel insurance invalid.
  • Visa: apply for the e-Tourist Visa only at indianvisaonline.gov.in.

Emergency numbers

  • Emergency (police, fire, ambulance): 112
  • Ministry of Tourism helpline, 24 hours, many languages: 1363 or 1800-11-1363

Questions

Is December a good time to visit Gulmarg?

Yes, if you want cold and a chance of snow. IMD's averages are about 4 °C by day and −5 °C at night. Snow is likely but not certain: some early winters are dry. For the surest snow and skiing, go from late December to February.

Will I see snow in Gulmarg in the first week of December?

Maybe. The higher slopes and the gondola stations often have snow by then, but the meadow may be patchy. This season, Gulmarg had snow in October and November 2026, so check the forecast in the week before you go.

Is the last week of December good for Gulmarg?

Snow is more likely than in early December, and it's festive, but it's the busiest week of the winter. Book hotels, cars and gondola tickets early, and expect traffic after fresh snow.

How cold is Gulmarg in December?

About 4 °C by day and −5 °C at night on IMD's long-term averages, and colder at the top of the gondola. The lowest December reading on record was −18 °C, on 4 December 1968.

How do I book the Gulmarg Gondola?

Online, on the J&K Cable Car Corporation's official site, etickets.jammukashmircablecar.com. Don't buy from touts: news reports say police have caught tourists with fake tickets. Phase 2 often stops in bad weather, so keep a spare day.

What is the difference between Gondola Phase 1 and Phase 2?

Phase 1 goes from Gulmarg to Kongdori, at about 3,100 m. Phase 2 goes from Kongdori up to about 3,980 m on Apharwat, with deeper snow and bigger views, and it's the first to close in bad weather.

Can I go skiing in Gulmarg in December?

Sometimes, if early snow is good, but January to March is safer. The Indian Institute of Skiing and Mountaineering runs 14-day courses in winter; its 2026–27 dates aren't published yet.

What should I wear in Gulmarg in December?

Thermals, a thick fleece and a heavy hooded jacket, plus a woollen cap, neck warmer, waterproof gloves and woollen socks. Wear waterproof boots with grip, and waterproof trousers or a rented snow coat for the snow.
