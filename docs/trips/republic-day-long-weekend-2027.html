Republic Day long weekend 2027: where to go, and how to see the parade

Updated 6 Nov 2026 · 4 min read

Republic Day weekend at a glance

DatesSaturday 23 to Tuesday 26 January 2027, with one day off on Monday 25
For forts and palacesJaipur
For snowManali (snow isn't guaranteed)
For a warm beachGoa
For backwaters and tea hillsKerala
For the paradeNew Delhi, tickets on the Aamantran site
Book trainsFrom about 24 November for a 23 January journey

Republic Day 2027 falls on a Tuesday. Take Monday 25 January off, and you get four days: Saturday to Tuesday. It's a good winter break, but many people will have the same idea, so plan early.

Quick comparison

Four days is enough for one place. Moving between them takes most of a day.

How to choose

  • Want snow? Manali has a better chance in January than in December, but check the forecast and keep your dates flexible. Roads can close after heavy snow.
  • Short on leave? Jaipur is the easiest of these from North India.
  • Want to escape the cold? Goa and the Kerala coast stay warm.
  • Travelling with parents or children? Jaipur and Kerala are calmer, with easy sightseeing.

Seeing the parade in New Delhi

The Republic Day parade takes place on Kartavya Path on 26 January. The 2027 ticket details aren't out yet. Here's how it worked in 2026:

2026
Where to buyThe Defence Ministry's Aamantran site, or six booths in Delhi
When sold5 to 14 January, from 9 AM each day until the day's quota ran out
Parade, 26 January₹100 and ₹20
Beating Retreat rehearsal, 28 January₹20
Beating Retreat, 29 January₹100

News reports said you needed to register with a mobile number and a government photo ID. Each day's quota can run out, so try early. Check Delhi Police's traffic advice before you set out, as many roads close for the parade.

Book your travel early

  • Trains: reservations open about 60 days before you travel, so a 23 January journey opens around 24 November. News reports say only Aadhaar-verified accounts can book on opening day. Check IRCTC for the exact rules.
  • Flights and hotels: book as soon as you can. Long weekends fill up quickly.
  • Fog: January fog in North India can delay early flights and trains, so leave spare time for connections.
  • Bags: check your airline's cabin and check-in limits and the train luggage rules.

Questions

Is there a long weekend on Republic Day 2027?

Yes, if you take one day off. 26 January 2027 is a Tuesday. With Monday 25 January off, you get four days from Saturday 23 January.

Where can I go for 4 days on Republic Day weekend?

Jaipur for forts, Goa for a warm beach, Kerala for backwaters and tea hills, or Manali if you want a chance of snow and don't mind a long road journey.

How do I buy Republic Day parade tickets?

In 2026, tickets were sold on the Aamantran website and at six booths in Delhi from 5 to 14 January. The 2027 details aren't out yet, so check the Defence Ministry's site in early January.

When does train booking open for Republic Day weekend?

Reservations open about 60 days before the journey, so around 24 November 2026 for travel on 23 January 2027. Check IRCTC for the exact rules.
