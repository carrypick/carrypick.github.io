Goa trip plan: itineraries for 3, 4 and 5 days, the best time to go, and the rules to know

Updated 26 Nov 2026 · 10 min read

Goa at a glance

Best timeNovember to February: dry, with days about 32–33 °C and nights about 20–21 °C
Days you need3 days for North Goa; 4 to add Old Goa and Panaji; 5 to add a South Goa beach
Fly toMopa (GOX) for North Goa, Dabolim (GOI) for central and South Goa
Train toThivim (North), Karmali (Panaji and Old Goa), Madgaon (South)
MonsoonJune to September, the sea is rough
Emergency112, Goa Tourism helpline 1364

Short answer: go between November and February, when Goa is dry and sunny. Three days is enough for North Goa's beaches and forts. With 4 days, add Old Goa's churches and Panaji's old quarter. With 5, spend the last two nights on a quieter South Goa beach such as Palolem or Agonda. Fly into Mopa (GOX) for the north and Dabolim (GOI) for the south, and book early for Christmas and New Year.

Goa itinerary for 3 days

The most searched plan. It stays in North Goa, so you don't lose time on long taxi rides.

DayWhere you sleepWhat to do
1North Goa (Candolim, Calangute or Baga)Land at Mopa, about 30 km from Calangute. Afternoon on the beach, sunset at Fort Aguada and its lighthouse
2North GoaMorning at Anjuna and Vagator, then Chapora Fort above Vagator. If it's a Wednesday, the Anjuna flea market. On a Saturday evening, the night bazaar at Arpora
3HomeFontainhas, Panaji's old Latin quarter, and the Basilica of Bom Jesus in Old Goa (about 10 km from Panaji). Fly out of Dabolim if your flight allows, since it's closer to both

Going in a group of friends? North Goa has the shacks, clubs and markets. Travelling with family? Stay in Candolim or Sinquerim, which are a little calmer than Baga.

Goa itinerary for 4 days

DayWhere you sleepWhat to do
1North GoaArrive, beach afternoon, Fort Aguada at sunset
2North GoaAnjuna, Vagator and Chapora Fort. Arambol's beach and freshwater lake if you want a quieter afternoon
3PanajiOld Goa's churches (a UNESCO World Heritage Site), Fontainhas, and an evening by the Mandovi river
4HomeA spice plantation near Ponda with a Goan lunch, then fly from Dabolim (about 30 km from Panaji)

Goa itinerary for 5 days

North and South Goa in one trip. Fly into Mopa and out of Dabolim so you only cross Goa once.

DayWhere you sleepWhat to do
1–2North GoaBeaches, Fort Aguada, Anjuna, Vagator, Chapora Fort
3PanajiOld Goa and Fontainhas
4South Goa (Palolem or Agonda)Drive south via Margao. Palolem is about 40 km beyond Margao. Afternoon on the beach
5HomeA slow morning, then Dabolim airport

Seven days? Add a day trip to Dudhsagar Falls (jeep trips usually restart in October after the monsoon; check the booking rules with Goa Tourism on 9307915913 first) and a second South Goa day at Colva or Benaulim. Only two days? Stay in one place, North or South, and skip the long drives.

Going after New Year? Our Goa in January guide covers each week of the month, the Feast of the Three Kings and January events.

North GoaSouth Goa
FeelBusy, with nightlife, shacks and marketsQuieter, with wider beaches and more green
Best forFriends, first trips, partiesCouples, families, a slow holiday
BeachesCalangute, Baga, Candolim, Anjuna, Vagator, ArambolColva, Benaulim, Palolem, Agonda
AirportMopa (GOX)Dabolim (GOI)
Train stationThivimMadgaon (Margao); Canacona for Palolem

On a honeymoon? South Goa's quieter beaches suit most couples. Compare Goa with other honeymoon destinations in India.

Best time to visit Goa

SeasonMonthsWhat to expect
Peak seasonNovember to FebruaryDry and sunny. IMD's averages: about 33 °C by day and 21 °C at night in December, with about 2.5 mm of rain in the whole month. Busiest and costliest at Christmas and New Year
Hot seasonMarch to MayStill mostly dry until May, and humid. Days about 32–34 °C. Fewer visitors and lower hotel prices
MonsoonJune to SeptemberVery wet: IMD's averages are about 890–910 mm of rain in each of June and July. Green and quiet, but the sea is rough, so stay out of the water. Many beach shacks close
After the monsoonOctoberRain tapers off (about 146 mm on average). Shacks start to reopen, and Dudhsagar jeep trips restart

IMD figures are 1981–2010 averages for Goa. Goa Tourism says light woollens can be worn on December and January evenings.

Getting there and around

  • Two airports: both have official taxi counters with fixed fares. Kadamba, the state bus company, runs airport buses to Panaji, Margao, Mapusa and Calangute.
  • Train: Goa is on the Konkan Railway, and every train stops at Madgaon. See the train luggage rules.
  • Taxis: agree the price before you get in. Goa Tourism recommends registered taxis and state-backed apps such as GoaMiles. News reports say Uber and Ola don't operate in Goa.
  • Bike taxis ("pilots"): motorbikes with yellow mudguards and yellow number plates. Agree the fare first.
  • Ferries: free for people on foot and two-wheelers on routes like Betim–Panaji and Old Goa–Divar.
  • Scooters: rent only from a licensed rental. Police say legal rental vehicles have a black number plate with yellow letters. Always wear a helmet (the fine is ₹1,000), never ride drunk, and carry your licence. Foreign visitors need an International Driving Permit.

Flying in? Check your airline's cabin bag rules, or use the baggage checker. Taking cashew feni home? See can you carry alcohol on a flight.

Beach rules and fines

Goa's 2022 order under its tourist places law fines these offences ₹5,000 to ₹50,000:

  • Drinking alcohol in the open and breaking glass bottles. Drink only in licensed shacks, bars and restaurants.
  • Cooking in the open, littering, touting, and selling tickets outside official counters.
  • Driving on the beach, and water sports outside the marked zones.

Also: no nudity in public places, don't photograph people swimming or sunbathing, keep away from marked turtle nests at Morjim, Galgibag and Agonda, and remember that drugs are a serious crime in India. Swim only between the red-and-yellow flags while lifesavers are on duty, never where there's a red flag, and never after drinking.

Christmas and New Year in Goa

  • Book early. November to February is peak season. Book only places registered with the Department of Tourism.
  • Music stops at midnight. Goa's environment department allows loudspeakers from 10 PM to midnight only on listed days, including Christmas Eve, Christmas and 26 to 31 December 2026, and only with permission. On other nights they stop at 10 PM.
  • Traffic: last New Year's Eve, police closed the Calangute–Baga stretch to vehicles from the afternoon. Walk where you can.
  • Church feasts: St Francis Xavier in Old Goa (3 December), the Immaculate Conception in Panaji (8 December) and Christmas.

Parties, the night's rules and where to stay are in our Goa New Year 2027 guide.

For foreign visitors

  • Visa: most nationalities need an e-Tourist Visa from the official site only, indianvisaonline.gov.in. Apply at least 4 days before you arrive. Both Goa airports accept e-Visa arrivals. Fill the e-Arrival card online within 72 hours before you land.
  • No special permit: Goa isn't a protected or restricted area.
  • Money: change money only at banks or authorised money changers, and keep the receipt.

Emergency numbers

  • Emergency (police, fire, ambulance): 112
  • Goa Tourism helpline: 1364
  • Women's helpline: 1091

Questions

How many days are enough for Goa?

Three days covers North Goa's beaches, forts and markets. Four days adds Old Goa and Panaji. Five days lets you add two nights on a quieter South Goa beach. With only two days, stay in one area.

What is a good 3-day Goa trip plan?

Day 1: arrive and spend the afternoon on a North Goa beach, with sunset at Fort Aguada. Day 2: Anjuna, Vagator and Chapora Fort. Day 3: Fontainhas in Panaji and the Basilica of Bom Jesus in Old Goa before your flight.

North Goa or South Goa: which is better?

North Goa for nightlife, markets and a first trip with friends. South Goa for quieter, wider beaches, which suit couples and families. With 5 days you can do both.

What is the best time to visit Goa?

November to February. It's dry and sunny, with days about 32–33 °C and nights about 20–21 °C on IMD's averages. June to September is the monsoon, when the sea is too rough for swimming.

What is the best time to visit Goa with family?

November to February for dry weather and calm seas. Avoid the last week of December if you don't want crowds: early December and mid-January have the same weather with fewer people.

How much does a Goa trip cost?

We don't publish a cost, because it depends on your dates, flights and hotel, and Christmas–New Year prices are much higher than the rest of the season. Add your own amounts in our trip budget calculator.

Which airport is best for Goa?

Mopa (GOX) if you're staying in North Goa, near Arambol, Anjuna, Calangute or Baga. Dabolim (GOI) for Panaji, Old Goa and South Goa beaches like Colva and Palolem.

Can you drink alcohol on Goa beaches?

No. Drinking in the open, including on beaches, can be fined ₹5,000 to ₹50,000. You can drink in licensed shacks, bars and restaurants.

Until what time can music play on New Year's Eve in Goa?

Until midnight for loudspeakers, with permission, on the days Goa's order lists, which include 26 to 31 December 2026. On other nights, loudspeakers must stop at 10 PM.
