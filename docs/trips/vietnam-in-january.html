Vietnam in January: weather by region, Tet 2027 and where to go

Updated 28 Nov 2026 · 10 min read

Vietnam in January at a glance

North in JanuaryHanoi about 20 °C by day and 15 °C at night, often grey with drizzle (official averages)
Sa PaAbout 12 °C by day and 6 °C at night. Frost on cold nights, and snow in a few winters
CentreDa Nang and Hoi An about 25 °C, the rains are ending
SouthHo Chi Minh City about 32 °C and dry; Phu Quoc sunny
Tet 2027Saturday 6 February. Official break 4 to 10 February. Trains busy from 23 January
Visa for IndiansNeeded: e-visa online, up to 90 days
Time1 hour 30 minutes ahead of India

Vietnam in January is really three trips in one. The south, Ho Chi Minh City, the Mekong Delta and Phu Quoc, is hot, sunny and dry. The centre, Hue, Da Nang and Hoi An, is coming out of its rainy season. The north, Hanoi, Ha Long Bay and Sa Pa, is cool, often grey, and cold in the mountains. In 2027 there's one more thing to plan around: Tet, the Lunar New Year, on 6 February, and the travel rush in the two weeks before it. This guide covers the weather by region, Tet, Ha Long in winter, where to go, a route and the e-visa.

Vietnam weather in January

January averages, from north to south. Hanoi, Da Nang and Ho Chi Minh City are from the Viet Nam Meteorological and Hydrological Administration on the WMO's World Weather Information Service; Sa Pa, Ha Long, Hue and Phu Quoc are from Vietnam's national building climate code (QCVN 02:2022/BXD), as quoted on Wikipedia:

Sa Pa (mountains, about 1,600 m)About 12 °CAbout 6 °CAbout 70 mm, on about 16 days
HanoiAbout 20 °CAbout 15 °CAbout 28 mm, on about 11 days
Ha LongAbout 19 °CAbout 14 °CAbout 28 mm, on about 8 days
HueAbout 24 °CAbout 18 °CAbout 129 mm, on about 16 days
Da Nang (for Hoi An)About 25 °CAbout 20 °CAbout 83 mm, on about 14 days
Ho Chi Minh CityAbout 32 °CAbout 23 °CAbout 12 mm, on about 2 days
Phu QuocAbout 31 °CAbout 23 °CAbout 28 mm, on about 5 days
  • The north is cool and grey. Hanoi has many "rainy" days, but it's mostly light drizzle and mist, with little rain in total. Indoors often has no heating, so pack warm layers.
  • Sa Pa is cold. Nights near freezing are normal on cold snaps, with frost on the peaks. In a few winters it snows: in January 2016, a cold wave brought snow to Sa Pa on 24 and 25 January at about −4 °C (news reports), and Fansipan tours stopped.
  • The centre is drying out. Hue still gets showers on half the days, Da Nang and Hoi An less. December was far wetter: Da Nang gets about 272 mm in December and only 83 mm in January.
  • The south is in its dry season: sunny, hot and good for beaches on Phu Quoc and Con Dao.

Tet 2027: what it means for a January trip

Tet (Lunar New Year) is on Saturday 6 February 2027. It's Vietnam's biggest holiday, when families go back to their home towns.

  • The official break for government staff is 7 days, Thursday 4 to Wednesday 10 February 2027, approved by the government in October 2026, as reported by state media (VnExpress, VietnamNet). Private companies choose their own days.
  • Transport fills up before Tet. Vietnam Railways' Tet travel period runs from 23 January to 25 February 2027, and Tet train tickets went on sale in September 2026 (news reports). Flights and buses between the big cities are busiest in the last week or so before Tet, roughly 27 January to 5 February. Book trains and domestic flights early if you travel then.
  • What closes: many family-run restaurants, shops and markets close from about 4 or 5 February, most of all on the first three days of Tet (6 to 8 February). Big hotels, malls and main sights mostly stay open.
  • The good part: in late January, streets fill with flower markets, peach blossom in Hanoi, apricot blossom in the south and kumquat trees everywhere, and cities put up lights. The Kitchen Gods' day, when families release carp into lakes and rivers, falls on 30 January 2027 by the lunar calendar.

Travelling in the first three weeks of January avoids the rush completely.

Vietnam in each part of January

  • First week: New Year's Day 2027 is a Friday, a public holiday, so the first weekend is busy in Ho Chi Minh City, Da Nang and Phu Quoc. (We don't show prices: compare them for your dates.)
  • Middle of January: the calmest time of the month, and the best time for a full north-to-south trip. Hoi An's full moon lantern night, held every lunar month, falls on about 21 January 2027 by the lunar calendar (not confirmed on an official page).
  • Last week: Republic Day is on Tuesday 26 January 2027. With Monday off, that's a 4-day weekend from India, enough for Hanoi and Ha Long, or Ho Chi Minh City and the Mekong Delta. It's also when the pre-Tet rush starts inside Vietnam.

Ha Long Bay in January

Ha Long is cool in January, about 19 °C by day and 14 °C at night, and often hazy or misty, which makes the limestone islands look moody rather than sunny. Wind on deck makes it feel colder, so take a warm jacket. Kayaking is still possible, but swimming is cold.

Cruise departures are stopped by the port authority when the weather is unsafe. Every suspension we found in the news was for typhoons in summer and autumn, not for January, but strong winds can still delay boats. Keep a spare day before your flight home.

Where to go in Vietnam in January

  • Hanoi and Ha Long Bay for the old quarter, street food and a night on the bay, in jacket weather.
  • Sa Pa or Ha Giang for rice terraces and mountain villages, if you're ready for cold nights. The terraces are bare in winter, not green.
  • Hoi An and Da Nang for the lantern-lit old town, tailors and beaches. The sea can still be rough in January, so swim only at lifeguarded beaches.
  • Ho Chi Minh City and the Mekong Delta for the war museums, the Cu Chi tunnels and boat trips on the delta.
  • Phu Quoc for a beach holiday in January. If you fly straight to Phu Quoc and stay only on the island, you can stay up to 30 days without a visa, says Vietnam's tourism authority. For the mainland, you need the e-visa.

A 7 to 9-day route in January: fly into Hanoi (2 nights), Ha Long Bay (1 night on a cruise), fly to Da Nang for Hoi An (2 nights), then fly to Ho Chi Minh City (2 nights) or Phu Quoc (2 nights) to end in the sun. Our Vietnam trip guide has the day-by-day plan.

Before you fly: e-visa and pre-arrival form

  • Indians need a visa for Vietnam. India isn't on Vietnam's visa exemption list, says the Vietnam National Authority of Tourism (read 24 Nov 2026).
  • Apply for the e-visa online only on the official portal, evisa.gov.vn: up to 90 days, US$25 for single entry or US$50 for multiple entry. It takes about 3 to 5 working days, so apply at least two weeks before you fly, and earlier if you travel near Tet, when offices close.
  • Your passport must have 6 months left and 2 blank pages, and you must enter through the airport named on your e-visa.
  • The pre-arrival form at prearrival.immigration.gov.vn is free and saves time at the airport.

What to wear in Vietnam in January

Pack for two seasons. For Hanoi, Ha Long and Sa Pa: thermals or a warm base layer, a sweater or fleece, a padded or windproof jacket, closed shoes and a cap. For the centre: a light rain jacket. For the south: light cotton clothes, sunscreen and sandals. Cover your shoulders and knees at temples and the Ho Chi Minh Mausoleum.

Help in an emergency

  • Police in Vietnam: 113. Ambulance: 115.
  • Embassy of India, Hanoi: contact details are on its website.
  • Keep a photo of your passport and e-visa and your insurance papers on your phone, and your hotel's address in Vietnamese.

Questions

Is January a good time to visit Vietnam?

Yes, especially for the south and centre. Ho Chi Minh City averages about 32 °C with almost no rain, and Hoi An is drying out after its rainy season. The north is cool and grey, about 20 °C by day in Hanoi, and cold in Sa Pa, so pack layers.

Is it cold in Hanoi in January?

Cool rather than cold: about 20 °C by day and 15 °C at night on average, often misty with light drizzle. Cold snaps can bring single-digit nights, and Sa Pa is much colder, around 6 °C at night.

Does it snow in Vietnam in January?

Rarely, and only in the far north mountains. Sa Pa had snow in January 2016 during a cold wave. Frost on the peaks is more common.

When is Tet 2027?

Saturday 6 February 2027. The official holiday for government staff runs from 4 to 10 February 2027, and trains are busy from 23 January.

Should I avoid Vietnam during Tet?

Not necessarily, but plan for it. Trains, flights and buses fill up in the week before Tet, and many small shops and restaurants close for the first days. Travelling in the first three weeks of January avoids this.
