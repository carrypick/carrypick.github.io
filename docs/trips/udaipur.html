Udaipur in winter: a 2–3 day trip plan for December and January

Updated 16 Nov 2026 · 13 min read

JanuaryAbout 25 °C by day and 7 °C at night. Almost no rain
How long2 days for the city, 3 with a day trip
Fly toMaharana Pratap Airport, Dabok (UDR), about 20–25 km from the old city
Train toUdaipur City (UDZ)

Emergency112

Udaipur, the city of lakes, is at its best in winter: sunny, dry days, cool evenings by Lake Pichola, and the Shilpgram crafts fair at the end of December. Two days are enough for the city, and a third day lets you see the great forts and temples nearby. This guide covers the weather, a simple 2-day and 3-day plan, opening hours, December events, getting there and what to wear.

Weather in December and January

Long-term averages for Udaipur from the India Meteorological Department (IMD), 1981–2010:

MonthDayNightRain
DecemberAbout 26 °CAbout 8 °CAbout 2 mm
JanuaryAbout 25 °CAbout 7 °CAbout 2 mm
FebruaryAbout 28 °CAbout 10 °CAbout 3 mm
  • Warm days, cold nights. Afternoons are pleasant, but the temperature drops by about 17 °C after dark. In most years the coldest night is about 2–5 °C.
  • It can freeze, but rarely. IMD's lowest readings are −0.8 °C on 15 December 1986 and −1.1 °C on 12 January 1989.
  • Almost no rain, and fog is rare in Udaipur itself. Fog in north India can still delay trains coming from Delhi.
  • The sun sets early: around 5:45–6 PM in December and 6–6:20 PM in January. Plan your sunset spot before you go.

Udaipur trip plan: 2 days

Most of the old city is close together, around the east bank of Lake Pichola, so you can walk between the first day's sights.

Day 1: City Palace and the lake

  1. City Palace museum in the morning. Allow at least 2 hours. Ticket counters are at the Badi Pol and Shitla Mata gates.
  2. Jagdish Temple, right next to the palace, before it closes at 1 PM.
  3. A boat on Lake Pichola after lunch. The official boats leave from Rameshwar Ghat, inside the City Palace, and stop at the Jagmandir island palace.
  4. Gangaur Ghat and Bagore ki Haveli at sunset, then the Dharohar folk dance show in the haveli's courtyard in the evening.
  5. Dinner by the lake, with a view of the lit-up palace from Ambrai Ghat on the other side.

Day 2: Fateh Sagar, gardens and a sunset view

  1. Saheliyon ki Bari in the morning, a royal garden whose fountains run without pumps.
  2. Fateh Sagar Lake and Moti Magri, the Maharana Pratap memorial on the hill above it.
  3. Shilpgram, the crafts village west of Fateh Sagar. From 21 to 30 December, this is where the Shilpgram Utsav is held.
  4. A sunset view: the Karni Mata ropeway above Doodh Talai, or the lakeside at Ambrai Ghat. Sajjangarh (the Monsoon Palace) is famous for sunsets, but in winter it closes at about 5:30 PM, before the sun goes down, so go there in the afternoon instead.

Udaipur trip plan: 3 days

Add one day trip:

  • Kumbhalgarh and Ranakpur together, a long day. Visit Ranakpur's temple from noon, when tourists are usually let in, then Kumbhalgarh's walls for the late afternoon, and stay for the light and sound show if you're spending the night.
  • Chittorgarh, about 115 km away, for its huge hilltop fort and the light and sound show after sunset.
  • A half day nearby: the Eklingji temple (22 km), the old Nagda temples, and Nathdwara's Shrinathji temple. Nathdwara closes for darshan in the afternoon, so go in the morning.

  • City Palace: a huge palace complex on the shore of Lake Pichola, part of which is still the royal family's home. The museum is run by the Maharana of Mewar Charitable Foundation. Its certified guides wear an orange lanyard.
  • Lake Pichola boats: the boats from Rameshwar Ghat are run by the Mewar family's hotel company and pass the palace, the ghats, Jag Niwas (the Lake Palace, now a hotel) and the Jagmandir island palace. Its page says times can change without notice, so check the day's sunset ride when you buy your ticket.
  • Jagmandir: an island palace begun in 1620. Prince Khurram, later the emperor Shah Jahan, once took shelter here.
  • Jagdish Temple: a large Vishnu temple from 1651, up 32 marble steps from the street.
  • Bagore ki Haveli: an 18th-century mansion with 138 rooms at Gangaur Ghat, run by the West Zone Cultural Centre. The Dharohar show of Rajasthani folk dances is held every evening; seats aren't reserved, so come early.
  • Fateh Sagar: the second big lake, with a boat ride to Nehru Park and the Udaipur Solar Observatory on its own island. Since February 2026 there's a 30 km/h speed limit on the lake's ring road.

Shilpgram Utsav and other December events

  • Shilpgram Utsav is a ten-day crafts and folk arts fair at Shilpgram, usually from 21 to 30 December. Craftspeople and folk performers come from many states. The 2026 dates haven't been announced yet, but the organisers, the West Zone Cultural Centre, closed stall applications for the 2026 fair on 15 November. Last year it opened on 21 December 2025. It gets very crowded in the evenings and at weekends.
  • Ranakpur Festival: Rajasthan Tourism says it's usually held on 21 and 22 December, with music and dance at Ranakpur.
  • Longer hours over the holidays: last winter, from 25 December to 5 January, the district opened Saheliyon ki Bari until 9 PM and Sajjangarh from 8 AM. A similar order may come this year.
  • Birds at Menar: in winter, migratory birds such as greater flamingos come to the lakes of Menar village, 48 km away on the Chittorgarh road.

Christmas and New Year crowds

Last New Year, hotels across Rajasthan were full, and news reports described traffic jams in Udaipur's old city. Book hotels and trains early for 24 December to 2 January. The lanes around Jagdish Chowk and City Palace are narrow and get one-way and no-entry rules at busy times, so walk in the old city and leave the car at your hotel.

Getting to Udaipur

  • By air: Maharana Pratap Airport at Dabok (UDR) is about 20–25 km from the old city. The City Palace museum says the flight from Delhi takes about 1 hour 20 minutes.
  • By train: the station is Udaipur City (UDZ). Overnight trains from Delhi include the Mewar Express from Hazrat Nizamuddin and the Chetak Express from Delhi Sarai Rohilla. Check times on the IRCTC site.
  • Vande Bharat changes: the Udaipur–Jaipur and Udaipur–Agra Vande Bharat trains stopped in February 2026. Since October 2026, a new Vande Bharat runs between Surat and Udaipur via Ahmedabad's Asarva station, every day except Tuesday. It reaches Udaipur at 1:40 PM and leaves at 3:35 PM. There's no Vande Bharat from Delhi or Jaipur now.
  • By bus: the City Palace museum gives about 5 hours by bus from Ahmedabad, about 7 hours from Jaipur, and about 11 hours from Delhi.

Getting around

  • Walk in the old city: City Palace, Jagdish Temple, Gangaur Ghat and Lal Ghat are a few minutes apart.
  • Autos and taxis for Fateh Sagar, Saheliyon ki Bari and Shilpgram (10–15 minutes away) and for Sajjangarh on its hill. Agree the fare first.
  • Hire a car with a driver for the day trips. Kumbhalgarh and Ranakpur together make a long day.

Day trips

  • Kumbhalgarh Fort is about 85–100 km north, depending on the route. It's one of the six Hill Forts of Rajasthan on UNESCO's World Heritage list (2013). Its wall runs for about 36 km, and Maharana Pratap was born here. There's a light and sound show every evening.
  • Ranakpur Jain Temple is about 90 km away. Its 15th-century marble hall has 1,444 carved pillars. Dress modestly, as Incredible India advises: cover your shoulders and knees, and take off your shoes at the entrance.
  • Chittorgarh Fort, about 115 km away, is the other UNESCO hill fort near Udaipur, with the Vijay Stambh (Tower of Victory) and Rani Padmini's palace.
  • Eklingji (22 km) and Nathdwara (about 50 km) are important temples. Darshan happens at set times, with long breaks in between.

Food to try

Incredible India suggests dal baati churma, gatte ki sabzi, ker sangri and laal maas, with kachori and mawa kachori as snacks and ghevar for something sweet. Hathi Pol Bazaar and Lake Palace Road are good places to eat.

What to wear in Udaipur in December

What should I wear? Try the what-to-wear helper for day and night temperatures and an outfit list by month.

Full list: our Rajasthan packing list, with picks for the desert nights, the sun and the temples.

Udaipur is warm in the day and cold at night, so pack for both.

  • For the day: light cotton clothes, a cap and sunglasses. The sun is strong on boats and palace terraces.
  • For the evening: a warm jacket or sweater. Boat rides, rooftop dinners and the dance show all get cold after sunset.
  • Shoes: comfortable walking shoes for the palace's many steps and the hill forts, plus sandals that are easy to take off at temples.
  • For temples: clothes that cover your shoulders and knees, especially at Ranakpur, and a scarf or shawl.
  • Sunscreen, even in December.

Safety and local tips

  • Travelling alone as a woman? See solo female travel in India for the Women Helpline 181, the Railway helpline 139 and the women's berth quota on trains.
  • Tourist Assistance Force: Rajasthan Tourism's tourist helpers work at major sights in Udaipur, and the city has a tourism police station. Ask them about authorised taxi stands or if touts bother you.
  • Use approved guides: Rajasthan Tourism publishes a list of guides, and at City Palace look for the orange lanyard.
  • Don't swim or bathe in the lakes. The Rajasthan High Court has banned bathing in Udaipur's lakes, and there have been drownings at Fateh Sagar. Wear the life jacket on boats.
  • Shop at reputed places, ask for a bill, and don't let a driver or guide pick the shop for you.

For foreign visitors

  • Visa: apply for the e-Tourist Visa only at the official site, indianvisaonline.gov.in.
  • Getting there: most visitors fly into Delhi, Mumbai or Ahmedabad, then take a domestic flight or an overnight train to Udaipur.

Emergency numbers and help

  • Emergency (police, fire, ambulance): 112
  • Ambulance in Rajasthan: 108
  • Ministry of Tourism helpline, 24 hours, many languages: 1363 or 1800-11-1363

Questions

Is December a good time to visit Udaipur?

Yes. Winter is the best season: IMD's December averages are about 26 °C by day and 8 °C at night, with almost no rain. The Shilpgram Utsav, usually from 21 to 30 December, adds a big crafts fair. Book early for Christmas and New Year.

How cold is Udaipur in December and January?

Days are warm, about 25–26 °C, but nights are cold, about 7–8 °C on average and 2–5 °C on the coldest nights of most years. January is a little colder than December. Carry a warm jacket for the evenings.

How many days are enough for Udaipur?

Two days are enough for City Palace, a Lake Pichola boat ride, Bagore ki Haveli, Fateh Sagar, Saheliyon ki Bari and a sunset view. Add a third day for Kumbhalgarh and Ranakpur, or Chittorgarh.

What should I wear in Udaipur in December?

Light clothes for the sunny days, and a warm jacket or sweater for the evenings and boat rides. Wear comfortable walking shoes, and cover your shoulders and knees at temples such as Ranakpur.

When is the Shilpgram Utsav?

Usually from 21 to 30 December, at Shilpgram, west of Fateh Sagar. The 2026 dates haven't been announced yet, so check the West Zone Cultural Centre's website before you go.

Is there a Vande Bharat train to Udaipur?

Yes, but only from Surat and Ahmedabad (Asarva). It started in October 2026 and runs every day except Tuesday. The Vande Bharat trains from Jaipur and Agra stopped in February 2026, and there's none from Delhi.

Can I see the sunset from the Monsoon Palace in winter?

Probably not. Sajjangarh closes at about 5:30 PM, and in December the sun sets at about 5:45 PM. For a winter sunset, try the Karni Mata ropeway hill, Ambrai Ghat or a sunset boat ride on Lake Pichola.
