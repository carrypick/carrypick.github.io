Nainital in December: weather, snow, a 2–3 day plan and what to wear

Updated 24 Nov 2026 · 9 min read

Nainital at a glance

Weather in DecemberAbout 14 °C by day and 4 °C at night (IMD averages for Mukteshwar, nearby)
JanuaryColder: about 12 °C and 2 °C
SnowNot certain. When it comes, it's usually late December or January, and mostly on the peaks
HeightAbout 2,000 m
How long2 days for the lake and viewpoints, 3 with Bhimtal or Mukteshwar
Get thereTrain to Kathgodam (35 km) or fly to Pantnagar (70 km); Delhi is 310 km by road
Emergency112, disaster 1070, tourism helpline 1364

Nainital in December is cold, clear and calm: a lake town ringed by forested hills, with sunny days and near-freezing nights. Snow isn't certain. This guide covers the weather, the chances of snow, a simple 2-day and 3-day plan, getting there from Delhi, Christmas and New Year traffic, and what to wear.

Nainital weather in December and January

IMD's long-term averages for Mukteshwar, 51 km from Nainital and a little higher (1991–2020):

MonthDayNightRain
DecemberAbout 14 °CAbout 4 °CAbout 18 mm
JanuaryAbout 12 °CAbout 2 °CAbout 46 mm
FebruaryAbout 14 °CAbout 3 °CAbout 68 mm
  • December is the driest month of winter. It rains on about one day in an average December. January and February are wetter.
  • Days are sunny, nights are cold. Out of the sun, and after dark by the lake, it's close to freezing.
  • IMD's older table for Nainital town (1961–1979) is similar: about 13 °C by day and 3 °C at night in December.

Does it snow in Nainital in December?

  • Not every year. No official page promises December snow in Nainital.
  • When it snows, it's usually late December or January, and mostly on the higher points: Naina Peak (China Peak), Snow View and Kilbury, more often than the lake town.
  • Some recent winters had none in December. News reports said Uttarakhand had no rain or snow at all in December 2025. On 23 January 2026, there was snow on China Peak and Kilbury, but only hail and rain in town (news reports).
  • Want surer snow? Go higher: Auli from January, or Gulmarg and Manali.

Nainital trip plan: 2 days

Day 1: the lake and the town

  1. Naina Devi temple, at the north end of the lake, in the morning. It's one of the Shakti Peeths. Incredible India lists it as open from 6 AM to 10 PM.
  2. Boating on Naini Lake. Rowing, paddle and yachting boats leave from both ends, Mallital in the north and Tallital in the south. Rates are shown at the prepaid booths.
  3. Walk Mall Road from Mallital to Tallital, and come back along Thandi Road on the other side of the lake, which has no traffic.
  4. The post office on the Tallital bridge, which the district calls the only post office on a lake bridge in the world.

Day 2: viewpoints

  1. Aerial ropeway to Snow View (2,270 m), from Mallital, about 200 m from Mall Road. The district page lists it from 10 AM to 4 PM. It can stop in bad weather, so check at the counter.
  2. Tiffin Top (Dorothy's Seat), at 2,292 m, about 4 km from town, for the view over the lake and hills.
  3. Nainital Zoo (High Altitude Zoo), about 1 km from the bus stand. It's closed on Mondays, national holidays, Holi and Diwali.
  4. Naina Peak (China Peak), the highest point at 2,611 m, if you're up for a longer walk.

Nainital trip plan: 3 days

Add one of these:

  • The lakes: Bhimtal, Sattal and Naukuchiatal. Bhimtal (22 km) is bigger than Naini Lake and has an island aquarium. Sattal (23 km) is a group of lakes in oak forest, and Naukuchiatal (26 km) is a nine-cornered lake good for birdwatching.
  • Mukteshwar, 51 km away at 2,286 m, for a Shiva temple on a rock and wide Himalayan views.
  • Kilbury, 12 km away at 2,194 m, for forest walks and mountain views.

Getting to Nainital

From the Nainital district website:

  • By train: the nearest station is Kathgodam, about 35 km away. Trains from Delhi include the Kathgodam Shatabdi Express (every day except Thursday), the Ranikhet Express (overnight from Old Delhi) and the Uttaranchal Sampark Kranti. Check times on NTES or IRCTC before you book.
  • By air: the nearest airport is Pantnagar, about 70 km away, with flights from Delhi.
  • By road: Delhi is about 310 km. Haldwani is 40 km and Kathgodam 34 km. State buses run daily from Delhi.
  • From Kathgodam: taxis and buses take about an hour up the hill.

Nainital in December end: Christmas and New Year

  • Book your stay early. Nainital is one of the most popular New Year trips from Delhi.
  • This year Christmas is on a Friday, and so is New Year's Day (1 January 2027), so both are long weekends.
  • Expect traffic plans and shuttles. For Christmas and New Year 2025–26, news reports said police made Narayan Nagar the main parking area, with shuttle buses into town, and ran a traffic plan at the toll plaza from 22 December to 7 January. In earlier years, cars were also held at the Rusi bypass when the town was full. (News reports, not an official notice.)
  • Carry your hotel booking on your phone, and check Nainital Police updates before you drive up.
  • Go by train to Kathgodam if you can, and take a taxi or bus up.

Things to do in winter

  • Boating on Naini Lake in the midday sun.
  • The ropeway to Snow View for the Himalayan view on clear winter days.
  • Tiffin Top and Naina Peak for walks with a view.
  • Naina Devi temple and the evening walk on Mall Road.
  • Bhimtal, Sattal and Naukuchiatal for quieter lakes.
  • Mukteshwar and Kilbury for forest and mountain views.
  • Jim Corbett, in the same district, for a safari. See Jim Corbett in December.

What to wear in Nainital in December

What should I wear? Try the what-to-wear helper for day and night temperatures and an outfit list by month.

Days are pleasant in the sun, but mornings, evenings and the boat ride are cold.

  • Layers: a thermal top, a sweater or fleece, and a warm jacket for the evenings.
  • Extras: a woollen cap, gloves and warm socks, especially for the lake at night and Snow View.
  • For ladies: jeans or warm leggings with a kurta or top, a cardigan, and a long jacket or shawl. Cover your shoulders at the temple.
  • Shoes: comfortable walking shoes with a good grip. Hill paths are steep, and can be icy on cold mornings. If snow is forecast, take waterproof boots.
  • Sunscreen, sunglasses and lip balm for the dry winter air.

Health and safety

  • Never sleep with a heater or coal fire in a closed room. It gives off carbon monoxide, which can kill without warning.
  • Wear the life jacket on the lake.
  • Watch your step on icy mornings, on steep lanes and the walk to the viewpoints.
  • Don't drive the hill roads after dark if you can avoid it, and use low gears going down.

For foreign visitors

  • Visa: apply for the e-Tourist Visa only at the official site, indianvisaonline.gov.in.

Emergency numbers and help

From the Uttarakhand State Disaster Management Authority, Tourist Care Uttarakhand and the Nainital district website:

  • Emergency (police, fire, ambulance, disaster): 112
  • Police: 100
  • Fire: 101
  • State Emergency Operation Centre: 1070
  • District Emergency Operation Centre: 1077
  • Uttarakhand tourist helpline: 1364
  • Child helpline: 1098
  • Ministry of Tourism helpline, 24 hours, many languages: 1363 or 1800-11-1363

Questions

Does it snow in Nainital in December?

Sometimes, but not every year. When it snows, it's usually in late December or January, and more often on the peaks (China Peak, Snow View, Kilbury) than by the lake. December 2025 had no snow, according to news reports.

What is the temperature in Nainital in December?

IMD's averages for Mukteshwar, nearby and a little higher, are about 14 °C by day and 4 °C at night in December. Nainital's own older averages are similar. Nights are close to freezing.

Is Nainital good in December end?

Yes, for clear views and cold, sunny days. It's also the busiest time, with Christmas and New Year long weekends, so book early and expect traffic plans and shuttle parking.

How many days are enough for Nainital?

Two days for the lake, the temple, the ropeway and Tiffin Top. Add a third for Bhimtal, Sattal and Naukuchiatal, or Mukteshwar.

How do I go from Delhi to Nainital?

By train to Kathgodam (the Shatabdi, the Ranikhet Express or the Sampark Kranti), then about an hour by taxi or bus. By road it's about 310 km. The nearest airport is Pantnagar, about 70 km away.

Is the Nainital ropeway open in December?

The district page lists the aerial ropeway to Snow View from 10 AM to 4 PM. It can stop in bad weather or for maintenance, so check at the Mallital counter.

Nainital or Mussoorie in December?

Both are similar in height and cold. Nainital has the lake and boating, and Kathgodam station is close. Mussoorie has the long Mall Road, Landour and the winter line sunsets, and is close to Dehradun. See Mussoorie in December.

What should I wear in Nainital in December?

A thermal, a sweater and a warm jacket, plus a woollen cap and gloves for the evenings. Wear walking shoes with good grip.
