Kerala in winter: Kochi, Munnar and the backwaters at Christmas and New Year

Updated 4 Nov 2026 · 9 min read

Kerala is at its best from November to February: the monsoon is over, the backwaters are calm, and the hills around Munnar are cool and green. A classic winter trip is Kochi, then Munnar, then a houseboat night in Alappuzha, ending at a beach in Varkala or Kovalam. This guide covers the weather, getting around, New Year in Fort Kochi and the rules that catch visitors out.

Coastal figures are long-term averages from the India Meteorological Department (1991–2020). Munnar's come from tea research station records for January 2026; on some winter nights the hills touch 0 °C, and frost forms on the grass.

So pack light cotton clothes for the coast and the backwaters, and a warm jacket for Munnar's evenings and mornings.

Getting there and around

  • Kochi airport (COK) is the main gateway for Kochi, Munnar and Alappuzha. It's about 28 km from Ernakulam Junction railway station.
  • Thiruvananthapuram airport (TRV) is best for Kovalam, about 14 km away, and Varkala, about 45–50 km away.
  • Trains: Ernakulam Junction and Ernakulam Town serve Kochi. Alappuzha station is in the town, and Varkala Sivagiri station is about 3–4 km from the cliff.
  • Kochi to Munnar: about 110–130 km, roughly 3–4.5 hours up winding hill roads. Munnar has no railway.
  • Kochi to Alappuzha: about 55 km.
  • KSRTC buses connect most towns. Book long trips at onlineksrtcswift.com, linked from keralartc.com.
  • In Kochi: the Kochi Metro runs from Aluva to Thrippunithura, and the Kochi Water Metro boats link Fort Kochi, Mattancherry, Vypin and the High Court jetty.

Kochi and Fort Kochi

  • Chinese fishing nets: giant cantilevered nets on teak and bamboo poles. Watch them from Vasco da Gama Square, best at sunset.
  • St Francis Church: first built in 1503, where Vasco da Gama was buried in 1524 before his body went back to Portugal. Open Monday to Friday 9 AM to 5 PM, Saturday 9 AM to 1 PM and Sunday 1 to 5 PM.
  • Mattancherry Palace (Dutch Palace): built around 1545, with famous murals. Open 10 AM to 5 PM, closed on Fridays.
  • Paradesi Synagogue, Jew Town: built in 1568. It's closed on Saturdays and Jewish holidays, and Kerala Tourism's pages disagree about Fridays, so go Sunday to Thursday. Enter barefoot.
  • Help for visitors: an International Tourism Police Station in Jew Town, Mattancherry, helps with passport, visa and other problems (+91 9497932659).
  • Kochi-Muziris Biennale: there's no Biennale this winter. The official site lists the next edition for 2027–28.

Munnar

  • The hills: Munnar town is about 1,600 m up, surrounded by tea estates.
  • Eravikulam National Park: about 13 km from town, home to about a third of the world's Nilgiri tahr (a mountain goat) and South India's highest peak, Anamudi. Visitors can enter only the Rajamala tourism zone. The official booking site lists entry from 8 AM to 2 PM, ₹200 for Indians and ₹500 for foreign visitors. Book online at eravikulamnationalpark.in. The park closes from 1 February to 1 April each year for the calving season, so December and January are fine.
  • Park rules: no plastic, food, alcohol or smoking, and no loud music.
  • Tea Museum: about 2 km from town, at the Nallathanni estate. Its operator says it's closed on Mondays.
  • Mattupetty: a dam and lake with boating, a 13 km drive away.
  • Top Station: about 40 km away, with views over Tamil Nadu.
  • Holiday traffic: roads between Pallivasal and Mattupetty have jammed in past New Year rushes, so start early.

Alappuzha (Alleppey) backwaters

  • Houseboats: converted rice barges (kettuvallams) that cruise the backwaters and Vembanad Lake, a protected wetland. Day cruises usually run from late morning to about 5 PM. Overnight trips include meals, and boats moor for the night.
  • Book a safe boat. Kerala Tourism points visitors to the District Tourism (DTPC) prepaid houseboat counter in Alappuzha (+91 477 2251796). Classified boats are graded Silver, Gold or Diamond and must display their KIV registration number, name and certificate.
  • Check the safety kit: life jackets for everyone, lifebuoys and fire extinguishers. Wear a life jacket on small boats.
  • Shikara rides: small boats that go into the narrow canals where houseboats can't.

Beaches: Varkala and Kovalam

  • Varkala: red laterite cliffs right above the sea, the only such cliffs in southern Kerala. Kerala Tourism warns the waves can be strong, so check before swimming.
  • Kovalam: Lighthouse Beach has a 36 m lighthouse and calmer water for swimming. Swim only in the flagged areas near lifeguards. Undercurrents can appear suddenly, even near the shore, and foreign tourists have drowned at both beaches.
  • Rough-sea alerts: stay out of the water when the weather office or INCOIS issues a high-wave warning.

Christmas and New Year in Kerala

  • Cochin Carnival, Fort Kochi: runs over the last two weeks of December. At midnight on 31 December, a giant effigy of an old man, the Pappanji, is burnt on the beach to see out the year, and a carnival rally follows on 1 January. Incredible India lists 13 December 2026 as the start date, but the organisers haven't confirmed the 2026 programme yet.
  • Huge crowds: police expected 2–3 lakh people in Fort Kochi last New Year's Eve. Vehicles were stopped from the afternoon, the Vypin–Fort Kochi Ro-Ro ferry stopped at 4 PM, and Water Metro and ferry boats ran only until 7 PM. The celebration zone was a no-drone area. Reach early, or stay in Fort Kochi itself.
  • Metro until late: last New Year's Eve, the Kochi Metro ran its last trains around 1:30 to 2 AM.
  • Dry day on 1 January: in Kerala the first day of every month is a dry day, so liquor shops (BEVCO) and bars stay closed on 1 January 2027, according to news reports. Plan ahead if you want a drink on New Year's Day.
  • Book early: November to February is peak season, and Christmas–New Year is the busiest time for houseboats and hotels.

Rules and tips

  • Plastic: Kerala has banned single-use plastic and plastic carry bags since 2020. Carry a cloth bag and a refillable bottle.
  • Alcohol: sold only at government BEVCO shops and licensed bars, and not on dry days.
  • Temples: Sree Padmanabhaswamy Temple in Thiruvananthapuram allows only Hindus inside. Men must wear a mundu or dhoti with no shirt, and women a sari or similar; dhotis can be rented at the gate. Guruvayur Temple has a similar dress code.
  • Drones: follow India's drone rules, check the Digital Sky map, and don't fly over festival crowds. India doesn't allow most drone imports, so a foreign visitor's drone may be held at customs.

For foreign visitors

  • Visa: apply for the e-Tourist Visa only at indianvisaonline.gov.in, at least 4 days before you travel. Kochi, Thiruvananthapuram, Calicut and Kannur airports all accept e-Visa arrivals, and so does Kochi seaport.
  • Eravikulam tickets: the park has a separate ticket for foreign visitors, and one payment option works only with foreign-issued cards.
  • Help: the International Tourism Police Station in Mattancherry (+91 9497932659) helps foreign visitors with passports, visas and complaints.

Emergency numbers

  • Emergency: 112 (connected to the police in Kerala)
  • Police: 100. Fire: 101. Ambulance: 108.
  • Kerala Tourism: 1-800-425-4747 (free, within India)

Questions

What is the best time to visit Kerala?

November to February. The monsoon is over, the backwaters are calm, and Munnar is cool. Kerala Tourism says houseboat cruises are best from October to February.

How cold is Munnar in December and January?

Cold at night. In January 2026, most nights were between 2 and 9 °C, and some winters touch 0 °C. Days are mild, around 23 °C.

What is the Pappanji burning in Fort Kochi?

At midnight on 31 December, a huge effigy of an old man called the Pappanji is burnt on the beach in Fort Kochi to say goodbye to the old year. It's the highlight of the Cochin Carnival, and lakhs of people come, so reach early.

Is 1 January a dry day in Kerala?

Yes, according to news reports. The first day of every month is a dry day in Kerala, so liquor shops and bars are closed on 1 January.

Is Eravikulam National Park open in December?

Yes. It usually closes from 1 February to 1 April for the Nilgiri tahr calving season. Book tickets online on the official site.

How do I choose a safe houseboat in Alappuzha?

Book through the DTPC prepaid counter in Alappuzha, or check that the boat shows its KIV registration number and classification certificate (Silver, Gold or Diamond). Make sure there are life jackets for everyone on board.
