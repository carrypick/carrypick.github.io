Dubai New Year's Eve 2027: Burj Khalifa fireworks, where to watch for free, and getting home

Updated 22 Nov 2026 · 11 min read

Dubai New Year at a glance

New Year's EveThursday 31 December 2026; 1 January 2027 is a Friday
Main showBurj Khalifa, Downtown Dubai: fireworks, a drone show, the Dubai Fountain and light projections at midnight, says Emaar
Watch for freeDowntown Dubai's public viewing areas, first come, first served. Arrive before road closures start, from about 4 PM
TicketedBurj Park. Emaar says tickets are "coming soon" (22 Nov 2026)
Other fireworks40 locations last year, including Global Village, Atlantis The Royal, Expo City, Dubai Frame and Hatta
Metro last yearNon-stop from 5 AM on 31 December to 11:59 PM on 1 January
TimeDubai is 1 hour 30 minutes behind India, so India's midnight is 10:30 PM in Dubai
EmergencyPolice 999, non-emergency police 901, ambulance 998

Dubai's New Year's Eve is one of the biggest in the world. At midnight the Burj Khalifa lights up with fireworks, and dozens of other places across the city have their own shows. You can watch the main show for free, but you have to plan: roads around Downtown Dubai close from the afternoon, and the crowds are huge. This guide covers what has been announced for New Year's Eve 2027 (the night of 31 December 2026), the free and paid ways to watch, other fireworks spots, getting there and back, and the visa if you're coming from India.

Burj Khalifa fireworks 2027: what's announced

Emaar, which owns the Burj Khalifa and runs the Downtown Dubai celebration, has put up its page for New Year's Eve 2027. On 22 November 2026 it said:

  • At midnight: "fireworks, a drone show, the Dubai Fountain and projection light up Downtown Dubai together". The last 60 seconds of the year are counted down on the tower's front.
  • Cirque du Soleil gives a special one-night performance in Downtown Dubai.
  • Burj Park, the ticketed area, has acts by Cirque du Soleil and the Blue Man Group. Tickets are "coming soon".
  • Road closures are "expected from 4 PM across Downtown Dubai".
  • Watch online: Emaar streams the show live for people who can't be there.

Not announced yet (22 Nov 2026): Burj Park ticket prices and dates, this year's road closure list, and RTA's metro and tram timings. We'll check again by 15 December 2026.

Where to watch for free in Downtown Dubai

Emaar's public viewing areas are free, with no ticket or badge needed. Here's how they worked last year:

  • First come, first served. When an area is full, it closes, and you must follow the staff's directions. Don't try to open the metal barriers.
  • Separate areas for families and for single men. Emaar's guide lists "family viewing areas" and "bachelors viewing areas", both free, so groups of men without families go to their own areas.
  • Big LED screens along Sheikh Mohammed bin Rashid Boulevard show the show up close.
  • Food and non-alcoholic drinks are allowed in the public viewing areas.
  • Arrive before road closures start, from about 4 PM. Taxis can reach Downtown only until then.
  • Take the metro to Burj Khalifa/Dubai Mall station, says Emaar. Its FAQ warns the station "may close depending on crowd conditions", so have a second station in mind.
  • The area around the Burj Khalifa itself is a safety zone. Stay out of the marked area.

Emaar's guide also asks people not to use baby strollers in crowded areas, not to eat or drink outside the food areas, and to cross roads only at zebra crossings.

Dubai Mall and restaurants

Last year, Dubai Mall restaurants and cafes with a view of the fountain and the tower gave their guests a badge to reach the viewing areas inside the mall. The badge doesn't let your car through after the roads close, so arrive early. Book directly with the restaurant, and ask what's included.

Dubai Creek Harbour

Emaar also runs a celebration at Dubai Creek Harbour, across the creek from Downtown, with free public viewing corridors and parking. It's quieter than Downtown, with the Downtown skyline in view, so it suits families who don't want the biggest crowds.

Burj Park tickets

Burj Park is the ticketed area right below the Burj Khalifa. For New Year's Eve 2027, Emaar shows tickets as "coming soon" (22 Nov 2026). Last year:

  • Tickets were sold online only, through the event's official website. Emaar's press release of 24 November 2025 gave AED 950 plus VAT for adults and AED 550 plus VAT for children aged 5 to 12. Children under 5 entered free but still needed a ticket for their badge. Prices for 2027 aren't out yet.
  • Badge pickup was compulsory: from 26 to 30 December, at Dubai Mall or Dubai Hills Mall. There was no badge pickup on 31 December, and lost badges weren't replaced.
  • Gates opened at 12 noon, with entertainment from 5 PM.
  • Not allowed inside: outside food and drinks (except baby items), drones and tripods without permission, laser pointers, horns and whistles, and flags or signs bigger than 30 x 30 cm.

Buy only through Emaar's official site or the seller it names. Resold badges may not work.

Other places to see fireworks in Dubai

You don't have to be in Downtown. Last New Year's Eve, Dubai's Event Security Committee said there were fireworks at 40 locations, with 48 shows. It named Burj Khalifa, Global Village, Bab Al Shams Desert Resort, Atlantis The Royal, Al Marmoom Oasis, Expo City Dubai, Dubai Frame, Emirates Golf Club, Dubai Parks and Resorts and Hatta. Dubai Frame had fireworks and a drone show for the first time.

  • Global Village held seven countdowns on one night last year, one for each of several countries' midnights, said Gulf News. One of them was for India: India's midnight is 10:30 PM in Dubai. Check Global Village's site for this year's times and tickets.
  • Public beaches were for families only on New Year's Eve, and 14 public parks stayed open until 1 AM as viewing areas, the committee said.
  • Hotels and beach clubs across the city sell their own New Year's Eve packages. Read what's included before you pay.
  • The full list for this year usually comes out a few days before New Year's Eve. We'll add it when it's out.

On the water

Last year, RTA ran special Dubai Ferry and abra trips on New Year's Eve from Dubai Marina, Al Ghubaiba, Bluewaters, Al Jaddaf and Al Fahidi, leaving between 10 PM and 10:30 PM and returning by 1:30 AM. Ferry tickets were AED 350 (Silver) and AED 525 (Gold), and abras AED 150 a person. Dubai's maritime authority also closed boat traffic under the Palm Jumeirah bridges from 10 PM to 2 AM. Watch RTA's site for this year's trips.

Getting there and back: metro, roads and taxis

These are last year's plans (New Year's Eve 2026). Expect something similar, and check RTA's website or the S'hail app for this year's timings.

  • Dubai Metro ran non-stop for about 43 hours, from 5 AM on 31 December to 11:59 PM on 1 January, on the Red and Green Lines. The tram ran from 6 AM on 31 December to 1 AM on 2 January.
  • Roads closed from 4 PM, and the closures grew until 11 PM. They included Al Asayel Street, Mohammed bin Rashid Boulevard, Burj Khalifa Street, the Lower and Upper Financial Centre Roads, Al Mustaqbal Street, Al Sukook Street and Sheikh Zayed Road.
  • Parking: about 20,000 spaces around the Burj Khalifa and about 8,000 at metro stations. Emaar said cars could leave Dubai Mall's car parks only between 1 and 2 AM.
  • Going to Abu Dhabi? Last year, the E100 bus from Al Ghubaiba stopped from the afternoon of 31 December until 4 January. RTA told passengers to use the E101 from Ibn Battuta instead.
  • On 1 January, public parking was free (except multi-storey car parks), and RTA's customer centres were closed.
  • Leaving after midnight takes time. More than 2.8 million people used public transport, taxis and ride-hailing apps last New Year's Eve, RTA said. Wait for the crowds to thin, and keep water and a charged phone.

Safety and rules on the night

  • Follow police instructions. Last year, about 9,900 police officers were on duty, with 37 help tents for first aid, lost and found, and lost children.
  • Write your phone number on a card in your child's pocket, and agree on a meeting point in case you get separated.
  • Emergency: police 999, ambulance 998, fire 997. For non-emergency police help, call 901.
  • No alcohol on the metro. Carrying alcohol on public transport can cost AED 500, says RTA. Eating and drinking on the metro costs AED 100.
  • Drones need permission. Don't fly one over the crowds.
  • Carry your medicines if you have diabetes, high blood pressure or a heart condition, Dubai's ambulance service advised last year. A field hospital was set up near the Burj Khalifa.

Visa and flights from India

Most Indians need a UAE visit visa before they fly, arranged through a tourism company, an airline or a hotel. GDRFA Dubai lists AED 200 for 30 days, plus VAT. Indians with a US green card or visa, or a UK or EU residence visa, can get a visa on arrival. The details, fees and documents are in our Dubai trip guide. Apply early: offices and agents slow down over Christmas.

Visiting from another country? Check the visa rules for your passport on the UAE government's official sites before you book.

  • Fares are highest around Christmas and New Year. Book as early as you can.
  • Arrive by 30 December. A delayed flight on 31 December could make you miss the night.
  • The weather is mild: about 26 °C by day and 15 °C at night in December, with rain on about 2 days. Take a light jacket for the night.

Where to stay for New Year's Eve

  • Downtown Dubai puts you closest to the Burj Khalifa show, but rooms cost the most and the roads close around you. Check with your hotel how you reach it after 4 PM.
  • Near a Red Line metro station (Business Bay, Al Barsha or Dubai Marina) is a good balance: ride in and out without a car.
  • Dubai Marina or JBR is good if you'd rather watch from the beach side, or see Atlantis The Royal's fireworks.

What to pack and wear

  • A light jacket or shawl. December nights are about 15 °C, and you'll stand outside for hours.
  • Comfortable shoes. Expect to walk a long way from the metro or your car.
  • A charged phone and a small power bank, in your cabin bag on the flight.
  • Modest clothes for malls and public places, and covered shoulders and knees for a mosque visit.

Questions

What time are the Dubai New Year fireworks?

At midnight on 31 December, Dubai time. Emaar says the Burj Khalifa show combines fireworks, a drone show, the Dubai Fountain and light projections. Some places, like Global Village last year, also have earlier countdowns.

Can I watch the Burj Khalifa fireworks for free?

Yes. Emaar has free public viewing areas in Downtown Dubai, on a first-come, first-served basis, with separate areas for families and for single men. Arrive before road closures start, from about 4 PM.

How much are Burj Park tickets for New Year 2027?

Not announced yet: Emaar shows tickets as "coming soon" (22 Nov 2026). Last year Emaar's press release gave AED 950 plus VAT for adults and AED 550 plus VAT for children aged 5 to 12.

Where else can I see fireworks in Dubai on New Year's Eve?

Last year there were 40 locations, including Global Village, Atlantis The Royal, Expo City Dubai, Dubai Frame, Dubai Parks and Resorts, Al Marmoom Oasis and Hatta. Dubai Creek Harbour is a quieter place to see the Downtown show.

Does the Dubai Metro run all night on New Year's Eve?

Last year it did: the Red and Green Lines ran non-stop from 5 AM on 31 December to 11:59 PM on 1 January. Burj Khalifa/Dubai Mall station may close if it gets too crowded. Check RTA for this year's timings.

What time is Indian New Year in Dubai?

10:30 PM in Dubai. The UAE is 1 hour 30 minutes behind India, so call home at 10:30 PM if you want to wish your family at India's midnight.
