Where to go for New Year 2027: the best New Year trips in India and abroad

Updated 22 Nov 2026 · 11 min read

New Year 2027 at a glance

DatesNew Year's Eve is Thursday 31 December 2026. 1 January 2027 is a Friday, so it makes a 3-day weekend where it's a holiday
Beach partyGoa New Year 2027
Fireworks abroadDubai New Year 2027, or Marina Bay in Singapore

BookNow. New Year week is the busiest and costliest time of the year almost everywhere

New Year's Eve 2027 falls on a Thursday, and New Year's Day on Friday 1 January 2027. If 1 January is a holiday for you, that's a 3-day weekend. Take Thursday off too, and you get four days, from 31 December to 3 January. This page compares the best places for a New Year trip, in India and abroad, with links to our full guides.

New Year in India

Goa: for India's biggest New Year party

New Year's Eve is Goa's busiest night. North Goa (Calangute, Baga, Anjuna, Vagator) has the parties and the traffic jams; South Goa (Colva, Palolem, Agonda) is calmer. Outdoor music is allowed until midnight from 24 to 31 December 2026, and drinking on the beach is fined. This year's party line-ups and prices aren't out yet.

Read our Goa New Year 2027 guide, and the full Goa trip guide.

Kerala: for the Cochin Carnival

Fort Kochi ends the year with the Cochin Carnival. At midnight on 31 December, a giant effigy called the Pappanji is burnt on the beach, and a carnival rally follows on 1 January. Lakhs of people come, so stay in Fort Kochi or reach early. Pair it with Munnar's tea hills and a houseboat night in Alappuzha. The first day of every month is a dry day in Kerala, so bars and liquor shops close on 1 January.

Read the full Kerala trip guide.

Andaman: for a quiet beach New Year

Andaman is warm, with clear sea and coral, and much calmer than Goa. Fly to Sri Vijaya Puram (Port Blair), then take a ferry to Swaraj Dweep (Havelock). Late December is the busiest time, so book flights, ferries and hotels early, and leave a spare day in case boats are cancelled.

Read Andaman in December.

Udaipur and Jaipur: for palaces and forts

Both are sunny by day and cold at night in winter. Udaipur has its lakes and palaces, and the Shilpgram crafts fair (usually 21–30 December). Jaipur has Amer Fort and the old city's markets. Both are full from Christmas to New Year, with traffic rules in the old cities, so book early and walk where you can.

Read the Udaipur and Jaipur guides.

Rishikesh: for a calm start to the year

A short trip from Delhi for the Ganga aarti, yoga and rafting. Days are sunny, nights are cold. New Year brings traffic jams on the Haridwar and Dehradun roads, so arrive early in the day.

Read Rishikesh in December.

Coorg and Meghalaya: for cool, green hills without snow

  • Coorg: cool, dry and green, with coffee estates, Abbey Falls and misty mornings. There's no airport or railway in Kodagu, so most people drive from Bangalore, Mysuru or Mangaluru. New Year's Day is a Friday, so expect full homestays and traffic on the Mysuru road.
  • Meghalaya: Shillong is busy with Christmas lights, and December is the driest month, so Dawki's river runs clear. Nights are cold and the sun sets around 4:30 PM. Fly to Guwahati, about 3 hours from Shillong, and leave early to beat holiday traffic.

Read Coorg in December and Meghalaya in December.

Manali, Shimla and Kashmir: for the mountains and a chance of snow

  • Manali: very cold nights and big New Year crowds. Snow isn't guaranteed: December 2025 was very dry. Rohtang Pass is closed in winter.
  • Shimla: easy from Delhi and Chandigarh, with the toy train from Kalka. Snow is more likely in January and February than at New Year.
  • Kashmir: Gulmarg often has snow in December, and the Gulmarg Gondola runs above it. Carry a postpaid SIM, as most prepaid SIMs from other states don't work there.

Read the Manali, Shimla in December and Kashmir in December guides, and see what to wear on a winter hill trip.

New Year abroad

Dubai: for the Burj Khalifa fireworks

Dubai's midnight show at the Burj Khalifa is free to watch from Downtown Dubai's public viewing areas, and last year there were fireworks at 40 places across the city. The weather is mild, about 26 °C by day. Most Indians need a visa before they fly, so apply early.

Read our Dubai New Year 2027 guide, and the full Dubai trip guide.

Thailand: for beaches and no visa

Since 15 September 2026, Indians can visit Thailand for up to 30 days without a visa. Fill in the free TDAC arrival card in the 3 days before you land. December is warm and mostly dry. Spend New Year's Eve in Bangkok, or on a beach in Phuket or Krabi.

Read the Thailand trip guide.

Singapore: for the Marina Bay countdown

Marina Bay holds Singapore's New Year's Eve countdown; this year's details weren't out on 21 November 2026. Orchard Road's Christmas lights stay up until 3 January. Indians need a visa, applied for through an agent, which usually takes 3 to 5 working days, so apply now.

Read the Singapore trip guide.

Bali, the Maldives, Sri Lanka, Vietnam and Malaysia

  • Bali: villas, beach clubs and temples. It's the wet season, with rain on most days, and prices are highest at New Year. See the Bali guide.
  • Maldives: a resort island for a couple's New Year. Short showers in December, and the costliest time of year. See the Maldives guide.
  • Sri Lanka: a free online ETA for Indians, the same clock as India, and drier weather from January. See the Sri Lanka guide.
  • Vietnam: a cool Hanoi and Ha Long Bay in the north, warm and dry Ho Chi Minh City in the south. You need an e-visa. See the Vietnam guide.
  • Malaysia: Kuala Lumpur, Langkawi and Penang. Our guide says Indians don't need a visa for up to 30 days until 31 December 2026, so check the rule if you arrive after that. See the Malaysia guide.

Travelling from outside India?

Goa, Kerala, Jaipur and Udaipur are easy New Year trips for first-time visitors. Most nationalities need an Indian e-Tourist Visa from the official site only; apply at least 4 days before you arrive. Our visiting India guide and India e-Visa guide cover the steps.

How to choose

  • Want to party? Goa, or Dubai for the fireworks.
  • Want a calm New Year with family? Kerala, Udaipur, Rishikesh, Coorg or Sri Lanka.
  • Want snow? Kashmir has the best chance; Manali and Shimla are more likely to get snow in January.
  • Honeymoon or couple's trip? Andaman, the Maldives, Bali or Udaipur.
  • Short on leave? Pick one place close to home. Moving between places takes most of a day.
  • Short on time to get a visa? Choose India, Thailand or Sri Lanka (free online ETA).

Book now, and travel light

  • Book flights and hotels now. New Year week is the busiest time almost everywhere, and prices rise every week.
  • Trains: reservations open about 60 days before you travel, so seats for late December are already on sale.
  • Leave a spare day before 31 December in case of fog delays in North India, or cancelled ferries.
  • Check your baggage limits: see the baggage rules for Indian airlines and what you can carry on a flight.

Questions

Where should I go for New Year 2027 in India?

Goa for parties, Kerala for the Cochin Carnival in Fort Kochi, Andaman for quiet beaches, Udaipur or Jaipur for palaces, Coorg or Meghalaya for cool green hills, and Kashmir, Manali or Shimla for the mountains and a chance of snow.

Where can I go abroad for New Year from India?

Dubai for the Burj Khalifa fireworks, Thailand with no visa needed for up to 30 days, Singapore for the Marina Bay countdown, or Sri Lanka, Bali, the Maldives, Vietnam or Malaysia.

Is there a long weekend for New Year 2027?

New Year's Day 2027 is a Friday, so you get a 3-day weekend if your office gives 1 January off. It isn't a national holiday everywhere in India, so check your holiday list.

Where can I see snow at New Year?

Gulmarg in Kashmir often has snow in December. Manali and Shimla can get snow at New Year, but it's more likely in January and February, and some winters are dry.
