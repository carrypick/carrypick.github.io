Hampi in December: weather, monuments, a 2-day plan and what to wear

Updated 24 Nov 2026 · 8 min read

Hampi at a glance

Weather in DecemberAbout 30 °C by day and 16 °C at night (IMD averages for Ballari, nearby), almost no rain
Best timeOctober to February, says Karnataka Tourism
MonumentsOpen sunrise to sunset; an ASI entry ticket is needed
Virupaksha temple6 AM to 12 noon and 4:30 to 9 PM
Get thereTrain to Hosapete Junction (about 13 km); Bengaluru is about 340 km
Emergency112; police 100; Karnataka Tourism Police 94485 88866

Hampi in December is warm and dry by day, with cool mornings: the best time to walk among the ruins of Vijayanagara, the 14th to 16th century capital that UNESCO listed in 1986. This guide covers the weather, monument hours and tickets, a simple 2-day plan, getting there from Bengaluru, and what to wear.

Hampi weather in December and January

There's no IMD table for Hampi or Hosapete. These are IMD's averages for Ballari, about 65 km away (1981–2010):

MonthDayNightRain
DecemberAbout 30 °CAbout 16 °CAbout 6 mm
JanuaryAbout 31 °CAbout 16 °CAbout 3 mm
FebruaryAbout 34 °CAbout 18 °CAbout 2 mm
  • December is almost dry, with rain on about one day in two Decembers.
  • Afternoons are hot among the rocks, even in winter. See the big sites early in the morning and late in the afternoon.
  • February is warmer. Karnataka Tourism names October to February as the best time, and summer is very hot.

Monument hours and tickets

From ASI's Hampi page and the Ministry of Tourism:

  • The monuments are open from sunrise to sunset, and an entry ticket is needed, says the Archaeological Survey of India (ASI).
  • Buy the ticket at the counter or online on ASI's ticket portals, asi.paygov.org.in or asi.payumoney.com. We couldn't confirm that Hampi is listed online, so keep cash or UPI for the counter.
  • Which sites are ticketed: the Vittala temple complex, the Zanana enclosure with the Lotus Mahal, and the Elephant Stables are the usual ticketed sites, and many visitors say one ticket covers all three on the same day. ASI's page doesn't list them, so keep your ticket with you.
  • Virupaksha temple is still a living temple, not a ticketed ASI monument. Incredible India gives its hours as 6 AM to 12 noon and 4:30 to 9 PM.
  • Archaeological Museum, Kamalapura: 10 AM to 5 PM, says Incredible India. Many visitors say it's closed on Fridays; that isn't on the official page, so check locally.
  • Battery vehicles run on the stretch to the Vittala temple, according to news reports. We couldn't find an official page, so be ready to walk about 1–2 km (our estimate).

Hampi trip plan: 2 days

Karnataka Tourism suggests 3 days. Two full days cover the main sights.

Day 1: the Sacred Centre

  1. Virupaksha temple in the morning, with its tall gopuram, then a walk down Hampi Bazaar.
  2. Hemakuta Hill and the big Kadalekalu and Sasivekalu Ganesha statues.
  3. Vittala temple in the afternoon light: the stone chariot (on the ₹50 note) and the musical pillars.
  4. Sunset from Hemakuta Hill or Matanga Hill, which the district calls the highest hill in central Hampi. Leave the hill before dark.

Day 2: the Royal Centre

  1. Lakshmi Narasimha and the Badavilinga shrine.
  2. The Royal Enclosure: the Mahanavami Dibba platform and the stepped tank, then the Hazara Rama temple.
  3. The Zanana enclosure and Lotus Mahal, then the Elephant Stables next door.
  4. Archaeological Museum at Kamalapura, before 5 PM.

Have a third day? Add a coracle ride on the Tungabhadra (river levels permitting), the Tungabhadra Dam at Hosapete, or the Daroji Sloth Bear Sanctuary. Across the river near Anegundi is the area the district identifies with Kishkindha of the Ramayana.

Getting to Hampi

From Karnataka Tourism and the Vijayanagara district site:

  • By train: the nearest station is Hosapete Junction, about 12–13 km from Hampi. Overnight trains run from Bengaluru, including the Hampi Express. Check times on NTES or IRCTC.
  • By road: Bengaluru to Hampi is about 340 km, says Karnataka Tourism.
  • By air: Jindal Vijayanagar Airport (Toranagallu) is about 38 km away, with daily UDAAN flights to Bengaluru and Hyderabad, says the district site. Hubballi Airport is about 150 km away, with more flights.
  • Getting around: autos, rented bikes or bicycles from Hampi Bazaar and Kamalapura. Many sites are a short walk apart.

Hampi in December end: Christmas and New Year

  • This year Christmas is on a Friday, and so is New Year's Day (1 January 2027), so both are long weekends. Expect more visitors at Virupaksha and the Vittala temple.
  • Book trains and stays early. Overnight trains from Bengaluru fill up before long weekends.
  • Hampi Utsav isn't in December. Karnataka Tourism's page still shows 13–15 February 2026, and news reports say it may move to early November. We couldn't confirm 2027 dates.

What to wear in Hampi in December

What should I wear? Try the what-to-wear helper for day and night temperatures and an outfit list by month.

  • Light cotton clothes for the day: it's about 30 °C in the sun among the rocks.
  • Cover your shoulders and knees for Virupaksha and other temples.
  • A light jacket or shawl for early mornings and evenings, which can dip to about 16 °C.
  • Shoes you can slip off. Leave your footwear outside temples, as Karnataka Tourism advises. Wear shoes with a good grip for the hills, not flip-flops.
  • A cap, sunglasses and sunscreen, and a water bottle. There's little shade at the big sites.
  • A small torch if you climb a hill for sunset.

For a full list, see the weekend trip packing checklist.

Health and safety

  • Carry water and rest in the shade at midday. Heat tires you quickly on the rocks.
  • Don't climb on the monuments or touch the carvings, and don't fly a drone: Karnataka Tourism advises against flying drones without permission, and news reports say ASI boards at Hampi ban them.
  • Take care on the river. Use a licensed coracle, wear a life jacket, and don't swim in the Tungabhadra: its currents and rocks are dangerous (general advice).
  • Come down from the hills before dark, and go with others.

For foreign visitors

  • Monument tickets for foreign visitors are sold at a different rate. Keep your passport with you.
  • Visa: apply for the e-Tourist Visa only at the official site, indianvisaonline.gov.in.

Emergency numbers and help

  • Emergency (police, fire, ambulance): 112
  • Police: 100. Ambulance: 108. Fire: 101
  • Karnataka Tourism Police: 94485 88866
  • Karnataka Tourism helpline: 89706 50021 (5:30 AM to 10 PM)
  • ASI Hampi Circle office, at the Virupaksha temple: 08394-241237
  • Ministry of Tourism helpline, 24 hours, many languages: 1363 or 1800-11-1363

Questions

Is December a good time to visit Hampi?

Yes. Karnataka Tourism names October to February as the best time. IMD's averages for nearby Ballari are about 30 °C by day and 16 °C at night in December, with almost no rain.

What is the temperature in Hampi in December?

About 30 °C in the afternoon and about 16 °C at night, from IMD's averages for Ballari, the nearest station with a table. Mornings and evenings are pleasant; midday in the sun is hot.

What are the Hampi monument timings?

ASI says the monuments are open from sunrise to sunset. The Virupaksha temple is open from 6 AM to 12 noon and 4:30 to 9 PM, and the museum at Kamalapura from 10 AM to 5 PM.

Do I need a ticket for Hampi?

Yes, for the ticketed ASI monuments, such as the Vittala temple complex. Buy it at the counter or on ASI's ticket portals. The Virupaksha temple is a living temple and isn't an ASI ticketed site.

How many days are enough for Hampi?

Two full days cover the main sights. Karnataka Tourism suggests 3 days, which leaves time for a coracle ride, the dam or Anegundi.

How do I reach Hampi from Bangalore?

By the overnight train to Hosapete Junction, about 12–13 km from Hampi, then an auto. By road it's about 340 km. Small flights also go from Bengaluru to Jindal Vijayanagar Airport, about 38 km away.

Is Hampi crowded at Christmas and New Year?

Busier than usual, as both fall on Fridays this year. Start early: the sites are quiet at sunrise.

What should I wear in Hampi in December?

Light cotton clothes that cover your shoulders and knees, a cap and sunscreen, a light layer for the mornings, and shoes with a good grip that you can slip off at temples.
