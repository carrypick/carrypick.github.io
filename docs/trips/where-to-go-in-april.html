Where to go in April: places to visit in India and abroad

Updated 25 Nov 2026 · 17 min read

India and abroad in April at a glance

Long weekendsMahavir Jayanti (17–19 April, no leave) and Ram Navami (15–19 April, 1 day off): up to 10 days with 4 days off
For cool hillsShimla, Manali, Mussoorie, Nainital, Darjeeling and Ooty
For flowersKashmir: the tulip garden in Srinagar opens in spring; 2027 dates to be announced
For the seaAndaman: the dry season runs to April
AbroadJapan, Europe, Uzbekistan, Turkey, Vietnam and Bali as its dry season starts

April is when the plains of India start to get hot, and the hills fill up. It's the start of the summer season in Shimla, Manali, Mussoorie, Nainital, Darjeeling and Ooty, Kashmir's tulip garden is in flower, the Char Dham temples in Uttarakhand open late in the month or in early May, and the tiger reserves have their last good months before the monsoon. The Andaman is at the end of its dry season.

Abroad, April is spring: the last cherry blossom in northern Japan, mild days in Europe, Turkey and Uzbekistan, the best weather of the year in Vietnam, and the start of Bali's dry season.

This page sums up our trip guides that fit April, in India and abroad, with the dates we could confirm and the long weekends of April 2027. Each place links to its full guide, where the official sources are listed.

Visiting India from abroad? Start here: our visiting India guide covers the e-Visa, what to pack, SIM cards and money. April is hot in most of the plains, so plan the hills or the coast for the middle of the day.

Going earlier? See where to go in March. Going a little later? See where to go in May, for the school summer holidays. Only looking abroad? Compare all our international trips from India by flight time, best months and visa type.

April 2027 dates to know

  • Ram Navami: Thursday 15 April 2027, and Mahavir Jayanti: Monday 19 April 2027, both gazetted holidays on the central government list from DoPT. Together they make the best long break of the spring: see below.
  • Ugadi, Gudi Padwa and Chaitra Navratri: Wednesday 7 April 2027, a restricted holiday on the DoPT list.
  • Baisakhi (Vaisakhi), Vishu and the Tamil New Year: Wednesday 14 April 2027, and Bahag Bihu and Poila Boishakh: Thursday 15 April 2027. All are restricted holidays on the central list, but they are full holidays in many states, so check your state's list.
  • Easter is early in 2027: Good Friday is 26 March and Easter Sunday is 28 March, so there's no Easter weekend in April this year.
  • Tulip garden, Srinagar: in 2026 the Indira Gandhi Memorial Tulip Garden opened on 16 March and closed on 16 April. The 2027 dates are to be announced. See below.
  • Char Dham: in 2026 Gangotri and Yamunotri opened on 19 April (Akshaya Tritiya), Kedarnath on 22 April and Badrinath on 23 April. The 2027 dates are to be announced. See below.
  • Golden Week in Japan: late April to early May (29 April and 3–5 May are national holidays), says JNTO. Trains and hotels in Japan fill up, so avoid it or book early.

Long weekends in April 2027

April 2027 has two gazetted holidays four days apart: Ram Navami (Thursday 15 April) and Mahavir Jayanti (Monday 19 April). On the DoPT holiday list for central government offices in Delhi, that gives:

  • Mahavir Jayanti: Saturday 17 to Monday 19 April, 3 days with no leave.
  • Ram Navami and Mahavir Jayanti: Thursday 15 to Monday 19 April, 5 days with 1 day off (Friday 16 April).
  • With Baisakhi: Wednesday 14 to Monday 19 April, 6 days with Friday 16 April off and your restricted holiday on 14 April.
  • The big one: Saturday 10 to Monday 19 April, 10 days with 4 days off (Monday 12, Tuesday 13, Wednesday 14 and Friday 16 April), or 3 if you use your restricted holiday on 14 April.
  • Ugadi and Gudi Padwa: Saturday 3 to Wednesday 7 April, 5 days with 3 days off (Monday 5, Tuesday 6 and Wednesday 7 April), or 2 with your restricted holiday on 7 April.

Hill stations to visit in April

April is the first hill month of the year for many families in the north. The hills are green, the snow is mostly gone from the towns, and the days are mild.

  • Shimla: an easy drive or toy-train ride from Kalka. Weekends from Delhi and Chandigarh get crowded, so go on weekdays if you can.
  • Manali: green valleys and snow still on the peaks. Himachal Tourism says Rohtang Pass usually reopens around May, so in April go up to Solang and the Atal Tunnel side only as far as the road is open. News reports say Kullu-Manali airport (Bhuntar) had no direct Delhi flights this winter, with flights from Jaipur and Dehradun until 27 March 2027; check the summer schedule before you plan.
  • Mussoorie and Nainital: March to June is their main season, with clear, mild days. Both are an easy trip from Delhi, and the new Delhi–Dehradun Expressway makes Mussoorie quicker.
  • Darjeeling: April is spring tea season and a good time for the gardens and the toy train. Autumn has clearer mountain views, so don't count on Kanchenjunga.
  • Sikkim (Gangtok): a common add-on from Darjeeling. Foreigners need a permit for Sikkim, so check the current rules first. We don't have a full Gangtok guide yet.
  • Ooty: in season from October to June, and the easiest hill escape from Bengaluru, Mysuru and Coimbatore.
  • Munnar: Eravikulam National Park usually closes from about 1 February to 1 April for the Nilgiri tahr calving season, so it should be open again in April. Check the booking site before you go.
  • Coorg: its best season is October to March, so April is warmer, but it's still a green, easy weekend from Bengaluru.

Pack light layers for the hills: warm days, cool evenings. Our hill trip checklist and what to wear helper help.

Tulip garden in Kashmir in April

The Indira Gandhi Memorial Tulip Garden in Srinagar, near Dal Lake, is billed by All India Radio as Asia's largest tulip garden. It opens for only about a month each spring.

  • In 2026 it opened on 16 March, with about 18 lakh tulips of more than 70 varieties, said All India Radio. Newspapers reported a notice from the Floriculture department closing it on the evening of 16 April 2026. In 2024 it opened on 23 March.
  • The 2027 dates are to be announced. The garden opens when the tulips are ready, so the date moves with the weather. Watch J&K Tourism and the Floriculture department for the announcement.
  • Go early in the season for the most flowers, and early in the morning for fewer crowds. Weekends in late March and early April are the busiest.
  • Ticket prices: we couldn't confirm them from an official source, and news reports differ, so check at the garden.

Read our Kashmir guide for getting there (including the Vande Bharat train to Srinagar), and pack with our Kashmir packing list. Spring nights in Srinagar are still cool, so bring a jacket.

Char Dham, Kedarnath and Badrinath opening

The Char Dham temples in Uttarakhand, Yamunotri, Gangotri, Kedarnath and Badrinath, close for the winter in November and open again in late April or early May. The 2027 opening dates are to be announced. Gangotri and Yamunotri traditionally open on Akshaya Tritiya, and the temple committees fix the other dates each year.

TempleOpened in 2026Closed in 2026
Gangotri19 April (Akshaya Tritiya)10 November
Yamunotri19 April (Akshaya Tritiya)11 November
Kedarnath22 April11 November (Bhai Dooj)
Badrinath23 AprilAbout 16 November
  • Registration is compulsory. The Uttarakhand government's portal, registrationandtouristcare.uk.gov.in, says you must register yourself and your vehicle before you start. It's free, and your registration is checked at the temples.
  • The first weeks are the busiest. More than 65 lakh pilgrims visited the four temples in 2026, says All India Radio. Book stays early, and expect long queues around the opening days and in May and June.
  • Start from Rishikesh or Haridwar. The road to the temples starts there, and Rishikesh is a good place to rest a day on the way.
  • Kedarnath is a steep walk at high altitude. Walk slowly, carry warm clothes and a rain jacket, and see a doctor first if you have a heart or lung problem.

Wildlife safaris in April

April and May are the last months before the parks close for the monsoon, and hot days bring animals to the water holes.

  • Jim Corbett: Dhikala is open from 15 November to 15 June, and Bijrani from mid-November to June. Book the permit only on the official site.
  • Ranthambore: the park is open from October to June. Morning safaris are cooler, so take an early slot.
  • Rajaji (near Rishikesh): the Chilla zone is open from 15 November to 15 June.

Carry water, a cap and sunscreen: the jeeps are open, and the afternoon sun is strong.

Beaches in April

  • Andaman: February to April is the driest stretch, with the calmest sea and clearest water. Jolly Buoy is open until mid-May.
  • Goa: hot and humid, and quiet after the winter season. Swim at beaches with lifeguards, and go out in the morning and evening.
  • Kerala: hot and humid on the coast before the monsoon. If you go, head up to the hills: see Kerala and Munnar.

Places to visit in April with family and kids

  • With family: Shimla, Mussoorie, Nainital and Ooty for cool air, the toy trains of Shimla and Darjeeling, or Kashmir in tulip season.
  • With kids or a baby: pick places with short drives and a good hotel: Nainital, Mussoorie or Ooty. Keep afternoons for rest. Abroad, Singapore is easy with children, and its drier months run to April.
  • With friends: rafting in Rishikesh, a 10-day Ram Navami break in Himachal or Darjeeling, or a short trip to Vietnam or Uzbekistan.
  • Near Delhi: Mussoorie, Nainital, Rishikesh and Shimla. See weekend getaways from Delhi, and from Mumbai and Bangalore.

Japan, South Korea and the last cherry blossom

Japan's cherry blossom starts in late March in Tokyo and Kyoto and moves north through April. JNTO says it reaches the Tohoku region from mid- to late April and Hokkaido from late April to early May, so April is the month for blossom in the north. Tokyo averages about 19 °C by day and 10 °C at night in April. South Korea has its spring flowers in April and May; in 2026 the first blossoms reached Seoul around 3 April.

Europe, Turkey and Central Asia

Europe is mild in April: about 13 °C by day in Zurich, 15 °C in Paris and 18 °C in Rome. Pack a warm layer for the evenings. Turkey is at its best from April to June. Uzbekistan has warm days and cool nights for walking around Samarkand and Bukhara. Kazakhstan and Georgia are green in spring, but April and May are rainy, and the mountain lakes and the Kazbegi road are best from May or June. See also Azerbaijan, where April to June is best.

Vietnam, Bali and Southeast Asia

Vietnam has its best weather of the year for the whole country from March to May, so it's a strong pick for April, north and south. Bali starts its drier season in April. Thailand is at its hottest: April is Bangkok's hottest month, about 35 °C by day. Singapore and the Maldives are at the end of their drier months.

Bhutan and Nepal

Bhutan and Nepal are good in spring, the second trekking season of the year after autumn. October to December has the clearest mountain views. Indians don't need a visa for either. Visitors from other countries pay Bhutan's Sustainable Development Fee of US$100 a night, which the Department of Tourism has kept at that rate until 31 December 2029; Indians pay a lower fee in rupees. See also Bhutan by road.

Questions

Which is the best place to visit in April in India?

For cool hills, Shimla, Manali, Mussoorie, Nainital, Darjeeling or Ooty. For flowers, Srinagar in tulip season. For the sea, the Andaman, at the end of its dry season. For a tiger safari, Corbett or Ranthambore before they close for the monsoon.

Which places are cold or cool in April in India?

The hill stations: Kashmir, Manali, Shimla, Mussoorie, Nainital, Darjeeling and Sikkim in the north and east, and Ooty and Munnar in the south. Higher up, around Rohtang and Gulmarg's upper slopes, there can still be snow.

Where can I go outside India in April?

Vietnam (the best weather of the year), Japan for the last cherry blossom, Europe, Turkey and Uzbekistan for mild spring days, Bali as its dry season starts, and Bhutan or Nepal with no visa for Indians. Thailand is at its hottest.

Is there a long weekend in April 2027?

Yes. Mahavir Jayanti on Monday 19 April gives 3 days with no leave (17–19 April). With Friday 16 April off you get 5 days from Ram Navami on Thursday 15 April. Take 4 days off (12, 13, 14 and 16 April) for 10 days from Saturday 10 April. See our long weekends 2027 planner.

When does the tulip garden in Srinagar open in 2027?

The 2027 dates are to be announced. In 2026 it opened on 16 March and closed on 16 April, so plan for late March or early April and watch for the official announcement from J&K's Floriculture department.

When do Kedarnath and Badrinath open in 2027?

Not yet. The 2027 opening dates are to be announced. In 2026, Kedarnath opened on 22 April and Badrinath on 23 April, and Gangotri and Yamunotri on 19 April, Akshaya Tritiya. The dates are fixed by the temple committees each year, so check the official Badrinath-Kedarnath Temple Committee site, and register for the yatra on the Uttarakhand government's portal before you go.
