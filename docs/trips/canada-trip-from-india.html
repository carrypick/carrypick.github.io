Canada trip from India: the visitor visa, direct flights, a 10-day plan and the best time to go

Updated 23 Nov 2026 · 15 min read

Visa validityUp to 10 years, or until your passport or biometrics expire

Canada is a big trip for many Indian families, often to visit relatives as well as to sightsee: the roar of Niagara Falls, the Toronto skyline, the old streets of Montreal and Quebec City, the turquoise lakes and glaciers of Banff in the Rocky Mountains, and the mountains-meet-the-sea city of Vancouver. There are direct flights from Delhi, but it's a long way, and Indians need a visa with biometrics, so plan early. This guide covers the visa, biometrics, the processing time, flights, a 10-day plan, the weather by month, customs rules, getting around and food for vegetarians.

Canada visitor visa for Indians

Indians need a visitor visa (also called a temporary resident visa, TRV) to visit Canada. India is on the list of countries whose citizens need a visitor visa to visit or transit through Canada, says Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC, read 23 Nov 2026).

  • Step 1: apply online. IRCC says you must apply online, through its portal on the visitor visa page. Choose "to visit as a tourist".
  • Step 2: pay the fees. The visitor visa fee is CAN$100 per person, and CAN$500 at most for a family of 5 or more applying together. The biometrics fee is CAN$85 per person, and CAN$170 at most for a family of 2 or more applying together, says IRCC's fee list. You pay by card at the end of the application.
  • Step 3: give your biometrics. After you apply you get a biometric instruction letter. You then have 30 days to give your fingerprints and photo at a visa application centre (VAC). See below.
  • Step 4: wait for the decision. If you're approved, you send your passport for the visa.
  • Your passport should be valid for at least 6 months from the date you plan to travel, says IRCC.
  • Don't buy tickets until you have the visa.
  • You can't work on a visitor visa.

Biometrics in India: where to give them

  • Where: at a visa application centre run by VFS Global. IRCC lists VACs in India in New Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata, Chandigarh, Jalandhar, Pune and Ahmedabad (read 23 Nov 2026). Book a free appointment through IRCC's where to give biometrics page.
  • Once every 10 years: visitors need to give biometrics only once every 10 years, says IRCC. If you gave them recently, you may not need to give them again.

Canada visa processing time from India

Processing times change every week. Check the current time for a visitor visa applied for from India on IRCC's official check processing times tool: choose "Visitor visa" and "India". We don't quote a number here because it changes. The time doesn't include giving biometrics, so apply well before your trip.

How long is the visa valid, and how long can you stay?

  • Validity: a visitor visa can be issued for up to 10 years, or until your passport or biometrics expire, whichever comes first, says IRCC. The officer decides which visa to give you.
  • Stay: most visitors can stay up to 6 months. The border services officer at the airport may allow you less or more, and may write the date in your passport. If there's no date, you can stay 6 months from the day you entered, or until your passport or biometrics expire, whichever comes first, says IRCC.

Can Indians use the eTA with a US visa?

No. Canada's eTA (electronic travel authorization) is for visa-exempt travellers and for citizens of a short list of countries with a US visa, and India isn't on that list, says IRCC (read 23 Nov 2026). Indians need the visitor visa, even with a valid US visa.

Flying through Canada to the USA or elsewhere? India isn't in Canada's transit-without-visa program, so Indians need a visa to transit through Canada too, says IRCC.

Flights from India to Canada

  • There are direct flights from India. Air India flies from Delhi to Toronto and Vancouver, and from Mumbai to Toronto in some seasons. Air Canada flies from Delhi to Toronto, say the airports' airline lists (Wikipedia, read 23 Nov 2026).
  • From other Indian cities, fly one-stop via Delhi, or through the Gulf (Dubai with Emirates, Abu Dhabi with Etihad) or Europe (London, Frankfurt, Amsterdam, Paris).
  • It's a long flight. Toronto is about 11,600 km from Delhi in a straight line, and Vancouver about 11,100 km: a direct flight takes roughly 14 to 16 hours.
  • Time difference: India is 10 hours 30 minutes ahead of Toronto and Montreal, and 13 hours 30 minutes ahead of Vancouver, in the Canadian winter. From mid-March to early November, during daylight saving time, India is one hour less ahead (9 hours 30 minutes and 12 hours 30 minutes).
  • Baggage: check the limits by airline in the international baggage rules and the carry-on size by airline guide.
  • Fares go up for the Indian summer holidays, Diwali, Christmas and New Year. Book early, but only after you have your visa.

A 10-day Canada itinerary: Toronto, Niagara, Banff and Vancouver

Most first trips from India combine Toronto and Niagara Falls in the east with the Rocky Mountains and Vancouver in the west, with one domestic flight between them. This plan uses 10 days.

1Land in Toronto and rest. The waterfront along Lake OntarioThe CN Tower

3Toronto: the Royal Ontario Museum, Kensington Market and the Distillery DistrictDinner in Gerrard Street's Indian Bazaar or Brampton
4Fly to Calgary (about 4 hours), then drive to Banff (about 1½ hours)Banff town

9A day trip on the Sea-to-Sky Highway to Whistler, or the Capilano Suspension Bridge and Grouse MountainBack in Vancouver

  • Two weeks? Add Ottawa, Montreal and Quebec City by train from Toronto, or Victoria on Vancouver Island by ferry.
  • Distances are huge. Toronto to Vancouver is a flight of about 4 to 5 hours, so don't try to see everything in one trip.
  • Banff National Park needs a Parks Canada admission pass for each day you stop in the park. Buy it on Parks Canada's website or at the park gate.

  • Late September and October bring autumn colours to Ontario and Quebec.

  • Vancouver is mild but rainy from October to March; summer is dry and sunny.

Getting around Canada

  • Between east and west, fly. Domestic flights are the only practical way to cover the distances. Low fares often don't include a checked bag.
  • Trains link Toronto, Ottawa, Montreal and Quebec City.
  • In Toronto and Vancouver, use the subway, SkyTrain and buses. Toronto's PRESTO and Vancouver's Compass card work on public transport, and you can also tap a contactless card on many routes.
  • In the Rockies, rent a car or join a tour. Canada drives on the right. Check the rules for driving on an Indian licence in the province you visit, and get an International Driving Permit from your RTO.

Food for Indian travellers: veg and Jain tips

Canada is one of the easiest countries abroad for Indian food:

  • Indian restaurants and grocery shops are everywhere in Toronto (especially Brampton and Mississauga) and Vancouver (especially Surrey), and in Calgary and Montreal.
  • Vegetarian options are on almost every menu, and "vegetarian" and "vegan" are widely understood.
  • Easy veg choices: pizza, pasta, burritos and bowls with beans, veggie burgers, and Thai, Chinese and Middle Eastern food. Poutine (chips with cheese curds and gravy) usually has meat gravy, so ask.
  • Jain travellers: most restaurant food uses onion and garlic. Ask for food without them, or carry ready-to-eat food.
  • Carrying food from India? Declare it. Canada limits the food you can bring for personal use, says the Canada Border Services Agency. Check what you can pack in our can I carry it on a flight? list.

Phone, SIM and eSIM in Canada

You have three ways to stay online in Canada:

  • Roaming on your own SIM: the easiest, but often the costliest. Check your mobile company's international pack before you fly.
  • A local SIM card: sold at phone shops and some airport shops.
  • An eSIM: a data plan you buy online and install on your phone before you go, with no plastic card. Your phone must support eSIM and must not be locked to one network.

Mobile coverage is weak on parts of the Icefields Parkway and other mountain roads. Download offline maps before you drive.

Install the eSIM at home over Wi-Fi and switch it on when you land. Most travel eSIMs give data only, with no local number, so keep your home SIM on for calls and bank OTP texts (roaming charges may apply).

Where to book your stay

In Toronto, stay downtown near Union Station to walk to the waterfront and the CN Tower. At Niagara, hotels near the falls have views but cost more. Money and rules to know

  • The currency is the Canadian dollar (CA$, CAD). Cards, including contactless, work almost everywhere.
  • Tipping is expected in restaurants, taxis and hotels.
  • Prices usually don't include sales tax, which is added at the till and differs by province.
  • Cash from India: RBI lets you buy up to US$3,000 in foreign currency notes per trip, and you can carry up to ₹25,000 in Indian notes. Put the rest on a forex card. See how much cash you can carry abroad.
  • CAN$10,000 or more? Declare it. You must declare currency and monetary instruments of CAN$10,000 or more when you enter or leave Canada, says the Canada Border Services Agency.
  • Plugs: Canada uses 120 V and flat two-pin or three-pin plugs, different from India's. Read our power bank and travel adapter guide.
  • Health insurance: healthcare in Canada is expensive for visitors. Buy travel insurance with medical cover before you fly.

What to wear in Canada

  • Winter (November to March): a warm, windproof coat, sweaters, thermal innerwear, gloves, a woolly cap and waterproof boots with a good grip for ice.
  • Spring and autumn: layers, a warm jacket and comfortable shoes.
  • Summer: light clothes, but carry a fleece for cool mountain evenings in Banff, and a rain jacket for Vancouver.

Help in an emergency

  • Emergency number in Canada: 911.
  • High Commission of India, Ottawa: contact details are on its website. India also has consulates in Toronto and Vancouver.
  • Keep a photo of your passport, your visa and your insurance papers on your phone, and save your hotel's address.

Questions

Do Indians need a visa for Canada?

Yes. Indians need a Canadian visitor visa, applied for online with IRCC, and usually have to give biometrics at a visa application centre in India.

What is the Canada tourist visa fee for Indians?

CAN$100 for the visitor visa plus CAN$85 for biometrics, per person, says IRCC. Families applying together pay at most CAN$500 for visas and CAN$170 for biometrics.

How long does a Canada visitor visa take from India?

It changes every week, so we don't quote a number. Check IRCC's official processing times tool for a visitor visa from India, and apply early: giving biometrics adds time.

Can Indians with a US visa go to Canada without a visa?

No. The eTA with a US visa is only for a short list of countries, and India isn't one. Indians need the Canadian visitor visa.

How long is a Canada tourist visa valid for Indians?

Up to 10 years, or until your passport or biometrics expire, whichever comes first, says IRCC. Each visit is usually up to 6 months; the border officer decides.

Where do I give biometrics for a Canada visa in India?

At a VFS Global visa application centre: IRCC lists New Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata, Chandigarh, Jalandhar, Pune and Ahmedabad. You have 30 days from your biometric instruction letter.

Are there direct flights from India to Canada?

Yes. Air India flies direct from Delhi to Toronto and Vancouver, and from Mumbai to Toronto in some seasons. Air Canada flies Delhi to Toronto.

How many days are enough for a Canada trip?

Ten days is enough for Toronto, Niagara Falls, Banff and Vancouver if you fly between east and west. With two weeks, add Montreal and Quebec City. With a week, pick one side.

What is the time difference between India and Canada?

In the Canadian winter, India is 10 hours 30 minutes ahead of Toronto and 13 hours 30 minutes ahead of Vancouver. During daylight saving time (mid-March to early November), it's 9 hours 30 minutes and 12 hours 30 minutes.
