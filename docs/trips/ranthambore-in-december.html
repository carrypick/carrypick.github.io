Ranthambore in December: safari booking, zones, timings and what to wear

Updated 24 Nov 2026 · 10 min read

Ranthambore at a glance

Weather in DecemberAbout 25 °C by day and 10 °C at night (IMD averages for Sawai Madhopur)
Safari times, 1 Nov–31 Jan7 to 10:30 AM and 2 to 5:35 PM
BookOnly on the official site the Rajasthan Forest Department links to, booking.forestrajasthan.com
Booking opens1 Nov for January to March; Tatkal 7 days ahead
Zones10, by 6-seat Gypsy or 20-seat Canter, with a guide
Get thereTrain to Sawai Madhopur (park 14 km away); Jaipur airport about 166 km
Emergency112; police 100; Ministry of Tourism 1363

Ranthambore in December is cold in the morning, sunny by day and good for a tiger safari: the park is open, the forest is drier and thinner than after the monsoon, and the old fort stands over the lakes. This guide covers how to book the safari on the official site, the zones, the winter timings, the rules, the weather, a simple 2-day plan, getting there from Jaipur and Delhi, and what to wear.

How to book the Ranthambore safari online

  • Which site is official: the Rajasthan Forest Department's safari booking page links "Online Booking/Permit for Ranthambore Tiger Reserve" to forestrajasthan.com. Bookings are made on booking.forestrajasthan.com, run by the Rajasthan Tiger Conservation Foundation. Start from the government page, so you know the link is the real one.
  • Look-alike sites: many private sites use "Ranthambore" in their names and sell safaris. The reserve says to book only on its official website, and that agents must say they're agents, or the ticket can be cancelled without a refund.
  • Old address: Rajasthan Tourism's page still names fmdss.forest.rajasthan.gov.in. That address now goes to the Forest Department's new portal, which doesn't list Ranthambore.

When booking opens (from the official site):

BookingFor visitsOpens
Advance1 Oct–31 Dec1 August, 12 noon
Advance1 Jan–31 Mar1 November, 12 noon
Tatkal7 days aheadDaily at 11 AM
Premium Tatkal3 days ahead (can be cancelled)Daily at 10 AM and 6 PM, from seats left
CurrentThis afternoon or tomorrow morningDaily at 10 AM
  • For December 2026, advance booking has been open since 1 August, so most seats may be gone. Try Tatkal 7 days ahead, or Premium Tatkal.
  • Choose your zone when you book. The reserve can change your zone or close a zone at any time.
  • ID: carry the same original ID (or DigiLocker ID) you entered when booking. If it doesn't match, the permit is treated as fake. The first person named must come on the safari. Bring two copies of the confirmation slip.
  • Children under 5 don't need a ticket.
  • Full payment is taken when you book, and includes the guide and vehicle.
  • Refunds: about 75% if you cancel a month or more ahead, 50% up to about 3 days before, and nothing in the last 3 days. Tatkal tickets can't be refunded, and there's no refund for bad weather.
  • Help: the reserve's booking office in Sawai Madhopur, +91 94614 12641.

Safari zones and vehicles

The official site lists 10 zones, each with its own route:

ZoneKey points
1Singhdwar, Raipur, Sultanpur, Kalapan
2Jogimahal, Jhalra, Kamaldha, Gandhria
3Jogimahal, Padam Talab, Rajbagh
4Lakarda, Berda, Adidant
5Jokha, Kachida, Bagdah, Bakola
6Kundal, Mansarovar, Guda
7Chidikho, Kushalidarra
8Balas, Kalibhat, Mahakho
9Qualji, Ghati ka Tiraha, Pandu Kho
10Halonda, Kailashpuri, Devpura
  • Zones 3 and 4 take in the lakes (Padam Talab and Rajbagh) below the fort.
  • Gypsy: an open jeep with 6 seats. Canter: an open bus with 20 seats, shared.
  • A trained guide goes with every vehicle.
  • Many sites call zones 1–5 "core" and 6–10 "buffer". We couldn't find this on an official page.

From the official timings table:

  • Be at the booking counter at least 45 minutes before your entry time.
  • The park is open from October to June. Some sites say safaris stop on Wednesday afternoons; the official table doesn't show this, so check your permit.

Ranthambore weather in December and January

IMD's averages for Sawai Madhopur (1991–2020):

MonthDayNightRain
DecemberAbout 25 °CAbout 10 °CAbout 2 mm
JanuaryAbout 23 °CAbout 8 °CAbout 8 mm
FebruaryAbout 28 °CAbout 12 °CAbout 8 mm
  • Almost no rain, and sunny days.
  • Cold mornings. The lowest on record is 1 °C in December and −1 °C in January. At 7 AM in an open jeep, it feels much colder.

Ranthambore trip plan: 2 days

Day 1: arrive and a first safari

  1. Reach Sawai Madhopur by train, or by road from Jaipur.
  2. Afternoon safari, 2 to 5:35 PM.

Day 2: morning safari and the fort

  1. Morning safari, 7 to 10:30 AM, in a different zone if you can.
  2. Ranthambore Fort, built in the 10th century and part of the UNESCO "Hill Forts of Rajasthan". Rajasthan Tourism says it's about half an hour's drive from town.
  3. Trinetra Ganesh temple, inside the fort, about 15 km from Sawai Madhopur. Rajasthan Tourism says about 20 lakh people visit it each year. We couldn't confirm its hours.

Have a third day? Add another safari, or go on to Jaipur, about 167 km away.

Getting to Ranthambore

From Rajasthan Tourism and the reserve's site:

  • By train: Sawai Madhopur station is in town, and the park is about 14 km away. Trains run from Delhi, Mumbai, Jaipur and Kota. Check times on NTES or IRCTC.
  • By road: Jaipur is about 166–167 km away, and Delhi about 352 km.
  • By air: the nearest airport is Jaipur, about 166 km away.

Ranthambore in December end: Christmas and New Year

  • This year Christmas is on a Friday, and so is New Year's Day (1 January 2027), so both are long weekends. Safaris sell out.
  • For January to March, book now: advance booking opened on 1 November.
  • If your zone is full, try Tatkal (7 days ahead) or Premium Tatkal (3 days ahead), or a Canter seat.
  • Book your hotel early. Most stays are on Ranthambore Road, between the town and the park.

What to wear on a Ranthambore safari in December

What should I wear? Try the what-to-wear helper for day and night temperatures and an outfit list by month.

Afternoons are warm, but a 7 AM safari in an open jeep is very cold.

  • Layers for the morning safari: a thermal top, a fleece or sweater and a warm, windproof jacket. Take layers off as the sun comes up.
  • Extras: a woollen cap, gloves, a scarf, and warm socks.
  • Colours: wear browns, greens and greys. Bright colours stand out in the forest.
  • A scarf or mask for the dust on the tracks, and sunglasses.
  • Sunscreen and a cap for the afternoon safari.
  • Binoculars if you have them, and a camera.

Rules and safety

From the reserve's terms:

  • Not allowed: alcohol, non-vegetarian food, firearms, pets and unregistered vehicles.
  • Never feed or harm animals. Breaking the rules can be punished under the Wildlife (Protection) Act, 1972.
  • Permits can't be transferred or changed.
  • Stay in the vehicle, keep your voice low, and follow the guide (general advice; the terms don't spell this out).
  • Never sleep with a heater or coal fire in a closed room. It gives off carbon monoxide, which can kill without warning.

For foreign visitors

  • Foreign visitors can book on the same official site. Carry the ID you booked with; we couldn't confirm which IDs are accepted for foreigners, so use your passport.
  • Visa: apply for the e-Tourist Visa only at the official site, indianvisaonline.gov.in.

Emergency numbers and help

  • Emergency (police, fire, ambulance): 112
  • Police: 100
  • Ranthambore Tiger Reserve booking office: +91 94614 12641
  • Rajasthan Tourism, Tourist Reception Centre, Sawai Madhopur: 07462-221333 (station counter 07462-220808)
  • Ministry of Tourism helpline, 24 hours, many languages: 1363 or 1800-11-1363

Questions

Is Ranthambore open in December?

Yes. The park is open from October to June, and December safaris run from 7 to 10:30 AM and 2 to 5:35 PM.

What is the official website for Ranthambore safari booking?

The Rajasthan Forest Department's booking page links to forestrajasthan.com, and bookings are made on booking.forestrajasthan.com. Many look-alike private sites exist, so start from the government page.

When does Ranthambore safari booking open?

For January to March, it opened on 1 November at 12 noon; for October to December, on 1 August. Tatkal opens 7 days ahead at 11 AM, and Premium Tatkal 3 days ahead.

Which zone is best in Ranthambore?

Every zone can have tigers. Zones 3 and 4 pass the lakes below the fort, which many visitors like. The reserve can change your zone, so don't pay extra to anyone who promises one.

What is the weather in Ranthambore in December?

IMD's averages for Sawai Madhopur are about 25 °C by day and 10 °C at night, with almost no rain. Early-morning safaris feel much colder.

How do I reach Ranthambore from Jaipur or Delhi?

By train to Sawai Madhopur, then 14 km to the park. By road, Jaipur is about 166 km and Delhi about 352 km. The nearest airport is Jaipur.

Can children go on a Ranthambore safari?

Yes. Children under 5 don't need a ticket. Dress them warmly for the morning safari.

What should I wear on a Ranthambore safari in December?

Warm layers for the morning: a thermal, a fleece, a windproof jacket, a cap and gloves, in muted colours. Take a scarf for the dust.
