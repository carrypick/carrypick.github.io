Kerala in January: weather, festivals and Munnar's cold mornings

Updated 24 Nov 2026 · 9 min read

Kerala in January at a glance

Weather in JanuaryKochi about 32 °C by day and 23 °C at night, rain on about 1 day (IMD averages)
MunnarCold mornings. Some tea valleys fall to 0 °C or below on the coldest nights
SabarimalaMakaravilakku on Thursday 14 January 2027. Busy trains and roads to Pathanamthitta that week
Kerala Literature Festival21–24 January 2027, Kozhikode Beach
Other festivalsArthunkal feast 10–27 January, Thaipooyam 22 January
Fly toKochi (COK), Thiruvananthapuram (TRV), Kozhikode (CCJ)
Emergency112

January is one of the best months to see Kerala. The rains are over, the backwaters are calm, the beaches are sunny, and up in Munnar the mornings are misty and cold, cold enough for frost in some tea valleys. January also has its own festivals: Makaravilakku at Sabarimala, the Kerala Literature Festival on the beach at Kozhikode, church feasts and temple Thaipooyam processions. This guide covers January's weather, Munnar's cold, each part of the month, festivals, where to go, getting there and a simple one-week route.

Kerala weather in January

The India Meteorological Department's (IMD) long-term averages for Kochi, 1981–2010:

DecemberAbout 31.9 °CAbout 23.2 °CAbout 39 mm
JanuaryAbout 31.9 °CAbout 23.0 °CAbout 24 mm, on about 1 day
FebruaryAbout 32.0 °CAbout 24.2 °CAbout 27 mm

Elsewhere in January, from IMD:

PlaceDayNightRain in January
Thiruvananthapuram (Kovalam, Varkala)About 32.4 °CAbout 22.3 °CAbout 20 mm
Alappuzha (backwaters)About 32.6 °CAbout 22.4 °CAbout 19 mm
Kottayam (Kumarakom)About 32.9 °CAbout 22.2 °CAbout 10 mm

  • January is dry. On average it rains on only about one day in the month. But some Januaries are wetter: in January 2021, Kochi got about 89 mm, says IMD.
  • It's warm on the coast, hot in the afternoon sun, and pleasant at night. The highest January temperature on record in Kochi is 36.4 °C (22 January 2009).
  • The hills are cool. IMD has no weather station page for Munnar, but it's much colder than the coast, with misty mornings and cold nights (see below).

Munnar in January: mist and frost

Munnar's tea valleys get their coldest mornings from late December to mid-January, though not every year:

  • In early January 2019, Chenduvarai near Munnar fell to about −4 °C, and frost covered the grass in several estates, news reports said.
  • In winter 2024–25, Kerala Tourism's newsletter reported 0 °C at the Chenduvarai estate, 2 °C at Silent Valley and 3 °C at Devikulam.
  • Last winter, Sevenmallay estate dipped below zero on 20 December 2025, news reports said.

These readings come from estate thermometers in the valleys. Munnar town is usually a few degrees warmer. For the best chance of frost, be out early, around sunrise, on the grasslands and estates. Carry a proper jacket, a cap and gloves.

Eravikulam National Park (the Nilgiri tahr, at Rajamala) usually closes for a few weeks around February and March for the tahr's calving season, so January is a good time to go. Check the closure dates for 2027 before you plan.

Kerala in the first week of January

New Year crowds leave Fort Kochi, Munnar and Varkala by about 3 January. New Year's Day 2027 is a Friday, so the first weekend is still busy. After that, hotels and houseboats are usually easier to find than in late December. (We don't show prices: compare them for your dates.)

The Arthunkal feast (the Feast of St Sebastian) at St Andrew's Basilica, Arthunkal, near Alappuzha, runs from 10 to 27 January 2027, says Kerala Tourism's event calendar.

Kerala in the middle of January

  • Sabarimala Makaravilakku is on Thursday 14 January 2027, says Kerala Tourism's calendar. Darshan continues until 19 January, and the temple closes on 20 January (news reports). Huge numbers of pilgrims travel that week, so trains to Chengannur, Kottayam and Kollam, and the roads in Pathanamthitta district, are crowded. If you're not going to Sabarimala, book trains into Kerala early for 10 to 16 January, and avoid driving through Pathanamthitta that week.
  • Makar Sankranti is also on 14 January, and Pongal falls around the same days. Pongal is a big holiday in neighbouring Tamil Nadu, so expect more visitors in Munnar and Thekkady, close to the border.
  • Kanjiramattom Kodikuthu at Kanjiramattom mosque, Ernakulam, is on 13 and 14 January (Kerala Tourism calendar).

Kerala in the last week of January

  • The Kerala Literature Festival, the 10th edition, is on 21 to 24 January 2027 at Kozhikode Beach, says Kerala Tourism's calendar. It's one of India's biggest literature festivals, with talks, books and music.
  • Thaipooyam processions, with kavadi dancers, are on 22 January 2027 at the Koorkancherry Sree Maheswara temple in Thrissur and the Harippad Sree Subramanya Swamy temple near Alappuzha (Kerala Tourism calendar).
  • Athirampuzha Perunnal, at St Mary's Forane Church near Kottayam, starts with the flag hoisting on 19 January (Kerala Tourism calendar).
  • Republic Day is on Tuesday 26 January 2027. With Monday off, that's a 4-day weekend, and Kochi, Munnar and Alappuzha fill up from Saturday 23 January. Book early for those dates.

Not on in January 2027: the Kochi-Muziris Biennale. The last edition closed on 31 March 2026, and the next is set for 12 December 2027 to 31 March 2028 (news reports). The Nishagandhi Dance Festival in Thiruvananthapuram is listed for January 2027 in Kerala Tourism's calendar, but without dates, and the 2026 edition moved to February, so check before you plan around it.

Kerala Tourism says its festival dates follow the Malayalam calendar and are approximate, so confirm locally.

Where to go in Kerala in January

  • Kochi: Fort Kochi's churches, the Chinese fishing nets, Mattancherry and Jew Town, at their most pleasant in January's dry weather.
  • Munnar: tea gardens, Eravikulam National Park, Mattupetty and Top Station, and frosty mornings in the valleys.
  • Thekkady (Periyar): boat rides on the lake, spice farms and forest walks.
  • Alappuzha and Kumarakom: houseboats on the backwaters. Choose a boat from Kerala Tourism's approved houseboat list, which shows each boat's category (Silver, Gold or Diamond) and the validity of its approval.
  • Varkala and Kovalam: cliff and beach towns near Thiruvananthapuram. Swim only where lifeguards are on duty.
  • North Kerala: Kozhikode for the literature festival, and Kannur and Kasaragod for the Theyyam season and quiet beaches.

A one-week route: Kochi (2 nights), Munnar (2 nights), Thekkady (1 night), a houseboat in Alappuzha or Kumarakom (1 night), and Varkala or Kovalam (1 night) before flying out of Thiruvananthapuram.

Getting to Kerala in January

  • By air: fly to Kochi (COK) for Kochi, Munnar, Thekkady and Alappuzha, Thiruvananthapuram (TRV) for Kovalam and Varkala, or Kozhikode (CCJ) for the north.
  • By train: Ernakulam (Kochi), Alappuzha, Kottayam, Varkala Sivagiri and Thiruvananthapuram are the main stations. Trains are busy in the Sabarimala week (10 to 16 January), and winter fog in north India can delay long-distance trains from Delhi.
  • To Munnar: about 4 hours by road from Kochi airport. Hill roads are narrow and winding, and mist can lower visibility in the early morning.

What to wear in Kerala in January

Light cotton clothes, a cap and sunglasses on the coast and the backwaters. For Munnar and Thekkady, add a warm jacket or fleece, a cap and socks for the cold mornings. Cover your shoulders and knees at temples and churches. Some Kerala temples ask men to remove their shirts and allow only traditional dress inside, so check at the gate.

  • Police: 100. Ambulance: 108.
  • Ministry of Tourism helpline, 24 hours, many languages: 1363 or 1800-11-1363

Questions

Is January a good time to visit Kerala?

Yes, one of the best. IMD's averages for Kochi are about 32 °C by day and 23 °C at night, with rain on only about one day in the month. The backwaters are calm, and Munnar is cool and misty.

How cold is Munnar in January?

Cold in the mornings. Munnar town is often a few degrees above freezing at dawn, and some tea valleys like Chenduvarai and Sevenmallay can drop to 0 °C or below on the coldest nights, news reports say. Pack a warm jacket.

What festivals are there in Kerala in January?

Sabarimala Makaravilakku on 14 January 2027, the Arthunkal feast from 10 to 27 January, Thaipooyam on 22 January and the Kerala Literature Festival in Kozhikode on 21 to 24 January, says Kerala Tourism's event calendar.

When is Makaravilakku 2027?

Thursday 14 January 2027, says Kerala Tourism's calendar. Expect heavy crowds and busy trains into Kerala that week.

Does it rain in Kerala in January?

Rarely. January is one of Kerala's driest months, with rain on about one day on average. Some years get a few heavy showers.

Is the Kochi-Muziris Biennale on in January 2027?

No. The next edition is set for 12 December 2027 to 31 March 2028, according to news reports.
