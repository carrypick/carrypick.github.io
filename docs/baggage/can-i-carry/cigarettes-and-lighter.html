Can I carry cigarettes and a lighter on a flight in India?

Checked on 15 Nov 2026 against IndiGo, Air India, Akasa Air and airport security rules

Quick answer

Cabin bagYes Yes. IndiGo's list allows cigarettes in the cabin bag. You can't smoke on board or carry a lighter.
Check-in bagYes Yes, IndiGo's list allows them in check-in too. Lighters and e-cigarettes are banned in both bags.

Cigarettes

IndiGo's list marks cigarettes as allowed in both the cabin bag and check-in. Air India's and Akasa Air's lists don't name cigarettes, and don't ban them either.

You can't smoke anywhere on the plane. Akasa Air's rules say smoking is strictly banned and the toilets have smoke detectors, and tampering with them is not allowed.

Lighter: banned in both bags

  • Air India's list bans lighters, gun lighters and lighter fluid in any bag.
  • The airport security list (based on BCAS rules) bans lighters in both the cabin bag and check-in.
  • The Airports Authority of India's list bans lighters in the cabin.

IndiGo's own page names lighter fuel, lighter refills and lighters that must be turned upside down to light. Airport security in India follows the BCAS rules for every airline, so leave lighters at home whichever airline you fly.

A rechargeable USB lighter doesn't get round the rule. Akasa Air's list of dangerous goods includes lighters powered by lithium batteries.

If you find a lighter in your bag at the airport, hand it over at security. Air India asks passengers to tell the security staff about such items and leave them as instructed.

Matches

The rules don't agree here:

  • IndiGo's and SpiceJet's pages say matches may be carried on your person, not in your bags.
  • The airport security list (based on BCAS rules) bans safety matches in both bags.

Security can take them, so don't carry matches.

E-cigarettes and vapes: banned completely

E-cigarettes, vapes, heated tobacco devices and e-hookahs are banned in both bags (IndiGo, Air India, Akasa Air). Akasa Air's rules add that e-cigarettes can't be taken out of India or brought into India, and it can refuse to fly a passenger who carries one.

Flying abroad

Other countries set their own limits on how many cigarettes you can bring in, and some ban vapes. Check the customs rules of the country you're flying to before you pack.

Coming back to India? Indian Customs allows 100 cigarettes, or 25 cigars, or 125 g of tobacco duty free. See our customs rules for alcohol and cigarettes.

What to do instead

  • Keep your cigarettes in their packet in your cabin bag.
  • Buy a lighter or matches after you land if you need them.
  • Pack nothing that can make a flame. Security checks every bag.

Questions

Can I carry cigarettes on a domestic flight in India?

Yes. IndiGo's list allows cigarettes in both the cabin bag and check-in. You can't smoke on board.

Can I carry a lighter in my check-in bag?

No. Lighters are banned in both bags (Air India, airport security list based on BCAS rules). Lighter fluid and refills are banned too.

Can I carry a matchbox on a flight?

The rules differ. IndiGo and SpiceJet allow matches only on your person, not in bags, and the airport security list bans them in both bags. Leave them at home.

Can I carry an e-cigarette or vape on a flight?

No. E-cigarettes and vapes are banned in both bags on flights in India.

Can I smoke on the plane?

No. Smoking is banned on board, and the toilets have smoke detectors.
