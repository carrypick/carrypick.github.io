Can I carry a hair dryer or straightener on a flight in India?

Checked on 15 Nov 2026 against IndiGo, Air India, Akasa Air and SpiceJet rules

Quick answer

Cabin bagYes Yes. IndiGo's list allows a hair dryer in your cabin bag.
Check-in bagYes Yes. IndiGo's list allows it in check-in too.

Checking more than one thing? See all 47 items on our Can I carry it on a flight? list.

The rule

Allowed in both bags (IndiGo). Hair curlers that run on gas are banned in any bag (Air India). Source

Checked on 15 Nov 2026. Security staff can still refuse any item, and airlines can be stricter or change their rules, so check your airline before you fly.

Hair dryer

IndiGo's list marks a hair dryer as allowed in both the cabin bag and check-in. It counts towards your bag's weight, so a light travel dryer helps if your cabin bag is close to 7 kg.

Hair straightener

Straighteners aren't named on IndiGo's, Air India's or Akasa Air's lists, or on SpiceJet's pages. None of them bans a plug-in straightener either, and IndiGo allows a hair dryer, another electric hair tool, in both bags.

Because no list names it, we can't give you a firm rule. If you want to be sure, ask your airline before you fly. Security staff make the final decision at the checkpoint.

Let it cool fully before you pack it, and keep it in a heat-proof pouch if it came with one.

Gas hair curlers: banned

Air India's list bans hair curlers that contain gas in any bag. IndiGo's list bans gases such as butane in both bags. So leave gas-powered curlers and their refills at home.

Cordless straighteners

A cordless straightener has a lithium battery. The Indian lists we checked don't name them, so ask your airline first. If it's allowed, battery rules apply: Akasa Air, for example, allows up to 15 battery devices per person, each battery up to 100 Wh, and in check-in a device must be fully switched off and protected from turning on. Spare batteries go only in your cabin bag.

Hair spray, gel and serum

These are liquids. IndiGo's list names hair gels and hair sprays. In the cabin, each one must be 100 ml or less, inside your clear 1-litre liquids bag. In check-in, Air India allows toiletry sprays up to 500 ml a can and 2 litres in total, with the cap on. See our 100 ml rule page.

What to do instead

  • Short trip with only a cabin bag? Your hotel may have a hair dryer, so ask before you pack one.
  • Going to a wedding? Pack the straightener in check-in, wrapped in clothes, and keep hair spray under 100 ml in your cabin bag.
  • Packing a trimmer too? See our trimmer rules.

Questions

Can I carry a hair dryer in my cabin bag?

Yes. IndiGo's list allows a hair dryer in both the cabin bag and check-in.

Can I carry a hair straightener in my cabin bag?

Straighteners aren't named on the airline lists we checked, and none of them bans a plug-in one. Ask your airline if you want to be sure.

Can I carry a hair straightener in my check-in bag?

The lists don't name it. Let it cool, wrap it in clothes, and ask your airline if you want to be sure.

Can I carry hair spray on a flight?

Yes. In the cabin, a can of 100 ml or less inside your clear liquids bag. In check-in, up to 500 ml a can and 2 litres in total.
