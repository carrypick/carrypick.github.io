Can I carry a razor on a flight in India?

Checked on 16 Nov 2026 against IndiGo, Air India, SpiceJet, Air India Express and airport security rules

Quick answer

Cabin bagWith limits Yes for a safety or disposable razor with the blades in a cartridge. Loose blades aren't allowed.
Check-in bagYes Yes, including loose blades, wrapped so nobody gets cut.

The rule

Safety razors, including disposable ones, are fine in both bags (Air India). Loose razor blades that aren't in a cartridge can't go in the cabin, so pack them in check-in (IndiGo, Air India). Source

Checked on 16 Nov 2026. Security staff can still refuse any item, and airlines can be stricter or change their rules, so check your airline before you fly.

Safety and disposable razors

Air India's list allows safety razors, including disposable ones, in both bags, and SpiceJet's list permits disposable razors and their cartridges. The Airports Authority of India's list bans razor blades that aren't in a cartridge, but leaves out safety razors. So an ordinary razor with the blades fixed in a cartridge can go in your cabin bag.

Loose blades

  • IndiGo's list marks blades as not allowed in the cabin, and allowed in check-in.
  • Air India bans razor blades not in a cartridge in the cabin, along with box cutters and utility knives, and allows them in check-in.
  • BCAS, India's aviation security regulator, lists "razor blades not in a cartridge" among the items banned in the cabin, but excludes safety razors. Kannur Airport's security list, based on BCAS rules, also bans razor blades in the cabin.

Keep loose blades in their packet or a blade case in your check-in bag. Air India asks that sharp things in check-in are covered or wrapped, so baggage staff can't get cut.

Old-style razors with loose blades

Some razors take a loose double-edge blade, and a barber's razor holds an open blade. The lists don't say how they treat a loose blade fitted inside a razor. To be safe, pack these razors and their spare blades in check-in.

Shaving foam, gel and aftershave

These are liquids. In the cabin, each one must be 100 ml or less, inside your clear 1-litre liquids bag. Bigger cans go in check-in, where Air India allows toiletry sprays up to 500 ml a can and 2 litres in total, with the cap on. See our perfume and deo rules for sprays.

Electric shavers and trimmers

Trimmers: IndiGo, SpiceJet and Air India Express all list a trimmer with a built-in battery as fine in both bags. In check-in it must be fully switched off. A trimmer without a battery goes in check-in only. Our trimmer page has the details.

Electric shavers: Electric shavers aren't named on any of the airline pages we checked (IndiGo, Air India, Akasa Air, SpiceJet and Air India Express), or on the airport security lists based on BCAS rules. BCAS's own list of items banned in the cabin doesn't name them either (we read an archived copy on 21 November 2026, because the link on BCAS's website is broken). A rechargeable shaver is a battery device, so:

  • Cabin bag: every airline we checked allows phones and other small battery devices in the cabin, so this is the safe place for a shaver.
  • Check-in: IndiGo's dangerous goods table and Akasa Air allow battery devices if they're fully switched off and can't turn on. Air India's page keeps phones and laptops with batteries out of check-in, and doesn't mention shavers.

If you're not sure, ask your airline before you fly.

What to do instead

  • Cabin bag only? Take a disposable or cartridge razor and a travel-size foam of 100 ml or less.
  • Leave loose blades at home and buy them at your destination.
  • Grooming kit with scissors? See our nail cutter and scissors rules.

Questions

Can I carry a razor in my cabin bag?

Yes, a safety or disposable razor with the blades in a cartridge (Air India, Airports Authority of India). Loose blades aren't allowed in the cabin.

Can I carry razor blades in my check-in bag?

Yes. Keep them in their packet or a blade case, so nobody gets cut.

Can I carry a disposable razor on a flight?

Yes. Air India's list allows disposable razors in both the cabin bag and check-in.

Can I carry an electric shaver on a flight?

The airline pages and airport lists we checked don't name shavers. A rechargeable shaver is a battery device, and small battery devices are allowed in the cabin, so pack it in your cabin bag. Ask your airline if you want to put it in check-in.

Can I carry shaving foam in my cabin bag?

Only a can of 100 ml or less, inside your clear 1-litre liquids bag. A bigger can goes in check-in.
