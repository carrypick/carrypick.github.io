Can I carry liquids on a flight in India? The 100 ml rule

Checked on 15 Nov 2026 against Air India, IndiGo and airport security rules

The rule

Cabin bag: each container 100 ml or less, all in one clear, resealable bag of up to 1 litre, shown separately at security. Anything bigger goes in check-in. Source

Checked on 15 Nov 2026. Security staff can still refuse any item, and airlines can be stricter or change their rules, so check your airline before you fly.

The 100 ml rule in short

  • Each bottle, tube or jar must be 100 ml or less. A bigger container isn't accepted even if it's partly empty (Air India).
  • All of them go in one clear, resealable plastic bag of up to 1 litre. Everything must fit and the bag must close (airport security list).
  • One bag per passenger (airport security list).
  • Take the bag out of your cabin bag and show it on its own at the security check.
  • The rule applies on domestic flights too, not just international ones.

Anything bigger goes in your check-in bag.

What counts as a liquid

Air India's list names water and other drinks, creams, lotions, cosmetics, oils, perfumes, deodorants and other sprays, gels, shaving foam, toothpaste, lip gloss and lip balm, and anything with a similar texture at room temperature. IndiGo's list adds shampoos, suntan lotions, hair gels and hair sprays.

Water bottles

IndiGo's list allows a water bottle in the cabin only up to 100 ml, so drink your water before the security check. The lists we checked don't say anything about empty bottles.

Makeup

Liquid foundation, mascara, lip gloss, lip balm and cream are liquids, so they go in the 1-litre bag. Powder makeup and solid lipstick aren't named as liquids on the lists we checked. Note that Air India's list keeps talcum powder out of the cabin.

Medicines and baby food: the exceptions

  • Medicines: Air India allows medicines over 100 ml if you need them during the journey, only as much as the journey needs. For prescribed medicines, carry the doctor's note; the name on the label should match your boarding pass. See our medicines page.
  • Baby food: milk, juice, sterilised water, wet wipes and baby meals are allowed if they're just enough for the journey (Air India).
  • Special diets: Air India allows food you need for a diet, such as lactose-free or gluten-free food, but security can stop a quantity that looks too large.

Liquids bought at the airport

Air India's list says liquids bought at airport shops or on board aren't allowed through a security check unless they are in a sealed, tamper-evident bag. If you fly into India and change to a domestic flight, put liquids over the limit in your check-in bag before the security check. Our alcohol page covers duty-free bottles.

Check-in limits

Bigger bottles are fine in check-in. For toiletry sprays such as deo, hair spray and perfume, Air India allows up to 500 ml a can and 2 litres in total, with the caps on. Ghee and cooking oil: up to 5 litres. Put every bottle in a zip bag in case it leaks.

Questions

Is the 100 ml rule only for international flights?

No. It applies on domestic flights in India too. In your cabin bag, each liquid must be 100 ml or less, all in one clear 1-litre bag.

Can I carry a 150 ml bottle that is half empty?

No. The size of the bottle counts, not what's left inside. Pack it in check-in.

Can I carry a water bottle on a flight?

Only 100 ml or less in the cabin (IndiGo). Drink your water before the security check.

Can I carry toothpaste in my cabin bag?

Yes, a tube of 100 ml or less, inside your clear 1-litre liquids bag.

Can I carry liquids in my check-in bag?

Yes. Bigger bottles go in check-in. Toiletry sprays can be up to 500 ml a can and 2 litres in total.
