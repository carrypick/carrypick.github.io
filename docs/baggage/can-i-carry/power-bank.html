Can I carry a power bank on a flight in India?

Checked on 10 Nov 2026 against IndiGo, Air India and Akasa Air rules

Quick answer

Cabin bagWith limits Up to 2 power banks of 100 Wh or less on Akasa Air and Air India. Keep it with you, not in the overhead bin, and don't use it on board.
Check-in bagNo IndiGo, Air India and Akasa Air all ban power banks in check-in bags, even switched off.

The rule

Cabin bag only. Keep it in the seat pocket, under the seat or on you, never in the overhead bin. You can't use or charge it during the flight. Akasa Air and Air India allow up to 2 power banks or spare batteries, each up to 100 Wh. IndiGo needs the rating printed on it and refuses any over 160 Wh. Source

Checked on 10 Nov 2026. Security staff can still refuse any item, and airlines can be stricter or change their rules, so check your airline before you fly.

How to read the Wh on your power bank

Airlines set the limit in watt-hours (Wh), but most power banks in India are sold by mAh. Look at the label on the back. Many show the Wh. If yours shows only mAh, work it out like this:

Wh = mAh x 3.7 ÷ 1,000

3.7 is the voltage of the cells inside most power banks. If your label shows a different cell voltage, use that number instead.

So the common 10,000 and 20,000 mAh power banks are fine on all three airlines. IndiGo's list also says the power rating must be printed on the power bank. If the label has worn off, take a different one.

Why it can't go in your check-in bag

Lithium batteries can overheat and catch fire. In the cabin, the crew can see a fire and deal with it fast. In the hold, nobody can. That's why IndiGo, Air India and Akasa Air all keep power banks out of check-in bags, and airport security lists name them too.

Take it out before you drop your bag. Akasa Air's rules say a check-in bag with a power bank in it won't be loaded on the flight.

Where to keep it on the plane

  • In the seat pocket, under the seat in front of you, or on you.
  • Not in the overhead bin. Air India and Akasa Air both say this.
  • Don't use it to charge your phone during the flight, and don't charge the power bank itself. Akasa Air's rules ban both.

How many can I take?

Akasa Air and Air India allow 2 spare batteries per person, and a power bank counts as one. So you can take two power banks, or one power bank and one spare camera battery. IndiGo's list doesn't give a number, so keep to two.

Keep each one in its box or in a separate plastic bag, so the ends can't touch metal. Akasa Air asks for this for all spare batteries.

Power banks you can't take at all

  • MOBIKINS MK-PB-04. DGCA, India's air safety regulator, has banned this model on all flights to, from and within India, in any bag (Air India, Akasa Air).
  • Over 160 Wh. No airline here takes it.
  • Over 100 Wh on Air India and Akasa Air.

A swollen, cracked or very hot power bank isn't safe on any trip. Leave it at home.

What to do instead

  • Charge your phone fully before you leave for the airport.
  • Carry one power bank of 20,000 mAh or less in your cabin bag or handbag. It is about 74 Wh, well under every limit here.
  • If yours is 30,000 mAh or bigger, check your airline first or leave it at home.
  • Checked your bag in by mistake with a power bank inside? Tell the check-in staff straight away.

Questions

Can I carry a 20,000 mAh power bank on a flight?

Yes, in your cabin bag. A 20,000 mAh power bank is about 74 Wh. That is under the 100 Wh limit of Air India and Akasa Air, and under IndiGo's 160 Wh limit.

Can I carry 2 power banks on a flight?

Yes on Akasa Air and Air India, which allow 2 spare batteries per person, each up to 100 Wh. IndiGo's list doesn't give a number. Both must be in your cabin bag.

Can I put a power bank in my check-in bag if it is switched off?

No. Switching it off doesn't change the rule. IndiGo, Air India and Akasa Air ban power banks in check-in bags, and Akasa Air says a check-in bag with one inside won't be loaded on the flight.

Can I use my power bank during the flight?

No. Akasa Air's rules say you can't use or charge a power bank on board. Charge your phone before you fly.

Can I keep my power bank in the overhead bin?

No. Air India and Akasa Air say power banks and spare batteries must not go in the overhead bin. Keep it in the seat pocket, under the seat or on you.

Can I carry a 30,000 mAh power bank?

It is about 111 Wh, over the 100 Wh limit of Air India and Akasa Air, so they won't take it. IndiGo's list allows up to 160 Wh. Check with your airline first, or take a smaller one.
