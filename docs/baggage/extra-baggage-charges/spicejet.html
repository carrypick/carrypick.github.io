SpiceJet extra baggage charges: per kg, prepaid and at the airport

Checked on 15 Nov 2026 against SpiceJet's official website · domestic flights

Quick answer

  • Free check-in baggage: 15 kg, 1 piece.
  • Prepaid online: 3 kg ₹2,085, 5 kg ₹3,475 and 10 kg ₹6,950 (about ₹695 per kg).
  • At the airport: ₹775 per kg.
  • Extra bag: ₹950 ahead, ₹1,000 at airport.
  • Cabin bag over 7 kg: ₹700/kg ahead, ₹750/kg at airport.

Extra bags

One extra bag costs ₹950 booked ahead (₹1,900 for two, the most you can add), or ₹1,000 per bag at the airport.

Cabin bag over 7 kg

Your free cabin baggage is 7 kg, and a laptop bag or purse counts towards it. Carry More On-board adds up to 5 kg (12 kg in all) for ₹700 per kg, booked at least 12 hours before departure. At the airport, extra cabin weight costs ₹750 per kg, up to 5 kg. Anything over 12 kg must be checked in at airport rates.

Good to know

  • Prepaid kilos can't be cancelled or saved: buy 5 kg and use 4, and the spare kilo is lost.
  • Passengers on the same booking (PNR) can combine their checked baggage, but no single bag may weigh more than 32 kg.
  • International connection packs: 8 kg ₹4,800, 15 kg ₹9,000, 30 kg ₹18,000.
  • On SpiceJet's Q400 planes the cabin bag limit is smaller: 50 x 35 x 23 cm.

How to pay less

  • Weigh your bag at home with a luggage scale, then buy the smallest slab that covers the extra weight. Prepaid kilos you don't use aren't refunded.
  • Check the cabin bag size in our cabin bag checker: a bag that's too big gets checked in.
  • Add up your packing list with the bag weight calculator.
