Air India Express extra baggage charges: per kg, prepaid and at the airport

Checked on 15 Nov 2026 against Air India Express's official website · domestic flights

Quick answer

  • Free check-in baggage: 15 kg, 1 piece (none on Xpress Lite).
  • Prepaid online: 3 kg ₹2,250, 5 kg ₹3,750 and 10 kg ₹7,500 (about ₹750 per kg).
  • At the airport: up to ₹850 per kg.
  • Extra bag: ₹950 ahead, ₹1,000 at airport.
  • Cabin bag over 7 kg: +3 kg ₹2,100, +5 kg ₹3,500.

Buy up to 2 hours before departure through Manage Booking, the Tia chat assistant or selected travel agents. Air India Express says prices can vary by travel date but won't be higher than its fee list. Its fees include taxes.

Extra bags

Each extra bag has a handling charge: ₹950 if you book it ahead, ₹1,000 at the airport. You pay it even if you have bought extra kilos.

Cabin bag over 7 kg

Your free cabin baggage is 7 kg. The Xtra Carry-On add-on adds 3 kg for ₹2,100 or 5 kg for ₹3,500, for up to 12 kg in one or two cabin bags. At the boarding gate, the fee list shows a gate baggage charge of ₹3,000.

Lite fares

Xpress Lite fares have no free check-in bag. Booked ahead, 10 kg costs ₹1,000, 15 kg ₹1,500, 20 kg ₹4,500, 30 kg ₹10,500 and 40 kg ₹16,500. At the airport, one 15 kg bag costs ₹2,000 and one 20 kg bag ₹5,500.

Good to know

  • Connecting to an international flight? International Connecting Baggage packs cost ₹7,000 for 8 kg, ₹12,500 for 15 kg and ₹25,000 for 30 kg. The airline checks your onward boarding pass.
  • No single item may weigh more than 32 kg.

How to pay less

  • Weigh your bag at home with a luggage scale, then buy the smallest slab that covers the extra weight. Prepaid kilos you don't use aren't refunded.
  • Check the cabin bag size in our cabin bag checker: a bag that's too big gets checked in.
  • Type your real bag weights into the excess baggage calculator to see how many kg you are over and what it costs.

Compare Air India Express with other airlines on our extra baggage charges page, and see the free allowance on the Air India Express baggage rules page.

Questions

What are Air India Express extra baggage charges per kg?

At the airport, Air India Express charges up to ₹850 per kg on domestic flights. Bought online before you fly: 3 kg ₹2,250, 5 kg ₹3,750 and 10 kg ₹7,500 (about ₹750 per kg).

How much is 5 kg extra baggage on Air India Express?

₹3,750 online, or up to ₹4,250 at the airport.

What are Air India Express excess baggage charges at the airport?

Up to ₹850 per kg above your free check-in allowance (15 kg, 1 piece (none on Xpress Lite)).

How to add extra baggage in Air India Express?

Buy up to 2 hours before departure through Manage Booking, the Tia chat assistant or selected travel agents. Air India Express says prices can vary by travel date but won't be higher than its fee list. Its fees include taxes.

What if my Air India Express cabin bag is more than 7 kg?

Your free cabin baggage is 7 kg. The Xtra Carry-On add-on adds 3 kg for ₹2,100 or 5 kg for ₹3,500, for up to 12 kg in one or two cabin bags. At the boarding gate, the fee list shows a gate baggage charge of ₹3,000.

Does Air India Express charge for an extra piece of baggage?

Each extra bag has a handling charge: ₹950 if you book it ahead, ₹1,000 at the airport. You pay it even if you have bought extra kilos.
