Air India extra baggage charges: per kg, prepaid and at the airport

Checked on 15 Nov 2026 against Air India's official website · domestic flights

Quick answer

  • Free check-in baggage: 15 to 25 kg, depending on fare.
  • Prepaid online: not listed by Air India (up to 20% less than the airport rate, it says).
  • At the airport: ₹800 per kg plus tax.
  • Extra bag: Not listed.
  • Cabin bag over 7 kg: Up to 12 kg for ₹3,000.

Air India says paying ahead saves up to 20% on the airport rate, but it doesn't list prepaid prices. You see them in Manage Booking after you book. Prepay up to 2 hours before departure (6 hours for a single bag of up to 32 kg), online, by phone or at an Air India office.

Extra bags

With less than 25 kg of free baggage you get 1 bag; with 25 kg or more, 2 bags. Air India says charges may apply for extra bags but doesn't list a domestic fee. A bag over 158 cm in total (length + width + height) costs ₹2,000 plus tax as oversized baggage.

Cabin bag over 7 kg

Your free cabin bag is 7 kg. On domestic flights you can carry a cabin bag of up to 12 kg if you pay an excess hand baggage charge of ₹3,000. A cabin bag over 12 kg must be checked in, and excess baggage charges apply.

Good to know

  • Prepaid baggage isn't refunded if your bags weigh less than what you bought.
  • Air India refunds prepaid baggage if it cancels the flight, or if you cancel the booking at least 48 hours before departure. No refund on a no-show.
  • On a return trip, the return flight needs its own excess baggage payment.
  • Bags over 32 kg or 200 cm in total aren't taken as checked baggage and must go as cargo.

How to pay less

  • Weigh your bag at home with a luggage scale, then buy the smallest slab that covers the extra weight. Prepaid kilos you don't use aren't refunded.
  • Check the cabin bag size in our cabin bag checker: a bag that's too big gets checked in.
  • Add up your packing list with the bag weight calculator.
