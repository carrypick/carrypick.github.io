Singapore Airlines baggage allowance from India: cabin bag and checked baggage

Checked on 3 Nov 2026 against Singapore Airlines's official website · economy on India routes

Quick answer

  • Cabin bag: One cabin bag up to 7 kg.
  • Size limit: 115 cm in total (L + W + H).
  • Extra small bag: One extra item free: a handbag, camera bag, document bag or laptop bag up to 40 x 30 x 10 cm, a coat, an umbrella, or a small amount of duty-free.
  • Checked baggage: 25 kg on Lite and Value, 30 kg on Standard and Flexi.

Cabin baggage

Economy allows one cabin bag up to 7 kg, with length, width and height adding up to no more than 115 cm. Bags that are too big are taken at check-in or the gate and put in the hold.

Checked baggage on India flights

Economy Lite25 kg
Economy Value25 kg
Economy Standard30 kg
Economy Flexi30 kg

India flights use the weight concept: you can check several bags as long as the total is within your allowance. No single bag may weigh more than 32 kg; heavier bags must be repacked.

Infants

Up to 10 kg checked, plus a folding stroller and a carrycot or car seat.

Good to know

  • Power banks: up to two, each up to 100 Wh, in your cabin bag only. You can't use or charge them at any time during the flight.
  • Smart bags whose battery can't be removed are refused.
  • Durians aren't allowed in any baggage, and strong-smelling food may be refused.

Questions

How much cabin baggage is allowed on Singapore Airlines?

One cabin bag up to 7 kg.

What is the Singapore Airlines cabin bag size limit?

The limit is 115 cm in total (L + W + H). Measure the bag with its wheels and handles.

Can I carry a laptop bag or handbag too?

One extra item free: a handbag, camera bag, document bag or laptop bag up to 40 x 30 x 10 cm, a coat, an umbrella, or a small amount of duty-free.

How much checked baggage do I get on Singapore Airlines from India?

25 kg on Lite and Value, 30 kg on Standard and Flexi. It depends on your fare type, so check your ticket.

How much baggage does an infant get on Singapore Airlines?

Up to 10 kg checked, plus a folding stroller and a carrycot or car seat.
