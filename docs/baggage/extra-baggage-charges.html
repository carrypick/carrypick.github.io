Extra baggage charges on domestic flights in India

Checked on 15 Nov 2026 against each airline's official website · domestic flights

Over your free baggage allowance? Here's what India's main airlines charge for extra kilos, extra bags and heavy cabin bags on domestic flights, and how to pay less.

Quick answer

  • At the airport: ₹775 to ₹850 per kg over your free check-in allowance (usually 15 kg).
  • Online, before you fly: about ₹695 to ₹750 per kg, sold in slabs such as 3, 5 and 10 kg.
  • An extra bag: about ₹950 to ₹1,000, and it includes no extra weight.
  • Cabin bag over 7 kg: the rules differ a lot by airline. See the table below.

Buying online is cheaper on every airline here, but only by about ₹50 to ₹100 per kg. The bigger saving comes from not paying for kilos you don't need:

  • Prepaid kilos aren't refunded. Buy 5 kg, carry 3 kg extra, and the other 2 kg are lost.
  • Only 1 or 2 kg over? The smallest slab online is 3 kg. On IndiGo, 2 kg at the airport costs ₹1,600, less than the 3 kg slab at ₹2,250. The same is true on the other airlines with a 3 kg slab.
  • Buy the slab after you weigh. Pack, weigh the bag at home, then buy the smallest slab that covers it.
  • The online window closes early: 2 hours before departure on IndiGo, Air India, Air India Express and Akasa Air, 3 hours on SpiceJet.

How to avoid extra baggage charges

  • Weigh your bags at home. A luggage scale costs less than a few kilos at the airport. Most check-in bags are allowed 15 kg, and most cabin bags 7 kg.
  • Choose a lighter bag. An empty trolley typically weighs about 2.5 kg in cabin size and 3.4 to 4.3 kg in medium and large sizes, all taken from your allowance. See typical weights in our trolley bag size chart.
  • Check the weights against your fare. Our excess baggage calculator shows how many kg you are over and the airline's fee.
  • Make sure the cabin bag fits. A cabin bag that's too big gets checked in and counts towards your check-in weight. Try the cabin bag checker.
  • Wear the heavy things. Put on your jacket and heaviest shoes for the flight.
  • Check your fare. IndiGo Lite and Air India Express Xpress Lite fares include no check-in bag at all.
  • Travelling together? SpiceJet lets passengers on one booking combine their check-in baggage. Other airlines count each passenger's allowance separately, so check your ticket.
  • Keep every bag under 32 kg. No airline here takes a heavier single bag, even if you pay.

Questions

What are extra baggage charges per kg on domestic flights in India?

At the airport counter, IndiGo, Air India, Air India Express, Akasa Air and SpiceJet charge ₹775 to ₹850 per kg above your free check-in allowance, which is usually 15 kg. Bought online before you fly, it works out to about ₹695 to ₹750 per kg, sold in slabs such as 3, 5 and 10 kg.

What are IndiGo extra baggage charges per kg?

₹800 per kg at the airport. Bought online, IndiGo charges ₹2,250 for 3 kg, ₹3,750 for 5 kg and ₹7,500 for 10 kg, which is ₹750 per kg.

What are Air India extra baggage charges for domestic flights?

₹800 per kg plus tax at the airport. Air India says paying ahead online saves up to 20%, but it doesn't list prepaid prices: you see them in Manage Booking.

Can I buy extra baggage at the airport?

Yes. You pay at the check-in counter at the airport rate, which costs more per kg than buying online before you fly. Online purchase usually closes 2 or 3 hours before departure.

What happens if my cabin bag is more than 7 kg?

It depends on the airline. Air India lets you carry up to 12 kg for ₹3,000. Air India Express sells 3 or 5 kg more (Xtra Carry-On), and SpiceJet charges ₹700 per kg ahead or ₹750 at the airport, up to 5 kg more. IndiGo and Akasa Air list no fee for a heavier cabin bag, so expect to check it in.

Does an extra bag include extra weight?

No. A second bag costs about ₹950 to ₹1,000 on IndiGo, Air India Express, Akasa Air and SpiceJet, and that fee buys no weight. Every kg over your free allowance is still charged.

Is prepaid excess baggage refundable?

Not for kilos you don't use: if you buy 5 kg and carry 3 kg extra, there's no refund. Airlines do refund it if they cancel the flight, and some refund it when you cancel the whole booking in time.
