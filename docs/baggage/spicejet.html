SpiceJet baggage rules: cabin bag size, weight and check-in

Checked on 3 Nov 2026 against SpiceJet's official website · domestic economy

Quick answer

  • Cabin bag: One cabin bag up to 7 kg.
  • Size limit: 55 x 35 x 25 cm, and 115 cm in total (L + W + H).
  • Extra small bag: A laptop bag or ladies' purse is allowed, but it counts inside the same 7 kg.
  • Free check-in: 15 kg, 1 piece.

Cabin baggage

SpiceJet allows one cabin bag up to 7 kg. On Boeing planes it must be within 55 x 35 x 25 cm (115 cm in total). On the smaller Q400 planes the limit is 50 x 35 x 23 cm (108 cm in total), so check the aircraft type on your booking. The Carry More On-board add-on lets you carry up to 12 kg.

Check-in baggage (domestic)

All domestic fares15 kg, 1 piece

Each checked bag can be up to 158 cm in total (L + W + H).

Good to know

  • When we checked, the baggage page on spicejet.com said "Coming Soon", so these rules come from SpiceJet's own corporate site (corporate.spicejet.com).

Questions

How much cabin baggage is allowed on SpiceJet?

One cabin bag up to 7 kg.

What is the SpiceJet cabin bag size limit?

The limit is 55 x 35 x 25 cm, and 115 cm in total (L + W + H). Measure the bag with its wheels and handles.

Can I carry a laptop bag or handbag too?

A laptop bag or ladies' purse is allowed, but it counts inside the same 7 kg.

How much free check-in baggage do I get on SpiceJet domestic flights?

15 kg, 1 piece. It depends on your fare type, so check your ticket.
