Kuwait Airways baggage allowance from India: cabin bag and checked baggage

Checked on 3 Nov 2026 against Kuwait Airways's official website · economy on India routes

Quick answer

  • Cabin bag: One cabin bag up to 7 kg.
  • Size limit: 56 x 46 x 25 cm.
  • Extra small bag: One extra item: a handbag, briefcase, coat, umbrella, small camera, walking stick, or one duty-free item bought that day.
  • Checked baggage: 2 x 23 kg on Economy, 1 x 32 kg on Economy Saver, none on Zero Bag.

Cabin baggage

Economy allows one cabin bag up to 7 kg and 56 x 46 x 25 cm. Extra items are weighed with your bags and may be charged. Kuwait Airways doesn't say whether wheels and handles count.

Checked baggage on India flights

Economy2 pieces, 23 kg each
Economy Saver1 piece, 32 kg
Economy Zero BagNo checked bag (cabin bag only)

Kuwait Airways uses pieces, not total weight: on Economy you get two bags of up to 23 kg each. No single piece may weigh more than 32 kg, and each bag can be up to 158 cm in total. Check your fare: Economy Zero Bag includes no checked bag on India routes.

Infants

One piece up to 10 kg and 115 cm in total. Infants can't buy extra baggage.

Good to know

  • Zamzam water: passengers starting their trip at a Saudi airport may carry one 5-litre Zamzam piece within their free allowance, properly packed and tagged.
  • Pack your things in a suitcase or suitable container. Money, jewellery and valuables are at your own risk in checked bags, so carry them with you.

Questions

How much cabin baggage is allowed on Kuwait Airways?

One cabin bag up to 7 kg.

What is the Kuwait Airways cabin bag size limit?

The limit is 56 x 46 x 25 cm. Measure the bag with its wheels and handles.

Can I carry a laptop bag or handbag too?

One extra item: a handbag, briefcase, coat, umbrella, small camera, walking stick, or one duty-free item bought that day.

How much checked baggage do I get on Kuwait Airways from India?

2 x 23 kg on Economy, 1 x 32 kg on Economy Saver, none on Zero Bag. It depends on your fare type, so check your ticket.

How much baggage does an infant get on Kuwait Airways?

One piece up to 10 kg and 115 cm in total. Infants can't buy extra baggage.
