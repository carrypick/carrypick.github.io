Air India international baggage allowance from India: cabin bag and checked baggage

Checked on 3 Nov 2026 against Air India (international)'s official website · economy on India routes

Quick answer

  • Cabin bag: One cabin bag up to 7 kg.
  • Size limit: 55 x 40 x 20 cm, and 115 cm in total (L + W + H).
  • Extra small bag: One small personal item up to 3 kg that fits under the seat, such as a handbag, camera bag or laptop bag up to 40 x 30 x 20 cm.
  • Checked baggage: 25 kg on Value, 30 kg on Classic and 35 kg on Flex to the Gulf and South-East Asia.

Cabin baggage

Economy allows one cabin bag up to 7 kg, no bigger than 55 x 40 x 20 cm and 115 cm in total. On international flights you can pay to carry a cabin bag up to 12 kg; anything heavier must be checked in.

Checked baggage on India flights

Value25 kg
Classic30 kg
Flex35 kg

These allowances cover flights between India and the Gulf (including Saudi Arabia) and South-East Asia (Singapore, Malaysia, Thailand, Indonesia, Vietnam, the Philippines and Hong Kong), in both directions. No single bag may weigh more than 32 kg, and each bag must be within 158 cm in total. They apply only to flights run by Air India itself, not Air India Express.

Infants

Up to 10 kg checked in Economy, plus a folding stroller, carrycot or car seat.

Good to know

  • Air India lists Gulf airport rules: Bahrain may reject round bags and bundles tied with rope; Muscat charges extra for bags without a flat side, tied with rope or wrapped in a blanket; Sharjah needs at least one flat side and no ropes; Dubai asks you to pack rope-tied items and plastic bags in boxes.
  • Zamzam water can't go inside your checked bag from Saudi airports. Only Hajj and Umrah visa holders may carry one official 5-litre bottle.
  • Power banks: cabin bag only, never checked, and not in the overhead bin.

Questions

How much cabin baggage is allowed on Air India (international)?

One cabin bag up to 7 kg.

What is the Air India (international) cabin bag size limit?

The limit is 55 x 40 x 20 cm, and 115 cm in total (L + W + H). Measure the bag with its wheels and handles.

Can I carry a laptop bag or handbag too?

One small personal item up to 3 kg that fits under the seat, such as a handbag, camera bag or laptop bag up to 40 x 30 x 20 cm.

How much checked baggage do I get on Air India (international) from India?

25 kg on Value, 30 kg on Classic and 35 kg on Flex to the Gulf and South-East Asia. It depends on your fare type, so check your ticket.

How much baggage does an infant get on Air India (international)?

Up to 10 kg checked in Economy, plus a folding stroller, carrycot or car seat.
