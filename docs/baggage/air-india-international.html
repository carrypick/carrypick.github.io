Air India international baggage allowance from India: cabin bag and checked baggage

Checked on 28 Nov 2026 against Air India (international)'s official website · economy on India routes

Quick answer

  • Cabin bag: One cabin bag up to 7 kg.
  • Size limit: 55 x 40 x 20 cm, and 115 cm in total (L + W + H).
  • Extra small bag: One small personal item up to 3 kg that fits under the seat, such as a handbag, camera bag or laptop bag up to 40 x 30 x 20 cm. An item over 90 cm in total counts as your cabin bag. Which bag? See our handbag and sling guide and laptop backpack guide.
  • Checked baggage: 25 kg on Value, 30 kg on Classic and 35 kg on Flex to the Gulf and South-East Asia; 1 bag of 23 kg on Value and 2 bags on Classic and Flex to the USA, UK and Europe.

Cabin baggage

Economy and Premium Economy allow one cabin bag up to 7 kg, no bigger than 55 x 40 x 20 cm and 115 cm in total. Indian security rules allow only one cabin bag, whatever your class or Maharaja Club tier. You can pay to carry a cabin bag of up to 12 kg: USD 75 on international flights. A cabin bag over 12 kg must be checked in.

Checked baggage on India flights

Value25 kg to the Gulf, Saudi Arabia, Africa, South-East Asia, China and Australia; 30 kg to Sri Lanka, Bangladesh and Myanmar; 20 kg to Nepal and the Maldives; 1 bag of 23 kg to the USA, Canada, the UK, Europe, Israel, Japan and South Korea
Classic30 kg to the Gulf, Saudi Arabia, Africa, South-East Asia and Australia (35 kg to China); 30 kg to Sri Lanka, Bangladesh and Myanmar; 20 kg to Nepal and the Maldives; 2 bags of 23 kg to the USA, Canada, the UK, Europe, Israel, Japan and South Korea
Flex35 kg to the Gulf, Saudi Arabia, Africa, South-East Asia and Australia (45 kg to China); 40 kg to Sri Lanka, 35 kg to Bangladesh and Myanmar; 30 kg to Nepal and the Maldives; 2 bags of 23 kg on piece routes
Premium Economy (Value / Flex)25 / 35 kg to the Gulf and South-East Asia (30 / 35 kg to Thailand); 2 bags of 23 kg to the USA, Canada, the UK and Europe

On weight routes (the Gulf, Asia, Australia) the kilos are for all your bags together, but no single bag may weigh more than 32 kg or measure more than 158 cm in total. On piece routes each bag may weigh 23 kg, and in Economy both bags together may add up to 271 cm (length + width + height; its USA page says 273 cm). Air India's USA and Canada page shows 2 bags of 23 kg on every Economy fare between India and Canada, while its main table shows 1 bag on Value, so check your ticket. If a domestic Air India flight and an international one are on the same ticket, the international allowance applies to both. On separate tickets you still get it if both flights are run by Air India and connect within 24 hours. These rules cover flights run by Air India itself, not Air India Express or codeshare partners.

Infants

An infant gets 10 kg of checked baggage in Economy (12 kg in Premium Economy, 15 kg in Business or First), plus a folding stroller, carrycot or car seat. In the cabin you can carry up to 5 kg of baby items, such as food and bottles for the flight. You can't buy prepaid extra baggage for an infant.

Extra baggage charges

Prepay extra baggage in Manage Booking up to 2 hours before the flight and save up to 20%; at the airport Air India charges per kg to the Gulf and Asia, and per extra bag to the USA, UK and Europe.

  • At the airport, from India: USD 17 a kg to the Gulf, Saudi Arabia, Africa, Nepal, Sri Lanka and Bangladesh, USD 22 a kg to South-East Asia (Singapore, Thailand, Hong Kong and others) and USD 37 a kg to Australia and New Zealand, plus taxes.
  • Coming back to India costs more: USD 22 a kg from the Gulf and Africa, USD 27 from South-East Asia and USD 25 from the SAARC countries.
  • On piece routes, one extra bag (up to 23 kg) from India costs USD 240 to the USA or the UK, CAD 360 to Canada, USD 220 to Germany, France, Italy and the Netherlands, and USD 180 to Japan and South Korea. From the USA to India it is USD 250.
  • A bag of 23 to 32 kg on piece routes pays USD 130 to the USA, CAD 190 to Canada, USD 100 to Europe and the UK and USD 90 to Japan and Korea.
  • A bag over 158 cm in total pays USD 125 (CAD 180 on Canada flights). Bags over 32 kg or over 200 cm aren't taken as baggage and must go as cargo.
  • A single overweight bag can be raised to 32 kg online until 6 hours before the flight. Prepaid baggage is only for tickets starting with 098, not codeshare or partner flights; it can't be moved to another person and is lost if you don't show up. Air India refunds it if it cancels the flight, or if you cancel at least 48 hours before.

How Air India abroad differs from IndiGo and the Gulf airlines

  • Its cabin bag is 55 x 40 x 20 cm: wider than IndiGo's 35 cm but thinner than IndiGo's 25 cm. Measure the depth of a hard trolley, which is often 23 to 25 cm.
  • Every fare, even Value, includes checked baggage abroad (25 kg to the Gulf). IndiGo Lite, Etihad Basic and flydubai Lite include none.
  • To the USA, the UK and Europe Air India counts pieces: 1 bag on Value and 2 on Classic and Flex, each up to 23 kg. Emirates and Qatar Airways count kilos to the UK and Europe.
  • Its power bank limit is 100 Wh, against 160 Wh on IndiGo.
  • Small cats and dogs can fly in the Economy cabin. IndiGo doesn't carry pets at all, and Emirates, Qatar Airways and flydubai don't take them in the cabin.

Air India baggage to the USA and Canada

  • Economy Value includes 1 bag of 23 kg; Classic, Flex and Premium Economy include 2 bags of 23 kg each; Business 2 bags of 32 kg.
  • In Economy, both bags together may add up to 271 cm (length + width + height), and each bag up to 158 cm. A large and a medium trolley together are often over that, so measure both bags.
  • An extra bag from India costs USD 240 to the USA and CAD 360 to Canada at the airport; a bag of 23 to 32 kg pays USD 130 or CAD 190 more.
  • On flights to the USA, powders such as spices, flour, coffee or baby formula over 350 ml in your cabin bag get extra screening and may be refused. Put them in your checked bag.

Sports gear, musical instruments and TVs

  • Sports equipment counts in your free allowance and also pays a handling fee: USD 45 on international flights (CAD 63.50 on Canada flights). The fee is waived on Economy Flex, Premium Economy Flex, Business and First, but not on award tickets.
  • Golf sets, cricket kits, bicycles (packed in a bike case), skis, fishing gear and archery sets are accepted, up to 2 items each, 300 cm in total and 32 kg.
  • Small musical instruments can go in the cabin as your one cabin bag. Bigger ones are checked in, up to 158 cm and 32 kg, or travel on a seat you buy (up to 75 kg).
  • Up to 2 LED or LCD TVs per person: up to 99 cm (39 inches) they go free within your allowance; from 99 to 139 cm an oversize charge applies; bigger TVs aren't accepted.

Pets on Air India

  • Only cats and dogs. A pet weighing up to 10 kg together with its soft kennel can fly with you in the Economy cabin, in the aisle seats of the last rows, one pet per passenger.
  • The soft kennel may be at most 17 x 10 x 9 inches (about 43 x 25 x 23 cm), leak-proof, ventilated on three sides and without wheels. Pets must be at least 8 weeks old, and pregnant or sedated pets aren't accepted.
  • Book through Air India's customer support or an airport or city office, with the pet's documents, at least 48 hours before the flight, and reach the airport 3 hours early.
  • Pets of 10 to 32 kg travel as checked baggage or cargo, depending on the aircraft, and pets over 32 kg only as cargo. Check the entry rules of the country you fly to.

Check-in times and web check-in

  • Web check-in opens 48 hours before the flight and closes 2 hours before an international flight. You get a confirmation slip, and still show your passport at the counter or bag drop. It isn't available on codeshare flights.
  • At Delhi Terminal 3, Air India's check-in counters for international flights stay open until 75 minutes before departure. Air India's pages for Mumbai, Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kochi and Kolkata give no closing time, so check your ticket.
  • Delhi Airport advises international passengers to arrive at least 3 hours before departure.
  • In Delhi you can also check in and drop bags at the city check-in at the Airport Express metro station; boarding gates close 20 minutes before departure.

If your bag is delayed, lost or damaged

  • Report it to Air India staff in the arrivals hall before you leave, and keep your copy of the Property Irregularity Report. You can follow the bag on WorldTracer.
  • A bag is treated as lost if it isn't traced within 21 days of the report. Claims need a list of what was inside, with the age of each item and proof of purchase.
  • Air India may pay essential expenses while a bag is delayed; ask customer support before you spend. It isn't responsible for normal wear, or for cash, jewellery, electronics or fragile items packed in checked bags.

Maharaja Club and Star Alliance extras

  • Platinum and Gold members (Star Alliance Gold) get one extra 23 kg bag on piece routes or 20 kg more on weight routes, also on Star Alliance flights.
  • Silver members get 5 kg more on weight routes, on flights run by Air India only. Red tier gets no extra baggage.
  • No extra allowance on Air India's ATR and CRJ aircraft or on codeshare flights run by partners.

Good to know

  • Power banks: at most two, each up to 100 Wh, in your cabin bag only and not in the overhead bin. You can't use or charge them on board.
  • Alcohol: up to 5 litres in shop packaging, at 24 to 70% alcohol, in checked bags only. Duty-free bought after security must stay sealed in its bag, and may not be allowed on a connecting domestic flight.
  • Food: ghee and cooking oil up to 5 kg in checked bags; chilli pickle only in checked bags; dried coconut (copra) is banned in all bags.
  • Gulf airports set their own rules, which Air India lists: Bahrain may refuse round bags and bundles tied with rope; Dubai charges USD 12 for an odd-sized bag and asks you to box rope-tied items and plastic bags; Muscat charges OMR 4 for bags without a flat side, tied with rope or wrapped in a blanket.
  • Zamzam water: Air India's baggage pages (read 28 Nov 2026) no longer say how it carries Zamzam, so ask Air India before you buy a 5-litre pack in Saudi Arabia.
  • India–Kathmandu (checked on Air India's SAARC baggage page on 21 Nov 2026): Economy includes 20 kg checked on Value and Classic and 30 kg on Flex, in both directions, plus one 7 kg cabin bag and a 3 kg personal item.

What you can pack on Air India (international)

Air India allows two power banks of up to 100 Wh, only in your cabin bag, and up to 5 litres of alcohol in checked bags. Ghee goes in checked bags up to 5 kg, and chilli pickle only in checked bags. For food on the way back, see sweets and home food, and check what India's customs allows duty-free.

Questions

How much cabin baggage is allowed on Air India (international)?

One cabin bag up to 7 kg.

What is the Air India (international) cabin bag size limit?

The limit is 55 x 40 x 20 cm, and 115 cm in total (L + W + H). Measure the bag with its wheels and handles.

Can I carry a laptop bag or handbag too?

One small personal item up to 3 kg that fits under the seat, such as a handbag, camera bag or laptop bag up to 40 x 30 x 20 cm. An item over 90 cm in total counts as your cabin bag.

How much checked baggage do I get on Air India (international) from India?

25 kg on Value, 30 kg on Classic and 35 kg on Flex to the Gulf and South-East Asia; 1 bag of 23 kg on Value and 2 bags on Classic and Flex to the USA, UK and Europe. It depends on your fare type, so check your ticket.

How much baggage does an infant get on Air India (international)?

An infant gets 10 kg of checked baggage in Economy (12 kg in Premium Economy, 15 kg in Business or First), plus a folding stroller, carrycot or car seat. In the cabin you can carry up to 5 kg of baby items, such as food and bottles for the flight. You can't buy prepaid extra baggage for an infant.

What are Air India (international) extra baggage charges from India?

What is the Air India international baggage allowance for economy class?

To the Gulf and South-East Asia: 25 kg on Value, 30 kg on Classic and 35 kg on Flex. To the USA, Canada, the UK and Europe: 1 bag of 23 kg on Value and 2 bags of 23 kg on Classic and Flex. Every fare also includes a 7 kg cabin bag and a 3 kg personal item.

What is the Air India international cabin baggage size?

55 x 40 x 20 cm and 115 cm in total, up to 7 kg in Economy and Premium Economy (10 kg in Business and First). You can pay USD 75 to carry a cabin bag of up to 12 kg.

What is the Air India baggage allowance to the USA?

In Economy, 1 bag of 23 kg on Value and 2 bags of 23 kg on Classic and Flex, with both bags together within 271 cm. Premium Economy also gets 2 bags of 23 kg and Business 2 bags of 32 kg. The cabin bag is 7 kg.

What are Air India international excess baggage charges per kg?

At the airport from India: USD 17 a kg to the Gulf and SAARC countries, USD 22 to South-East Asia and USD 37 to Australia, plus taxes. To the USA, UK and Europe you pay per extra bag instead, for example USD 240 to the USA. Prepaying online saves up to 20%.

What is the Air India premium economy baggage allowance on international flights?

25 kg on Premium Economy Value and 35 kg on Flex to the Gulf and South-East Asia (30 and 35 kg to Thailand), and 2 bags of 23 kg to the USA, Canada, the UK and Europe. The cabin bag is 7 kg.

What is the Air India baggage allowance to Europe and the UK?

Europe and the UK use pieces: 1 bag of 23 kg on Economy Value and 2 bags of 23 kg on Classic and Flex. An extra bag costs USD 240 to the UK and USD 220 to Germany, France, Italy and the Netherlands at the airport.

Does Air India give extra baggage for students on international flights?

Yes, on the Student fare (choose Student as the concession type when you book). Read on 28 Nov 2026: Economy gets 10 kg more than the Value fare on weight routes, up to 40 kg, and 2 bags of 23 kg on piece routes such as the USA, Canada and Europe. It is for full-time students aged 12 to 30, who may be asked for a student ID, visa or admission letter at the airport.
