Akasa Air baggage rules: cabin bag size, weight and check-in

Checked on 3 Nov 2026 against Akasa Air's official website · domestic economy

Quick answer

  • Cabin bag: One cabin bag up to 7 kg.
  • Size limit: 55 x 35 x 25 cm, and 115 cm in total (L + W + H).
  • Extra small bag: One personal item up to 3 kg that fits under the seat in front of you.
  • Free check-in: 15 kg, 1 piece.

Cabin baggage

Akasa Air allows one cabin bag up to 7 kg and 115 cm in total (L + W + H). Its Conditions of Carriage give the size as 55 x 35 x 25 cm.

Check-in baggage (domestic)

Standard fares15 kg, 1 piece
Student fare25 kg, 1 piece

Each checked bag can be up to 158 cm in total and 32 kg.

Extra baggage charges

  • Prepaid online: 3 kg ₹2,250, 5 kg ₹3,750 and 10 kg ₹7,500 (about ₹750 per kg).
  • At the airport: ₹800 per kg.
  • Extra bag: ₹1,000.
  • Cabin bag over 7 kg: No fee listed.

See every slab and rule on Akasa Air extra baggage charges.

Good to know

  • Infants get no check-in allowance.

Questions

How much cabin baggage is allowed on Akasa Air?

One cabin bag up to 7 kg.

What is the Akasa Air cabin bag size limit?

The limit is 55 x 35 x 25 cm, and 115 cm in total (L + W + H). Measure the bag with its wheels and handles.

Can I carry a laptop bag or handbag too?

One personal item up to 3 kg that fits under the seat in front of you.

How much free check-in baggage do I get on Akasa Air domestic flights?

15 kg, 1 piece. It depends on your fare type, so check your ticket.

What are Akasa Air extra baggage charges?

On domestic flights, Akasa Air charges ₹800 per kg at the airport. Online, before you fly: 3 kg ₹2,250, 5 kg ₹3,750 and 10 kg ₹7,500 (about ₹750 per kg). An extra bag costs ₹1,000.

Source

Official Akasa Air pages: Baggage Conditions of carriage
