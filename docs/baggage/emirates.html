Emirates baggage allowance from India: cabin bag and checked baggage

Checked on 3 Nov 2026 against Emirates's official website · economy on India routes

Quick answer

  • Cabin bag: One cabin bag up to 7 kg.
  • Size limit: 55 x 38 x 22 cm, and 115 cm in total (L + W + H).
  • Extra small bag: No separate laptop bag or handbag is listed for Economy from India: it's one piece only. Reasonable duty-free shopping is allowed on top.
  • Checked baggage: 20 kg to 35 kg, depending on your fare.

Cabin baggage

Economy passengers can take one cabin bag up to 7 kg and 55 x 38 x 22 cm. When you board in India, only one piece is allowed, and its length, width and height together must not be more than 115 cm. Emirates doesn't say whether wheels and handles count, so measure them in.

Checked baggage on India flights

Economy Special20 kg
Economy Saver25 kg
Economy Flex30 kg
Economy Flex Plus35 kg

India flights use the weight concept: you can split the total into as many bags as you like. No single bag may weigh more than 32 kg, and each bag's length, width and height together should be no more than 203 cm.

Infants

An infant ticket includes up to 10 kg of checked baggage, one 5 kg handbag for baby things, and a fully folding stroller or carrycot.

Good to know

  • Round bags, odd-shaped parcels, bags with loose straps and bags without a flat side don't travel well on the belt. Emirates asks you to repack them in a cardboard box.
  • Power banks: since 1 October 2025 you can't use or charge a power bank on board. You may carry one under 100 Wh in your cabin bag, kept in the seat pocket or under the seat, never in the overhead bin. Power banks aren't allowed in checked bags.
  • Zamzam water: Haj and Umrah pilgrims whose trip includes Madinah or Jeddah may carry up to 5 litres in a sealed container, on top of their checked allowance.
  • Medicines into the UAE: controlled medicines need approval from the UAE Ministry of Health before you travel. Carry the prescription and no more than 3 months' supply.
  • Smart bags are allowed only if the battery can be removed.

Questions

How much cabin baggage is allowed on Emirates?

One cabin bag up to 7 kg.

What is the Emirates cabin bag size limit?

The limit is 55 x 38 x 22 cm, and 115 cm in total (L + W + H). Measure the bag with its wheels and handles.

Can I carry a laptop bag or handbag too?

No separate laptop bag or handbag is listed for Economy from India: it's one piece only. Reasonable duty-free shopping is allowed on top.

How much checked baggage do I get on Emirates from India?

20 kg to 35 kg, depending on your fare. It depends on your fare type, so check your ticket.

How much baggage does an infant get on Emirates?

An infant ticket includes up to 10 kg of checked baggage, one 5 kg handbag for baby things, and a fully folding stroller or carrycot.
