How to avoid extra baggage charges

  • Measure with wheels and handles. Airlines measure the whole bag, not just the shell.
  • Weigh it at home. A luggage scale costs less than one excess-baggage fee.
  • Remember the bag's own weight. A hard cabin trolley often weighs 2.5–3 kg of your 7 kg.
  • Check your fare. Some cheap fares include cabin baggage only.
