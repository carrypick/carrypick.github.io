Vietjet baggage allowance from India: cabin bag and checked baggage

Checked on 15 Nov 2026 against Vietjet's official website · economy on India routes

Quick answer

  • Cabin bag: One cabin bag and one small item, 7 kg in total.
  • Size limit: 56 x 36 x 23 cm.
  • Extra small bag: One small item within the same 7 kg: a handbag up to 30 x 20 x 10 cm or a laptop bag up to 40 x 30 x 10 cm.
  • Checked baggage: None on Eco, 20 kg on Deluxe and 30 kg on SkyBoss.

Cabin baggage

On Eco and Deluxe fares you can take one main cabin bag up to 56 x 36 x 23 cm plus one small personal item, but both together must weigh no more than 7 kg. SkyBoss allows 10 kg. Every cabin bag needs a Vietjet cabin baggage tag from the check-in counter, or it can be refused at boarding, and bags may be weighed again at the gate, where fees are higher. Vietjet doesn't say whether wheels and handles count, so measure them in.

Checked baggage on India flights

EcoNo free checked bag (you can buy one)
Deluxe20 kg
SkyBoss30 kg, plus one golf set

These are Vietjet's allowances for flights of under 7 hours, which includes its direct flights between India and Vietnam (longer routes, such as to Australia, get more). No single bag may weigh more than 32 kg or measure more than 119 x 119 x 81 cm. Checked baggage is cheapest when bought with your ticket or within 24 hours of booking, and dearest at the airport.

Infants

Infants under 2 get no baggage allowance on Vietjet. Strollers, wheelchairs and mobility aids travel free.

Extra baggage charges

Buying 20 kg with your ticket costs 680,000 Vietnamese dong on India routes; at the airport it's 1,360,000 dong per 20 kg.

  • Prices are per person, per flight, in Vietnamese dong (VND); the booking page shows them in rupees. Vietjet's fee table puts India in its Japan, Korea and South Asia price group.
  • With your ticket or within 24 hours of booking: 20 kg costs 680,000 VND, 30 kg 1,040,000 VND and 40 kg 1,370,000 VND.
  • Later, up to 3 hours before departure: 20 kg costs 820,000 VND, 30 kg 1,300,000 VND and 40 kg 1,650,000 VND.
  • At the airport, within 3 hours of departure: 1,360,000 VND per 20 kg, and 340,000 VND for each kg over what you bought.
  • Eco and Deluxe passengers can add 2 kg of priority cabin baggage: 530,000 VND when booking, 630,000 VND later and 800,000 VND at the airport.

Good to know

  • Power banks: up to two, each up to 100 Wh, in your cabin bag only, never in checked baggage. Using them during the flight is strictly prohibited, and they must be kept where crew can see them.
  • E-cigarettes and heated tobacco devices aren't accepted at all, not even in checked baggage.
  • Vietjet's India travel page lists direct flights to Vietnam from New Delhi, Mumbai, Ahmedabad, Bengaluru and Hyderabad.
  • Keep cash, jewellery, cameras, laptops and phones in your cabin bag, not in checked baggage.
