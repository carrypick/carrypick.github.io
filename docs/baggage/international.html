Rules that catch travellers out

  • 32 kg per bag. Even with a 35 or 40 kg allowance, no single bag can weigh more than 32 kg. Split heavy luggage into two bags.
  • Weight or pieces? Most airlines here count your total kg. Kuwait Airways counts pieces (2 x 23 kg on Economy), and so does Thai Airways since March 2026 (one or two 23 kg bags). British Airways and Lufthansa give Economy one 23 kg bag. Saudia's economy bags are 23 kg each. Some airlines also limit the number of bags: IndiGo 2, Air Arabia 2, flydubai 3.
  • Bags need a flat side. Qatar Airways and Gulf Air refuse round bags and bundles tied with rope or wrapped in blankets. Emirates and flydubai ask you to repack odd-shaped items in a box, and Muscat airport charges extra for them.
  • Power banks stay in the cabin. Most of these airlines don't allow using or charging power banks on board, and Qatar Airways bans charging them. Never pack one in a checked bag.
  • Cheap fares may have no checked bag. Etihad Basic, flydubai Lite, Air Arabia Basic, IndiGo Lite, Kuwait Airways Zero Bag, Vietjet Eco, British Airways Basic and Lufthansa Economy Light include only a cabin bag. Buying extra baggage online before you fly is much cheaper than at the airport.
  • Medicines into the UAE. Controlled medicines need approval from the UAE health ministry before you travel. Carry the prescription. See what you can carry from India to Dubai.
  • Keep gold, cash and documents with you. flydubai says not to pack money, jewellery or precious metals in checked bags.

Flying back into India? Indian Customs allows goods worth ₹75,000 per person duty free since February 2026, plus 2 litres of alcohol. Gold, iPhones and cash have their own limits: see our Indian customs rules.

Questions

How many kg can I take from India to Dubai?

It depends on the airline and fare: Emirates gives 20–35 kg, IndiGo 30 kg on regular fares, Air India 25–35 kg, and flydubai 20–30 kg (none on Lite). All allow a 7 kg cabin bag.

How much baggage can I take from India to the UK or Europe?

In Economy, British Airways and Lufthansa each include one checked bag of up to 23 kg (none on BA's Basic or Lufthansa's Light fares), plus a cabin bag and a small personal item. Heavier bags, up to 32 kg, cost extra.

Does Vietjet include checked baggage from India?

Not on Eco fares: you get only 7 kg of cabin baggage and buy checked bags separately, cheapest with your ticket. Deluxe includes 20 kg and SkyBoss 30 kg.

How much baggage can I take from India to Bangkok?

On Thai Airways, one 23 kg bag on Saver and Standard fares and two 23 kg bags on Flexi and Full Flex, plus a 7 kg cabin bag. IndiGo gives 20 kg to Bangkok and Phuket on regular fares (25 kg on UpFront).

Which airline has the smallest cabin bag limit?

Gulf Air: 6 kg and 45 x 40 x 30 cm. Qatar Airways is also strict at 50 x 37 x 25 cm. Most 55 cm trolleys sold in India are too big for both.

Can I carry a bag tied with rope or a cloth bundle?

Usually not. Qatar Airways and Gulf Air refuse bags tied with rope or wrapped in blankets, and Dubai, Sharjah, Bahrain and Muscat airports have similar rules. Pack things in a suitcase or a sealed box.

Can I use a power bank on Emirates?

No. Since 1 October 2025 Emirates doesn't allow using or charging power banks on board. You can still carry one under 100 Wh in your cabin bag, never in checked baggage. Most other airlines here have similar rules.

What is the maximum weight of one checked bag?

32 kg on every airline here, even if your total allowance is higher. On Saudia and Kuwait Airways economy, each piece is 23 kg. Thai Airways economy bags are 23 kg each too.
