Rules change often and can depend on your fare, route and aircraft. Always check your ticket and the airline's official website before you fly.

Rules that catch travellers out

  • 32 kg per bag. Even with a 35 or 40 kg allowance, no single bag can weigh more than 32 kg. Split heavy luggage into two bags.
  • Weight or pieces? Most airlines here count your total kg. Kuwait Airways counts pieces (2 x 23 kg on Economy), and Saudia's economy bags are 23 kg each. Some airlines also limit the number of bags: IndiGo 2, Air Arabia 2, flydubai 3.
  • Bags need a flat side. Qatar Airways and Gulf Air refuse round bags and bundles tied with rope or wrapped in blankets. Emirates and flydubai ask you to repack odd-shaped items in a box, and Muscat airport charges extra for them.
  • Power banks stay in the cabin. Most of these airlines don't allow using or charging power banks on board, and Qatar Airways bans charging them. Never pack one in a checked bag.
  • Cheap fares may have no checked bag. Etihad Basic, flydubai Lite, Air Arabia Basic, IndiGo Lite and Kuwait Airways Zero Bag include only a cabin bag. Buying extra baggage online before you fly is much cheaper than at the airport.
  • Medicines into the UAE. Controlled medicines need approval from the UAE health ministry before you travel. Carry the prescription.
  • Keep gold, cash and documents with you. flydubai says not to pack money, jewellery or precious metals in checked bags.

Questions

How many kg can I take from India to Dubai?

It depends on the airline and fare: Emirates gives 20–35 kg, IndiGo 30 kg on regular fares, Air India 25–35 kg, and flydubai 20–30 kg (none on Lite). All allow a 7 kg cabin bag.

Which airline has the smallest cabin bag limit?

Gulf Air: 6 kg and 45 x 40 x 30 cm. Qatar Airways is also strict at 50 x 37 x 25 cm. Most 55 cm trolleys sold in India are too big for both.

Can I carry a bag tied with rope or a cloth bundle?

Usually not. Qatar Airways and Gulf Air refuse bags tied with rope or wrapped in blankets, and Dubai, Sharjah, Bahrain and Muscat airports have similar rules. Pack things in a suitcase or a sealed box.

Can I use a power bank on Emirates?

No. Since 1 October 2025 Emirates doesn't allow using or charging power banks on board. You can still carry one under 100 Wh in your cabin bag, never in checked baggage. Most other airlines here have similar rules.

What is the maximum weight of one checked bag?

32 kg on every airline here, even if your total allowance is higher. On Saudia and Kuwait Airways economy, each piece is 23 kg.

Flying within India?

Domestic allowances are smaller: usually a 7 kg cabin bag and 15 kg of check-in baggage. See our baggage rules for Indian airlines.
