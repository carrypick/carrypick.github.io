Rules that catch travellers out

  • 32 kg per bag. Even with a 35 or 40 kg allowance, no single bag can weigh more than 32 kg. Split heavy luggage into two bags.
  • Weight, not pieces. All five airlines use the weight concept on India routes, so your total kg is what counts. flydubai allows up to 3 bags, and Air Arabia up to 2.
  • Bags need a flat side. Qatar Airways refuses round bags and bundles tied with rope or wrapped in blankets. Emirates and flydubai ask you to repack odd-shaped items in a box.
  • Power banks stay in the cabin, switched off. Emirates, Etihad, flydubai and Air Arabia don't allow using them on board. Qatar Airways bans charging a power bank on board. Never pack one in a checked bag.
  • Cheap fares may have no checked bag. Etihad Basic, flydubai Lite and Air Arabia Basic include only a cabin bag. Buying extra baggage online before you fly is much cheaper than at the airport.
  • Medicines into the UAE. Controlled medicines need approval from the UAE health ministry before you travel. Carry the prescription.
  • Keep gold, cash and documents with you. flydubai says not to pack money, jewellery or precious metals in checked bags.

Questions

How many kg can I take from India to Dubai?

On Emirates, 20 kg to 35 kg of checked baggage depending on your fare; on flydubai, none on Lite, 20 kg on Value and 30 kg on Flex; on Air Arabia, none on Basic. All three allow a 7 kg cabin bag.

Which Gulf airline has the smallest cabin bag limit?

Qatar Airways: 50 x 37 x 25 cm and 7 kg, smaller than most 55 cm trolleys sold in India. Measure your bag before you fly.

Can I carry a bag tied with rope or a cloth bundle?

Qatar Airways won't accept bags tied with rope or string or wrapped in blankets. flydubai and Emirates ask for bags with a flat side and no loose straps, and may make you repack in a box.

Can I use a power bank on Emirates?

No. Since 1 October 2025 Emirates doesn't allow using or charging power banks on board. You can still carry one under 100 Wh in your cabin bag, never in checked baggage. Etihad, flydubai and Air Arabia also ban using them on board.

What is the maximum weight of one checked bag?

32 kg on all five airlines, even if your total allowance is higher. Split heavy luggage into two bags.

Indian airlines too

IndiGo and Air India Express also fly to the Gulf, but their international allowances differ from their domestic ones. Check your ticket, and see our baggage rules for Indian airlines for domestic flights.
