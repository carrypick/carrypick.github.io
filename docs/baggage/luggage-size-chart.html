Luggage size chart: inches to cm for cabin, medium and large bags

Updated 3 Nov 2026

Quick answer

  • 1 inch is 2.54 cm. So a 20-inch bag is 50.8 cm, a 24-inch bag is 61 cm and a 28-inch bag is 71.1 cm.
  • Indian brands mostly sell trolleys by height in cm: about 55 cm for cabin, 65–69 cm for medium and 75–79 cm for large.
  • A 22-inch bag (55.9 cm) is closest to the usual 55 cm cabin height. A 20-inch bag is smaller.
  • IndiGo's cabin limit, 55 x 35 x 25 cm, is 21.7 x 13.8 x 9.8 inches.
  • The inch size is only the height. Airlines check length, width and height, wheels and handles included.

Check-in bag size

Most Indian airlines limit each checked bag to 158 cm in total (length + width + height), which is about 62.2 inches, and 32 kg (about 70.5 lb). A large 75–79 cm trolley usually stays within 158 cm; an extra-large one may not.

Questions

What is 20 inch luggage in cm?

20 inches is 50.8 cm. That's smaller than the usual 55 cm cabin trolley sold in India.

Is a 24 inch suitcase cabin or check-in?

Check-in. 24 inches is 61 cm, too tall for the 55–56 cm cabin limits of Indian airlines. It's a small medium-size bag, good for 4–6 days.

How many inches is a 55 cm cabin bag?

55 cm is 21.7 inches.

What is 22 x 14 x 9 inches in cm?

22 x 14 x 9 inches is 55.9 x 35.6 x 22.9 cm, 114.3 cm in total. By size it fits Air India Express, but it's a little over the other Indian airlines' limits.

How many pounds is 23 kg?

23 kg is about 50.7 lb. 7 kg, the usual Indian cabin bag limit, is about 15.4 lb.
