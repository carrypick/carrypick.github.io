Quick answer

  • 1 inch is 2.54 cm. So a 20-inch bag is 50.8 cm, a 24-inch bag is 61 cm and a 28-inch bag is 71.1 cm.
  • Indian brands mostly sell trolleys by height in cm: about 55 cm for cabin, 65–69 cm for medium and 75–79 cm for large.
  • A 22-inch bag (55.9 cm) is closest to the usual 55 cm cabin height. A true 20-inch bag is smaller, but some brands sell 55 cm bags as "20 inch" and 65 cm bags as "24 inch", so check the cm.
  • IndiGo's cabin limit, 55 x 35 x 25 cm, is 21.7 x 13.8 x 9.8 inches.
  • The inch size is only the height. Airlines check length, width and height, wheels and handles included.

Check-in bag size

Most Indian airlines limit each checked bag to 158 cm in total (length + width + height), which is about 62.2 inches, and 32 kg (about 70.5 lb). Many large trolleys go over that: of the 31 large models we checked on Amazon.in on 6 Nov 2026, 26 were listed over 158 cm. See the 5 that weren't.

Questions

What are the standard suitcase sizes?

Indian brands mostly sell trolleys by height: about 55 cm (21.7 inches) for a small cabin trolley, 65–69 cm (25.6–27.2 inches) for medium and 75–79 cm (29.5–31.1 inches) for large. The tables above show each size in cm and inches.

What is the size of a small trolley bag in cm?

A small trolley usually means a cabin trolley, about 55 cm (21.7 inches) tall. Many are 36–41 cm wide, but IndiGo's cabin limit is 55 x 35 x 25 cm, so check all three sides before you buy.

What is the size of a medium trolley bag in cm?

Most medium trolleys sold in India are 65–69 cm (25.6–27.2 inches) tall and 135–147 cm in total, from the 36 models we read. They listed 45–93 litres, and 69 litres was typical.

What is 20 inch luggage in cm?

20 inches is 50.8 cm. That's smaller than the usual 55 cm cabin trolley sold in India, though some brands sell 55 cm bags as "20 inch".

Is a 24 inch suitcase cabin or check-in?

Check-in. 24 inches is 61 cm, too tall for the 55–56 cm cabin limits of Indian airlines. It's a small medium-size bag, good for 4–6 days.

How many inches is a 55 cm cabin bag?

55 cm is 21.7 inches.

What is 22 x 14 x 9 inches in cm?

22 x 14 x 9 inches is 55.9 x 35.6 x 22.9 cm, 114.3 cm in total. By size it fits Air India Express, but it's a little over the other Indian airlines' limits.

How many pounds is 23 kg?

23 kg is about 50.7 lb. 7 kg, the usual Indian cabin bag limit, is about 15.4 lb.
