Lufthansa baggage allowance from India: cabin bag and checked baggage

Checked on 28 Nov 2026 against Lufthansa's official website · economy on India routes

Quick answer

  • Cabin bag: One cabin bag up to 8 kg, plus a small personal item.
  • Size limit: 55 x 40 x 23 cm.
  • Extra small bag: One personal item up to 40 x 30 x 15 cm, such as a handbag, shopping bag or laptop bag, kept under the seat in front of you. Which bag? See our handbag and sling guide and laptop backpack guide.
  • Checked baggage: None on Economy Light, 1 bag of 23 kg on other Economy fares, 2 bags in Premium Economy.

Cabin baggage

On long-haul flights, such as those to and from India, every Economy and Premium Economy fare allows one cabin bag up to 8 kg and 55 x 40 x 23 cm, plus one personal item up to 40 x 30 x 15 cm. Business and First allow two cabin bags of 8 kg each, but on flights from India Business and First passengers must check in the second one. A folding garment bag, a pocket buggy, a small musical instrument and duty-free shopping all count within your cabin allowance. Lufthansa checks cabin bags at the airport: a bag that breaks the rules goes in the hold at your cost, EUR 60 / USD 75 to EUR 110 / USD 125 depending on the route, paid by credit card only. Lufthansa doesn't say whether wheels and handles count, so measure them in.

These are Lufthansa's allowances for intercontinental flights, which include all flights between India and Germany, the rest of Europe, the USA and Canada. Lufthansa counts pieces, not total kilos. Each Economy or Premium Economy bag can weigh up to 23 kg and measure up to 158 cm (length + width + height). A heavier or bigger bag is excess baggage, and nothing over 32 kg is taken as baggage. Lufthansa's baggage page lists only "Economy Class" next to Economy Light, and doesn't name other fares such as Classic or Flex there. Your ticket or booking email shows the allowance that applies to you. If your trip mixes airlines, the rules of the airline flying the longest part usually apply.

Infants

A baby under 2 gets one checked bag up to 23 kg and 158 cm, except on Light fares. Each child can also take one free item: a folding stroller, baby carriage or bicycle trailer in the hold, or a certified child seat or baby carrier in the cabin. A fully folding pocket buggy (up to 55 x 40 x 23 cm) can go in the cabin but counts as your cabin bag. Electric pushchairs are not allowed. On Economy Light, only the adult travelling with the baby can buy an extra bag. Children over 2 get the same allowance as adults.

Extra baggage charges

Lufthansa charges flat rates per bag by distance band: an extra 23 kg bag costs EUR 120 to 220 online or EUR 160 to 260 at the check-in desk on intercontinental routes.

  • Lufthansa's page has three intercontinental bands (short-, medium- and long-haul) but doesn't say which band flights from India fall into. The ranges below cover all three.
  • Extra standard bag (up to 23 kg and 158 cm) in Economy or Premium Economy, per route: EUR 120-140, 160-180 or 200-220 online; EUR 160, 210 or 260 at the check-in desk.
  • Extra bag that is over 23 kg or over 158 cm: EUR 250-255 to 400-405 online, EUR 270 to 420 at the desk. Over both: EUR 350-355 to 550-555 online, EUR 370 to 570 at the desk.
  • If a bag within your allowance is too heavy (23 to 32 kg) or too big (158 to 292 cm), you pay a flat EUR 100, 120 or 150; if it is both, EUR 200, 240 or 300.
  • Economy Light first bag: EUR 70-85 on lufthansa.com or EUR 105 at check-in, per direction (EUR 80 to and from the USA or Canada).
  • Fees are charged in the currency of your departure airport, and local taxes may be added. Book under My Bookings, during online check-in (up to 2 hours before departure, up to five bags), at the airport or through the Service Center. A paid bag can't be refunded on its own.

How Lufthansa differs from other airlines from India

  • Lufthansa counts bags, not kilos: 1 bag of 23 kg on Economy, even to Europe. Gulf airlines such as Emirates and Qatar Airways give a total weight in kilos on many routes.
  • Economy Light includes no checked bag at all on flights from India, only an 8 kg cabin bag and a personal item.
  • Its cabin bag limit is 8 kg and 23 cm deep, a little more than the 7 kg and 20 cm on Air India.
  • Power banks: 100 Wh and no charging on board, and they can't go in the overhead locker.
  • Small cats and dogs (up to 8 kg with the carrier) can fly in the cabin on Lufthansa-operated flights.

Sports equipment and pets

  • Sports gear counts in your free allowance if it fits the number of bags and weight for your class. On Economy Light it is always charged. Register it at least 72 hours before departure.
  • Over your allowance, sports items pay a flat rate per route, for example EUR 150, 200 or 250 for a golf bag or a packed bicycle on intercontinental routes. Bicycles must be in a bike case or bag.
  • Lufthansa's sports list has no entry for cricket kits, so these are charged as normal excess baggage if they are over your allowance.
  • Pets: only cats and dogs. A pet of up to 8 kg together with its soft carrier (max. 55 x 40 x 23 cm) can travel in the cabin; heavier pets go in the air-conditioned hold. The pet must be at least 12 weeks old (15 weeks to, from or via Germany).
  • Register the pet at least 72 hours before departure. Each passenger may take up to two animals. You pay the fee at check-in, but Lufthansa's site doesn't publish the amount, and it doesn't say whether pets can fly in the cabin on flights from India. Check with Lufthansa and the entry rules of every country on your route.

Check-in times at Indian airports

  • Lufthansa's check-in deadline tool (read 28 Nov 2026) shows these closing times for check-in and bag drop: Delhi T3, Bengaluru, Chennai and Hyderabad 90 minutes before departure; Mumbai T2 (counters A5-A7) 60 minutes before departure.
  • The tool lists Kolkata and Pune but gives no deadline for them, so check your booking.
  • Online check-in opens 30 hours before departure on lufthansa.com or the Lufthansa app. You must still have your boarding pass and have dropped your bags before the deadline.
  • Travelling with a pet in the cabin? You must collect your boarding pass at the check-in counter.

Changing planes in Frankfurt or Munich

  • On one ticket, your bags are usually checked through to your final airport. You don't collect them in Germany.
  • Frankfurt: transfer security checkpoints still use the 100 ml rule. Liquids must be in containers of up to 100 ml in one clear, resealable 1-litre bag. The 2-litre rule with new CT scanners applies only at some departure checkpoints.
  • Munich: if your trip started outside the EU, such as in India, your cabin liquids must already meet the 100 ml and 1-litre bag rule when you transfer. Duty-free bought in India should stay sealed in its security bag with the receipt.
  • Neither Frankfurt nor Munich Airport publishes a minimum connection time. Both say that on one ticket the airline plans enough time, and that separate tickets need extra time for security, passport control and re-checking bags.
  • Passport control: Munich Airport says that if you arrive from outside the Schengen area (such as India) and fly on outside it (such as to the USA or Canada), you usually don't pass passport control if you stay in the gate area. If you fly on to a Schengen country such as France or Italy, you do, and you may need a Schengen visa.

If your bag is delayed or damaged

  • Report a missing bag before you leave the airport, online or at the baggage tracing desk, and keep the reference number (PIR, for example FRALH12345). You can track it online with that number and get status updates by email.
  • If the bag isn't found within five days, fill in the contents list on the baggage status page. A bag delayed for more than 21 days counts as lost.
  • You can buy essential items while you wait and claim them back within two years: clothes you can keep using are repaid at 50%, toiletries at 100%.
  • Damaged bag: report it straight away at the airport, or online within seven days if you notice it later. Missing items from inside a bag must also be reported in writing within seven days.
  • Lufthansa's liability follows the Montreal Convention, up to 1,519 SDR (about EUR 1,900) per passenger.

Good to know

  • Power banks: up to two per passenger, each up to 100 Wh, in your cabin bag or on you only, never in checked bags. Power banks of 100 to 160 Wh need Lufthansa's approval, which you request through its Help Center. Charging or using a power bank on board is prohibited (except with approved medical devices), and power banks must not go in the overhead locker: keep them under the seat in front, in the seat pocket or on you.
  • Other devices with lithium batteries (phones, laptops, cameras) up to 100 Wh: up to 15 per passenger, best in your cabin bag. Spare batteries go only in the cabin, with the terminals taped or in a pouch.
  • Flying into India and connecting to a domestic flight? You must collect your bags, clear customs and check them in again. The same applies on arrival in the USA or Canada.
  • Flying to the USA: TSA may force open locked bags, so Lufthansa suggests a TSA-approved lock. Leaving the USA, you may take only one cabin bag and one personal item through security, whatever your class.
  • Tag every bag with your name and mobile number, inside and out. Free tags are available at the check-in counter.

What you can pack on Lufthansa

Lufthansa allows two power banks of up to 100 Wh, only in your cabin bag and never in the overhead locker; see our power bank guide. When you change planes in Frankfurt or Munich, the 100 ml liquids rule applies to your cabin bag, so pack pickle, ghee-based sweets and home food and wet masala in your checked bag.

Questions

How much cabin baggage is allowed on Lufthansa?

One cabin bag up to 8 kg, plus a small personal item.

What is the Lufthansa cabin bag size limit?

The limit is 55 x 40 x 23 cm. Measure the bag with its wheels and handles.

Can I carry a laptop bag or handbag too?

One personal item up to 40 x 30 x 15 cm, such as a handbag, shopping bag or laptop bag, kept under the seat in front of you.

How much checked baggage do I get on Lufthansa from India?

None on Economy Light, 1 bag of 23 kg on other Economy fares, 2 bags in Premium Economy. It depends on your fare type, so check your ticket.

How much baggage does an infant get on Lufthansa?

A baby under 2 gets one checked bag up to 23 kg and 158 cm, except on Light fares. Each child can also take one free item: a folding stroller, baby carriage or bicycle trailer in the hold, or a certified child seat or baby carrier in the cabin. A fully folding pocket buggy (up to 55 x 40 x 23 cm) can go in the cabin but counts as your cabin bag. Electric pushchairs are not allowed. On Economy Light, only the adult travelling with the baby can buy an extra bag. Children over 2 get the same allowance as adults.

What are Lufthansa extra baggage charges from India?

Lufthansa charges flat rates per bag by distance band: an extra 23 kg bag costs EUR 120 to 220 online or EUR 160 to 260 at the check-in desk on intercontinental routes.

What is the Lufthansa baggage allowance from India to Germany?

Economy Light includes no checked bag; other Economy fares include 1 bag of 23 kg, and Premium Economy 2 bags of 23 kg. Every Economy fare also includes an 8 kg cabin bag and a personal item.

What is the Lufthansa baggage allowance from India to the USA?

The same as to Europe: no checked bag on Economy Light, 1 bag of 23 kg on other Economy fares and 2 bags of 23 kg in Premium Economy. Lufthansa's rules usually apply to the whole trip if it is the first airline on your ticket.

What is the Lufthansa cabin baggage size and weight?

One cabin bag up to 8 kg and 55 x 40 x 23 cm (about 21.6 x 15.7 x 9 inches), plus a personal item up to 40 x 30 x 15 cm. Business and First allow two 8 kg cabin bags.

What are Lufthansa extra baggage fees from India?

An extra 23 kg bag costs EUR 120 to 220 online or EUR 160 to 260 at the check-in desk on intercontinental routes, depending on distance. Lufthansa doesn't say which distance band India is in, and fees are charged in the departure country's currency.

What is the maximum size of a Lufthansa checked bag?

158 cm in total (length + width + height) and 23 kg in Economy. Bigger or heavier bags, up to 292 cm and 32 kg, travel as excess baggage for a fee.

How do I track delayed Lufthansa baggage?

Report it online or at the airport baggage desk before you leave, and keep your reference number (PIR). You can check the status online with that number and subscribe to email updates.

How do I make a Lufthansa baggage damage claim?

Report the damage at the airport straight away, or online within seven days of getting your bag. Lufthansa's partner Dolfi1920 then decides on repair, replacement or payment.
