Can I carry it on a flight? Cabin and check-in rules in India

Checked on 3 Nov 2026 against BCAS, DGCA and airline pages

The rules most people get wrong

  • Power banks go only in your cabin bag. Keep them with you, not in the overhead bin, and don't use or charge them during the flight.
  • Liquids in your cabin bag: each container 100 ml or less, all in one clear, resealable 1-litre bag. This applies on domestic flights too.
  • Dry coconut (copra), camphor, lighters, matches, e-cigarettes and firecrackers are banned in both cabin and check-in baggage.
  • Knives, scissors, tools, cricket bats and spice powders can't go in your cabin bag.
  • One power bank model, the MOBIKINS MK-PB-04, is banned on flights in any bag. Akasa Air's rules list it by name.

Questions

Can I carry a power bank on a flight in India?

Yes, but only in your cabin bag, never in check-in. Keep it with you, not in the overhead bin, and don't use or charge it during the flight. Akasa Air allows up to 2, each up to 100 Wh.

Is the 100 ml liquid rule only for international flights?

No. In your cabin bag, each liquid, gel or cream must be 100 ml or less, all in one clear 1-litre bag, on domestic flights too. Bigger bottles go in check-in.

Can I carry dry coconut on a flight?

No. Dry coconut (copra) is banned in both cabin and check-in baggage because it can catch fire.

Can I carry ghee on a flight?

In check-in, up to 5 litres or 5 kg is allowed. In your cabin bag, only in containers of 100 ml or less inside the clear 1-litre bag.

Can I carry perfume in my cabin bag?

Yes, if each bottle is 100 ml or less and fits in your clear 1-litre liquids bag. Bigger bottles go in check-in.

Can I carry a razor on a flight?

A safety razor with the blades in a cartridge is fine in your cabin bag. Loose razor blades must go in check-in.

Can I carry alcohol on a domestic flight?

In check-in only, up to 5 litres per person of 24–70% alcohol in retail packaging (SpiceJet), and not to or from dry states. Sealed duty-free alcohol can go in the cabin.

Can I carry medicines on a flight?

Yes. Keep them in your cabin bag. Liquid medicines and inhalers over 100 ml are allowed with a prescription.
