Customs rules for iPhones, laptops and electronics in India (2026)

Checked on 15 Nov 2026 against the Baggage Rules, 2026 and other official CBIC documents

Quick answer

  • Laptop: one new laptop is duty free for passengers aged 18 or older, on top of your allowance.
  • New iPhone or other phone: it uses your duty-free allowance of ₹75,000 (Indian residents, NRIs, OCI cardholders) or ₹25,000 (foreign tourists).
  • Above the allowance: you pay 35% duty on the value above it, not on the whole price.
  • The phone and laptop you already use are free, as your used personal things.
  • Declare a TV, drone or satellite phone at the Red Channel. TVs aren't covered by the allowance at all.

This page explains the rules in simple words. It is not legal advice. Customs officers make the final decision, and rules and duty rates can change, so check with Indian Customs (CBIC) or the Customs help desk at the airport if you're unsure.

How much duty on a new phone?

New electronics bought abroad, such as phones, watches, cameras, earbuds and gaming consoles, all share your duty-free allowance. Duty is charged only on the value above it.

These examples assume nothing else new in your bags. Customs decides the value in rupees and works out the final amount, which can include a surcharge. Keep the bills and boxes with you.

Laptops

  • One new laptop is free for every passenger aged 18 or older (not airline crew). It's on top of the ₹75,000 or ₹25,000 allowance.
  • A second new laptop uses your allowance like any other item.
  • Tablets: the rule says "laptop including notepad". It doesn't name tablets such as the iPad, so ask the officer before you count one as your free laptop.
  • Flying with it: keep laptops and power banks in your cabin bag. See our laptop rules for flights.

How many iPhones can I bring?

The rules don't give a number. What matters:

  • It must be for personal use. Goods in commercial quantity, more than you'd need for yourself and your family, can't be cleared as baggage, even if you offer to pay duty.
  • Allowances can't be pooled. Each passenger has their own allowance for what they carry, but two people can't add theirs together to clear one costly phone.
  • Declare if the total value of your new goods is above your allowance, then pay at the Red Channel.

The phone and laptop you already use

Your used personal things are always duty free, so you don't declare the phone, laptop or camera you use every day.

Taking a costly new laptop or camera abroad and bringing it back? Before you leave India, get an export certificate on the Atithi app. It lists the items, so you can bring them back duty free without questions. It's valid until you first return, or for 6 months.

TVs, drones and satellite phones

  • Televisions aren't covered by the duty-free allowance. Flat-screen TVs also don't get the 35% baggage rate, so they pay the normal customs duty.
  • Drones and satellite phones are on the customs form. If you carry one, declare it and go to the Red Channel. Ask Customs before you buy a drone abroad.
  • PlayStations, smartwatches, cameras and earbuds count towards your allowance like a phone.

Moving back to India?

If you're moving home after 3 months or more abroad, the transfer of residence rules give a bigger allowance, from ₹1.5 lakh to ₹7.5 lakh, with one of each listed item such as a laptop, desktop computer, tablet, TV or gaming console. See the table in our Indian customs rules.

Questions

How many iPhones can I bring from Dubai to India?

The rules give no number. Phones for your own and your family's use are allowed, but goods in commercial quantity can't be cleared as baggage. New phones use your ₹75,000 allowance, and you pay 35% duty on the value above it.

Is there customs duty on an iPhone bought abroad?

Only if it, plus your other new goods, is worth more than your duty-free allowance: ₹75,000 for Indian residents, NRIs and OCI cardholders, or ₹25,000 for foreign tourists. Duty is 35% of the value above the allowance.

Can I bring a laptop from abroad duty free?

Yes. One new laptop is duty free for each passenger aged 18 or older, on top of the duty-free allowance.

Can I bring two laptops to India?

Yes. The first new laptop is free, and the second uses your duty-free allowance. If it's worth more than the allowance, you pay 35% duty on the extra.

Can I bring a PlayStation from Dubai to India?

Yes. A new gaming console counts towards your duty-free allowance like any other new item.

Can I bring a TV from Dubai to India?

Yes, but it isn't covered by the duty-free allowance and doesn't get the 35% baggage rate. Declare it at the Red Channel and pay the normal customs duty.

Do I have to declare the phone I use every day?

No. The phone, laptop and camera you already use are your used personal things, which are duty free.
