What can I carry from India to Dubai? UAE customs rules (2026)

Checked on 16 Nov 2026 against official UAE government pages (the UAE government portal, Dubai Customs, Abu Dhabi Customs, ICP and the Ministry of Health and Prevention)

Flying from India to Dubai, Abu Dhabi or Sharjah? These are the UAE's own customs rules for what you bring in: the duty-free limits, cash, banned things, medicines and food. For the trip home, see our Indian customs rules.

Quick answer

  • Duty free: gifts, perfumes and personal luggage worth up to AED 3,000, plus your own phone, laptop, camera, jewellery and clothes.
  • Cigarettes: up to 200 cigarettes, or 50 cigars, or 500 g of tobacco.
  • Alcohol: up to 4 litres, or 2 cartons of 24 beer cans. Extra alcohol is confiscated. Nobody under 18 can bring in alcohol or tobacco.
  • Cash: declare more than AED 60,000 in cash, cheques, gold or precious stones, on the Afseh app, the iDeclare app or the customs form. Declaring is free.
  • Banned: drugs, paan and betel leaves, naswar, poppy seeds (khus khus), fake goods and fake money.
  • Medicines: prescribed medicines for your own use, in normal amounts. Controlled ones, such as tramadol, pregabalin and codeine, need a prescription or medical report, for up to 3 months.

This page explains the UAE's rules in simple words. It is not legal advice. UAE customs officers make the final decision, and rules can change, so check with Dubai Customs, the UAE's ICP or the Ministry of Health and Prevention if you're unsure.

Duty-free allowance in the UAE

These come from the UAE government portal and Dubai Customs' 2025 passenger guide. You can bring these in without paying customs duty:

WhatDuty-free limit
Gifts, perfumes and personal luggageWorth up to AED 3,000
Cigarettes and tobacco200 cigarettes, or 50 cigars, or 500 g of tobacco
Alcohol4 litres, or 2 cartons of beer (24 cans of up to 355 ml each)
Your own thingsPhone, laptop, camera, personal jewellery, clothes, toiletries, a stroller or wheelchair and your medicines
  • Under 18? Cigarettes, tobacco and alcohol aren't allowed in at all.
  • Over the limit? You pay customs duty plus VAT on the extra. Extra alcohol is confiscated.
  • For yourself, not for selling. Your baggage and gifts must be personal and in small amounts. The allowance isn't for someone who brings goods to sell or travels with goods regularly.
  • 400 or 200 cigarettes? Some older official pages still say 400. The UAE government portal and Dubai Customs' 2025 passenger guide say 200, so plan for 200.

Cash, gold and the AED 60,000 rule

  • Declare more than AED 60,000 when you arrive in or leave the UAE. This counts cash in any currency, cheques and other payment papers, precious metals and precious stones.
  • Gold counts. Carrying a lot of gold? Add it to your cash when you check the AED 60,000 limit, and declare if you're over it.
  • Children: money carried by someone under 18 is added to the parent's or guardian's limit. Each adult has their own AED 60,000.
  • How to declare: on the Afseh app or ICP website, on Dubai Customs' iDeclare app, or on the customs declaration form at the airport. ICP says declaring costs nothing.
  • If you don't declare, ICP says you can get a customs fine, or be referred to the police and other authorities.

India has its own limit for leaving the country: up to ₹25,000 in Indian notes, and up to US$3,000 in foreign currency notes per trip from a bank or money changer. That's far below AED 60,000. See how much cash you can carry abroad from India.

Banned: don't pack these

The UAE government portal, Dubai Customs and Abu Dhabi Customs list these as banned:

  • Drugs of any kind.
  • Poppy seeds (khus khus). Abu Dhabi Customs lists poppy and its seeds with banned drugs. Check your masala box before you pack.
  • Paan and betel leaves, and naswar (a smokeless tobacco).
  • Fake branded goods and fake money.
  • Gambling tools and machines.
  • Indecent material, and books or art that go against Islamic values.
  • Things used in black magic or witchcraft.
  • Pirated films, music and software.
  • Laser pens with a red beam (Abu Dhabi Customs).

Need a permit first

These are restricted. You need approval from the right UAE authority before you bring them in:

  • Medicines and medical equipment (see below)
  • Pets and other live animals
  • Plants and seeds. Seedlings with soil aren't allowed at all.
  • Drones, wireless and transmission devices
  • Weapons, ammunition and fireworks
  • E-cigarettes and electronic hookahs
  • Food products, beyond what you carry for yourself (see below)

Medicines

  • Ordinary prescribed medicines: the UAE government portal says you may bring them for personal use "in normal quantity". Carry the prescription, and keep them in their original packs.
  • Controlled medicines: narcotic, psychotropic and some other medicines are controlled in the UAE. The health ministry's list for travellers allows most of them only for your stay or 3 months, whichever is less, with a prescription or an attested medical report.
  • Common Indian medicines on that list include tramadol, pregabalin, gabapentin, codeine, alprazolam, clonazepam, diazepam, zolpidem, methylphenidate and pseudoephedrine (found in some cold tablets). Read the generic name on your strip and check the full list.
  • Some are banned outright, such as cannabis products. A medicine missing from the list may still be covered by the law, the list itself says.
  • A permit for some medicines. The UAE government portal says some medicines need a permit from the Ministry of Health and Prevention. Check the ministry's e-service for importing medicines for personal use well before you fly.
  • No bulk. Dubai Customs says medicines in bulk can be impounded, even for personal use, and it can become a criminal case. Customs can pass medicines to the health ministry, and keeps them if the ministry doesn't approve.

Keep medicines in your cabin bag. See can I carry medicines on a flight? for airline rules.

Food from home

  • Food products need permits in Dubai's 2025 passenger guide, and food in commercial amounts isn't allowed. Abu Dhabi Customs also lists food products as restricted.
  • We found no official limit in kg for ghee, pickles, sweets or spices carried for yourself. Keep amounts small, sealed and clearly for your own use.
  • Cooked, home-made meals are the riskiest. An older Dubai Customs page listed them as banned.
  • Your airline has rules too. Ghee and pickle usually go in check-in, not the cabin. See ghee, pickle, sweets and home food and spices and masala.

E-cigarettes and vapes

  • Not banned, but restricted. The UAE lists e-cigarettes and electronic hookahs as restricted goods, and Dubai Customs' iDeclare app asks if you carry them.
  • Your airline decides the bag. Vapes and their batteries usually go only in your cabin bag, never in check-in. Check your airline's page before you fly.

Packing tips for the UAE

  • Weigh your bag before you leave home, and again before the flight back with your shopping. Most airlines won't take one bag over 32 kg.
  • Keep receipts for costly new things, in case Customs asks their value.
  • Pack medicines with their prescriptions in your cabin bag.

Questions

What can I carry from India to Dubai duty free?

Gifts, perfumes and personal luggage worth up to AED 3,000, plus 200 cigarettes (or 50 cigars or 500 g of tobacco) and 4 litres of alcohol if you're 18 or older. Your own phone, laptop, camera, jewellery and clothes are free too.

How much cash can I carry from India to Dubai?

Indian rules let you take up to ₹25,000 in Indian notes and up to US$3,000 in foreign currency notes per trip. The UAE asks you to declare more than AED 60,000 in cash, cheques, gold or precious stones.

Can I carry medicines from India to Dubai?

Yes, prescribed medicines for your own use in normal amounts, in their original packs with the prescription. Controlled medicines such as tramadol, pregabalin and codeine are allowed only for your stay or 3 months, whichever is less, with a prescription or medical report. Some medicines need a health ministry permit.

Which medicines are banned in Dubai?

Drugs such as cannabis products are banned. Many common medicines, such as tramadol, pregabalin, gabapentin, codeine, alprazolam and zolpidem, are controlled rather than banned: you need a prescription or medical report, and no more than 3 months' supply.

Can I carry khus khus (poppy seeds) to Dubai?

No. Abu Dhabi Customs lists poppy and its seeds among banned drugs, so leave khus khus at home.

Can I carry paan to Dubai?

No. Paan and betel leaves are banned by Dubai Customs and Abu Dhabi Customs.

Can I carry ghee and pickle to Dubai?

Small amounts for yourself are usually fine. We found no official limit in kg, but Dubai Customs says food products need permits and food in commercial amounts isn't allowed. Pack them sealed, in your check-in bag.

Can I carry gold from India to Dubai?

Your personal jewellery is duty free. If your gold and cash together are worth more than AED 60,000, declare them. Before you leave India, ask Indian Customs for an export certificate for costly jewellery, so you can bring it back without duty. See our Indian customs rules.

Can I carry a vape to Dubai?

Vapes aren't banned, but the UAE lists e-cigarettes as restricted, and Dubai Customs' iDeclare app asks if you carry them. Keep them in your cabin bag, as airlines usually require.

How much alcohol can I take to Dubai?

Up to 4 litres, or 2 cartons of 24 beer cans, if you're 18 or older. Extra alcohol is confiscated.
