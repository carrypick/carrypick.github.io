Air India Express baggage rules: cabin bag size, weight and check-in

Checked on 3 Nov 2026 against Air India Express's official website · domestic economy

Quick answer

  • Cabin bag: One cabin bag up to 7 kg.
  • Size limit: 56 x 36 x 23 cm, and 115 cm in total (L + W + H).
  • Extra small bag: A laptop bag up to 30 x 40 x 10 cm, but it shares the same 7 kg with your cabin bag.
  • Free check-in: 15 kg, 1 piece (none on Xpress Lite).

Cabin baggage

Air India Express allows one cabin bag up to 7 kg, no bigger than 56 x 36 x 23 cm (115 cm in total), including handles, wheels and side pockets. The Xtra Carry-On add-on lets you carry up to 12 kg.

Check-in baggage (domestic)

Xpress LiteNo check-in bag (cabin bag only)
Xpress Value and Classic15 kg, 1 piece
Xpress Flex20 kg (see note)

Air India Express's booking page shows 20 kg for Flex, but its FAQ says 15 kg for every fare except Lite, so check the allowance on your ticket. Each checked bag can be up to 81 x 119 x 119 cm and 32 kg.

Good to know

  • Air India Express's FAQ says the 7 kg includes your laptop and personal items. Plan for 7 kg in total across everything you carry on.
  • Infants get an extra 10 kg of check-in baggage but no cabin allowance.

Questions

How much cabin baggage is allowed on Air India Express?

One cabin bag up to 7 kg.

What is the Air India Express cabin bag size limit?

The limit is 56 x 36 x 23 cm, and 115 cm in total (L + W + H). Measure the bag with its wheels and handles.

Can I carry a laptop bag or handbag too?

A laptop bag up to 30 x 40 x 10 cm, but it shares the same 7 kg with your cabin bag.

How much free check-in baggage do I get on Air India Express domestic flights?

15 kg, 1 piece (none on Xpress Lite). It depends on your fare type, so check your ticket.
