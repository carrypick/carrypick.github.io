How to measure your bag

How this works

  • We compare your bag's longest side with the airline's longest allowed side, and so on, because a bag can be turned to fit.
  • When an airline gives a total size (length + width + height), we add up your three sides.
  • These are the standard domestic economy limits. Your fare, route or aircraft can change them.
