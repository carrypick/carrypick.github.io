How to measure your bag

How this works

  • We compare your bag's longest side with the airline's longest allowed side, and so on, because a bag can be turned to fit.
  • When an airline gives a total size (length + width + height), we add up your three sides.
  • These are the standard economy limits: domestic flights for Indian airlines, India routes for international airlines, and the airlines' general rules for US and European airlines such as American, Delta, Ryanair and easyJet. Your fare, route or aircraft can change them.
