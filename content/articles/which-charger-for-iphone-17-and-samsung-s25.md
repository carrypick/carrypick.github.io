New phones from Apple, Samsung and Google come **without a charger in the box**. So the first question is which one to buy, and how many watts. The short answer: a **USB-C charger with USB PD**, about **45 W for a Samsung Ultra or Pixel 10 Pro XL**, and **20 W or more for an iPhone**. A 65 W GaN charger covers all of them and a laptop too, which is handy on a trip.

## Quick answer

- **iPhone 18 Pro and 18 Pro Max:** Apple says 50% in about **15 minutes** with a **60 W-capable adapter with AVS** (Adjustable Voltage Supply), like its 40W Dynamic Power Adapter with 60W Max.
- **iPhone Duo:** 50% in about **20 minutes** with a **60 W-capable adapter** (Apple).
- **iPhone 17, 17 Pro, 17 Pro Max:** Apple says 50% in about 20 minutes with a **40 W adapter or higher**. A 20 W charger also works, just slower.
- **iPhone Air, 17e, 16, 15:** a **20 W** USB-C PD charger or higher gives 50% in about 30 minutes (Apple).
- **Galaxy S25 Ultra, S25+ and S25 FE:** **45 W** Super Fast Charging 2.0 needs a 45 W charger with **PPS** and a **5 A cable**. The base S25 takes 25 W.
- **Pixel 10 and 10 Pro:** 30 W **PPS** charger. **Pixel 10 Pro XL and 10a:** 45 W PPS.
- **Is a bigger charger safe?** Yes. The phone takes only what it needs. A 65 W USB PD charger on an iPhone is fine.

## Which charger for iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone Duo?

Apple's support page says the **iPhone 18 Pro and 18 Pro Max** reach 50% in about **15 minutes**, and the **iPhone Duo** in about **20 minutes**, with the USB-C cable from the box and one of two adapters:

- Apple's own **40W Dynamic Power Adapter with 60W Max**, or
- a third-party adapter that is **60 W-capable (or more)**, uses **AVS** and supports **USB PD 3.2**.

The iPhone 18 Pro spec page's small print says USB PD 3.1 or better with AVS. The iPhone Duo spec page asks only for a 60 W-capable USB PD adapter and a 60 W-capable cable. Apple doesn't say how many watts either phone draws at its peak.

Our picks are 65 W, but their listings say USB PD 3.0 and don't mention AVS. They charge an iPhone 18 Pro or Duo safely and fast, but Apple only promises the 15 or 20-minute figure with the adapter types above. A 20 W charger also works, just slower: Apple doesn't give a time for it on these phones.

## Which charger for iPhone 17?

Apple's figure for the iPhone 17, 17 Pro and 17 Pro Max is **50% in about 20 minutes with a 40 W adapter or higher**. On its support page Apple names its own **40W Dynamic Power Adapter with 60W Max**, or a third-party charger that is **60 W-capable, supports USB PD 3.2 and AVS**.

Many chargers in India are USB PD 3.0 and don't mention AVS. All our picks are like that, including Samsung's own 45 W adapter. They still charge an iPhone 17 safely and fast, but Apple only promises the 20-minute figure with the charger type above. If you want that exact speed, check the box for "PD 3.2" or "AVS".

A **20 W** USB-C charger still works with every iPhone 17. Apple gives 50% in about 30 minutes with 20 W for the Air and 17e, and older iPhones.

### Can I use a 65W charger for iPhone 17?

Yes. USB PD chargers and the phone agree on the power first. The iPhone asks for what it can take, and the charger gives no more than that. The same is true for the iPhone 18 Pro, Duo, 16 and 15.

## Which charger for Samsung S25 Ultra?

The S25 Ultra and S25+ reach **45 W** with Samsung's Super Fast Charging 2.0. Samsung's own 45 W adapter page says it needs a **USB PD 3.0 device with PPS**, and its box has a **5 A cable**. Samsung UK's help page says 45 W is only possible with a 45 W charger **and a 5 A cable**.

So you need two things:

- A charger that says **PPS** and gives **45 W on one port**.
- A **5 A USB-C to USB-C cable**. The 3 A cable in the phone's box caps it at 25 W.

The **S25 FE** also takes **45 W** wired: Samsung India's page says 65% in about 30 minutes. The base **Galaxy S25** takes **25 W**, so any 25 W PPS charger is enough, like Samsung's own 25 W adapter (EP-T2510N, see [our picks](#our-charger-picks)). The **A36** also does 45 W Super Fast Charge, by Samsung's page.

The surest way is **Samsung's own 45 W adapter (EP-T4511)**. Samsung India's page for it says USB PD 3.0 with PPS, and tells you to use a genuine Samsung 5 A cable. Our pick is the version sold with a cable. Of our other picks, the Spigen listing states **45 W Super Fast Charging for Samsung** when one port is used alone. The Belkin charger lists PD 3.0 and PPS and 45 W on a port, but doesn't name Samsung's 45 W mode.

### Which cable for Samsung 45W charging?

A USB-C to USB-C cable rated **5 A** (often sold as **100 W**). Most cables are 3 A (60 W), and with one the phone stays at about 25 W. Our [USB-C cable guide](which-usb-c-cable.html) explains 3 A vs 5 A, the e-marker chip and data speeds. Look for "5A" in the listing, not just "fast charging". Our pick is a 2 m Belkin rated 5 A and 100 W, and USB-IF certified, as listed. It also charges a laptop from a 65 W charger.

## Pixel, Nothing and OnePlus

- **Pixel 10 and 10 Pro:** Google says fast charging needs a **30 W USB-C PPS** charger or higher. The **10 Pro XL** and **10a** need **45 W PPS**. All three of our chargers support PPS: the Spigen and Belkin by their listings, the Samsung by Samsung's own page.
- **Nothing Phone (3):** 65 W, and Nothing lists PPS and PD 3.0, so a 65 W PPS charger gets close.
- **OnePlus 13 and 15:** their top speed (100 W and 120 W) is **SUPERVOOC**, which needs the OnePlus charger in the box. OnePlus doesn't state the speed on other USB PD chargers, so take the box charger on trips.

## GaN charger vs normal charger

**GaN** (gallium nitride) is a material inside the charger. It wastes less power as heat, so a GaN charger is **smaller and lighter** for the same watts. A 65 W GaN charger is often about the size of an old 20 W phone charger.

It doesn't charge your phone faster by itself. Speed comes from the **watts** and the **standard** (USB PD, PPS) that both the phone and charger support.

**Is a GaN charger safe?** As safe as any charger from a known brand. In India, chargers must carry the **BIS** mark. Our Spigen and Belkin GaN picks list BIS.

## One charger for phone and laptop on a trip

On a trip, one 65 W charger with two or three ports can replace a phone charger, an earbuds charger and often the laptop brick:

- **65 W** runs most USB-C laptops, like a MacBook Air. A big 14 or 16-inch laptop may want more.
- **Ports share the power.** With two devices plugged in, each gets less. The Belkin splits 45 W + 20 W.
- **Abroad you still need the right plug.** Our chargers have Indian pins. Use a [travel adapter](travel-adapter-by-country.html) for the UK, Europe or the USA.
- **Voltage:** our chargers' listings don't say "100-240 V". Most countries use 220-240 V, like India. For the USA, Canada or Japan, read the small print on the charger itself before you go.
- **Chargers are fine in both bags.** Power banks are not: they go in the cabin only. See [power bank rules on flights](power-bank-rules-on-flights.html).

Keep the charger and cables together in a [gadget organiser](gadget-organiser.html).

## Questions

### Which charger for iPhone 18 Pro?

Apple names its 40W Dynamic Power Adapter with 60W Max, or a 60 W-capable third-party adapter with USB PD 3.2 and AVS, for 50% in about 15 minutes. Any USB-C PD charger works, just slower.

### Does iPhone 18 Pro or iPhone Duo come with a charger?

No. Apple's spec pages list the phone, a 1 m USB-C Charge Cable and papers in the box.

### Which charger is best for iPhone 17?

Any USB-C charger with USB PD works. Apple says 40 W or higher gives 50% in about 20 minutes, and names its 40W Dynamic Power Adapter or a 60 W-capable charger with USB PD 3.2 and AVS. A 20 W charger is slower but fine.

### How many watts does iPhone 17 support?

Apple doesn't give one top number. It says 50% in about 20 minutes with a 40 W adapter or higher. For the iPhone Air and 17e it says 20 W or higher.

### Does iPhone 17 come with a charger?

No. Apple's box has the phone, a USB-C cable and papers. The charger is sold separately.

### Which charger for Samsung S25 Ultra?

A 45 W charger with PPS and a 5 A USB-C cable, for Super Fast Charging 2.0, like Samsung's own EP-T4511. The S25 Ultra box has no charger.

### Does the S25 FE support 45W charging?

Yes. Samsung India's page says 45 W wired, and 65% in about 30 minutes. It needs a 45 W PPS charger and a 5 A cable, like the S25 Ultra.

### Can I use a Samsung charger for an iPhone?

Yes, if it's USB-C with USB PD, like Samsung's 25 W or 45 W adapters. The iPhone takes what it needs.

### Is a GaN charger better than a normal charger?

It's smaller and runs cooler for the same watts. It isn't faster on its own: watts and the charging standard decide speed.

### Can I carry a charger in a flight?

Yes, a wall charger is fine in cabin and check-in bags. Power banks are the ones that must go in the cabin.
