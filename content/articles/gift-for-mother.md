## How to choose a gift for your mom

- **Choose something she'll use every day.** A watch, a handbag or walking shoes get used far more than a showpiece.
- **Make it easy to use.** A watch with a clear dial and a sliding clasp, and shoes that slip on without laces.
- **Look at what she makes do with.** An old bag with a broken zip, a worn-out purse or tired chappals tells you the gift.
- **Shoes need her size.** Check an old pair, or choose a watch, a bag or an attar set instead.
- **For a mother-in-law, keep it safe.** A known brand and something useful: a watch, a wallet, an attar set or a thermal set.
- **Mother's Day 2027 is Sunday 9 May.** Order a few days early, or have it delivered to your address and give it yourself.

## What to write in the card

- **From a daughter:** "Maa, everything I know about being strong, I learned from you. Thank you for everything."
- **From a son:** "Thank you for every packed tiffin and every prayer. Love you, Maa."
- **Mother's Day:** "Happy Mother's Day to the one who always puts us first. Today, you come first."
- **Mother-in-law:** "Thank you for welcoming me like your own. I'm lucky to have you."

Add one memory, like a trip or a dish she makes best. That's the line she'll read twice.

Something she'll use every day and wouldn't buy for herself: a Titan watch, a small tote or satchel, slip-on walking shoes, or a thermal set for winter.
