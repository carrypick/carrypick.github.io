## How to choose a Valentine's gift

- **Think about what they carry every day.** A watch, a wallet, a bag or sunglasses get used daily, so the gift doesn't end up in a drawer.
- **First Valentine's together? Keep it simple.** A perfume gift set or a smaller watch is personal without being too much. Save pair watches for when you're sure.
- **For a wife or husband, upgrade something old.** A worn-out wallet, a bag with a broken zip or a watch with a faded strap are easy clues.
- **Perfume gift sets are safer than one big bottle.** Four small bottles let them find a scent they like.
- **Check the colour you click.** One Amazon page can show several colours or sizes. Make sure the one you choose is the one they'd wear.
- **Add a handwritten note.** A short note in your own words means more than any card from a shop.
- **Order by early February.** Valentine week is busy, so check the delivery date shown on Amazon before you pay.

## Questions

### What is a good Valentine's gift for a girlfriend?

A dressy watch like the Titan Raga, a perfume gift set of four small bottles, or a vegan leather sling for dates and days out. If it's your first Valentine's together, a perfume set or a smaller watch is a safe choice.

### What is a good Valentine's gift for a wife?

An elegant watch like the Titan Karishma, a tote that fits what she carries every day, or a zip-around wallet as a smaller gift. Pair watches you both wear are a nice idea too.

### What is a good Valentine's gift for a boyfriend?

A watch like the Timex Classics, a men's perfume gift set, polarized sunglasses or a leather sling. Think about what he uses every day and choose a better version of it.

### What is a good Valentine's gift for a husband?

A classic watch like the Titan Classique, a leather wallet gift set, or an office-style perfume set. If his wallet or watch strap is worn out, that's your clue.

### What are the days of Valentine week?

Rose Day (7 February), Propose Day (8), Chocolate Day (9), Teddy Day (10), Promise Day (11), Hug Day (12), Kiss Day (13) and Valentine's Day (14 February).

### What can I give for a long distance Valentine's Day?

Send something they'll use every day, so they think of you: a watch, a perfume set or a wallet. Check that the seller delivers to their city and pick a delivery date before the 14th.

### When should I order a Valentine's gift?

By early February. Valentine's Day 2027 is on a Sunday, and Valentine week is busy, so check the delivery date shown on Amazon before you pay.
