## What the leather words mean

Listings use these words a lot. From best to cheapest:

| Listing says | What it means |
|---|---|
| Full grain leather | The top layer of the hide, with its natural surface. The strongest; it darkens nicely with use. |
| Top grain leather | The top layer, sanded smooth. Still real, good leather, a little less tough. |
| Genuine leather | Real leather, but usually lower layers of the hide. Fine for the money, wears out sooner. |
| PU, vegan or faux leather | Not leather: a plastic coating on fabric. Light and cheap, but it can peel after a year or two. |

Leather and PU both work for gifts. If the person cares about animal products, PU (vegan) is the kind choice.

## How to choose a wallet

- **Card slots.** Count what's in your wallet now: Aadhaar, licence, PAN, debit and credit cards, a metro card. 6 to 10 slots suit most people; 14 suits people who carry everything.
- **Thickness.** A slim bifold (about 2 cm) fits a front trouser pocket. Wallets with zips and many slots get thick when full.
- **Coin pocket.** Useful in India, where change still comes in coins.
- **Zip or open.** A zip-around wallet keeps coins and receipts in, and is common for women's wallets.
- **RFID blocking.** It stops contactless cards from being read through the wallet. It's a nice extra for trips abroad and crowded places; your cards already need you to be very close to be read.

## How to choose a belt size

Belts are sold by waist size in inches, like trousers. Choose your trouser waist size, or one size up if you wear the belt over a thick shirt.

- **Auto-lock (slide) buckles** have no holes: the strap slides into the buckle and locks at any point. The fit is easier to get right.
- **Pin buckles with holes** are classic. Check how many holes there are and how far apart they are.
- **Cut-to-size belts** come long and you trim the strap at home, so one size fits a range of waists.
- **Width.** About 3 to 3.5 cm is a dressy office width; 3.8 cm (1.5 inch) is a regular width that also suits jeans. Check that it fits your trouser loops.
- **Reversible belts** have black on one side and brown on the other, so one belt goes with both shoe colours. Handy when you pack light.

At airport security in India, a belt with a metal buckle can set off the scanner, so you may be asked to take it off. Wear it on the flight, but be ready to put it in the tray.

## Questions

### Is a wallet a good gift?

Yes. Almost everyone uses one every day, and it doesn't need an exact size like clothes. A wallet in a gift box is easy to give to a colleague, boss, husband or father.

### What is the difference between genuine leather and full grain leather?

Both are real leather. Full grain is the top layer of the hide with its natural surface, and lasts longest. Genuine leather usually comes from lower layers, and wears out sooner.

### Is PU leather real leather?

No. PU, vegan and faux leather are a plastic coating on fabric. They're light and cost less, but can peel over time.

### Do I need an RFID blocking wallet?

It isn't a must. RFID blocking stops contactless cards from being read through the wallet, which some people like for travel abroad and crowded places.

### How do I choose a belt size?

Buy your trouser waist size in inches, or one size up if you wear it over thick clothes. Auto-lock belts and cut-to-size belts fit a range of waists, so they're the safe choice for a gift.
