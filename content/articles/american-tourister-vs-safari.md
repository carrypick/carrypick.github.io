**American Tourister vs Safari trolley bag: which is better?** Choose **American Tourister** if your fare is tight on weight: its typical bags were 0.2 to 0.5 kg lighter. Choose **Safari** if you want a large bag that fits the 158 cm check-in limit, or a polycarbonate shell. On warranty, neither brand covers airline damage, and both cover manufacturing defects only.

We compared both brands' listings on Amazon.in by what they list, and read both warranty policies on 26 Nov 2026. We haven't tested these bags, so this says nothing about how long they last.

## Quick answer

- **Lighter: American Tourister.** Typical medium bags were 3.3 kg against 3.5 kg, and large bags 4 kg against 4.5 kg.
- **Large bags within 158 cm: Safari.** Its typical large bag was 160 cm in total, against 167 cm, and 3 of 10 fitted, against 1 of 4. Most large bags from both brands still go over.
- **More listed litres: Safari.** But litres are often unreliable, so go by the full size.
- **Warranty length:** the models we picked list 3 years, except Safari's Pentagon Pro medium, which lists 5. Both brands say the length is on the warranty card.
- **Warranty abroad:** American Tourister says it's global, with service centres in 120+ countries. Safari says its warranty applies in 200+ countries.

Safari's list of exclusions is longer and names cracks and zip bursts. American Tourister's policy names "abrasion" and wear and tear. Both say the company decides whether a fault is covered.

### How to claim

- **American Tourister:** call +91-93709-54059 or email service@samsonite.com, the after-sales contacts on americantourister.in. Take the bag to an approved Samsonite service centre with the warranty certificate or receipt.
- **Safari:** call 080 691 48000 for the warranty claim link, then send a front photo of the bag, the purchase date, the invoice, the warranty card, photos of the defect and your postal address. Safari's site also has a Warranty Registration link in its footer.

### If an airline damages your bag

Neither warranty helps, so claim from the airline. Check the bag at the belt, report damage at the airline's baggage desk before you leave, and keep the reference number and photos. American Tourister's damage page says an authorised repair centre can give you an assessment report to take back to the airline.

## Questions

### American Tourister vs Safari trolley bag: which is better?

Neither wins on everything. In the listings we read, American Tourister was lighter, and Safari had more large bags within 158 cm. Both warranties cover manufacturing defects only. We haven't tested them, so we can't say which lasts longer.

### Is Safari a good brand for luggage?

Safari Industries (India) Ltd is a Mumbai company, and its website says "Since 1974". Its bags we read were a little heavier than American Tourister's, and many were polycarbonate. Its warranty excludes cracks, dents and zip bursts, so read the card in the box.

### Is American Tourister lighter than Safari?

In the listings we read, usually yes: 3.3 kg against 3.5 kg in medium, and 4 kg against 4.5 kg in large. Check the bag you're buying, because models vary.

### Which has the better warranty, American Tourister or Safari?

On paper, they are close. The models we picked list 3 years, except Safari's Pentagon Pro medium at 5. American Tourister's is repaired at Samsonite service centres worldwide. Safari's policy lists more exclusions, including cracks.

### How do I register or claim an American Tourister warranty in India?

Keep the warranty certificate or the original receipt. For a claim, contact American Tourister's after-sales team on +91-93709-54059 or service@samsonite.com, and use an approved Samsonite service centre. Repairs anywhere else void the warranty.

### How do I claim a Safari bag warranty?

Call Safari on 080 691 48000 for the claim link. Send photos of the bag and the defect, the invoice, the warranty card, the purchase date and your address. You need the warranty card stamped by the seller.

### American Tourister vs Safari vs VIP: which is better?

VIP gives 3 years on hard and soft luggage under its own policy. We read too few VIP bags on Amazon.in to compare weights fairly. See [VIP vs American Tourister](vip-vs-american-tourister.html), [VIP vs Safari](vip-vs-safari.html) and [trolley bag brands compared](best-trolley-bag-brands-india.html).

### American Tourister vs Safari vs Skybags: which is lighter?

In our listings, American Tourister was lightest in medium (3.3 kg), then Safari (3.5 kg) and Skybags (3.57 kg). See [Safari vs Skybags](safari-vs-skybags.html).

### Is Kamiliant the same as American Tourister?

Not quite. Listings call it "Kamiliant by American Tourister", it's a separate brand in the Samsonite group, and it uses the same Samsonite warranty terms. Its medium bags were among the lightest we read.
