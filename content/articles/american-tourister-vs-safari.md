American Tourister and Safari are two of the first names people see when buying a trolley in India. Neither wins on everything. We compared what their listings say, so you can choose by the number that matters for your trip.

## Quick answer

- **Lighter: American Tourister.** Its typical bags were 0.2 kg lighter in medium and 0.5 kg lighter in large.
- **More listed litres: Safari.** But litres are often unreliable, so check the full size too.
- **Large bags near the 158 cm limit: Safari.** Its typical large bag was 160 cm in total, against 167 cm. Most large bags from both brands still go over 158 cm.
- **Warranty: no clear winner.** The models we picked from both brands list 3 years.

**Our view:** choose American Tourister if your fare is tight on weight. Choose Safari if you want more listed room or a large polycarbonate bag. We haven't tested these bags, so this says nothing about how long they last.

## Questions

### Which is better, American Tourister or Safari?

Neither wins on everything. In the listings we read, American Tourister was lighter, and Safari listed more litres and a smaller total size in large bags. We haven't tested them, so we can't say which lasts longer.

### Is American Tourister lighter than Safari?

In the listings we read, usually yes. Typical medium bags were 3.3 kg against 3.5 kg, and typical large bags 4 kg against 4.5 kg. Check the bag you're buying, because models vary.

### Which has the better warranty, American Tourister or Safari?

The models we picked from both brands list 3 years, and some say it's international. Warranty can differ by model, so check the listing and keep your invoice.

### Is Safari an Indian brand?

Yes. Safari Industries (India) Ltd is a Mumbai-based company founded in 1980. American Tourister belongs to the international Samsonite group.

### Is Kamiliant the same as American Tourister?

Not quite. Listings call it "Kamiliant by American Tourister", and Samsonite names it as a separate brand in its group. Its medium bags were among the lightest we read. See [trolley bag brands compared](best-trolley-bag-brands-india.html).
