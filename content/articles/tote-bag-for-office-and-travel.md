A good tote carries your laptop, charger, lunch box and water bottle to the office on weekdays, and becomes your personal bag on a flight at the weekend. The things that matter are simple: does your laptop fit, does it zip shut, and is it light enough to carry all day? Here's how to choose, and the totes we picked by their listed specs.

## Quick answer

- **Carry a 15.6-inch laptop?** Choose a tote that lists 15.6 inch for its laptop compartment.
- **Carry a 13 or 14-inch laptop?** A smaller, lighter tote is enough.
- **For travel, choose a zip top** and a trolley sleeve, so the tote rides on your suitcase handle.
- **No laptop?** A small tote or satchel is lighter and neater.

## Which laptop size fits?

Laptops are measured across the screen, corner to corner. The body is bigger than the screen size suggests:

| Laptop | Typical body size | What to look for |
|---|---|---|
| 13 to 14 inch | About 30-32 x 21-23 cm | A compartment listed for 13 or 14 inch |
| 15.6 inch | About 36 x 24-25 cm | A compartment listed for 15.6 inch |
| 16 inch | About 36 x 25-26 cm | A tote listed for 16 inch |

These are typical sizes. Measure your own laptop and compare it with the listing, because some 14-inch laptops are wider than others. A padded sleeve protects the laptop when you put the bag down.

## Zip, magnet or open top?

- **Zip:** the safest for buses, metros and airports. Nothing falls out when the bag tips over.
- **Magnet or button:** quick to open, but easy for someone else to open too.
- **Open top:** fine for the car and the office, not for crowded places.

For travel, choose a zip over the main compartment. All four of our picks list a zip.

## A trolley sleeve, pockets and weight

- **A trolley sleeve** is a strap at the back that slides over your suitcase handle. At the airport, the tote rides on the trolley and your shoulder gets a rest. Our DailyObjects pick has one.
- **A bottle pocket** keeps water away from your laptop.
- **Small inside pockets** for keys, cards and a phone save digging.
- **Weight empty:** a tote that weighs nearly 1 kg empty feels heavy by evening with a laptop in it. Listed weights on our picks go from 350 g to 800 g where the listing gives one.

## Will it fit as your personal bag on a flight?

Most Indian airlines let you carry one small personal bag with your cabin bag. The rules differ:

| Airline | Personal bag rule |
|---|---|
| IndiGo | One personal item up to 3 kg, like a ladies' purse or a small laptop bag |
| Air India | One small bag up to 3 kg and 40 x 30 x 20 cm, under the seat |
| Akasa Air | One personal item up to 3 kg, under the seat |
| Air India Express | A laptop bag up to 30 x 40 x 10 cm, but it shares your 7 kg |

From our [airline baggage rules](../baggage/index.html). Our DailyObjects tote is listed at 44 cm long, which is longer than Air India's 40 cm limit; the Zouk tote, at 39.5 cm, is inside it. Soft totes squash a little, but staff can ask to measure. Check your airline before you fly.

## Questions

### Which tote bag is good for office use?

One with a padded laptop compartment that fits your laptop, a zip over the main compartment, a bottle pocket and a few small pockets. Keep it light: under about 800 g empty.

### Can a tote bag carry a 15.6-inch laptop?

Only if the listing says it fits 15.6 inch. Many totes fit only 13 or 14 inch. Our DailyObjects pick lists a padded compartment for up to 15.6 inch.

### Can I use a tote bag as my personal item on a flight?

Usually, yes. IndiGo, Air India and Akasa Air allow a small personal bag up to 3 kg. Air India also sets 40 x 30 x 20 cm, so check your tote's size. On Air India Express, it counts inside your 7 kg.

### Is a tote bag good for travel?

A tote with a zip and a trolley sleeve is handy at airports and on trains. For sightseeing in crowds, a sling is safer, because you wear it in front. See our [sling bag for travel guide](sling-bag-for-travel.html).

### Tote or laptop backpack for the office?

A backpack spreads the weight over both shoulders, which helps on a long commute. A tote looks smarter and is easier to open at a desk. See our [laptop backpack guide](laptop-backpack-guide.html) to compare.

### Is a tote bag a good gift for a woman who works?

Yes, if it fits her laptop. Ask what size laptop she carries, or choose a no-laptop tote. See more ideas in our [birthday gift ideas](birthday-gift-ideas.html).
