Manali in winter means cold nights, sunny but chilly days and, with luck, snow at Solang or Sissu. IMD's averages put Manali at about −1 °C at night in January, and the snow points above town are colder still. This list covers what to wear, what to carry for the snow, the overnight bus and the hotel, and which bag to take, whether it's a 3-day Manali trip or a week on a Shimla–Manali tour.

## Manali weather in winter

IMD's 1991–2020 averages for Manali, as in our [Manali in January guide](../trips/manali-in-january.html) (read 25 Nov 2026):

| Month | Day | Night | Rain or snow |
|---|---|---|---|
| December | About 13.5 °C | About 0.3 °C | About 39 mm |
| January | About 10.5 °C | About −1 °C | About 90 mm |
| February | About 12 °C | About 0.3 °C | About 114 mm |

- **Nights are below or near freezing** all winter. Himachal Tourism says December and January nights can fall below −5 °C. The coldest January night on IMD's record is −11.6 °C.
- **Solang, Sissu and the Atal Tunnel are colder** than Manali town, and windy. Pack for the snow point, not for the Mall Road.
- **Sunny days feel mild,** so you'll take layers off at lunch and put them back on at 4 pm.
- **Snow isn't certain.** January and February are wetter than December. Check the forecast for your dates and our [Manali guide](../trips/manali.html#will-there-be-snow) before you pack.

## The short list

If you pack only these, you'll be fine:

1. Two thermal sets (top and bottom)
2. Two sweaters or fleeces
3. A warm jacket with a hood
4. Warm trousers. Not only jeans: wet jeans stay cold
5. Waterproof gloves, a woollen cap that covers your ears, and a neck warmer or muffler
6. Woollen socks, two pairs more than your days
7. Waterproof shoes or boots with a good grip
8. Sunglasses, sunscreen and lip balm: the sun on snow is strong
9. Moisturiser and cold cream: the air is dry
10. Phone, charger and a power bank, in your hand bag
11. Your ID, hotel booking and some cash

## How to layer for Manali

1. **Next to your skin:** a thermal top and bottom. Take a spare set, because one gets damp in the snow.
2. **Middle:** a sweater or fleece. Two thin ones are more useful than one thick one.
3. **Outside:** a warm, hooded jacket that covers your hips. For Solang and Sissu, wear it over a fleece.
4. **Extras:** cap, neck warmer, gloves and woollen socks. Your ears, fingers and toes get cold first.

Take a layer off before you start to sweat. Damp clothes make you cold the moment you stop. For more, read [what to wear in Manali in December](what-to-wear-in-manali-in-december.html) and [what to wear on a winter hill trip](winter-clothes-for-hill-trips.html).

## For the snow at Solang and Sissu

- **Waterproof gloves.** Knit gloves are fine on the Mall Road, but they soak through the first time you touch snow.
- **Waterproof trousers, or rent a snow suit.** Shops near Solang rent snow suits and gumboots by the day. If you rent boots, wear your own thick socks.
- **Woollen socks, and a dry pair in your day bag.** Wet socks are the fastest way to cold feet.
- **Sunglasses.** Sun on snow is very bright. Polarized lenses cut the glare.
- **A day bag with a rain cover** for a spare layer, water, snacks and the power bank.
- **Skiing?** Solang's ski courses run from January to March if there's enough snow. See [skiing at Solang](../trips/manali-in-january.html#skiing-at-solang-in-january).

## Shoes for Manali in winter

- **Waterproof, with a deep, grippy sole.** Paths and hotel steps are icy in the morning, and snow turns to slush by noon.
- **Avoid mesh sports shoes.** They soak through in minutes.
- **Room for thick socks.** Go half a size up if you'll wear woollen socks.
- **Slippers or sandals with socks** for the hotel room.
- See [shoes for a Manali or Kashmir trip](shoes-for-manali-and-kashmir-trip.html) for boots, hiking shoes and renting gumboots.

## Skin, health and medicines

- **Lip balm with SPF, a rich moisturiser and sunscreen.** Cold, dry air cracks lips fast, and the mountain sun burns even when it's cold.
- **Cold and headache tablets, and your own medicines,** with the prescription. Pharmacies in Manali close early in winter.
- **A water bottle.** You feel less thirsty in the cold, but you still need water.
- **Motion sickness tablets** if the hill road from Mandi or Kiratpur bothers you.

## Phone, power and the cold

- **Cold drains phone batteries fast.** Keep your phone in an inside pocket, and carry a power bank.
- **Power cuts happen after heavy snow.** A power bank and a small torch help. Ask your hotel about backup power and heating when you book.
- **Save tickets and your hotel address offline.** Signal is weak at Solang and beyond the Atal Tunnel.

## On the overnight Volvo bus

Most people reach Manali on an overnight bus from Delhi or Chandigarh. Wear your thermals and jacket, because buses stop at night in the cold, and keep in your hand bag:

- A neck pillow and eye mask
- A shawl or light blanket, socks and your cap
- Water, snacks, tissues and wet wipes
- Phone, power bank, ID and tickets
- Motion sickness tablets

## What to pack for women

- Two thermal sets, and leggings to wear under trousers or kurtas
- Two sweaters or fleeces, and a long, warm jacket with a hood
- Warm trousers, plus waterproof trousers or a rented snow suit for the snow
- A woollen cap, a stole or muffler, waterproof gloves and woollen socks
- Waterproof shoes or boots, and slippers for the hotel
- A crossbody bag that zips shut for the Mall Road and Old Manali

## What to pack for men

- Two thermal sets
- Two or three full-sleeve T-shirts or shirts, and two sweaters or fleeces
- A warm, hooded jacket
- Two pairs of warm trousers, not only jeans
- A cap, neck warmer, waterproof gloves and woollen socks
- Waterproof shoes or winter boots, and slippers

## What to pack for kids

- Thermals, two sweaters, a warm jacket with a hood, and a cap that covers their ears
- Waterproof gloves and a spare pair: kids always get them wet in the snow
- More socks than days, and waterproof shoes
- Snacks, a water bottle and something to do on the bus
- Their own medicines, and cold cream

## What not to pack

- **Too many jeans.** One pair is enough. Warm trousers dry faster and keep you warmer.
- **Mesh sports shoes and canvas shoes** for the snow.
- **Heavy hair dryers.** Most hotels have one, and power cuts are common.
- **Lots of cash in one wallet.** Carry some cash for taxis and small shops, and use UPI where you can.

## Which bag to take

- **3 to 5 days:** a soft duffle or a medium trolley, plus a day backpack. Winter clothes are bulky, so pack the jacket and boots you'll wear on the bus last, or just wear them.
- **Roll thermals and sweaters** into packing cubes. They take less space and you'll find things in a hotel with bad light.
- **Flying to Bhuntar (Kullu) or Chandigarh?** Check your fare's checked baggage and the cabin bag size on our [domestic baggage rules](../baggage/index.html) page.

## Driving to Manali in winter

If you drive or take a taxi, Himachal Police advise carrying warm clothes, blankets, food, water, a first-aid kit, a torch and a power bank, and snow chains or anti-skid tyres. See [driving in snow](../trips/manali.html#driving-in-snow) in our Manali guide. After snow, the road to Solang and the Atal Tunnel is often closed to tourist cars or open to 4x4s only.

## Questions

### What should I pack for Manali in December?

Two thermal sets, two sweaters or fleeces, a warm hooded jacket, warm trousers, a woollen cap, a neck warmer, waterproof gloves, woollen socks and waterproof shoes with a good grip. Add sunglasses, sunscreen, lip balm, moisturiser and a power bank. IMD's December averages are about 13.5 °C by day and 0.3 °C at night.

### What should I wear in Manali in January?

Layers: thermals, a sweater or fleece and a warm jacket, plus a cap, gloves and waterproof shoes. January is Manali's coldest month, about 10.5 °C by day and −1 °C at night on IMD's averages, and the best chance of snow.

### Do I need snow boots for Manali?

Not always. Waterproof hiking shoes or boots with a deep, grippy sole are enough for most trips. If you'll play in deep snow at Solang, you can rent gumboots there by the day. Avoid mesh sports shoes.

### Can I rent snow clothes in Manali?

Yes. Shops on the way to Solang rent snow suits, gumboots and gloves by the day. Still carry your own thermals, warm socks and a jacket, because you need them in town and at night.

### Is a Manali packing list different for a Shimla–Manali trip?

Not much. Shimla is a little milder than Manali, but nights are still near freezing in December and January. Pack the same layers. See our [Shimla in December guide](../trips/shimla-in-december.html).

### How many clothes should I pack for 5 days in Manali?

Two thermal sets, two sweaters, one jacket, two pairs of warm trousers, three or four T-shirts or tops, and seven pairs of socks. Wear the same jacket and sweaters again: you won't sweat much in the cold.

### Should I carry a power bank to Manali?

Yes. Cold drains phone batteries, and power cuts are common after heavy snow. Keep it in your hand bag, and in your cabin bag if you fly. See [can I carry a power bank on a flight?](../baggage/can-i-carry/power-bank.html)
