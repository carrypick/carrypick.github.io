**Which trolley bag brand is best in India?** No brand is best at everything. American Tourister and Kamiliant listed the lightest bags. Safari had the most large bags that fit the 158 cm check-in limit. VIP, Skybags and Aristocrat come from one company and share one warranty policy. Pick the bag that fits your airline and your fare, then check the warranty on that model.

We compared seven brands in two ways. First, 146 trolley listings on Amazon.in, by their listed weight and size. Second, each brand's own warranty page, re-read on 26 Nov 2026. We haven't tested these bags, so nothing here says how long they last.

## Quick answer

- **Lightest:** Kamiliant, then American Tourister. Kamiliant's large Rock-Lite was the lightest large bag we found, at 3.48 kg.
- **Large bags within 158 cm:** L'Attitude and Safari had the most.
- **Most soft-case choices:** Aristocrat. Skybags and VIP have some too.
- **Warranty length:** most models we picked list 3 years. VIP's policy gives 3 years on hard and soft luggage. American Tourister and Kamiliant say the length is on the warranty card or the product page.
- **Airline damage:** American Tourister, Kamiliant and VIP say their warranty doesn't cover damage by airlines. Safari excludes damage from rough handling in transit. If your bag breaks on a flight, claim from the airline before you leave the airport.

## Warranty: what each brand covers

We read each brand's own warranty page on 26 Nov 2026. In short, they all cover manufacturing defects only, and the brand decides what counts as one.

| | American Tourister and Kamiliant | Safari | VIP, Skybags and Aristocrat |
|---|---|---|---|
| How long | As stated on the warranty card or the product's features | As stated on the authorised warranty card | Hard and soft luggage 3 years; backpacks and duffels 6 months |
| Who it covers | First buyer or gift recipient | First buyer or gift recipient | Buyers from VIP or its authorised channels |
| Airline damage | Not covered ("transport damage, by airlines for example") | Not covered ("mishandling and abuse in transit") | Not covered (airlines, airports, railways and other carriers) |
| Also not covered | Misuse, accidents, abrasion, water, wear and tear | Cracks, dents, zip bursts, scratches, wear on wheels and zips | Wear and tear, scratches, dents, misuse, accidents |
| What you need | Warranty certificate or original receipt | Stamped warranty card and original invoice | Original invoice, and warranty papers where given |
| Outside India | Global: any Samsonite service centre (120+ countries, says American Tourister) | Valid in 200+ countries; you pay to send the bag | Support "may vary" by country |
| Who pays shipping to the service centre | You | You | Not stated |

Two things follow. First, a bag cracked by baggage handlers is usually the airline's claim, not the brand's. Report it at the airline's baggage desk before you leave the airport and get a reference number. Second, keep the invoice and the warranty card, because both Safari and Samsonite ask for them. If you buy on Amazon, keep the Amazon invoice.

## The brands, in short

- **American Tourister** is part of the Samsonite group. Its bags were among the lightest we read, and its Liftoff+ range is listed with a TSA lock. Head to head with Safari? See [American Tourister vs Safari](american-tourister-vs-safari.html).
- **Safari** is a Mumbai company; its website says "Since 1974". It listed many polycarbonate bags. Its warranty policy lists more exclusions than the others, including cracks and zip bursts.
- **Kamiliant** is sold as "Kamiliant by American Tourister" and uses Samsonite's warranty terms. It had the lightest typical bags, all hard shells. We didn't find a warranty length on the two Kamiliant listings we picked, so check the card in the box.
- **Aristocrat** has many soft-case bags as well as hard ones. It's sold on VIP's own store, under VIP's warranty policy.
- **Skybags** is also a VIP Industries brand. Its range mixes hard bags with the soft Rubik. Some Skybags cabin listings on Amazon.in say 5 years, more than VIP's policy, so check the card in your bag. See [Safari vs Skybags](safari-vs-skybags.html).
- **VIP** showed few bags in our Amazon searches, so our numbers say little about it. VIP's own store lists 19 lines: see [VIP's ranges, warranty and returns](vip-bags-guide.html), [VIP vs American Tourister](vip-vs-american-tourister.html) and [VIP vs Safari](vip-vs-safari.html).
- **L'Attitude** lists its bags by both cm and inches. Its Meridian large bag was among the heaviest we read, at 5.16 kg.

## Questions

### Which trolley bag brand is best in India?

No brand wins on everything. In the listings we read, Kamiliant and American Tourister were the lightest, and Safari had the most large bags within 158 cm. All the big brands cover only manufacturing defects. Choose the model that fits your airline's limits and your fare.

### Which trolley bag company is best in India?

The big names come from three companies: Samsonite (American Tourister, Kamiliant), VIP Industries (VIP, Skybags, Aristocrat) and Safari Industries (Safari). Each sells direct on its own website as well as through shops and Amazon.

### Which trolley bag brand is best for international travel?

Look for a warranty that works abroad. American Tourister says its warranty is global, with service centres in over 120 countries. Safari says its warranty applies in 200+ countries, but you pay to send the bag in. VIP says international support may vary. For flights abroad, check your airline's limits too: see [trolley bag size for international travel](trolley-bag-size-for-international-travel.html).

### Which trolley bag brand is the lightest?

Kamiliant had the lowest typical weights we read: 2.97 kg in medium and 3.96 kg in large. American Tourister was close behind in large, at 4 kg.

### Does a trolley bag warranty cover airline damage?

Usually not. American Tourister, Kamiliant and VIP say so in their policies, and Safari excludes damage from mishandling in transit. Report the damage to the airline at the airport, and keep the photos and reference number.

### VIP or American Tourister: which is better?

We read too few VIP listings on Amazon.in to judge fairly. VIP's policy gives 3 years on luggage. American Tourister's length depends on the model, and its warranty is global. See [VIP vs American Tourister](vip-vs-american-tourister.html) for two hard polycarbonate VIP bags side by side.

### VIP or Safari: which is better?

We read only 3 VIP medium and large bags, against 18 from Safari, so we can't compare them fairly. On paper, VIP gives 3 years on luggage, and Safari's length is on the warranty card. See [VIP vs Safari](vip-vs-safari.html).

### Skybags or American Tourister: which is better?

In our listings, American Tourister's typical bags were lighter: 3.3 kg against 3.57 kg in medium, and 4 kg against 4.6 kg in large. Skybags' Swirl large was smaller in total, at 152 cm. See also [Mokobara vs American Tourister](mokobara-vs-american-tourister.html).

### Are Aristocrat and Skybags part of VIP?

Yes. VIP Industries' warranty policy names VIP, Skybags, Caprese, Aristocrat and Alfa as its brands, and its store sells them all.
