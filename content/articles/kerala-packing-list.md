A Kerala trip usually means two climates. The coast, from Kochi to Alleppey, Kumarakom, Varkala and Kovalam, is hot and humid by day, around 32 °C even in January. Up in Munnar and Thekkady the mornings are misty and cold, and some tea valleys touch 0 °C. So you pack light cotton for most of the trip, one real warm layer for the hills, good sun care and mosquito repellent. This list covers a 5- to 7-day trip from November to February.

## Kerala weather from November to February

IMD's long-term averages (1981–2010) for the coast, as in our [Kerala in January guide](../trips/kerala-in-january.html) (read 25 Nov 2026):

| Place | Day | Night | Rain in January |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kochi | About 32 °C | About 23 °C | About 24 mm, on about 1 day |
| Thiruvananthapuram (Kovalam, Varkala) | About 32 °C | About 22 °C | About 20 mm |
| Alappuzha (backwaters) | About 33 °C | About 22 °C | About 19 mm |
| Munnar (about 1,600 m) | About 23 °C | Mostly 2–9 °C | – |

- **The coast is hot all winter.** In December, Kochi's averages are about 32 °C by day and 23 °C at night, with a few showers. Humid air makes it feel hotter, so pack loose cotton or linen.
- **Munnar is much colder.** IMD has no long-term averages for Munnar. In January 2026 most nights were 2–9 °C (tea research station records), and some estates touched −1 °C in late December 2025, news reports said. It doesn't snow, but you may see frost on the grass at dawn.
- **November and early December can still bring showers.** January and February are the driest months. Carry a light rain layer or a small umbrella until mid-December.
- **Check the forecast** on [IMD Thiruvananthapuram](https://mausam.imd.gov.in/thiruvananthapuram/) in the week before you go.

## The short list

If you pack only these, you'll be fine:

1. Light, loose cotton or linen clothes, one set a day
2. One warm jacket or fleece, a cap and socks for Munnar and Thekkady
3. Long, loose clothes that cover shoulders and knees, for temples and churches
4. Sunscreen, sunglasses and a cap or hat
5. Mosquito repellent for the backwaters and evenings
6. A waterproof phone pouch for boats and the beach
7. A light rain poncho or umbrella if you go before mid-December
8. Sandals or flip-flops, and walking shoes with a grip for Munnar
9. A cloth bag and a refillable bottle: Kerala has banned single-use plastic
10. A power bank, your photo ID, bookings and some cash

## Clothes for the coast and the backwaters

- **Loose cotton or linen,** in light colours. Synthetics feel sticky in the humidity.
- **Full-sleeved tops and long trousers or skirts** for evenings on a houseboat: they also keep mosquitoes off.
- **Swimwear for the beach,** and a cover-up for walking back. Varkala's cliff paths and Kovalam's town are not beachwear places.
- **Swim only in the flagged areas near lifeguards** at Kovalam and Varkala. Our [Kerala guide](../trips/kerala.html#beaches-varkala-and-kovalam) has the beach safety rules.

## Clothes for Munnar and Thekkady

- **A full-sleeve top or thermal, a sweater or fleece, and a warm jacket** for evenings and early mornings.
- **For a sunrise at Top Station or frost at dawn:** your warmest jacket, a woollen cap, gloves and a muffler.
- **Walking shoes with a good grip.** Estate paths are wet with dew in the morning.
- **Elephants and gaur** use the roads around Munnar. Don't stop to feed or photograph them from close by.

For more, read [what to wear on a winter hill trip](winter-clothes-for-hill-trips.html).

## Rain, boats and the beach

- **A waterproof phone pouch** for houseboats, shikara rides, kayaking and the beach.
- **A rain poncho or a small umbrella** for November and early December showers.
- **Wear a life jacket on small boats.** Choose a houseboat from Kerala Tourism's [approved houseboat list](https://www.keralatourism.org/houseboatlist).
- **A dry bag or zip pouch** for your passport, cash and cards on boat days.

## Mosquitoes, sun and health

- **Mosquito repellent.** The backwaters and Kochi's evenings bring mosquitoes. Put it on before sunset, and wear long sleeves on the houseboat.
- **Sunscreen with a high SPF,** and put it on again after a swim. See our [travel sunscreen guide](travel-sunscreen-guide.html).
- **Sunglasses and a cap or hat.** The midday sun on water and sand is strong.
- **Drink plenty of water,** bottled or filtered. Coconut water is everywhere.
- **Your own medicines,** with the prescription, plus stomach and travel-sickness tablets for the winding roads to Munnar.

## What to wear at temples in Kerala

Many Kerala temples keep a traditional dress code, and the strictest is at the **Sree Padmanabhaswamy Temple** in Thiruvananthapuram. Its [official site](https://spst.in/) says (read 25 Nov 2026):

- **Men:** a traditional Kerala mundu and veshti (upper cloth) are mandatory. Trousers, shorts and shirts must be left at the locker counter.
- **Women:** a saree, half-saree or pattu pavadai with blouse. Trousers and short dresses are not allowed.
- **There's a locker counter** for the clothes you take off.

Other temples set their own rules, and some ask men to remove their shirts, so check at the gate. At churches and mosques, cover your shoulders and knees. A light cotton mundu is easy to buy in Kerala, and a stole or dupatta is handy for women everywhere.

## Plastic rules

Kerala has banned single-use plastic and plastic carry bags since 2020, and Munnar has its own ban on small plastic water bottles, carry bags and straws (news reports, June 2026). **Carry a steel bottle and a cloth bag,** and take your rubbish back with you.

## What to pack for women

- Five or six loose cotton kurtas, tops or dresses, and light trousers, palazzos or long skirts
- Leggings and a cardigan or warm jacket for Munnar
- A stole or dupatta for temples and churches
- Swimwear and a cover-up for the beach
- Sandals and walking shoes
- A crossbody bag that zips shut
- Read [travel essentials for women](travel-essentials-for-women.html) and [solo female travel in India](solo-female-travel-india.html)

## What to pack for men

- Five or six cotton or linen shirts and T-shirts
- Two pairs of light trousers and a pair of shorts for the beach
- A sweater or fleece and a light jacket for Munnar
- Swim shorts
- Sandals and walking shoes
- A mundu is easy to buy there if you plan to visit temples

## What to pack for kids

- Light cotton clothes for the coast, and a warm jacket, cap and socks for Munnar
- A sun hat, kids' sunscreen and a mosquito repellent your doctor is happy with
- Swimwear, and make sure they wear a life jacket on every boat
- Travel-sickness tablets your doctor suggests for the hill roads
- Snacks and a water bottle

## Kerala packing list for 7 days

- 6 light cotton tops or shirts, 3 pairs of light trousers, skirts or shorts
- 1 long, loose outfit for temples and churches
- 1 sweater or fleece, 1 warm jacket, 1 cap, 2 pairs of warm socks for Munnar
- 1 swimsuit and a cover-up
- 7 pairs of socks and underwear
- 1 stole or dupatta
- 1 pair of sandals, 1 pair of walking shoes
- Sunscreen, mosquito repellent, sunglasses, a cap or hat
- A rain poncho or small umbrella (November and early December)
- Waterproof phone pouch, power bank, charger, steel bottle, cloth bag
- ID, cash, bookings and your medicines

## Which bag to take

- **5 to 7 days, flying:** a cabin trolley is enough for light cotton. Wear your jacket on the flight, and check your fare's cabin bag on our [domestic baggage rules](../baggage/index.html) page.
- **Houseboats and homestays** have little space. A soft duffle or backpack is easier to carry on and off boats than a big hard trolley.
- **A day backpack** for Munnar walks and boat days.
- **Bringing home spices, tea or banana chips?** Leave room, and pack them sealed.

## Questions

### What should I pack for a Kerala trip?

Light, loose cotton clothes for the coast, one warm jacket or fleece for Munnar, a long outfit for temples, sunscreen, sunglasses, mosquito repellent, a waterproof phone pouch, sandals and walking shoes. Add a rain poncho before mid-December. IMD's averages for Kochi are about 32 °C by day and 23 °C at night in December and January.

### What should I wear in Kerala in December?

Loose cotton or linen on the coast and the backwaters, with a cap and sunscreen, and a warm jacket, cap and socks for Munnar's cold mornings. Carry a light rain layer for early December showers.

### Do I need warm clothes for Kerala?

Only for the hills. The coast stays warm all winter, but Munnar's nights were mostly 2–9 °C in January 2026, and some tea estates touch 0 °C. A fleece or light warm jacket, a cap and socks are enough for most people.

### What is the dress code for Kerala temples?

It depends on the temple. At the Sree Padmanabhaswamy Temple in Thiruvananthapuram, the official site says men must wear a mundu and veshti, with no trousers, shorts or shirts, and women a saree, half-saree or pattu pavadai with blouse. Elsewhere, cover your shoulders and knees, and check the rule at the gate.

### What should women wear in Kerala?

Loose cotton kurtas, tops, dresses and long skirts or trousers, with a stole or dupatta for temples and churches. Swimwear is fine on the beach, with a cover-up for the walk back. Add a cardigan or warm jacket for Munnar.

### Do I need mosquito repellent in Kerala?

Yes, for the backwaters, houseboat evenings and Kochi after sunset. Wear long sleeves in the evening too.

### How many clothes should I pack for 7 days in Kerala?

Six light tops, three trousers or skirts, one temple outfit, one warm layer and a jacket for Munnar, a swimsuit, sandals and walking shoes. See the 7-day list above.
