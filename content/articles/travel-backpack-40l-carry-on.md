A 40 litre backpack is the biggest bag most people can carry on a plane. It holds about **4–5 days of clothes**, so many travellers skip the check-in bag altogether. But "40 L" says nothing about the outside size, and that is what airlines measure. Here's which airlines our 40–45 L picks fit, by their listed size.

## Quick answer

- Most cabin limits are about **55 x 35 x 25 cm** or **56 x 36 x 23 cm**, and **7 kg** on Indian and Gulf airlines.
- A 40 L backpack listed around **50 x 34 x 23 cm** fits IndiGo, Akasa, SpiceJet, Qatar Airways, Etihad, Lufthansa and US airlines.
- **Air India, flydubai and Air Arabia** allow only **20 cm** of depth, and **Emirates 22 cm**. A full 40 L backpack is usually deeper than that.
- Weigh it packed. An 898 g empty bag leaves about **6 kg** for everything else on a 7 kg airline.

## 40 L vs 30 L vs 45 L

- **30 litres:** an office bag that stretches to 2 days. See [office backpacks](office-backpack-for-men.html).
- **40 litres:** 4–5 days of light clothes, a laptop and a toiletry bag. The sweet spot for carry-on only.
- **45 litres and up:** often still within 55 x 35 x 25 cm by its listed size, but hard to keep under 7 kg once full. Trekking rucksacks are often too tall: our [Gear Alphamount](../picks/B097KFYPCG.html) is listed at 65.5 cm.

## What to look for in a carry-on travel backpack

1. **Listed size in cm.** Compare it with your airline's limit before you buy, not after.
2. **Light empty weight.** Every gram the bag weighs is a gram less for your clothes on a 7 kg airline.
3. **A laptop sleeve.** Then the backpack is your one cabin bag, and you can still take a small personal item on IndiGo, Akasa or Air India.
4. **A trolley sleeve.** It slides over a suitcase handle if you also check a bag in.
5. **A shoe compartment or packing cubes.** They keep a 40 L bag tidy. See our [packing cubes and organisers](../category/travel-gear.html).
6. **Rain cover.** Bags get wet on the tarmac and in the monsoon.

## Questions

### Is a 40L backpack carry-on size?

Often, yes. A 40 L backpack listed at about 50 x 34 x 23 cm fits IndiGo, Akasa, SpiceJet, Qatar Airways, Lufthansa and US airlines. It's too deep for Air India, Emirates, flydubai and Air Arabia when packed full.

### Can I carry a 40L backpack on IndiGo?

Yes, if it measures 55 x 35 x 25 cm or less and weighs 7 kg or less with everything in it. All three of our picks are listed within that size.

### Is 40L enough for a week?

For light summer clothes, yes, if you wash once. For winter or a work trip with formal clothes, a 40 L bag is tight. Our [packing list maker](../tools/packing-list-maker.html) helps you cut down.

### How heavy is a 40 litre backpack?

Empty, our picks weigh 600 g to 898 g. Packed, most people reach 7 kg quickly, so weigh it at home. Our [bag weight tool](../tools/bag-weight.html) helps.

### Can I take a backpack and a laptop bag on a flight?

On IndiGo, Akasa and Air India you can take one small personal item as well, up to 3 kg. On Air India Express and SpiceJet the laptop bag shares the same 7 kg. Check your airline on our [baggage rules pages](../baggage/index.html).
