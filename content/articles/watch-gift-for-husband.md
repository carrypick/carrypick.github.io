## How to choose a watch for your husband

1. **Look at the watch he wears now.** Its dial size and strap tell you what he's comfortable with. If he has none, measure his wrist with a string and use our [watch size tool](../tools/watch-size.html). Most men suit **38–42 mm**.
2. **Choose the strap for his day.** Steel for office and rain, leather for a classic look, silicone for gym and travel.
3. **Match his style, not yours.** A slim dress watch for formals, a bold or diver-style watch if he likes sporty things.
4. **Check the water resistance.** 30 m and 50 m are splash-proof only. For swimming, 100 m or more, like the Invicta's 200 m.
5. **Keep the warranty.** Titan lists 2 years on our picks; keep the invoice in the box.

## Questions

### Is a watch a good gift for a husband?

Yes. He'll wear it every day, it lasts for years, and you can choose one that suits his office or casual style. Some families avoid gifting watches for traditional reasons, so if his family does, a wallet or a perfume set is a safe alternative.

### Which watch brand is good for a husband's gift in India?

Titan is the classic choice for office and occasions. Timex and Casio suit smart casual wear, Fastrack is young and sporty, and Invicta makes diver-style watches.

### What size watch should I buy for my husband?

Most men suit a 38–42 mm dial. If his wrist is slim, choose 38–40 mm; if it's wide, 42–45 mm. Our watch size tool helps.

### What should I write with a watch gift for my husband?

Something about time together, like "Every second with you is my favourite" or "From today, all my time is ours". See our list above.

### Can I give a watch to my husband on our wedding day?

Yes. A classic Titan in steel or leather goes with both wedding outfits and office wear afterwards. A couple watch set is another nice wedding-day idea.
