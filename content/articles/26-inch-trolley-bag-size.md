A 26 inch trolley bag is **66 cm** tall. That's the most common **medium** size in India: Safari, for example, sells its medium bags at 66 cm. It's a check-in bag for about a week, and it's well within the 158 cm limit of Indian airlines.

## Quick answer

- **26 inches is 66 cm.** It's a medium check-in bag, not cabin and not large.
- **It suits a trip of 5–8 days.**
- **Every 66–70 cm bag we read is within 158 cm,** at 136–143.5 cm in total, so it's fine on IndiGo, Air India, Akasa Air and SpiceJet.
- **Most weigh 3.2–3.5 kg empty.** On a 15 kg fare, that leaves about 11.5 kg for your things.

## Is a 26 inch trolley medium or large?

Medium. In India, trolleys come in three main sizes:

| Size | Height | In inches | Trip length |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cabin (small) | About 55 cm | About 22 | 2–4 days |
| Medium | 65–69 cm | About 26 | 5–8 days |
| Large | 75–80 cm | About 30 | 8–12 days, families |

A 26 inch bag is too tall for the cabin: Indian airlines allow about 55 cm. For the cabin, see our [22 inch trolley guide](22-inch-trolley-bag-size.html).

## How many kg can a 26 inch trolley hold?

Usually more than your ticket allows. With 68–78 litres, it could hold roughly **10–23 kg of clothes**, by our rough estimate from the listed litres. The real limit is your fare, and the bag's own weight counts:

| Your allowance | 2.9 kg bag | 3.3 kg bag | 4.5 kg bag |
|---|---|---|---|
| 15 kg | 12.1 kg | 11.7 kg | 10.5 kg |
| 20 kg | 17.1 kg | 16.7 kg | 15.5 kg |
| 23 kg | 20.1 kg | 19.7 kg | 18.5 kg |

On a 15 kg domestic fare, a 26 inch bag is about right. For one 23 kg bag abroad, it can fill up before 23 kg with light clothes: see [which trolley size fits a 23 kg bag](trolley-bag-size-for-international-travel.html). Weigh the packed bag at home with a [luggage scale](../picks/B07Q822CWX.html).

## On a train

Indian Railways allows suitcases up to 100 x 60 x 25 cm in most classes, and only 55 x 45 x 22.5 cm in AC 3-tier and AC Chair Car. The 66 cm bags we read are 26–32 cm deep, so they're over these limits on paper. See our [train luggage rules](../baggage/train-luggage-rules.html).

## Questions

### What is a 26 inch trolley bag in cm?

26 inches is 66 cm. Indian brands usually list this size as "66 cm" or "medium".

### Is a 26 inch trolley bag medium or large?

Medium. Large trolleys in India are 75–80 cm tall, about 30 inches.

### How many kg can a 26 inch trolley carry?

It can hold roughly 10–23 kg of clothes, but your ticket sets the limit, and the bag's own weight counts. On a 15 kg fare, a 3.3 kg bag leaves 11.7 kg for your things.

### How many litres is a 26 inch trolley?

The 66–70 cm bags we read list 68–78 litres. Similar bags can list quite different figures, so compare the size in cm too.

### Is a 26 inch trolley allowed in flight?

Yes, as a checked bag. The 66–70 cm bags we read are 136–143.5 cm in total, within the 158 cm limit of Indian airlines. It's too big for the cabin.

### Is a 26 inch suitcase big enough for 2 weeks?

For one person, often yes, if you can wash clothes on the way. With winter clothes, or packing for two, a [large trolley](28-inch-trolley-bag-size.html) is easier.
