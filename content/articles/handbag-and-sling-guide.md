**The short answer:** for sightseeing and crowded markets, take a crossbody sling with a zip and wear it in front. For a flight day or an office trip, take a zipped tote that fits under the seat. A satchel is for smart days, when you carry little. On a flight, IndiGo, Air India and Akasa Air let you carry this bag on top of your 7 kg cabin bag if it weighs 3 kg or less. SpiceJet and Air India Express count it inside the 7 kg.

## Which bag for which trip

| Trip or day | Best bag | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Flight day | A zipped tote or a small sling | It fits under the seat, and holds your ID, phone, medicines and liquids bag |
| City sightseeing, markets, metro | A crossbody sling with a zip | Both hands stay free, and you can keep it in front of you in a crowd |
| Office or a work trip | A tote with a padded laptop sleeve | Holds a laptop, papers and a bottle in one bag |
| Beach | A light nylon sling or tote | Nylon shrugs off sand and splashes better than leather |
| Weddings and evenings | A small satchel or sling | Smart, but holds only your phone, cards and keys |
| Hill walks and day treks | A small backpack, not a handbag | Weight on one shoulder gets tiring on slopes. See our [laptop backpack guide](laptop-backpack-guide.html) |

## Sling, tote or satchel?

| | Crossbody sling | Tote | Satchel |
|---|---|---|---|
| How you carry it | Across your body, in front or at your side | On one shoulder, or in your hand | On one shoulder, or by its handle |
| Holds | Phone, wallet, passport, a power bank | A laptop or tablet, a bottle, a shawl, papers | Phone, cards, keys, a small diary |
| Safe in a crowd | Best, if it has a zip and you wear it in front | Only if it has a zip | Often a flap or a clasp, so less safe |
| Watch out for | Rarely fits a tablet | Heavy on one shoulder by evening | Small inside |

A sling is one kind of crossbody bag: it usually sits higher, across your chest or back. Any crossbody bag with a zip is a good travel bag. More slings, with locks and what fits inside: see [sling bag for travel](sling-bag-for-travel.html).

**Three things to check on any listing:**

1. **How it closes.** A zip is safer than a magnet or an open top. Two of our picks close with a magnet.
2. **Its weight when empty.** Our picks are listed from 140 g to 800 g. On a 3 kg personal bag allowance, an 800 g bag leaves you 2.2 kg for what's inside.
3. **The laptop size,** if you carry one. Look for a padded sleeve and the size it takes.

## What to keep in your handbag on a trip

- **ID and travel papers:** your ID or passport, boarding pass, and visa or hotel papers on international trips.
- **Phone, wallet and keys,** in a zipped inside pocket.
- **A power bank and a cable.** Power banks must go in your cabin bags, never in check-in. See our [power bank rules](../baggage/can-i-carry/power-bank.html).
- **Medicines you need on the way,** with the prescription if you have one.
- **Your clear 1-litre liquids bag,** if it doesn't fit in your cabin bag: every bottle 100 ml or less. See [the 100 ml rule](../baggage/can-i-carry/liquids.html).
- **A shawl or scarf** for a cold cabin or a temple visit, a pen for arrival forms, and earphones.

## Questions

### Is a handbag included in cabin baggage?

On IndiGo, Air India and Akasa Air, no: you can carry one handbag of up to 3 kg on top of your 7 kg cabin bag. On SpiceJet and Air India Express, yes: it counts inside the same 7 kg.

### Is a ladies' purse allowed in a flight with a cabin bag?

Yes, on every Indian airline. The difference is the weight: IndiGo, Air India and Akasa Air give it its own 3 kg, while SpiceJet and Air India Express count it in your 7 kg.

### Can I carry a sling bag in a flight?

Yes. A sling bag counts as your personal item, like a handbag. Keep it small enough to fit under the seat in front of you. Air India's limit is 40 x 30 x 20 cm and 3 kg.

### Sling bag or tote bag: which is better for travel?

A sling for sightseeing, because it sits in front of you and leaves your hands free. A tote for flights and work, because it holds a laptop, a bottle and a shawl. Many travellers carry a tote on the flight and a small sling for the days out.

### Sling bag or crossbody bag for travel?

They are nearly the same thing: a sling is a crossbody bag worn higher, across the chest or back. For travel, what matters more is a zip closure and wearing the bag in front of you.

### What should I put in my handbag when travelling?

Your ID or passport, boarding pass, phone, wallet, keys, a power bank, medicines you need on the way, your 1-litre liquids bag if it won't fit in your cabin bag, and a shawl for the cold cabin.
