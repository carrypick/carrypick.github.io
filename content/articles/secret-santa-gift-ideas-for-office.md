## Simple rules for an office Secret Santa

- **Stick to the limit your group agreed.** A gift that costs much more than everyone else's can make people uncomfortable.
- **Choose something for work or travel.** Bottles, wallets, organisers and travel kits suit almost anyone.
- **Skip personal gifts.** Perfume, clothes and skincare depend on taste and size. Keep them for people you know well.
- **Think about their day.** Do they carry a laptop, travel for work or take the train? A gift for a real habit gets used.
- **Check the delivery date before you pay.** Christmas week is busy, so order by mid-December.
- **Wrap it and add their name, not yours.** It's a secret! Some sellers offer gift wrap at checkout.

## Questions

### What is a good Secret Santa gift for an office colleague?

Something they will use at work or on a trip, like a steel bottle, a gadget organiser, a wallet or a toiletry bag. These suit almost anyone, so they're safe when you don't know the person well.

### What is a good Secret Santa gift for a male colleague?

A leather wallet set, a small leather sling or a simple watch. The WildHorn set above comes in a gift box with a wallet, a keychain and a pen.

### What is a good Secret Santa gift for a female colleague?

A zip-around wallet, a light crossbody bag or a steel bottle for her desk. Skip perfume and skincare unless you know what she likes.

### What should I not give in an office Secret Santa?

Avoid jokes that could upset someone, clothes in a size you have to guess, and perfume or skincare. Also avoid anything that costs much more than the limit your group agreed.

### When should I order a Secret Santa gift?

Order by mid-December, and check the delivery date shown on Amazon before you pay. Deliveries can take longer in Christmas week.
