Most packing lists are written for everyone. This one is for women who travel, alone, with friends or with family, in India or abroad: the small things that make a trip easier, the bag to keep them in, and what to keep within reach on a flight or a train. Tick it off, then pack your clothes with our [packing list maker](../tools/packing-list-maker.html).

## Quick answer

- **One day bag that zips:** a crossbody sling for sightseeing, or a tote for flights and work trips.
- **A toiletry bag that hangs up,** with your liquids in 100 ml bottles if you're flying with a cabin bag only.
- **Packing cubes and a jewellery roll,** so nothing tangles or gets lost at the bottom of the suitcase.
- **A power bank and the right plug adapter,** both in your cabin bag.
- **Sunscreen, lip balm with SPF and sunglasses,** whatever the season.
- **A passport holder** (or a zip wallet for India trips) and a door stop alarm for hotel rooms, if it gives you peace of mind.

## Toiletries and a hanging toiletry bag

Hotel and homestay bathrooms often have no shelf space. A toiletry bag with a hook hangs on the door or towel rail and opens like a small cupboard.

- **Flying with a cabin bag only?** Each liquid, gel or cream must be 100 ml or less, and they all go in one clear, resealable bag of about 1 litre. That includes sunscreen, moisturiser, liquid foundation, perfume and contact lens solution.
- **Solids skip the liquids bag:** a solid shampoo bar, a stick deodorant and powder make-up save space.
- **Decant into small bottles,** and wrap each lid with tape so it doesn't leak in the hold.

## A door stop alarm for hotel rooms

Some women like extra peace of mind in a hotel room or homestay at night, alongside the room's own lock and chain. A door stop alarm is a rubber or plastic wedge that slides under the door: it helps hold the door shut, and its siren sounds if the door is pushed.

- **Check your door first.** Most door stop alarms only work on doors that **open into the room**, and need a small gap under the door.
- **Carry a spare battery.** This one runs on a 9V battery that isn't included.
- **It's an extra, not a guarantee.** It doesn't replace the room lock, choosing a well-reviewed stay, or calling for help. In India, the emergency number is **112**.

## On a plane: what to keep in your handbag

- Passport or ID, boarding pass, phone and a pen for any arrival form
- Power bank, charging cable and earphones
- Your medicines, and period care for the flight
- A scarf or light cardigan: cabins get cold
- Lip balm, a small moisturiser and hand cream, inside your 100 ml liquids bag
- An empty water bottle to fill after security
- A neck pillow for a night flight: see [neck pillows for flights](neck-pillow-for-flights.html)

On most Indian airlines, your handbag can go on board alongside your cabin bag, but on some fares it counts inside your 7 kg. Check your airline on our [baggage rules page](../baggage/index.html).

## On a train: what to keep handy

- Your ticket on your phone, and the ID you booked with
- A sling or small bag that stays with you on the berth
- A chain lock for your suitcase, and a water bottle
- A shawl or light blanket for AC coaches, and slippers for the night
- Wet wipes, tissues and hand sanitiser
- A power bank: not every berth has a working charging point

Indian Railways keeps a quota of berths for women in long-distance trains. See how it works, with the railway helpline, in our [solo female travel in India guide](solo-female-travel-india.html#on-trains), and the [train luggage rules](../baggage/train-luggage-rules.html).

## Travelling abroad

- **Read your destination's dress norms** for religious sites, and pack a scarf that covers your hair and shoulders.
- **Save the local emergency number** on your phone before you land, and your country's embassy or consulate.
- **Pack the right plug adapter,** and check the voltage of hair dryers and straighteners.
- **Carry your medicines in their original packs,** with a copy of the prescription.
- **Coming to India?** Start with our [visiting India guide](../trips/visiting-india.html) and [what to pack for India](what-to-pack-for-india.html), which covers what to wear as a woman.

## Questions

### What are the travel essentials for women?

A zip day bag, a toiletry bag, sunscreen and lip balm with SPF, period care, your medicines, a power bank, a plug adapter for trips abroad, sunglasses, packing cubes, a scarf or stole, and your passport or ID in a holder you always keep closed.

### What toiletries should a woman pack for travel?

Sunscreen, lip balm, moisturiser, face wash, deodorant, shampoo and conditioner, a hairbrush and hair ties, period care, and any make-up you use daily. For a cabin bag, keep each liquid to 100 ml or less in one clear 1-litre bag, or switch to solid bars and sticks.

### What should I carry in my handbag on a plane?

Your passport or ID, boarding pass, phone, power bank and cable, earphones, medicines, period care, a scarf, lip balm, and an empty water bottle. Power banks must stay in the cabin, never in checked bags.

### What should a woman carry on a train journey in India?

Your ticket and ID, a small bag that stays with you, a chain lock for your suitcase, a shawl for the AC coach, wet wipes, a water bottle and a power bank. Save the railway helpline, 139, on your phone.

### Does a door stop alarm work on hotel doors?

Most work only on doors that open into the room and need a small gap under the door. The one we picked lists a gap of 0.6 to 1.2 inches. It's an extra layer, not a guarantee, so keep using the room's lock and chain.

### What is a good travel kit gift for a woman?

A hanging toiletry bag, a jewellery roll, a set of packing cubes, a travel journal or a passport holder. See more in [gifts for travellers](gifts-for-travellers.html) and [Christmas gifts for her](christmas-gift-for-girlfriend-or-wife.html).
