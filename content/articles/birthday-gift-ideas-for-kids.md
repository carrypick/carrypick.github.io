## How to choose a birthday gift for a child

- **Go by age first.** Every pick below gives the age range from its Amazon.in listing. Toys and art kits with small parts aren't safe for babies and toddlers, so follow the age on the box.
- **Ask the parents what they already have.** Many families get the same board books or art kits twice. A quick message saves a duplicate.
- **Think about what they love.** A child who draws all day will enjoy a big art kit; a child who likes rules and winning will love a board game.
- **Useful is good too.** A watch, a school bag or a steel bottle gets used every day. Kids of 8 and up often like a "grown-up" gift.
- **Books last longest.** A story book read at bedtime gets read again and again, long after toys are forgotten.
- **Keep the invoice.** If something arrives broken, you'll need it for a return.

## Questions

### What is a good birthday gift for a 1 year old?

A baby toy set with stacking rings, a teether and a rattle, or a box of thick board books. Check that the age on the listing includes your child's age.

### What is a good birthday gift for a 5 year old boy?

A drawing kit like Faber-Castell's Doodle and Draw Kit, a big story book like 101 Panchatantra tales, or a box of sketch pens. Most of these suit a 5 year old girl just as well.

### What should I gift a girl on her birthday?

By age: board books at 1, story books at 2 to 5, an art kit or a Zoop watch with a pink strap at 5 to 9, and a board game like Sequence or a world fact book at 10 to 12. Let her choose the colour if you can.

### What is a good birthday gift for a 10 year old?

A board game the whole family can play, like Sequence (ages 7 and up), a world fact book, a simple watch or a big school bag.

### What is a useful birthday gift for kids?

A watch, a school bag or a steel bottle for school. They get used every day, and parents like them too.

### What can I gift a child who has everything?

Something for the next family trip, like their own kids' cabin trolley or a travel neck pillow, or a book they haven't read.

### What return gifts should I give at a kids' birthday party?

The same small gift for every child, like an art kit or sketch pens. See our [return gift ideas](return-gift-ideas.html).
