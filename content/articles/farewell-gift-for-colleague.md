## How to choose a farewell gift for a colleague

- **Think about what comes next.** A work bag suits a new job. Travel gear suits a move or a long break. A watch suits almost anyone.
- **Give one gift from the whole team.** Everyone puts in a little, so the gift can be better than one person could give.
- **Add a card signed by everyone.** Write a real memory, not only "All the best". People keep these cards for years.
- **Keep it useful, not too personal.** Wallets, watches, bags and bottles are safe. Skip perfume and clothes unless you know their taste and size.
- **Moving abroad? Keep it small and light.** Their suitcase will already be full, so a passport holder or a luggage scale is better than something big.
- **Check the delivery date before you pay.** The last day is fixed, so order a few days early.

## Questions

### What is a good farewell gift for a colleague?

Something useful for their next step: a wallet or a watch for anyone, a work bag for a new job, or a passport holder if they're moving abroad. A gift from the whole team with a signed card works best.

### What is a good farewell gift for a male colleague?

A leather wallet set, a small leather sling or a classic watch like the Timex Classics. The WildHorn set above comes in a gift box with a wallet, a keychain and a pen.

### What is a good farewell gift for a female colleague?

A zip-around wallet, an elegant watch like the Titan Karishma, or a work tote that holds a laptop. Skip perfume and clothes unless you know her taste.

### What is a good farewell gift for a boss or manager?

A watch from a known brand, like the Titan Classique, or a smart laptop backpack. Give it from the whole team, and check your company's gift rules first.

### What is a good retirement gift for a colleague?

A classic watch, a weekender bag for the trips they now have time for, or a couple watch set if they're retiring with a husband or wife.

### What should I give a colleague who is moving abroad?

Small, light things they can pack easily: a passport holder, a luggage scale or packing cubes. For a bigger team gift, choose a large trolley within their airline's size limit.
