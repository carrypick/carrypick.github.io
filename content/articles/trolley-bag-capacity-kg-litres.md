Trolley listings show litres, but almost none show a kg limit. So how many kg does a bag really hold? In most cases, your airline ticket decides that, not the bag.

## Quick answer

| Size | Often sold as | Listed litres (typical) | Clothes it could hold (rough) | Empty bag (typical) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cabin, about 55 cm | 20–22 inch | 39 L | 6–12 kg | 2.5 kg |
| Medium, 65–69 cm | 24–26 inch | 69 L | 10–21 kg | 3.4 kg |
| Large, 75–80 cm | 28–30 inch | 108 L | 16–32 kg | 4.3 kg |

The kg column is **our rough estimate, not a test**. We used about 0.15–0.3 kg per litre for packed clothes. Shoes, books and food weigh more for the space they take.

- **Cabin bag:** it could hold more than most airlines allow. Most Indian airlines allow 7 kg, including the bag.
- **Medium bag:** this is the best match for a 15 kg fare.
- **Large bag:** it can hold more than a 15 kg fare allows, and it weighs more when empty.

Need the height in inches and cm? See our [trolley bag size chart](../baggage/luggage-size-chart.html).

## Your ticket usually sets the limit

Take the empty bag's weight away from your allowance. That's what you can really pack:

| Your allowance | Cabin bag (2.5 kg) | Medium bag (3.4 kg) | Large bag (4.3 kg) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 7 kg in the cabin | 4.5 kg | Too big | Too big |
| 15 kg check-in | – | 11.6 kg | 10.7 kg |
| 20 kg check-in | – | 16.6 kg | 15.7 kg |
| 25 kg check-in | – | 21.6 kg | 20.7 kg |

So on a 15 kg fare, a large bag lets you pack about 1 kg less than a medium one. Check your ticket, because allowances vary by airline and fare. See our [baggage rules for Indian airlines](../baggage/index.html).

## Don't trust the litres too much

Listed litres are the least reliable number on a listing:

- **Same bag, two numbers.** Two listings for the Skybags Rubik large, both 78 x 47 x 37 cm, showed 86 L and 132 L.
- **Same size, different litres.** Safari's Thorium Neo and Thorium Royale are both listed at 66 x 46 x 27 cm. One shows 60 L and the other 74 L.
- **A simple check.** Multiply length x width x height in cm, then divide by 1,000. A 66 x 46 x 27 cm bag comes to 82 L from the outside. Most listed litres we read were about 80% of that outside figure.

If a bag's litres look far above its outside figure, don't choose it for that number.

## Work out your own bag

1. Take the listed litres, or the outside size x 0.8.
2. Multiply by 0.15 and by 0.3. That's your rough range in kg for clothes.
3. Compare it with your allowance, after taking off the bag's own weight.
4. Add up your real things with our [bag weight calculator](../tools/bag-weight.html).
5. Weigh the packed bag at home with a [luggage scale](../picks/B07Q822CWX.html).

Example: a 70 L bag gives about 10.5–21 kg. On a 15 kg fare, it's more than big enough.

## Which size for which fare?

- **7 kg cabin bag only:** a [cabin bag](cabin-luggage-guide.html) that weighs close to 2 kg.
- **15 kg check-in:** a light medium bag. Don't pay for extra space you can't use.
- **20–25 kg check-in:** medium for clothes, large for bulky winter things.
- **Large bags:** most Indian airlines limit a checked bag to 158 cm in total, and [most large bags we read](28-inch-trolley-bag-size.html) went over that. Check the full size first.

## Questions

### How many kg can a 55 cm trolley bag hold?

About 6–12 kg of clothes, by our rough estimate. But most Indian airlines allow only 7 kg in the cabin, including the bag. A typical 2.5 kg cabin trolley leaves about 4.5 kg for your things.

### How many kg can a 65 cm trolley bag hold?

About 10–21 kg of clothes, by our rough estimate for a typical 69 L bag. On a 15 kg fare, a typical 3.4 kg medium bag leaves about 11.6 kg.

### How many kg can a 75 cm trolley bag hold?

About 16–32 kg of clothes, by our rough estimate for a typical 108 L bag. Your fare is usually the real limit. On a 25 kg fare, a typical 4.3 kg large bag leaves about 20.7 kg.

### How many kg is a 28 inch trolley bag?

Empty, the large bags we read weighed 3.48–5.25 kg, and 4.3 kg was typical. Packed, it depends on your fare. Most standard domestic fares allow 15 kg, and that includes the bag.

### How many litres is a 24 inch trolley bag?

Medium bags sold in India listed 45–93 L, and 69 L was typical. A true 24 inch bag is 61 cm, so it may hold less than a 65–69 cm bag.

### Is a 75 cm trolley bag allowed on a flight?

Yes, as a check-in bag, if it's within your airline's limits. It's too big for the cabin. Most Indian airlines limit checked bags to 158 cm in total, and many 75–80 cm bags go over that.
