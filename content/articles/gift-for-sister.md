## How to choose a gift for your sister

- **Think about her normal day.** College, office, home or always on the move? A gift that fits that day gets used, and she remembers who gave it.
- **Look at what she carries now.** A bag with a broken zip, a watch with a scratched glass or a wallet that's falling apart tells you the gift.
- **Pick the colour with care.** One Amazon page can show several colours. Check that the one you click is the one she'd wear.
- **Skip clothes unless you know her size.** A watch, a bag, sunglasses or a wallet always fits.
- **Perfume is easier as a set.** Four small bottles let her find a favourite, so you don't have to guess one scent.
- **For Rakhi or Bhai Dooj, order early.** Raksha Bandhan 2027 is on Tuesday 17 August and Bhai Dooj on Sunday 31 October (Government of India holiday list). Deliveries slow down in festival weeks, so check the date before you pay.

## What to write in the card

- **Any day:** "Thank you for always picking up when I call. You're the best sister anyone could ask for."
- **Rakhi:** "You tie the rakhi, but you're the one who protects me. Happy Raksha Bandhan, didi."
- **Younger sister:** "Still my favourite little troublemaker. So proud of who you're becoming."
- **Sister-in-law:** "Thank you for making our family bigger and happier. So glad you're one of us."

Add one memory, like a trip or a childhood joke. That's the line she'll read twice.

## Questions

### What is the best gift for a sister?

Something she'll use every week: a Fastrack or Titan watch, a light sling bag, sunglasses or a perfume gift set with four small bottles. A zip-around wallet is a good smaller gift or an add-on.

### What should I give my sister on Rakhi?

A watch, a sling bag or a perfume set make a lasting Rakhi gift. For a small gift with the thali, an SPF lip balm or sunglasses. Raksha Bandhan 2027 is on Tuesday 17 August, so order a few days before.

### What is a good gift for a sister in college?

A light crossbody bag, a Fastrack watch with a pink dial, cat-eye sunglasses or packing cubes for hostel trips home.

### What can I gift my sister-in-law?

A known-brand gift that isn't too personal: a zip-around wallet, a perfume gift set, a tote handbag or a Titan watch. Skip clothes unless you know her size.

### What can I gift my sister on her birthday?

Any of the ideas above work. See our [birthday gift ideas](birthday-gift-ideas.html#for-your-sister) for more, with lines for the birthday card.
