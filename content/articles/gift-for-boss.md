## How to choose a gift for your boss

- **Give it from the whole team.** A group gift can be bigger, and nobody looks like they want a favour.
- **Check your company's gift rules.** Some companies limit gifts to managers. HR can tell you.
- **Keep it professional.** Watches, bags and travel gear are safe. Skip perfume, clothes and jokes.
- **Choose a known brand.** Titan, Timex and American Tourister have service centres across India, so warranty claims are easier.
- **Add a card signed by everyone.** It often means more than the gift.
- **Order early.** Deliveries can take longer around Christmas, so check the delivery date before you pay.

## Questions

### What is a good Christmas gift for a boss?

A watch from a known brand, a smart work bag or a travel item they will use on business trips. If you're a team, put money together for one better gift and a card signed by everyone.

### What is a good gift for a male boss?

A Titan or Timex watch, a leather wallet set or a laptop backpack. If he flies for work, a cabin trolley with a laptop section is a good team gift.

### What is a good gift for a female boss?

An elegant watch like the Titan Karishma, a work tote that holds a laptop, or a neat everyday handbag. A zip-around wallet is a good smaller gift.

### What is a good farewell gift for a boss?

Something for their next step: a watch for a new job, a passport holder if they're moving abroad, or a weekender bag if they're taking a break. More ideas are in our [farewell gift guide](farewell-gift-for-colleague.html).

### Is it OK to give a gift to your boss?

Usually, yes, if it's modest and from the team. Some companies have rules about gifts to managers, so check with HR first.
