## EDP, EDT, deo or attar: what's the difference?

Perfumes are perfume oils mixed with alcohol. The name on the bottle says roughly how much oil is inside. More oil means a stronger smell that lasts longer.

| On the box | Perfume oil (roughly) | How strong |
|---|---|---|
| Parfum or extrait | 15 to 30% | Strongest, a dab is enough |
| Eau de parfum (EDP) | 15 to 20% | Strong |
| Eau de toilette (EDT) | 5 to 15% | Medium, fresher |
| Eau de cologne (EDC) | 2 to 3% | Light |
| Body mist, deo, "no gas" body perfume | Usually light | Light, for a quick freshen-up |
| Attar (itr) | Pure oil, no alcohol | Strong close to the skin |

These ranges come from the usual industry terms, but brands differ. One brand's EDT can be stronger than another brand's EDP, so treat the label as a guide, not a promise.

**Deo vs perfume:** a deodorant is made to fight body odour and smells lighter. A perfume is made for the scent. Many Indian "body perfumes" and "no gas deos" sit in between.

**Attar vs perfume:** attar is a perfume oil without alcohol, sold in small roll-on or dab bottles. It sits close to the skin and doesn't spread far, so it suits people who don't like strong sprays, and those who avoid alcohol for religious reasons. Oud and rose attars are popular gifts for Eid and weddings.

## How long does each one last on skin?

There's no fixed answer: it depends on the brand, your skin, the weather and how much you spray. As a rough guide:

- **EDP:** often most of a working day. A good choice for gifts and for evenings out.
- **EDT:** a few hours. Nice for daytime, the office and hot weather.
- **Cologne, body mist and deo:** an hour or two. Spray again when you need to.
- **Attar:** often lasts long close to the skin, but other people smell it less.
- **Pocket perfume:** made for top-ups, not for all day.

Want it to last longer? Spray on your wrists, neck and behind the ears right after a bath, when your skin is clean and a little moist. Don't rub your wrists together. A spray on your clothes can last longer than on skin, but test it first, because some perfumes stain light fabric.

## Travel sizes and the 100 ml cabin rule

Perfume counts as a liquid at airport security:

- **Each bottle must be 100 ml or less** to go in your cabin bag, and all your liquids go in **one clear 1-litre bag**. A 150 ml bottle that is half empty still isn't allowed.
- **IndiGo doesn't allow perfume or deo in the cabin at all.** Pack them in your check-in bag.
- **In check-in,** IndiGo, Air India and SpiceJet allow up to 2 litres or 2 kg of toiletries in total, with no single bottle over 500 ml.

That's why **20 ml gift sets and pocket sprays are handy for trips**: they fit the clear bag on Air India, Akasa Air and most airlines abroad, and they don't break your suitcase if one leaks. ## Gift tips for men and women

- **Don't know their taste? Give a gift set.** Four small bottles let them try four scents and find a favourite. They also travel well.
- **For men:** woody, oud, citrus and "aqua" or marine scents are the safe picks. For the office, choose something fresh; for evenings, woody or oud.
- **For women:** floral and fruity scents are the most popular; rose, vanilla and caramel feel warmer and suit evenings and winter.
- **For a couple or an office gift:** a mixed box with scents for him and her.
- **Already has a favourite?** Buy a full bottle of that instead. Perfume is personal, and a new scent can be a miss.
- **Add a small extra** like a [wallet](wallet-and-belt-buying-guide.html) or a [watch](first-analog-watch-guide.html) if it's a big occasion.

## Questions

### Which is better, EDP or EDT?

EDP has more perfume oil, so it usually smells stronger and lasts longer. EDT is lighter and fresher, which many people like for daytime and hot weather. For a gift, EDP is the safer choice.

### Attar or perfume: which lasts longer?

Attar is pure oil without alcohol, so it often lasts long close to the skin, but it doesn't spread as far. A spray perfume (EDP) is easier for other people to notice. It depends on what the person likes.

### Can I carry perfume in my cabin bag on a flight?

On Air India, Akasa Air and most airlines abroad, yes, in bottles of 100 ml or less inside a clear 1-litre bag. On IndiGo, no: perfume and deo go in check-in only. See [can I carry perfume?](../baggage/can-i-carry/perfume.html)

### What is a good perfume gift for my husband or boyfriend?

A gift set of four small EDPs, so he can try a few scents. Woody, oud and citrus are safe choices for men. If he already wears one perfume he loves, buy a bottle of that.

### What is a good perfume gift for my wife or girlfriend?

A floral or fruity EDP gift set is a safe start. If she likes warm scents, look for rose, vanilla or caramel. Notice what she already wears and pick something close to it.

### Is deo the same as perfume?

No. A deo is made to stop body odour and smells lighter for a shorter time. A perfume is made for the scent and lasts longer. Many people use both: deo for the body, perfume for the neck and wrists.
