## The short list

If you pack only these, you'll be fine:

1. Your passport with your visa, and the port of entry letter of introduction from IRCC
2. A copy of your letter of acceptance from your school, proof of money, and any letters the visa office told you to bring, in your hand bag
3. Your medicines in their original packs, with the prescription
4. Thermals, sweaters, a warm jacket, a cap, gloves and boots for snow
5. A laptop and its charger, a travel adapter for Canada's flat-pin sockets, and a power bank in your cabin bag
6. A card that works abroad, and some Canadian dollars for the first days
7. A check-in trolley within 158 cm and 23 kg, and a luggage scale

## Documents to have at the border

Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada's (IRCC) page on preparing to arrive as a student (page dated 28 Aug 2026, read 23 Nov 2026) says you must:

- **Have a valid passport** (or other travel document) and your visa
- **Have the port of entry letter of introduction** the visa office sent you. It has the reference number used to issue your study permit at the airport.
- **Have a copy of a valid letter of acceptance** from your school
- **Have letters of reference or any other documents** the visa office told you to bring
- **Have enough money** for your stay. The amount varies.
- **Have your immigration medical exam results,** if you needed one

Keep all of these in your hand bag, not in your check-in trolley, so they're with you when you meet the border officer. If you need proof of entry, IRCC says to make sure the officer stamps your passport.

## Baggage allowance for students

Students on these airlines can get more checked baggage than a normal ticket, as each airline's own page says (read 23 Nov 2026):

- **Air India student fare:** on routes where bags are counted by piece, such as Canada, Economy and Premium Economy get **2 bags of 23 kg each**. Choose "Student" as the concession type when you book. International student fares are for full-time students aged 12 to 30, and you must show a student ID, student visa or acceptance letter at the airport if asked.
- **Virgin Atlantic student offer:** from Delhi, Mumbai or Bengaluru to Canada (and to London and the USA), Economy Classic and Delight and Premium get **3 bags of 23 kg**, 69 kg in all. It isn't valid on Economy Light, it's for the outbound flight only, and you show your student visa at check-in. Virgin says to book it through its India contact centre.

On a normal fare, check what your ticket includes. From our airline data: Air India gives 1 bag of 23 kg on Value and 2 on Classic and Flex to Canada, and Lufthansa and British Airways 1 bag of 23 kg (none on their cheapest fares). Emirates and Qatar Airways depend on your fare. See our [international baggage rules](../baggage/international.html) and, if you'll pay for extra bags, the [excess baggage calculator](../tools/bag-weight.html).

- **Each bag can usually be up to 158 cm in total** (length + width + height). Air India's page also limits both Economy bags together to 271 cm. See [trolley bag size for international travel](trolley-bag-size-for-international-travel.html).
- **Cabin bag:** Air India allows 7 kg and 55 x 40 x 20 cm in Economy. Check yours with the [cabin bag checker](../baggage/checker.html).

## Which bags to take

- **Large bag:** one of 75–79 cm that's listed within 158 cm. Winter clothes are bulky, so use the full size.
- **Second bag:** a medium bag of 65–69 cm. On Air India, keep both bags together within 271 cm, so pair it with a large bag of about 152 cm or less.
- **Buying a set?** Check the large bag's listed total in our [trolley bag set of 3 guide](trolley-bag-set-of-3.html), and read the [28 inch trolley guide](28-inch-trolley-bag-size.html).
- **Hard or soft?** Hard shells protect better; soft bags can squeeze in a jacket. See [hard vs soft luggage](hard-vs-soft-luggage.html).

## What to wear in Canada

A Canadian winter is much colder than anything in India. In January, Toronto averages about −1 °C by day and −7 °C at night, with snow, Vancouver about 6 °C and 1 °C and wet, and Banff about −5 °C and −14 °C (Meteorological Service of Canada averages on the WMO site, as in our [Canada trip guide](../trips/canada-trip-from-india.html)). Cities like Winnipeg and Montreal can be colder.

- **Arriving in December or January?** Wear thermals, a sweater and your warmest jacket on the plane, and have a cap and gloves in your hand bag for the walk out of the airport.
- **A jacket from India is not enough for deep winter** in most of Canada. Many students buy a proper winter coat and boots after they arrive, once they know their city. Pack enough to stay warm for the first week.
- **Layers:** thermals, two sweaters or fleeces, and warm trousers. Buildings and buses are heated, so you'll take layers off indoors.
- **Boots with a good grip** for snow and ice, and plenty of woollen socks.
- **Vancouver** is wet more than snowy, so take a waterproof jacket.

## Indian food: what to declare

The Canada Border Services Agency (CBSA) says **"You must declare all food, plants, animal and related products"** that you bring into Canada (page modified 25 Nov 2025, read 23 Nov 2026). Not declaring can get your goods taken.

- **The rules for each food** are in the Canadian Food Inspection Agency's Automated Import Reference System (AIRS), and CBSA says they can change daily.
- **Some foods have quantity or value limits,** above which a high duty applies without a permit.
- **Declare everything** on your arrival declaration, then let the officer decide. Keep spices and snacks in sealed shop packs, together in one bag.

## Medicines

Health Canada's guidance on bringing health products for personal use (page dated 13 Jul 2026, read 23 Nov 2026) says:

- **Visitors, including people with a study permit, may bring a personal quantity of a prescription drug** for their own use.
- **No more than a 90-day supply,** or a single course of treatment, whichever is less.
- **Keep them in the original package,** or with the original label attached.
- **Keep them in your cabin bag** with the prescription. See [can I carry medicines on a flight?](../baggage/can-i-carry/medicines.html)

## Laptop, phone, plugs and power bank

- **Canada uses 120 V and flat two-pin or three-pin plugs,** different from India's. Take an adapter. See our [power bank and travel adapter guide](power-bank-and-travel-adapter-guide.html).
- **Check your chargers.** Laptop and phone chargers marked 100–240 V work with just an adapter. Indian hair dryers and straighteners are often 230 V only, so they won't work properly on 120 V.
- **Power bank:** in your cabin bag only, never in check-in. See [can I carry a power bank?](../baggage/can-i-carry/power-bank.html)
- **Cold drains batteries faster.** Keep your phone in an inside pocket.

## Small things students are glad they packed

- **Packing cubes,** so thermals, sweaters and jackets stay apart
- **Lip balm and a moisturiser:** cold air and heated rooms dry your skin
- **A steel water bottle** and a small lunch box
- **A toiletry bag** with a week of toiletries, in sizes of 100 ml or less for your cabin bag
- **Photos and a few things from home**

## What not to pack

- **Undeclared food.** Declare it all.
- **More than a 90-day supply of medicines**
- **A 32 inch bag** that's over 158 cm: see the [32 inch trolley guide](32-inch-trolley-bag-size.html)
- **Too many light cotton clothes.** You'll need warm layers more.
- **Power banks in check-in.**

## Money

- **CAN$10,000 or more? Declare it.** CBSA says there's no limit on how much money you can bring, but currency and monetary instruments of CAN$10,000 or more must be declared.
- **Cash from India:** see [how much cash you can carry abroad](../baggage/customs/cash-and-currency.html).

## Questions

### What should Indian students pack for Canada?

Your passport and visa, the port of entry letter of introduction, a copy of your letter of acceptance and proof of money in your hand bag, your medicines with the prescription, thermals, sweaters, a warm jacket, a cap, gloves and boots, a laptop with a flat-pin adapter, and a power bank in your cabin bag. Pack a large trolley within 158 cm and 23 kg.

### What documents should I carry to Canada as a student?

IRCC says to have a valid passport and visa, the port of entry letter of introduction, a copy of your letter of acceptance, any letters the visa office told you to bring, proof of enough money, and your medical exam results if you needed one.

### Can I bring Indian food to Canada?

You must declare all food, says CBSA. What's allowed is set by the Canadian Food Inspection Agency's AIRS, and some foods have limits. Declare everything and keep it in sealed packs.

### How many months of medicine can I bring to Canada?

Health Canada says no more than a 90-day supply or a single course of treatment, whichever is less, in the original package or with the original label.

### How much baggage can students carry to Canada?

Air India's student fare gives 2 bags of 23 kg in Economy on piece-based routes such as Canada, and Virgin Atlantic's student offer from India to Canada gives 3 bags of 23 kg, as their pages said on 23 Nov 2026.

### Should I buy winter clothes in India or Canada?

Pack enough from India to stay warm for the first week: thermals, sweaters, a jacket, a cap, gloves and boots. Many students buy a heavier winter coat in Canada, made for the local cold, after they arrive.

### Do Indian plugs work in Canada?

No. Canada uses 120 V and flat-pin plugs. Take a travel adapter, and check that your chargers say 100–240 V.
