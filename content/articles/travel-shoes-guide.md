**The short answer:** for most trips you need two pairs: one comfortable pair to walk in all day, and one light pair (sandals or flip-flops) for the hotel, the beach or wet days. Add a third pair only for snow or a trek. Wear the heaviest pair on the journey, and break new shoes in at home first.

## Which shoes for which trip

| Trip | Walk in | Second pair | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Snow, such as [Manali](../trips/manali.html) or [Gulmarg](../trips/gulmarg-in-december.html) | Waterproof shoes or boots with a grippy sole | Warm slippers or sandals for the hotel | Melting snow soaks ordinary shoes, and roads get icy |
| Forts and cities, such as [Jaipur](../trips/jaipur.html) | Cushioned walking shoes | Sandals | Long days, with slopes and steps |
| Europe or Japan, lots of walking | Walking shoes you've already worn in | Light sneakers or sandals | You may walk for many hours a day. See [walking shoes for a Europe trip](walking-shoes-for-europe-trip.html) |
| Beach, such as [Goa](../trips/goa.html) | Sandals | Flip-flops | Sand and water get into closed shoes |
| Monsoon, or rainy places such as [Kerala](../trips/kerala.html) | Sandals or shoes that can get wet | Flip-flops | They dry faster than closed shoes |
| Temples and pilgrimages | Slip-on shoes or sandals | Socks for hot or cold floors | You take shoes off often |
| Treks | Hiking shoes with ankle support | Sandals for camp | Rough, loose ground |
| Flights and trains | Slip-on shoes | — | Quick to take off on the berth or for a long flight |

## How many pairs to pack

This is our suggestion, not a rule. Pack for what you'll do, not for every outfit.

| Trip length | City or beach trip | Snow or trek trip |
|---|---|---|
| A weekend (2–3 days) | 1 pair worn, plus flip-flops | 1 pair of boots worn, plus hotel slippers |
| 1 week | 2 pairs: walking shoes and sandals | 2 pairs: boots and light shoes |
| 2–3 weeks | 2 pairs, or 3 if you need smart shoes for an evening | 3 pairs: boots, light shoes and sandals |

Shoes are heavy and take up room. Each extra pair can mean leaving clothes at home, or paying for extra kilos.

## Waterproof or water resistant?

These don't mean the same thing:

- **Waterproof** shoes are made to keep water out, even when you step in a puddle.
- **Water resistant** shoes handle light rain and splashes only.
- **Mesh shoes** let air in, and water too. Don't wear them in snow.

## Shoes and your bags

- **Wear your heaviest pair on the journey.** Most Indian airlines allow 7 kg in the cabin, and the shoes on your feet aren't in your bag. A pair of boots listed at 1.38 kg would use about a fifth of a 7 kg cabin bag.
- **Shoes are fine in check-in or cabin bags.** IndiGo's list of things you can't carry, which we read on 26 Nov 2026, doesn't name shoes. Pack them in a shoe bag or a [packing cube](packing-cubes.html) so they don't dirty your clothes.
- **Stuff socks inside your shoes** to save space and keep their shape.
- **Dry wet shoes before you pack them,** or keep them in a separate plastic bag.

## Buying tips

- **Most sizes in India are UK sizes.** Find yours in US, EU and cm with our [shoe size converter](../tools/shoe-size.html), then check the size chart on the Amazon page, because brands differ.
- **For snow, allow for thick socks.** Try shoes on with the socks you'll wear.
- **Never take new shoes on a trip without breaking them in.** Wear them at home for a few days first, and return them if they hurt.

## Questions

### How many shoes should I pack for a 1-week trip?

Two pairs for most trips: walking shoes you wear on the journey and a light pair of sandals or flip-flops. For snow, take boots and a pair of light shoes for the hotel.

### Can I carry shoes in check-in baggage?

Yes. Shoes aren't on IndiGo's list of things you can't carry. Put them in a shoe bag so they don't dirty your clothes, and dry them first if they're wet.

### Which shoes are best for travelling in India?

Cushioned walking shoes for cities and forts, sandals for beaches and the monsoon, and waterproof shoes or boots for the snow. Slip-ons help on trains and at temples, where you take your shoes off often.

### Do I need waterproof shoes for Manali in winter?

If you'll walk in snow or slush, yes. Mesh shoes and "water resistant" shoes let melting snow in. See [shoes for a Manali or Kashmir trip](shoes-for-manali-and-kashmir-trip.html).

### Should I wear or pack my heaviest shoes on a flight?

Wear them. The shoes on your feet aren't part of your cabin bag, and most Indian airlines allow only 7 kg in the cabin.
