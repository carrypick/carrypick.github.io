Dubai, Abu Dhabi and the rest of the UAE use **type G** sockets, the same three rectangular pins as the UK, at **230 V and 50 Hz**. An Indian plug doesn't fit, so you need an adapter. You don't need a voltage converter: the voltage is the same as India's.

## Quick answer

- **Plug type:** G (three rectangular pins, BS 1363). The same plug works in Saudi Arabia, Oman, Bahrain, the UK, Singapore and Hong Kong.
- **Voltage:** 230 V, 50 Hz, as in India. Any Indian charger, trimmer or hair dryer works with just a plug adapter.
- **From India:** take a type G adapter. A three-pack is handy if the family has three chargers.
- **From the USA or Canada:** take an adapter, and check each charger says **100-240V**. A 120 V-only hair dryer needs a converter or a dual-voltage one.
- **From the UK:** your plugs fit. No adapter needed.

## Which adapter to buy for Dubai

- **Only going to the Gulf, the UK or Singapore?** A plain type G adapter is the cheapest. It works in all of them.
- **More trips coming?** A universal adapter costs more but also works in Thailand, Europe, the USA and Australia.
- **Lots of phones?** A universal adapter with USB-C ports (see the [travel adapter by country](travel-adapter-by-country.html#our-travel-adapter-picks) picks) charges two or three phones from one socket.
- **Hair dryer or straightener?** Use an adapter with no USB ports and a high load. Both picks below list a 10 A or 2500 W limit.

## Questions

### What plug is used in Dubai?

Type G, three rectangular pins, the same plug as the UK. It runs at 230 V and 50 Hz.

### Do Indian plugs work in Dubai?

No. Neither the thin 2-pin nor the 3-pin Indian plug fits a type G socket. Your Indian chargers and appliances do work, because the voltage is the same, so a plug adapter is all you need.

### Do I need a voltage converter for Dubai from India?

No. India and the UAE both use 230 V and 50 Hz.

### Can I buy an adapter in Dubai?

Yes, but buying one before you fly means you can charge your phone as soon as you land. If you forget, ask at your hotel's front desk.

### Is the Dubai plug the same as the UK plug?

Yes, both use type G (BS 1363). A UK adapter works in Dubai.

### Which adapter do I need for Dubai from the USA?

A type G adapter or a universal one. Check that your chargers say 100-240V, since the UAE runs at 230 V, not the USA's 120 V.
