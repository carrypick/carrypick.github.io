## How to choose a gift for your girlfriend

- **Think about how long you've been together.** In the first months, keep it small and thoughtful. A watch or a matching pair can wait for a birthday or an anniversary.
- **Look at what she already wears.** Gold or silver tones? Big bags or small slings? Pick the same style, not your own taste.
- **Pick the colour with care.** One Amazon page can show several colours. Choose the one she'd wear.
- **Perfume is easier as a set.** Four small bottles let her find a favourite.
- **Long distance?** Have it delivered to her address and check the delivery date for her pin code.
- **The note matters most.** A short, honest card turns a simple gift into a memory.

## Questions

### What is the best gift for a girlfriend?

A pretty watch like the Titan Raga Charm, a sling bag for dates, cat-eye sunglasses or a perfume gift set. A zip-around wallet or lip balm is a good smaller gift.

### What should I gift a new girlfriend?

Something small and thoughtful, not too costly: a zip-around wallet, sunglasses, a lip balm or a small perfume set, with a short note.

### What can I gift my girlfriend on her birthday?

See our [birthday gifts for a girlfriend](birthday-gift-ideas.html#for-your-boyfriend-or-girlfriend), with lines for the card. A watch or a sling bag works well.

### What can I gift a girlfriend who lives far away?

Order online to her address and check the delivery date. A watch, a perfume set or something for your next trip together says you're planning to meet.

### What can I gift my girlfriend on our anniversary?

A dressy watch, a couple watch set so you both have one, or travel gear for a trip together. See our [anniversary gift ideas](anniversary-gift-ideas.html).
