Goa needs fewer shoes than most trips. Days are hot and dry from December to February, you spend hours on sand, and you'll often walk in and out of the sea. Two pairs are enough for most people. The trick is choosing the right two. Here's how, and the beach footwear we picked by their listed specs.

## Quick answer

- **Flip-flops or slides** for the beach, the pool and the hotel bathroom.
- **Strap sandals or clogs** for markets, scooter rides and long walks. Flip-flops slip off on a scooter.
- **Walking shoes with grip** only if you'll go to Dudhsagar Falls, a fort trek or a spice farm.
- **One pair of party shoes** if you plan a night out at a club or a nice restaurant.

## Goa weather and your feet

The India Meteorological Department's long-term averages put Goa at about 33 °C by day and 21 °C at night in December, with almost no rain. That means:

- **The sand gets hot by midday.** Walking barefoot from the road to the water can burn your feet.
- **Your shoes will get wet and sandy.** Closed shoes fill with sand and take hours to dry.
- **Rocks near the sea are slippery,** at places such as Vagator, Cabo de Rama and the rocks between beaches.

In the monsoon (June to September), paths are muddy and wet every day. Clogs and rubber sandals work better than flip-flops then.

## Flip-flops, sandals or clogs?

| | Flip-flops | Strap sandals | Clogs |
|---|---|---|---|
| Beach and sea | Yes | Yes, if they can get wet | Yes |
| Scooter rides | Slip off easily | Stay on | Stay on with the heel strap |
| Long walks | Tiring | Comfortable | Comfortable |
| Hot sand | Covers the sole | Covers the sole | Covers most of the foot |
| Packed weight | Lightest | Light | Bulkier |

- **Most people need flip-flops plus one strapped pair.** That covers the beach, markets, churches in Old Goa and scooter rides.
- **Clogs are the one-pair choice** if you want a single pair for sand, rain and walking.
- **Leather or faux-leather sandals** don't like seawater. Keep them for town.

## Walking shoes and party shoes

- **Dudhsagar Falls and treks:** the rocks are wet and slippery. Wear shoes with a rubber grip sole, not flip-flops. Our [travel shoes guide](travel-shoes-guide.html) has walking shoes for men and women.
- **A night out:** one pair of clean sneakers or loafers looks right at a nice restaurant or club, and covers your toes in a crowd.
- **Churches and temples:** you'll take shoes off at temples, so slip-ons help.

## Size and packing tips

- **Choose your usual size** for flip-flops and sandals. Your heel shouldn't hang over the back edge. Check UK, US and EU sizes in our [shoe size converter](../tools/shoe-size.html).
- **Clogs often run in men's and women's sizes on one page.** Read the size names carefully before you order.
- **Pack sandy shoes in a separate bag** so sand doesn't get into your clothes. A [packing cube](packing-cubes.html) or a plastic bag works.
- **Wear your heaviest shoes on the flight.** They don't count towards your bag's weight.

## Questions

### Which footwear is good for a Goa trip?

Flip-flops for the beach, plus one pair of strap sandals or clogs for markets, scooter rides and walking. Take walking shoes with grip only for Dudhsagar Falls or treks.

### Can I wear shoes on Goa beaches?

Yes, but closed shoes fill with sand and get wet. Most people wear flip-flops or sandals on the beach and carry them near the water.

### Are Crocs good for Goa?

Clogs suit Goa well: they can get wet, they stay on with the heel strap, and they cover your feet on hot sand. Our Crocs pick is listed in men's and women's UK sizes.

### Can I ride a scooter in flip-flops?

Flip-flops slip off easily and give no protection. Strap sandals or closed shoes are safer on a scooter. Wear a helmet: it's the law across India.

### Do I need sneakers in Goa?

Only for a smart night out, or for treks and Dudhsagar Falls. Otherwise, flip-flops and sandals are enough.

### How many pairs of shoes should I pack for Goa?

Two for most trips: flip-flops and one strapped pair. Add sneakers or party shoes if you plan a night out.
