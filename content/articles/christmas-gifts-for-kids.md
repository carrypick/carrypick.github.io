## How to choose a Christmas gift for a child

- **Choose something for every day.** A watch, a school bag or a bottle gets used on every school day, not just on Christmas morning.
- **Think about their age and size.** The Zoop watches below have a 40 mm case, so they suit school-age kids better than toddlers. A 37 litre bag is a lot for a small child.
- **Let them pick the colour.** The same Zoop watch comes in blue and pink, and many school bags come in several prints. One Amazon page can show several colours, so check the one you've clicked before you pay.
- **Know what "30 m water resistant" means.** It's splash-proof: fine for washing hands and rain, but not for swimming or a bath.
- **Keep the box and the invoice.** You'll need them if something breaks and you claim the warranty.
- **Order by mid-December.** Deliveries can take longer in Christmas week, so check the delivery date before you pay.
