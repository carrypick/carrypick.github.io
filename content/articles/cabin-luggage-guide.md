On a domestic flight in India you can take **one cabin bag of up to 7 kg**, and its length, width and height together must not add up to more than **115 cm**. All five Indian airlines we track use those two numbers. What differs is the shape of the box: **IndiGo, Akasa Air and SpiceJet allow 55 x 35 x 25 cm, Air India 55 x 40 x 20 cm and Air India Express 56 x 36 x 23 cm**. So a bag that fits one airline can be too wide or too thick for another.

Looking at a bag sold as "20 inch" or "22 inch"? See what [20 inch](20-inch-trolley-bag-size.html) and [22 inch](22-inch-trolley-bag-size.html) really mean in cm, or every size in our [luggage size chart](../baggage/luggage-size-chart.html).

## Cabin bag rules by Indian airline

Read on each airline's own website on 26 Nov 2026, for Economy on domestic flights:

| Airline | Cabin bag size | In inches | Weight | Small personal bag |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| IndiGo | 55 x 35 x 25 cm, 115 cm in total, wheels and handles included | 21.7 x 13.8 x 9.8 in | 7 kg | One more, up to 3 kg, such as a purse or a small laptop bag |
| Air India | 55 x 40 x 20 cm, 115 cm in total | 21.7 x 15.7 x 7.9 in | 7 kg | One more, up to 3 kg and 40 x 30 x 20 cm, under the seat |
| Air India Express | 56 x 36 x 23 cm, including wheels, handles and side pockets | 22 x 14.2 x 9.1 in | 7 kg | No extra bag: a laptop and personal items count in the same 7 kg |
| Akasa Air | 55 x 35 x 25 cm, 115 cm in total | 21.7 x 13.8 x 9.8 in | 7 kg | One more, up to 3 kg, under the seat |
| SpiceJet, Boeing planes | 55 x 35 x 25 cm, 115 cm in total | 21.7 x 13.8 x 9.8 in | 7 kg | No extra bag: a laptop bag or purse counts in the same 7 kg |
| SpiceJet, Q400 planes | 50 x 35 x 23 cm, 108 cm in total | 19.7 x 13.8 x 9.1 in | 7 kg | As above |

IndiGo's own page writes its limit as "22x16x9 in", but 35 cm is 13.8 inches, not 16. Go by the cm.

**Need more than 7 kg in the cabin?** Air India lets you carry up to 12 kg if you pay, and lists ₹3,000 for this on domestic flights. Air India Express sells an Xtra Carry-On add-on up to 12 kg in all. SpiceJet's Carry More add-on also takes you to 12 kg, and its FAQ says a heavy bag is charged ₹750 per kg at the gate, for up to 5 kg extra. Each airline's full rules are on its own page: [IndiGo](../baggage/indigo.html), [Air India](../baggage/air-india.html), [Air India Express](../baggage/air-india-express.html), [Akasa Air](../baggage/akasa-air.html) and [SpiceJet](../baggage/spicejet.html).

## Flying abroad

Most international airlines we track also allow 7 kg in Economy on their India routes, but not all: Lufthansa and Turkish Airlines allow 8 kg, and Gulf Air 6 kg. Their size limits vary more, from 50 x 37 x 25 cm on Qatar Airways to 56 x 45 x 25 cm on Saudia, Thai Airways and British Airways. ## Why "55 cm cabin size" isn't the full story

Most cabin trolleys are sold as "55 cm". That's only the **height**. Airlines check all three sides and the total, wheels and handles included.

[[diagram:measure-bag]]

Here's how three popular 55 cm bags add up, using the dimensions listed by the sellers:

- Safari Pentagon Pro 55 cm: 55 + 38 + 23 = **116 cm**
- American Tourister Liftoff 55 cm: 55.5 + 39.5 + 22 = **117 cm**
- Safari Thorium Royale 55 cm: 55 + 40 + 23 = **118 cm**

All three are a little over 115 cm on paper, and all three are wider than IndiGo's 35 cm. Airline staff don't measure every bag, but they can. For zero risk, look for a total of **115 cm or less**, check each side too, and don't open expandable zips.

On 3 Nov 2026 we checked the listed sizes of 83 hard-shell "55 cm" cabin trolleys on Amazon.in. **70 of them were 36–41 cm wide**, over IndiGo's 35 cm limit, and most of the narrow ones were too deep or over 115 cm in total. Two that do fit by their listed size:

- [Safari 55 cm polycarbonate cabin trolley with laptop compartment](../picks/B097JLKP8X.html): **55 x 35 x 25 cm**, exactly IndiGo's limit. Also fits Akasa Air and SpiceJet's Boeing flights.
- [American Tourister Liftoff+ Small](../picks/B0F5HGLC5Q.html): **55 x 40 x 20 cm**, exactly Air India's limit, and only 2.2 kg.

No single hard trolley we found fits every Indian airline, because IndiGo wants it narrow (35 cm) and Air India wants it thin (20 cm). A 28–30 litre **laptop backpack** is often the safest cabin bag: our [Safari Omega](../picks/B097JJ2CK6.html) and [American Tourister Valex](../picks/B0BTD4S4XF.html) both fit every Indian airline's limit by their listed size. ## Weight matters as much as size

An empty hard-shell cabin trolley usually weighs **2.5–3 kg**. With a 7 kg limit, that leaves only about **4–4.5 kg** for your things. Our [trolley bag capacity guide](trolley-bag-capacity-kg-litres.html) adds up what 7 kg really holds for 3 days.

## Shell, wheels and locks

For a cabin bag, the shell matters less than the size. Polycarbonate flexes instead of cracking and is tougher; polypropylene is lighter for the money ([what the listings weigh](polycarbonate-vs-polypropylene-luggage.html)). Eight spinner wheels roll more smoothly than four ([4 vs 8 wheels](4-wheel-vs-8-wheel-trolley.html)). A 3-digit combination lock is enough in India; a [TSA lock](tsa-lock.html) matters only if you fly to or through the USA.

## Before you fly: checklist

1. Check the cabin limit for your airline, and for SpiceJet, the aircraft type on your booking.
2. Measure your bag's three sides and the total (L + W + H), wheels and handles included.
3. Weigh the packed bag at home, including any laptop bag if your airline counts it in the 7 kg.
4. Keep power banks, laptops and medicines in your cabin bag. [Power banks can't go in checked baggage](../baggage/can-i-carry/power-bank.html).
5. Liquids, perfume or food in the cabin? See [can I carry it on a flight?](../baggage/can-i-carry.html)

## Questions

### What is the cabin bag size in cm?

On most Indian domestic flights it's 55 x 35 x 25 cm and 115 cm in total (IndiGo, Akasa Air and SpiceJet's Boeing planes). Air India allows 55 x 40 x 20 cm, Air India Express 56 x 36 x 23 cm and SpiceJet's Q400 planes 50 x 35 x 23 cm.

### What is the cabin bag size in inches?

55 x 35 x 25 cm is 21.7 x 13.8 x 9.8 inches, and 115 cm in total is 45.3 inches. Air India's 55 x 40 x 20 cm is 21.7 x 15.7 x 7.9 inches.

### What is the cabin bag size for IndiGo?

One bag of up to 7 kg, no bigger than 55 x 35 x 25 cm and 115 cm in total, wheels and handles included. You can also carry one personal item of up to 3 kg, such as a purse or a small laptop bag.

### What is the cabin bag size for Air India?

One bag of up to 7 kg in Economy and Premium Economy, no bigger than 55 x 40 x 20 cm and 115 cm in total, plus one personal item of up to 3 kg that fits under the seat. First and Business allow 10 kg.

### Is a 55 cm bag allowed in the cabin?

Yes, if its width and depth also fit your airline's limit and it weighs 7 kg or less, packed. Most 55 cm hard trolleys we read were 36–41 cm wide, which is over IndiGo's and Akasa Air's 35 cm.

### What is the cabin bag weight limit on domestic flights?

7 kg on IndiGo, Air India, Air India Express, Akasa Air and SpiceJet. On Air India Express and SpiceJet, a laptop bag or purse counts inside that 7 kg; IndiGo, Air India and Akasa Air allow one more small bag of up to 3 kg.

### What is the cabin bag size in litres?

A 55 x 35 x 25 cm box holds about 48 litres, and Air India's 55 x 40 x 20 cm about 44 litres. A bag isn't a perfect box: the cabin trolleys we read listed 30–57 litres, and 39 litres was typical. Brands measure litres differently, so go by the size in cm.

### Can I carry a laptop bag with a 7 kg cabin bag?

On IndiGo, Air India and Akasa Air, yes, as your one personal item of up to 3 kg. On Air India Express and SpiceJet, the laptop bag counts in the same 7 kg as your cabin bag.
