Most of mainland Europe uses round-pin plugs, **types C, E and F**, at **230 V and 50 Hz**. The voltage matches India's. A thin two-pin Indian phone charger often fits; a three-pin Indian plug never does. Switzerland, Italy and Denmark have their own extra types, and the UK and Ireland use type G.

## Quick answer

- **Mainland Europe:** types C, E and F, 230 V. A European (type C) adapter or a universal one.
- **Switzerland (J), Italy (L), Denmark (K):** a thin type C plug fits their sockets too. A thick type E or F plug may not fit in Switzerland.
- **The UK and Ireland:** type G. You need a separate UK adapter, or a universal one.
- **From India:** no converter needed. Every Indian charger and appliance works on 230 V.
- **From the USA or Canada:** an adapter, and only chargers marked **100-240V**.

## Questions

### Which plug is used in Europe?

Mostly types C, E and F, all round pins, at 230 V. France, Belgium, Poland and the Czech Republic use E; Germany, Spain, the Netherlands and most others use F. A type C plug fits both. Moving to Germany to study? Our [Germany packing list for Indian students](germany-packing-list-for-indian-students.html) covers the papers, clothes and plugs to take.

### Do Indian plugs work in Europe?

A thin two-pin Indian charger (type C) fits most sockets. A three-pin Indian plug doesn't. Indian chargers work on Europe's 230 V.

### Do I need a converter for Europe from India?

No. Europe and India both use 230 V and 50 Hz.

### Which adapter do I need for Switzerland?

Switzerland uses types C and J. A thin type C plug fits; a thicker German-style F plug often doesn't. A universal adapter with a slim EU plug is safest.

### Does Italy need a different adapter?

Italy uses C, F and L. A type C plug fits most sockets, including type L ones.

### Is the UK plug the same as Europe?

No. The UK and Ireland use type G. See [travel adapter for the UK](travel-adapter-for-uk.html).
