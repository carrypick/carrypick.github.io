Goa in December and January is hot in the day and pleasant at night, with almost no rain. So you need less than you think: light clothes, good sun care, one warm layer and the right shoes. This list tells you what to pack and why.

## Goa weather in December and January

The India Meteorological Department's long-term averages for Goa:

| Month | Day | Night | Rain |
|---|---|---|---|
| December | About 33 °C | About 21 °C | About 2.5 mm |
| January | About 32 °C | About 20 °C | About 1 mm |

- **Days are hot and sunny.** Pack for the sun, not for rain.
- **Evenings are cooler.** Goa Tourism says light woollens can be worn in December and January. One light sweater, shawl or jacket is enough.
- **Flights, AC buses and trains are cold.** Keep that warm layer in your cabin bag.

## The short list

If you pack only these, you'll be fine:

1. Light cotton or linen clothes, one outfit for each day
2. Swimwear, and a shirt or kaftan to wear over it
3. One outfit that covers shoulders and knees, for churches and temples
4. One light sweater, shawl or jacket for evenings and flights
5. Flip-flops, plus sandals with straps or walking shoes
6. Sunscreen, sunglasses, a cap or hat and lip balm with SPF
7. Mosquito repellent
8. A waterproof pouch for your phone and cash
9. Photo ID, some cash, UPI and a debit card
10. Phone charger and a power bank, in your cabin bag

## Clothes

- **Cotton and linen.** They dry fast and stay cool. Avoid heavy jeans for the daytime.
- **Shorts, skirts, dresses and T-shirts** are normal on the beaches and in the beach towns.
- **One modest outfit.** Churches and temples expect covered shoulders and knees. The Basilica of Bom Jesus in Old Goa has asked visitors to dress modestly for years, and it has given shawls to visitors who weren't covered.
- **Swimwear for the beach and pool only.** Put a shirt, kaftan or sarong over it when you walk to shops and restaurants. Nudity on beaches and in public places is punishable by law in India.
- **One nicer outfit** for a party, a club night or a good dinner.
- **A light layer for the evening.** A thin sweater, shawl or light jacket.

## Shoes

- **Flip-flops or slides** for the beach. The sand gets hot by midday.
- **Sandals with straps** for markets and scooter rides. Flip-flops slip off easily on a scooter.
- **Walking shoes** for Old Goa, the forts and long sightseeing days. If you're going to Dudhsagar Falls, choose shoes with a good grip for wet rocks.
- **One pair of party shoes** if you plan a night out.

Most people need only two pairs: flip-flops and one comfortable pair. See our [travel shoes guide](travel-shoes-guide.html).

## Sun, skin and health

- **Sunscreen, SPF 30 or higher.** Put it on again every 2 hours and after swimming. An adult needs about 30 ml for one full coat, so a small tube runs out fast. Read our [sunscreen guide](travel-sunscreen-guide.html).
- **Sunglasses and a cap or hat.** The sea and sand reflect the glare.
- **Lip balm with SPF,** and after-sun or aloe gel.
- **Mosquito repellent,** especially for evenings.
- **Your regular medicines,** plus something for an upset stomach, plasters and ORS.
- **A refillable water bottle.** Drink more water than usual in the heat.

## Beach bag

- **A small sling or tote** for your phone, cash and sunscreen. Beaches are open from 7 AM to sunset, says Goa Tourism, and you'll carry things all day.
- **A waterproof pouch** for your phone, cash and room key on the beach and on boat trips.
- **A quick-dry towel or sarong.**
- **A plastic or zip bag** for wet swimwear.

## What to pack for women

- Three or four cotton dresses, or tops with shorts and skirts
- Swimwear and a kaftan or sarong to wear over it
- A long skirt or loose trousers and a top with sleeves, for churches and temples
- A light shawl or stole: it works for churches, cool evenings and the flight
- Flip-flops and one pair of comfortable sandals
- A small crossbody bag that zips shut

## What to pack for men

- Shorts and T-shirts, plus one or two cotton shirts
- Swim shorts
- One pair of long trousers, for churches and dinners
- One light sweater or jacket for evenings
- Flip-flops and sandals with straps or walking shoes
- A cap

## What to pack for couples

- Share one medium trolley for both of you, or take two cabin bags. A packing cube each keeps things in order.
- One nice outfit each for a dinner or a party night.
- One power bank and a charger with two ports for both phones.

## What to pack for kids

- More clothes than days: kids get wet and sandy
- A swimsuit and a sun hat
- A kids' sunscreen and a light long-sleeved top for the sun
- Slip-on sandals that can get wet
- Snacks and a water bottle for the journey
- Their medicines and a small first-aid kit

Keep children close to you on the beach, and swim only between the red-and-yellow flags, where lifesavers are on duty.

## Which bag to take

- **About 4 nights:** a cabin trolley or a 30–40 litre backpack is enough. Beach clothes are light.
- **About a week:** a medium trolley, or a cabin bag if you plan to wash clothes once.
- **Riding a scooter around Goa?** A backpack is easier to carry than a trolley.

## What not to pack

- **Expensive jewellery.** You won't need it on the beach.
- **Lighters and matches.** They're banned in both cabin and check-in bags on flights.
- **Big bottles in your cabin bag.** Each liquid must be 100 ml or less, in one clear 1-litre bag. Bigger bottles go in check-in.
- **Too many shoes.** Two pairs are enough for most trips.

## Flying home from Goa

Many people bring back cashews, Goan sweets, spices or feni. Check these rules before you pack:

- **Dry coconut (copra) is banned** in both cabin and check-in bags, because it can catch fire.
- **Spice powders** can't go in your cabin bag. Pack them in check-in.
- **Feni and other alcohol** go only in check-in, up to 5 litres per person, in its shop packing. You can't take it to a dry state such as Gujarat or Bihar. See [can I carry alcohol on a flight?](../baggage/can-i-carry/alcohol.html)
- **Sweets and home food:** see [can I carry sweets and home food?](../baggage/can-i-carry/sweets-and-home-food.html)

For everything else, search our [can I carry it on a flight?](../baggage/can-i-carry.html) list.

## Questions

### What should I pack for Goa in December?

Light cotton clothes, swimwear, one modest outfit for churches, one light sweater or shawl for the evening, flip-flops, walking shoes or sandals, sunscreen, sunglasses, a cap and mosquito repellent. Days are about 33 °C, and nights about 21 °C.

### Is it cold in Goa in December?

No. Days are hot, at about 33 °C, and nights are about 20–21 °C, according to IMD averages. A light sweater or shawl is enough for the evening and for cold flights or AC buses.

### What should girls wear in Goa?

Dresses, shorts, skirts and tops are normal in the beach towns. Wear swimwear only on the beach or at the pool, and put a kaftan or sarong over it when you leave. Carry one outfit that covers your shoulders and knees for churches and temples.

### Can I wear shorts in Goa?

Yes, on the beaches, in the beach towns and in most restaurants. For churches and temples, wear long trousers or a long skirt and cover your shoulders.

### Which shoes are best for Goa?

Flip-flops for the beach, and one pair of sandals with straps or walking shoes for markets, scooter rides and sightseeing. Take shoes with a good grip if you're going to Dudhsagar Falls.

### Can I bring cashews and feni back from Goa on a flight?

Yes. Cashews are fine in either bag. Feni goes only in your check-in bag, up to 5 litres per person, in its shop packing, and not to dry states such as Gujarat or Bihar. Dry coconut is banned in all bags.
