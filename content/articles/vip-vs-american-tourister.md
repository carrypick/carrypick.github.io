VIP and American Tourister are both big names in Indian luggage, and people often choose between them. We compared what their Amazon.in listings say and what each brand's own website says about the warranty. We haven't tested these bags, so nothing here tells you how long they last.

## Quick answer

VIP or American Tourister, which is better? It depends on what you need most:

- **Polycarbonate with a TSA lock: VIP.** VIP's Vero medium is polycarbonate, 3.1 kg empty and has a TSA lock. American Tourister's polycarbonate Ivy NXT 68 cm weighs 3.98 kg and lists a combination lock that isn't TSA.
- **Lightest bag: American Tourister.** Its polypropylene Liftoff+ 67 cm weighs 2.9 kg and still has a TSA lock.
- **Cabin bag that fits an Indian airline: American Tourister.** The Liftoff+ Small is 55 x 40 x 20 cm, inside Air India's limit. VIP's Vero cabin is 39 cm wide and 23 cm deep, over the limits of the five airlines we checked.
- **Warranty: similar.** Both brands cover manufacturing defects only, not airline damage. The bags we picked list 3 years, international.

## Cabin bags: check the full size

Most Indian airlines allow 55 x 35 x 25 cm (IndiGo, Akasa Air, SpiceJet) or 55 x 40 x 20 cm (Air India). Air India Express allows 56 x 36 x 23 cm. By listed size:

- **VIP Vero cabin:** too wide for IndiGo, too deep for Air India, and 117 cm in total, over all five airlines' 115 cm.
- **American Tourister Liftoff+ Small:** fits Air India's 55 x 40 x 20 cm.
- **American Tourister Ivy NXT 55:** over all five by listed size.

Being over the listed limit doesn't mean you'll be stopped every time, but it's your risk. Test any bag in our [cabin bag checker](../baggage/checker.html).

VIP's warranty page applies to bags bought on vipbags.com. For a bag bought on Amazon.in, go by the listing and the warranty card or tag in the bag. Our VIP picks list 3 years, international.

Neither site gave a count of service centres in India. VIP's corporate page also asks you to register the bag online before a claim, so do that when it arrives. American Tourister says repairs by anyone other than an authorised centre end the warranty.

Keep your Amazon invoice for both brands: it's your proof of purchase.

## Questions

### VIP or American Tourister: which is better?

Neither wins on everything. In the bags we compared, VIP's Vero medium was a lighter polycarbonate bag with a TSA lock, and American Tourister's Liftoff+ was the lightest bag overall and had a cabin size that fits Air India. We haven't tested them, so we can't say which lasts longer.

### Is VIP an Indian brand?

Yes. VIP belongs to VIP Industries Ltd, a Mumbai company established in 1968. The VIP listings we read say they're made in India.

### Is American Tourister an Indian brand?

No. American Tourister belongs to the Samsonite group. It has an India office in Mumbai and sells many bags made for India.

### Does the VIP or American Tourister warranty cover airline damage?

No. Both brands' warranty pages exclude damage by airlines. Report it to the airline before you leave the airport, and take photos.

### Is the American Tourister warranty valid abroad?

Its India site says the warranty is global and any Samsonite service centre can repair the bag. You pay to get the bag there.

### Are Skybags and Aristocrat made by VIP?

Yes. Both belong to VIP Industries, and VIP's warranty terms name Skybags too. See [Safari vs Skybags](safari-vs-skybags.html).
