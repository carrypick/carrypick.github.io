## Before the sale: choose your size

Sale days are busy, and stocks can change quickly. Decide this first:

- **Which bag?** Cabin, medium or large? See our [luggage size chart](../baggage/luggage-size-chart.html).
- **Does it fit your airline?** Test the size in our [cabin bag checker](../baggage/checker.html). IndiGo's cabin limit, for example, is 55 x 35 x 25 cm and 7 kg: see [IndiGo baggage rules](../baggage/indigo.html).
- **How big is it really?** Inch sizes can be confusing. See our guides to [20 inch](20-inch-trolley-bag-size.html), [24 inch](24-inch-trolley-bag-size.html) and [28 inch](28-inch-trolley-bag-size.html) trolleys.
- **Which watch size?** Measure your wrist with our [watch size tool](../tools/watch-size.html).

## What to check on any listing

1. **The exact size and colour you've selected.** One Amazon page can show many sizes, and each has its own details. We've seen one set of listings where a colour showed a size that couldn't be right.
2. **All three sides, not just the height.** Add them up. A checked bag over 158 cm breaks the limit of most Indian airlines.
3. **The bag's own weight.** A lighter bag leaves more of your allowance for your things.
4. **The warranty.** Look for how long it is and whether it's international. Some listings don't say.
5. **Who sells it, and the return period.** They can differ for the same product.
6. **Bank offers.** They depend on your card and change from sale to sale, so check them at checkout.

## Questions

### When is the Amazon Republic Day Sale 2027?

Amazon hasn't announced it yet, as of 26 Nov 2026. Past sales started on 17 January (2021 and 2022), 13 January (2025) and 16 January (2026), so mid-January is likely, but it isn't confirmed.

### When will Amazon announce the Republic Day sale dates?

In past years, only a few days before the sale. Amazon announced the 2025 sale on 11 January and the 2026 sale on 9 January, both less than a week before they began.

### When does the Amazon Republic Day sale end?

It depends on the year. The 2025 sale ended on 19 January, and the 2021 and 2022 sales on 20 January. Amazon's pages for 2026 don't give an end date. We'll add the 2027 end date when Amazon announces it.

### Do I need Prime for the Republic Day sale?

No, the sale is open to everyone. In 2021, 2022 and 2025, Prime members got early access, from 12 to 24 hours. In 2026, Amazon promised Prime members extra savings. Check Amazon's terms for 2027 when they're out.

### Are all bags and watches cheaper in the sale?

We can't say. Not every product gets an offer, and we don't track prices. Check the price on Amazon, and compare the size and warranty too.

### Should I buy luggage now or wait for the sale?

If you're travelling before mid-January, buy in time for your trip. If not, you can choose your size now and check the offers when the sale starts.
