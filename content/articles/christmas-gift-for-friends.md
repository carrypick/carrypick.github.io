## How to choose a Christmas gift for a friend

- **Think about their weekends.** A friend who's always out needs a sling or sunglasses; one who travels needs packing cubes.
- **Unisex gifts are safe.** A plain black watch, round sunglasses or a crossbody bag suit men and women.
- **Agree on the size of the gift first.** If your group swaps gifts, decide together how big a gift to buy, so nobody feels awkward.
- **Skip clothes and strong perfumes.** They depend on size and taste. A small perfume set lets your friend try a few scents.
- **For a group, buy the same small gift for everyone.** A pocket perfume or a lip balm is easy to wrap and fair to all.
- **Order by mid-December.** Deliveries can take longer in Christmas week, so check the delivery date before you pay.

## Questions

### What is a good Christmas gift for a female friend?

A vegan leather sling, a zip-around wallet, cat-eye sunglasses or a small perfume set. If she travels, packing cubes or a light crossbody bag get used straight away.

### What is a good Christmas gift for a male friend?

A simple watch like the Fastrack all-black, a wallet gift set, wayfarer sunglasses or a power bank with a built-in cable. A beard trimmer suits a friend who keeps a beard.

### What is a good Christmas gift for my best friend?

Something a little special that they'd use often: polarized sunglasses, a perfume gift set, or a crossbody bag for your next trip together.

### What is a good Christmas gift for a couple we're friends with?

A gift for both, like a perfume pack with scents for men and women, or pair watches for a newly married couple. See our [Christmas gifts for couples](christmas-gift-for-couples.html) for more.

### When should I order a Christmas gift?

By mid-December. Check the delivery date shown on Amazon before you pay, because deliveries can take longer in Christmas week.
