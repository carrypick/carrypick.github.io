## How to choose a Christmas gift for her

- **Think about what she uses every day.** A watch, a handbag or a wallet she carries daily is a gift she'll see often.
- **Check the colour you click.** One Amazon page can show several colours. Make sure the one you pick is the one she'd wear.
- **Match the watch to her style.** A dressy watch with crystals suits functions; a plain metal strap suits office wear. If she likes simple things, choose the plainer one.
- **Skip perfume and clothes in a guessed size.** They depend on taste and fit. If you're not sure, choose a bag or a watch.
- **Plan around a trip.** Going away for Christmas or New Year? A crossbody bag, packing cubes or a warm jacket get used straight away.
- **Order by mid-December.** Deliveries can take longer in Christmas week, so check the delivery date before you pay.

## Questions

### What is a good Christmas gift for a girlfriend?

A watch like the Titan Raga, a vegan leather sling or a light crossbody bag. If you're going away together, a travel gift like packing cubes or a toiletry bag gets used straight away.

### What is a good Christmas gift for a wife?

An elegant watch like the Titan Karishma, a tote that fits what she carries every day, or a zip-around wallet as a smaller gift. If she carries a laptop, choose a work tote with a laptop section.

### What is a good Christmas gift for mom?

Pair watches she can wear with dad, a thermal set for the winter, or a steel bottle that keeps tea hot. Simple, useful things suit most moms.

### What should I not give my girlfriend or wife for Christmas?

Avoid clothes in a size you have to guess, and perfume or make-up unless you know exactly what she likes. Also avoid "useful" gifts for the house, like kitchen things, unless she's asked for them.

### When should I order a Christmas gift?

By mid-December. Check the delivery date shown on Amazon before you pay, because deliveries can take longer in Christmas week.
