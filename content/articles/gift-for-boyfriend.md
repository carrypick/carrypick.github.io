## How to choose a gift for your boyfriend

- **Think about how long you've been together.** In the first months, keep it small: a wallet, sunglasses or a pocket perfume. Save a watch for a birthday or an anniversary.
- **Look at his day.** College, office, gym or bike rides? A gift that fits that day gets used.
- **Notice what's worn out.** A torn wallet, a scratched pair of sunglasses or a trimmer that won't charge tells you the gift.
- **Check the dial size.** Around 39–42 mm suits most men's wrists.
- **Perfume is easier as a set.** Four small bottles let him find a favourite.
- **Long distance?** Have it delivered to his address and check the delivery date for his pin code.

## Questions

### What is the best gift for a boyfriend?

A watch he'll wear every day, polarized sunglasses, a wallet gift set, a perfume set or a beard trimmer. If he travels, a leather sling or a weekender bag.

### What should I gift a new boyfriend?

Something small and useful, not too costly: a slim wallet, sunglasses or a pocket perfume, with a short note.

### What can I gift my boyfriend on his birthday?

See our [birthday gifts for a boyfriend](birthday-gift-ideas.html#for-your-boyfriend-or-girlfriend), with lines for the card. A watch or polarized sunglasses works well.

### What can I gift a boyfriend who lives far away?

Order online to his address and check the delivery date. A watch, a perfume set or a weekender bag for your next trip together says you're planning to meet.

### What can I gift my boyfriend on our anniversary?

A watch, a couple watch set so you both have one, or a weekender bag for a trip together. See our [anniversary gift ideas](anniversary-gift-ideas.html).
