## How to choose a gift for your brother

- **Look at what he uses every day.** A cracked phone, an old wallet or a dead trimmer tells you the gift.
- **Pick useful over showy.** A watch, sunglasses or a power bank gets used for years. A showpiece sits in a cupboard.
- **Check the size of a watch.** For a slim wrist choose around 38–40 mm. For a big wrist, a larger dial looks better.
- **Polarized or not?** Polarized lenses cut glare off roads, water and snow. Plain UV lenses are fine for city use.
- **Skip clothes and shoes unless you know his size.** A watch, a wallet or a bag always fits.
- **For Rakhi or Bhai Dooj, order early.** Raksha Bandhan 2027 is on Tuesday 17 August and Bhai Dooj on Sunday 31 October (Government of India holiday list). Check the delivery date before you pay.

### What is the best gift for a brother?

Something he'll use every day: a Fastrack or Titan watch, polarized sunglasses, a beard trimmer, a perfume gift set or a pocket power bank.

### What should I give my brother on Rakhi?

A watch, sunglasses or a wallet gift box make a lasting Rakhi gift. For a small gift with the rakhi, a pocket perfume. Raksha Bandhan 2027 is on Tuesday 17 August, so order a few days before.

A Fastrack Minimal watch, a pocket power bank, polarized sunglasses or packing cubes for trips home from college or hostel.

### What can I gift my brother-in-law?
