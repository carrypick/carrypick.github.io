A 20 inch trolley bag is **50.8 cm** tall. But in India, the number on a listing doesn't always mean that. We found brands selling 55–58 cm cabin bags as "20 inch" on Amazon.in, so always check the size in cm.

## Quick answer

- **20 inches is 50.8 cm.** A 55 cm cabin bag is really 21.7 inches.
- **It's a cabin size.** It suits a trip of about 2–4 days.
- **Height isn't enough.** Airlines also check width and depth, wheels and handles included.
- **Weight counts too.** Most Indian airlines allow 7 kg in the cabin, and that includes the bag.

## "20 inch" on the listing and the real size

These are the sizes shown on Amazon.in on 6 Nov 2026:

| What the listing says | Listed height | Full listed size |
|---|---|---|
| L'Attitude Meridian "55 cm 20 inch" | 56 cm | 56 x 38 x 21 cm |
| L'Attitude Core "55 cm 20 inch" | 58 cm | 58 x 41 x 22 cm |
| Junior Joe kids' suitcase "20 inch" | 51 cm | 51 x 34 x 23 cm |

Only the kids' suitcase is really about 20 inches tall. Brands rarely explain the difference. The inch number may leave out the wheels, but the listings don't say so.

## Does it fit the cabin?

Here are the limits we checked on each airline's site on 3 Nov 2026:

| Airline | Limit in cm | Limit in inches | Weight |
|---|---|---|---|
| IndiGo | 55 x 35 x 25 | 21.7 x 13.8 x 9.8 | 7 kg |
| Air India | 55 x 40 x 20 | 21.7 x 15.7 x 7.9 | 7 kg |
| Air India Express | 56 x 36 x 23 | 22 x 14.2 x 9.1 | 7 kg |
| Akasa Air | 55 x 35 x 25 | 21.7 x 13.8 x 9.8 | 7 kg |
| SpiceJet (Boeing) | 55 x 35 x 25 | 21.7 x 13.8 x 9.8 | 7 kg |
| SpiceJet (Q400) | 50 x 35 x 23 | 19.7 x 13.8 x 9.1 | 7 kg |

A true 20 inch bag is short enough for all of these except SpiceJet's small Q400 planes, where it's just over. The problem is usually the width or depth. On 3 Nov we checked 83 hard "55 cm" trolleys on Amazon.in, and 70 were 36–41 cm wide. That's too wide for IndiGo, Akasa Air and SpiceJet. ## How much does it hold?

- **Litres:** cabin trolleys we checked on 6 Nov listed about 30–57 litres. Bags of the same size can list very different numbers, so treat litres as a rough guide.
- **Weight:** the same bags weighed about 2–3 kg empty. A 2.5 kg bag leaves you only **4.5 kg** for your things on a 7 kg limit.
- **What to do:** if you can't get under 7 kg, buy a lighter bag, or move heavy items to a check-in bag. Our [bag weight calculator](../tools/bag-weight.html) can help you plan.

## On a train

In AC 3-tier and AC Chair Car, the Indian Railways limit for a suitcase in the coach is 55 x 45 x 22.5 cm. Many cabin trolleys are a little deeper than that on paper. See our [train luggage rules](../baggage/train-luggage-rules.html).

## Questions

### What is a 20 inch trolley bag in cm?

20 inches is 50.8 cm. Some brands in India also use "20 inch" for cabin bags that are listed at 55–58 cm, so check the size in cm.

### Is a 20 inch trolley allowed in the cabin?

Usually, yes, but only if its width and depth fit too, and it weighs 7 kg or less with your things inside. On SpiceJet's Q400 planes the height limit is 50 cm, so a true 20 inch bag is just over.

### Is 20 inch the same as 55 cm?

No. 55 cm is 21.7 inches, and 20 inches is 50.8 cm. Some brands still list 55 cm bags as "20 inch".

### How many days is a 20 inch trolley good for?

About 2–4 days. It depends on how much you pack and on the 7 kg limit, not only on the size of the bag.
