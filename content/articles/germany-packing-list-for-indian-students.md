## The short list

If you pack only these, you'll be fine:

1. Your passport with your German national (D) visa
2. Your admission letter, blocked account confirmation and health insurance certificate, in your hand bag, with copies
3. Your medicines in their original packs, with the prescription
4. Thermals, two sweaters, a warm waterproof jacket, a cap and gloves
5. Waterproof shoes with a good grip
6. A laptop and its charger, a travel adapter with a Type C plug, and a power bank in your cabin bag
7. A card that works abroad, and some euros for the first days
8. A check-in trolley within 158 cm and 23 kg, and a luggage scale

## Documents to carry in your hand bag

The German Missions in India's page on the visa for studies (dated 16 Feb 2026, read 23 Nov 2026) lists the documents for your visa application, including:

- **Your passport** (issued within the last 10 years, with at least 2 empty pages)
- **Proof of admission** to a full-time degree programme, a preparatory college (Studienkolleg) or a language course
- **Proof that you can pay for your stay,** for most students a **blocked account (Sperrkonto)**. The page gives €11,904 for the first year, with no more than €992 a month to withdraw. A formal obligation letter from a sponsor in Germany, a scholarship or an education loan can also be used.
- **Health insurance from your date of arrival** in Germany
- **Your school or degree certificates, language certificates and the APS certificate,** if you needed one

Pack the originals (or copies, if the originals stay at home) of these in your hand bag. Your university, bank and the local authorities in Germany will ask for many of the same papers in your first weeks. Keep scans on your phone and in your email too.

## What German customs lets students bring

German customs (Zoll) has a page just for students (read 23 Nov 2026). Students who come to Germany from outside the EU to study full time can bring these free of import duty and import VAT:

- **Household effects:** bed linen, household linen, underwear and clothing (they need not be used)
- **Study materials:** objects and instruments students normally use for their studies, such as **computers and printers**
- **Used furniture** that is the usual furnishing for a student's room

They must be for your own use, not in quantities that look commercial. New goods ordered online from outside the EU, or sent by post by someone else, don't get this relief.

**How to declare:** at airports with red and green exits, use the green exit ("Nothing to declare") if these goods are worth €430 or less, or the red exit ("Goods to declare") and say so if they're worth more.

## Indian food: the EU rules

- **No meat or dairy products** from outside the EU, says the EU's Your Europe site (read 21 Nov 2026 for our [Europe packing list](europe-packing-list.html)). That means no ghee, paneer, khoya or milk sweets. Powdered baby milk and special medical food are exceptions.
- **You can bring a limited amount of fruit, vegetables, eggs, honey and fish.**
- **German customs** says strict animal health rules apply to meat, fish and milk products, even for your own use, and to parcels sent by post.
- **Sealed spices, masalas, rice, pulses and dry snacks** without meat or milk are what most students pack. Keep them in shop packs with labels.

## Medicines

From German customs' page on medicines (read 23 Nov 2026):

- **You can bring medicines for your usual personal needs,** up to **three months' supply** of the recommended dose of each.
- **Food supplements, vitamins and herbal medicines** that are freely sold in India may count as medicines in Germany.
- **Medicines that fall under narcotics law** need more: arriving from outside the Schengen area, carry a certificate from your doctor in several languages with the doses, the active ingredient and the length of your stay, authenticated by the health authority in India.
- **Keep medicines in your cabin bag,** in their original packs, with the prescription. See [can I carry medicines on a flight?](../baggage/can-i-carry/medicines.html)

## Baggage allowance for students

- **Air India student fare:** on routes where bags are counted by piece, such as Europe, Economy and Premium Economy get **2 bags of 23 kg each**, says Air India's student page (read 23 Nov 2026). Choose "Student" as the concession type when you book. International student fares are for full-time students aged 12 to 30, and you must show a student ID, student visa or acceptance letter at the airport if asked.
- **Normal fares:** [Lufthansa](../baggage/lufthansa.html) gives 1 bag of 23 kg in Economy (none on Economy Light), with an 8 kg cabin bag of 55 x 40 x 23 cm plus a small personal item, and Air India 1 bag of 23 kg on Value and 2 on Classic and Flex. Emirates, Qatar Airways and [Turkish Airlines](../baggage/turkish-airlines.html) depend on your fare. See our [international baggage rules](../baggage/international.html), and the [excess baggage calculator](../tools/bag-weight.html) if you'll pay for more.
- **Each bag can usually be up to 158 cm in total.** Air India's page also limits both Economy bags together to 271 cm. See [trolley bag size for international travel](trolley-bag-size-for-international-travel.html).
- **Check your cabin bag** with the [cabin bag checker](../baggage/checker.html).

## Which bags to take

- **Large bag:** one of 75–79 cm that's listed within 158 cm.
- **Second bag:** a medium bag of 65–69 cm. On Air India, keep both bags together within 271 cm, so pair it with a large bag of about 152 cm or less.
- **Trains and stairs:** you may carry your bags on trains and up stairs in old buildings. Four wheels and a light bag help.
- **Buying a set?** Check the large bag's listed total in our [trolley bag set of 3 guide](trolley-bag-set-of-3.html), and read the [28 inch trolley guide](28-inch-trolley-bag-size.html).

## What to wear in Germany

German winters are cold, often grey and wet, with snow in some places, and days are short. Check the forecast for your city in the week before you fly.

- **Layers:** thermals, two sweaters or fleeces, and warm trousers. Rooms, trams and libraries are heated.
- **A warm, waterproof jacket with a hood.** Wear it on the plane.
- **A cap, gloves and a scarf** for winter mornings.
- **Waterproof shoes with a good grip,** worn in before you fly.
- **One formal outfit** for presentations and interviews.

## Laptop, phone, plugs and power bank

- **Germany uses 230 V and Type C and F plugs.** Indian two-pin plugs often fit Type C sockets, but three-pin plugs don't, so take an adapter. See our [power bank and travel adapter guide](power-bank-and-travel-adapter-guide.html).
- **The voltage is the same as India's,** so Indian chargers and hair dryers work with just a plug adapter.
- **Power bank:** in your cabin bag only, never in check-in. See [can I carry a power bank?](../baggage/can-i-carry/power-bank.html)

## What not to pack

- **Ghee, paneer, milk sweets and anything with meat**
- **More than three months of medicines,** or narcotic medicines without the certificate
- **A 32 inch bag** that's over 158 cm: see the [32 inch trolley guide](32-inch-trolley-bag-size.html)
- **Too many light cotton clothes**
- **Power banks in check-in**

## Cash

- **€10,000 or more** in cash, arriving from outside the EU, must be declared to customs in writing, says German customs.
- **Cash from India:** see [how much cash you can carry abroad](../baggage/customs/cash-and-currency.html).

## Questions

### What should Indian students take to Germany?

Your passport with the D visa, your admission letter, blocked account confirmation and health insurance papers in your hand bag, your medicines with the prescription, thermals, sweaters, a warm waterproof jacket, waterproof shoes, a laptop with a Type C adapter, and a power bank in your cabin bag. Pack a large trolley within 158 cm and 23 kg.

### Do students pay customs duty on a laptop in Germany?

German customs says students from outside the EU who come to study full time can bring study materials, such as computers and printers, and their clothes and linen free of import duty and VAT. If these are worth more than €430, use the red exit and declare them.

### Can I take Indian food to Germany?

Not meat or dairy products: the EU doesn't let travellers from outside the EU bring them in, so no ghee, paneer or milk sweets. Sealed spices, rice, pulses and dry snacks without meat or milk are what most students pack.

### How many months of medicines can I take to Germany?

Up to three months' supply for your personal needs, says German customs. Medicines with narcotics also need a doctor's certificate authenticated by the health authority in India.

### How much is the blocked account for Germany?

The German Missions in India's study visa page gives €11,904 for the first year, with up to €992 a month to withdraw (page dated 16 Feb 2026). Check the page before you apply, as the amount can change.

### How much baggage can students take to Germany?

Air India's student fare gives 2 bags of 23 kg in Economy on piece-based routes such as Europe. On a normal Lufthansa Economy fare, it's 1 bag of 23 kg (none on Economy Light).

### Do Indian plugs work in Germany?

Two-pin Indian plugs often fit Germany's Type C sockets, but three-pin ones don't. The voltage is 230 V, like India, so a plug adapter is all you need.
