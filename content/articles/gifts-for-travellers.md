## How to choose a gift for a traveller

- **Think about how they travel.** Flights, trains and road trips need different things. A luggage scale suits flyers; a bottle and a neck pillow suit trains.
- **Small things get used most.** Most travellers already own a suitcase, but few have a good toiletry bag or a case for chargers. Our guides compare [toiletry bags](toiletry-bag-for-travel.html), [packing cubes](packing-cubes.html), [neck pillows](neck-pillow-for-flights.html), [passport holders](passport-holder.html) and [TSA locks](tsa-lock.html).
- **Check bag sizes against their airline.** A cabin bag that's too big gets sent to check-in. Our [cabin bag checker](../baggage/checker.html) compares any size with Indian airlines' limits.
- **Remember the 100 ml rule.** Liquids in a cabin bag must be in bottles of 100 ml or less, so small bottles make good add-ons.
- **Order early.** Deliveries can take longer around Christmas and New Year. Check the delivery date before you pay.

## Questions

### What is a good gift for someone who travels a lot?

Small things they use on every trip: a toiletry bag, a gadget organiser, a luggage scale or a neck pillow. Most frequent travellers already own a suitcase, so these are less likely to be repeats.

### What is a good gift for a man who travels a lot?

A weekender duffle, a leather wallet set, a toiletry bag or a gadget organiser. If he flies for work, a cabin trolley with a laptop section is a bigger gift that gets used every trip.

### What is a good gift for a woman who travels a lot?

A light crossbody bag for sightseeing, packing cubes, a toiletry bag or travel-size skincare under 100 ml.

### What is a good gift for a frequent flyer?

A luggage scale, so they can weigh bags at home. A passport holder is good if they fly abroad, and a toiletry bag suits anyone who stays in hotels.

### Is a cabin bag a good gift?

Yes, if it fits their airline. Many "55 cm" cabin trolleys are too wide for IndiGo's 55 x 35 x 25 cm limit, so check the listed size first. The Safari trolley above is within it.
