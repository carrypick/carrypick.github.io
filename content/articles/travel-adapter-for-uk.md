The UK uses **type G** sockets, three rectangular pins, at **230 V and 50 Hz**. That's the same voltage as India, so your Indian chargers work, but the plug doesn't fit. You need a plug adapter, not a converter.

## Quick answer

- **Plug type:** G (BS 1363), three rectangular pins with a fuse in the plug. Ireland uses the same plug.
- **Voltage:** 230 V and 50 Hz. UK law sets these values for homes.
- **From India:** a type G adapter for each charger, or one universal adapter.
- **From the USA or Canada:** an adapter, plus chargers marked **100-240V**. 120 V-only appliances need a converter.
- **From Europe:** an adapter. The voltage is the same.

## Questions

### Which plug is used in the UK?

Type G: three rectangular pins in a triangle, with a fuse inside the plug. It runs at 230 V and 50 Hz.

### Do Indian plugs work in the UK?

No. Indian 2-pin and 3-pin plugs don't fit UK sockets. Your Indian chargers do work on UK voltage with a plug adapter.

### Do I need a voltage converter for the UK from India?

No. The UK and India both use 230 V at 50 Hz.

### Is the UK plug the same as Dubai and Singapore?

Yes. The UK, Ireland, the UAE, Singapore, Malaysia and Hong Kong all use type G.

### Which adapter do I need for the UK from the USA?

A US-to-UK (type G) adapter or a universal one. The UK runs at 230 V, so only bring chargers marked 100-240V.

### Can I use a European adapter in the UK?

No. European type C, E and F plugs don't fit UK sockets. You need a type G adapter.
