## How to choose a gift for a colleague

- **Keep it useful, not personal.** Desk and travel gifts are safe. Skip clothes and anything with a size.
- **Match the gift to how well you know them.** A bottle or organiser for someone you sit near. A watch or a bag from the whole team for a wedding or a promotion.
- **Unisex picks are easiest.** A steel bottle, a gadget organiser, a black watch or a shared perfume box suit anyone.
- **Check office rules.** Some companies set a limit on gifts, especially from clients or juniors to seniors.
- **Pool money for big days.** A team gift and one card signed by everyone feels warmer than ten small gifts.
