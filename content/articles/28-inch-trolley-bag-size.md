A 28 inch trolley bag is **71.1 cm** tall. In India, brands sell large bags at 75–80 cm, and some call a 75 cm bag "28 inch". Before you buy one, check its total size: **most large trolleys we checked are listed over the 158 cm limit** that most Indian airlines set for a checked bag.

## Quick answer

- **28 inches is 71.1 cm.** A 75 cm bag is really 29.5 inches, and a 79 cm bag is 31.1 inches.
- **It's a check-in bag** for long trips or families.
- **26 of the 31 large models we checked are listed over 158 cm** in total (length + width + height).
- **On a 15 kg fare, you may not need one.** A [medium trolley](24-inch-trolley-bag-size.html) is lighter and easier to handle.

How much does each size hold? See [trolley bag capacity in kg and litres](trolley-bag-capacity-kg-litres.html).

## The 158 cm limit

IndiGo, Air India, Akasa Air and SpiceJet all limit a checked bag to **158 cm in total**. Air India Express uses a different limit: 81 x 119 x 119 cm. A single checked bag also can't weigh more than 32 kg on most of these airlines. Rules vary, so see our [baggage rules](../baggage/index.html).

Airline staff don't measure every bag, but they can. If you're close to the limit, don't overfill the bag, because a bulging shell or an open expansion zip adds centimetres.

## Do you really need a large trolley?

- **On a 15 kg fare, probably not.** A 4.3 kg bag leaves you 10.7 kg, and a medium trolley can usually hold that.
- **With 20–25 kg or more, it starts to make sense,** for example on some Air India fares and many [international flights](../baggage/international.html).
- **For a family,** one large bag can hold things for two people, but it can only be as heavy as one person's allowance allows, unless your airline lets you pool baggage. Ask your airline.

## Questions

### What is a 28 inch trolley bag in cm?

28 inches is 71.1 cm. Some brands in India also use "28 inch" for bags listed at 75–76 cm, so check the size in cm.

### Is a 28 inch trolley allowed on flights?

Yes, as a check-in bag. It's too big for the cabin. Most Indian airlines limit a checked bag to 158 cm in total, and many large trolleys are listed over that, so add up all three sides first.

### How many days is a 28 inch trolley good for?

About 8–12 days, or longer for a family. On a 15 kg fare, you'll probably reach the weight limit long before the bag is full.

### Which large trolley bags are within 158 cm?

Of the 31 models we checked on 6 Nov 2026, these were listed at 158 cm or less: Skybags Swirl 75 cm, L'Attitude Meridian 75 cm, Safari Astra Neo 76 cm, Safari Crescent 76 cm and American Tourister Liftoff+ 79 cm.
