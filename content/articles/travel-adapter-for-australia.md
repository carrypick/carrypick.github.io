Australia and New Zealand use **type I** sockets: two flat pins set at an angle, with a flat earth pin below. The supply is **230 V and 50 Hz**, the same as India. Your Indian chargers work, but the plug doesn't fit, so take an adapter.

## Quick answer

- **Plug type:** I (AS/NZS 3112). Fiji and China use the same shape.
- **Voltage:** 230 V and 50 Hz. No converter needed from India.
- **From India:** a universal adapter with an Australia plug.
- **From the USA or Canada:** an adapter, and chargers marked **100-240V**.
- **From the UK or Europe:** an adapter. Voltage is the same.

## Questions

### Which plug is used in Australia?

Type I: two flat pins in a V shape and an earth pin, at 230 V and 50 Hz.

### Do Indian plugs work in Australia?

No. Indian plugs don't fit type I sockets. Indian chargers and appliances work on Australian voltage with a plug adapter.

### Do I need a converter for Australia from India?

No. Australia and India both use 230 V and 50 Hz.

### Is the Australian plug the same as New Zealand?

Yes. Both use type I at 230 V. See our [New Zealand trip guide](../trips/new-zealand-trip-from-india.html).

### Is the Australian plug the same as China?

The shape is the same (type I). China also uses type A.
