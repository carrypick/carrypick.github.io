## 15.6 inch laptop size in cm

The screen is 15.6 inches (39.6 cm) across the diagonal. The laptop itself is usually:

- **Width:** about 35–36 cm
- **Depth:** about 23–25 cm
- **Thickness:** about 1.8–2.5 cm, and up to 3 cm for gaming laptops

Gaming laptops and older models can be wider and thicker. See typical sizes for 13 to 17 inch laptops in our [laptop backpack tool](../tools/laptop-backpack.html).

## How to measure your laptop

1. Close the laptop and put it on a table.
2. Measure the **width** (left to right) and the **depth** (front to back) in cm with a tape or ruler.
3. Measure the **thickness** at the thickest point, often at the back.
4. Add about **1–2 cm** to the width and depth for an easy fit. That's the sleeve size to look for.

The laptop's exact size is also on the maker's website, under "dimensions".

## 15.6 inch laptop bag size in litres

Litres tell you the space for everything else, not the laptop:

- **20–25 L:** laptop, charger, a notebook and a water bottle. A slim office bag.
- **25–30 L:** add a lunch box and a jacket. Good for college.
- **30–40 L:** add clothes for a night or two. Good for work trips.

## Questions

### Will a 15.6 inch laptop fit in a 15 inch bag?

Sometimes, but it can be tight. Measure your laptop's width: if it's more than the sleeve's width, it won't fit. A bag for 15.6 inch or bigger is safer.

### What size bag do I need for a 15.6 inch laptop?

A backpack about 45–48 cm tall and 30–33 cm wide, with a sleeve that says 15.6 inch or bigger. 20–30 litres is enough for daily use.

### What is a 15.6 inch laptop size in cm?

Usually about 35–36 cm wide and 23–25 cm deep. The 15.6 inches (39.6 cm) is the screen's diagonal.

### Can a 15.6 inch laptop fit in a 16 or 17 inch bag?

Yes. A bigger sleeve fits it, though the laptop can move a little. A padded sleeve with a strap holds it in place.

### Is a 15.6 inch laptop bag good for a 14 inch laptop?

Yes. 14 inch laptops are smaller, so they fit easily in a 15.6 inch sleeve.
