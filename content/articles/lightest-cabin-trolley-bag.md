Airlines weigh the bag, not just your things. A hard cabin trolley usually weighs 2 to 3 kg empty, so on a 7 kg cabin allowance the bag can take more than a third of it. Here are our luggage picks sorted by the empty weight each Amazon.in listing states.

## Quick answer

- **Lightest cabin trolley:** 2 kg, the [American Tourister Splash 55 cm](../picks/B0B6BVXCCD.html). It leaves 5 kg of a 7 kg allowance.
- **Lightest that fits an airline by listed size:** 2.2 kg, the [American Tourister Liftoff+ Small](../picks/B0F5HGLC5Q.html), within Air India's 55 x 40 x 20 cm.
- **Lightest medium trolley:** 2.52 kg, the [Kamiliant Rock-Lite 68 cm](../picks/B0GNSKLF5Z.html).
- **Lightest large trolley:** 3.3 kg, the soft [it luggage Census 81 cm](../picks/B0BLNY575N.html). The lightest hard one is 3.5 kg.
- **Light isn't everything.** A light cabin bag that is too wide for your airline still gets sent to check-in.

## How much can you pack?

Take the bag's weight away from your allowance:

| Allowance | 2 kg bag | 2.5 kg bag | 3.5 kg bag | 4.5 kg bag |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 7 kg cabin | 5 kg | 4.5 kg | 3.5 kg | 2.5 kg |
| 15 kg check-in | 13 kg | 12.5 kg | 11.5 kg | 10.5 kg |
| 23 kg check-in | 21 kg | 20.5 kg | 19.5 kg | 18.5 kg |

Weigh the packed bag at home with a [luggage scale](luggage-scale.html), then check it against your fare with our [excess baggage calculator](../tools/bag-weight.html).

## What makes a bag light?

- **Material matters less than the model.** The lightest cabin bag above is polycarbonate, and the lightest medium bag is polypropylene. See [polycarbonate vs polypropylene](polycarbonate-vs-polypropylene-luggage.html).
- **Soft bags can be lighter in big sizes.** Our lightest large bag is soft. See [hard vs soft luggage](hard-vs-soft-luggage.html).
- **Wheels and extras add weight,** but in our picks 4-wheel bags weren't lighter than 8-wheel ones. See [4 wheel vs 8 wheel trolleys](4-wheel-vs-8-wheel-trolley.html).
- **Litres aren't weight.** A bigger bag tempts you to pack more, so a light large bag can still go over 15 kg.

## Questions

### Which is the lightest cabin trolley bag?

Of our picks, the American Tourister Splash 55 cm, listed at 2 kg. The Kamiliant Rock-Lite 55 cm is close at 2.03 kg. Neither fits IndiGo or Air India by its listed size.

### How heavy should a cabin bag be when empty?

Aim for 2.5 kg or less. On a 7 kg allowance that leaves at least 4.5 kg for your things. Our light picks are 2 to 2.7 kg.

### Is a lightweight trolley good for international travel?

Yes, if it has a lock you trust and a warranty that works abroad. Our light large pick, the American Tourister Liftoff+ 79 cm, lists a TSA lock and a 3-year international warranty. See [trolley bag size for international travel](trolley-bag-size-for-international-travel.html).

### Are soft bags lighter than hard ones?

Not always. Among our picks, the lightest large bag is soft, but the lightest cabin and medium bags are hard.

### Does the airline count the bag's weight?

Yes. The cabin and check-in limits are for the bag and everything in it.
