We have moved this guide into our **[luggage size chart](../baggage/luggage-size-chart.html)**, so the sizes and the advice on which one to buy are on one page.

In short: a **small cabin trolley** (about 55 cm) suits 2–4 days, a **medium trolley** (65–69 cm) about a week, and a **large trolley** (75–79 cm) 10 days or more. Your fare's baggage allowance matters more than the trip length, because the bag's own weight counts in it.

On the size chart you'll find:

- [Small, medium and large trolley sizes](../baggage/luggage-size-chart.html#small-medium-large) in cm and inches, with litres and empty weights
- [Which size you need](../baggage/luggage-size-chart.html#which-size), by trip length and by baggage allowance, and hard or soft
- [Cabin limits of each airline in inches](../baggage/luggage-size-chart.html#airline-limits) and [the 158 cm check-in limit](../baggage/luggage-size-chart.html#check-in-size)

For the cabin rules (7 kg, 115 cm and the small personal bag), see our [cabin bag guide](cabin-luggage-guide.html).
