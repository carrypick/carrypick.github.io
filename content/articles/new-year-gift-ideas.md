## How to choose

- **Think about what they already do.** A gift for a real habit, like travel or office work, gets used.
- **Choose a known brand for watches.** Titan, Sonata and Timex have service centres across India, so warranty claims are easier.
- **Check the size you click on.** One Amazon page can show several colours and sizes.
- **Order early.** Deliveries can take longer around the holidays, so check the delivery date before you pay.
- **Keep the invoice.** They'll need it for a warranty claim.

## Questions

### What is a good New Year gift for parents?

Pair watches are an easy choice, because you don't have to choose for one person. If they travel often, a cabin trolley or a luggage scale is useful too.

### What is a good small New Year gift for a colleague or friend?

Choose something practical for what they do. A luggage scale or packing cubes suit someone who flies often. A small sling suits someone who wants a lighter everyday bag.

### When should I order a New Year gift?

As early as you can, and check the delivery date shown on Amazon before you pay. Deliveries can take longer around the holidays.
