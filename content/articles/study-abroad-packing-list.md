Leaving India to study is a big move, and packing for it is not like packing for a holiday. You're carrying a year of your life in two bags, the documents decide whether you get through the border, and every country has its own rules on Indian food and medicines. Start with the list for your country, then use the common list below.

## Packing lists by country

| Country | Key documents in your hand bag | Indian food | Medicines | Plugs |

## In your hand bag, not your check-in bag

- **Your passport and visa,** and the letters your visa or university told you to carry. The US Study in the States site, for one, says not to put your original documents in checked baggage.
- **Copies of everything,** on paper and on your phone. Leave one set with your family.
- **Your medicines** in their original packs, with the prescription, and a few days' extra in case your bag is late.
- **Laptop, phone, chargers and power bank.** Power banks must go in your cabin bag.
- **One change of clothes** and a warm layer for arrival.
- **A card that works abroad** and a little local cash.

## Student baggage allowance

Some airlines give students more checked baggage, as their own pages say (read 23 Nov 2026):

- **Air India student fare:** 2 bags of 23 kg each in Economy and Premium Economy on routes where bags are counted by piece (such as the USA, Canada and Europe), and 10 kg more on routes where bags are weighed. For full-time students aged 12 to 30; choose "Student" when you book and carry a student ID, visa or acceptance letter.
- **Virgin Atlantic student offer:** 3 bags of 23 kg from Delhi, Mumbai or Bengaluru to London, Canada and the USA, on Economy Classic and Delight and Premium, outbound only. Show your student visa at check-in.
- **Other airlines:** see what your fare includes in our [international baggage rules](../baggage/international.html), and work out extra baggage charges with the [excess baggage calculator](../tools/bag-weight.html).

## Which bags to take

- **One large check-in trolley of 75–79 cm within 158 cm in total.** Most airlines flying from India use the 158 cm limit for each checked bag. See [trolley bag size for international travel](trolley-bag-size-for-international-travel.html).
- **A medium second bag** of 65–69 cm, if your fare includes two bags. On Air India, both Economy bags together must be within 271 cm.
- **A cabin bag or laptop backpack** within your airline's limit. Check it with the [cabin bag checker](../baggage/checker.html).
- **Don't buy a 32 inch bag:** almost all are over 158 cm. See our [28 inch](28-inch-trolley-bag-size.html) and [32 inch](32-inch-trolley-bag-size.html) trolley guides, and the [trolley bag set of 3 guide](trolley-bag-set-of-3.html).

## Clothes

- **Pack for your first two or three weeks,** not the whole year. You can buy everyday clothes and a heavy winter coat after you arrive, once you know the local weather.
- **Thermals, two sweaters or fleeces, and a warm jacket** for the USA, the UK, Canada and Germany in winter. Wear the bulky jacket on the plane.
- **Waterproof shoes with a good grip,** worn in before you fly.
- **One formal outfit** for presentations and interviews.
- **Australia's seasons are the opposite of India's:** summer clothes for a February start, warm clothes for July.

## What not to pack

- **Meat products, and milk products for the UK and Germany**
- **Fresh fruit and homemade food for Australia,** and undeclared food anywhere
- **More medicine than the country allows** (usually about 3 months)
- **A bag over 158 cm, or one heavier than 23 kg** on a piece fare
- **Power banks in check-in**
- **Too many clothes**

## Questions

### What should Indian students pack when going abroad?

Your passport, visa and university papers in your hand bag, your medicines with the prescription, clothes for the first few weeks, a laptop with a universal adapter, a power bank in your cabin bag, and a card that works abroad. Pack one large trolley within 158 cm and 23 kg, a medium bag and a laptop backpack.

### What documents should I carry in my hand luggage when going abroad to study?

Your passport and visa, and the papers your visa and university told you to carry: for example Form I-20 for the USA, your CAS for the UK, the port of entry letter for Canada, your CoE and health cover for Australia, and your admission and blocked account papers for Germany. Keep copies with your family.

### How much luggage can I carry as an Indian student?

On Air India's student fare, 2 bags of 23 kg in Economy to piece-based destinations such as the USA, Canada and Europe. Virgin Atlantic's student offer gives 3 bags of 23 kg to London, Canada and the USA. Other airlines depend on your fare.

### Can I carry Indian food abroad as a student?

Some. The USA and Canada ask you to declare all food. The UK and the EU (including Germany) don't allow meat or milk products from India. Australia asks you to declare almost all food and doesn't allow fresh fruit or homemade food. Sealed spices are the safest to pack.

### What should I not pack for study abroad?

Meat products, milk products for the UK and Germany, fresh fruit and homemade food for Australia, power banks in your check-in bag, a suitcase over 158 cm, and more clothes than you need for the first weeks.
