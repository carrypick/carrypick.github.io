## How to choose a Christmas gift for a teenager

- **Choose something they'll use in front of friends.** A watch, sunglasses or a bag gets seen every day. A showpiece stays in a cupboard.
- **Keep the look simple.** Plain black, a classic wayfarer or a small sling suit most tastes. Skip loud prints unless you know they like them.
- **Skip clothes in a size you'd guess.** Teens grow fast. Caps, gloves, bags and watches fit everyone.
- **Ask a sibling or a parent.** They'll know if the teen already has a watch or wants a trimmer.
- **Choose a known brand.** Fastrack, Philips, Skybags and Vincent Chase are easy to find in India, and warranty claims are easier. Keep the invoice.
- **Order by mid-December.** Deliveries can take longer in Christmas week, so check the delivery date before you pay.

## Questions

### What is a good Christmas gift for a teenage boy?

A simple watch like the Fastrack black, a first beard trimmer, wayfarer or sports sunglasses, or a college backpack. A power bank is useful for any teen with a phone.

### What is a good Christmas gift for a teenage girl?

A small sling bag, a watch with a pink dial, cat-eye or round sunglasses, a perfume set with a few scents to try, or a jewellery case for earrings.

### What is a good Christmas gift for teenagers, boys and girls alike?

A unisex watch, a power bank, a small padded sling, Crocs clogs or a fleece-lined beanie. These suit any teen and don't need a size.

### Should I buy clothes for a teenager?

Only if you know their size and style. Teens grow fast and have firm tastes, so a watch, sunglasses or a bag is safer.

### When should I order a Christmas gift?

By mid-December. Check the delivery date shown on Amazon before you pay, because deliveries can take longer in Christmas week.
