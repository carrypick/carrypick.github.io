A luggage scale is a small hand-held weighing machine: you hook your bag on, lift it, and read the weight. It tells you at home whether your bag is within your airline's limit, so you can move a few things out **before** the check-in counter charges you for every extra kilo.

## Quick answer

- **Why:** on flights within India the airport rate is about **₹775 to ₹850 per extra kg**, by the airlines' own fee pages. A scale costs less than one of those kilos at most counters.
- **How:** loop the strap through the bag's handle, hold the scale, lift the bag off the floor with both hands, and wait until the reading stops (data lock holds it on the screen).
- **Tare:** press it to zero the scale with something already hanging on it, like a strap or a shopping bag.
- **Leave a margin:** pack to about **0.5 kg under** your limit. A hand-held scale and the airport scale won't agree to the gram.
- **Limits:** most Indian domestic fares give **15 kg check-in and 7 kg cabin**. Abroad it's often **20 to 35 kg**, or **23 kg per bag**. See the table below.

## Why weigh your bag at home?

- **The airport charges more than prepaid kilos.** IndiGo, Akasa Air and SpiceJet list prepaid slabs online (for example 3 kg for about ₹2,085 to ₹2,250), and a higher per-kg rate at the airport. See [extra baggage charges by airline](../baggage/extra-baggage-charges.html).
- **Bags fill up before you notice.** A 65 cm trolley can hold well over 15 kg of clothes and shoes. See [how many kg a trolley bag holds](trolley-bag-capacity-kg-litres.html).
- **The trip home is heavier.** Shopping, sweets and gifts add up. Pack the scale and weigh again at the hotel.
- **Cabin bags are weighed too.** Most airlines allow 7 kg in the cabin, and a heavy cabin bag may have to be checked in.

## How to use a luggage scale

1. **Switch it on** and wait for it to show 0.0. Choose **kg** if it shows lb.
2. **Loop the strap** through your bag's top or side handle and close the hook or buckle. Use the handle, not the telescopic trolley handle.
3. **Hold the scale's handle** with both hands, so the bag hangs straight below it.
4. **Lift slowly** until the bag is fully off the floor. Bend your knees, not your back: 15 to 23 kg is heavy to lift with straight arms.
5. **Keep it still** for 2 or 3 seconds. Most digital scales beep or flash and then **lock** the reading.
6. **Put the bag down** and read the screen. Data lock keeps the number there, so you don't have to read it mid-lift.

Weigh twice. If the two readings are more than about 0.2 kg apart, the bag was swinging or touching the floor.

### What is tare on a luggage scale?

**Tare** sets the scale back to zero with something already hanging on it. Hang an empty shopping bag or a long strap, press tare, then add your things: the screen shows only what's inside. You don't need tare for a normal suitcase: just weigh the whole bag, because the airline counts the bag too.

### How accurate is a luggage scale?

Our two picks show the weight in **0.1 kg** (GoTrippin) and **10 g** (Destinio) steps, as listed. That's how finely they display, not a promise that they match the airport scale. Airport scales and hand-held ones can differ a little, and a swinging bag adds error. Keep a margin of about **0.5 kg** below your limit, and more on a strict fare.

Low batteries can give wrong readings. Most screens show a low-battery sign; change the batteries when you see it.

### Can I weigh luggage without lifting it?

Yes, with a bathroom scale: weigh yourself, then weigh yourself holding the bag, and subtract. It's fine once in a while, but hard with a 23 kg bag and less exact. A hanging scale is easier and you can take it on the trip.

Two kinds of allowance:

- **Weight concept** (Emirates, Qatar, Singapore Airlines to and from India): a total in kg, split across bags as you like. Weigh every bag and add them up.
- **Piece concept** (British Airways, Lufthansa, Thai, Cathay): each bag up to 23 kg. A 25 kg bag is overweight even if your second bag is light, so weigh each one.

## What to look for in a luggage scale

- **Capacity:** 50 kg is enough for any single bag an airline takes (most stop at 32 kg).
- **Strap or hook:** a strap fits round any handle; a hook is quicker on a thin handle. The Destinio comes with both.
- **Data lock and backlit screen:** you can read the weight after you put the bag down, and in a dim hotel room.
- **Weight of the scale itself:** 150 g or 250 g matters little at home, a bit more in a cabin bag.
- **Batteries:** the Destinio takes 2 AAA batteries, which you can buy anywhere. The GoTrippin listing says its battery is included but doesn't name the type.

## Questions

### How do I use a luggage weighing scale?

Switch it on, loop the strap through the bag's handle, hold the scale and lift the bag off the floor with both hands. Keep it still until the reading locks, then put the bag down and read the screen.

### How do I use a luggage scale with a hook?

Hang the bag's handle on the hook so it sits in the deepest part of the curve, then lift as above. For a thick handle, use the strap instead.

### Is a luggage scale accurate?

Close enough to stay inside your limit if you leave about 0.5 kg spare. Our picks display 0.1 kg and 10 g steps, as listed. Hold the bag still and change weak batteries for the best reading.

### What does tare mean on a luggage scale?

It sets the scale to zero with something already hanging on it, like an empty bag or a strap, so the screen shows only what you add.

### Can I carry a luggage scale in my cabin bag?

Yes. It's a small electronic device that runs on ordinary batteries. Keep it in your cabin bag or handbag so you can weigh your bags at the hotel before you fly home.

### What is the luggage weight limit on IndiGo?

15 kg check-in in one bag and a 7 kg cabin bag on Saver and Flexi Plus fares. Lite fares have no free check-in bag. See [IndiGo baggage rules](../baggage/indigo.html).

### What is the luggage limit on Indian trains?

It depends on the class, from 35 kg in Second Class to 70 kg in AC First, by Indian Railways' rules. See [train luggage rules](../baggage/train-luggage-rules.html).

### What can I do if my bag is overweight?

Move heavy things to your cabin bag (within 7 kg), wear your jacket and heaviest shoes, or buy prepaid extra kilos online, which costs less than paying at the airport. Our [excess baggage calculator](../tools/bag-weight.html) shows the fee.
