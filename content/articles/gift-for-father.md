## How to choose a gift for your dad

- **Replace something old.** Look at his watch strap, his wallet and his belt. The most worn one is your gift.
- **Make it easy to use.** A large, clear dial, a date window and slip-on shoes without laces.
- **Choose a known brand.** Casio, Titan and Skechers have service centres and stores across India, so a warranty claim is easier. Keep the invoice.
- **Shoes need his size.** Check an old pair, or choose a watch, a wallet or a belt.
- **For a father-in-law, keep it safe.** A known-brand watch, a wallet, a perfume set or a steel bottle.
- **Father's Day 2027 is Sunday 20 June.** Order a few days early so it arrives in time.

- **From a son:** "Papa, everything good in me, I learned from you. Thank you for every sacrifice you never talked about."
- **From a daughter:** "To my first hero. Thank you for always believing in me."

- **Father-in-law:** "Thank you for treating me like your own. Lucky to have you in my life."

## Questions

### What is the best gift for dad?

Something that replaces what he's used for years: a Casio or Titan watch, a leather wallet with lots of card slots, an auto-lock belt or slip-on walking shoes.

### What should I gift my father on his birthday?

A watch with a date window, a leather wallet or a grooming kit. See our [birthday gift ideas](birthday-gift-ideas.html#for-your-father) for more, with lines for the card.

### What is a good gift for dad from a daughter?

A watch he'll wear every day, or walking shoes for his morning walk, plus a card with one memory from your childhood.

### What can I gift my father on his retirement?

A guidebook for a trip he wants to take, pair watches for him and your mom, or a steel bottle for his walks. More in our [retirement gifts](farewell-gift-for-colleague.html#retirement-gifts-for-a-colleague).

### What can I gift my father-in-law?

A known-brand gift that's useful: a Titan watch, a leather wallet, a perfume set or a steel bottle. Skip clothes unless you know his size.
