## How to choose a Christmas gift for grandparents

- **Make it easy to use.** A large, clear watch dial, shoes that slip on without laces, and a bottle with a simple flip lid.
- **Warm is always welcome.** Indian homes get cold in December, and thermals and winter caps get worn every morning until March.
- **Choose something to share with the grandkids.** A story book or a family game brings everyone together over the holidays.
- **One gift for both works well.** Pair watches or matching thermal sets are a gift to both of them.
- **Skip gadgets that need an app.** Most grandparents prefer things that just work.
- **Order by mid-December.** Deliveries can take longer in Christmas week, so check the delivery date before you pay.

## Questions

### What is a good Christmas gift for grandparents?

Pair watches like the Titan Bandhan are a gift for both. A thermal set, a winter cap set, slip-on walking shoes or a steel bottle are useful gifts they'll use every day.

### What should I give grandparents who have everything?

Something they use up or wear out: thermals, a winter cap set, walking shoes, or a guidebook for a trip they want to take. A story book or game to share with the grandkids also works well.

### What can grandkids give their grandparents for Christmas?

A story book to read together, like the Panchatantra collection, or a family board game like Sequence. Add a handmade card: grandparents often keep those for years.

### What is a good Christmas gift for my grandmother?

A thermal set for winter, comfortable pull-on walking shoes, a Titan Karishma watch, or a fragrance-free moisturiser for dry winter skin.

### What is a good Christmas gift for my grandfather?

A Casio watch with a large dial and a date window, a cotton thermal set, Skechers slip-on walking shoes or a leather wallet.

### When should I order a Christmas gift?

By mid-December. Check the delivery date shown on Amazon before you pay, because deliveries can take longer in Christmas week.
