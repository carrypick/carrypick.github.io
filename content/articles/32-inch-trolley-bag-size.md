A 32 inch trolley bag is **81.3 cm** tall. That's the biggest size most brands make. In India, the largest bags are 79–82 cm, and almost every one we checked is **over the 158 cm total** that most Indian airlines allow for a checked bag.

## Quick answer

- **32 inches is 81.3 cm.** 30 inches is 76.2 cm.
- **It's a check-in bag only.** It's far too big for the cabin.
- **None of the 11 bags of 79–82 cm we read on 15 Nov is within 158 cm.** Their totals (length + width + height) are 159–175 cm. The one exception we know of is the Liftoff+ 79 cm below.
- **Airlines with bigger limits take it,** such as Emirates (203 cm) and Qatar Airways (300 cm) on flights from India.
- **On a 15 kg fare, it's too much bag.** A typical one weighs 4.15 kg empty, so only 10.85 kg is left for your things.

## Is a 32 inch trolley allowed in flight?

Yes, as a checked bag, but only where the airline's size limit is big enough. A typical 32 inch bag is about 167 cm in total. These are the limits for one checked bag in Economy, from our [baggage rules](../baggage/index.html) and [international baggage](../baggage/international.html) pages:

| Airline | Limit for one checked bag | A 167 cm bag |
|---|---|---|
| IndiGo, Air India, Akasa Air, SpiceJet (domestic) | 158 cm in total | Too big |
| Air India Express | 81 x 119 x 119 cm | Fits by size |
| IndiGo and Air India (international) | 158 cm in total | Too big |
| Air Arabia, Gulf Air, Kuwait Airways, Oman Air, Saudia | 158 cm in total | Too big |
| Emirates, flydubai | 203 cm in total | Fits |
| Qatar Airways | 300 cm in total | Fits |

A bag that's too big isn't always refused, but you can be asked to pay. Air India and Akasa Air list an oversize charge for a bag over 158 cm on domestic flights: see [extra baggage charges](../baggage/extra-baggage-charges.html). Most airlines also cap one bag at **32 kg**, however big it is.

## What about 30 inch?

A 30 inch bag is **76.2 cm** tall. That's close to the 75–77 cm "large" size most Indian brands sell. Some of these are within 158 cm: on 6 Nov 2026, 5 of the 31 large models we read were. See them in our [28 inch trolley guide](28-inch-trolley-bag-size.html).

## How much can you pack?

The bag's own weight counts against your allowance:

| Your allowance | 3.3 kg bag | 4.15 kg bag | 5.05 kg bag |
|---|---|---|---|
| 15 kg (most domestic fares) | 11.7 kg | 10.85 kg | 9.95 kg |
| 23 kg (one piece abroad) | 19.7 kg | 18.85 kg | 17.95 kg |
| 32 kg (the most for one bag) | 28.7 kg | 27.85 kg | 26.95 kg |

A bag of 100 litres or more can easily hold over 23 kg, so weigh it at home with a [luggage scale](../picks/B07Q822CWX.html). On a 15 kg fare, a [medium trolley](24-inch-trolley-bag-size.html) is lighter and holds enough.

## Questions

### What is a 32 inch trolley bag in cm?

32 inches is 81.3 cm. The biggest trolleys sold in India are 79–82 cm tall, and brands usually list them in cm, not inches.

### Is a 32 inch trolley bag allowed in flight?

Only as a checked bag. Most Indian airlines limit one checked bag to 158 cm in total, and every 79–82 cm bag we read was over that. Emirates allows 203 cm and Qatar Airways 300 cm on flights from India.

### How many kg can a 32 inch trolley carry?

More than most fares allow. Bags this size list 102–138 litres. Your limit is your ticket: 15 kg on most domestic fares, often 23 kg per bag abroad, and never more than 32 kg for one bag.

### What is a 30 inch trolley bag in cm?

30 inches is 76.2 cm. It's close to the 75–77 cm large size sold in India, and some of those bags are within the 158 cm limit.

### Which is bigger, a 30 or 32 inch trolley?

A 32 inch trolley is about 5 cm taller. It's usually wider and deeper too, which is why it goes over 158 cm.
