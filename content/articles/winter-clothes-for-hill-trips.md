You don't need expensive clothes for a winter trip to the hills, but you do need the right layers. Nights in Manali can fall below −5 °C, while a sunny afternoon can feel mild, so one thick jacket isn't enough. You need clothes you can add and take off.

## Dress in three layers

1. **Thermals, next to your skin.** They keep warm air close to your body.
2. **A sweater or fleece in the middle.** This is what holds in most of the heat. You probably own one already.
3. **A jacket on the outside.** It stops wind, snow and light rain.

Then add a cap, a neck warmer and gloves. Your ears and fingers get cold first.

Take a layer off before you start to sweat. Wet clothes make you cold once you stop moving.

## Cotton or synthetic thermals?

- **Cotton thermals** are soft and fine for sightseeing, cold nights and sleeping.
- **For hard treks,** cotton holds sweat and stays wet. Outdoor guides suggest wool or synthetic base layers instead.
