**The short answer:** dress in three layers you can add and take off: thermals next to your skin, a sweater or fleece, and a jacket that stops the wind. Add a cap, gloves, a neck warmer and warm socks. How warm the jacket needs to be depends on where you go: nights in Gulmarg average below −4 °C in December, while Ooty's nights stay around 7 °C.

## How cold it gets, and what to wear

| Place | December, day / night | January, day / night | What to wear |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ooty | About 21 °C / 7 °C | About 21 °C / 6 °C | A sweater or fleece and a light jacket for the evening. Thermals are optional |
| Darjeeling | About 14 °C / 4 °C | About 11 °C / 2 °C | Thermals at night and for Tiger Hill at dawn, a sweater and a padded jacket |
| Shimla | About 12 °C / 4 °C | About 9 °C / 2 °C | Thermals, a sweater and a padded jacket. Waterproof shoes if it snows |
| Srinagar | About 10 °C / −1 °C | About 7 °C / −2 °C | All three layers every day, plus a cap and gloves |
| Manali | About −3 to 17 °C | About −6 to 16 °C | All three layers. Nights can fall below −5 °C |
| Gulmarg | About 4 °C / −5 °C | About 1 °C / −8 °C | Thermals, a fleece and a warm, windproof jacket, plus snow boots and waterproof gloves |

Ooty, Darjeeling, Shimla, Srinagar and Gulmarg figures are India Meteorological Department (IMD) long-term averages, rounded, as given in our trip guides. Manali's ranges are from Incredible India, and the −5 °C is from Himachal Tourism. Averages hide cold snaps: Shimla's coldest December night on record was −12.2 °C. Check the forecast a few days before you go.

## The three layers

| Layer | What it does | Choose | How many to pack for a week |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Thermals (base layer) | Keeps warm air close to your skin | Snug, but not tight. Cotton for sightseeing and sleeping; wool or synthetic for treks | 2 sets: wear one, wash or air one |
| 2. Sweater or fleece (middle) | Holds in most of the heat | A fleece or a wool sweater. You probably own one already | 1–2 |
| 3. Jacket (outer) | Stops wind, snow and light rain | A padded jacket for hill towns; a down or warmer synthetic jacket for deep snow | 1, worn on the journey |
| Cap, neck warmer, gloves | Your ears and fingers get cold first | Waterproof gloves if you'll touch snow | 1 set |
| Socks | Cold feet make you feel cold all over | Wool or thick socks, with room in your shoes | 1 pair a day, or wash and dry |

**Take a layer off before you start to sweat.** Wet clothes make you cold once you stop moving.

## Cotton or synthetic thermals?

- **Cotton thermals** are soft and fine for sightseeing, cold nights and sleeping.
- **For hard treks,** cotton holds sweat and stays wet. Outdoor guides suggest wool or synthetic base layers instead.
- **Fit:** thermals work best close to the skin, so choose a snug fit that still lets you move. Loose thermals let warm air escape.

## Questions

### What should I wear in Manali in December?

Three layers every day: thermals, a sweater or fleece, and a padded or warmer jacket, plus a cap, gloves and warm socks. Incredible India gives about −3 to 17 °C for December, and nights can fall below −5 °C. See [what to wear in Manali in December](what-to-wear-in-manali-in-december.html) for more detail.

### What should I wear in Shimla in December?

IMD's averages are about 12 °C by day and 4 °C at night. Wear thermals in the evening, a sweater, and a padded jacket. If snow is forecast, add waterproof shoes and gloves.

### Is thermal wear necessary for a hill trip?

For nights near or below 0 °C, as in Manali, Srinagar and Gulmarg, yes: it's the lightest way to add warmth. In Ooty, where nights average about 7 °C in December, a sweater is usually enough.

### Should thermal wear be tight?

Snug, not tight. Thermals work by keeping warm air close to your skin, so a loose fit lets the warmth escape, but they shouldn't press or stop you moving.

### Cotton or polyester thermals: which is better?

Cotton for sightseeing and sleeping, because it's soft. Polyester, other synthetics or wool for treks and active days, because cotton holds sweat and stays wet.

### What should I wear in the snow?

Thermals, a fleece, and a warm, windproof jacket, with waterproof gloves, a cap, wool socks and waterproof shoes or boots with a grippy sole. Add sunglasses and sunscreen, because snow reflects sunlight.
