An office backpack has a different job from a college bag. It should look smart with formals, hold a laptop and charger safely, survive a monsoon commute and not stick out on a crowded metro. Here's how to choose one, and six picks compared by what each listing says.

## Quick answer

- **Size:** 20–30 litres. Big enough for a laptop, charger, lunch box and a jacket, slim enough to look neat.
- **Monsoon:** a **rain cover** protects best. "Water resistant" fabric handles a short shower, not a downpour.
- **Look:** dark colours, few outer straps, and **faux or vegan leather** trims look most formal.
- **Laptop sleeve:** match your laptop. Most office laptops are 14 or 15.6 inches.

## Waterproof vs water resistant: what to look for

Very few backpacks are truly **waterproof**. Listings use the words loosely, so here's what they usually mean:

- **Water resistant / water repellent:** the fabric is coated. Rain runs off for a few minutes, but water gets in through the zips in heavy rain.
- **Rain cover:** a stretchy cover, stored in a bottom pocket, that goes over the whole bag. This is the best protection for a laptop in an Indian monsoon.
- **Waterproof:** only trust it if the listing also says the zips are sealed. For a commute on a bike or a scooter, a rain cover is safer.

## Leather office backpacks

Real leather backpacks are heavy and don't like rain. Most "leather" office backpacks on Amazon.in are **faux leather** (PU) or **vegan leather** trims on nylon, which are lighter and easy to wipe clean.

- [Gear Classic 4](../picks/B0D87KZP1Z.html): water-resistant **faux leather** all over, teal and brown, 31 litres. The listing says hand wash only.
- [Mokobara Transit](../picks/B0B6CM781M.html): water-resistant **nylon with vegan leather** trims, 30 litres. It's heavy at 1.6 kg empty.

We haven't found a genuine-leather office backpack whose listing gives clear specs yet. For a leather wallet and belt to match, see our [wallet and belt guide](wallet-and-belt-buying-guide.html).

## Size and shape for the office

- **20–22 litres** for a laptop, charger and a few things. Slim bags like the [Tommy Hilfiger Joshua](../picks/B07N8GRWTZ.html) look best with formals.
- **28–30 litres** if you also carry a lunch box, a jacket or gym clothes.

## Features that matter at work

1. **A padded laptop sleeve** that stops above the bottom of the bag.
2. **A trolley or luggage sleeve** for work trips. Our [business trip packing list](business-trip-packing-list.html) covers what goes inside.
3. **A hidden back pocket** for your wallet and ID card on the metro. See our [anti-theft backpack guide](anti-theft-backpack.html).
4. **A front organiser** for cables, a pen and a power bank.
5. **A warranty** of at least 1 year, as zips fail first.

## Questions

### Which backpack is good for office use?

A slim 20–30 litre bag in a dark colour, with a padded laptop sleeve, a hidden pocket and a rain cover or water-resistant fabric. The Tommy Hilfiger Joshua, Mokobara Transit and Swiss Military 30L are our office picks.

### Is there a fully waterproof office backpack?

Few are. Most say water resistant, which handles a light shower. For heavy monsoon rain, choose a bag with a rain cover, like the Safari Forge or the American Tourister RON.

### Are leather backpacks good for office?

They look smart, but real leather is heavy and needs care in the rain. Faux or vegan leather looks similar, weighs less and wipes clean.

### What size backpack is best for the office?

20–30 litres for most people. Go bigger only if the bag is also your weekend or travel bag.

### Can I carry an office backpack as cabin baggage?

Yes, as your one cabin bag. Five of the six list a size within IndiGo's 55 x 35 x 25 cm limit; the Tommy Hilfiger doesn't list its size, but at 21 litres it's slim. Most office backpacks are too tall to count as the small personal item (often 40 x 30 cm or less). Check with our [cabin bag checker](../baggage/checker.html).
