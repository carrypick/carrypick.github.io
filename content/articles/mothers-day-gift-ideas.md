## How to choose a Mother's Day gift

- **Choose something she'll use every day.** A watch, a bag or walking shoes get used far more than a showpiece or a mug.
- **Look at what she makes do with.** A bag with a broken zip, a purse that's falling apart or worn-out chappals tells you the gift.
- **Make it easy to use.** A watch with a clear dial and a sliding clasp, and shoes that slip on without laces.
- **Shoes need her size.** Check an old pair, or choose a watch, a bag or an attar set instead.
- **Living in another city?** Order early and pick a delivery date before Sunday 9 May. Sunday delivery isn't available everywhere, so aim for Friday or Saturday.
- **Add your time.** Cook her favourite dish, take her out, or call her with the whole family. A gift with a plan means more.

## Questions

### When is Mother's Day 2027 in India?

Sunday 9 May 2027. In India, Mother's Day is the second Sunday of May, the same day as in the US, Canada and Australia. The UK marks Mothering Sunday on 7 March 2027 (three weeks before Easter), so check the date if your mom lives abroad.

### What is a good Mother's Day gift for an Indian mom?

Something she'll use every day and wouldn't buy for herself: a Titan or Timex watch with a clear dial, a small tote, slip-on walking shoes or an attar gift set. Add a card with one memory.

### What can a daughter give her mother on Mother's Day?

A perfume gift set, a rose gold Timex watch or a handbag she can carry to functions. Choosing it together on a video call is a nice touch.

### What can a son give his mother on Mother's Day?

An attar set, a zip-around wallet, a light satchel or a thermal set for winter trips. If you live away, plan the delivery for the Friday or Saturday before.

### What can kids give their mom on Mother's Day?

A homemade card or poster, made with an art kit or sketch pens, and one small gift picked with help from dad or an older sibling.

### What should a husband give his wife on Mother's Day?

A watch like the Titan Raga Charm, an office tote for a working mom, or a day off: take the kids out and let her rest. See our [gift for wife](gift-for-wife.html) guide for more.

### What can I give a mom who doesn't want anything?

Your time, plus something small she'll use daily: a steel bottle, an attar set or a lotion. A promise card for a lunch or a day trip is a gift she can't say no to.
