Mokobara is a newer brand from Bengaluru, and American Tourister belongs to the Samsonite group. People often compare the two. We read their Amazon.in listings and each brand's own warranty pages. We haven't tested these bags, so nothing here tells you how long they last.

## Quick answer

Mokobara or American Tourister, which is better? It depends on what matters to you:

- **Lighter bags: American Tourister.** Its Liftoff+ 67 cm weighs 2.9 kg. Mokobara's medium bags we picked weigh 3.87 to 4.06 kg.
- **Polycarbonate with a TSA lock in every size: Mokobara.** All four Mokobara bags we picked are polycarbonate with a TSA lock. American Tourister's polycarbonate Ivy NXT 68 cm has a combination lock that isn't TSA.
- **Large bag within 158 cm: both.** Mokobara's Aisle Trunk 76.5 cm is 156.5 cm in total, and American Tourister's Liftoff+ 79 cm is 158 cm.
- **Airline damage cover: Mokobara, but not on Amazon.** Mokobara replaces airline-damaged luggage for free, but only for bags bought from Mokobara directly. Neither brand's warranty covers airline damage.

**Mokobara's Ctrl+Z cover:** Mokobara replaces luggage damaged by an airline or airport for free if you file the airline's damage report (PIR) within 7 days. Its policy page says this does not apply to bags bought on marketplaces such as Amazon. So a Mokobara bag bought on Amazon.in gets the warranty, but not Ctrl+Z.

Keep your Amazon invoice for either brand: it's your proof of purchase.

## Questions

### Mokobara or American Tourister: which is better?

Neither wins on everything. In the bags we compared, American Tourister had the lighter bags and a cabin size that fits Air India, and Mokobara had a polycarbonate shell and a TSA lock on every bag. We haven't tested them, so we can't say which lasts longer.

### Is Mokobara an Indian brand?

Yes. Mokobara's website is run by Mokobara Lifestyle Private Limited, a company with its registered office in Bengaluru.

### What is Mokobara's warranty?

Mokobara's site gives 6 years on its Iconic, Access and Em luggage and 3 years on Transit, for manufacturing defects. The Mokobara bags we picked list 3 years on Amazon.in, so check the card on yours.

### Does Mokobara's Ctrl+Z cover work if I buy on Amazon?

No. Mokobara's Ctrl+Z page says the free airline damage replacement doesn't apply to marketplace purchases such as Amazon. The normal warranty still applies.

### Is Mokobara heavier than American Tourister?

In the bags we picked, yes. Mokobara's medium bags weigh 3.87 to 4.06 kg, against 2.9 kg for American Tourister's polypropylene Liftoff+ 67 cm. Between polycarbonate bags the gap is small.
