An analog watch, the kind with hands, still looks smart in an interview, at the office or at a wedding. It also makes a safe gift. Here's how to pick one without confusion.

## Quick answer

- **Dial size:** 38–40 mm suits most men's wrists, 28–34 mm most women's. Medium (36–40 mm) works for anyone.
- **Strap:** steel for daily office wear, leather for formal, silicone for sport and college.
- **Water resistance:** 30 m (3 bar) is only for splashes and rain. You need 100 m (10 bar) to swim.
- **Movement:** quartz (battery) for a first watch: accurate, cheap to run, no winding.
- **Warranty:** at least 1 year, from a brand with service centres near you.

## 1. Pick the right dial size

Dial (case) size is measured in millimetres (mm) across the watch face.

- **Under 36 mm:** small, common in women's watches.
- **36–40 mm:** medium. Suits most wrists, men or women.
- **41–45 mm:** large and bold. Looks best on wider wrists.

Not sure? Choose **38–40 mm** for men and **28–34 mm** for women. These sizes suit most people.

## 2. Choose a strap for how you'll wear it

- **Leather:** classic and formal. Keep it away from water and sweat or it wears out faster.
- **Stainless steel or metal:** durable, easy to clean and good for office wear. Links can be removed at a watch shop to fit your wrist.
- **Silicone or rubber:** sporty, light and sweat-friendly. Good for daily college wear and the gym.

## 3. Understand water resistance

This confuses many buyers. The number on the watch is **not** a diving depth. Casio, which follows the ISO 22810 standard, says the metres are only "a pictorial representation of the test pressure", and that moving your arm while swimming puts much more pressure on the watch than standing still in water.

| Label on the watch | Splashes, rain, hand washing | Bath or shower | Swimming, snorkelling | Diving |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| No water resistance mark | No | No | No | No |
| Water resistant (often 30 m / 3 bar) | Yes | No | No | No |
| 50 m / 5 bar | Yes | Yes | No | No |
| 100 m / 10 bar | Yes | Yes | Yes | No |
| 200 m / 20 bar | Yes | Yes | Yes | Free-diving without equipment |

Most everyday watches are rated 30 m. That's fine as long as you take the watch off before a shower or a swim. Leather straps shouldn't get wet at all, whatever the rating. Seals also wear out with age, heat and soap, so don't trust an old watch in water.

## 4. Quartz or automatic?

- **Quartz** watches run on a battery. They're very accurate, low-maintenance and affordable. A battery lasts about 2–3 years and is cheap to replace.
- **Automatic** watches wind themselves from your wrist movement. They're loved by watch fans but cost more and need servicing.

For a first watch, **quartz is the sensible choice**.

## 5. Check the brand and warranty

Well-known brands in India, such as Titan, Fastrack, Sonata, Timex and Casio, have service centres in many cities. Torn between two of them? See [Titan vs Fastrack](titan-vs-fastrack.html). Look for at least a **1-year warranty**. Some, like Casio, offer 2 years on some models.

## Questions

### What size analog watch should I buy?

Measure your wrist. Most men's wrists suit a 38–40 mm dial and most women's a 28–34 mm one; 41 mm and up looks best on wide wrists. Our [watch size tool](../tools/watch-size.html) suggests a size from your wrist measurement.

### Can I wear a 30 m water resistant watch in the shower?

No. A 30 m (3 bar) watch is only for splashes, rain and hand washing. Casio's chart allows a bath or shower only from 5 bar (50 m), and swimming from 10 bar (100 m).

### Is quartz or automatic better for a first watch?

Quartz. It runs on a battery that lasts about 2–3 years, keeps better time and needs almost no care. An automatic watch is a hobby choice: it costs more, needs regular wear or a winder, and needs servicing.

### Leather or steel strap: which is better?

Steel is better for daily wear: it doesn't mind sweat or rain, and a shop can remove links to fit your wrist. Leather looks more formal but wears out faster in heat and sweat, and must be kept dry.

### Which brand is best for a first analog watch in India?

Pick a brand with service centres in your city and at least a 1-year warranty, such as Titan, Fastrack, Sonata, Timex or Casio. See how two of them compare in [Titan vs Fastrack](titan-vs-fastrack.html).
