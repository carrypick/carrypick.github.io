## Reading VIP's sizes

VIP model names often include the height in cm:

| Model | Sizes in the name |
|---|---|
| Dual Tone NXP | 55, 67 and 79 |
| Alumina | 55, 69 and 80 |
| Zorro | 55, 69 and 80 |
| Stargaze | 55 and 65 |

So "55" is a cabin size by height, "65–69" is medium and "79–80" is large. Not sure which you need? See [what size luggage you need](luggage-size-guide.html).

## What to check before you order

- **The full size.** The VIP product pages we read gave no width or depth. Airlines check all three sides, so ask VIP for the full size if your trip depends on it. Most Indian airlines allow only 55 x 35 x 25 cm or 55 x 40 x 20 cm in the cabin. Test any size with our [cabin bag checker](../baggage/checker.html).
- **Large bags.** "79" or "80" is tall. Most Indian airlines limit a checked bag to 158 cm in total, and [most large bags we checked](28-inch-trolley-bag-size.html) from other listings went over that. Measure it when it arrives, inside the 3-day return period.
- **Capacity.** Some VIP pages we read showed capacity figures that can't be right, such as "2 Litres" for a cabin trolley. Don't choose by that number.
- **Weight.** We didn't find the bag's own weight on these pages. Weigh it when it arrives, because it counts towards your airline allowance.
- **Warranty.** We only read the warranty on a few models. Check it on the bag you pick, and keep your invoice.
- **Returns.** You have only 3 days from delivery. Unpack and measure the bag quickly, and keep all tags and packaging until you're sure.
