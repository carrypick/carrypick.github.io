## Which VIP line suits you?

- **Want a hard shell?** VIP's pages name polycarbonate for Vero, Pod NXT and Barclay, and polypropylene for Maxxplus and Classic. Not sure which is better? See [polycarbonate or polypropylene](polycarbonate-vs-polypropylene-luggage.html).

## VIP's warranty: what it covers and how to claim

VIP's warranty terms say:

- **Who:** products bought from VIP, its authorised channels or vipbags.com. The period starts on the invoice date.
- **What:** manufacturing defects and functional failures caused by them. VIP inspects the bag and decides; its decision is "final and binding".
- **Not covered:** normal wear and tear, cosmetic damage, scratches, dents, stains, fading, misuse, accidents, extreme conditions, changes you made, loss or theft. Damage during transport by **airlines, airports, railways or other carriers** is excluded too, and VIP says to report that to the carrier straight away.
- **If approved:** VIP may replace the faulty part, the whole bag with the same model, or an equivalent model. A repair or replacement doesn't restart the warranty.
- **Abroad:** service may be available at centres in India and "selected international locations", and support may vary by country.
- **Commercial use:** bags used for rental or business may get different terms.

### How to claim a VIP warranty

1. Find your original invoice. VIP may reject a claim without proof of purchase.
2. Call VIP customer care on 022-41724444 or use the email on VIP's warranty page. They can also tell you the nearest authorised service centre.
3. Take or send the bag for inspection. VIP decides whether it's a manufacturing defect.

VIP's site also has a "Warranty Check" tool (on its Product Utility page), which asks for your mobile number and the bag's barcode. We haven't used it.

## What to check before you order

- **The full size.** The VIP product pages we read gave no width or depth. Airlines check all three sides, so ask VIP for the full size if your trip depends on it. IndiGo, for example, allows 55 x 35 x 25 cm, and Air India 55 x 40 x 20 cm. Test any size with our [cabin bag checker](../baggage/checker.html).
- **Large bags.** "79" or "80" is tall. Most Indian airlines limit a checked bag to 158 cm in total, and [most large bags we checked](28-inch-trolley-bag-size.html) from other listings went over that. Measure it when it arrives, inside the 3-day return period.
- **Capacity.** Some VIP pages we read showed capacity figures that can't be right, such as "2 Litres" for a cabin trolley. Don't choose by that number.
- **Weight.** We didn't find the bag's own weight on these pages. Weigh it when it arrives, because it counts towards your airline allowance.
- **Invoice.** Keep it for the full 3 years: it starts the warranty, and VIP asks for it in a claim.
- **Returns.** You have only 3 days from delivery, and no exchanges. Unpack and measure the bag quickly, and keep all tags and packaging until you're sure.
- **Where you buy.** The warranty covers VIP's authorised channels. If you buy from another seller, keep that seller's invoice and check the card in the box.

## Questions

### What is the warranty on VIP trolley bags?

VIP's warranty terms, effective 30 Jun 2026, give 3 years on hard and soft luggage and 6 months on backpacks and duffel bags. It covers manufacturing defects only, from the invoice date.

### How do I claim a VIP bag warranty?

Call VIP customer care on 022-41724444 or visit an authorised service centre, with your original invoice. VIP inspects the bag and decides whether it's a manufacturing defect. If it is, VIP repairs the part or replaces the bag with the same or an equivalent model.

### How do I register or check a VIP bag warranty?

VIP's warranty terms don't mention registering: the warranty runs from the date on your invoice. VIP's site has a "Warranty Check" tool that asks for your mobile number and the bag's barcode.

### Where is the VIP bags service centre near me?

VIP's warranty policy says customer care (022-41724444) can tell you the nearest authorised service centre. VIP's site also has a store locator.

### Does VIP's warranty cover airline damage?

No. VIP excludes damage during transport by airlines, airports, railways and other carriers. Report it to the airline before you leave the airport and get a reference number.

### Can I return a VIP bag bought from its website?

Yes, if you ask within 3 days of delivery. The bag must be unused, undamaged, with all tags, labels and packaging, and pass VIP's inspection. Website orders can't be exchanged.
