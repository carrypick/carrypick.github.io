Most visitors to India need a visa, and for a holiday the easiest one is the **e-Tourist Visa**. You apply online, pay online, and get an email that lets you fly. Everything on this page comes from the Government of India's e-Visa website, which we read on 21 Nov 2026.

New to India? Start with our [visiting India guide](visiting-india.html) for the whole first-trip checklist.

## Use only the official website

The government says plainly that **[indianvisaonline.gov.in/evisa](https://indianvisaonline.gov.in/evisa/tvoa.html) is the only official website** for the e-Visa. The whole process is online, and it says no agent or "facilitator" is needed.

- **Beware of look-alike sites.** A search for "India e-Visa" can show unofficial sites that look official, add their own fees and ask for your passport details. The government warns visitors not to fall for such middlemen, and not to share personal data with unauthorised websites.
- **Check the address bar** before you type anything: the address must end in **.gov.in**.
- **Nothing more to pay at the airport.** The site says you have already paid the fee online, and warns about anyone who asks for extra money.

## Which e-Tourist Visa to pick

| Visa | How long it's valid | Entries | Stay |
|---|---|---|---|
| 30-day e-Tourist Visa | 30 days from your first arrival in India | Multiple, within the 30 days | Up to 30 days |
| 1-year e-Tourist Visa | 365 days from the date it's granted | Multiple | Up to 180 days in a calendar year |
| 5-year e-Tourist Visa | 5 years from the date it's granted | Multiple | Up to 180 days in a calendar year |

- **The 30-day visa starts when you land,** not when it's granted. Your first arrival must fall between its issue and expiry dates.
- **The 1-year and 5-year visas start the day they're granted,** so don't apply too early.
- **You can't extend or convert an e-Visa** into another visa.
- **Protected and restricted areas** (some border and island areas) need a separate permit, even with an e-Visa.
- Japan, South Korea and some UAE nationals can also get a visa on arrival. Everyone else eligible must get the e-Visa before they fly, the site says.

## How much the e-Tourist Visa costs

The fee depends on your nationality. This is the government's e-Tourist Visa fee table, in US dollars, dated 8 September 2026:

| Nationality | 30 days (April–June) | 30 days (July–March) | 1 year | 5 years |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| United States | US$10 | US$25 | US$40 | US$160 |
| United Kingdom | US$10 | US$25 | US$40 | US$484 |
| Canada | US$10 | US$25 | US$40 | US$200 |
| Australia | US$10 | US$25 | US$40 | US$200 |
| Germany, France, Italy, Spain, Netherlands, Ireland | US$10 | US$25 | US$40 | US$200 |
| Japan | US$10 | US$25 | US$25 | US$25 |
| Singapore | US$10 | US$25 | US$25 | US$200 |

- **Add a 3% bank charge** on top of the fee. The FAQ says PayPal charges 4%.
- **Some nationalities pay nothing,** for example Argentina and Mauritius in the table.
- **The fee isn't refunded,** even if the visa is refused or you cancel the trip.
- **Fees change.** Open the [fee table](https://indianvisaonline.gov.in/evisa/images/Etourist_fee_final.pdf) for your country before you pay.

## When to apply

- **At least 4 days before you arrive.** Pay the fee at least 4 days before you travel too, or the application isn't processed.
- **Up to 120 days before** your trip, says the FAQ.
- **How long it takes:** the site doesn't give a processing time. You get an email when it's granted or refused, and you can check the status on the site. Apply early so you have time to fix a mistake.

## What you need to apply

- **A passport valid for at least 6 months** when you apply, with **at least 2 blank pages** for the immigration stamp. Children need their own e-Visa.
- **A photo:** JPEG, 10 KB to 1 MB, square (height and width equal), full face, eyes open, no glasses, plain light or white background, no shadow or border.
- **A scan of your passport's photo page** as a PDF, 10 KB to 300 KB.
- **A return or onward ticket,** and enough money for your stay.
- **A yellow fever card** if you arrive within 6 days of leaving a yellow fever country, or you may be quarantined for 6 days. The list is on the [health ministry's site](https://mohfw.gov.in/sites/default/files/Updated%20List%20of%20Yellow%20Fever%20Endemic%20Countries%20by%20WHO_0.pdf).

Check your name, passport number and date of birth before you submit. You are responsible for any mistake.

## Where you can arrive

You must land at a **designated e-Visa airport or seaport**, and you can leave from any immigration checkpoint. The list includes Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Chennai, Kolkata, Hyderabad, Kochi, Thiruvananthapuram, both Goa airports (Dabolim and Mopa), Jaipur, Varanasi, Amritsar, Ahmedabad, Port Blair and many more.

- **No land borders.** The FAQ says you can't enter by road with an e-Visa.
- **Cruise passengers** can enter at Mumbai, Chennai, Kochi, Goa and Mangalore.
- The site gives different counts on different pages (33 or 37 airports), so check that your airport is on the [official list](https://indianvisaonline.gov.in/evisa/tvoa.html) before you book.

## When you land

- **e-Arrival card:** foreigners can fill in the e-Arrival card online **within 72 hours before** they arrive, on the e-Visa website, the Bureau of Immigration website or the "Indian Visa Su-Swagatam" app. It's arrival information, not a visa.
- **Fingerprints and photo** are taken at immigration.
- **Customs:** as a foreign tourist you can bring in goods worth up to ₹25,000 duty free, plus your used personal things. Declare foreign cash over US$5,000 in notes, or US$10,000 in all. See our [Indian customs guide](../baggage/customs.html) and [cash limits](../baggage/customs/cash-and-currency.html).

## Questions

### What is the official website for the India e-Visa?

Only indianvisaonline.gov.in/evisa. The government says it's the only official website, and that no agent is needed.

### How much is the India e-Visa for US citizens?

The government's fee table (8 Sept 2026) shows US$25 for the 30-day visa (US$10 from April to June), US$40 for 1 year and US$160 for 5 years, plus a 3% bank charge.

### How much is the India e-Visa for UK citizens?

US$25 for 30 days (US$10 from April to June), US$40 for 1 year and US$484 for 5 years, plus a 3% bank charge, in the fee table dated 8 Sept 2026.

### How long does the India e-Visa take?

The official site gives no processing time. Apply at least 4 days before you arrive, and up to 120 days before. You get the result by email.

### Can I enter India by land with an e-Visa?

No. With an e-Visa you must arrive at a designated airport or seaport. You can leave from any immigration checkpoint.

### Can I extend my e-Visa in India?

No. The e-Visa can't be extended or converted to another visa.

### What are the photo requirements for the India e-Visa?

A square JPEG of 10 KB to 1 MB: full face, eyes open, no glasses, on a plain light or white background, with no shadow or border.
