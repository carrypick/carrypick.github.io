**Short answer:** Republic Day 2027 falls on **Tuesday 26 January**, a gazetted holiday on the central government's 2027 list (DoPT). Take **Monday 25 January** off and you get four days, Saturday to Tuesday. Take five days off (25 and 27 to 29 January) and you get nine, Saturday 23 to Sunday 31 January. It's the 78th Republic Day.

## January 2027 long weekends calendar

From DoPT's 2027 holiday list for central government offices (Delhi), dated 16 July 2026. Restricted holidays are optional days off for central government staff. Private companies and state governments set their own lists, so check yours.

| Dates | Days off | Leave you need | What the holiday is |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fri 1 – Sun 3 Jan | 3 | None, if your office closes for New Year's Day (a restricted holiday) | New Year's Day |
| Thu 14 – Sun 17 Jan | 4 | Two restricted holidays, or 2 days' leave | Makar Sankranti / Magha Bihu (14th), Pongal and Guru Gobind Singh's Birthday (15th) |
| **Sat 23 – Tue 26 Jan** | **4** | **1 day: Monday 25** | **Republic Day (gazetted)** |
| Tue 26 – Sun 31 Jan | 6 | 3 days: 27, 28, 29 | Republic Day, then the weekend |
| Sat 23 – Sun 31 Jan | 9 | 4 days: 25, 27, 28, 29 | Republic Day in the middle |

## Book trains on these dates

Indian Railways opens reservations 60 days before the journey, not counting the journey day. For this weekend:

| Journey date | Booking opens |

## Where to go for 4 days

Four days is enough for one place. Moving between two takes most of a day.

| Trip | Best for | Weather in January | Fly to | A 4-day plan |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Jaipur](jaipur.html) | Forts, palaces, markets | Sunny days about 22–24 °C, cold nights about 8–9 °C | Jaipur (JAI) | City and Amer Fort, without rushing |
| [Goa](goa.html) | Beaches, churches | Warm: about 20–32 °C, almost no rain | Mopa (GOX) or Dabolim (GOI) | One part of Goa, North or South |
| [Kerala](kerala.html) | Backwaters, tea hills | Coast about 21–34 °C; Munnar nights 2–9 °C | Kochi (COK) | Kochi plus Munnar or Alappuzha. See our [4-day Kerala itinerary](kerala.html) |
| [Manali](manali.html) | Snow, mountains | Freezing nights; can fall below −5 °C | Chandigarh, then drive | Long road journey, so little spare time |

- **Want snow?** Manali has a better chance in January than in December, but snow isn't guaranteed and roads can close after heavy snow. Keep your dates flexible. Read [Manali in January](manali-in-january.html).
- **Short on leave?** Jaipur is the easiest of these from North India. See [Rajasthan in January](rajasthan-in-january.html).
- **Want to escape the cold?** Goa and the Kerala coast stay warm. See [Goa in January](goa-in-january.html) and [Kerala in January](kerala-in-january.html).
- **Travelling with parents or children?** Jaipur and Kerala are calmer, with easy sightseeing.
- **Got 9 days?** That's enough for a short trip abroad. See [Thailand in January](thailand-in-january.html), [Dubai in January](dubai-in-january.html), [Vietnam in January](vietnam-in-january.html) or [Bali in January](bali-in-january.html).
- **Fog:** January fog in North India can delay early flights and trains, so leave spare time for connections.

The parade is on Kartavya Path on the morning of 26 January, and Beating Retreat is usually on 29 January. The 2027 ticket dates and prices aren't out yet. ## Questions

### Is there a long weekend in January 2027?

Yes, if you take one day off. Republic Day, 26 January 2027, is a Tuesday. With Monday 25 January off, you get four days from Saturday 23 January. If your office gives the restricted holidays for Makar Sankranti (Thursday 14 January) and Pongal (Friday 15 January), that's another four-day weekend.

### What day is Republic Day 2027?

Tuesday, 26 January 2027. It's a gazetted holiday on the central government's 2027 list, issued by DoPT on 16 July 2026.

### How many years is Republic Day 2027?

It's the 78th Republic Day. India's Constitution came into force on 26 January 1950, and the 2026 celebration was the 77th.

### Who is the chief guest for Republic Day 2027?

It hadn't been announced when we checked on 26 November 2026. The Ministry of External Affairs announces it, sometimes only a few weeks before 26 January. In 2026, the chief guests were European Council President António Costa and European Commission President Ursula von der Leyen.

### Where can I go for 4 days on Republic Day weekend?

Jaipur for forts, Goa for a warm beach, Kerala for backwaters and tea hills, or Manali if you want a chance of snow and don't mind a long road journey.

### When does train booking open for Republic Day weekend 2027?

60 days before the journey, not counting the journey day: 24 November 2026 for travel on 23 January, and 27 November 2026 for a return on 26 January. Check IRCTC for the exact rules.

### How do I buy Republic Day 2027 parade tickets online?

The 2027 details aren't out yet. In 2026, tickets were sold on the Aamantran website and at six booths in Delhi from 5 to 14 January. Our [parade tickets guide](republic-day-parade-tickets-2027.html) has the full steps.
