March is the last pleasant month for most of India before the summer heat. The north is warming up but still comfortable, Holi fills the month with colour, the ski season in Kashmir and Uttarakhand is in its last weeks, and the hill stations of Uttarakhand start their season. In the south and on the west coast it's already hot and dry, so plan beach days for the mornings and evenings.

Abroad, March is a good month for Vietnam, Sri Lanka, the Maldives and Singapore, and at the end of the month the cherry blossom starts in Tokyo.

This page sums up our trip guides that fit March, in India and abroad, with the dates we could confirm and the long weekends of March 2027. Each place links to its full guide, where the official sources are listed.

## March 2027 dates to know

- **Holi:** **Tuesday 23 March 2027**, with Holika Dahan on Monday 22 March, says the central government holiday list from DoPT. Some state lists can differ. See [Holi 2027: where to celebrate](holi-2027.html).
- **Holi in Mathura, Vrindavan and Barsana:** UP Tourism's Rangotsav page still shows the 2026 programme, so the **2027 dates, including Lathmar Holi, are to be announced**. In 2026 the Braj events ran for about two weeks around Holi. See our [Holi guide](holi-2027.html#holi-2027-in-mathura-vrindavan-and-barsana).
- **Shigmo in Goa:** Goa Tourism's 2026–27 calendar lists Shigmo for **March 2027** in Panaji, Margao, Vasco and other towns. The dates are tentative and not yet announced. See [Goa](goa.html).
- **International Yoga Festival, Rishikesh:** **9–15 March 2027**. See [Rishikesh](rishikesh-in-december.html).
- **Rann Utsav** ends on **7 March 2027**. Gujarat Tourism gives that date, but the Incredible India site gives a shorter season, so confirm before you plan an early-March trip. See [Rann Utsav](rann-utsav-2026.html).
- **Id-ul-Fitr:** Wednesday 10 March 2027 on the DoPT list. It depends on the moon, so the date may move.
- **Nyepi in Bali:** Monday 8 March 2027. The whole island shuts for the day, including the airport. See [Bali in January](bali-in-january.html).
- **Good Friday:** Friday 26 March 2027, and Easter Sunday is 28 March.
- **Cherry blossom in Japan** usually starts around 24 March in Tokyo and 26 March in Kyoto. See [Japan](japan-trip-from-india.html).

## Long weekends in March 2027

March 2027 is the best month of the year for a long break. On the DoPT holiday list for central government offices in Delhi, three holidays fall in March: Id-ul-Fitr (Wednesday 10 March), Holi (Tuesday 23 March) and Good Friday (Friday 26 March).

- **Holi: Saturday 20 to Tuesday 23 March, 4 days with 1 day off** (Monday 22 March, which is also a restricted holiday for Holika Dahan).
- **Good Friday: Friday 26 to Sunday 28 March, 3 days with no leave.**
- **Holi and Good Friday together: Saturday 20 to Sunday 28 March, 9 days** with 3 days off (Monday 22, Wednesday 24 and Thursday 25 March), or 2 if you use your restricted holiday on 22 March.
- **Id-ul-Fitr: Saturday 6 to Wednesday 10 March, 5 days** with Monday 8 and Tuesday 9 off, or Wednesday 10 to Sunday 14 March with Thursday 11 and Friday 12 off. The date may move with the moon, so keep bookings flexible.
- **Friday 5 March (Jamat-ul-Vida)** is a restricted holiday: with it, Friday to Sunday 5–7 March is 3 days.

See every 2027 long weekend, and filter by month and leave days, in our [long weekends 2027 planner](../tools/long-weekends-2027.html).

## Holi trips in March

Holi is on **Tuesday 23 March 2027**, and many people turn it into a 4-day or 9-day trip. The best-known celebrations are in Braj, the land of Krishna: Barsana and Nandgaon for Lathmar Holi, the temples of Vrindavan and Mathura, and the Huranga in Baldev and Nandgaon the day after Holi. Varanasi's ghats and the towns of Rajasthan are also popular.

Read our [Holi 2027 guide](holi-2027.html) for the places, the 2026 Braj schedule from UP Tourism, safety and etiquette, and what to wear and pack.

## Places to visit in March for snow and hill stations

March is the end of the snow season, so the snow is deep in Kashmir but melting lower down.

- **[Gulmarg](gulmarg-in-january.html):** the ski slopes are usually best from January to March, and February and March are even snowier than January on average. Book gondola tickets only on the official site. See also [Kashmir in January](kashmir-in-january.html).
- **[Auli](auli-in-december.html):** Uttarakhand Tourism and the Chamoli district name January to March as the season for snow and skiing, and GMVN's 2025–26 ski courses ran until 31 March.
- **[Manali](manali-in-january.html):** the ski courses at Solang run from January to March if there's enough snow. News reports say there are no direct Delhi flights this winter: until 27 March 2027 the only flights are from Jaipur and Dehradun. See [Manali](manali.html).
- **[Shimla](shimla-in-december.html):** snow usually comes from late December to early March, so March snow is a matter of luck.
- **[Mussoorie](mussoorie-in-december.html) and [Nainital](nainital-in-december.html):** March to June is their main season, with clear, mild days. Both are an easy trip from Delhi.
- **[Darjeeling](darjeeling-in-december.html):** the first flush, the first tea picking of the year, starts around March. Higher up, late snow in March 2026 left more than 100 tourists stuck at Sandakphu, so check the forecast.

Pack with our [snow trip packing list](../guides/snow-trip-packing-list.html), and see [what to wear on a winter hill trip](../guides/winter-clothes-for-hill-trips.html).

## Beaches in March

- **[Andaman](andaman-in-december.html):** February to April has the calmest sea and the clearest water. Take the ferries from Sri Vijaya Puram to Swaraj Dweep (Havelock) and Shaheed Dweep (Neil).
- **[Goa](goa.html):** hot and dry, with fewer crowds than in winter. Shigmo, Goa's spring festival, is listed for March 2027 in Panaji, Margao and Vasco, with dates to come. Swim at beaches with lifeguards, and plan the beach for the morning and evening.
- **Kerala and Pondicherry:** past their best months (Kerala's is November to February), and March is hot on the coast. If you go, see [Kerala](kerala.html) and [Pondicherry](pondicherry-in-december.html).

## Rajasthan, the Ganga and the safaris

- **[Jaipur](jaipur.html) and [Udaipur](udaipur.html):** both are in season until March. Days get hot by the end of the month, so see the forts early. For Holi in a palace city, Udaipur and Jaipur are easy picks.
- **[Varanasi](varanasi-in-december.html)** is in season from October to March, and Holi on the ghats is one of its biggest days.
- **[Rishikesh](rishikesh-in-december.html):** the International Yoga Festival is on 9–15 March 2027, and rafting season runs from September to June.
- **[Jim Corbett](jim-corbett-in-december.html)** and **[Ranthambore](ranthambore-in-december.html)** are open. Ranthambore's January to March bookings opened on 1 November, so book soon, only on the official site.

## Places to visit in March in South India

- **[Coorg](coorg-in-december.html):** its season runs from October to March, and it's an easy drive from Bengaluru.
- **[Munnar](munnar-in-december.html):** the tea hills are in season until March, but Eravikulam National Park usually closes from about 1 February to 1 April for the Nilgiri tahr calving season.
- **[Ooty](ooty-in-december.html):** in season from October to June, so it's a good escape when the plains warm up.
- **[Hampi](hampi-in-december.html):** Karnataka Tourism's best season ends in February, so in March go early in the morning and rest at midday.

### Vietnam, Sri Lanka and the Maldives

**[Vietnam](vietnam-trip-from-india.html)** is one of the best picks for March: the tourism authority says March to May has the best weather for the whole country, Hanoi is warm, and the centre and south are dry. **[Sri Lanka](sri-lanka-trip-from-india.html)** is in the last month of its best season for Colombo, Galle, Kandy and the hills. **The [Maldives](maldives-trip-from-india.html)** is in its driest stretch, from February to April, around Malé.

### Singapore, Japan and cherry blossom

**[Singapore](singapore-trip-from-india.html)** is in its driest months, February to April, after the Northeast Monsoon ends in early March.

**[Japan](japan-trip-from-india.html)** is the March trip for cherry blossom: it usually starts around 24 March in Tokyo and 26 March in Kyoto. Fares go up in the blossom season, so book early. In South Korea, the first blossoms reached Jeju and Busan around 25 March in 2026, and Seoul in April. See [South Korea](south-korea-trip-from-india.html).

### Dubai, Oman and Egypt

**[Dubai](dubai-trip-from-india.html)** is at the end of its season (November to March). Ramadan is expected to end around 10 March with Eid, so check opening hours if you go early in the month. **[Oman](oman-trip-from-india.html)** and **[Egypt](egypt-trip-from-india.html)** are still pleasant, with warm days in Muscat and Cairo.

### Thailand, Bali and Europe

**[Thailand](thailand-trip-from-india.html)** is very hot from March to May, and smoke from crop fires is usually worst in the north in March and April, so pick the beaches. **[Bali](bali-trip-from-india.html)** shuts for Nyepi on 8 March 2027, and its dry season starts in April. **[Europe](europe-trip-from-india.html)** is cold in early March, and snow in the Alps is most likely from December to March.

## Questions

### Which is the best place to visit in March in India?

For Holi, Mathura, Vrindavan or Varanasi. For the last snow, Gulmarg or Auli. For cool hills, Mussoorie, Nainital or Ooty. For the sea, the Andaman, where February to April is the driest time.

### Which hill stations are good in March?

Mussoorie and Nainital start their season in March, Ooty is in season, and Coorg and Munnar are good until March. For snow, go higher: Gulmarg and Auli are still in their ski season.

### Where can I go outside India in March?

Vietnam (the best weather for the whole country), Sri Lanka, the Maldives and Singapore. Japan's cherry blossom usually starts in late March. Thailand is very hot, and Bali shuts for Nyepi on 8 March 2027.

### Is there a long weekend in March 2027?

Yes, two. Holi falls on Tuesday 23 March, so with Monday 22 March off you get 4 days from Saturday 20 March. Good Friday on 26 March gives 3 days with no leave. With 3 days off (22, 24 and 25 March) you can join them into 9 days. See our [long weekends 2027 planner](../tools/long-weekends-2027.html).

### When is Holi in 2027?

Tuesday 23 March 2027, with Holika Dahan on Monday 22 March, says the central government holiday list. See [where to celebrate Holi](holi-2027.html).

### Is March a good time to visit Goa?

Yes, if you don't mind heat. It's dry and quieter after the winter crowds, and Shigmo is listed for March 2027. Go to the beach in the morning and evening.
