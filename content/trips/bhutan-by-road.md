Yes, Indians can go to Bhutan by road. Most people fly to Bagdogra or take a train to New Jalpaiguri (NJP), then go by road to the border towns of Jaigaon (India) and Phuentsholing (Bhutan). You need your passport or original voter ID, an entry permit, the daily Sustainable Development Fee (SDF) and a guide. This page covers each step, with the official rules checked on 21 November 2026.

Going in winter? See [Bhutan in December](bhutan-in-december.html) for the weather, festivals and a 6-day plan. Packing? Use our [Bhutan packing list](../guides/bhutan-packing-list.html). Adding Darjeeling on the way? See [Darjeeling in December](darjeeling-in-december.html).

## Can we go to Bhutan by road from India?

Yes. Bhutan's Department of Tourism lists **four official road entry points from India**:

| Entry point | Part of Bhutan | Indian side |
|---|---|---|
| Phuentsholing | West | Jaigaon, West Bengal |
| Samtse | West | West Bengal |
| Gelephu | Central | Assam |
| Samdrup Jongkhar | East | Assam |

- **Phuentsholing is the usual crossing** for trips to Paro, Thimphu and Punakha. It is the one most tour packages from Siliguri, NJP and Kolkata use.
- **Gelephu and Samdrup Jongkhar** are closer for people coming from Assam and Guwahati. Ask your hotel or tour operator if they can process your permit there before you choose them.
- **Bhutan has no railway.** The last part of the trip is always by road.

## How to reach the border: Siliguri, Kolkata, Delhi and Guwahati

| Starting from | Usual way |
|---|---|
| Siliguri or Bagdogra | By road to Jaigaon and Phuentsholing, about 4–5 hours (a travel-guide estimate) |
| Kolkata | Train to NJP or Hasimara, or fly to Bagdogra, then by road |
| Delhi and other cities | Fly to Bagdogra, or take a train to NJP, then by road. Or fly straight to Paro |
| Guwahati | By road to Samdrup Jongkhar or Gelephu, or go to Phuentsholing via NJP |

- **Bagdogra airport** is near Siliguri and is run by the Airports Authority of India. It is the nearest airport to Phuentsholing on the Indian side.
- **NJP (New Jalpaiguri)** is the big station for Siliguri, with trains from Kolkata, Delhi and Guwahati. **Hasimara** is a smaller station that is closer to Jaigaon. Check trains and times on Indian Railways' [NTES](https://enquiry.indianrail.gov.in/mntes/) or IRCTC.
- **Road times are estimates.** Hill roads, weather and traffic at the border change them. Don't plan a long drive after dark.
- **Flying straight to Paro** saves two days of driving. Drukair flies to Paro from Delhi, Kolkata, Bagdogra and Guwahati; Bhutan Airlines flies from Delhi and Kolkata. See [Bhutan in December](bhutan-in-december.html#getting-to-bhutan-from-india) for flight notes.

## The Phuentsholing entry permit for Indians

- **Indians don't need a visa** for Bhutan, but you need an **entry permit**. No fee is paid in advance for the permit itself, says the Department of Tourism; you pay the SDF when you apply.
- **Apply online first** on the Bhutan Immigration portal, [immi.gov.bt](https://immi.gov.bt/home/), or let your hotel or tour operator apply for you. You can also apply at the border, but the Department of Tourism warns there may be a long wait.
- **Where at the border:** the Regional Immigration Office in Phuentsholing, on the border opposite Jaigaon. The Embassy of India lists its phone numbers as +975 05 252383 and 254639.
- **Office hours:** we could not find the current hours on an official page. Online permits avoid the queue. If you apply at the border, call ahead or ask your hotel.
- **Route permits are no longer needed** to move around Bhutan if you travel with a guide certified by the Department of Tourism. An older Indian Embassy page still mentions a route permit from Thimphu for places beyond Thimphu and Paro, so ask your tour operator if your plan goes further east.

### Documents you need

- **An Indian passport valid for at least 6 months, or your original voter ID card.** Only these two are accepted. Aadhaar and driving licence don't work for the permit.
- **Passport-size photos.** Online, you upload a recent digital photo. At the border, carry printed photos.
- **Children under 18** can use a passport or their original birth certificate, and must travel with a parent or legal guardian.
- **Your SDF payment, hotel bookings and guide booking**, printed or on your phone.

## SDF and day visits to Phuentsholing

- **SDF for Indians:** Nu 1,200 (₹1,200) per person per night. Children aged 6 to 11 pay Nu 600 a night. Children under 6 pay nothing.
- **Day visits are free of SDF** until August 2027. Since 2 June 2026, tourists who stay up to 24 hours in Phuentsholing, Samtse, Gelephu or Samdrup Jongkhar don't pay the SDF, says the Department of Tourism.
- **The free zone ends at Rinchending Checkpost,** just above Phuentsholing town. Go beyond it and the SDF applies. You still need your passport or voter ID at the border.
- **Indian rupees work** in Bhutan (1 ngultrum = 1 rupee). The Indian Embassy says you can carry up to ₹25,000 in notes of ₹500 or smaller.

## Do you need a guide?

Yes. The Department of Tourism says a guide must go with you at all times in Bhutan, including visitors who enter by road and **travel beyond the border towns**. Guides are also needed at dzongs and monuments.

- **Book your guide before you reach the border,** with a hotel or a licensed tour operator. The Department of Tourism's certified tour operators, hotels and guides are listed at [services.bhutan.travel](https://services.bhutan.travel/).
- **You can't hire a self-drive car in Bhutan.** Hire a car with a driver through a tour operator or your hotel, or bring your own car (below).

## Driving your own car to Bhutan

You can drive your own Indian car or motorbike into Bhutan. The Department of Tourism's rules, checked on 21 November 2026:

- **Fee: Nu 4,500 per car, per day,** on top of the SDF. The same applies to motorbikes. A guide is also needed, at extra cost. Bhutan's national broadcaster BBS reported in 2022 that the Road Safety and Transport Authority (RSTA) collects this fee, that it covers other road fees such as the route permit, and that you don't pay it if you don't drive beyond the first checkpoint.
- **Papers:** a valid driving licence (an Indian licence is accepted), insurance, a pollution-control (PUC) certificate and your entry permit. The vehicle must be in good condition and meet RSTA's rules.
- **Not your car?** Carry an authorisation letter from the owner.
- **A guide must ride with you** the whole time. Pre-book one before you reach the border.

For a short trip, a car with a Bhutanese driver often works out simpler than paying the daily car fee.

## A simple 7-day plan by road

| Day | Plan | Night |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Reach Bagdogra or NJP, drive to Jaigaon and Phuentsholing | Phuentsholing |
| 2 | Permit formalities if not done online, then drive to Thimphu (about 5–6 hours, a travel-guide estimate) | Thimphu |
| 3 | Thimphu: Buddha Dordenma, Memorial Chorten, Tashichho Dzong | Thimphu |
| 4 | Over Dochula to Punakha: Punakha Dzong and Chimi Lhakhang | Punakha |
| 5 | Drive to Paro: Rinpung Dzong and the National Museum | Paro |
| 6 | Hike to Tiger's Nest (Paro Taktsang) | Paro |
| 7 | Drive back to Phuentsholing and on to Bagdogra or NJP | Home |

- **Keep a spare day** in the monsoon (June to September), when landslides can close roads.
- **In winter,** passes such as Dochula can get snow. See [Bhutan in December](bhutan-in-december.html#does-it-snow-in-bhutan-in-december).

## What to pack for a road trip to Bhutan

- **A backpack or a light trolley.** It's easier at the immigration office and on hill roads.
- **Motion sickness tablets** if winding roads upset you. The drive from Phuentsholing climbs steeply.
- **Layers.** Phuentsholing is warm and low, but Thimphu and Paro are above 2,000 m and cold at night.
- **Long sleeves and long trousers** for dzongs and temples.
- **Snacks and water** for the long drives.

## Questions

### Can we go to Bhutan by road from India?

Yes. The usual crossing is Jaigaon to Phuentsholing in north Bengal. Bhutan also has road entry points at Samtse, Gelephu and Samdrup Jongkhar. You need a passport or original voter ID, an entry permit, the SDF and a guide.

### How do I go to Bhutan from Siliguri?

Go by road from Siliguri or Bagdogra to Jaigaon and Phuentsholing, about 4–5 hours. Get your entry permit online first or at the Phuentsholing immigration office, then drive on to Thimphu with your guide.

### How do I go to Bhutan from Kolkata?

Take a train to NJP or Hasimara, or fly to Bagdogra, then go by road to Phuentsholing. You can also fly from Kolkata straight to Paro.

### How do I go to Bhutan by road from Delhi?

Most people fly from Delhi to Bagdogra or take a train to NJP, then go by road to Phuentsholing. Driving all the way is possible with your own car, but Bhutan charges Nu 4,500 a day for the car and you need a guide.

### Where is the Phuentsholing permit office?

The Regional Immigration Office is in Phuentsholing, on the border opposite Jaigaon, West Bengal. You can skip the queue by applying online first at immi.gov.bt.

### Can we stay in Phuentsholing without a permit?

Day visits of up to 24 hours to Phuentsholing have no SDF until August 2027, as long as you don't go beyond Rinchending Checkpost. You still show your passport or voter ID at the border. For a longer stay or a trip inland you need the entry permit and the SDF.

### Can I drive my own car to Bhutan from India?

Yes. You pay Nu 4,500 per car per day on top of the SDF, carry your driving licence, insurance, PUC certificate and entry permit, and a guide must travel with you.

### Is a guide compulsory for Indians in Bhutan?

Yes. Bhutan's Department of Tourism says a guide must accompany you at all times, including if you enter by road and go beyond the border towns.
