January is the heart of the Kashmir winter. Chillai Kalan, the 40 coldest days, runs until the end of the month, the hills are usually deep in snow, and parts of Dal Lake can freeze. It's also the month when snow most often shuts the airport and the highway for a day or two. This guide covers January's weather, Srinagar, Pahalgam, Sonamarg and Gulmarg, what happened in January 2026, the train and roads in snow, Republic Day, and what to wear.

## Kashmir weather in January

The India Meteorological Department's (IMD) long-term January averages, 1981–2010:

| Place | Day | Night | Rain and snow |
|---|---|---|---|
| Srinagar | About 7 °C | About −2 °C | About 54 mm, on about 5 days |
| Pahalgam | About 4 °C | About −7 °C | About 120 mm, on about 8 days |
| Gulmarg | About 0.5 °C | About −8 °C | About 158 mm, on about 8 days |

- **January is colder than December** everywhere. Srinagar's average day is about 3 °C colder than in December, and the nights are well below freezing.
- **Pahalgam and Gulmarg get two to three times Srinagar's snow and rain.** That's why the hills stay white even when the city is dry.
- **The coldest January nights on record,** says IMD: −14.4 °C in Srinagar (1893), −18.6 °C in Pahalgam (10 January 1986) and −19.8 °C in Gulmarg (4 January 1991).
- **Check the forecast** on [IMD's Srinagar centre](https://mausam.imd.gov.in/srinagar/) in the week you go, and again the day before you travel.

## Chillai Kalan and the end of January

- **Chillai Kalan** is Kashmir's name for the 40 harshest days of winter. It starts on 21 December and ends around **30–31 January**: news reports differ by a day, and we found no official page that sets the date.
- **After it** come **Chillai Khurd** ("small cold", about 20 days, to about mid-February) and **Chillai Bachha** ("baby cold", about 10 days, to the end of February). Snow can still be heavy then.
- **What it means for you:** freezing taps in the morning, icy roads, short days, and the best chance of deep snow. Plan for warm rooms and spare days.

## How January 2026 went

Last January shows what to expect (all from news reports):

- **A cold, dry start.** Srinagar fell to about **−6 °C** around 8–9 January 2026, the season's coldest night, and parts of **Dal Lake froze** on 9, 10 and 14 January.
- **Big snow on 23 January.** Heavy snow ended a dry spell of about three months. All flights at Srinagar were cancelled that day and the Jammu–Srinagar highway was shut. Flights resumed the next day, but the highway stayed closed.
- **More snow on 27 January.** The highway closed again and morning flights were cancelled. The Mughal Road and the Sonamarg road were shut too.

So: January can bring a frozen lake and blue skies, or a snowstorm that holds you for a day. **Keep at least one spare day** before your flight or train home.

## Srinagar, Pahalgam, Sonamarg and Gulmarg in January

- **Srinagar:** shikara rides on Dal Lake on clear days (boatmen stop when ice or wind makes it unsafe), the Mughal gardens under snow, the old city, and hot kahwa. Houseboats get very cold; ask about heating before you book.
- **Pahalgam:** about 95 km from Srinagar, deep in snow in a normal January, with pine forests along the Lidder river. Side valleys such as Aru are often hard to reach from January to early March. Ask locally before you plan a day there.
- **Sonamarg:** since the Sonamarg (Z-Morh) tunnel opened in January 2025, Sonamarg is reachable in winter, and news reports say the road now stays open most of the season. It can still close for a while after heavy snow. The road beyond, over Zojila to Ladakh, is closed in January.
- **Gulmarg:** the snowiest of the four, with the gondola and the ski season in full swing. See our [Gulmarg in January guide](gulmarg-in-january.html).
- **Gulmarg Winter Carnival:** last winter it ran from 31 December 2025 to 6 January 2026, with a snow marathon on 3 January (news report). No 2027 dates yet.

## Getting to Kashmir in January

- **By air:** fly to Srinagar (SXR). Heavy snow can close the airport for a day, as it did on 23 January 2026. Keep a spare day before your flight home.
- **By train:** Vande Bharat trains run from Jammu Tawi and Katra to Srinagar. Indian Railways says the trains are built to run in temperatures down to −20 °C. On 23 January 2026 the train kept running while flights and the highway stopped, but on 27 and 28 January it didn't run, and the railways ran special Katra–Srinagar trains instead (news reports). Check NTES or IRCTC on the day.
- **By road:** the Jammu–Srinagar highway (NH-44) closes during heavy snow and after landslides. The Mughal Road is usually shut for much of the winter: in 2026 it was closed from 31 December to 11 January (All India Radio). Sinthan Top is closed in winter.
- **Snow chains:** after snow, police often allow only 4x4s or cars with chains on hill roads like Tangmarg–Gulmarg. Ask your driver about the day's rule.

## Republic Day in Kashmir

- **26 January 2027 is a Tuesday,** so it's a 4-day weekend with one day off on Monday 25 January.
- **The main function in Srinagar** is held at Bakshi Stadium, with a full-dress rehearsal two days before. Expect tight security, closed roads near the stadium and patchy mobile data around the day. Plan sightseeing away from the area.

## Phones, money and power cuts

- **Prepaid SIMs from other states usually don't work** in Jammu and Kashmir. Carry a postpaid SIM.
- **Power cuts** are more common in deep winter. Carry a power bank and keep your phone warm.
- **Carry some cash** for shikaras, taxis and small shops, in case card machines or the network are down after snow.

## What to wear in Kashmir in January

What should I wear? Try the [what-to-wear helper](../tools/what-to-wear.html#kashmir) for Srinagar and Gulmarg temperatures by month.

January is colder than December, so add a layer:

| Layer | What to wear |
|---|---|
| 1. Next to your skin | Thermal top and bottom, and a spare set |
| 2. Middle | A thick fleece or sweater, or two thin ones |
| 3. Outside | A heavy, hooded down or padded jacket |
| 4. Extras | Woollen cap, neck warmer, waterproof gloves, two pairs of woollen socks |

- **Shoes:** waterproof boots with a deep grip. Pavements in Srinagar ice over at night.
- **For the snow:** waterproof trousers, or rent a snow coat and gumboots in Gulmarg or Pahalgam.
- **Locals wear a pheran,** a long woollen gown. It's warm, and easy to buy in Srinagar.

## Safety in the cold

- **Never sleep with a gas heater, charcoal or a kangri burning in a closed room.** Each winter, people in Kashmir die from carbon monoxide this way. Switch heaters off before you sleep and keep a window slightly open.
- **Don't walk on frozen parts of Dal Lake.** The ice is thin and uneven.
- **Change out of wet clothes quickly.** Shivering, confusion and clumsiness are signs of hypothermia.
- **Avalanche warnings:** if the authorities warn of avalanches, stay off the slopes and the high roads.

## For foreign visitors

- **Read your government's advice.** The UK's travel advice (updated 7 October 2026, still current on 24 November) is against all travel to Jammu and Kashmir, including Srinagar, Gulmarg, Pahalgam, Sonamarg and the Jammu–Srinagar highway. The only exceptions are flying into or out of Jammu city and travel within it. The US advice says "Do Not Travel" to Jammu and Kashmir, except eastern Ladakh and Leh. Travel against this advice can make your travel insurance invalid.
- **Visa:** apply for the e-Tourist Visa only at [indianvisaonline.gov.in](https://indianvisaonline.gov.in/evisa/tvoa.html).

## Questions

### Is January a good time to visit Kashmir?

Yes, if you want snow and don't mind real cold. IMD's averages are about 7 °C by day and −2 °C at night in Srinagar, and colder in Pahalgam and Gulmarg. It's peak snow season, but heavy snow can stop flights and the highway for a day, so keep a spare day.

### Will there be snow in Kashmir in January?

In the hills, almost always: Gulmarg and Pahalgam get two to three times Srinagar's snow and rain. Srinagar city gets snow on some days. In January 2026 the big snow came on 23 and 27 January.

### Does Dal Lake freeze in January?

Parts of it can, on the coldest nights. In January 2026, parts of the lake froze on 9, 10 and 14 January. It rarely freezes hard enough to walk on, so never try.

### When does Chillai Kalan end?

Around 30–31 January. It starts on 21 December and lasts 40 days. Then come Chillai Khurd (about 20 days) and Chillai Bachha (about 10 days).

### Is Sonamarg open in January?

Usually, yes. Since the Sonamarg tunnel opened in January 2025, the road from Srinagar stays open most of the winter, but it can close for a while after heavy snow. Zojila, beyond Sonamarg, is closed.

### Does the Vande Bharat train to Srinagar run in snow?

Usually. Indian Railways says it's built for −20 °C, and it ran on 23 January 2026 when flights stopped. But it didn't run on 27 and 28 January 2026, so check NTES or IRCTC on the day.

### What should I wear in Kashmir in January?

Thermals, a thick fleece, a heavy hooded jacket, a woollen cap, waterproof gloves, woollen socks and waterproof boots with grip. A pheran from Srinagar is a warm extra layer.
