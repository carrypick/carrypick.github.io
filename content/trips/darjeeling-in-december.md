Darjeeling in December is cold, dry and clear. The days are sunny, the nights are close to freezing, and the views of Kanchenjunga are among the best of the year. Many people hope for snow, but snow in Darjeeling town is rare. This guide covers the weather, the real chances of snow, Tiger Hill at sunrise, the toy train, how to get there and what to wear.

## Darjeeling weather in December and January

Long-term averages for Darjeeling from the India Meteorological Department (IMD, 1991–2020):

| Month | Day | Night | Rain |
|---|---|---|---|
| December | About 14 °C | About 4 °C | About 3 mm |
| January | About 11 °C | About 2 °C | About 11 mm |
| February | About 12 °C | About 3 °C | About 11 mm |

- **January is the coldest month.** The lowest January reading on record is −7.2 °C. The lowest in December is −4.6 °C.
- **December is very dry.** It rains on less than one day in an average December, so most days are clear.
- **Mornings are the clearest.** Kanchenjunga is easiest to see early in the day. Clouds and haze often build up by noon.
- **Sun feels warm, shade feels cold.** Dress in layers that you can take off at midday and put back on by 4 PM.

## Does it snow in Darjeeling?

- **Snow in Darjeeling town is rare and can't be predicted.** On 27 December 2018 the town had light snow and sleet, the first in about ten years. Before that, the last snowfall was on 28 January 2008. Light snow also fell in February 2022.
- **Tiger Hill gets snow a little more often.** It is higher than the town, at about 2,590 m. In late February 2020 Tiger Hill had about an inch of snow.
- **Sandakphu gets snow most winters.** At 3,636 m, on the Nepal border, it is the highest point in West Bengal. Snow there usually comes from late December to March.
- **Last winter was dry.** Sandakphu had no snow in December 2025 or January 2026. Its first snow of the season came only in late February 2026, and late snow in March left more than 100 tourists stuck there.

So go to Darjeeling for the cold, clear weather and the mountain views, not for snow. If snow matters most, choose [Kashmir](kashmir-in-december.html) or [Manali](manali.html), or plan a guided trip to Sandakphu in January or February.

## Getting to Darjeeling

- **By air:** Bagdogra airport (IXB), near Siliguri, is the nearest airport. From there it's a drive of a few hours up the hills by taxi.
- **By train:** New Jalpaiguri (NJP) is the main railway station, with trains from Delhi, Kolkata and many other cities. The toy train line from NJP to Darjeeling is 88 km long.
- **By shared jeep:** shared jeeps to Darjeeling leave from Siliguri. There is usually no shared jeep from the airport itself, so take a taxi or go to Siliguri first.

### The toy train

The Darjeeling Himalayan Railway opened to Darjeeling in 1881. It is a narrow-gauge line, just 2 feet wide, and a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 1999, as one of the Mountain Railways of India.

- **Joy rides are the easiest way to ride it.** From 1 October 2026 four diesel joy rides run between Darjeeling and Ghum. They leave Darjeeling at 9:20 AM and 11:25 AM, and Ghum at 10:25 AM and 12:30 PM. Steam rides also run on the line from Kurseong.
- **Batasia Loop** is the famous spiral between Ghum and Darjeeling, with a war memorial and views of the peaks. Joy rides pass through it.
- **Ghum** is India's highest railway station, at about 2,258 m.
- **Book on IRCTC,** and book early for Christmas and New Year.
- **The full NJP–Darjeeling train can stop after landslides.** In 2024 it was suspended from July to mid-November. Check the latest status before you plan your trip around it.

## Things to do in winter

- **Tiger Hill sunrise.** At dawn, the Kanchenjunga peaks catch the first light before the hills below. On clear days you can also see the tops of Everest, Makalu and Lhotse far away. Jeeps leave Darjeeling at about 4 AM. Since September 2019 every vehicle needs a free pass from the Darjeeling traffic office, collected the day before, and the number of vehicles each day is limited. Your hotel or driver usually arranges it, but ask them early in peak season.
- **Padmaja Naidu Himalayan Zoological Park.** Known for red pandas and snow leopards. In winter it is open from 8:30 AM to 4:00 PM. It is closed on Thursdays.
- **Himalayan Mountaineering Institute,** next to the zoo, with a museum on Everest climbs. Check its closed day before you go.
- **Tea gardens.** Leaves are not picked from December to February, while the bushes rest. You can still walk the gardens and taste tea. The first flush (the first picking) starts around March.
- **Chowrasta and the Mall** for walks and views, the Peace Pagoda, and Ghum Monastery.
- **Day trips:** Mirik (a lake town) and Kalimpong are a few hours away by road.
- **Sandakphu for snow:** the trek or jeep road from Manebhanjan goes through Singalila National Park. You need an entry permit and a registered guide. Darjeeling Police posts a health advice note for visitors to Sandakphu and Phalut; read it before you go, and don't go when police say roads are closed.

## Christmas and New Year in Darjeeling

- **Book early.** Darjeeling is busy from Christmas to New Year, and hotels near the Mall fill up first.
- **Expect traffic on the hill roads,** especially between Ghum and Darjeeling. Leave early for Tiger Hill and the airport.
- **Small festivals come and go.** The Ghum Festival around the toy train has been held from late November to early December in past years. Dates for this winter haven't been announced yet.

## What to wear in Darjeeling in December

What should I wear? Try the [what-to-wear helper](../tools/what-to-wear.html#darjeeling) for day and night temperatures and an outfit list by month.

Darjeeling is a little less cold than Manali or Shimla, but nights still fall near 0 °C, and Tiger Hill at dawn is colder still.

- **Layers:** thermals, a sweater or fleece, and a warm jacket. You will take some off on sunny afternoons.
- **For Tiger Hill at sunrise:** your warmest jacket, a woollen cap, gloves and a neck warmer. You will stand outside in the dark before dawn.
- **Shoes:** comfortable shoes with a good grip. Darjeeling's streets and steps are steep.
- **Sunglasses and sunscreen:** the sun is strong at this height.
- **For Sandakphu:** add waterproof gloves and trousers, and boots for snow.

## Health and safety

- **Never sleep with a heater burning in a closed room.** Gas heaters and coal fires give off carbon monoxide, which can kill without warning. Keep a window slightly open, and switch heaters off before you sleep.
- **Walk carefully on steep paths,** and carry a torch for the walk to Tiger Hill or for early trains.
- **Sandakphu is high, at 3,636 m.** Go slowly, drink water, and come down if you get a bad headache, feel sick or get breathless.
- **Dress for the cold,** and change out of wet clothes quickly.

## Questions

### Does it snow in Darjeeling in December?

Very rarely. Snow in Darjeeling town is rare in any month. The town had light snow on 27 December 2018, the first in about ten years. Sandakphu, much higher up, gets snow most winters, usually from late December to March.

### What is the temperature in Darjeeling in December?

IMD's long-term averages are about 14 °C in the day and 4 °C at night. The coldest December night on record was −4.6 °C. January is colder, at about 11 °C by day and 2 °C at night.

### What should I wear in Darjeeling in December?

Layers: thermals, a sweater or fleece, and a warm jacket, plus a woollen cap, gloves and a neck warmer for the evenings and Tiger Hill at dawn. Wear comfortable shoes with a good grip, because the streets are steep.

### Is December a good time to visit Darjeeling?

Yes, for clear mountain views and dry weather. It rains on less than one day in an average December, so Kanchenjunga is often visible in the mornings. It is cold, and busy from Christmas to New Year, so book early.

### Is the toy train running in December?

Joy rides between Darjeeling and Ghum run all year. From 1 October 2026 there are four a day. The full train from New Jalpaiguri can stop after landslides, so check the status on IRCTC before you plan around it.

### Do I need a pass for Tiger Hill?

Since September 2019 every vehicle going to Tiger Hill needs a free pass from the Darjeeling traffic office, taken the day before, and the number of vehicles is limited. Hotels and drivers usually arrange it. Ask them early in peak season.

### Where can I see snow near Darjeeling?

Sandakphu and Phalut, in Singalila National Park, get snow most winters. You need a park permit and a registered guide. Tiger Hill gets light snow in some years, usually in January or February.

### Darjeeling or Shimla in December?

Both are cold and mostly dry, and neither has snow for certain in December. Darjeeling has the better mountain views, with Kanchenjunga close by. Shimla is easier to reach from Delhi and gets snow more often, mostly in January and February. See our [Shimla in December guide](shimla-in-december.html).
