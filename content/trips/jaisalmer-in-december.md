Jaisalmer, the golden city of the Thar desert, is at its best in winter. December brings warm, sunny days for the fort and the havelis, clear skies over the Sam dunes, and cold, starry nights in the desert camps. This guide covers the temperature by day and night, a simple 2-day and 3-day plan, the dunes and camps, December crowds, getting there and what to wear.

## Jaisalmer temperature in December

Long-term averages for Jaisalmer from the India Meteorological Department (IMD), 1981–2010:

| Month | Day | Night | Rain |
|---|---|---|---|
| December | About 25 °C | About 10–11 °C | About 2 mm |
| January | About 24 °C | About 9 °C | About 1 mm |

- **Warm days, cold nights.** Afternoons are pleasant for walking, but it gets about 15 °C colder after dark.
- **In the desert at night** it feels colder than in the town, because there's nothing to hold the day's warmth and the wind blows across the open sand. Pack for nights close to single digits in a camp.
- **It can freeze, but rarely.** IMD's lowest readings are −0.6 °C on 27 December 1950 and −5.9 °C on 12 January 1967. In recent years, the coldest December night was 1.7 °C, on 31 December 2013.
- **Almost no rain:** IMD counts less than one rainy day in December, on average.
- **The end of December** is colder than the start, and January is the coldest month.
- **Sunset** is at about 5:50 PM in early December and about 6 PM at the end of the month (timeanddate.com). Plan to reach the dunes or Bada Bagh by 5 PM.

## Jaisalmer trip plan: 2 days

**Day 1: The fort and the old city**

1. **Jaisalmer Fort** (Sonar Qila) in the morning. Incredible India calls it the only "living fort" in Rajasthan: nearly 3,000 people still live inside its walls, among shops, hotels and havelis. Visit the palace museum and the Jain temples inside.
2. **Patwon ki Haveli,** one of the largest and most finely carved havelis, five storeys high in a narrow lane. Nearby are other old havelis of the merchant families.
3. **Gadisar Lake** in the late afternoon, for a boat ride and its old gateways and shrines.
4. **Dinner on a rooftop** with a view of the fort lit up at night.

**Day 2: The dunes and a desert camp**

1. **Bada Bagh** in the morning, the royal chhatris (cenotaphs) about 6 km north of the city.
2. **Kuldhara,** the abandoned village about 18 km away, on the way to the dunes.
3. **Sam sand dunes,** about 40 km from the city, for a camel or jeep ride at sunset.
4. **A night in a desert camp,** with folk music and dance, dinner, and the stars. Carry a warm jacket.

## Jaisalmer trip plan: 3 days

Add one of these:

- **Khuri dunes,** a quieter set of dunes south-west of the city, with camel and jeep safaris and camps.
- **Desert National Park,** home to blackbuck, chinkara, desert foxes and the rare Great Indian Bustard, and to birds of prey in winter, says Rajasthan Tourism.
- **Tanot Mata temple and Longewala,** towards the border. Tanot is about 120 km away and run by a Border Security Force trust. Longewala's war memorial marks the battle of 4 December 1971. It's a long day, so start early. Foreign visitors should read the permit note below first.

## The Sam dunes and desert camps

- **What to expect:** Incredible India lists camel and jeep safaris, quad biking, tent camps, folk programmes and stargazing at Sam. Plan around sunrise or sunset.
- **Choose your camp carefully.** Read recent reviews, ask what the price includes (transfers, dinner, the camel ride, the show), and check that the tents have proper blankets and heating for December nights.
- **Follow the local rules.** The district administration sometimes sets rules for the dunes, vehicles and camps in the busy season. Ask your camp or hotel what applies on your dates.
- **Camel rides:** agree the price and the length of the ride before you start. Wear closed shoes: sand gets hot by day and very cold at night.

## Desert Festival 2027 and December events

- **Desert Festival 2027:** Rajasthan Tourism's festivals calendar lists it in Jaisalmer from **18 to 20 February 2027** (checked on 22 November 2026). The city gets very busy during the festival, so book early.
- **In December:** there's no official festival in Jaisalmer itself. On the same calendar, Rajasthan Tourism lists the Kumbhalgarh Festival (1–3 December 2026) and the Ranakpur Jawai Bandh Festival (21–22 December 2026), near Udaipur. In January, the Bikaner Camel Festival is on 9–10 January 2027.

## Christmas and New Year crowds

Christmas to New Year is the busiest week of the winter in Jaisalmer. Desert camps hold New Year's Eve parties, and hotels inside and near the fort fill up early. Book your stay, camp and transport well ahead for 24 December to 2 January, and expect busy roads to Sam at sunset.

## Getting to Jaisalmer

- **By air:** Jaisalmer airport (JSA) has winter-season flights. Air India says it added a daily Mumbai–Jaisalmer flight from 25 October 2026, alongside its Delhi–Jaisalmer flights. Check other airlines on their own sites; schedules here change by season.
- **Or fly to Jodhpur,** which has flights all year, and take a train or road trip to Jaisalmer. It's a long drive across the desert, so plan most of a day.
- **By train:** the station is Jaisalmer (JSM). Rajasthan Tourism says there's a direct train between Jaisalmer and Delhi, and trains run from Jodhpur and Jaipur. Check times on the IRCTC or NTES site. There's no Vande Bharat to Jaisalmer: the Vande Bharat from Delhi goes only as far as Jodhpur.
- **Getting around:** walk inside the fort and the old city. Hire a car or jeep with a driver for Sam, Kuldhara, Khuri and the longer trips.

## Food to try

Rajasthani dishes such as dal baati churma, ker sangri and gatte ki sabzi, plus local sweets. Many rooftop restaurants around the fort serve them with a view.

## What to wear in Jaisalmer in December

Jaisalmer's clothes problem is the gap between the fort at noon and a desert camp at midnight. IMD's December averages are about 25 °C by day and 10–11 °C at night in the town, and the open sand at Sam or Khuri feels colder still.

| When | What to wear |
|---|---|
| Day in the fort and havelis | Light cotton clothes, a cap or hat, sunglasses and a scarf against the sun and sand |
| Sunset on the dunes | A sweater or fleece in your day bag: the temperature falls fast once the sun goes down, at about 5:50–6 PM |
| Night in a desert camp | A warm jacket, a thermal layer underneath, a woollen cap and warm socks |

- **Shoes:** closed walking shoes for the fort's stone lanes and the soft sand. Sandals fill with sand on a camel ride.
- **Sand and wind:** a scarf or buff to cover your nose and mouth on a jeep safari, and a zip pouch for your phone and camera.
- **Temples:** the Jain temples inside the fort expect covered shoulders and knees.
- **Dry desert air:** lip balm, moisturiser and sunscreen.

## Safety and local tips

- **Choose approved guides,** and don't let a driver or guide pick the shop for you.
- **Drink plenty of water** in the day, even when it feels cool.
- **Agree prices first** for camel rides, jeep safaris and taxis.
- **Respect the fort's residents:** it's a living town, so ask before photographing people and their homes.

## For foreign visitors

- **Visa:** apply for the e-Tourist Visa only at the official site, [indianvisaonline.gov.in](https://indianvisaonline.gov.in/evisa/tvoa.html).
- **Border areas:** Jaisalmer is a border district. News reports on a Home Ministry order of 18 June 2026 say foreigners don't need a special permit for Jaisalmer town, Amar Sagar, Ludrava, Kuldhara, Bada Bagh, Akal, Sam, Unda and Khuri, but other areas may need one. Tanot and Longewala aren't on that list, so ask your hotel or the local police before you go.
- **Getting there:** most visitors fly into Delhi or Mumbai, then take a flight to Jaisalmer or Jodhpur, or an overnight train.

## Emergency numbers

- **Emergency (police, fire, ambulance):** 112
- **Ambulance in Rajasthan:** 108
- **Ministry of Tourism helpline, 24 hours, many languages:** 1363 or 1800-11-1363

## Questions

### What is the temperature in Jaisalmer in December?

IMD's long-term averages are about 25 °C by day and 10–11 °C at night. The end of December is colder than the start, and a night in a desert camp feels colder than the town.

### How cold is Jaisalmer at night in December?

About 10–11 °C on average in the town, colder in the desert, and on the coldest nights of some years only a few degrees above freezing. IMD's lowest December reading was −0.6 °C, in 1950. Carry a warm jacket and a cap for the evening.

### Is December a good time to visit Jaisalmer?

Yes. December and January are the best months: sunny, dry and cool. Book early for Christmas and New Year, when desert camps and hotels near the fort fill up.

### What is the Sam sand dunes temperature in December?

There's no official weather station at Sam, so use Jaisalmer's averages: about 25 °C by day and 10–11 °C at night. It feels colder on the open dunes after sunset, so carry warm layers.

### When is the Jaisalmer Desert Festival 2027?

From 18 to 20 February 2027, says Rajasthan Tourism's festivals calendar. Check the official site again closer to the date.

### How many days are enough for Jaisalmer?

Two days are enough for the fort, the havelis, Gadisar Lake and a sunset and night at the Sam dunes. Add a third day for Khuri, the Desert National Park or Tanot and Longewala.

### What should I wear in Jaisalmer in December?

Light clothes, sunglasses and a scarf for the day, and a warm jacket, a warm layer and a woollen cap for the evening and the desert camp. Wear closed shoes for the fort and the sand.

### Is there a Vande Bharat to Jaisalmer?

No. The Vande Bharat from Delhi goes only to Jodhpur. Take a direct train from Delhi to Jaisalmer, or change at Jodhpur.
