The Indian wedding season runs from November to February, and so does the best weather for most of India. The monsoon is over, the skies are clear, and you can choose between a warm beach, a snowy meadow, a palace by a lake or a quiet tea estate in the hills. This page compares the best honeymoon places in India for those months, with links to our full trip guides for each one.

Every fact here comes from our trip guides, where the official source is listed with the date we checked it. Looking for a trip abroad instead? See our [honeymoon destinations outside India](honeymoon-destinations-outside-india.html), with flight times and visa rules for Indians.

## Honeymoon places in India by style

### Beach honeymoon

- **[Goa](goa.html):** warm days, cool evenings and almost no rain from November to February. North Goa is busier, with nightlife, shacks and markets; South Goa is quieter, with wider beaches and more green cover, which suits most couples. Palolem is a curved white-sand bay between two headlands, and Agonda is small and quiet. Christmas and New Year are Goa's busiest weeks.
- **[Andaman](andaman-in-december.html):** clear sea and coral, with ferries from Phoenix Bay Jetty to Swaraj Dweep (Havelock) and Shaheed Dweep (Neil). Indians need no permit to visit the main islands; carry a photo ID. December can still be wet, January is drier and February is the driest.
- **[Kerala](kerala.html):** the classic winter trip is Kochi, then Munnar, then a houseboat night on the backwaters in Alappuzha, ending at a beach in Varkala or Kovalam.

### Snow honeymoon

- **[Gulmarg](gulmarg-in-december.html) and [Kashmir](kashmir-in-december.html):** Gulmarg is a wide snow meadow about 52 km from Srinagar, with a gondola that climbs high up Apharwat. Snow often comes in December, but some early winters are dry; it's surest from late December to February. The coldest weeks, Chillai Kalan, run from 21 December to the end of January. Prepaid SIMs from other states usually don't work in Kashmir, so carry a postpaid SIM.
- **[Manali](manali.html):** India's favourite winter hill station. Snow is possible from December to February, but not every year, and roads close when it falls. Rohtang Pass is closed in winter.
- **[Shimla](shimla-in-december.html):** an easy hill trip with the UNESCO toy train from Kalka. December is mostly dry and cold; snow is much more likely in January and February.

Snow is never guaranteed on a fixed date. If snow matters most to you, January and February are safer than early December.

### Heritage honeymoon: palaces and lakes

- **[Udaipur](udaipur.html):** the city of lakes, with sunny, dry days and cool evenings by Lake Pichola. Two days are enough for the city, and three let you see the forts and temples nearby. The Shilpgram crafts fair is usually from 21 to 30 December.
- **[Jaipur](jaipur.html):** the Pink City, with forts, palaces and markets. Winter runs from mid-December to mid-February, with sunny days and chilly nights. It's about 3.5 to 5 hours by road from Delhi.

### Nature honeymoon: tea hills, forests and rivers

- **[Munnar](munnar-in-december.html):** green tea hills, sunny days and cold nights. It doesn't snow, but frost forms on the grass on the coldest mornings. Eravikulam National Park is open in December and January; book online.
- **[Coorg](coorg-in-december.html):** coffee estates, misty mornings and cool nights. About 257 km by road from Bangalore; there's no airport or railway in Kodagu.
- **[Ooty](ooty-in-december.html):** tea estates and the UNESCO toy train from Mettupalayam. January is dry and sunny, with frosty mornings. Cars from outside the Nilgiris need an e-pass.
- **[Darjeeling](darjeeling-in-december.html):** clear views of Kanchenjunga, sunrise at Tiger Hill and the toy train. December is one of the driest months; snow in town is rare.
- **[Meghalaya](meghalaya-in-december.html):** Shillong, Sohra (Cherrapunji), the glass-clear river at Dawki and the living root bridges. Plan 4 or 5 days. The sun sets around 4:30 PM in winter, so start early.
- **[Rishikesh](rishikesh-in-december.html):** the Ganga aarti, riverside camps and rafting, which is open from September to June. This winter Lakshman Jhula and Ram Jhula are closed, and the Haridwar Kumbh starts on 14 January 2027, so expect crowds around that date.

## Honeymoon destinations in India by month

### November

The season starts. [Goa](goa.html) and [Kerala](kerala.html) are both in their best months (November to February). [Udaipur](udaipur.html), [Jaipur](jaipur.html), [Coorg](coorg-in-december.html), [Ooty](ooty-in-december.html), [Darjeeling](darjeeling-in-december.html) and [Meghalaya](meghalaya-in-december.html) are all in season too. It's too early for reliable snow in the hills.

### December

- **Warm:** [Goa](goa.html), [Kerala](kerala.html) and [Andaman](andaman-in-december.html) (some rain is still possible in Andaman).
- **Cool and dry:** [Udaipur](udaipur.html), [Jaipur](jaipur.html), [Coorg](coorg-in-december.html), [Munnar](munnar-in-december.html), [Rishikesh](rishikesh-in-december.html), and [Meghalaya](meghalaya-in-december.html), where December is the driest month.
- **Mountain views:** [Darjeeling](darjeeling-in-december.html), where the Kanchenjunga views are among the best of the year.
- **A chance of snow:** [Gulmarg](gulmarg-in-december.html) and [Kashmir](kashmir-in-december.html) often get snow in December; [Manali](manali.html) and [Shimla](shimla-in-december.html) may, but it's less likely than in January.

Christmas to New Year is the busiest and costliest week everywhere. Book early, or plan your honeymoon just before or after it. Going for New Year's Eve? See [where to go for New Year 2027](new-year-trips-2027.html).

### January

- **Snow:** the surest month for [Gulmarg](gulmarg-in-december.html) and [Kashmir](kashmir-in-december.html), and snow is more likely in [Shimla](shimla-in-december.html) and [Manali](manali.html) than in December. January is also the coldest month, so pack proper winter clothes.
- **Beach:** [Goa](goa.html) and [Kerala](kerala.html) are still in season, and [Andaman](andaman-in-december.html) is drier than in December.
- **Hills:** [Ooty](ooty-in-december.html) is dry and sunny with frosty mornings, [Coorg](coorg-in-december.html) is drier, and [Munnar](munnar-in-december.html) has its coldest nights.
- **Heritage:** [Udaipur](udaipur.html) is about 25 °C by day and 7 °C at night, with almost no rain.

### February

- **Beach:** [Andaman](andaman-in-december.html) is at its driest. [Goa](goa.html) and [Kerala](kerala.html) are at the end of their best season.
- **Snow:** [Gulmarg](gulmarg-in-december.html) and [Shimla](shimla-in-december.html) still have their best chance of snow.
- **Heritage:** [Jaipur](jaipur.html)'s winter lasts to mid-February, and [Udaipur](udaipur.html) is in season until March.
- **Nature:** [Meghalaya](meghalaya-in-december.html), [Coorg](coorg-in-december.html), [Munnar](munnar-in-december.html) and [Rishikesh](rishikesh-in-december.html) are all still in season.

Valentine's Day 2027 is a Sunday. For every February trip, in India and abroad, with festival dates, see [where to go in February](where-to-go-in-february.html).

## Questions

### Which place is good for honeymoon in India?

It depends on what you both like. For a beach, Goa, Andaman or Kerala. For snow, Gulmarg and Kashmir, Manali or Shimla. For palaces and lakes, Udaipur or Jaipur. For quiet green hills, Munnar, Coorg, Ooty, Darjeeling or Meghalaya.

### What are the best honeymoon destinations in India in December?

Goa, Kerala and Andaman for warm beaches; Udaipur, Jaipur and Rishikesh for sunny, cool days; Darjeeling for clear mountain views; Meghalaya, Munnar and Coorg for quiet hills; and Gulmarg and Kashmir for a good chance of snow. Christmas to New Year is the busiest week, so book early.

### Which honeymoon places in India are best in January and February?

January is the surest month for snow in Gulmarg and Kashmir, and Shimla and Manali are more likely to get snow than in December. In February, Andaman is at its driest, Goa and Kerala are at the end of their season, and Jaipur's winter lasts until mid-February.

### Where can we see snow on our honeymoon in India?

Gulmarg in Kashmir is the surest, from late December to February. Manali and Shimla can also get snow from December to February, but not every year. Munnar, Ooty, Coorg and Meghalaya don't get snow.

### What are the best honeymoon places in Rajasthan?

Udaipur, the city of lakes, and Jaipur, the Pink City. Both are sunny and dry in winter, with cold nights, from about October to March.

### Which are the best low-budget honeymoon places in India?

Places you can reach by train or road save the most, like Coorg or Ooty from Bangalore, Jaipur or Rishikesh from Delhi, or Goa by train. Avoid the week from Christmas to New Year, when prices are highest. Add your own amounts in our [trip budget calculator](../tools/trip-budget-calculator.html) to compare.

### What should we pack for a honeymoon in India?

See our [honeymoon packing list](../guides/honeymoon-packing-list.html). For Kashmir, Manali or Shimla in winter, you need thermals, a warm jacket, gloves and shoes with a good grip; for Goa or Andaman, light clothes, sunscreen and sandals.
