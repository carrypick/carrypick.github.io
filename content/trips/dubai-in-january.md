January is Dubai's coolest month and one of its best. Days are warm and sunny, evenings are pleasant for the souks, the creek and the desert, and the sea is cool but swimmable. The Dubai Shopping Festival runs until 17 January, with drone shows over the sea every night, and the outdoor parks, Global Village and the Miracle Garden, are in full season. It's also busy with big events, so plan your dates around them. This guide covers January's weather, the Shopping Festival, events week by week, winter fog at the airport, where to stay and the visa for Indians.

## Dubai weather in January

Long-term averages for Dubai from the UAE's National Center of Meteorology (NCM), published by the World Meteorological Organization:

| Month | Day | Night | Rain |
|---|---|---|---|
| December | About 26.2 °C | About 15.4 °C | About 14 mm, on about 2 days |
| **January** | **About 24.0 °C** | **About 13.7 °C** | **About 11 mm, on about 2 days** |
| February | About 24.6 °C | About 14.5 °C | About 36 mm, on about 4 days |

- **January is the coolest month.** Sunny days around 24 °C, and nights around 14 °C. Dubai's lowest January temperature on record is about 1 °C (Dubai's met office, as quoted on Wikipedia).
- **The desert is colder at night.** A dune camp after sunset feels much colder than the city, so take a warm jacket for an evening safari.
- **Rain is rare but heavy when it comes.** A single storm can flood roads for a day.
- **Morning fog:** on winter mornings, NCM often issues fog alerts from about midnight to 9 or 10 AM, and fog has diverted dozens of flights at Dubai airport, most recently on 3 January 2026 (news reports). Leave extra time for early-morning connections, and check your airline's app.
- **The sun is still strong at midday.** Wear sunscreen and a cap.

## Dubai Shopping Festival, January 2027

The 32nd Dubai Shopping Festival (DSF) runs from **4 December 2026 to 17 January 2027**, says the Dubai Media Office (23 Nov 2026). In January:

- **DSF Light Show:** drone and light displays over the bay between The Beach at JBR and Bluewaters, **every night until 17 January**, with drone shows at 8 PM and 10 PM. Free to watch from the beach. Timings can change, so check [Visit Dubai's DSF page](https://www.visitdubai.com/en/festivals-and-events/dsf) on the day.
- **Market Outside The Box (MOTB):** an outdoor market with food, crafts and music at The Square, Nad Al Sheba, **8 to 24 January 2027**.
- **DSF Nights at Festival Bay,** Dubai Festival City Mall: the IMAGINE water, light and fire show daily, and Looney Tunes characters from 1 to 10 January.
- **DSF Auto Season:** car and bike shows, including Middle East Cars and Bikes Week at Dubai Studio City on 9 and 10 January.
- **Mall offers and raffles** run across the city until 17 January.

We don't show prices or discounts: check them in the shops. No DSF fireworks had been announced when we checked.

## Dubai events in January 2027

- **Friday 1 January:** New Year's Day, a UAE public holiday, so the first weekend is busy.
- **Sunday 17 January: the Dubai Marathon,** says its [official site](https://www.dubaimarathon.org). Some roads close in the morning. It's also the last day of DSF.
- **21 to 24 January: the Hero Dubai Desert Classic** golf tournament at Emirates Golf Club, says its [official site](https://dubaidesertclassic.com).
- **25 to 28 January: WHX (World Health Expo) Dubai,** the big healthcare expo, at Dubai Exhibition Centre in Expo City and Dubai World Trade Centre, says its [official site](https://www.worldhealthexpo.com/events/healthcare/dubai/en.html). Hotels near Sheikh Zayed Road and Expo City fill up that week, and it overlaps with the Republic Day weekend from India.
- **All month: Global Village** (Season 31, open since 14 October 2026 until May 2027, from 4 PM daily; Tuesdays are for families and ladies only, except on public holidays), and the **Dubai Miracle Garden** (8 October 2026 to 31 May 2027, 9 AM to 9 PM), say their official sites.

**Republic Day** in India is on **Tuesday 26 January 2027.** With Monday off, that's a 4-day weekend, long enough for Dubai with an Abu Dhabi day trip. Flights from India fill up, so book early.

**Ramadan** is expected to begin on about **8 February 2027**, depending on the moon, say astronomers quoted in the news. January is fully before Ramadan.

## What to do in Dubai in January

January's cool weather is the time for things that are too hot later in the year:

- **A desert safari** with dune drives and a camp dinner. Take a jacket.
- **The beach:** JBR, Kite Beach and Jumeirah public beaches. The sea is cool, about 21 °C, but swimmable for most people.
- **Walking tours** of Al Fahidi, the creek and the gold and spice souks, with an abra ride.
- **Evenings outdoors** at Global Village, the DSF drone shows and the Dubai Fountain.
- **Abu Dhabi for a day:** the Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque and the Louvre Abu Dhabi.

## Before you fly: the visa for Indians

- **Most Indians need a UAE visit visa before the flight,** through a tourism company, an airline or a hotel. Our [Dubai trip guide](dubai-trip-from-india.html#dubai-visa-for-indians) explains the options and the GDRFA fees.
- **Visa on arrival** is only for Indian passport holders with a US visa or green card, or a UK or EU residence visa (and some other residence permits), each valid for at least 6 months, says Dubai's GDRFA (page updated 24 Nov 2026). It gives 14 days, extendable once by 14 days, and needs online pre-approval before travel.
- **Customs:** UAE rules ban some things Indians often carry, such as paan and poppy seeds. See our [UAE customs rules](../baggage/customs/uae-dubai.html).

## What to wear in Dubai in January

Light clothes by day, and a sweater or light jacket for the evenings, the desert and cold malls. Cover your shoulders and knees in malls, souks and government buildings, and wear long, loose clothes and (for women) a headscarf at mosques. Swimwear is for the beach and pool only.

## Questions

### Is January a good time to visit Dubai?

Yes, one of the best. It's Dubai's coolest month: about 24 °C by day and 14 °C at night, with rain on only about 2 days, by NCM's averages. It's good for the desert, the beach and outdoor evenings, and the Shopping Festival runs until 17 January.

### Is Dubai cold in January?

Not by day. Nights are cool, about 14 °C, and the desert is colder after sunset, so take a sweater or light jacket for the evenings and a desert safari.

### When is the Dubai Shopping Festival 2027?

From 4 December 2026 to 17 January 2027, says the Dubai Media Office. The nightly drone shows at JBR run until 17 January.

### Can you swim in Dubai in January?

Yes, but the sea is cool, about 21 °C. Many people swim at public beaches and hotel pools. Swim only at beaches with lifeguards.

### Is Dubai crowded in January?

Busy in the first week, again around the Dubai Marathon and the last DSF weekend (16 and 17 January), and in the last week, when WHX and the Republic Day weekend from India overlap. Hotels are easier to find in the second week.

### Is Ramadan in January 2027?

No. Ramadan is expected to begin on about 8 February 2027, depending on the moon sighting, so January is before it.
