## Short flights from India: 3 hours or less

Short trips abroad mean less time in the air and less tiredness, so you get more days of holiday. They also suit a long weekend.

- **[Nepal](nepal-in-december.html):** the closest trip abroad. Indians need no visa, and you can even go by road through a border crossing. Kathmandu's temples, Pokhara's lake and Himalayan sunrises. Best for families and first trips abroad.
- **[Bhutan](bhutan-in-december.html):** a short flight to Paro, or [by road through Phuentsholing](bhutan-by-road.html). Indians need no visa, but there is an entry permit and a daily Sustainable Development Fee. Dzongs, mountain passes and clean air.
- **[Sri Lanka](sri-lanka-trip-from-india.html):** close to South India and on the same clock as India. Indians get a free ETA online. Tea hills, Sigiriya, beaches and Ramayana sites.
- **[Maldives](maldives-trip-from-india.html):** a short flight from South India and Mumbai. Indians get a visa on arrival. Best for a beach honeymoon.
- **[Kazakhstan](kazakhstan-trip-from-india.html):** direct flights from Delhi and Mumbai to Almaty, about 2½ hours from Delhi. Indians need no visa for up to 14 days. A modern city below snowy mountains, the Shymbulak ski resort, lakes and canyons.
- **[Dubai](dubai-trip-from-india.html):** a short flight from most big Indian cities. Malls, the desert, theme parks and family trips. Indians need a visa before flying, usually through a tourism company, airline or hotel.

## Southeast Asia: 4 to 6 hours

- **[Thailand](thailand-trip-from-india.html):** Bangkok, Phuket and Krabi. Indians don't need a visa for a tourist stay of up to 30 days. Beaches, islands, temples and street food; good for friends, couples and families.
- **[Vietnam](vietnam-trip-from-india.html):** Ha Long Bay, Hanoi, Hoi An and Ho Chi Minh City. Apply for the e-visa online before you fly. Cool winters in the north and warm, dry winters in the south.
- **[Malaysia](malaysia-trip-from-india.html):** Kuala Lumpur, Genting, Langkawi and Penang. Indians don't need a visa for up to 30 days until 31 December 2026. Easy Indian food, so it's good for families.
- **[Singapore](singapore-trip-from-india.html):** gardens, Sentosa, Universal Studios and clean, safe streets. Indians need a visa through an agent. The easiest city trip abroad with kids. Many Indians combine it with Malaysia: see the [Singapore + Malaysia combo](malaysia-trip-from-india.html#singapore-malaysia-combo).
- **[Bali](bali-trip-from-india.html):** the longest flight on this page. Indians get a visa on arrival. Rice terraces, temples, villas and beaches; a favourite honeymoon trip.

## East Asia: Hong Kong and Japan

- **[Hong Kong](hong-kong-trip-from-india.html):** about 5 hours from Delhi. Indians need no visa for up to 14 days after a free online pre-arrival registration. Disneyland, Ocean Park, Victoria Peak and the harbour; a short city trip that suits families.
- **[Japan](japan-trip-from-india.html):** the longest flight on this page, about 8 hours from Delhi to Tokyo. Indians need a visa, now an eVISA through VFS. Tokyo, Mount Fuji, Kyoto, Osaka and bullet trains; cherry blossom in late March and early April.

## Mauritius: an island with no visa

- **[Mauritius](mauritius-trip-from-india.html):** blue lagoons, beach resorts, Grand Bassin's Shiva temple, Chamarel and Le Morne, with Indian food everywhere. Indians need no visa: carry a return ticket and a hotel booking, and fill in the free travel form online. Direct flights from Delhi, Mumbai, Chennai and Bengaluru; a favourite honeymoon.

## Europe: Switzerland, Paris and Italy

- **[Europe](europe-trip-from-india.html):** the classic first Europe trip is about 10 days in Paris, Switzerland and Italy, by train. One Schengen visa covers all three: apply to the country where you spend the most nights, at least 15 days before you fly. Direct flights from Delhi, Mumbai and Bengaluru to Zurich and Paris.

## The UK: London, Oxford and Edinburgh

- **[UK](uk-trip-from-india.html):** London's palaces, free museums and the Thames, day trips to Oxford and Windsor, and Edinburgh's castle and old town. Direct flights to London from Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, Ahmedabad and Amritsar. Indians apply online for the Standard Visitor visa, £135 for up to 6 months, with a decision usually within 3 weeks; the UK ETA isn't open to Indian passports.

## Georgia: mountains, old churches and snow

- **[Georgia](georgia-trip-from-india.html):** Tbilisi's old town, Gergeti Trinity Church below Mount Kazbek, wine villages, and skiing at Gudauri from late December. Indians apply for an e-visa online, or need no visa with a valid US, UK, EU or Schengen visa or residence permit. There are no direct flights from India now, so fly one-stop via Dubai, Sharjah, Doha or Istanbul.

## Turkey and Azerbaijan: Istanbul, Cappadocia and Baku

- **[Turkey](turkey-trip-from-india.html):** Istanbul's mosques, bazaars and the Bosphorus, then hot air balloons and cave hotels in Cappadocia. Direct flights from Delhi and Mumbai to Istanbul. Most Indians need a visa from a Turkish mission; with a valid US, UK, Schengen or Irish visa or residence permit, a one-month e-visa online.
- **[Azerbaijan](azerbaijan-trip-from-india.html):** Baku's walled Old City and Flame Towers, the Ateshgah fire temple, mud volcanoes at Gobustan, and an easy add-on to Georgia. Indians apply for an e-visa online: 3 working days, or 3 hours for an urgent one.

## Australia: Sydney, Melbourne and the Great Ocean Road

- **[Australia](australia-trip-from-india.html):** Sydney's harbour and Opera House, the Blue Mountains, Melbourne's laneways and trams, and the Great Ocean Road. Direct flights from Delhi to Sydney and Melbourne, and from Bengaluru to Sydney, about 12 hours. Indians apply online for the Visitor visa (subclass 600), from AUD 250; the ETA and eVisitor aren't open to Indian passports. The seasons are the opposite of India's.

## Best international trips from India in December

December and January are holiday months in India, and they suit these trips best:

- **[Thailand](thailand-trip-from-india.html):** the cool, dry season. Bangkok has almost no rain in December.
- **[Sri Lanka](sri-lanka-trip-from-india.html):** some rain on the west coast in December, drier in January.
- **[Maldives](maldives-trip-from-india.html):** warm sea and the start of the drier months.
- **[Dubai](dubai-trip-from-india.html):** warm days and cool evenings, the best time of year there.
- **[Malaysia](malaysia-trip-from-india.html):** good for Langkawi and Penang; avoid the east coast islands, where the monsoon is at its peak.
- **[Vietnam](vietnam-trip-from-india.html):** dry and warm in the south; cool in Hanoi.
- **[Hong Kong](hong-kong-trip-from-india.html):** its driest month, about 20 °C by day; cool evenings.
- **[Japan](japan-trip-from-india.html):** cold (Tokyo about 12 °C by day, 4 °C at night) but mostly dry and sunny, with winter lights.
- **[Mauritius](mauritius-trip-from-india.html):** warm, about 31 °C by day, with rain on about 6 days in Port Louis; the start of the busy season.
- **[Europe](europe-trip-from-india.html):** cold, with Christmas markets and snow in the Swiss Alps; Zurich averages about −2 °C at night.
- **[Nepal](nepal-in-december.html) and [Bhutan](bhutan-in-december.html):** clear mountain views and sunny days, but very cold nights.
- **[Kazakhstan](kazakhstan-trip-from-india.html):** snow and skiing at Shymbulak above Almaty; days around 0 °C and nights well below freezing.
- **[Georgia](georgia-trip-from-india.html):** snow in the mountains and skiing in Gudauri from 20 December; Tbilisi is cold, about 4 °C on average.
- **[Turkey](turkey-trip-from-india.html):** the low season: Istanbul about 11 °C by day and rainy, Cappadocia below freezing at night.
- **[Azerbaijan](azerbaijan-trip-from-india.html):** cool and windy in Baku, about 10 °C by day, with little rain.
- **[UK](uk-trip-from-india.html):** cold, about 8 °C by day in London and dark by 4 PM, but with Christmas lights and markets.
- **[Australia](australia-trip-from-india.html):** early summer: Sydney about 26 °C by day, Melbourne about 24 °C, and beach weather; the Australian school holidays make it busy.
- **Less ideal in December:** [Singapore](singapore-trip-from-india.html) (its wettest month, with short showers) and [Bali](bali-trip-from-india.html) (the wet season).

Christmas to New Year is the busiest time everywhere, so book early. Going away for New Year's Eve? ## Trips abroad without a visa, or with an easy visa

Many people asking for a "cheap" foreign trip really want a short flight and an easy visa, with no visa office and no long wait. These are the simplest for an Indian passport right now:

- **No visa at all:** [Nepal](nepal-in-december.html), [Bhutan](bhutan-in-december.html) (Bhutan has an entry permit and a daily fee) and [Mauritius](mauritius-trip-from-india.html) (carry a return ticket and a hotel booking).
- **No visa for a short tourist stay:** [Kazakhstan](kazakhstan-trip-from-india.html) (14 days), [Thailand](thailand-trip-from-india.html) (30 days), [Malaysia](malaysia-trip-from-india.html) (30 days, until 31 December 2026) and [Hong Kong](hong-kong-trip-from-india.html) (14 days, after a free online pre-arrival registration).
- **A free visa online, or a visa on arrival:** [Sri Lanka](sri-lanka-trip-from-india.html) (free ETA), the [Maldives](maldives-trip-from-india.html) and [Bali](bali-trip-from-india.html) (visa on arrival).
- **Apply before you fly:** [Vietnam](vietnam-trip-from-india.html) (e-visa online), [Georgia](georgia-trip-from-india.html) (e-visa online, or none with a US, UK or Schengen visa), [Azerbaijan](azerbaijan-trip-from-india.html) (e-visa online), [Turkey](turkey-trip-from-india.html) (visa from a Turkish mission, or an e-visa with a US, UK, Schengen or Irish visa), [Singapore](singapore-trip-from-india.html) (through an agent in India), [Japan](japan-trip-from-india.html) (eVISA through VFS), [Australia](australia-trip-from-india.html) (Visitor visa online), [UK](uk-trip-from-india.html) (Standard Visitor visa), [Europe](europe-trip-from-india.html) (Schengen visa, at least 15 days ahead) and [Dubai](dubai-trip-from-india.html) (through a tourism company, airline or hotel).

Rules change, and the officer at the airport decides on entry. Check our [visa guide](../guides/visa-free-countries-for-indians.html) and the trip guide before you book, and carry your return ticket and hotel booking.

## Questions

### Which is the best international trip from India for a first time?

Nepal, Sri Lanka, Thailand and Singapore are the easiest first trips. Nepal needs no visa and is a very short flight. Sri Lanka is close with a free ETA. Thailand needs no visa for 30 days and has direct flights from many cities. Singapore needs a visa, but is very easy to get around.

### Which foreign countries can Indians visit without a visa?

Among the trips on this page, Nepal, Bhutan and Mauritius need no visa, and Thailand and Malaysia (until 31 December 2026) need no visa for a short tourist stay. Sri Lanka gives a free ETA online, and the Maldives and Bali give a visa on arrival. See our [visa guide](../guides/visa-free-countries-for-indians.html) for the official rule for each country.

### Which international trip from India has the shortest flight?

Nepal is the closest, about 820 km from Delhi. Bhutan, Sri Lanka and the Maldives are also short flights, about 2 to 3 hours or less from the nearest big Indian city.

### Where should I go abroad from India in December?

Thailand, Sri Lanka, the Maldives, Dubai, Malaysia (Langkawi and Penang) and southern Vietnam have good weather in December. Nepal and Bhutan have clear skies but cold nights. Singapore and Bali are in their wettest season.

### Which foreign trip is best for a family with kids?

Singapore, Dubai, Malaysia and Thailand are the easiest with kids: theme parks, easy food and simple transport. Sri Lanka and Nepal are good too, with short flights.

### Which foreign trip is best for a honeymoon from India?

The Maldives, Mauritius and Bali are India's favourite honeymoon trips abroad, and Switzerland is the dream for many couples. Thailand's islands and Sri Lanka's hills and beaches are also popular.

### How do I make a foreign trip cost less?

Pick a country with a short flight and an easy visa, travel outside Christmas, New Year and school holidays, and book flights early. Use our [trip budget calculator](../tools/trip-budget-calculator.html) to add up your own costs.
