## Short flights from India: 3 hours or less

Short trips abroad mean less time in the air and less tiredness, so you get more days of holiday. They also suit a long weekend.

- **[Nepal](nepal-in-december.html):** the closest trip abroad. Indians need no visa, and you can even go by road through a border crossing. Kathmandu's temples, Pokhara's lake and Himalayan sunrises. Best for families and first trips abroad.
- **[Bhutan](bhutan-in-december.html):** a short flight to Paro, or [by road through Phuentsholing](bhutan-by-road.html). Indians need no visa, but there is an entry permit and a daily Sustainable Development Fee. Dzongs, mountain passes and clean air.
- **[Sri Lanka](sri-lanka-trip-from-india.html):** close to South India and on the same clock as India. Indians get a free ETA online. Tea hills, Sigiriya, beaches and Ramayana sites.
- **[Maldives](maldives-trip-from-india.html):** a short flight from South India and Mumbai. Indians get a visa on arrival. Best for a beach honeymoon.
- **[Dubai](dubai-trip-from-india.html):** a short flight from most big Indian cities. Malls, the desert, theme parks and family trips. Indians need a visa before flying, usually through a tourism company, airline or hotel.

## Southeast Asia: 4 to 6 hours

- **[Thailand](thailand-trip-from-india.html):** Bangkok, Phuket and Krabi. Indians don't need a visa for a tourist stay of up to 30 days. Beaches, islands, temples and street food; good for friends, couples and families.
- **[Vietnam](vietnam-trip-from-india.html):** Ha Long Bay, Hanoi, Hoi An and Ho Chi Minh City. Apply for the e-visa online before you fly. Cool winters in the north and warm, dry winters in the south.
- **[Malaysia](malaysia-trip-from-india.html):** Kuala Lumpur, Genting, Langkawi and Penang. Indians don't need a visa for up to 30 days until 31 December 2026. Easy Indian food, so it's good for families.
- **[Singapore](singapore-trip-from-india.html):** gardens, Sentosa, Universal Studios and clean, safe streets. Indians need a visa through an agent. The easiest city trip abroad with kids. Many Indians combine it with Malaysia: see the [Singapore + Malaysia combo](malaysia-trip-from-india.html#singapore-malaysia-combo).
- **[Bali](bali-trip-from-india.html):** the longest flight on this page. Indians get a visa on arrival. Rice terraces, temples, villas and beaches; a favourite honeymoon trip.

## Best international trips from India in December

December and January are holiday months in India, and they suit these trips best:

- **[Thailand](thailand-trip-from-india.html):** the cool, dry season. Bangkok has almost no rain in December.
- **[Sri Lanka](sri-lanka-trip-from-india.html):** some rain on the west coast in December, drier in January.
- **[Maldives](maldives-trip-from-india.html):** warm sea and the start of the drier months.
- **[Dubai](dubai-trip-from-india.html):** warm days and cool evenings, the best time of year there.
- **[Malaysia](malaysia-trip-from-india.html):** good for Langkawi and Penang; avoid the east coast islands, where the monsoon is at its peak.
- **[Vietnam](vietnam-trip-from-india.html):** dry and warm in the south; cool in Hanoi.
- **[Nepal](nepal-in-december.html) and [Bhutan](bhutan-in-december.html):** clear mountain views and sunny days, but very cold nights.
- **Less ideal in December:** [Singapore](singapore-trip-from-india.html) (its wettest month, with short showers) and [Bali](bali-trip-from-india.html) (the wet season).

Christmas to New Year is the busiest time everywhere, so book early. Comparing with trips inside India too? See [where to go in December and January](where-to-go-in-december-january.html).

## Trips abroad without a visa, or with an easy visa

Many people asking for a "cheap" foreign trip really want a short flight and an easy visa, with no visa office and no long wait. These are the simplest for an Indian passport right now:

- **No visa at all:** [Nepal](nepal-in-december.html) and [Bhutan](bhutan-in-december.html) (Bhutan has an entry permit and a daily fee).
- **No visa for a short tourist stay:** [Thailand](thailand-trip-from-india.html) (30 days) and [Malaysia](malaysia-trip-from-india.html) (30 days, until 31 December 2026).
- **A free visa online, or a visa on arrival:** [Sri Lanka](sri-lanka-trip-from-india.html) (free ETA), the [Maldives](maldives-trip-from-india.html) and [Bali](bali-trip-from-india.html) (visa on arrival).
- **Apply before you fly:** [Vietnam](vietnam-trip-from-india.html) (e-visa online), [Singapore](singapore-trip-from-india.html) (through an agent in India) and [Dubai](dubai-trip-from-india.html) (through a tourism company, airline or hotel).

Rules change, and the officer at the airport decides on entry. Check our [visa guide](../guides/visa-free-countries-for-indians.html) and the trip guide before you book, and carry your return ticket and hotel booking.

## Questions

### Which is the best international trip from India for a first time?

Nepal, Sri Lanka, Thailand and Singapore are the easiest first trips. Nepal needs no visa and is a very short flight. Sri Lanka is close with a free ETA. Thailand needs no visa for 30 days and has direct flights from many cities. Singapore needs a visa, but is very easy to get around.

### Which foreign countries can Indians visit without a visa?

Among the trips on this page, Nepal and Bhutan need no visa, and Thailand and Malaysia (until 31 December 2026) need no visa for a short tourist stay. Sri Lanka gives a free ETA online, and the Maldives and Bali give a visa on arrival. See our [visa guide](../guides/visa-free-countries-for-indians.html) for the official rule for each country.

### Which international trip from India has the shortest flight?

Nepal is the closest, about 820 km from Delhi. Bhutan, Sri Lanka and the Maldives are also short flights, about 2 to 3 hours or less from the nearest big Indian city.

### Where should I go abroad from India in December?

Thailand, Sri Lanka, the Maldives, Dubai, Malaysia (Langkawi and Penang) and southern Vietnam have good weather in December. Nepal and Bhutan have clear skies but cold nights. Singapore and Bali are in their wettest season.

### Which foreign trip is best for a family with kids?

Singapore, Dubai, Malaysia and Thailand are the easiest with kids: theme parks, easy food and simple transport. Sri Lanka and Nepal are good too, with short flights.

### Which foreign trip is best for a honeymoon from India?

The Maldives and Bali are India's favourite honeymoon trips abroad. Thailand's islands and Sri Lanka's hills and beaches are also popular.

### How do I make a foreign trip cost less?

Pick a country with a short flight and an easy visa, travel outside Christmas, New Year and school holidays, and book flights early. Use our [trip budget calculator](../tools/trip-budget-calculator.html) to add up your own costs.
