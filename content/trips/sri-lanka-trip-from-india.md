Sri Lanka is one of the closest trips abroad from India. Direct flights leave from many Indian cities, the clock is the same as India's, and Indians get a free tourist visa (ETA) for 30 days. In one week you can see an old rock fortress, the Temple of the Tooth in Kandy, tea hills, a famous train ride and the beaches of the south. ## Sri Lanka visa for Indians: the free ETA

Since 25 May 2026, Indians are among the nationals of 40 countries who get a tourist visa (ETA) free of charge for 30 days, says the official ETA site of Sri Lanka's Department of Immigration and Emigration (read 21 Nov 2026).

- **You still have to apply.** All foreign nationals, including Indians, must get an Electronic Travel Authorization (ETA) before they arrive in Sri Lanka, says the ETA site. It's free for Indians, but it isn't automatic.
- **Apply only on the official site:** [eta.gov.lk](https://www.eta.gov.lk/slvisa/). The High Commission of Sri Lanka in New Delhi links its visa service to the same site.
- **30 days, double entry.** The free visa is valid for 30 days from your first arrival. If you leave and come back, you get only the days left of those 30.
- **Staying longer?** You can apply for an extension, but you pay the normal visa fee for it.
- **Ordinary, diplomatic, official and service passports** all qualify, says the ETA site.
- **The free scheme has changed before.** Read the current notice on eta.gov.lk before you fly.

## A simple 6-day Sri Lanka itinerary

This plan makes a loop from Colombo through the Cultural Triangle and the hills. The roads are slow and winding, so start each day early and don't plan too much.

| Day | Plan | Night |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Land at Colombo. Galle Face Green at sunset, a walk around the Fort area | Colombo |
| 2 | Drive to Sigiriya. Dambulla cave temple on the way | Sigiriya or Dambulla |
| 3 | Climb Sigiriya rock early, before the heat. Drive to Kandy | Kandy |
| 4 | Temple of the Tooth in the morning, Kandy Lake. Drive up to Nuwara Eliya past Ramboda's Hanuman temple and the Sita Amman Temple | Nuwara Eliya |
| 5 | The hill train towards Ella through tea country. Nine Arches Bridge at Demodara | Ella |
| 6 | Little Adam's Peak at sunrise, then the long drive back to the airport and fly home | |

- **Beach version:** after Kandy (day 4), drive down to Galle Fort and the south coast for the last two days instead of the hills.
- **Only 5 days?** Skip Ella. Spend day 5 in Nuwara Eliya and drive back to Colombo on day 6.
- **7 to 10 days?** Add Ella and Galle both, a safari at a national park, or Trincomalee on the east coast from May to September.
- **With kids or parents?** Pick either the hills or the beaches. Long drives every day tire everyone.
- **Climbing Sigiriya:** go early, carry water and your passport, and follow the staff's instructions about wild elephants and wasps, says the Central Cultural Fund.

## The Ramayana trail in Sri Lanka

Sri Lanka Tourism says the island keeps the memories of the Ramayana in over 50 locations. Many are near Nuwara Eliya and Kandy, so they fit into the plan above. Places on its Ramayana list (read 21 Nov 2026):

| Place | Where | The story linked to it |
|---|---|---|
| Sita Amman Temple | Seetha Eliya, about 3 km from Nuwara Eliya | Sita is believed to have bathed in the stream beside it; there is a temple for Rama, Sita, Lakshmana and Hanuman |
| Gayathri Peedam | Nuwara Eliya, about 5 km from town | Where Ravana's son Meghanath is believed to have prayed to Lord Shiva |
| Sri Baktha Hanuman Temple | Ramboda, on the Kandy to Nuwara Eliya road | Where Hanuman is believed to have searched for Sita |
| Sita Kotuwa and Gurulupotha | Near Hasalaka, about 65 km from Kandy, with a 2 km trek down | Believed to be where Sita was kept before Ashoka Vatika |
| Ussangoda | South coast, off the Matara to Hambantota road | Believed to be an area burnt by Hanuman |
| Muneeshwaram | About 1.2 km from Chilaw town, about 86 km from Colombo | A Shiva temple linked to Lord Rama; open 6 AM to 8 PM |
| Thiru Koneswaram and Kanniya hot wells | Trincomalee, on the east coast | A Shiva temple inside the Dutch Fort, and seven wells linked to Ravana |

- **For a Ramayana trip,** spend two nights near Nuwara Eliya. Sita Amman Temple, Gayathri Peedam and the Hanuman temple at Ramboda are all close by.
- **Trincomalee and Ussangoda are far** from the Kandy hills. Add them only on a longer trip.
- **Dress modestly** at temples, and take off your shoes where asked.

## Sri Lanka weather by month

Long-term averages (1961–1990) from Sri Lanka's National Meteorological Centre, published by the World Meteorological Organization:

| Place | December | January | November rain |
|---|---|---|---|
| Colombo | About 30 °C by day, 23 °C at night, 175 mm of rain on about 10 days | About 31 °C and 22 °C, 58 mm on about 5 days | About 414 mm on 15 days |
| Kandy | About 28 °C and 20 °C, 196 mm on about 14 days | About 28 °C and 18 °C, 79 mm on about 6 days | About 296 mm on 16 days |
| Nuwara Eliya | About 19 °C and 11 °C, 196 mm on about 15 days | About 20 °C and 9 °C, 101 mm on about 8 days | About 222 mm on 17 days |
| Galle | About 29 °C and 23 °C, 177 mm on about 12 days | About 29 °C and 23 °C, 85 mm on about 8 days | About 321 mm on 16 days |

- **January to March is the best time** for Colombo, Galle and the hills. January and February are the driest months on the west coast.
- **December is in between.** It is much drier than November on the west coast, but it still rains on about 10 to 15 days. Carry an umbrella and keep the afternoons flexible.
- **October and November are wet almost everywhere,** sometimes with floods and landslides, says Sri Lanka's Climate Change Secretariat.
- **The east coast is the other way round.** Trincomalee and the east are best from May to September.
- **The monsoons:** the south-west monsoon runs from May to September and the north-east monsoon from December to February, says Sri Lanka's Ministry of Foreign Affairs.
- **Nuwara Eliya and Ella are cold at night,** about 9 to 11 °C from December to February. Pack a warm layer.

## Getting around Sri Lanka

- **A car with a driver** is how most visitors from India get around. It's the easiest way to cover Sigiriya, Kandy and the hills in a few days. Agree on the route, the days and what's included before you start.
- **Trains:** the hill line through Kandy, Nanu Oya (for Nuwara Eliya) and Ella is famous for its views. Reserved seats can be booked on the [Sri Lanka Railways seat reservation site](https://seatreservation.railway.gov.lk/mtktwebslr/). Its refund rules: 75% back if you cancel more than 7 days before the train, 50% between 2 and 7 days, nothing within 2 days.
- **Tuk-tuks** (three-wheelers) are good for short trips in towns. Agree on the fare first, or use a ride-hailing app.
- **Sigiriya, Anuradhapura and Polonnaruwa tickets** are issued by the Central Cultural Fund. You can order them on its [eTicket site](https://eservices.ccf.gov.lk/) and collect them at the site's counter. Tourists from SAARC countries, including India, get a cheaper "relief" ticket on showing a valid passport.
- **Temple of the Tooth, Kandy:** open every day from 5:30 AM to 8 PM. The ticket for SAARC visitors is 2,500 Sri Lankan rupees (3,000 for most other countries), and covers the temple and its museums, says the temple. Buy only from the temple's machines; tickets from anyone else aren't valid.

## Food for Indian travellers

- **Rice and curry** is the everyday meal: rice with several small vegetable, dal and meat or fish curries. It feels close to South Indian food, but can be spicier.
- **Hoppers** (like appam), **string hoppers** (like idiyappam), **dosa** and **kottu roti** are easy to find.
- **Vegetarians:** ask for vegetable rice and curry or dal. Colombo and Kandy have South Indian vegetarian restaurants. In small towns, say clearly if you don't eat fish or egg, as dried fish is often added to curries.
- **Jain or no onion-garlic food** is hard to find outside Colombo. Carry some ready-to-eat food from home, within the customs rules.
- **Drink sealed bottled water,** including for ice and brushing your teeth, says Sri Lanka Tourism.

## Money, customs and rules

- **The currency is the Sri Lankan rupee (LKR).** Carry a forex card and some US dollars, and change money at banks or authorised money changers. See [how much cash you can carry abroad](../baggage/customs/cash-and-currency.html) for India's own rules.
- **Declare cash over US$15,000** (or the same in other currencies) when you arrive, and over US$10,000 when you leave, says Sri Lanka Customs.
- **Cigarettes, cigars, beedis and snuff can't be brought in** as personal baggage, says Sri Lanka Customs. Leave them at home.
- **Alcohol:** foreigners may bring 1.5 litres of spirits and two bottles of wine duty free.
- **Temples:** cover your shoulders and legs, and take off your shoes and hats where asked, says Sri Lanka Tourism. Shorts and sleeveless tops aren't allowed at the Temple of the Tooth.
- **Buddha images:** don't pose disrespectfully with them, and ask before photographing people.
- **Drones and filming** can need approval first.

## What to wear in Sri Lanka

- **Light cotton clothes** for Colombo, Sigiriya and the beaches.
- **Clothes that cover shoulders and knees** for temples. A shawl or sarong helps.
- **A warm layer** for Nuwara Eliya, Ella and early trains.
- **An umbrella or light rain jacket,** whatever the month.
- **Walking shoes** for Sigiriya and Little Adam's Peak, and sandals that slip off easily at temples.

## Questions

### Is Sri Lanka visa free for Indians?

Indians get a tourist visa (ETA) free of charge for 30 days, since 25 May 2026, says Sri Lanka's official ETA site. But you must still apply for the ETA online at eta.gov.lk before you arrive.

### How do Indians apply for the Sri Lanka ETA?

Online, on eta.gov.lk, the official site of Sri Lanka's Department of Immigration and Emigration. For Indians the tourist ETA is free. Don't pay other websites that offer to apply for you.

### How many days does the free Sri Lanka visa give?

30 days from your first arrival, with double entry. To stay longer, apply for an extension and pay the visa fee.

### What is the best time to visit Sri Lanka from India?

January to March for Colombo, Galle, Kandy and the hills, when the west coast is driest. May to September for Trincomalee and the east coast. October and November are wet almost everywhere.

### Is December a good time to visit Sri Lanka?

Yes, for the west and south coasts and the hills, but expect some rain. Colombo gets about 175 mm on about 10 days in December, much less than in November. The east coast is at its wettest in December.

### How many days are enough for Sri Lanka?

Five or six days cover Colombo, Sigiriya, Kandy and either the hills (Nuwara Eliya and Ella) or the south coast (Galle). Take 8 to 10 days to see both without rushing.

### Which Ramayana places can I see in Sri Lanka?

Sri Lanka Tourism lists over 50 Ramayana sites. The easiest to visit are near Nuwara Eliya: the Sita Amman Temple at Seetha Eliya, Gayathri Peedam, and the Sri Baktha Hanuman Temple at Ramboda. Others include Sita Kotuwa near Kandy, Ussangoda on the south coast, Muneeshwaram near Chilaw and Thiru Koneswaram in Trincomalee.

### Which cities in India have direct flights to Sri Lanka?

Chennai, Bengaluru, Delhi, Mumbai, Hyderabad, Kochi, Thiruvananthapuram, Madurai, Tiruchirappalli, Coimbatore and Ahmedabad, on SriLankan Airlines, IndiGo and Air India. IndiGo also flies Chennai to Jaffna.

### Is there a time difference between India and Sri Lanka?

No. Sri Lanka uses UTC +5:30, the same as India.

### Can I carry cigarettes to Sri Lanka?

No. Sri Lanka Customs says cigarettes, cigars, beedis and snuff can't be brought in as personal baggage.
