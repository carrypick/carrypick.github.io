New Year's Eve is Goa's biggest night of the year. Beaches fill up, shacks and clubs hold parties, and fireworks go off along the coast at midnight. It is also the most crowded and costly time to visit, with traffic jams in North Goa and strict rules on music, drinking and driving. This guide covers what's announced for New Year 2027 so far, where to go, the rules, and how to plan the trip.

## When is New Year's Eve 2027?

New Year's Eve falls on **Thursday 31 December 2026**, and New Year's Day on **Friday 1 January 2027**. If your office gives 1 January off, you get three days from Friday to Sunday. Take Thursday off too, and you have four.

Christmas to New Year is Goa's busiest week, so book travel and hotels early.

## Goa New Year parties 2027: what's announced

Not much yet. On 22 November 2026, the line-ups, venues and ticket prices for this year's New Year's Eve parties had not been announced. **We'll check again by 15 December 2026** and update this page.

What we know:

- **Goa Tourism's tentative calendar** lists a Sea Food Festival and the Ramponkaranche Fest in Arambol in December 2026, a Food and Cultural Festival in Margao in January 2027, and Carnaval in February 2027. It doesn't list an official New Year's Eve party.
- **Sunburn:** news reports say the December 2026 Sunburn festival is in Mumbai, not Goa.
- **Parties are run by hotels, clubs and beach shacks,** not by the government. Each sets its own tickets and rules.

Before you buy a party ticket:

- **Buy from the venue or a ticket site you know.** Fake ticket pages and "passes" sold on social media are common at New Year.
- **Check what the ticket includes:** entry only, or food and drinks too, and whether children are allowed.
- **Ask about the exits.** After the nightclub fire at Arpora in December 2025, which killed 25 people, authorities inspected clubs and sealed several. Wherever you party, note the fire exits when you arrive.
- **Music outdoors stops at midnight** (see below), so an outdoor party can't legally go on with loudspeakers after 12.

## North Goa or South Goa for New Year?

| | North Goa | South Goa |
|---|---|---|
| Feel on New Year's Eve | Loud and crowded, with parties at clubs and shacks | Quieter, with hotel dinners and calmer beaches |
| Beaches | Calangute, Baga, Candolim, Anjuna, Vagator, Arambol | Colva, Benaulim, Palolem, Agonda |
| Traffic | Heavy jams around Calangute, Baga, Anjuna and Vagator | Lighter, but busy near Colva and Palolem |
| Airport | Mopa (GOX) | Dabolim (GOI) |
| Good for | Friends and party groups | Couples, families and older parents |

- **Want the big party atmosphere?** Stay in North Goa, near enough to walk to the beach or club you've picked.
- **Travelling with children or parents?** South Goa is calmer. Palolem and Colva still have fireworks and events, without the North Goa crowds.
- **Panaji** has New Year events too, and it's a good base for Old Goa's churches and the Fontainhas quarter on New Year's Day.

## The rules for the night

### Music until midnight

Goa's government allows outdoor loudspeakers until midnight from **24 to 31 December 2026**, with permission. Nothing is allowed after midnight. On other nights, loudspeakers must stop at 10 PM.

### Drinking and the beach fines

Goa's 2022 order under its tourist places law fines these, from **₹5,000 up to ₹50,000**:

- Drinking alcohol in the open, including on the beach, and breaking glass bottles. Drink only in licensed shacks, bars and restaurants.
- Cooking in the open.
- Littering.
- Touting, and selling tickets outside official counters.
- Driving on the beach.

Drugs are a serious crime in India, with punishments from 6 months up to 30 years in jail. Don't accept anything from strangers at a party.

### Driving, scooters and drink-driving

- **Never drink and drive.** Riding drunk can mean a ₹10,000 fine, up to 6 months in jail, or both. Last New Year's Eve, police set up extra checkpoints across Goa.
- **Wear a helmet.** The fine is ₹1,000, and you can lose your licence for 3 months.
- **Rent only from licensed rentals,** with black number plates and yellow letters.
- **Book a taxi in advance for the ride back,** or stay within walking distance of your party. Agree the price before you get in.

## Traffic on New Year's Eve

Last New Year's Eve, roads around Calangute, Baga, Anjuna and Vagator were jammed from the evening. Police:

- made the Calangute–Baga stretch from Bharat Junction to Tito's Circle a **no-vehicle zone from 3 PM to 6 AM**,
- ran one-way routes in the Calangute–Baga belt,
- kept buses out of Calangute from 21 December to 31 January.

These were last year's rules, reported in the news. Goa Traffic Police usually announces this year's plan a few days before New Year's Eve. **We'll add it when it's out.** Until then, plan to walk in North Goa on the night, and reach your party before the afternoon closures.

## Beach safety at night

- **Don't swim at night or after drinking.** Lifesavers are on duty only from 7 AM to sunset. Swim only between the red-and-yellow flags, and never where there's a red flag.
- **Keep away from fireworks being lit on the sand,** and keep children close in the crowd.
- **Keep your phone and cash in a zip pouch,** and leave your passport in the hotel locker.
- **Don't photograph people** who are swimming or sunbathing without asking.
- **Emergency:** call 112. Police 100, ambulance 108, Goa Tourism helpline 1364, women's helpline 1091.

## Getting to Goa for New Year

- **Fly** into Mopa (GOX) for North Goa, or Dabolim (GOI) for central and South Goa. Fares are highest around Christmas and New Year, so book early.
- **Train:** Goa is on the Konkan Railway. Thivim is the station for North Goa, Karmali for Panaji and Old Goa, and Madgaon for South Goa. Reservations open about 60 days before you travel, so seats for late December are already on sale. Watch for extra holiday trains.
- **Road:** Goa is about 594 km from Mumbai, 632 km from Bengaluru and 471 km from Pune.
- **Arrive by 30 December** if you can. Roads in North Goa are slowest on 31 December.

## New Year's Day in Goa

- **Old Goa's churches** (a UNESCO World Heritage Site) and the Fontainhas quarter in Panaji are calmer days out.
- **The Feast of the Three Kings** is on 6 January, if you stay into the new year.
- **Heading home on 1 or 2 January?** Leave extra time for the airport, as everyone leaves at once.

## What to pack and wear

- Light cotton clothes for the day, and something smarter for a party or dinner.
- A light sweater or shawl. Goa Tourism says light woollens can be worn in the evenings in December and January.
- Comfortable sandals you can walk in on sand, since you may walk a long way on the night.
- Sunscreen, a cap, mosquito repellent and a waterproof pouch for your phone.

### Is Sunburn in Goa for New Year 2027?

News reports say the December 2026 Sunburn festival is in Mumbai, not Goa.

### What is the name of Goa's New Year festival?

Goa has no single official New Year's Eve festival. Parties are held by clubs, hotels and beach shacks. Goa's big festival is Carnaval, in February.

### Until what time is music allowed in Goa on New Year's Eve?

Until midnight for outdoor loudspeakers, with permission. Goa's order allows this only from 24 to 31 December 2026, and nothing after midnight.

### Which is better for New Year, North Goa or South Goa?

North Goa for parties and crowds: Calangute, Baga, Anjuna and Vagator. South Goa for a calmer night: Colva, Benaulim, Palolem and Agonda.

### Can you drink on Goa beaches on New Year's Eve?

No. Drinking in the open, including on beaches, is banned, with fines from ₹5,000 up to ₹50,000. Drink only in licensed shacks, bars and restaurants.
