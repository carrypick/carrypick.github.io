May is the hottest month in much of India and the main school summer holiday month, so everyone heads for the hills. Kashmir, Himachal, Uttarakhand, Darjeeling, Sikkim, Meghalaya and the Nilgiris are all in season, the Char Dham yatra is on, and the high roads to Ladakh and Spiti start to open. The beaches of the west coast are hot and humid, and the monsoon usually reaches Kerala around the end of May or in early June.

Abroad, May is a lovely month in Europe, Japan, Georgia, Uzbekistan and Bali, while the Gulf is very hot.

This page sums up our trip guides that fit May, in India and abroad, with the dates we could confirm and the long weekends of May 2027. Each place links to its full guide, where the official sources are listed.

Visiting India from abroad? Start here: our [visiting India guide](visiting-india.html) covers the e-Visa, what to pack, SIM cards and money. In May, plan the plains for early mornings and spend most of your trip in the hills.

Going earlier? See [where to go in April](where-to-go-in-april.html). Only looking abroad? Compare all our [international trips from India](international-trips-from-india.html) by flight time, best months and visa type.

## May 2027 dates to know

- **Bakrid (Id-ul-Zuha):** **Monday 17 May 2027** on the DoPT list for central government offices. It depends on the moon, so the date may move by a day.
- **Buddha Purnima:** **Thursday 20 May 2027**, a gazetted holiday on the DoPT list. It's a good day to be in Bodh Gaya or Sarnath, the places of the Buddha.
- **Rabindranath Tagore's birthday:** Sunday 9 May 2027, a restricted holiday.
- **School summer holidays:** most states close schools for summer from about mid-May to June, but every state and board sets its own dates, so check your school's calendar.
- **Char Dham:** all four temples should be open by May. In 2026 they opened between 19 and 23 April. **The 2027 dates are to be announced.** Registration is compulsory.
- **Ladakh and Spiti roads:** the Manali–Leh road opened to light vehicles on 15 May in 2026, and Zoji La from Srinagar usually opens earlier. **The 2027 dates are to be announced.**
- **Monsoon:** the south-west monsoon usually reaches Kerala in late May or early June, and IMD announces its arrival. Plan Kerala and the west coast for early May.

## Long weekends in May 2027

May 2027 has two gazetted holidays in one week: Bakrid (Monday 17 May) and Buddha Purnima (Thursday 20 May). On the DoPT holiday list for central government offices in Delhi, that gives:

- **Bakrid: Saturday 15 to Monday 17 May, 3 days with no leave.** The date may move with the moon, so keep bookings flexible.
- **Buddha Purnima: Thursday 20 to Sunday 23 May, 4 days with 1 day off** (Friday 21 May).
- **Both together: Saturday 15 to Sunday 23 May, 9 days** with 3 days off (Tuesday 18, Wednesday 19 and Friday 21 May).
- **Saturday 15 to Thursday 20 May, 6 days** with 2 days off (Tuesday 18 and Wednesday 19 May).

## Summer vacation hill stations in May

These are the classic summer holiday places. They are at their busiest in May and June, so book stays and transport early, and travel on weekdays if you can.

- **[Kashmir](kashmir-in-december.html):** Srinagar, Gulmarg, Pahalgam and Sonamarg are green and cool. The Vande Bharat train now runs from Jammu Tawi and Katra to Srinagar. Pack with our [Kashmir packing list](../guides/kashmir-packing-list.html).
- **[Manali](manali.html):** Himachal Tourism says Rohtang Pass usually reopens around May. Vehicles going up to Rohtang need a permit from the official site, rohtangpermits.hp.gov.in, and the roads are jammed at weekends. Pack with our [Manali packing list](../guides/manali-packing-list.html).
- **[Shimla](shimla-in-december.html):** the Ridge, Mall Road, Kufri and the toy train from Kalka. Book the train early.
- **[Mussoorie](mussoorie-in-december.html) and [Nainital](nainital-in-december.html):** March to June is their main season. Both are a short trip from Delhi, so they're very crowded at weekends.
- **[Darjeeling](darjeeling-in-december.html) and [Sikkim](sikkim-trip-plan.html):** cool and green before the monsoon. Clouds hide Kanchenjunga more often than in autumn. Tsomgo Lake and North Sikkim need permits, and foreigners need a permit for all of Sikkim: see our [Sikkim trip plan](sikkim-trip-plan.html).
- **[Meghalaya](meghalaya-in-december.html):** pre-monsoon showers start, the waterfalls begin to fill, and Shillong stays cool. Paths to the root bridges get slippery, so wear shoes with grip.
- **[Ooty](ooty-in-december.html):** in season until June. Cars and bikes from outside the Nilgiris need an e-pass from the official site, and on busy days the number of vehicles is limited, so get it before you set off.
- **[Munnar](munnar-in-december.html):** warm and green, with its flower show in May. The south-west monsoon usually reaches Kerala around the start of June, so go early in the month.

## Ladakh and Spiti in May

The two roads to Ladakh are closed by snow in winter, and the Border Roads Organisation (BRO) clears them in spring. The dates change every year, so check before you go.

- **Srinagar–Sonamarg–Zoji La–Leh:** this is usually the first road to open. In 2025 BRO reopened Zoji La on 1 April after a 32-day closure, says PIB. In the winter of 2025–26 it was closed for only four days, said BRO's Project Vijayak in March 2026. By May it's normally open, but snow can still close it for a day.
- **Manali–Atal Tunnel–Baralacha La–Leh:** this one opens later. In 2026 BRO finished clearing it on 12 May, and it opened to light vehicles over Baralacha La on 15 May, newspapers reported (we couldn't find an official release). So in early May, don't plan to drive from Manali to Leh. **The 2027 opening date is to be announced.**
- **Flying to Leh** is the easier way in May. Rest for the first full day: Leh is very high, and altitude sickness is common if you rush.
- **[Spiti](spiti-valley-trip-guide.html):** the road from Shimla through Kinnaur is open, but Kunzum La from Manali opens later: in 2026 it opened to light 4x4 vehicles around 20 May, news reports said. Check the current road status with the Lahaul-Spiti district before you plan. Foreigners need an Inner Line Permit for some border areas of Kinnaur and Spiti.
- **Rohtang Pass** above Manali usually reopens around May, says Himachal Tourism. Vehicles need a permit from [rohtangpermits.hp.gov.in](https://rohtangpermits.hp.gov.in/Home/InformatoryScreen), and only a set number of vehicles are allowed each day, so book online up to 2 days ahead and carry a printout.

Read our full [Ladakh trip guide](ladakh-trip-guide.html) for permits, the 48-hour rest rule and a 7-day plan, and our [Spiti Valley trip guide](spiti-valley-trip-guide.html) for both roads and an 8-day circuit. Pack for cold: even in May, nights in Leh and on the passes are cold, so see our [winter clothes guide](../guides/winter-clothes-for-hill-trips.html).

## Char Dham yatra in May

The Char Dham temples in Uttarakhand, Yamunotri, Gangotri, Kedarnath and Badrinath, close for the winter in November and open again in late April or early May. **The 2027 opening dates are to be announced.** Gangotri and Yamunotri traditionally open on Akshaya Tritiya, and the temple committees fix the other dates each year.

| Temple | Opened in 2026 | Closed in 2026 |
|---|---|---|
| Gangotri | 19 April (Akshaya Tritiya) | 10 November |
| Yamunotri | 19 April (Akshaya Tritiya) | 11 November |
| Kedarnath | 22 April | 11 November (Bhai Dooj) |
| Badrinath | 23 April | About 16 November |

- **Registration is compulsory.** The Uttarakhand government's portal, [registrationandtouristcare.uk.gov.in](https://registrationandtouristcare.uk.gov.in/), says you must register yourself and your vehicle before you start. It's free, and your registration is checked at the temples.
- **The first weeks are the busiest.** More than 65 lakh pilgrims visited the four temples in 2026, says All India Radio. Book stays early, and expect long queues around the opening days and in May and June.
- **Start from [Rishikesh](rishikesh-in-december.html) or Haridwar.** The road to the temples starts there, and Rishikesh is a good place to rest a day on the way.
- **Kedarnath is a steep walk** at high altitude. Walk slowly, carry warm clothes and a rain jacket, and see a doctor first if you have a heart or lung problem.
- **Plan it step by step** with our [Char Dham Yatra 2027 guide](char-dham-yatra-2027.html): registration, helicopter booking only on IRCTC, health tips and an 11-day route. Pack with our [Kedarnath packing list](../guides/kedarnath-packing-list.html).

May and June are the busiest months of the yatra, with the school holidays. In the monsoon, landslides often block the hill roads, so keep a spare day in your plan.

## Heat wave safety in May

May is the peak month for heat waves in India, says IMD: heat waves come mainly from March to June. IMD declares a heat wave in the plains when the temperature is at least 40 °C and well above normal, or 45 °C and above; in the hills the threshold is 30 °C, and on the coast 37 °C.

- **Check IMD's colour-coded warnings** (yellow, orange and red) for your route before you travel, on the IMD website or the Mausam app.
- **Travel early and late.** Sightsee in the morning and evening, and rest indoors from about noon to 4 PM. Long drives through the plains are best started at dawn.
- **Drink water often, even if you're not thirsty,** says IMD, and use ORS, lemon water, lassi or buttermilk. Carry water on every drive and train journey.
- **Wear loose, light-coloured cotton clothes and cover your head,** with a cap, hat or scarf.
- **Watch children and older people.** Never leave anyone, or a pet, in a parked car.

Going abroad to escape the heat? See below: Dubai and the Gulf are even hotter.

## Places to visit in May in India with family and kids

- **With family:** Shimla, Mussoorie, Nainital, Ooty and Kashmir are easy, with plenty of hotels and short sightseeing days. Book the toy trains in Shimla, Darjeeling and Ooty early.
- **With kids or a baby:** pick one place and stay put, with short drives and a hotel near the main sights. Keep children out of the midday sun on travel days, and carry water and ORS.
- **With friends:** rafting in Rishikesh, the Manali–Rohtang side, or a 9-day Bakrid and Buddha Purnima trip to Kashmir or Darjeeling.
- **On a budget or for 3 days:** Mussoorie, Nainital or Rishikesh from Delhi; Ooty or Coorg from Bengaluru. See [weekend getaways from Delhi](weekend-getaways-from-delhi.html), [Mumbai](weekend-getaways-from-mumbai.html) and [Bangalore](weekend-getaways-from-bangalore.html).

### Europe in May

**[Europe](europe-trip-from-india.html)** is one of the best picks for May: about 17 °C by day in Zurich, 19 °C in Paris and 23 °C in Rome, with long days and fewer crowds than July and August. Apply for the Schengen visa early, because summer appointments fill up: the EU says you can apply up to 6 months before you travel.

### Japan, Georgia and Central Asia

**[Japan](japan-trip-from-india.html)** is mild and green in May. Golden Week (late April to early May) is Japan's own holiday season, so trains and hotels are full and fares go up; go after it. **[Georgia](georgia-trip-from-india.html)** opens its main season in May, with the Kazbegi road and the wine villages, though May and June are its wettest months. **[Uzbekistan](uzbekistan-trip-from-india.html)** is warm, about 27 °C by day in Tashkent, before the summer heat. **[Kazakhstan](kazakhstan-trip-from-india.html)** is green but rainy in May; the mountain lakes are best from June.

### Bali, Bhutan and Singapore

**[Bali](bali-trip-from-india.html)** is in its drier season, from April to October. **[Bhutan](bhutan-in-december.html)** is pleasant before the monsoon, which runs from June to September. **[Singapore](singapore-trip-from-india.html)** is hot and humid all year and easy with kids: keep Universal Studios and the aquarium for the afternoons. **[Sri Lanka](sri-lanka-trip-from-india.html)**'s south-west monsoon starts in May, so pick the east coast.

### Dubai and the Gulf in May

**[Dubai](dubai-trip-from-india.html)** is very hot from May to September. Our Dubai guide gives about 37–41 °C by day from May to September, and the sea and humid air make it feel hotter. Plan only indoor things for the middle of the day, or save Dubai for November to March.

## Questions

### Which is the best place to visit in May in India?

For the hills, Kashmir, Manali, Shimla, Mussoorie, Nainital, Darjeeling or Ooty. For a pilgrimage, the Char Dham yatra. For high mountains, Ladakh once the roads open, or fly to Leh. Avoid the plains and Rajasthan in the midday heat.

### Which are the best summer vacation places in India with family?

Shimla, Mussoorie, Nainital and Ooty are the easiest with children, with short drives and lots of hotels. Kashmir and Darjeeling are good for a longer trip. Book the toy trains and hotels early, because May and June are the busiest months.

### Which places are cool in May in India?

The hill stations: Kashmir, Manali, Shimla, Mussoorie, Nainital, Darjeeling, Sikkim and Meghalaya, and Ooty and Munnar in the south. Higher places like Rohtang and Ladakh can still be cold, with snow on the passes.

### Where can I go outside India in May?

Europe, Japan after Golden Week, Georgia, Uzbekistan, Bali and Bhutan are all in a good season. Singapore is fine for kids with indoor afternoons. Dubai is very hot.

### Is there a long weekend in May 2027?

Yes. Bakrid on Monday 17 May gives 3 days with no leave (15–17 May), though the date may move with the moon. Buddha Purnima on Thursday 20 May gives 4 days with Friday 21 May off. With 3 days off (18, 19 and 21 May) you get 9 days from 15 to 23 May. See our [long weekends 2027 planner](../tools/long-weekends-2027.html).

### When do Kedarnath and Badrinath open in 2027?

Not yet. The 2027 opening dates are to be announced. In 2026, Kedarnath opened on 22 April and Badrinath on 23 April, and Gangotri and Yamunotri on 19 April, Akshaya Tritiya. The dates are fixed by the temple committees each year, so check the official Badrinath-Kedarnath Temple Committee site, and register for the yatra on the Uttarakhand government's portal before you go.

### Is the Manali–Leh road open in May?

Usually not in early May. In 2026 BRO finished clearing the road on 12 May, and it opened to light vehicles on 15 May. The 2027 date is to be announced. The Srinagar–Zoji La road usually opens earlier, and you can fly to Leh all year.
