Bhutan in December is cold, dry and sunny. The skies are mostly clear and the mountain views are good. Nights in Paro and Thimphu fall to about 0 °C or below, but the days are often warm enough for a sweater. This guide covers the weather, the real chances of snow, the December festivals, a simple 6-day plan, how to get there from India and what to wear.

## Bhutan weather in December and January

Long-term averages from Bhutan's National Center for Hydrology and Meteorology (NCHM), as tabled on Wikipedia:

| Place | Height | December day / night | January day / night |
|---|---|---|---|
| Paro | About 2,200 m | About 15 °C / 0.5 °C | About 13 °C / −1 °C |
| Thimphu | About 2,320 m | About 17 °C / −1 °C | About 15 °C / −2 °C |
| Punakha | About 1,240 m | About 20 °C / 7 °C | About 19 °C / 6 °C |
| Bumthang (Jakar) | Higher than Paro | About 13 °C / −3 °C | About 11 °C / −4 °C |

- **Winter is December to February,** says Bhutan's Department of Tourism.
- **Days are sunny, nights are freezing** in Paro, Thimphu and Bumthang. Dress in layers you can take off at noon.
- **Punakha is the warm one.** It is about 1,000 m lower than Thimphu, so days feel mild.
- **December is dry.** The monsoon ends in late September, so most winter days are clear. This is a good time for views of the high Himalaya from Dochula.

## Does it snow in Bhutan in December?

- **Snow is possible, but not certain.** It falls most often on the high passes, and less often in the towns.
- **The passes get it first.** Dochula (about 3,100 m, on the Thimphu–Punakha road) and Chele La (about 3,988 m, on the Paro–Haa road) have both been closed by heavy snow in past winters, says the Bhutan Broadcasting Service (BBS).
- **Towns get light snow in some winters.** In one first snowfall of the season reported by BBS, Thimphu had 9 cm and Haa had 24 cm, and Paro had some snow too.
- **Roads reopen faster now.** Snow blockages on Bhutan's roads fell from 79 in 2021-22 to 5 in 2024-25, and clearing ice usually takes 3–4 hours, reported The Bhutanese in April 2026.
- **January and February** give the best chance of snow. If snow matters most, keep a spare day in your plan and ask your guide about the passes each morning.

## Festivals in December 2026

Dates are from the tentative 2026 festival list published with Bhutan's Department of Tourism. Festivals follow the Bhutanese lunar calendar, so confirm with your guide before you book.

| Festival | Where | Dates |
|---|---|---|
| Druk Wangyel Tshechu | Dochula pass, between Thimphu and Punakha | 13 December |
| Trongsa Tshechu | Trongsa Dzong, central Bhutan | 17–21 December |
| Lhuentse Tshechu | Lhuentse Dzong, east Bhutan | 17–21 December |
| Samdrup Jongkhar Tshechu | Samdrup Jongkhar, near the Assam border | 22–24 December |
| Nabji Lhakhang Drup | Nabji, Trongsa | 24–26 December |

- **Druk Wangyel Tshechu is the easy one to see.** It is a one-day festival in the open at Dochula, about an hour's drive from Thimphu, with mask dances in front of the 108 chortens. Leave Thimphu early for a good spot.
- **National Day is on 17 December** (a Thursday in 2026). It marks the crowning of Bhutan's first king in 1907, and is celebrated across the country.
- **The Black-necked Crane Festival** at Gangtey (Phobjikha) was on 11 November 2026, so it's over for this year. The cranes themselves stay all winter (see below).
- **No big festivals are listed for January.** Punakha's festival is usually in February or early March; 2027 dates aren't confirmed yet.
- **At a festival,** wear long sleeves and cover your legs, as at any dzong or temple. Don't walk across the dance ground, and ask before you take close-up photos of people.

## Black-necked cranes in Phobjikha

- **Phobjikha valley** (around 2,900 m, near Gangtey) is the winter home of the rare black-necked crane.
- **The cranes arrive from late October** and most are there by December, says the Royal Society for Protection of Nature (RSPN). They leave between the last week of February and mid-March.
- **December and January are good months to see them.** Watch from a distance, keep quiet, and don't fly a drone.
- **Phobjikha is cold.** It is higher than Thimphu, so pack your warmest layers for an overnight stay.

## A simple 6-day plan for December

Most first trips from India cover Paro, Thimphu and Punakha. A guide is needed in Bhutan, so your hotel or a licensed tour operator will help you set the plan.

| Day | Plan | Night |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Fly into Paro. Drive about an hour to Thimphu. Rest; go slow at this height | Thimphu |
| 2 | Thimphu: Buddha Dordenma, the Memorial Chorten, Tashichho Dzong and the weekend vegetable market | Thimphu |
| 3 | Drive over Dochula (108 chortens, mountain views on clear mornings) to Punakha: Punakha Dzong and Chimi Lhakhang | Punakha |
| 4 | Day trip to Phobjikha for the cranes, or stay in Punakha for the suspension bridge and a valley walk | Punakha or Phobjikha |
| 5 | Drive back to Paro. Paro Dzong (Rinpung) and the National Museum | Paro |
| 6 | Hike to Tiger's Nest (Paro Taktsang) in the morning, then fly out or stay one more night | Paro |

- **Going on 13 December?** Make day 3 your Dochula day and watch the festival on the way to Punakha.
- **Keep Tiger's Nest near the end.** By then your body is used to the height. The hike takes about 5–6 hours there and back. In winter the path can be icy in the shade, so wear shoes with a good grip and start early.
- **Only 4 days?** Paro and Thimphu, with a day trip to Dochula.
- **Coming by road?** Add a day at each end for Phuentsholing to Thimphu, about 5–6 hours each way.

## Getting to Bhutan from India

- **By air to Paro:** Drukair flies to Paro from Delhi, Kolkata, Bagdogra and Guwahati, and from Gaya in winter. Bhutan Airlines flies from Delhi and Kolkata. Check each airline's current schedule.
- **Paro flights run in daylight only,** in clear weather, says Bhutan's Department of Air Transport. Winter flights can be delayed or cancelled, so don't book a tight onward connection.
- **Bag limits are small on Drukair:** the cabin bag is 5 kg and 45 x 35 x 20 cm. See the airline table in our [Bhutan packing list](../guides/bhutan-packing-list.html#which-bag-to-take).
- **By road:** fly to Bagdogra or take a train to New Jalpaiguri (NJP), then go by road to the border at Jaigaon and Phuentsholing (about 4–5 hours). Indians get the entry permit at the Phuentsholing immigration office, or online first. From there, Thimphu is about 5–6 hours by road. Step by step: [Bhutan by road from India](bhutan-by-road.html).
- **Getting the permit:** at Paro airport or a land border, or apply online first. You need your passport or original voter ID, and the Sustainable Development Fee (SDF) paid for each night.

## Documents, SDF and money

- **No visa for Indians.** Carry an Indian passport valid for at least 6 months, or your original voter ID card, plus passport-size photos. Aadhaar and driving licence aren't accepted for the permit.
- **Children under 18** can use a passport or their original birth certificate, and must travel with a parent or guardian.
- **SDF:** Nu 1,200 (₹1,200) per person per night for Indians. Children aged 6 to 11 pay Nu 600, and children under 6 pay nothing.
- **A guide** must go with you at all times, including if you come by road and go beyond the border towns, and is needed at dzongs and monuments (Department of Tourism FAQ, rechecked 21 Nov 2026).
- **Indian rupees work.** The ngultrum is fixed 1:1 with the rupee. Carry notes of ₹500 or smaller: the Indian Embassy in Thimphu says you can carry up to ₹25,000 in such notes, and more only in ₹100 notes or smaller.

## Phone, SIM and eSIM in Bhutan

You have three ways to stay online in Bhutan:

- **Roaming on your own SIM:** the easiest, but often the costliest. Check your mobile company's international pack before you fly.
- **A local SIM card:** sold at the airport and in phone shops. Shops usually ask for your passport to register it.
- **An eSIM:** a data plan you buy online and install on your phone before you go, with no plastic card. Your phone must support eSIM and must not be locked to one network. On an iPhone, look in Settings > Mobile Service (or Cellular). On Android, look in Settings > SIM or Connections.

Install the eSIM at home over Wi-Fi and switch it on when you land. Most travel eSIMs give data only, with no local number, so keep your home SIM on for calls and bank OTP texts (roaming charges may apply).

## What to wear in Bhutan in December

What should I wear? Try the [what-to-wear helper](../tools/what-to-wear.html#bhutan) for day and night temperatures and an outfit list by month.

- **Layers:** thermals, a full-sleeve top, a sweater or fleece, and a warm jacket. Take layers off in the midday sun.
- **For mornings, evenings and the passes:** a woollen cap, gloves, a neck warmer and woollen socks.
- **For dzongs, temples and festivals:** long sleeves and long trousers or a long skirt. Take off your cap inside, and your shoes in temples.
- **Shoes:** hiking or walking shoes with a good grip for Tiger's Nest, and one comfortable pair for towns.
- **Skin:** lip balm, moisturiser, sunscreen and sunglasses. The winter air is dry and the sun is strong at this height.

## Health and safety

- **Go slow on the first day.** Paro and Thimphu are above 2,000 m, and Tiger's Nest is about 3,120 m. Drink water, and ask a doctor before you go if you have heart or lung problems.
- **Never sleep with a gas heater or a fire burning in a closed room.** It can cause carbon monoxide poisoning. Keep a window slightly open and switch heaters off before you sleep.
- **Hill roads are slow and winding.** Carry something for motion sickness if you need it, and don't plan long drives after dark.
- **Smoking is banned in public places** outside smoking areas.

## Questions

### Is December a good time to visit Bhutan?

Yes, if you're fine with the cold. December is dry and mostly sunny, with clear mountain views. Days are about 15–20 °C, but nights fall to about 0 °C in Paro and below 0 °C in Thimphu. You can also see the Druk Wangyel Tshechu at Dochula on 13 December.

### What is the weather in Bhutan in December?

Long-term averages are about 15 °C by day and 0.5 °C at night in Paro, 17 °C and −1 °C in Thimphu, and 20 °C and 7 °C in Punakha. It rarely rains in December.

### Does it snow in Bhutan in December?

It can. Snow falls most often on high passes such as Dochula and Chele La, and some winters bring light snow to Thimphu and Paro. It is not certain in December; January and February give a better chance.

### What is the temperature in Bhutan in January?

January is the coldest month. Paro averages about 13 °C by day and −1 °C at night, and Thimphu about 15 °C and −2 °C. Bumthang drops to about −4 °C at night.

### Which festivals are in Bhutan in December?

The Druk Wangyel Tshechu at Dochula on 13 December, Trongsa and Lhuentse Tshechus on 17–21 December, Samdrup Jongkhar Tshechu on 22–24 December and Nabji Lhakhang Drup on 24–26 December 2026. National Day is on 17 December. Dates follow the lunar calendar, so confirm them with your guide.

### Is Bhutan good in the last week of December?

Yes. The weather stays dry and cold, and you can catch the Samdrup Jongkhar or Nabji festivals. Christmas and New Year are busier with visitors from India, so book hotels and flights early.

### How many days are enough for Bhutan?

Six days cover Paro, Thimphu and Punakha with the Tiger's Nest hike. Four days are enough for Paro and Thimphu only. Add two days if you go by road through Phuentsholing.

### What should I wear in Bhutan in December?

Layers: thermals, a full-sleeve top, a sweater and a warm jacket, plus a cap, gloves and woollen socks. For dzongs and festivals, wear long sleeves and cover your legs. See our [Bhutan packing list](../guides/bhutan-packing-list.html).

### Can Indians visit Bhutan without a passport?

Yes. Your original voter ID card also works for the entry permit. Aadhaar and driving licence don't. You still pay the SDF of Nu 1,200 per night.
