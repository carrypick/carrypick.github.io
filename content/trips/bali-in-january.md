January is Bali's wettest month, so is it worth going? For many people, yes. It's still hot, the rain usually comes as heavy showers in the afternoon or at night, the rice terraces are at their greenest, and once the New Year crowds leave, hotels and roads are calmer. In 2027 you also get **Galungan**, Bali's biggest Hindu festival, when bamboo poles line every village street. The catch: rough seas can stop boats to Nusa Penida, and Kuta's beaches collect rubbish in the wet season. This guide covers January's weather, each part of the month, Galungan, where to stay, the rain risks and getting there from India.

## Bali weather in January

Long-term averages for Denpasar from Indonesia's weather agency BMKG, published by the World Meteorological Organization:

| Month | Day | Night | Rain |
|---|---|---|---|
| December | About 33.0 °C | About 23.5 °C | About 276 mm, on about 22 days |
| **January** | **About 33.0 °C** | **About 24.1 °C** | **About 345 mm, on about 27 days** |
| February | About 33.4 °C | About 24.2 °C | About 274 mm, on about 22 days |

- **January is the wettest month of the year in Denpasar,** with the most rain and the most rainy days.
- **A rainy day isn't a wet day.** Most rain falls as heavy showers, often from the afternoon into the night, with sunny spells in the morning. Plan sightseeing early and keep a spa, a cooking class or a café for the afternoon.
- **It's hot and sticky.** About 33 °C by day and 24 °C at night on the coast. Ubud, in the hills, is a few degrees cooler at night.
- **Check BMKG's Bali office** ([bbmkg3.bmkg.go.id](https://bbmkg3.bmkg.go.id/)) for rain and high-wave warnings before a boat trip or a sunrise trek.

## Is Bali good in January?

- **Good for:** green rice fields and waterfalls, Ubud, temples, Galungan, spa and villa holidays, and a calmer island after New Year.
- **Not so good for:** beach days in Kuta and Legian, snorkelling and diving trips, the Nusa Penida day trip and the Gili boats, which all depend on calm seas.
- **If you want dry weather,** go from April to October. June to September is the driest and also the busiest.

## Bali in the first week of January

New Year's Day 2027 is a Friday, and Bali stays busy for the first weekend. **Isra Mikraj, on Tuesday 5 January 2027,** is a national holiday on Indonesia's 2027 list, so expect domestic travellers. From about the second week, Bali is calmer than at Christmas and New Year. (We don't show prices: compare them for your dates.)

## Galungan and Kuningan in January 2027

Galungan celebrates the victory of good (dharma) over evil, and it's the biggest event in the Balinese Hindu calendar. It comes every 210 days, on the Pawukon calendar.

- **Galungan: Wednesday 13 January 2027.** **Kuningan,** which closes the festival, falls 10 days later: **Saturday 23 January 2027.** These dates follow the 210-day cycle from Galungan on 19 November 2025 and match published Balinese calendars. Bali's government sets the local Galungan holidays in a circular that wasn't out when we checked, so treat them as unconfirmed.
- **What you'll see:** tall, curved bamboo poles (penjor) decorated with palm leaves outside every home, families in white and temple clothes, offerings and temple ceremonies. Many Balinese go back to their home villages.
- **What it means for you:** some local shops, warungs and offices close for a day or two around Galungan, and roads near temples get busy in the morning. Hotels, big restaurants and tourist sights usually stay open.
- **Be a good guest:** watch ceremonies from the side, don't walk in front of people praying, wear a sarong and sash at temples, and ask before photographing people. Don't step on offerings (canang) on the ground.

## Bali in the last week of January

**Republic Day** is on **Tuesday 26 January 2027.** With Monday off, that's a 4-day weekend from India, and flights from Delhi and Bengaluru fill up. Book early for those dates.

Coming in February or March instead? **Chinese New Year is on Saturday 6 February 2027,** and **Nyepi, the Day of Silence, is on Monday 8 March 2027,** both on Indonesia's 2027 holiday list. On Nyepi the whole island, the airport included, shuts for 24 hours, so don't plan to fly that day.

## Where to stay and what to do in January

- **Ubud** is the best base in January: rice terraces, temples, waterfalls, the Monkey Forest, yoga and spas, and Galungan in its villages. Rain makes the terraces greener.
- **Sanur and Nusa Dua** have calmer water and cleaner, well-kept beaches. Good for families.
- **Seminyak and Canggu** for cafés, restaurants and sunsets. Big waves and rip currents: swim only where lifeguards are on duty.
- **Uluwatu** for the cliff temple and the Kecak dance at sunset. Check the weather that day, since it's an open-air show.
- **Kuta and Legian:** every wet season, rubbish from rivers and the sea washes up on these beaches. Badung regency's teams clear it every day (news reports say more than 3,100 tonnes in the 2024–25 season), but some days are bad. Don't plan a whole trip around these beaches in January.

## Rain, floods and boat crossings

- **Boats to Nusa Penida, Lembongan and the Gilis:** in January, BMKG's Bali office often warns of high waves in the Badung Strait (Sanur to Nusa Penida) and the Lombok Strait. In January 2026, news reports carried warnings of waves up to 2.5 to 4 metres. Crossings may be delayed or cancelled on rough days, so don't plan a boat trip on your last day before your flight.
- **Floods:** heavy rain can flood low parts of Denpasar, Kuta and Seminyak. In September 2025, floods in Bali killed 18 people, says Indonesia's disaster agency BNPB, as reported in the news. Don't walk or ride through flooded streets.
- **Scooters in the rain** are dangerous. Hire a car with a driver, or use ride-hailing apps.
- **Volcanic ash:** eruptions of Mount Lewotobi Laki-laki, on the island of Flores, cancelled dozens of Bali flights in November 2024 and June and July 2025 (news). Buy travel insurance that covers delays, and check your flight status before you leave for the airport.

## Before you land

- **Visa:** Indians can get the B1 visa on arrival, Rp 500,000 for up to 30 days, extendable once for 30 more, or apply online before you fly (the e-VOA) on [evisa.imigrasi.go.id](https://evisa.imigrasi.go.id/), says Indonesia's e-Visa website. You need a passport valid for 6 months and an onward ticket.
- **All Indonesia arrival card:** free, online, within 3 days before you land, on [allindonesia.imigrasi.go.id](https://allindonesia.imigrasi.go.id). It covers immigration, customs and health in one form.
- **Bali tourist levy:** Rp 150,000 per person, once per trip, on the Bali government's [Love Bali](https://lovebali.baliprov.go.id/) site.

Full details, with the documents to carry, are in our [Bali trip guide](bali-trip-from-india.html).

## What to wear in Bali in January

Light cotton clothes, a rain poncho or a light rain jacket (umbrellas are hard in the wind), sandals that can get wet, and walking shoes with grip for wet temple steps and waterfall paths. Pack a sarong, or a top with sleeves and long trousers or a skirt, for temples, and mosquito repellent: there are more mosquitoes in the wet season.

## Questions

### Is January a good time to visit Bali?

It's good if you don't mind rain. January is Bali's wettest month, about 345 mm in Denpasar by BMKG's averages, but rain usually comes in heavy showers, and it's green, hot and calmer after New Year. It's not the best month for boat trips and beach days. For dry weather, go from April to October.

### Does it rain all day in Bali in January?

Usually not. Rain often comes as heavy showers in the afternoon and at night, with sunny mornings. Some days stay grey and wet, so keep a few indoor plans.

### When is Galungan in 2027?

Wednesday 13 January 2027, with Kuningan on Saturday 23 January, following the 210-day Balinese calendar. Bali's government hadn't published its 2027 holiday circular when we checked, so confirm closer to the date.

### Can I go to Nusa Penida in January?

Yes, on calm days, but boats from Sanur may be delayed or cancelled when BMKG warns of high waves. Keep a spare day, and don't book the trip for the day before your flight.

### Is Bali crowded in January?

Busy until the first weekend after New Year and again on the Republic Day weekend from India (23 to 26 January 2027). The middle of the month is calmer.

### Is January a good month for a Bali honeymoon?

It can be: villas with private pools, spas and green Ubud suit the rainy season. If you want beaches and boat trips, choose a drier month or a drier place.
