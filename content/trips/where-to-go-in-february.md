February is the last cool month for most of India, and one of the best months of the year to travel. The snow in Kashmir and the Himalayas is at its deepest, the sea around the Andaman is at its calmest, Rajasthan's desert festivals are on, and the crowds of Christmas and New Year have gone home. Abroad, February is still dry season in Thailand, Sri Lanka and the Maldives, and Singapore's best months begin.

This page sums up our trip guides that fit February, in India and abroad, with the festival dates we could confirm and the long weekends of February 2027. Each place links to its full guide, where the official sources are listed.

Visiting India from abroad? Start here: our [visiting India guide](visiting-india.html) covers the e-Visa, what to pack, SIM cards and money.

Going earlier? See [where to go in December and January](where-to-go-in-december-january.html). Only looking abroad? Compare all our [international trips from India](international-trips-from-india.html) by flight time, best months and visa type.

## February 2027 dates to know

- **Goa Carnival:** Goa Tourism's calendar lists Carnival for February 2027 in Panaji, Margao, Vasco, Mapusa, Porvorim and Morjim, but **the dates haven't been announced**. Carnival is held in the days just before Lent, which starts on Wednesday 10 February 2027. See [Goa in January](goa-in-january.html).
- **Udaipur World Music Festival:** 9–11 February, on Rajasthan Tourism's list. See [Rajasthan in January](rajasthan-in-january.html).
- **Nagaur Fair:** 12–15 February, on Rajasthan Tourism's list.
- **Jaisalmer Desert Festival:** **18–20 February 2027**, says Rajasthan Tourism's festivals calendar (checked on 22 November 2026). See [Jaisalmer](jaisalmer-in-december.html).
- **Rann Utsav:** runs until 7 March 2027, and the full moon is on Sunday 21 February (the official full-moon window is 19–21 February). See [Rann Utsav](rann-utsav-2026.html).
- **Haridwar Kumbh baths:** 6, 11 and 20 February, so Rishikesh and Haridwar will be crowded on those days. See [Rishikesh](rishikesh-in-december.html).
- **Eravikulam National Park** near Munnar usually closes from 1 February to about 1 April for the Nilgiri tahr calving season. See [Munnar](munnar-in-december.html).
- **Chinese New Year and Tet:** Saturday 6 February 2027. Bangkok's Chinatown is packed, and Vietnam's official Tet break is 4–10 February, with busy trains from 23 January. See [Vietnam in January](vietnam-in-january.html).
- **Ramadan** is expected to start on about 8 February 2027, depending on the moon. See [Dubai in January](dubai-in-january.html).
- **Valentine's Day** is Sunday 14 February 2027.

## Long weekends in February 2027

The central government holiday list from DoPT (for offices in Delhi) has **no gazetted holiday in February 2027**, so there's no long weekend without leave. Restricted holidays can still help, if your office lets you take one (central government staff can choose a few from the list):

- **Thursday 11 February (Basant Panchami):** take Friday 12 February off for 4 days, Thursday to Sunday 14 February, Valentine's Day.
- **Friday 19 February (Shivaji Jayanti):** 3 days, Friday to Sunday, the weekend of the Jaisalmer Desert Festival.
- **Tuesday 2 March (Swami Dayananda Saraswati's Birthday):** take Monday 1 March off for 4 days, from Saturday 27 February.

See every 2027 long weekend, and the leave days that stretch them to 5 or 9 days, in our [long weekends 2027 planner](../tools/long-weekends-2027.html). Holi is on Tuesday 23 March 2027: see [where to celebrate Holi](holi-2027.html).

## Places to visit in February for snowfall

February is one of the best months for snow in India, but snow never comes on a fixed date. Keep a spare day, and check the IMD forecast before you go.

- **[Gulmarg](gulmarg-in-january.html):** Kashmir's snow meadow, about 52 km from Srinagar. The ski season runs well past January, and February and March are even snowier on average. The Khelo India Winter Games snow events were held here on 23–26 February 2026. Book gondola tickets only on the official site. See also [Kashmir in January](kashmir-in-january.html): the cold eases slowly, with Chillai Khurd to about mid-February.
- **[Auli](auli-in-december.html):** Uttarakhand's ski slope above Joshimath. Most of its snow falls in January and February, and the National Skiing Championship and Winter Carnival began here on 13 February 2026.
- **[Manali](manali-in-january.html):** the second half of January and February give the best odds. Heavy snow can close the road beyond Solang: in 2026 it reopened to 4x4s only on 9 February.
- **Shimla:** snow is much more likely in January and February than in December. See [Shimla](shimla-in-december.html).

Pack properly: see [what to wear on a winter hill trip](../guides/winter-clothes-for-hill-trips.html).

## Beaches in February

- **[Andaman](andaman-in-december.html):** February is the driest month, and February to April has the calmest sea and clearest water. Take the ferries from Sri Vijaya Puram to Swaraj Dweep (Havelock) and Shaheed Dweep (Neil).
- **[Goa](goa.html):** still in its best season, with warm, dry days and fewer crowds than at New Year. Time your trip for Carnival once Goa Tourism announces the dates. See [Goa in January](goa-in-january.html) for the weather and beaches.
- **[Pondicherry](pondicherry-in-december.html):** January and February are nearly dry, with warm days and pleasant nights. Walk White Town and the Promenade, and visit Auroville.
- **Kerala's beaches:** Varkala and Kovalam are at the end of their season. See [Kerala](kerala.html).

## Rajasthan and Gujarat: desert festivals in February

February is the month of Rajasthan's desert festivals. The **Jaisalmer Desert Festival** is on 18–20 February 2027, the **Nagaur Fair** on 12–15 February, and the **Udaipur World Music Festival** is on 9–11 February. Jaipur's winter lasts to mid-February, with sunny days and cool nights, and Udaipur is pleasant until March.

In Gujarat, the **Rann Utsav** at Dhordo runs until 7 March 2027, and the White Rann looks its best on full-moon nights, around 21 February.

Read [Jaisalmer](jaisalmer-in-december.html), [Rajasthan in January](rajasthan-in-january.html) (most of it holds for February), [Jaipur](jaipur.html), [Udaipur](udaipur.html) and [Rann Utsav](rann-utsav-2026.html).

## Places to visit in February in South India

- **[Kerala](kerala.html):** houseboats in Alappuzha, the tea hills of [Munnar](munnar-in-december.html), and the cliffs at Varkala. Kerala Tourism says houseboat cruises are best from October to February. Eravikulam National Park, home of the Nilgiri tahr, usually closes from 1 February, so see the tea estates instead. See [Kerala in January](kerala-in-january.html).
- **[Coorg](coorg-in-december.html):** coffee estates and an easy drive from Bengaluru; the season runs October to March.
- **[Hampi](hampi-in-december.html):** Karnataka Tourism names October to February as the best time, and it gets warmer through February, so start early in the morning.
- **[Pondicherry](pondicherry-in-december.html):** one of its two driest months.

## Tiger safaris and the Ganga

- **[Jim Corbett](jim-corbett-in-december.html)** and **[Ranthambore](ranthambore-in-december.html)** are open, with cold early-morning safaris. Book only on the official sites: Ranthambore's January to March bookings opened on 1 November.
- **[Varanasi](varanasi-in-december.html)** is in season from October to March. Fog can still delay trains in early February.
- **[Rishikesh](rishikesh-in-december.html)** is good for yoga, the Ganga aarti and rafting, but avoid the Haridwar Kumbh bath days (6, 11 and 20 February) if you dislike crowds.

## Near Delhi, with family or with friends

- **Near Delhi:** Jaipur, Rishikesh and Jim Corbett are a few hours away, and Shimla is an overnight trip. See [weekend getaways from Delhi](weekend-getaways-from-delhi.html), and from [Mumbai](weekend-getaways-from-mumbai.html) and [Bangalore](weekend-getaways-from-bangalore.html).
- **With family and kids:** Jaipur, Udaipur, Kerala and Goa are easy, with mild days. Abroad, Singapore and Dubai are easy city trips with children.
- **With friends:** Goa for the beaches and Carnival, Jaisalmer for a desert camp, or Manali and Gulmarg for snow.

### Thailand, Sri Lanka and the Maldives: warm and dry

These three are the easiest warm trips abroad in February. **[Thailand](thailand-trip-from-india.html)** is in the last month of its best season: Bangkok is almost dry and Phuket's beaches are calm. Chinese New Year (6 February) packs Bangkok's Chinatown, and smoke from crop fires starts to build in the north from February, so pick the beaches over Chiang Mai late in the month. See [Thailand in January](thailand-in-january.html).

**[Sri Lanka](sri-lanka-trip-from-india.html)** has its driest months on the west coast in January and February, and the hills of Nuwara Eliya and Ella are cold at night. **The [Maldives](maldives-trip-from-india.html)** is in its driest season around Malé from February to April.

### Singapore and Malaysia

**[Singapore](singapore-trip-from-india.html)** starts its driest stretch in February, after the wet months of November to January. **[Malaysia](malaysia-trip-from-india.html)** is at its best in Langkawi and Penang from December to February; avoid the east coast islands until the monsoon ends. Indians could visit Malaysia without a visa only until 31 December 2026, so check the rule before you book.

### Dubai and Vietnam

**[Dubai](dubai-trip-from-india.html)** is still warm, not hot, in February. Ramadan is expected to start on about 8 February 2027: tourists can still visit, but some places change their hours, so check before you plan. See [Dubai in January](dubai-in-january.html) for the season's events.

**[Vietnam](vietnam-trip-from-india.html)** has Tet on 6 February, with the official break from 4 to 10 February, when many shops close and trains are full. Go after the break: Hoi An's sunny season starts in February, and the south stays dry. See [Vietnam in January](vietnam-in-january.html).

### Bali, Japan and Europe

**[Bali](bali-trip-from-india.html)** is still in its wettest season in February, with rain on most days, but it's green and quieter. Nyepi, the Day of Silence, is on 8 March 2027. See [Bali in January](bali-in-january.html).

**[Japan](japan-trip-from-india.html)** is cold but mostly dry in Tokyo, and many cities keep their winter lights until February. Cherry blossom comes later, from late March.

**[Europe](europe-trip-from-india.html)** is in winter: cold cities, with snow in the Alps. Pack a padded jacket, thermals and waterproof shoes, and apply for the Schengen visa early.

## Questions

### Which is the best place to visit in February in India?

For snow, Gulmarg, Auli or Manali. For beaches, the Andaman (its driest month) or Goa. For a festival, the Jaisalmer Desert Festival on 18–20 February 2027. For the south, Kerala, Hampi and Pondicherry are still in season.

### Which places in India get snowfall in February?

Gulmarg in Kashmir is the surest, and February is one of its snowiest months. Auli and Manali also get most of their snow in January and February, and Shimla can get snow too, but no place has snow on a fixed date, so check the forecast.

### Where can I go outside India in February?

Thailand, Sri Lanka and the Maldives are warm and dry, and easy for Indians (no visa, a free ETA, or a visa on arrival). Singapore starts its driest months, and Dubai is still pleasant. Bali is in its rainy season.

### Is there a long weekend in February 2027?

Not without leave: the central government list has no gazetted holiday in February 2027. With restricted holidays, you can make 4 days from Thursday 11 February (Basant Panchami) or 3 days from Friday 19 February (Shivaji Jayanti). See our [long weekends 2027 planner](../tools/long-weekends-2027.html).

### When is the Goa Carnival in 2027?

Goa Tourism lists Carnival for February 2027, but the dates hadn't been announced when we checked. It's held in the days before Lent, which begins on 10 February 2027, so expect it in early February.

### Is February a good month for a honeymoon?

Yes. Most of India is still cool and dry, the Andaman and the Maldives have calm seas, and the snow is deep in Gulmarg. See our [honeymoon destinations in India](honeymoon-destinations-in-india.html) and [abroad](honeymoon-destinations-outside-india.html).
