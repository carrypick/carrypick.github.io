Holi, the festival of colours, falls on **Tuesday 23 March 2027**. The night before, on **Monday 22 March**, towns light the Holika Dahan bonfires. Both dates come from the central government's holiday list for 2027 (DoPT), where Holi is a gazetted holiday and Holika Dahan a restricted holiday. State holiday lists can differ, so check yours.

This guide covers where to celebrate Holi in India, what we know about the 2027 events in Mathura, Vrindavan and Barsana, how to make a long weekend of it, and what to wear and pack so the colours don't ruin your phone, skin or holiday.

Planning the whole month? See [where to go in March](where-to-go-in-march.html).

## Holi 2027 date and long weekend

| Day | Date | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Holika Dahan | Monday 22 March 2027 | Bonfires in the evening. A restricted holiday on the central list |
| Holi (Dhulandi, the day of colours) | Tuesday 23 March 2027 | Colours in the morning. A gazetted holiday |

Holi on a Tuesday makes an easy long weekend:

- **4 days:** Saturday 20 to Tuesday 23 March, with Monday 22 March off (or your restricted holiday).
- **9 days:** Saturday 20 to Sunday 28 March, with 3 days off (22, 24 and 25 March), because **Good Friday is on 26 March 2027**.

See every 2027 long weekend in our [long weekends 2027 planner](../tools/long-weekends-2027.html). Trains open for booking about 60 days before you travel, so for a 20 March journey that's around 19 January. Check IRCTC for the exact rules.

## Holi 2027 in Mathura, Vrindavan and Barsana

Braj, the land of Krishna around Mathura, celebrates Holi for about two weeks, not one day. UP Tourism calls it **Rangotsav**, and publishes the programme on its website each year.

**The 2027 dates are to be announced.** When we checked on 25 November 2026, UP Tourism's Rangotsav page still showed the 2026 programme. We'll add the 2027 dates when UP Tourism publishes them, usually in the weeks before Holi.

Here is the **2026** programme from UP Tourism, to show how the days run. Holi itself was on 4 March 2026:

| 2026 date | Event | Place |
|---|---|---|
| Tue 24 Feb | Phag Aamantran, and Laddu Holi | Nandgaon; Barsana |
| Wed 25 Feb | **Lathmar Holi** | Barsana |
| Thu 26 Feb | Lathmar Holi | Nandgaon |
| Fri 27 Feb | Rang and Phoolon ki Holi (colours and flowers) | Shri Krishna Janmasthan and Dwarkadhish Temple, Mathura; Banke Bihari Temple, Vrindavan |
| Sat 28 Feb | Chhadi Mar Holi | Gokul |
| Tue 3 Mar | Holika Dahan (night) | Falain village |
| Wed 4 Mar | Dhulandi, the day of colours | Mathura |
| Thu 5 Mar | Huranga | Baldev (Dauji), Nandgaon and Jab village; Charkula dance in Mukhrai |
| Tue 10 Mar | Chhadi Mar Holi | Mahavan |
| Thu 12 Mar | Holi Rath Mela | Shree Rangji Temple, Vrindavan |

If 2027 follows the same pattern, **Lathmar Holi in Barsana would fall about a week before Holi, in mid-March 2027**. That's our reading of the 2026 calendar, not an official date, so don't book on it alone.

Tips for Braj:

- **Expect huge crowds.** Holi week is the busiest time of the year in Mathura and Vrindavan. Barsana's lanes are narrow and steep.
- **Stay in Mathura or Vrindavan** and go to Barsana and Nandgaon for the day. Book early.
- **Getting there:** Mathura is about 150 km from Delhi by road on the Yamuna Expressway, 2½ to 3½ hours (our estimates), and trains run to Mathura Junction. See [weekend getaways from Delhi](weekend-getaways-from-delhi.html).
- **Dress modestly** at the temples, and keep phones and bags away from the monkeys in Vrindavan.

## Holi in Varanasi

Varanasi is one of the most popular places for Holi, and March is the end of its season (October to March). The colours are played on the ghats and in the lanes, and the crowds can be rough.

Many travellers, especially women, prefer to watch from a guest house rooftop on the ghats, or go out with a group or a local guide in the morning, when the colour play is at its height. Keep your phone in a waterproof pouch and your money in an inside pocket. Read our [Varanasi guide](varanasi-in-december.html) for the ghats, the aarti and where to stay.

## Holi in Rajasthan and Goa

- **[Jaipur](jaipur.html) and [Udaipur](udaipur.html):** both are in season until March, so Holi is a good last trip before the heat. Holika Dahan bonfires light up the old cities the night before, and many hotels hold their own Holi for guests, which is calmer than the street.
- **[Goa](goa.html):** Goa's own spring festival is **Shigmo**. Goa Tourism's calendar lists it for **March 2027** in Panaji, Margao, Vasco and other towns, with tentative dates still to be announced.

## Holi in Delhi, Mumbai and Bangalore

If you stay home, the big cities have ticketed Holi parties and events, as well as Holi in housing societies. Buy tickets only from the organiser's own page or a known ticket site, check what's included (water, food, music, safety), and leave early before roads get busy with people returning home.

For a short trip instead, see weekend getaways from [Delhi](weekend-getaways-from-delhi.html), [Mumbai](weekend-getaways-from-mumbai.html) and [Bangalore](weekend-getaways-from-bangalore.html): each has the Holi weekend in its long-weekend table.

## What to wear for Holi

- **Old clothes you can throw away.** Colours often don't wash out. White or light cotton is the classic choice: it shows the colours and keeps you cool.
- **Full sleeves and full-length trousers or a long kurta** protect your skin. Avoid thin fabric that turns see-through when wet.
- **Old closed shoes or sports shoes** you don't mind losing. Wet floors get slippery, and flip-flops come off in a crowd.
- **Sunglasses** keep powder and water out of your eyes. Leave contact lenses at home and wear glasses.
- **A cap, bandana or scarf** covers your hair.
- **No jewellery or good watches.** They get lost or stained.

## Skin and hair care before and after Holi

We're not doctors, so these are simple, common tips. If you have a skin problem or allergy, ask a dermatologist.

- **Before:** put on moisturiser or a little oil on your skin and hair, and sunscreen on top for the sun. Tie long hair up and cover it. Some people apply clear nail polish so colour doesn't stain the nails.
- **Choose your colours with care.** If you buy your own, pick ones labelled herbal or natural, test a little on your arm first, and avoid anything that looks oily or shiny.
- **After:** brush off dry powder before you wet it, then wash with plenty of plain water and a mild face wash. Don't scrub hard. The colour fades over a few days.
- **Eyes:** if colour gets in your eyes, rinse with clean water and don't rub. See a doctor if it still hurts.

## Safety and etiquette

- **Consent matters.** "Bura na mano, Holi hai" (don't mind, it's Holi) is a saying, not permission. Don't put colour on people who say no, on older people who don't join in, or on animals.
- **Women travellers:** go in a group, prefer hotel or society Holi, or watch from a rooftop. Crowds in some places get rough, especially by afternoon.
- **Bhang** (cannabis drinks and sweets) is common at Holi and much stronger than it seems. If you don't know what's in a drink or sweet, don't have it.
- **Kids:** keep them away from water balloons thrown at the face, and from the road.
- **Roads:** many drivers are drunk on Holi afternoon. Travel in the morning, or the next day.
- **Emergency (police, fire, ambulance):** 112. Ministry of Tourism helpline, 24 hours: 1363 or 1800-11-1363.

## Questions

### When is Holi in 2027?

Holi is on **Tuesday 23 March 2027**, and Holika Dahan is on Monday 22 March, says the central government holiday list from DoPT. Some state lists can differ.

### What is the date of Lathmar Holi 2027 in Barsana?

It hasn't been announced. When we checked on 25 November 2026, UP Tourism's Rangotsav page still showed the 2026 programme, when Lathmar Holi was on 25 February in Barsana and 26 February in Nandgaon, a week before Holi. If 2027 follows the same pattern, expect it in mid-March. We'll add the date when UP Tourism publishes it.

### Which is the best place to celebrate Holi in India?

For tradition, Barsana and Nandgaon (Lathmar Holi), Vrindavan and Mathura. For a Holi by the Ganga, Varanasi or Rishikesh. For a calmer family Holi in a palace city, Jaipur or Udaipur.

### Is Holi in Varanasi safe for women?

It can be rough in crowded lanes and ghats. Go in a group or with a local guide, go out in the morning, or watch from a guest house rooftop. Keep your phone and money in a waterproof pouch.

### Is there a long weekend for Holi 2027?

Yes. Holi is a Tuesday, so with Monday 22 March off you get 4 days from Saturday 20 March. Good Friday is on 26 March, so with 3 days off you can make 9 days, to Sunday 28 March.

### What should I wear for Holi?

Old white or light cotton clothes with full sleeves, old closed shoes, sunglasses, and a cap or scarf for your hair. Leave jewellery and contact lenses at home.

### How do I protect my skin before Holi?

Put on moisturiser or a little oil, then sunscreen, before you go out, and cover your hair. Afterwards, brush off dry colour, then wash gently with plenty of water.
