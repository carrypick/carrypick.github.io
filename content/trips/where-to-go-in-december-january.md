December and January are the best months for most of India: the monsoon is long over, the skies are clear, and it's cool almost everywhere except the far south. They are also the busiest months, so it pays to choose well and book early. Here's how India's favourite winter trips compare, plus Bhutan and Nepal, two easy trips abroad without a visa, and Singapore.

## Going in January?

January has its own weather and events, so we've written month guides for the places people ask about most:

- **[Manali in January](manali-in-january.html):** the best odds of snow (last winter it came on 23 January), the Winter Carnival and skiing at Solang.
- **[Kashmir in January](kashmir-in-january.html):** the end of Chillai Kalan, a frozen Dal Lake on the coldest nights, and snow days that can stop flights and the highway.
- **[Gulmarg in January](gulmarg-in-january.html):** deep snow, IISM ski courses from 3 and 17 January, and the gondola.
- **[Goa in January](goa-in-january.html):** the same sunny weather as New Year, fewer crowds, and the Feast of the Three Kings on 6 January.
- **[Rajasthan in January](rajasthan-in-january.html):** the Bikaner Camel Festival (9–10 January), kites over Jaipur on 14 January and the Jaipur Literature Festival.
- **[Kerala in January](kerala-in-january.html):** dry, sunny backwaters, frosty mornings in Munnar's tea valleys, Makaravilakku on 14 January and the Kerala Literature Festival.
- **[Thailand in January](thailand-in-january.html):** one of the driest months, with the Andaman coast around Phuket and Krabi at its best and cool nights in Chiang Mai.
- **[Vietnam in January](vietnam-in-january.html):** sunny in the south, cool and grey in Hanoi, and Tet on 6 February 2027, with busy trains from 23 January.
- **[Dubai in January](dubai-in-january.html):** the coolest month, the Shopping Festival until 17 January and the Dubai Marathon.
- **[Bali in January](bali-in-january.html):** the wettest month, but green and calmer after New Year, with Galungan on 13 January.

## Goa: for beaches and New Year parties

Goa is warm, dry and sunny in winter, and New Year's Eve is its biggest night. Fly into Mopa for North Goa's busy beaches, or Dabolim for central and South Goa. Remember the rules that bring big fines: no drinking or cooking on the beach, helmets on scooters, and loudspeakers only until midnight from 24 to 31 December.

Read the full [Goa trip guide](goa.html), our [Goa New Year 2027 guide](goa-new-year-2027.html) and our [Goa packing list](../guides/goa-packing-list.html).

## Andaman: for clear sea and coral

Andaman is warm in winter, with calm seas on most days, though December can still bring showers. Fly to Sri Vijaya Puram (Port Blair), then take a ferry to Swaraj Dweep (Havelock) for Radhanagar Beach, or to Shaheed Dweep (Neil). Jolly Buoy's coral reef opens from mid-November. Leave a spare day in case ferries are cancelled.

Read the full [Andaman in December and January guide](andaman-in-december.html).

## Manali: for snow and mountains

Manali is the classic snow trip, but snow isn't guaranteed: December 2025 was almost completely dry, and the first big snowfall came only in late January 2026. Rohtang Pass is closed in winter, and police often stop cars beyond Solang after snow. Keep your dates flexible, and pack thermals.

Read the full [Manali winter guide](manali.html), and see [what to wear in Manali in December](../guides/what-to-wear-in-manali-in-december.html). Want colder weather and deeper snow? See [Kashmir in December](kashmir-in-december.html).

## Shimla: for an easy hill trip

Shimla is the easiest hill station to reach from Delhi and Chandigarh, and the toy train from Kalka is a trip in itself. December is cold and mostly dry, with sunny days. Snow is much more likely in January and February: last winter, the first snow came only on 23 January 2026. For snow on a short trip, go up to Kufri or Narkanda.

Read the full [Shimla in December and January guide](shimla-in-december.html).

## Rishikesh: for the Ganga, yoga and rafting

Rishikesh has sunny days and cold nights in winter, and no snow. Rafting is open, and the evening Ganga aarti at Triveni Ghat and Parmarth Niketan starts around sunset. This winter, Ram Jhula and Lakshman Jhula are closed, so you cross on Bajrang Setu or Janki Setu. The Haridwar Ardh Kumbh starts on 14 January 2027, so expect crowds in mid-January.

Read the full [Rishikesh in December and January guide](rishikesh-in-december.html).

## Darjeeling: for mountain views and the toy train

Darjeeling is cold, dry and clear in winter, and December mornings often give the best views of Kanchenjunga, from Tiger Hill at sunrise or from the Mall. Snow in the town itself is rare. Ride the toy train between Darjeeling and Ghum, past the Batasia Loop, and walk a tea garden.

Read the full [Darjeeling in December and January guide](darjeeling-in-december.html).

## Bhutan: for dzongs, passes and a winter festival

Bhutan is dry and sunny in December, with freezing nights in Paro and Thimphu. Indians need no visa, only a passport or original voter ID and the daily fee of ₹1,200. Watch the Druk Wangyel Tshechu at Dochula on 13 December, see the black-necked cranes in Phobjikha, and hike to Tiger's Nest.

Read the full [Bhutan in December and January guide](bhutan-in-december.html).

## Nepal: for temples, mountain views and Pokhara

Nepal is dry and sunny in December, with mild days and cold nights in Kathmandu. Indians need no visa, only a passport or original voter ID. Spend two days among the temples of the Kathmandu valley, watch the sunrise over the Himalaya from Nagarkot, and end by Phewa Lake in Pokhara. Snow falls only on the high trekking routes, and some passes can close in winter.

Read the full [Nepal in December and January guide](nepal-in-december.html), and see our [Nepal packing list](../guides/nepal-packing-list.html).

## Singapore: for a city trip abroad with kids

Singapore is warm in December, about 30 °C by day, and it's the wettest month, but showers are usually short. Orchard Road has its Christmas lights until 3 January, and Gardens by the Bay has Christmas Wonderland from 27 November. Indians need a visa, applied for through an agent; it usually takes 3 to 5 working days, so apply early.

Read the full [Singapore trip guide](singapore-trip-from-india.html), and see our [Singapore packing list](../guides/singapore-packing-list.html).

## Dubai: for a warm city break with shopping and the desert

Dubai is at its best in December and January: about 26 °C by day and 15 °C at night, with rain on only about 2 days a month. Most Indians need a visa before they fly, usually through a tourism company, airline or hotel; GDRFA lists AED 200 for 30 days, plus VAT.

Read the full [Dubai trip guide](dubai-trip-from-india.html) and our [Dubai New Year 2027 guide](dubai-new-year-2027.html) for the Burj Khalifa fireworks, and see our [Dubai packing list](../guides/dubai-packing-list.html) and [UAE customs rules](../baggage/customs/uae-dubai.html).

## Sri Lanka: for an easy trip abroad with a free visa

Sri Lanka's west coast and hills dry out after November: Colombo gets about 175 mm of rain in December and only about 58 mm in January. Indians get a free 30-day tourist ETA, applied for online at eta.gov.lk before you fly, and the clock is the same as India's. Nuwara Eliya is cold at night, about 9 to 11 °C.

Read the full [Sri Lanka trip guide](sri-lanka-trip-from-india.html), and see our [Sri Lanka packing list](../guides/sri-lanka-packing-list.html).

## Thailand: for temples, beaches and island trips abroad

Thailand is warm and mostly dry in December: Bangkok averages about 31 °C by day and 21 °C at night, with almost no rain, and Pattaya is dry too. Phuket can still get a few showers. Since 15 September 2026, Indians can visit for up to 30 days without a visa; fill in the free TDAC arrival card in the 3 days before you land. Spend 2 or 3 days in Bangkok, then go to Phuket, Krabi or Pattaya.

Read the full [Thailand trip guide](thailand-trip-from-india.html), and see our [Thailand packing list](../guides/thailand-packing-list.html).

## Hong Kong: for Disneyland and a short city trip abroad

Hong Kong is at its best in December: it's the driest month, about 20 °C by day and 16 °C at night on the Hong Kong Observatory's averages. Indians need no visa for up to 14 days, but must complete a free online pre-arrival registration first and carry the signed slip. Spend two days in the city and one at Disneyland or Ocean Park.

Read the full [Hong Kong trip guide](hong-kong-trip-from-india.html).

## Japan: for winter lights, Mount Fuji and bullet trains

Japan is cold in December, Tokyo about 12 °C by day and 4 °C at night, but mostly dry and sunny, so Mount Fuji views are often clear. Indians need a visa (an eVISA through VFS), and applications open 3 months before travel, so apply early. Pack a warm jacket and layers.

Read the full [Japan trip guide](japan-trip-from-india.html).

## Vietnam: for Ha Long Bay and a cool Hanoi winter

In December, Hanoi is cool and dry, about 22 °C by day and 15 °C at night, and Ho Chi Minh City is hot and dry, about 32 °C. Da Nang and Hoi An are rainy, with rain on about 21 days, so plan fewer days there. Indians need an e-visa (US$25 for a single entry), applied for online on Vietnam's official portal before you fly.

Read the full [Vietnam trip guide](vietnam-trip-from-india.html), and see our [Vietnam packing list](../guides/vietnam-packing-list.html).

## Bali: for a honeymoon abroad, if you don't mind rain

Bali is hot in December, about 33 °C by day, but it's the wet season, with rain on about 22 days, often in the afternoon. Indians get a visa on arrival for Rp 500,000 (up to 30 days), or buy it online as an e-VOA before flying, and pay the Rp 150,000 Bali tourist levy. Spend 3 nights in Ubud and 3 near the beach.

Read the full [Bali trip guide](bali-trip-from-india.html), and see our [Bali packing list](../guides/bali-packing-list.html).

## Maldives: for a beach honeymoon abroad

The Maldives is about 30 °C in December, with short showers on about 12 days in Malé, and it gets drier in January. Indians get a tourist visa on arrival with no pre-approval: carry a return ticket and a paid booking, and file the free IMUGA declaration in the 96 hours before you land. Choose a resort island for privacy, or a guesthouse on a local island to save money.

Read the full [Maldives trip guide](maldives-trip-from-india.html), and see our [Maldives packing list](../guides/maldives-packing-list.html).

## Mauritius: for a beach honeymoon abroad without a visa

Mauritius is warm in December, about 31 °C by day and 23 °C at night in Port Louis, with rain on about 6 days; January is wetter. Indians need no visa: carry a return ticket, a hotel booking and enough money, and fill in the free All-in-One Travel Digital Form before you fly. There are direct flights from Delhi, Mumbai, Chennai and Bengaluru.

Read the full [Mauritius trip guide](mauritius-trip-from-india.html), and see our [honeymoon packing list](../guides/honeymoon-packing-list.html).

## Georgia: for snow, mountain churches and Tbilisi

Georgia's ski season opens on 20 December 2026 in Gudauri and Bakuriani, says the Mountain Trails Agency that runs the lifts. Tbilisi averages about 4 °C in December, with Christmas lights and New Year parties, and the road north to Kazbegi passes Gudauri's snowy slopes. Indians apply for a Georgian e-visa online, at least 5 working days ahead, or need none with a valid US, UK, EU or Schengen visa or residence permit. There are no direct flights from India right now.

Read the full [Georgia trip guide](georgia-trip-from-india.html), and see our guide to [winter clothes for snow and hill trips](../guides/winter-clothes-for-hill-trips.html).

## Kazakhstan: for snow abroad without a visa

Almaty, Kazakhstan's biggest city, is about 2½ hours from Delhi by direct flight, and Indians need no visa for up to 14 days. In December it averages about 0 °C by day and −9 °C at night, and the Shymbulak ski resort in the mountains above the city has beginner slopes, ski lessons and gear to rent. Pack thermals, a padded jacket and boots with a good grip.

Read the full [Kazakhstan trip guide](kazakhstan-trip-from-india.html), and see our guide to [winter clothes for snow and hill trips](../guides/winter-clothes-for-hill-trips.html).

## Turkey: for Istanbul and a snowy Cappadocia

Istanbul averages about 11 °C by day and 5 °C at night in December, its wettest month, and Cappadocia drops below freezing at night, with a chance of snow on the fairy chimneys, on the Turkish State Meteorological Service's averages. It's the low season, so the old city's sights are less crowded, but balloon flights are cancelled more often in winter, so keep a spare morning. There are direct flights from Delhi and Mumbai to Istanbul. Most Indians need a visa from a Turkish mission; with a valid US, UK, Schengen or Irish visa or residence permit, a one-month e-visa online.

Read the full [Turkey trip guide](turkey-trip-from-india.html), and see our guide to [winter clothes for snow and hill trips](../guides/winter-clothes-for-hill-trips.html).

## Europe: for Christmas markets and snow in the Alps

A December trip to Paris, Switzerland and Italy means Christmas lights, markets and snow in the Swiss mountains, but also cold days and short daylight: Zurich averages about 3 °C by day and −2 °C at night, while Rome is milder, about 13 °C. Indians need a Schengen visa: apply at least 15 days, and ideally several weeks, before you fly, because December appointments fill up.

Read the full [Europe trip guide](europe-trip-from-india.html), and see our guide to [winter clothes for snow and hill trips](../guides/winter-clothes-for-hill-trips.html).

## Ooty: for tea hills and the toy train

Ooty is cool by day and cold at night in winter. It doesn't snow, but clear January mornings bring frost on the grass. Ride the Nilgiri toy train up from Mettupalayam (it stops after landslides, so check first), and visit the Botanical Garden, Doddabetta and the tea estates. Cars from outside the Nilgiris need an e-pass.

Read the full [Ooty in December and January guide](ooty-in-december.html).

## Coorg: for coffee estates and an easy drive from Bangalore

Coorg is cool, dry and often misty in the morning in winter, with no snow. Two days cover Abbey Falls, Raja's Seat and Madikeri, then Dubare and the Golden Temple at Bylakuppe. There's no airport or railway in Kodagu, so most people drive from Bangalore, about 257 km.

Read the full [Coorg in December guide](coorg-in-december.html).

## Munnar: for tea hills and frosty mornings

Munnar has mild days and cold nights in winter, and the tea estates touched −1 °C in late December 2025. It's frost, not snow. Eravikulam National Park is open in December and January. Combine it with Kochi and the Alappuzha backwaters.

Read the full [Munnar in December guide](munnar-in-december.html).

## Meghalaya: for root bridges and Christmas in Shillong

Meghalaya is dry and clear in December, with cold nights and short days. Walk to the living root bridges near Sohra (Cherrapunji), take a boat on Dawki's clear river, and see Shillong lit up for Christmas. It doesn't snow. Your hotel or homestay must register you with the state.

Read the full [Meghalaya in December guide](meghalaya-in-december.html).

## Uttarakhand: Nainital, Mussoorie, Auli and Jim Corbett

- **Nainital** is cold and clear in December, with boating on the lake and the ropeway to Snow View. Snow is rare before January. Read [Nainital in December](nainital-in-december.html).
- **Mussoorie** has sunny days, cold nights and the Winterline at sunset, from mid-October to mid-January. Read [Mussoorie in December](mussoorie-in-december.html).
- **Auli** has India's best-known ski slopes after Gulmarg, but its snow and ski season really starts in January, and the Joshimath ropeway is closed. Read [Auli in December](auli-in-december.html).
- **Jim Corbett** has all its safari zones open in December, including Dhikala. Book the permit only on the official site. Read [Jim Corbett in December](jim-corbett-in-december.html).

## Ranthambore: for a tiger safari in Rajasthan

Ranthambore is sunny by day and cold in the early morning in winter, with December safaris from 7 to 10:30 AM and 2 to 5:35 PM. Book only on the official site the Rajasthan Forest Department links to; January to March bookings opened on 1 November. Combine it with Jaipur, about 166 km away.

Read the full [Ranthambore in December guide](ranthambore-in-december.html).

## Hampi: for the ruins of Vijayanagara

Hampi is dry and warm in December, about 30 °C by day, with cool mornings: the best season to walk among the temples and boulders. Two days cover the Virupaksha temple, the stone chariot at Vittala and the Royal Centre. Take the overnight train from Bengaluru to Hosapete.

Read the full [Hampi in December guide](hampi-in-december.html).

## Pondicherry: for the French Quarter and Auroville

Pondicherry is warm in winter, but December is the last month of the northeast monsoon here, with about 248 mm of rain on average and a cyclone risk early in the month. Late December and January are better bets. Walk White Town and the Promenade, and book the Matrimandir's Inner Chamber up to 5 working days ahead.

Read the full [Pondicherry in December guide](pondicherry-in-december.html).

## Varanasi: for the ghats and the Ganga aarti

Varanasi is dry with sunny afternoons in winter, but mornings are cold and often foggy, which delays trains and flights. Take a boat along the ghats, watch the evening aarti at Dashashwamedh Ghat (around 6 PM in winter), and visit Sarnath (its museum is closed on Fridays). Kashi Vishwanath is extremely crowded at New Year.

Read the full [Varanasi in December and January guide](varanasi-in-december.html).

## Jaipur: for forts, palaces and markets

Jaipur has warm sunny afternoons and chilly mornings, perfect for walking around forts. Combine it with Delhi and Agra for the Golden Triangle. In January, the Jaipur Literature Festival (14–18 January 2027) and the kite festival around 14 January add more to see.

Read the full [Jaipur trip guide](jaipur.html).

## Udaipur: for lakes and palaces

Udaipur is warm and dry in winter, with cold evenings by Lake Pichola. Two days cover City Palace, a boat to the Jagmandir island palace, Bagore ki Haveli's evening dance show and Fateh Sagar. The Shilpgram Utsav crafts fair is usually held from 21 to 30 December. Add a third day for Kumbhalgarh and Ranakpur, or Chittorgarh.

Read the full [Udaipur winter trip plan](udaipur.html).

## Jaisalmer: for the desert and a golden fort

Jaisalmer has sunny days of about 25 °C in December and cold nights, colder still in a desert camp at the Sam dunes. Walk the living fort and the carved havelis, then ride a camel at sunset and spend a night under the stars. The Desert Festival 2027 is on 18–20 February.

Read the full [Jaisalmer in December guide](jaisalmer-in-december.html).

## Rann of Kutch: for the White Rann and Rann Utsav

Rann Utsav 2026–27 runs from 1 November to 7 March at the Dhordo tent city, beside a salt desert that turns silver under the full moon (24 December and 22 January). Days are warm and dry, nights cool. Fly or take a train to Bhuj, about 81 km away.

Read the full [Rann Utsav 2026–27 guide](rann-utsav-2026.html).

## Gulmarg: for snow and the gondola

Gulmarg is Kashmir's snow meadow, about 52 km from Srinagar, with nights well below freezing. Snow is likely in December but surest from late December to February. Book gondola tickets only on the official site, and keep a spare day for Phase 2.

Read the full [Gulmarg in December guide](gulmarg-in-december.html).

## Spiti: for a hard, snowy winter adventure

Spiti is for experienced travellers in winter: only the Shimla–Kinnaur–Kaza road is open, Kunzum La and Chandratal are cut off, and nights fall far below freezing (IMD's January average at Kalpa, lower down on the way in, is about −4 °C). In return you get monasteries in the snow, quiet villages and a chance of snow leopards around Kibber. Keep spare days for snow.

Read the full [Spiti in winter guide](spiti-in-winter.html).

## Kerala: for backwaters, tea hills and beaches

Kerala is warm on the coast and cool in the Munnar hills. Spend a night on a houseboat in Alappuzha, walk the tea estates in Munnar, and end on the cliffs at Varkala. In Fort Kochi, New Year's Eve ends with the Pappanji burning on the beach at midnight. Note that 1 January is a dry day in Kerala.

Read the full [Kerala trip guide](kerala.html), and our [Munnar in December guide](munnar-in-december.html) for the hills.

## How to choose

- **Want snow?** Go to Manali or Shimla, but plan for January or February for a better chance, and check the forecast. Kashmir has the most snow: see [Gulmarg in December](gulmarg-in-december.html).
- **Want to party on New Year's Eve?** Goa, or the Cochin Carnival in Fort Kochi.
- **Travelling with parents or children?** Jaipur and Kerala are calmer, with easy sightseeing and mild days.
- **Want a hill trip in the south?** Ooty, easy from Bangalore and Coimbatore. Expect frost, not snow.
- **Want temples and the ghats?** Varanasi, but keep spare time for fog delays.
- **Want a short, calm trip from Delhi?** Rishikesh, for the Ganga aarti, yoga and rafting. Avoid the Kumbh bath days around 14 January.
- **Want the desert?** Jaisalmer for golden dunes and camps, or the Rann of Kutch for the white salt desert and Rann Utsav.
- **Love palaces and lakes?** Udaipur, with a day trip to Kumbhalgarh and Ranakpur. It pairs well with Jaipur if you have a week.
- **Visiting India for the first time?** Jaipur with Delhi and Agra, or Kerala, are easy first trips. Goa is popular with foreign visitors for a beach holiday.
- **Short on time?** Pick one place. Moving between them takes most of a day. For 2 or 3 days, see our weekend getaways from [Delhi](weekend-getaways-from-delhi.html), [Mumbai](weekend-getaways-from-mumbai.html) and [Bangalore](weekend-getaways-from-bangalore.html).

## Questions

### Where should I go in India in December?

For beaches and parties, Goa. For snow, Manali, though snow isn't guaranteed in December. For forts and palaces, Jaipur. For backwaters, hills and quieter beaches, Kerala. All four are at their best in winter.

### Which place in India has snow in December?

Manali often gets snow from December to February, but not every year: December 2025 was almost completely dry. For a better chance of snow, go in January or February and check the forecast.

### Where can I celebrate New Year in India?

Goa is India's biggest New Year party, and Fort Kochi in Kerala ends the year with the Cochin Carnival and the Pappanji burning at midnight. Book early, because both get very crowded. See [where to go for New Year 2027](new-year-trips-2027.html) for more ideas, in India and abroad.

### Is December a good time to visit Kerala?

Yes. The monsoon is over, the backwaters are calm, and Munnar is cool. A few showers are still possible on the coast in December.

### Is it cold in Jaipur in December and January?

Mornings and nights are cold, around 8–9 °C on average and sometimes 2–3 °C, while afternoons are sunny and around 22–24 °C. Carry a warm jacket.
