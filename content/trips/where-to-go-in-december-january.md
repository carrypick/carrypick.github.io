**Short answer:** for beaches and New Year parties, go to [Goa](goa.html). For snow, go to [Gulmarg](gulmarg-in-december.html) or [Manali](manali.html), but plan for January or later, because December is often dry. For forts and palaces, [Jaipur](jaipur.html) or [Udaipur](udaipur.html). For backwaters and tea hills, [Kerala](kerala.html). Abroad without a visa: [Bhutan](bhutan-in-december.html), [Nepal](nepal-in-december.html) or [Thailand](thailand-trip-from-india.html). Christmas to New Year is peak season everywhere, so book as early as you can. The tables below compare every trip we cover; each name links to its full guide.

## Questions

### Where should I go in India in December?

Goa for beaches and parties, Jaipur or Udaipur for forts and palaces, Kerala for backwaters and tea hills, and Gulmarg for snow. Manali often has no snow in December, so go there in January or February if snow matters.

### Where should I go in December in India with family?

Jaipur and Kerala are the easiest with parents or children: mild days, easy sightseeing and good trains and flights. Goa works too if you stay away from the party beaches. Avoid hill roads after heavy snow with small children.

### Which places in India have snowfall in December?

Gulmarg in Kashmir is the surest, from late December. Manali, Shimla and Auli can get snow in December, but not every year: December 2025 was almost completely dry in Manali. January and February are surer.

### Where should I go in January in India?

All the December trips still work, with fewer crowds after New Year. January is better than December for snow in Manali and Shimla, for skiing in Gulmarg and Auli, and for a dry trip to Pondicherry or Kerala's coast.

### Where can I go in December outside India?

Without a visa: Bhutan and Nepal (passport or voter ID), Thailand (up to 30 days) and Mauritius. With a visa: Dubai, Singapore, Vietnam or Europe. Sri Lanka gives Indians a free online ETA. Bali is in its rainy season.

### Which places are less crowded in December?

Auli and Spiti are quiet in December, and Hampi gets busy only on long weekends. Everywhere is busier from Christmas to New Year, so early December and mid-January are calmer.

### Where can I celebrate New Year in India?

Goa is India's biggest New Year party, and Fort Kochi in Kerala ends the year with the Cochin Carnival and the Pappanji burning at midnight. See [where to go for New Year 2027](new-year-trips-2027.html).
