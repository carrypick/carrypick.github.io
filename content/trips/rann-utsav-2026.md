Rann Utsav is Gujarat's winter festival on the edge of the White Rann of Kutch, a salt desert so flat and white that it seems to go on forever. A tent city at Dhordo village hosts visitors through the winter, with folk music, Kutchi crafts and walks on the salt at sunset and under the full moon. This guide covers the official 2026–27 dates, how booking works, the permit, the weather, a short Kutch plan, getting there and what to wear.

## Rann Utsav 2026–27 dates

- **Start date:** 1 November 2026.
- **Last date:** 7 March 2027.
- **Where:** the tent city at Dhordo village in Kutch district, Gujarat, right next to the White Rann.

Both Gujarat Tourism and the official Rann Utsav site give these dates (checked on 22 November 2026). The Ministry of Tourism's Incredible India site gives a shorter "Nov 2026 to Feb 2027", so if you plan for early March, confirm on the official site first.

**Busiest dates:** the official site lists Christmas week, from 18 December 2026 to 2 January 2027, as a festive period, along with the full-moon dates below. Book these weeks early.

## Full-moon nights at the White Rann

The White Rann glows under a full moon, and these are the most popular nights of the season. The official site lists these full-moon windows for the tent city:

| Month | Full-moon window (official site) | Full moon |
|---|---|---|
| December 2026 | 22–24 December | 24 December, early morning |
| January 2027 | 21–23 January | 22 January, evening |
| February 2027 | 19–21 February | 21 February, early morning |

The official site says a peak-season surcharge applies from one day before the full-moon dates. Full-moon times are from timeanddate.com for Bhuj. The night before a full moon is almost as bright, and much easier to book.

## Rann Utsav packages and booking

- **Book on the official site only.** Gujarat Tourism's Rann Utsav page links to [rannutsav.net](https://rannutsav.net/) as the official site, with bookings at booking.rannutsav.net. The official site says the tent city is operated by Evoke Experiences. Other sites with similar names are not the official one, so check the address before you pay.
- **Types of stay:** Gujarat Tourism lists Swiss Cottages, Premium Tents, Super Premium Tents, Rajwadi Suites and Darbari Suites, subject to availability.
- **What packages include:** the official site describes vegetarian Kutchi and Indian meals (Jain food is available), cultural performances and craft displays, and some activities, of which some are chargeable. It says packages may include shared transfers from Bhuj airport and Bhuj railway station at fixed times. Check exactly what your package covers before you pay.
- **Ticket price:** we don't show prices. The official booking site has the current package prices for your dates, and they are higher on full-moon and festive dates.
- **Not staying at the tent city?** Many visitors stay in Bhuj or at resorts and homestays near Dhordo, and visit the White Rann for sunset. See the hotel links below.

## Rann of Kutch weather in December

Long-term averages for Bhuj, the nearest city with an IMD station, from the India Meteorological Department (IMD), 1981–2010:

| Month | Day | Night | Rain |
|---|---|---|---|
| December | About 29 °C | About 11 °C | Almost none (0.2 mm) |
| January | About 27 °C | About 10 °C | About 1 mm |

- **Warm, sunny days and cold nights.** The temperature drops by about 17 °C after dark, and the open salt flat feels colder still in the wind.
- **The coldest nights on record** at Bhuj were 0.6 °C on 31 December 1990 and −0.2 °C on 28 January 1977, says IMD.
- **Almost no rain** from November to February, which is why this is the season to go.
- **Sunset** is at about 6:05 PM in early December and about 6:15 PM at the end of the month (timeanddate.com). Be on the salt half an hour before.

## The White Rann permit

Visitors going onto the White Rann near Dhordo have needed a permit, checked on the road at Bhirandiyara. We couldn't find a current official government page that explains how to get it, so:

- **Ask your tent city or hotel** whether your package covers the permit, or where to get it.
- **Carry original photo ID** for everyone in your group, and keep it handy at the check post.
- **Be careful with "permit" websites.** An old online permit address mentioned in news reports from 2018 now goes to an unrelated website, so don't pay on a site you can't trace to the Gujarat government.

## Rann of Kutch trip plan: 2 or 3 days

**Day 1: Bhuj to the White Rann**

1. Arrive in Bhuj in the morning, by flight or the overnight train.
2. **Prag Mahal and Aina Mahal,** the old palaces in one walled compound in Bhuj. Prag Mahal, built in Italian Gothic style in the 1860s, has a 45 m bell tower.
3. Drive about 81 km (about 1 hour 20 minutes) to Dhordo after lunch, and check in.
4. **Sunset on the White Rann,** then the evening's music and dance at the tent city.

**Day 2: Kalo Dungar and the villages**

1. **Sunrise on the salt,** when it's coldest and quietest.
2. **Kalo Dungar** (the Black Hill), Kutch's highest point at 462 m, about 90 km north of Bhuj, for a view over the Rann. Gujarat Tourism says the area is under the army and photography isn't allowed.
3. **Craft villages** near Dhordo and Bhuj, known for embroidery, block printing and other Kutchi crafts. Buy directly from the makers.
4. **Full-moon night** on the White Rann, if your dates match.

**Day 3: Dholavira, or Mandvi and Bhuj**

- **Dholavira,** "one of the five largest Harappan sites", says the Kutch district site, on Khadir Bet island. UNESCO added it to the World Heritage list on 27 July 2021. It's a long drive, so make it a full day, or stay nearby.
- **Or Smritivan and Mandvi:** Smritivan, on Bhujiyo hill in Bhuj, is the memorial and museum for the victims of the 2001 earthquake. Mandvi beach, on the coast, is known for sunsets and water sports.

## Getting to the Rann of Kutch

- **By air:** fly to Bhuj (BHJ). Air India has flights to Bhuj from Mumbai, Delhi and Ahmedabad, and news reports say a new morning flight from Delhi started on 26 October 2026. Check schedules on the airlines' own sites. Ahmedabad's airport, a long drive or train ride away, has many more flights.
- **By train:** the station is Bhuj (BHUJ). The Kutch Express runs overnight from Mumbai's Bandra Terminus, and since May 2026, news reports say a new Bhuj–Delhi Express runs too. The Namo Bharat Rapid Rail (Vande Metro) between Bhuj and Ahmedabad started in September 2024; check whether and when it runs on the IRCTC or NTES site.
- **Bhuj to Dhordo:** about 81 km, about 1 hour 20 minutes by road, says Incredible India. Official packages may include shared transfers from Bhuj airport and station at fixed times; otherwise, take a taxi.

## What to wear for Rann Utsav

What should I wear? The [what-to-wear helper](../tools/what-to-wear.html#kutch) gives Bhuj's day and night temperatures and an outfit list by month.

- **For the day:** light cotton clothes, a cap or hat and sunglasses. The sun on the white salt is very bright.
- **For the evening and night:** a warm jacket or a fleece, plus a shawl or scarf. Sunset, full-moon walks and outdoor shows get cold, and the wind on the Rann is chilly.
- **Shoes:** closed walking shoes or sneakers. Salt crust can be rough and damp in places, and white salt sticks to everything.
- **Sunscreen and lip balm.** The air is dry and the glare is strong.

For our picks and full lists, see [which shoes to take](../guides/travel-shoes-guide.html), the [sunscreen guide](../guides/travel-sunscreen-guide.html) and the [weekend trip packing checklist](../packing/weekend.html).

## Local tips

- **Carry some cash.** Small craft sellers and village stalls may not take cards.
- **Respect the photo rules** at Kalo Dungar and near army areas.
- **Stay on the paths** marked for visitors on the White Rann. Kutch is a border district, and some areas near the border are restricted.
- **Book early for Christmas, New Year and full-moon dates.** These are the first to fill up.

## For foreign visitors

- **Visa:** apply for the e-Tourist Visa only at the official site, [indianvisaonline.gov.in](https://indianvisaonline.gov.in/evisa/tvoa.html).
- **Carry your passport** to check posts on the way to the White Rann, and ask your tent city or hotel whether any extra permission is needed for your nationality.
- **Getting there:** most visitors fly into Mumbai, Delhi or Ahmedabad, then take a domestic flight or train to Bhuj.

## Emergency numbers

- **Emergency (police, fire, ambulance):** 112
- **Ambulance in Gujarat:** 108
- **Ministry of Tourism helpline, 24 hours, many languages:** 1363 or 1800-11-1363

## Questions

### What are the Rann Utsav 2026 dates?

Rann Utsav 2026–27 runs from 1 November 2026 to 7 March 2027 at the tent city in Dhordo, says Gujarat Tourism. The busiest dates are Christmas week (18 December to 2 January) and the full-moon nights.

### When is the full moon at Rann Utsav?

The full moon is on 24 December 2026, 22 January 2027 and 21 February 2027. The official site lists the windows as 22–24 December, 21–23 January and 19–21 February.

### How do I book Rann Utsav packages?

Only on the official site, rannutsav.net, which Gujarat Tourism links to; bookings are at booking.rannutsav.net. Check what each package includes before you pay, and be careful with similar-looking websites.

### What is the Rann Utsav ticket price?

We don't show prices. The official booking site shows the current package prices for each type of tent or suite, and prices are higher on full-moon and festive dates.

### Do I need a permit for the White Rann?

Visitors have needed one, checked at Bhirandiyara on the road to the White Rann. We couldn't find a current official page about it, so ask your tent city or hotel, and carry original photo ID.

### How cold is the Rann of Kutch in December?

Days are warm, about 29 °C on IMD's averages for Bhuj, but nights drop to about 11 °C, and it feels colder on the open salt in the wind. Carry a warm jacket for sunset and the night.

### What is the best time to visit the Rann of Kutch?

From November to February, when it's dry and the salt is white. Plan your visit around a full moon for the famous moonlit White Rann, or the night before it.

### How far is the White Rann from Bhuj?

About 81 km, or about 1 hour 20 minutes by road to Dhordo, says Incredible India.
