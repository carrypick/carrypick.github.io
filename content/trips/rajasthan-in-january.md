January is one of the best months to see Rajasthan. Days are sunny and pleasant for forts and markets, nights are cold, and the month is full of festivals: camels in Bikaner, kites over Jaipur on Makar Sankranti, and the Jaipur Literature Festival. This guide covers January's weather city by city, the festival dates, where to go, a simple one-week route, getting around in the fog season, and what to wear.

## Rajasthan weather in January

The India Meteorological Department's (IMD) long-term January averages, 1981–2010:

| City | Day | Night | Rain | Coldest night on record |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Jaipur | About 22.6 °C | About 8.6 °C | About 6 mm | −2.2 °C (31 January 1905) |
| Udaipur | About 24.6 °C | About 7.3 °C | About 2 mm | −1.1 °C (12 January 1989) |
| Jaisalmer | About 23.8 °C | About 9.1 °C | About 1 mm | −5.9 °C (12 January 1967) |
| Bikaner | About 23.4 °C | About 7.3 °C | About 6 mm | −4.0 °C (26 January 1964) |

- **Warm sun, cold shade.** Middle-of-the-day sightseeing is comfortable in a T-shirt, but early mornings and evenings need a warm jacket.
- **The desert gets coldest.** Nights in the dunes outside Jaisalmer and Bikaner are colder than in town, and can drop near freezing. Desert camps should give you heavy quilts; ask before you book.
- **It's almost dry.** On average, less than one rainy day in the whole of January, though a western disturbance can bring a cloudy, drizzly day and a cold wave after it.
- **Fog:** cold-wave mornings can bring thick fog, especially in the north and east of the state and on the way from Delhi.

## January festivals in Rajasthan

### Bikaner Camel Festival: 9 and 10 January 2027

- **When:** Rajasthan Tourism lists the **Bikaner Camel Festival 2027** on **9 and 10 January**. It's held every year on the second Saturday and Sunday of January.
- **What happens:** a procession of decorated camels, camel dances and races, camel fur-cutting art, folk music, and contests such as turban tying and the best moustache.
- **Plan:** Bikaner is about 330 km from Jaipur by road (our rough estimate) and has a railway junction. Book a stay early for the festival weekend.

### Kite Festival and Makar Sankranti in Jaipur: 14 January 2027

- **When:** Makar Sankranti, **Thursday 14 January 2027**. Rajasthan Tourism lists its **Kite Festival 2027** on that day, and Incredible India says it's held at **Jal Mahal on Amer Road**, with illuminated kites in the evening and folk music.
- **Across Jaipur,** the whole city flies kites from the rooftops from morning till dusk. Book a hotel with a rooftop if you can.
- **Watch for kite string.** Glass-coated *manjha* can cut, and is a danger to riders on two-wheelers and to birds. Wear a scarf if you ride, and look up when you cross streets.

### Jaipur Literature Festival: 14 to 18 January 2027

- **When:** news reports say the 20th edition runs from **Thursday 14 to Monday 18 January 2027** at Hotel Clarks Amer in Jaipur. The festival's own site didn't show the dates when we checked, so confirm on [jaipurliteraturefestival.org](https://jaipurliteraturefestival.org/) before you book.
- **It overlaps with Makar Sankranti,** so Jaipur is very busy that week. Book early.

### Republic Day: 26 January 2027

Republic Day is on **Tuesday 26 January 2027**, so take Monday off for a 4-day weekend. Jaipur, Udaipur and Ranthambore get busy. Government offices and some monuments may change hours on the day.

### Coming in February

The **Desert Festival in Jaisalmer** (18 to 20 February), the **Nagaur Fair** (12 to 15 February) and the **Udaipur World Music Festival** (9 to 11 February) are on Rajasthan Tourism's list for February. Check the dates there nearer the time.

## Where to go in Rajasthan in January

- **Jaipur:** Amer Fort, City Palace, Hawa Mahal and the bazaars, plus kites and the literature festival in mid-January. See our [Jaipur guide](jaipur.html).
- **Udaipur:** the City Palace, Lake Pichola boat rides and sunsets, the mildest nights after Jaipur's. See our [Udaipur guide](udaipur.html).
- **Jaisalmer:** the living fort, the havelis and a night in the Sam dunes. Pack your warmest clothes for the dunes. See [Jaisalmer in December](jaisalmer-in-december.html).
- **Bikaner:** Junagarh Fort and the Camel Festival on 9–10 January.
- **Ranthambore:** January is a good month for a tiger safari. Book only on the official site the Forest Department links to; our [Ranthambore guide](ranthambore-in-december.html) has the steps and winter safari times.
- **Jodhpur:** Mehrangarh Fort and the blue city, a good stop between Jaipur and Jaisalmer.

## A one-week Rajasthan route for January

1. **Days 1–2: Jaipur.** Amer Fort in the morning sun, the old city in the afternoon. Time it with 14 January for the kites, if you can.
2. **Day 3: to Jodhpur** (about 335 km by road), Mehrangarh at sunset.
3. **Days 4–5: Jaisalmer.** The fort and havelis, then a night at a camp in the dunes.
4. **Day 6: to Udaipur** by train or a long drive via Jodhpur, or fly back from Jaisalmer if flights run.
5. **Day 7: Udaipur.** The City Palace and a boat on Lake Pichola, then home.

For the Camel Festival, add Bikaner after Jaipur (2 days) and go on to Jaisalmer from there. Distances are rough road distances; check your route on a map.

## Getting to Rajasthan in January

- **By air:** Jaipur (JAI) has the most flights. Udaipur (UDR) and Jodhpur (JDH) have good connections; Jaisalmer and Bikaner have few.
- **By train:** Rajasthan is well connected to Delhi, Mumbai and Ahmedabad. In January, **fog in north India** delays many trains from Delhi in the early morning, and Northern Railway has already cancelled some trains for the fog season (news report). Check NTES on the day, and avoid tight connections.
- **By road:** the Delhi–Jaipur road takes about 5 to 6 hours. Start after the morning fog lifts, and use low beams, not high beams, in fog.

## What to wear in Rajasthan in January

What should I wear? Try the [what-to-wear helper](../tools/what-to-wear.html) for Jaipur, Udaipur and Jaisalmer temperatures by month.

- **By day:** light cotton layers, a cap and sunglasses. The sun is strong.
- **Morning and night:** a warm jacket or fleece, plus a shawl or scarf. For a desert camp night, add thermals, a cap and warm socks.
- **Shoes:** comfortable walking shoes for fort ramps and old-city lanes. You'll take them off at temples.
- **Temples and mosques:** cover your shoulders and knees.

## Safety and health

- **Cold waves:** IMD issues cold-wave warnings for Rajasthan in January. On those nights, stay warm and don't sit in a closed room with a coal brazier or heater.
- **Kite string** can cut on Makar Sankranti. Cover your neck if you ride a two-wheeler that day.
- **Drink bottled or filtered water,** and carry sunscreen: the dry January air and sun burn easily.
- **Only book official safaris and monument tickets,** on the official sites or at the counters, not from touts.

## For foreign visitors

- **Visa:** apply for the e-Tourist Visa only at [indianvisaonline.gov.in](https://indianvisaonline.gov.in/evisa/tvoa.html). Jaipur airport accepts e-Visa arrivals.
- **Ask before photographing people,** especially women and performers at festivals; some performers expect a tip.

## Emergency numbers

- **Emergency (police, fire, ambulance):** 112
- **Ministry of Tourism helpline, 24 hours, many languages:** 1363 or 1800-11-1363

## Questions

### Is January a good time to visit Rajasthan?

Yes, one of the best. IMD's January averages are about 22–25 °C by day and 7–9 °C at night in Jaipur, Udaipur, Jaisalmer and Bikaner, with almost no rain. Nights are cold, so pack a warm jacket.

### How cold is Rajasthan in January?

Days are pleasant, about 22–25 °C, but nights are cold, about 7–9 °C on average, and colder in the desert. Jaisalmer's record January low is −5.9 °C.

### When is the Bikaner Camel Festival 2027?

On Saturday 9 and Sunday 10 January 2027, says Rajasthan Tourism. It's always on the second weekend of January.

### When is the Kite Festival in Jaipur?

On Makar Sankranti, Thursday 14 January 2027. Rajasthan Tourism's Kite Festival is at Jal Mahal on Amer Road, and kites fly from rooftops all over Jaipur.

### When is the Jaipur Literature Festival 2027?

From 14 to 18 January 2027, say news reports, at Hotel Clarks Amer in Jaipur. Confirm on the festival's official site, which didn't show the dates when we checked.

### Is the Desert Festival in January?

No. Rajasthan Tourism lists the Jaisalmer Desert Festival for 18 to 20 February.

### What should I wear in Rajasthan in January?

Light layers and sun protection by day, and a warm jacket, shawl or fleece for mornings and evenings. Add thermals for a night in a desert camp.
