January may be Goa's best-kept secret. The weather is the same as at New Year, sunny, dry and pleasant in the evenings, but once the New Year crowds leave in the first days of the month, the beaches, roads and restaurants calm down. January also has its own festivals: the Feast of the Three Kings, with kings on horseback in Chandor, and a food festival in Margao. This guide covers the weather, each part of the month, events, where to go, getting there, and staying safe on the beach.

## Goa weather in January

The India Meteorological Department's (IMD) long-term averages for Goa, 1981–2010:

| Month | Day | Night | Rain |
|---|---|---|---|
| December | About 32.9 °C | About 20.9 °C | About 2.5 mm |
| **January** | **About 32.4 °C** | **About 20.2 °C** | **About 1 mm** |
| February | About 32.2 °C | About 20.7 °C | About 0.1 mm |

- **January has Goa's coolest nights** of the year, about 20 °C on average. The coldest January night on record was 14.4 °C, on 3 January 1991, says IMD.
- **It almost never rains.** On average, there's less than one rainy day in all of January.
- **Days are hot in the sun.** Even in January, the afternoon sun is strong, so carry sunscreen, a cap and water, and rest in the shade from about noon to 3 PM.
- **The sea is calm** and good for swimming at lifeguarded beaches. See the safety rules below.

## Goa in the first week of January

The New Year crowds go home by about 3 January, and Goa quietens quickly after that. **New Year's Day 2027 is a Friday,** so the first weekend is still busy. From about 4 January, hotels and flights are usually easier to find than in the last week of December. (We don't show prices: compare them for your dates.)

**The Feast of the Three Kings** is on **Wednesday 6 January**. Goa Tourism lists the celebrations at three churches:

- **Reis Magos,** in Bardez near Panaji, Goa's only church dedicated to the three kings (the Magi), next to the old Reis Magos fort.
- **Chandor,** in South Goa, where the three kings arrive on horseback.
- **Cansaulim,** in South Goa.

Expect mass, processions and a village fair. These are religious feasts: dress modestly, ask before you photograph people, and don't park on the procession route.

## Goa in the middle of January

The quietest part of the month, and many people's favourite. Sunny days, empty-ish beaches, and good weather for Old Goa's churches, the spice farms, Fontainhas in Panaji and the forts.

- **Makar Sankranti** is on Thursday 14 January 2027, a restricted holiday on the central government's list.
- **The Goa Food and Cultural Festival:** Goa Tourism's 2026–27 calendar lists it for **January 2027 in Margao**. The exact dates weren't fixed when we checked, and the calendar marks every date as tentative.

## Goa in the last week of January

**Republic Day** is on **Tuesday 26 January 2027**. With Monday off, that's a 4-day weekend, and Goa fills up again from Saturday 23 January. Book early if you want those dates, especially in North Goa.

**Carnival** comes in February: Goa Tourism's calendar lists it for February 2027 in Panaji, Margao, Vasco, Mapusa, Porvorim and Morjim. The dates hadn't been announced when we checked (Lent starts on 10 February 2027, and Carnival is held in the days before it).

## Where to go in Goa in January

- **North Goa** (Calangute, Baga, Anjuna, Vagator, Arambol) is livelier, with more clubs, shacks and night markets. In January it's busy but not packed, except on weekends.
- **South Goa** (Colva, Benaulim, Agonda, Palolem) is calmer, with wider, quieter beaches. Good for families and couples in the middle of January.
- **Inland:** Old Goa's UNESCO churches, Chandor's old mansions, Fontainhas in Panaji and the spice farms near Ponda are pleasant in January's dry air.
- **Turtle nesting beaches:** Olive Ridley turtles nest on **Morjim, Mandrem, Agonda and Galgibaga** in winter, and the Forest Department protects the nests (news reports put Morjim–Mandrem at 276 nests in 2024–25). Keep away from fenced nests, don't use torches or bonfires on these beaches at night, and keep dogs away.

## Getting to Goa in January

- **By air:** fly to **Mopa (GOX)** for North Goa or **Dabolim (GOI)** for Panaji and South Goa. Both have official taxi counters with fixed fares. Our [Goa guide](goa.html) compares the two.
- **By train:** the Konkan Railway stops at Thivim (North Goa), Karmali (Panaji and Old Goa), Madgaon (South Goa) and Vasco. Winter fog in north India can delay long-distance trains from Delhi in January.
- **Getting around:** use the official taxi apps and stands, or rent a scooter only from a licensed renter, wear a helmet, and carry your licence.

## Beach safety

From Goa Tourism's beach guidelines and Goa's 2022 order for tourist places:

- **Swim only where lifeguards are on duty,** between the red-and-yellow flags. Never swim where there's a red flag.
- **Don't swim after drinking,** or after dark.
- **No drinking or cooking in the open on the beach,** and no driving on the sand. Both can bring a fine.
- **Keep your valuables in your room,** and carry a copy of your ID.

## What to wear in Goa in January

Light cotton clothes, a cap and sunglasses by day, and a light shawl, jacket or long sleeves for the evening breeze. Wear swimwear only at the beach or pool, with a shirt or kaftan over it in town, and cover your shoulders and knees for church feasts and temples.

## For foreign visitors

- **Visa:** apply for the e-Tourist Visa only at [indianvisaonline.gov.in](https://indianvisaonline.gov.in/evisa/tvoa.html). Both Goa airports accept e-Visa arrivals.
- **Register with your hotel:** hotels record every foreign guest's passport and visa.

## Questions

### Is January a good time to visit Goa?

Yes, one of the best. IMD's averages are about 32 °C by day and 20 °C at night, with almost no rain, and it's calmer than late December once the New Year crowds leave.

### Is Goa crowded in January?

Only at the start and the end. It's busy until about 3 January and again on the Republic Day weekend, 23 to 26 January 2027. The middle of the month is the quietest.

### What festivals are there in Goa in January?

The Feast of the Three Kings on 6 January at Reis Magos, Chandor and Cansaulim, and the Goa Food and Cultural Festival in Margao (dates not fixed yet). Carnival comes in February.

### When is Goa Carnival 2027?

In February 2027, says Goa Tourism's calendar, but the exact dates haven't been announced. Carnival is held in the days before Lent, which starts on 10 February 2027.

### Is the sea safe for swimming in Goa in January?

It's usually calm in January, but swim only at lifeguarded beaches, between the red-and-yellow flags, and never where there's a red flag or after drinking.

### Is it cold in Goa in January?

No, just pleasant at night: about 20 °C on average. A light jacket or shawl is enough for the evenings.
