## Weekend getaways from Mumbai within 100 km

### Lonavala: the classic monsoon weekend

- **Distance:** Maharashtra Tourism says Lonavala is a two-hour drive from Mumbai, about 84 km by road, and 65 km from Pune. Local and express trains on the Mumbai–Pune line stop at Lonavala.
- **Best months:** June to September, when the hills turn green and waterfalls appear. October to February is drier and clearer.
- **What to do:** Tiger Point and Lion's Point for the views, the Karla and Bhaja caves, Bhushi Dam in the rain, and chikki from the town's sweet shops. Trekkers go up to Rajmachi fort.
- **Official info:** [Maharashtra Tourism: Lonavala](https://maharashtratourism.gov.in/nature/lonavala/).

### Matheran: a car-free hill town

- **Distance:** Maharashtra Tourism gives about 88 km by road from Mumbai, and 82 km by train from CSMT. The nearest railhead is Neral, 20 km below the town.
- **Getting up:** cars stop at a car park below Matheran, because no motor vehicles are allowed in the town. Walk, ride a horse or take a hand-pulled rickshaw. The Neral–Matheran toy train may not run in the monsoon, so check with Central Railway before you go.
- **Best months:** October to May. The monsoon is beautiful but very wet, and trails get slippery.
- **What to do:** walk to Panorama Point, Louisa Point and Charlotte Lake, and watch the sunset from Porcupine Point.
- **Official info:** [Maharashtra Tourism: Matheran](https://maharashtratourism.gov.in/nature/matheran/).

### Alibaug: beaches by ferry or road

- **Distance:** about 90 km by road, about 2½ to 3½ hours (our estimates). The faster way is a ferry from the Gateway of India to Mandwa jetty, then a bus or taxi to Alibaug. Ferries usually stop in the monsoon, so check before you go.
- **Best months:** October to March.
- **What to do:** Alibaug, Kihim, Nagaon and Varsoli beaches, and Kolaba Fort, which you can walk to at low tide. Check the tide times first.
- **Official info:** [Maharashtra Tourism: Raigad district](https://maharashtratourism.gov.in/districts/raigad/).

## Weekend getaways from Mumbai in the monsoon

The Western Ghats are at their greenest from June to September, and that's when weekend crowds are biggest.

### Igatpuri: waterfalls and quiet hills

- **Distance:** Maharashtra Tourism gives about 130 km by road from Mumbai, and 121 km by train from CSMT. Nashik is about 57 km further on.
- **Best months:** June to September for waterfalls and mist, October to February for clear, cool days.
- **What to do:** the Bhatsa river valley, waterfalls and viewpoints on the ghat, and the old forts nearby for trekkers. Igatpuri is also home to a large Vipassana meditation centre, which runs its own courses.
- **Official info:** [Maharashtra Tourism: Igatpuri](https://maharashtratourism.gov.in/nature/igatpuri/).

**Monsoon safety:** ghat roads get foggy and slippery, and waterfalls and dams can flood fast. Local police often close risky spots after heavy rain, so follow the signs, don't go into the water, and drive in daylight.

## Weekend getaways from Mumbai for couples and families

### Mahabaleshwar: viewpoints and strawberries

- **Distance:** about 220–270 km by road, depending on the route, about 5 to 6 hours (our estimates). There's no railway: Maharashtra Tourism says the nearest station is Satara, 61 km away, and Pune is 121 km by road.
- **Best months:** October to May. Winter is strawberry season. The monsoon brings very heavy rain, and many viewpoints are lost in cloud.
- **What to do:** Arthur's Seat and other viewpoints, boating on Venna Lake, nearby Panchgani, and Pratapgad Fort, about 24 km away (Maharashtra Tourism).
- **Official info:** [Maharashtra Tourism: Mahabaleshwar](https://maharashtratourism.gov.in/nature/mahabaleshwar/).

### Nashik: vineyards and temples

- **Distance:** about 180 km by road, about 3½ to 4½ hours (our estimates). Trains run to Nashik Road station.
- **Best months:** October to March. The grape harvest runs through the winter months, when many wineries are busiest.
- **What to do:** a vineyard tour and tasting (for adults, and only where it's allowed), Panchavati and the Ramkund ghats, and Trimbakeshwar temple, one of the 12 jyotirlingas.
- **Official info:** [Maharashtra Tourism: Nashik](https://maharashtratourism.gov.in/districts/nashik/).

### Daman: beaches and old forts

- **Distance:** about 190 km by road, about 4 to 5 hours (our estimates). The nearest big station is Vapi, a short taxi ride away.
- **Best months:** October to March.
- **What to do:** Devka and Jampore beaches, the old Portuguese forts of Moti Daman and Nani Daman, and the cathedral inside Moti Daman fort.
- **Official info:** [Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu administration](https://ddd.gov.in/).

### Ajanta and Ellora: cave temples

- **Distance:** about 340 km by road to Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar (Aurangabad), about 6 to 7 hours (our estimates), or a train. Maharashtra Tourism says Ellora is about 29 km from the city's railway station. Ajanta is further, about a 2½ hour drive (our estimate), so see one per day.
- **Best months:** July to March: green after the rains, and cool in winter. Summer is very hot.
- **Closed days:** check before you go. The Ellora caves usually close on Tuesdays and Ajanta on Mondays.
- **What to do:** Ellora's Kailasa temple, carved from a single rock, the Buddhist paintings at Ajanta, and Daulatabad Fort on the way.
- **Official info:** [Maharashtra Tourism: Ellora](https://maharashtratourism.gov.in/cave/ellora/) and [Ajanta](https://maharashtratourism.gov.in/cave/ajanta/).

## Longer weekends: Goa, Udaipur and Jaipur

### Goa: beaches, for 3 days or more

Our guide says Goa is about 594 km from Mumbai by road. The fastest trains take about 8–9 hours, so an overnight train or a short flight is the best way to make a 3-day weekend work. November to February is the best time. Read our full [Goa guide](goa.html), and see our [Goa New Year 2027 guide](goa-new-year-2027.html) if you're going for New Year's Eve.

### Udaipur: lakes and palaces

Take a direct flight, or an overnight train, and you can see Lake Pichola, the City Palace and Jag Mandir in two days. Read our full [Udaipur guide](udaipur.html).

### Jaipur: forts, palaces and markets

Jaipur is too far to drive for a weekend, but direct flights make it a good 3-day trip: Amber Fort, City Palace, Hawa Mahal and the bazaars. Read our full [Jaipur guide](jaipur.html).

## Weekend getaways from Mumbai in December

- **For the beach:** Goa, Alibaug or Daman. All three are warm and dry in December.
- **For cool hills:** Mahabaleshwar and Matheran have cool mornings and clear views.
- **For heritage:** Ajanta and Ellora, Udaipur or Jaipur, with sunny days and cold nights up north.
- **Christmas and New Year** are the busiest dates for Goa, Lonavala and Alibaug. Book early.

For more winter ideas, see [where to go in December and January](where-to-go-in-december-january.html) and [New Year trips 2027](new-year-trips-2027.html).

## Questions

### What are the best weekend getaways from Mumbai?

For two days, Lonavala, Matheran and Alibaug are the easiest, each about 2 to 3 hours away. Igatpuri, Nashik and Mahabaleshwar work well for a relaxed 2-day trip. For 3 days or more, take an overnight train or a short flight to Goa.

### Which weekend getaways from Mumbai are best in the monsoon?

Lonavala and Igatpuri, for green hills and waterfalls, and Matheran if you don't mind heavy rain. Check that ferries and the Matheran toy train are running, drive the ghats in daylight, and keep away from fast water.

### Which weekend getaways from Mumbai are good for couples?

Mahabaleshwar for viewpoints and strawberries, Alibaug for a quiet beach, and Goa or Udaipur for a longer weekend.

### Which places near Mumbai can I visit for 2 days by train?

Lonavala and Igatpuri are on main lines from Mumbai. Matheran is a train to Neral, then the toy train or a taxi. Nashik Road and Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar (for Ellora) have direct trains. Check times on IRCTC.

### Where can I go from Mumbai for a weekend in December?

Goa, Alibaug or Daman for the beach, Mahabaleshwar or Matheran for cool hills, and Ellora or Udaipur for heritage. December is dry everywhere on this list.
