Mussoorie in December is cold, sunny and clear, with long views of the snow peaks and the famous "Winterline" at sunset. Snow is possible but not certain. This guide covers the weather, the chances of snow, a simple 2-day and 3-day plan, getting there from Delhi and Dehradun, New Year traffic, and what to wear.

## Mussoorie weather in December and January

IMD's long-term averages for Mussoorie (1971–2000):

| Month | Day | Night | Rain |
|---|---|---|---|
| December | About 13 °C | About 5 °C | About 10 mm |
| January | About 10 °C | About 3 °C | About 50 mm |
| February | About 11 °C | About 3 °C | About 65 mm |

- **December is the driest winter month.** It rains on about one day in an average December, so skies are often clear.
- **Nights are cold.** The lowest reading on record is −3.9 °C in December and −5 °C in January.
- **Wind makes it feel colder** on the ridge, at Gun Hill and Lal Tibba.

## Does it snow in Mussoorie in December?

- **Possible, not certain.** Uttarakhand Tourism invites visitors to come in winter "to witness the snowfall", but gives no dates.
- **Recent first snows came in January.** News reports said the first snow of winter 2025–26 in Mussoorie and Dhanaulti fell on 23 January 2026, with none in December.
- **Higher spots are your best chance:** Landour and Lal Tibba at the top of town, and Dhanaulti, about 24 km away.
- **Want surer snow?** Go to [Auli](auli-in-december.html) from January, or [Manali](manali.html) and [Gulmarg](gulmarg-in-december.html).

## The Winterline

- **What it is:** at sunset, a grey or mauve band appears on the western horizon, like a false horizon. Uttarakhand Tourism says it's seen from only a few places in the world.
- **When:** from mid-October to mid-January, so December is a good time.
- **Where to watch:** Camel's Back Road and the Mall Road side facing west, on a clear evening.

## Mussoorie trip plan: 2 days

**Day 1: the Mall and Gun Hill**

1. **Mall Road,** from Library Chowk to Kulri Bazaar, for shops, cafés and the view down to the Doon valley.
2. **Gun Hill by cable car,** from the Mall. The district site says it's Mussoorie's second-highest peak, at 2,122 m. A gun here once fired at noon.
3. **Christ Church,** built in 1836, one of the oldest churches in the Himalaya. It's lovely around Christmas.
4. **Camel's Back Road at sunset,** for the Winterline on a clear evening.

**Day 2: Landour and George Everest's house**

1. **Landour,** at the east end of town, an old British cantonment with quiet lanes and Landour Bazaar.
2. **Lal Tibba,** Mussoorie's highest point (2,438 m, says Uttarakhand Tourism), at sunrise. On clear days you can see Badrinath, Kedarnath, Bandarpoonch and Nanda Devi.
3. **George Everest's house (Park Estate),** about 6 km from Gandhi Chowk. It was the home of Sir George Everest, Surveyor General of India from 1830 to 1843. It has been restored, and Incredible India lists it as a museum.
4. **Company Garden** in the afternoon.

## Mussoorie trip plan: 3 days

Add one of these:

- **Dhanaulti and Surkanda Devi.** Dhanaulti is about 24 km away, with valley and snow-peak views. Surkanda Devi temple, a Shakti Peeth at 3,030 m, is a 2 km climb from Kaddukhal. (A ropeway to the temple opened in 2022, news reports said; check if it's running.)
- **Kempty Falls,** about 15 km away on the Yamunotri road. Winter flow is lower, and the water is very cold.

## Getting to Mussoorie

From the Dehradun district website:

- **By train:** the nearest station is Dehradun, about 36 km away. Trains from Delhi include the Shatabdi Express, Jan Shatabdi Express, Doon Express and Dehradun AC Express. A Vande Bharat also runs from Delhi's Anand Vihar to Dehradun. Check times on [NTES](https://enquiry.indianrail.gov.in/mntes/) or IRCTC.
- **By air:** the nearest airport is Jolly Grant, about 20 km from Dehradun city, with direct flights from Delhi and Mumbai.
- **By road:** Mussoorie is 38 km from Dehradun. The district site says the drive from Delhi to Dehradun takes about 4 hours.
- **By bus:** buses for Mussoorie leave from the Mussoorie bus station at Dehradun railway station.

## Mussoorie in December end: Christmas and New Year

- **Book early.** Mussoorie is one of the most popular New Year trips from Delhi.
- **This year Christmas is on a Friday,** and so is New Year's Day (1 January 2027), so both are long weekends.
- **The Winterline Carnival:** Mussoorie holds a winter carnival at the end of December. The 2025 edition ran from 24 to 29 December (news reports). The 2026 dates had not been announced when we checked.
- **Expect diversions.** On busy days, news reports say Dehradun Police send traffic via Kimadi and Hathipaon, with parking at King Craig and taxis or shuttles into town. (Not an official notice: check Dehradun Police updates before you drive.)
- **Carry your hotel booking** on your phone.

## Things to do in winter

- **The Winterline** at sunset, from mid-October to mid-January.
- **Gun Hill** by cable car, and the walk along **Mall Road.**
- **Landour** and **Lal Tibba** for sunrise views of the snow peaks.
- **George Everest's house** at Park Estate.
- **Christ Church** around Christmas.
- **Dhanaulti and Surkanda Devi** for a day trip.

## What to wear in Mussoorie in December

What should I wear? Try the [what-to-wear helper](../tools/what-to-wear.html#mussoorie) for day and night temperatures and an outfit list by month.

Sunny afternoons are mild, but mornings, evenings and the ridge are cold and windy.

- **Layers:** a thermal top, a sweater or fleece, and a warm, windproof jacket.
- **Extras:** a woollen cap, gloves and warm socks for sunrise at Lal Tibba and the Winterline in the evening.
- **For ladies:** jeans or warm leggings with a kurta or top, a cardigan, and a long jacket or shawl.
- **Shoes:** comfortable walking shoes with a good grip. Mussoorie is all steep lanes. If snow is forecast, take waterproof boots.
- **Sunscreen, sunglasses and lip balm** for the dry winter air.

## Health and safety

- **Never sleep with a heater or coal fire in a closed room.** It gives off carbon monoxide, which can kill without warning.
- **Watch your step** on steep lanes on icy mornings.
- **Don't drive the hill road after dark** if you can avoid it, and use low gears going down.

## Questions

### Does it snow in Mussoorie in December?

Sometimes, but not every year. Recent first snows came in January: in winter 2025–26, news reports said the first snow fell on 23 January 2026. Landour, Lal Tibba and Dhanaulti get snow more often than the Mall.

### What is the temperature in Mussoorie in December?

IMD's averages are about 13 °C by day and 5 °C at night. The coldest December night on record was −3.9 °C. January is colder.

### What is the Winterline in Mussoorie?

A grey or mauve band that appears on the western horizon at sunset, like a false horizon. Uttarakhand Tourism says it's visible from mid-October to mid-January. Watch from Camel's Back Road on a clear evening.

### Is Mussoorie good in December end?

Yes, for clear skies, the Winterline and the end-of-year carnival. It's also very busy at Christmas and New Year, so book early and expect traffic diversions.

### How many days are enough for Mussoorie?

Two days for the Mall, Gun Hill, Landour and George Everest's house. Add a third for Dhanaulti and Surkanda Devi.

### How do I go from Delhi to Mussoorie?

Take a train to Dehradun (Shatabdi, Jan Shatabdi, Vande Bharat and others) or fly to Jolly Grant. Then it's 38 km by road from Dehradun. The district site says the drive from Delhi to Dehradun takes about 4 hours.

### Mussoorie or Nainital in December?

Both are similar in height and cold. Mussoorie has the Winterline, Landour and an easy link to Dehradun and Rishikesh. Nainital has the lake and boating. See [Nainital in December](nainital-in-december.html).

### What should I wear in Mussoorie in December?

A thermal, a sweater and a warm, windproof jacket, plus a woollen cap and gloves for the evenings. Wear walking shoes with a good grip.
