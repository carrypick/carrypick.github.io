**Short answer:** go between November and February, fly into Kochi, and follow the classic loop: Kochi, then the tea hills of Munnar, then a houseboat night on the backwaters at Alappuzha (Alleppey). With 5 days you can see all three. With 7, end at a cliff or beach in Varkala or Kovalam and fly home from Thiruvananthapuram. The tables below give each day, the travel time between stops, and what to book ahead.

## Kerala itinerary for 5 days

The most popular plan. It uses Kochi airport at both ends, so no long drive back at the end.

| Day | Where you sleep | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Kochi | Land at COK. Fort Kochi in the evening: the Chinese fishing nets at sunset and St Francis Church |
| 2 | Munnar | Leave early: about 3–4.5 hours up hill roads. Tea estates and the Tea Museum in the afternoon |
| 3 | Munnar | Eravikulam National Park in the morning (book online, closed 1 Feb to 1 Apr). Mattupetty dam and lake after lunch |
| 4 | Alappuzha, on a houseboat | Drive down from the hills. Board a houseboat around midday. It cruises the backwaters, then moors for the night |
| 5 | Home | Leave the boat in the morning, then about 55 km back to Kochi airport. Mattancherry Palace or Jew Town on the way, if your flight is late |

## Kerala itinerary for 4 days

Four days isn't enough for both the hills and the backwaters without rushing. Pick one:

| Day | Hills plan | Backwaters plan |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Kochi: Fort Kochi and the fishing nets | Kochi: Fort Kochi and the fishing nets |
| 2 | Drive to Munnar (3–4.5 hours), tea estates | Mattancherry and Jew Town in the morning, then Alappuzha (about 55 km) |
| 3 | Eravikulam and Mattupetty | Houseboat or shikara day on the backwaters, night on the boat |
| 4 | Back to Kochi airport | Back to Kochi airport |

A shikara is a small boat that goes into narrow canals where houseboats can't. It's a shorter way to see the backwaters if you don't want a night on a boat.

## Kerala itinerary for 7 days

| Day | Where you sleep | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | Kochi | Fort Kochi, Mattancherry Palace (closed Fridays), Paradesi Synagogue (go Sunday to Thursday), a Water Metro boat ride |
| 3–4 | Munnar | Tea estates, Eravikulam, Mattupetty, and Top Station (about 40 km away) for views over Tamil Nadu |
| 5 | Alappuzha, on a houseboat | Backwaters and Vembanad Lake |
| 6 | Varkala | Train from Alappuzha to Varkala Sivagiri station, then about 3–4 km to the cliff |
| 7 | Home | About 45–50 km to Thiruvananthapuram airport (TRV) |

Prefer a calmer beach? Stay at Kovalam instead of Varkala: it's about 14 km from TRV. Book an open-jaw ticket (into Kochi, out of Thiruvananthapuram) so you don't drive back north.

## Travel times between stops

| From | To | Distance and time |
|---|---|---|
| Kochi airport (COK) | Ernakulam Junction station | About 28 km |
| Kochi | Munnar | About 110–130 km, roughly 3–4.5 hours on winding hill roads. Munnar has no railway |
| Kochi | Alappuzha | About 55 km |
| Alappuzha | Varkala | By train to Varkala Sivagiri (see the [train luggage rules](../baggage/train-luggage-rules.html)), or by road |
| Varkala | Thiruvananthapuram airport (TRV) | About 45–50 km |
| Kovalam | Thiruvananthapuram airport (TRV) | About 14 km |

We haven't found an official figure for the Munnar–Alappuzha drive, so check a maps app on the day: it's a long descent from the hills, so allow most of a morning. KSRTC buses link most towns: book long trips at onlineksrtcswift.com, linked from keralartc.com. In Kochi, the Metro runs from Aluva to Thrippunithura, and Water Metro boats link Fort Kochi, Mattancherry, Vypin and the High Court jetty.

## Best time to visit Kerala

| Season | Months | What to expect |
|---|---|---|
| Peak season | November to February | Dry, sunny coast and calm backwaters. Cool hills, with cold nights in Munnar in December and January. Busiest and most expensive at Christmas and New Year |
| Hot season | March to May | Hot and humid on the coast. Eravikulam is closed until 1 April for the Nilgiri tahr calving season |
| Southwest monsoon | June to September | The monsoon usually reaches the Kerala coast around 1 June (IMD's normal date). Heavy rain, green hills and fewer visitors, but rough seas: stay out of the water |
| After the monsoon | October | Showers taper off. Kerala Tourism says houseboat cruises are best from October to February |

### Weather in December and January

| Place | December | January |
|---|---|---|
| Kochi | 22–33 °C, a few showers | 21–34 °C, almost dry |
| Alappuzha | 23–33 °C, a few showers | 23–33 °C, mostly dry |
| Thiruvananthapuram (Kovalam, Varkala) | 23–32 °C, some showers | 22–32 °C, mostly dry |
| Munnar | Cold nights, mild days | Nights about 2–9 °C, days about 23 °C |

Coastal figures are IMD long-term averages (1991–2020). Munnar's come from tea research station records for January 2026; on some winter nights the hills touch 0 °C, and frost forms on the grass. Pack cotton for the coast and a warm jacket for Munnar. More on the hills: [Munnar in December](munnar-in-december.html).

## Kochi and Fort Kochi

- **Chinese fishing nets:** giant nets on teak and bamboo poles. Watch them from Vasco da Gama Square at sunset.
- **St Francis Church:** first built in 1503. Vasco da Gama was buried here in 1524 before his body went back to Portugal. Open Monday to Friday 9 AM to 5 PM, Saturday 9 AM to 1 PM and Sunday 1 to 5 PM.
- **Mattancherry Palace (Dutch Palace):** built around 1545, with famous murals. Open 10 AM to 5 PM, closed on Fridays.
- **Paradesi Synagogue, Jew Town:** built in 1568. Closed on Saturdays and Jewish holidays, and Kerala Tourism's pages disagree about Fridays, so go Sunday to Thursday. Enter barefoot.
- **Kochi-Muziris Biennale:** the art festival isn't on this winter. Its official site lists the seventh edition for 2027–28.

## Munnar

- **The hills:** Munnar town is about 1,600 m up, surrounded by tea estates.
- **Eravikulam National Park:** about 13 km from town, home to the Nilgiri tahr (a mountain goat) and South India's highest peak, Anamudi. Visitors can enter only the Rajamala tourism zone. Book on [eravikulamnationalpark.in](https://www.eravikulamnationalpark.in/). When we checked on 3 Nov 2026, it listed entry from 8 AM to 2 PM, ₹200 for Indians and ₹500 for foreign visitors. The park is closed from 1 February to 1 April every year. No plastic, food, alcohol, smoking or loud music inside.
- **Tea Museum:** about 2 km from town, at the Nallathanni estate. Its operator says it's closed on Mondays.
- **Mattupetty:** a dam and lake with boating, a 13 km drive away.
- **Holiday traffic:** roads between Pallivasal and Mattupetty have jammed in past New Year rushes, so start early.

## Alappuzha (Alleppey) backwaters

- **Houseboats:** converted rice barges (kettuvallams) that cruise the backwaters and Vembanad Lake. Day cruises usually run from late morning to about 5 PM. Overnight trips include meals, and boats moor for the night.
- **Book a safe boat.** Kerala Tourism points visitors to the District Tourism (DTPC) prepaid houseboat counter in Alappuzha (+91 477 2251796). Under the state's classification scheme, approved boats are graded Silver, Gold or Diamond (Diamond has the most facilities) and must show their KIV registration number, name and certificate.
- **Check the safety kit:** life jackets for everyone, lifebuoys and fire extinguishers. Wear a life jacket on small boats.

## Beaches: Varkala and Kovalam

- **Varkala:** red laterite cliffs right above the sea. Kerala Tourism warns the waves can be strong.
- **Kovalam:** Lighthouse Beach has calmer water. Swim only in the flagged areas near lifeguards. Undercurrents can appear suddenly, even near the shore, and tourists have drowned at both beaches.
- **Rough-sea alerts:** stay out of the water when the weather office or INCOIS issues a high-wave warning, and during the monsoon.

## Christmas and New Year in Kerala

- **Cochin Carnival, Fort Kochi:** Kerala Tourism says it starts on the second Sunday of December, which is 13 December 2026, and runs through the last two weeks of the month. At midnight on 31 December a giant effigy of an old man, the Pappanji, is burnt on the beach, and a carnival rally follows on 1 January. The organisers haven't published the 2026 programme yet.
- **Huge crowds:** police expected 2–3 lakh people in Fort Kochi last New Year's Eve. Vehicles were stopped from the afternoon, the Vypin–Fort Kochi Ro-Ro ferry stopped at 4 PM, and Water Metro and ferry boats ran only until 7 PM. Reach early, or stay in Fort Kochi itself. The Kochi Metro ran until about 1:30 to 2 AM.
- **Dry day on 1 January:** in Kerala the first day of every month is a dry day, so liquor shops and bars stay closed on 1 January 2027, according to news reports.
- **Book early:** Christmas to New Year is the busiest time for houseboats and hotels.

## Rules and tips

- **Plastic:** Kerala has banned single-use plastic and plastic carry bags since 2020. Carry a cloth bag and a refillable bottle.
- **Alcohol:** sold only at government BEVCO shops and licensed bars, and not on dry days.
- **Temples:** Sree Padmanabhaswamy Temple in Thiruvananthapuram allows only Hindus inside. Men must wear a mundu or dhoti with no shirt, and women a sari or similar; dhotis can be rented at the gate. Guruvayur Temple has a similar dress code.
- **Drones:** follow India's drone rules and check the Digital Sky map. Don't fly over festival crowds.
- **Flying home with banana chips or halwa?** See [can you carry sweets and home food on a flight](../baggage/can-i-carry/sweets-and-home-food.html).

## For foreign visitors

- **Visa:** apply for the e-Tourist Visa only at [indianvisaonline.gov.in](https://indianvisaonline.gov.in/evisa/tvoa.html), at least 4 days before you travel. Kochi, Thiruvananthapuram, Calicut and Kannur airports and Kochi seaport accept e-Visa arrivals. More in our [visiting India guide](visiting-india.html).
- **Eravikulam tickets:** there's a separate ticket for foreign visitors, and one payment option works only with foreign-issued cards.
- **Help:** the International Tourism Police Station in Jew Town, Mattancherry (+91 9497932659) helps with passports, visas and complaints. India doesn't allow most drone imports, so a visitor's drone may be held at customs.

## Emergency numbers

- **Emergency:** 112 (connected to the police in Kerala)
- **Police:** 100. **Fire:** 101. **Ambulance:** 108.
- **Kerala Tourism:** 1-800-425-4747 (free, within India)

## Questions

### What is the best time to visit Kerala?

November to February. The monsoon is over, the backwaters are calm, the beaches are sunny and Munnar is cool. Kerala Tourism says houseboat cruises are best from October to February. Christmas and New Year are the busiest weeks.

### How many days are enough for a Kerala trip?

Five days covers the classic route: Kochi, Munnar and a houseboat night in Alappuzha. With 4 days, choose either Munnar or the backwaters. With 7 days, add Varkala or Kovalam and fly home from Thiruvananthapuram.

### What is a good 5-day Kerala itinerary?

Day 1 Kochi, days 2 and 3 Munnar (tea estates, Eravikulam, Mattupetty), day 4 a houseboat from Alappuzha with a night on board, and day 5 back to Kochi airport, about 55 km away.

### What is the best time to visit the Kerala backwaters?

October to February, according to Kerala Tourism. The water is calm and the weather dry. In the monsoon (June to September) boats still run, but expect heavy rain.

### Is it a good idea to visit Kerala in the monsoon?

It can be, if you want green hills, fewer crowds and don't mind rain. The monsoon usually reaches Kerala around 1 June. Hill roads can be slow, and you shouldn't swim in the sea during high-wave warnings.

### How much does a Kerala trip cost?

We don't publish a cost, because it depends on your dates, hotels and houseboat. Some fixed costs: Eravikulam entry was listed at ₹200 for Indians and ₹500 for foreign visitors when we checked in November 2026. Add your own flights, stays and food in our [trip budget calculator](../tools/trip-budget-calculator.html).

### Which airport is best for a Kerala trip?

Kochi (COK) for Kochi, Munnar and Alappuzha. Thiruvananthapuram (TRV) for Kovalam and Varkala. Fly into one and out of the other on a 7-day trip.

### How cold is Munnar in December and January?

Cold at night. In January 2026, most nights were between 2 and 9 °C, and some winters touch 0 °C. Days are mild, around 23 °C.

### Is Eravikulam National Park open in December?

Yes. It closes from 1 February to 1 April for the Nilgiri tahr calving season. Book tickets on the official site.

### How do I choose a safe houseboat in Alappuzha?

Book through the DTPC prepaid counter in Alappuzha, or check that the boat shows its KIV registration number and classification certificate (Silver, Gold or Diamond). Make sure there are life jackets for everyone on board.

### What is the Pappanji burning in Fort Kochi?

At midnight on 31 December, a huge effigy of an old man called the Pappanji is burnt on the beach in Fort Kochi to say goodbye to the old year. It's the highlight of the Cochin Carnival. Lakhs of people come, so reach early.

### Is 1 January a dry day in Kerala?

Yes, according to news reports. The first day of every month is a dry day in Kerala, so liquor shops and bars are closed on 1 January.
