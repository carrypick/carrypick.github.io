## Weekend getaways from Bangalore within 100 and 200 km

### Nandi Hills: a sunrise above the clouds

- **Distance:** Karnataka Tourism says Chikkaballapur district, where Nandi Hills is, is around 60 km from Bengaluru and a favourite weekend getaway for the city. Allow about 1½ to 2 hours by road (our estimate).
- **Best months:** September to February, when early mornings are often misty. On weekends the hill road is very busy, so go early on a weekday if you can.
- **What to do:** watch the sunrise from the viewpoints, walk to Tipu's Drop, and visit the Bhoga Nandeeshwara temple at the foot of the hill.
- **Official info:** [Karnataka Tourism: Chikkaballapur](https://karnatakatourism.org/en/destinations/chikkaballapur/).

### Mysuru: the palace and Chamundi Hills

- **Distance:** Karnataka Tourism says Mysuru is about 140 km southwest of Bengaluru. By road it's about 2½ to 3 hours on the Bengaluru–Mysuru Expressway (our estimate), and frequent trains include Shatabdi and Vande Bharat services.
- **Best months:** October to March. Mysuru Dasara, in October, is the city's biggest festival, with very big crowds.
- **What to do:** Mysore Palace (lit up on some evenings: check the palace's timings), Chamundi Hills, Devaraja Market, and Srirangapatna and Brindavan Gardens on the way.
- **Official info:** [Karnataka Tourism: Mysuru](https://karnatakatourism.org/en/destinations/mysuru/).

## Weekend getaways from Bangalore within 300 km

### Coorg: coffee estates and homestays

The Kodagu district says Coorg is about 257 km from Bangalore by road, via Mysuru and Kushalnagar. There's no railway: take a train to Mysuru and go on by road, or a KSRTC bus. Stay on a coffee estate, see Abbey Falls and Raja's Seat, and stop at Bylakuppe's Golden Temple on the way. Read our full [Coorg guide](coorg-in-december.html).

### Chikkamagaluru: coffee hills and treks

- **Distance:** about 250 km by road, about 5 to 6 hours (our estimates). Most people drive or take a KSRTC bus.
- **Best months:** Karnataka Tourism gives September to February, though it says the weather is pleasant all year. The monsoon brings heavy rain and leeches on the trails.
- **What to do:** drive up Mullayanagiri, Karnataka's highest peak, visit Baba Budangiri and Kemmanagundi, and stay on a coffee estate. Belur and Halebeedu temples are on the way.
- **Official info:** [Karnataka Tourism: Chikkamagaluru](https://karnatakatourism.org/en/destinations/chikkamagalur/).

### Wayanad: forests, caves and waterfalls

- **Distance:** about 280 km by road via Mysuru, about 6 to 7 hours (our estimates). There's no railway in Wayanad.
- **Forest road at night:** the road through Bandipur is shut to traffic at night, from 9 PM to 6 AM, so leave Bangalore early and cross in daylight.
- **Best months:** October to May. The monsoon, from June to September, is very wet.
- **What to do:** the Edakkal Caves and their carvings, Banasura Sagar dam, the trek to Chembra Peak, and a jeep safari at Muthanga.
- **Official info:** [Kerala Tourism: Wayanad](https://www.keralatourism.org/wayanad).

### Ooty: tea hills and the toy train

Our guide says Ooty is about 290 km from Bangalore, via Mysore and the Bandipur and Mudumalai forests, which are shut to traffic from 9 PM to 6 AM. Cars from outside the Nilgiris need an e-pass. No train runs from Bangalore to Ooty, but you can take a train to Coimbatore or Mettupalayam for the toy train. Read our full [Ooty guide](ooty-in-december.html).

## Beaches and heritage for a 3-day weekend

### Puducherry: the French Quarter and Auroville

- **Distance:** about 310–330 km by road, about 6 to 7 hours (our estimates). Few direct trains run, so most people drive or take a bus.
- **Best months:** December to March. October and November bring the northeast monsoon rains to this coast.
- **What to do:** walk the French Quarter and the Promenade at sunrise, visit Auroville and the Matrimandir viewing point, and eat at the cafés.
- **Official info:** [Government of Puducherry](https://py.gov.in/).

### Hampi: the ruins of Vijayanagara

- **Distance:** Karnataka Tourism says Hampi is about 340 km from Bengaluru, and the nearest station is Hosapete Junction, about 13 km away. Overnight trains to Hosapete make it a good 2-day trip.
- **Best months:** October to February. Summer days are very hot among the rocks.
- **What to do:** the Virupaksha temple, the stone chariot at the Vittala temple, the Royal Enclosure, and sunset from Hemakuta or Matanga Hill.
- **Official info:** [Karnataka Tourism: Hampi](https://karnatakatourism.org/en/destinations/hampi/).

### Gokarna: quiet beaches and a temple town

- **Distance:** Karnataka Tourism says Gokarna is about 485 km from Bangalore, about 9–10 hours by road or 10–12 hours by bus. The nearest station, Gokarna Road, is 10 km from town. Take a long weekend.
- **Best months:** October to March (Karnataka Tourism).
- **What to do:** Om, Kudle and Half Moon beaches, the Mahabaleshwar temple in town, and Mirjan Fort, about 22 km north.
- **Official info:** [Karnataka Tourism: Gokarna](https://karnatakatourism.org/en/destinations/gokarna/).

## Longer weekends: Kerala and Goa

### Munnar and Kerala, by flight or overnight train

Munnar is too far to drive for two days, but fly to Kochi and our guide says Munnar is about 108 km away, roughly 3 to 4 hours up the hills. Or take an overnight train to Ernakulam. Add a night on the backwaters at Alappuzha, about 55 km from Kochi. Read our [Munnar guide](munnar-in-december.html) and our full [Kerala guide](kerala.html).

### Goa: beaches, for 3 days or more

Our guide says Goa is about 632 km from Bengaluru by road. A short direct flight, or an overnight train, makes a 3-day weekend easy. November to February is the best time. Read our full [Goa guide](goa.html).

## Weekend trips from Bangalore in December

- **For cold, frosty mornings:** Ooty and Munnar. Both can see frost, but not snow. Pack a warm jacket.
- **For cool, dry days:** Coorg and Chikkamagaluru, with misty mornings on the coffee estates.
- **For sightseeing in the sun:** Hampi and Mysuru are at their best.
- **For the beach:** Goa or Gokarna. Puducherry can still get some rain in early December.
- **Christmas and New Year** are the busiest dates everywhere. Ooty may limit cars on busy days, so apply for the e-pass early.

For more winter ideas, see [where to go in December and January](where-to-go-in-december-january.html) and [New Year trips 2027](new-year-trips-2027.html).

## Questions

### What are the best weekend getaways from Bangalore?

For two days, Mysuru, Coorg and Chikkamagaluru are the easiest by road. Nandi Hills is a sunrise day trip. With an overnight train, Hampi works for a weekend. For 3 days or more, fly to Kochi for Munnar and Kerala, or to Goa.

### Which weekend getaways from Bangalore are within 200 km?

Nandi Hills (about 60 km) and Mysuru (about 140 km), by Karnataka Tourism's figures. Srirangapatna is on the way to Mysuru.

### Which weekend getaways from Bangalore are within 300 km?

Coorg (about 257 km), Chikkamagaluru (about 250 km, our estimate), Wayanad (about 280 km, our estimate) and Ooty (about 290 km).

### Can I drive to Ooty or Wayanad at night from Bangalore?

No. The forest roads through Bandipur and Mudumalai are shut to traffic from 9 PM to 6 AM. Leave Bangalore early in the morning so you cross the forests in daylight.

### Where can I go from Bangalore for a weekend in December?

Ooty or Munnar for cold, frosty mornings, Coorg for cool, dry days, Hampi or Mysuru for sightseeing, and Goa or Gokarna for the beach.

### Is a 2-day trip from Bangalore by car or by bus better?

By car, you can stop on the way and split the fuel with friends: work it out in our [road trip fuel cost calculator](../tools/road-trip-cost-calculator.html). KSRTC buses, including overnight services, run to Mysuru, Coorg, Chikkamagaluru, Ooty and Hampi, so you skip the driving.
