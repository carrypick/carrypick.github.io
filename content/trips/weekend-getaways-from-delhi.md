## Weekend getaways from Delhi within 200 km

Only two of our picks are within about 200 km of central Delhi by road, and both are easy for one night, or even a long day.

### Mathura and Vrindavan: temples and Holi

- **Distance:** about 150 km by road on the Yamuna Expressway, about 2½ to 3½ hours (our estimates). Trains run to Mathura Junction, and Vrindavan is a short ride from there.
- **Best months:** October to March. Holi week, in March, is the busiest time of the year, with famous celebrations at the temples of Barsana, Nandgaon and Vrindavan. Expect very big crowds.
- **What to do:** visit Shri Krishna Janmabhoomi in Mathura, the Banke Bihari and ISKCON temples in Vrindavan, and an evening aarti at Vishram Ghat on the Yamuna. Dress modestly, and keep your phone and bag safe from monkeys in Vrindavan.
- **Official info:** [Mathura district website](https://mathura.nic.in/) and [UP Tourism](https://www.uptourism.gov.in/).

### Agra: the Taj Mahal and the fort

- **Distance:** about 210 km by road, about 3 to 4 hours on the Yamuna Expressway (our estimates). The fastest trains to Agra Cantt take under 2 hours; check times on IRCTC.
- **Best months:** October to March, when the days are mild. Summer afternoons are very hot.
- **What to do:** the Taj Mahal at sunrise, Agra Fort, and Mehtab Bagh across the river for a sunset view of the Taj. Fatehpur Sikri, on the Jaipur road, makes a good stop on day two. The Taj Mahal is closed on Fridays.
- **Official info:** [UP Tourism: Agra](https://www.uptourism.gov.in/en/page/agra).
- Going on to Jaipur? Agra to Jaipur is roughly 240 km by road (from our [Jaipur guide](jaipur.html)), the classic Golden Triangle.

## Weekend getaways from Delhi to the mountains

Want the hills? These are all a day's drive or an overnight train or bus away. In winter, roads above the towns can close after snow, and the drives back to Delhi on Sunday evening are slow, so leave early.

### Rishikesh: the Ganga, yoga and rafting

The easiest hill trip from Delhi. Our guide says Rishikesh is about 240 km by road, and the new Delhi–Dehradun Expressway, opened on 14 April 2026, cuts Delhi to Dehradun to about 2.5 hours. Fast trains go to Haridwar, 25 km away. Watch the Ganga aarti at Triveni Ghat or Parmarth Niketan, do a yoga class, and raft in season (September to June). Read our full [Rishikesh guide](rishikesh-in-december.html).

### Mussoorie: the nearest hill town on this list

- **Distance:** about 260 km by road, about 5 to 6 hours (our estimates), via the new expressway to Dehradun and the hill road up. Trains run from Delhi to Dehradun, about 35 km below Mussoorie (our estimate).
- **Best months:** March to June for cool days, and September to November for clear views. Snow is possible on some winter days, but not every year.
- **What to do:** walk Mall Road and Camel's Back Road, take the ropeway to Gun Hill, and visit Kempty Falls and the quieter Landour above town.
- **Official info:** [Uttarakhand Tourism: Mussoorie](https://uttarakhandtourism.gov.in/destination/mussoorie).

### Nainital: a lake in the hills

- **Distance:** about 280 km by road, about 6 to 7 hours (our estimates). Trains run from Delhi to Kathgodam, about 35 km below Nainital (our estimate), then a taxi or bus up.
- **Best months:** March to June, and October to December for clear skies. Winter nights are cold.
- **What to do:** go boating on Naini Lake, walk the Mall Road, take the ropeway to Snow View Point, and visit Bhimtal and Sattal nearby.
- **Official info:** [Nainital district website](https://nainital.nic.in/) and [Uttarakhand Tourism](https://uttarakhandtourism.gov.in/).

### Shimla: the toy train and an easy hill town

Our guide says Shimla is about 350 km from Delhi by road. The overnight Kalka Mail reaches Kalka early in the morning, in time for the first toy train, a UNESCO World Heritage railway that takes about 5 to 6 hours to climb 96 km. Snow is more likely in January and February than in December. Read our full [Shimla guide](shimla-in-december.html).

### Manali: for a 3 or 4 day long weekend

Manali is too far for two days: our guide gives about 550–570 km from Delhi, and news reports put the overnight Volvo bus at about 10–12 hours each way. Take it on a long weekend of 3 or 4 days. Snow is possible from December to February, but not every year, and Rohtang Pass is closed in winter. Read our full [Manali guide](manali.html).

## Weekend getaways from Delhi that are not mountains

### Jaipur: forts, palaces and markets

Our guide says the drive takes about 5 hours on the old NH-8, and about 3.5 hours on the Delhi–Mumbai Expressway section. Shatabdi and other trains run to Jaipur Junction. See Amber Fort, City Palace, Hawa Mahal and Nahargarh at sunset in two days. Read our full [Jaipur guide](jaipur.html).

### Amritsar: the Golden Temple and Wagah

- **Distance:** about 470 km by road, about 7 to 8 hours (our estimates). Fast trains and direct flights make it easier for a weekend; check times on IRCTC or the airline.
- **Best months:** October to March. December and January are cold and foggy, and summer is very hot.
- **What to do:** the Golden Temple at night and in the early morning, a meal at the langar, Jallianwala Bagh, and the evening flag-lowering ceremony at the Attari–Wagah border.
- **Official info:** [Amritsar district website](https://amritsar.nic.in/).

### Udaipur: lakes and palaces, by overnight train or a short flight

Our guide says overnight trains from Delhi include the Mewar Express and the Chetak Express, and the City Palace museum says the flight takes about 1 hour 20 minutes. Take a boat on Lake Pichola, see the City Palace, and watch the sunset from Ambrai Ghat. Read our full [Udaipur guide](udaipur.html).

### Varanasi: the ghats and the Ganga aarti

Our guide says Vande Bharat trains run between New Delhi and Varanasi in about 8 hours, and there are direct flights. Two days is enough for a dawn boat ride, the evening aarti at Dashashwamedh Ghat, Kashi Vishwanath and Sarnath. Read our full [Varanasi guide](varanasi-in-december.html).

### Kashmir: snow, by flight

Fly to Srinagar for Dal Lake, and Gulmarg about 50 km away for snow and the gondola. Since 2 May 2026, Vande Bharat trains also run from Jammu Tawi and Katra to Srinagar, so you can take a train from Delhi and change. It's best for a long weekend of 4 days or more, because snow can delay flights. Read our [Kashmir in December guide](kashmir-in-december.html) and [Gulmarg guide](gulmarg-in-december.html).

## Weekend getaways from Delhi in December

- **For snow:** Shimla, Manali or Kashmir, but snow isn't guaranteed in December. January and February are better bets.
- **For sun:** Jaipur, Udaipur, Agra and Rishikesh have sunny winter days and cold nights.
- **Fog:** dense fog is common from late December to January. It delays trains and flights and makes early-morning driving risky. Don't plan a tight connection, and keep a spare morning.
- **Christmas and New Year** are the busiest days of the year in all the hill towns. Book early, or go the weekend before.

For more winter ideas, see [where to go in December and January](where-to-go-in-december-january.html) and [New Year trips 2027](new-year-trips-2027.html). Packing for the cold? See [what to wear on a winter hill trip](../guides/winter-clothes-for-hill-trips.html).

## Weekend getaways from Delhi with kids

- **Easy and short:** Agra (a short train ride) and Jaipur (forts to explore and the Albert Hall museum).
- **Hills without a long drive:** Rishikesh and Mussoorie, now quicker with the Dehradun expressway.
- **A trip they'll remember:** the Kalka–Shimla toy train. Book early for holidays.
- Pack snacks and a spare set of clothes in the car, and stop every 2 hours or so. Flying with a little one? See our [flying with a baby or toddler guide](../guides/flying-with-baby-or-toddler.html).

## Questions

### What are the best weekend getaways from Delhi?

For two days, Rishikesh, Jaipur, Agra and Mussoorie are the easiest, each about 4 to 6 hours by road or a short train ride. With an overnight train, Shimla, Udaipur and Varanasi also work. Manali and Kashmir need a long weekend of 3 or 4 days.

### Which weekend getaways from Delhi are within 200 km?

Mathura and Vrindavan (about 150 km) and Agra (about 210 km) are the closest on our list, by our estimates from an OpenStreetMap route. Both work as one night away, or a long day trip.

### Which is the nearest hill station to Delhi for a weekend?

Of the hill towns on this page, Mussoorie is the nearest by road, about 260 km by our estimate, and quicker now with the Delhi–Dehradun Expressway. Rishikesh, in the foothills, is about 240 km away.

### Can I do Manali in a weekend from Delhi?

Only just. The overnight bus takes about 10–12 hours each way, so a 2-day weekend leaves about one day in Manali. Take a Friday or Monday off, or use a long weekend such as Republic Day (23–26 January 2027).

### Where can I go from Delhi for a weekend in December?

Jaipur, Udaipur, Agra and Rishikesh for sunny days, or Shimla and Manali for a chance of snow. Leave room for fog, which delays trains and flights from late December.

### Is a 2-day trip from Delhi by car better than by train?

By car, you can stop where you like and split the fuel with friends: work it out in our [road trip fuel cost calculator](../tools/road-trip-cost-calculator.html). By train, you skip the traffic and fog on the roads: Agra, Jaipur, Haridwar for Rishikesh, Dehradun for Mussoorie and Kathgodam for Nainital all have fast or overnight trains.
