January is one of the best months for Thailand. The rains are over almost everywhere, Bangkok is as cool as it gets, the hills of the north have crisp mornings, and the Andaman Sea around Phuket and Krabi is calm and clear for island trips. The only part still catching showers is the Gulf, around Ko Samui. It's also high season, so plan the first week and the Republic Day weekend early. This guide covers January's weather by region, where to go, festivals, the island season, getting there and the entry rules for Indians.

## Thailand weather in January

Long-term January averages from the Thai Meteorological Department, published by the World Meteorological Organization:

| Place | Day | Night | Rain in January |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bangkok | About 32 °C | About 21 °C | About 9 mm, on about 1 day |
| Chiang Mai | About 29 °C | About 14 °C | About 8 mm, on about 1 day |
| Pattaya | About 31 °C | About 23 °C | About 14 mm, on about 2 days |
| Phuket | About 32 °C | About 24 °C | About 23 mm, on about 4 days |
| Ko Samui | About 29 °C | About 24 °C | About 138 mm, on about 11 days |

- **Bangkok is dry and a little less humid** than the rest of the year, but still hot by midday. See temples early in the morning.
- **The north is cool at night.** Chiang Mai drops to about 14 °C, and hilltops like Doi Inthanon are much colder at sunrise. Carry a jacket for early starts.
- **The Andaman coast (Phuket, Krabi, Phi Phi, Khao Lak) is at its best:** little rain, calm seas and clear water for snorkelling and boat trips.
- **The Gulf (Ko Samui, Ko Pha Ngan, Ko Tao) is drier than in November and December,** when Samui gets most of its rain, but January can still bring a few wet days. Pick the Andaman side if beaches are the main reason for your trip.

## Where to go in Thailand in January

- **Bangkok:** temples, river boats, markets and food. Two or three days is enough for a first trip.
- **Chiang Mai and the north:** old-city temples, night markets, elephant sanctuaries (choose ones with no riding), and the cool hills of Doi Inthanon and Doi Suthep. January is before the smoky season (below).
- **Phuket and Krabi:** the best beaches of the month. Base yourself in Kata, Karon or Patong in Phuket, or Ao Nang in Krabi, for day trips to Phi Phi, the James Bond island in Phang Nga Bay and the four-island tour.
- **The Similan Islands** are open for the 2026–27 season, from 15 October 2026 to about 15 May 2027 (news reports of the national parks department's order; the closing date wasn't on an official page when we checked). Day trips leave from Khao Lak.
- **Pattaya** is the closest beach to Bangkok, about 2 hours by road, and it's dry in January.

## Thailand in the first week of January

New Year's Day 2027 is a Friday, the only public holiday in January on the Bank of Thailand's 2027 list. The first weekend is still busy with New Year travellers in Bangkok, Phuket and Pattaya. From about 4 January, hotels and flights are usually easier to find. (We don't show prices: compare them for your dates.)

## Thailand in the middle of January

- **National Children's Day** is on the second Saturday of January, **9 January 2027**. It isn't a public holiday, but many places hold events for children, so expect busy family attractions that day.
- **The Bo Sang Umbrella Festival** in Bo Sang village, just east of Chiang Mai, is usually held on the third weekend of January, with painted paper umbrellas, a parade and craft stalls. The 2026 festival ran from 16 to 18 January. The 2027 dates hadn't been announced when we checked: listing sites expect 15 to 17 January.
- **The Thailand International Boat Show** in Phuket is listed for 14 to 17 January 2027 in one news report. We couldn't confirm it on an official page.

## Thailand in the last week of January

**Republic Day** is on **Tuesday 26 January 2027.** With Monday off, that's a 4-day weekend from India, long enough for Bangkok and Pattaya, or Phuket alone. Flights fill up, so book early.

Coming in February instead? **Chinese New Year is on Saturday 6 February 2027,** when Bangkok's Chinatown (Yaowarat) is packed. **The Chiang Mai Flower Festival** is usually on the first weekend of February (2027 dates not announced). From February, smoke from crop and forest fires starts to build in the north, and it's usually worst in March and April, say Thailand's Pollution Control Department and news reports. Check the air quality on [Air4Thai](http://air4thai.pcd.go.th/) before a late trip to Chiang Mai.

## Before you fly: visa and TDAC

- **Visa:** Indians don't need a visa for a holiday of up to 30 days, says the Royal Thai Embassy in New Delhi. Carry your return ticket and hotel bookings. For a longer stay, apply for a Thai e-visa on the official [Thai e-Visa website](https://www.thaievisa.go.th) before you go.
- **TDAC arrival card:** every traveller must fill in the free Thailand Digital Arrival Card within 3 days before arriving, only on [tdac.immigration.go.th](https://tdac.immigration.go.th/).
- **Entry fee:** Thailand has talked about a tourist entry fee for air arrivals, but none had started when we checked (news reports, November 2026).
- **Vapes and e-cigarettes are illegal in Thailand.** Don't bring them.

## What to wear in Thailand in January

Light cotton clothes, a cap and sunscreen for Bangkok and the beaches, one temple outfit (sleeves and long trousers or a long skirt), and sandals you can slip off. Add a jacket or fleece for Chiang Mai's mornings and evenings, and for the cold air-conditioning on buses and flights.

## Questions

### Is January a good time to visit Thailand?

Yes, one of the best. The Thai Meteorological Department's averages give Bangkok about 32 °C by day and 21 °C at night with almost no rain, and Phuket gets only about 23 mm in the month. It's high season, so book early.

### Which part of Thailand is best in January?

The Andaman coast, Phuket, Krabi, Phi Phi and the Similan Islands, for beaches, and Bangkok and Chiang Mai for cities and culture. Ko Samui is fine but gets more rain, about 138 mm in January.

### Is it cold in Thailand in January?

Not in Bangkok or on the beaches. The north is cool at night: about 14 °C in Chiang Mai, and colder on the mountains at sunrise. Pack a light jacket for the north.

### Do Indians need a visa for Thailand in January 2027?

Not for a holiday of up to 30 days, says the Royal Thai Embassy in New Delhi. You must fill in the free TDAC arrival card online within 3 days before you land. Check the embassy's page again before you fly, as the rules have changed several times.

### When is Chinese New Year in Thailand in 2027?

Saturday 6 February 2027, so it isn't in January.

### Is Thailand crowded in January?

Busy in the first week, around the New Year, and again on the Republic Day weekend from India (23 to 26 January 2027). The middle of the month is a little calmer, but it's high season throughout.
