Shimla in December is cold and mostly dry, with sunny days and nights close to freezing. Many people go hoping for snow at Christmas or New Year. Snow can come in late December, but in Shimla it is much more likely in January and February. This guide covers the weather, the real chances of snow, how to get there, the toy train, winter roads and what to wear.

## Weather in December and January

Long-term averages for Shimla from the India Meteorological Department (IMD):

| Month | Day | Night | Rain and snow |
|---|---|---|---|
| December | About 12 °C | About 4 °C | About 21 mm |
| January | About 9 °C | About 2 °C | About 53 mm |
| February | About 10 °C | About 2 °C | About 64 mm |

- **January is the coldest month.** IMD says daytime highs stay around 10–11 °C from the end of December to mid-February.
- **Some nights are far colder than the average.** Shimla's lowest December reading was −12.2 °C, on 13 December 1963. These very cold nights usually come on clear nights just after snow.
- **On Christmas Day,** IMD says the day is usually 10–12 °C and the night 2–4 °C.
- **Sunny days feel warm, shade feels cold.** Dress in layers that you can take off at midday and put back on by 4 PM.

## Will it snow in Shimla?

- **Snow usually comes from late December to early March.** IMD's records from 20 winters (1990–91 to 2010–11) show about 7 cm of snow in an average December, but around 40 cm in January and again in February.
- **Snow is coming later than it used to.** IMD notes that snow in Shimla, which used to fall in December, has lately been coming in January or early February.
- **A white Christmas is rare.** Rain or snow fell on only 9 of the 40 Christmas Days from 1971 to 2010.
- **Last winter was dry until late January.** IMD's Shimla station recorded no rain or snow at all in December 2025. The first snow in Shimla town came on 23 January 2026, after a dry spell of about three months, according to news reports.
- **Higher places get snow more often.** Kufri (about 20 km away), Fagu (22 km) and Narkanda (65 km) are higher than Shimla town and often see snow when Shimla doesn't.

So if snow matters most, plan for January or February, keep your dates flexible, and check the forecast in the week before you go.

## Getting to Shimla

- **By air:** Chandigarh (IXC) is the nearest big airport, about 120 km from Shimla by road. Shimla's own airport at Jubbarhatti is 23 km from the city, but the Shimla district says it works only some of the time, depending on the season and weather. Alliance Air's Delhi flights have stopped and restarted several times; in January 2026 the state agreed to pay to keep them running every day. Check the current schedule before you plan around it, and expect delays in bad weather.
- **By train:** take a train from Delhi to Kalka, then the toy train up to Shimla (see below). The overnight Kalka Mail from Delhi reaches Kalka early in the morning, in time for the first toy trains.
- **By road:** Shimla is about 350 km from Delhi. Himachal Pradesh Tourism says ordinary and luxury buses run regularly from Delhi and Chandigarh, and many people take an overnight Volvo.

### The Kalka–Shimla toy train

The narrow-gauge line from Kalka to Shimla was built between 1898 and 1903 and has more than 100 tunnels. It is a UNESCO World Heritage Site, as one of the Mountain Railways of India. The 96 km ride takes about 5 to 6 hours, and several trains run each way every day, including the Himalayan Queen, the Shivalik Express and a Vistadome coach. Book early for the holidays, because seats fill up fast, and carry a warm layer: the coaches get cold as the train climbs.

## Getting around Shimla

- **Walk.** The Ridge and the Mall are walking areas, and most of central Shimla is up and down steep paths. Wear shoes with a good grip.
- **Jakhoo Temple:** about 2.5 km from the Ridge, a steep half-hour climb. A ropeway also goes up.
- **Taxis** wait at the bus stands and the railway station. For Kufri, take a taxi from the Old Bus Stand or the ISBT, or a Himachal Roadways bus from Lakkar Bazaar bus stand.

## Things to do in winter

- **The Ridge and Christ Church.** The Ridge is Shimla's open square, with clear views of snow peaks on fine days. The neo-Gothic Christ Church stands on it and is decorated for Christmas.
- **Ice skating.** Shimla's open-air rink, below the Ridge near Lakkar Bazaar, freezes naturally on cold nights. It has been running since 1920. Last winter's sessions began on 4 December 2025. Skating depends on the cold, so there are fewer sessions in warm winters.
- **Shimla Winter Carnival.** Last year the Shimla Municipal Corporation held a winter carnival on the Ridge from 24 December 2025 into the first days of January, with music and cultural shows. Dates for this winter haven't been announced yet.
- **Jakhoo Temple.** Shimla's highest point, at 2,445 m, with a temple to Lord Hanuman.
- **Viceregal Lodge.** The 1888 stone building where the Viceroys lived, now the Indian Institute of Advanced Study. Parts of it are open to visitors.
- **Kufri, Fagu and Narkanda for snow.** These are the closest places to find snow. Narkanda, near Hatu Peak, has slopes for skiing when there's enough snow.
- **Mashobra and Naldehra** (12 km and 23 km) are quieter spots with pine forests, and **Tattapani** (53 km) has hot springs by the Sutlej river.

## Christmas, New Year and snow days

- **Book early.** Shimla is very busy from Christmas to New Year, and even more on the days just after a snowfall, when people drive up from Delhi, Punjab and Haryana.
- **Roads close after snow.** In past winters, traffic to upper Shimla beyond Dhalli was stopped after snowfall, and the highway (NH-5) stayed shut at Kufri, Fagu and Narkanda until the snow was cleared. Follow Shimla Police's updates before you drive.
- **Watch for black ice.** Melted snow freezes on the road at night, and the mornings after snow are the most slippery. Don't drive your own car on snow unless you have done it before. Hire a local taxi with snow chains or a 4x4.
- **Expect traffic jams.** Park at a public parking (ISBT, Lift Parking, Railway Station, Old Bus Stand, Chhota Shimla or Sanjauli) and walk.

## What to wear in Shimla in December

What should I wear? Try the [what-to-wear helper](../tools/what-to-wear.html#shimla) for day and night temperatures and an outfit list by month.

Shimla needs the same clothes as Manali, just a little less on the coldest nights. Himachal Pradesh Tourism says heavy woollens and jackets are needed from November to March.

- **Layers:** thermals, a sweater or fleece, and a warm jacket. You will take some off on sunny afternoons.
- **Head and hands:** a woollen cap, a neck warmer and gloves, for evenings on the Ridge.
- **Shoes:** waterproof shoes with a good grip. Shimla's paths are steep, and they get icy after snow.
- **For the snow at Kufri or Narkanda:** waterproof gloves and trousers, or rent a snow suit there.
- **Sunglasses and sunscreen:** the sun is strong at this height, and snow reflects it.

## Health and safety

- **Never sleep with a heater burning in a closed room.** Gas heaters, charcoal and angithis give off carbon monoxide, which can kill without warning. Keep a window slightly open, and switch heaters off before you sleep.
- **Watch the monkeys,** especially on the way to Jakhoo. They snatch glasses, phones and food. Keep bags closed and don't carry food in your hand.
- **Walk carefully after snow.** Steep paths and steps get icy, so take small steps and hold the railings.
- **Dress for the cold,** and change out of wet clothes quickly. Shivering, confusion and clumsiness are signs of hypothermia.

## Questions

### Does it snow in Shimla in December?

Sometimes, but not every year. IMD says snow usually comes from late December to early March, and lately more often in January or early February. December 2025 had no snow at all, and the first snow came on 23 January 2026. For a better chance, go in January or February.

### How cold is Shimla in December?

IMD's long-term averages are about 12 °C in the day and 4 °C at night. Some nights fall below freezing, and the lowest December reading ever was −12.2 °C, in 1963. January is colder, at about 9 °C by day and 2 °C at night.

### What should I wear in Shimla in December?

Layers: thermals, a sweater or fleece, and a warm jacket, plus a woollen cap, gloves and a neck warmer for the evenings. Wear waterproof shoes with a good grip. If you go to Kufri or Narkanda for snow, add waterproof gloves and trousers.

### Is it safe to visit Shimla in December?

Yes. December is usually dry and sunny. The main risks come after snow: icy roads, closed stretches beyond Dhalli and long traffic jams. Follow police advice, use a local taxi with snow chains on snowy roads, and never sleep with a heater on in a closed room.

### Where can I see snow near Shimla?

Kufri, Fagu and Narkanda are higher than Shimla and often have snow when the town doesn't. Narkanda is the farthest, about 65 km away, and has ski slopes when there's enough snow.

### Shimla or Manali in December?

Shimla is easier to reach, with the toy train from Kalka and good roads from Chandigarh, and it's a little less cold. Manali is colder, with more snow nearby at Solang and the Atal Tunnel. Neither has snow for certain in December. See our [Manali winter guide](manali.html).

### Does the toy train run in winter?

Yes, the Kalka–Shimla toy train runs all year. Book early for Christmas and New Year, and expect delays after heavy snow.
