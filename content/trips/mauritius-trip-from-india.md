You don't need a visa, there are direct flights from four Indian cities, and with its Indian-origin population, Hindi and Bhojpuri words, temples and vegetarian food, many Indians feel at home. It's a favourite for honeymoons: blue lagoons, resorts on white beaches and a mountain backdrop. ## Mauritius visa for Indians

Indians don't need a visa to visit Mauritius. The Passport and Immigration Office of Mauritius lists India as "No visa required" in its country-wise visa list (read 21 Nov 2026).

You still have to meet the entry rules at the airport. Every visitor must, says the Passport and Immigration Office:

- **Hold a passport valid beyond your stay.** Most airlines also like to see at least 6 months left and a couple of blank pages.
- **Have a return ticket** to India or the country where you live.
- **Have enough money** for your stay.
- **Have a confirmed hotel booking,** or a sponsor in Mauritius who will host you.
- **Not work** without a permit.

How long can you stay? The immigration officer decides at the airport. For tourists, the office says up to six months in a calendar year may be given, case by case. Most holidays are 5 to 7 days, so carry your return ticket and hotel booking, printed or on your phone, and you should have no trouble.

## All-in-One Travel Digital Form: fill it before you fly

- **Every traveller must fill in the Mauritius All-in-One Travel Digital Form** before travelling, under Mauritian law, says the Ministry of Health and Wellness. It asks for your flight, where you will stay and an emergency contact, with a health declaration.
- **Airlines ask for proof at boarding,** so fill it in before you go to the airport.
- **It's free.** Use only the official site, [safemauritius.govmu.org](https://safemauritius.govmu.org/), which the Passport and Immigration Office links to. The government doesn't charge any fee for it.
- **Give true answers.** Refusing to fill it in or giving false information can lead to a fine of up to MUR 500,000 or prison, says the form.

- **No direct flight from your city?** Fly to Mumbai, Delhi, Chennai or Bengaluru first, or connect through Dubai or another Gulf hub.
- **Mauritius is 1 hour 30 minutes behind India.** A flight that lands at 2 PM in Mauritius lands at 3:30 PM India time.
- **The airport is far from the north.** The drive to Grand Baie in the north takes a while, so book your transfer with the hotel or a taxi in advance.
- **Baggage:** check how much your fare includes. See the [international baggage rules](../baggage/international.html).
- **Fares go up for Christmas, New Year, Diwali and the wedding season.** Honeymooners should book early.

## A 5-day Mauritius itinerary

Mauritius is small, so you can stay in one hotel and see the island on day trips by taxi or with a driver. This plan does one area per day.

| Day | Plan | Evening |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Land and check in. Rest on your hotel's beach | Sunset on the beach |
| 2 | The north: the Pamplemousses botanical garden (giant water lilies), Port Louis market, Caudan Waterfront and the Aapravasi Ghat, where Indian workers first landed | Grand Baie for dinner |
| 3 | The south-west: Grand Bassin (Ganga Talao) and its Shiva temple, Black River Gorges viewpoint, Chamarel waterfall and the Seven Coloured Earth | Le Morne or Flic en Flac beach |
| 4 | The east: a boat to Île aux Cerfs for its beach, water sports and lagoon | Rest |
| 5 | A catamaran or dolphin trip on the west coast, or a lazy morning, then fly home | |

- **Seven days?** Add a day at Le Morne beach under the mountain in the photo, and a day for an underwater sea walk, snorkelling or a glass-bottom boat at Blue Bay.
- **Grand Bassin is very special for Hindu visitors.** Pilgrims walk there for Maha Shivaratri, when it gets very crowded. Dress modestly and take your shoes off at the temples.
- **With kids?** Choose a hotel with a calm lagoon beach and a kids' club. The east and north coasts have shallow lagoons.
- **Book sea trips with licensed operators,** and don't go out if the sea is rough or a cyclone warning is in force.

## Mauritius honeymoon tips

- **Choose your coast.** The north (Grand Baie) is lively with restaurants and shops; the east has long white beaches and calm lagoons; the west has the best sunsets; Le Morne in the south-west is quiet with a dramatic mountain.
- **Check the meal plan.** Half board or all-inclusive can be easier than going out for every meal, especially at resorts far from towns.
- **Tell the hotel it's your honeymoon** when you book, and ask what it offers. Get any extras in writing.
- **Plan one special day:** a private catamaran trip with lunch, a spa for two or a sunset dinner on the beach.
- **For the photos,** pack bright or white outfits, and go to the Seven Coloured Earth and Le Morne in the morning light. See our [honeymoon packing list](../guides/honeymoon-packing-list.html#beach-honeymoon-maldives-bali-goa-and-kerala).
- **Mauritius or the Maldives?** Mauritius has more to see beyond the beach (mountains, waterfalls, temples, markets) and needs no visa. The Maldives is about staying on one island. See our [Maldives trip guide](maldives-trip-from-india.html).

## Mauritius weather in December and the best time to go

Mauritius has two seasons, says Mauritius Meteorological Services (MMS): a warm, humid summer from November to April, and a cooler, dry winter from June to September. May and October are in between. Long-term averages for Port Louis from MMS, published by the World Meteorological Organization:

| Month | Day | Night | Rain |
|---|---|---|---|
| October | About 29 °C | About 20 °C | About 15 mm, on about 3 days |
| November | About 30 °C | About 22 °C | About 24 mm, on about 3 days |
| December | About 31 °C | About 23 °C | About 85 mm, on about 6 days |
| January | About 32 °C | About 24 °C | About 131 mm, on about 9 days |
| February | About 31 °C | About 24 °C | About 160 mm, on about 10 days |
| July | About 27 °C | About 19 °C | About 18 mm, on about 4 days |

- **May to November is the most comfortable time:** less rain and less humidity. October is the driest month, says MMS.
- **December is warm and still a good month,** with rain on about 6 days in Port Louis. It's the start of the busy holiday season, so book early.
- **January to March is the hottest and wettest time.** February and March are the wettest months, and summer is also the cyclone season in the south-west Indian Ocean. Follow MMS warnings, and buy travel insurance that covers cancellations.
- **June to September is the cool season,** with nights around 16 to 19 °C. Carry a light jacket for evenings and the hills.
- **The coast is sunny:** 7.5 to over 8 hours of sunshine a day, says MMS. The central plateau is cloudier and cooler.

## Getting around Mauritius

- **Taxis or a driver for the day** are the easiest way to see the island. Agree the price before you set off, or ask your hotel to book one.
- **Hiring a car?** Mauritius drives on the left, like India. Check what licence the rental company needs before you book.
- **Buses** connect the main towns and are cheap, but slow for sightseeing.
- **The Metro Express light rail** runs between Port Louis and Curepipe through the central towns.
- **Hotel transfers from the airport** are worth booking in advance, especially if you land late.

## Food for Indian travellers: veg tips

- **Vegetarian food is easy to find.** Mauritius has a large Indian-origin population, and Indian dishes are part of everyday food.
- **Try the street food:** dholl puri and farata (flatbreads with curries and chutney), gateau piment (chilli fritters) and fresh fruit with chilli salt.
- **Indian restaurants** are common in Grand Baie, Port Louis and the tourist areas, and most resorts have Indian dishes on the buffet.
- **Seafood is everywhere,** so ask if a dish has fish or prawns. Say "vegetarian" or "no fish" clearly.
- **Jain travellers** should tell the hotel when booking, and carry some ready-to-eat food for day trips. Check what you can pack in our [can I carry it on a flight?](../baggage/can-i-carry.html) list.
- **Drink bottled or filtered water** if you are not sure about the tap water where you stay.

## Money and rules to know

- **The currency is the Mauritian rupee (Rs, MUR).** Cards work in hotels, malls and big restaurants. Carry some cash for markets, street food and small taxis.
- **Cash from India:** RBI lets you buy up to US$3,000 in foreign currency notes per trip, and you can carry up to ₹25,000 in Indian notes. Put the rest on a forex card. See [how much cash you can carry abroad](../baggage/customs/cash-and-currency.html).
- **Don't take shells or coral** from the beaches, and don't touch or stand on coral when you snorkel.
- **Swim where there are lifeguards,** and follow the beach flags.
- **Dress modestly away from the beach,** especially at temples, mosques and in Port Louis.
- **Plugs:** carry a universal travel adapter.

## What to wear in Mauritius

- **Light cotton clothes,** shorts, T-shirts and dresses for the heat.
- **Swimwear, a cap, sunglasses and reef-safe sunscreen** for the beach and boat trips.
- **One modest outfit** with sleeves and long trousers or a long skirt for Grand Bassin and other temples.
- **Water shoes or sandals with a strap** for boat trips and rocky beaches.
- **A light jacket** for the cool season (June to September) and for evenings in the hills.

## Questions

### Do Indians need a visa for Mauritius?

No. Mauritius's Passport and Immigration Office lists India as "No visa required". At the airport, show a passport valid for your stay, a return ticket, a hotel booking and enough money for your trip. The immigration officer decides how long you can stay.

### Is there a visa on arrival for Indians in Mauritius?

Indians don't need a visa at all, says Mauritius's Passport and Immigration Office. You are given permission to stay at the airport if you meet the entry rules.

### What is the Mauritius visa fee for Indians?

There's no visa fee, because Indians don't need a visa. The All-in-One Travel Digital Form is also free.

### What documents do Indians need for Mauritius?

A passport valid for your whole stay, a return ticket, a confirmed hotel booking (or a sponsor), proof of enough money, and the All-in-One Travel Digital Form filled in online before you fly.

### How many days are enough for Mauritius?

Five days is enough to see the north, the south-west and Île aux Cerfs. Six or seven days lets you add a beach day and a sea trip. For a honeymoon, many couples stay a week.

### Is December a good time to visit Mauritius?

Yes. December is warm, about 31 °C by day and 23 °C at night in Port Louis, with rain on about 6 days. It's busy with holidaymakers, so book early. January to March is wetter and is the cyclone season.

### What is the weather in Mauritius in December?

Hot and humid, with some showers. Port Louis averages about 31 °C by day and 23 °C at night, with about 85 mm of rain, on long-term averages from Mauritius Meteorological Services.

### Is Mauritius good for a honeymoon?

Yes. It has calm lagoons, beach resorts, sunset cruises and sights beyond the beach, and Indians don't need a visa. Pick the east or Le Morne for quiet beaches, or the north for restaurants.

### Which Indian cities have direct flights to Mauritius?

Delhi, Mumbai and Chennai (Air Mauritius), Mumbai (Air India) and Bengaluru (IndiGo).

### What is the time difference between India and Mauritius?

Mauritius is 1 hour 30 minutes behind India.
