The Maldives is India's favourite beach honeymoon abroad: white sand, clear blue water, and islands that are a short flight from South India. Indians get a tourist visa on arrival, with no pre-approval, as long as they carry a return ticket and a paid booking. The big choice is where to stay: a private resort island, or a guesthouse on a local island, which costs much less but has stricter rules. This guide covers the visa and the IMUGA form, flights, resort vs local island, transfers by speedboat and seaplane, a 5-day plan, honeymoon tips, the weather and the rules to know.

## Maldives visa for Indians

The Maldives gives a tourist visa on arrival, says Maldives Immigration (page updated 10 Sep 2026, checked 21 Nov 2026). Tourists don't need pre-approval, but they must meet the entry rules at the airport:

- **Passport:** machine-readable, valid for at least 1 month. Passports with an extended validity aren't accepted.
- **A complete travel plan:** a confirmed return ticket and a prepaid, confirmed booking at a registered resort, hotel or guesthouse.
- **Enough money** for your stay.
- **The IMUGA Traveller Declaration,** filed online before you fly (see below).
- **A yellow fever vaccination certificate,** only if you are coming from, or recently passed through, a country with a yellow fever risk.
- **The officer decides.** A full travel plan doesn't guarantee entry, says Maldives Immigration.
- **How many days?** The visa page doesn't state the number of days or a fee. Many travel sites say 30 days and free, but we couldn't confirm that on the official page, so ask the officer if you plan a long stay.

## IMUGA Traveller Declaration: fill it before you fly

- **Every traveller must submit it** within 96 hours (4 days) before arriving, says Maldives Immigration. Children need their own form.
- **It's free** on the official site, [imuga.immigration.gov.mv](https://imuga.immigration.gov.mv). Avoid other sites that charge a fee.
- **Fill it again before you leave.** The IMUGA site says the form is needed within 96 hours of arrival or departure.
- **Keep the QR code** on your phone for immigration.

## Flights from India to the Maldives

The main airport is Velana International Airport (MLE), on its own island next to Malé. Direct flights from India, from the airport's list of airlines and destinations (read 21 Nov 2026):

| Airline | Direct from |
|---|---|
| IndiGo | Bengaluru, Delhi, Kochi, Mumbai, Thiruvananthapuram, and Hyderabad in some seasons |
| Maldivian (the state airline) | Kochi, Thiruvananthapuram |

- **From Kochi or Thiruvananthapuram,** the flight is short, which makes South India the easiest place to fly from.
- **The Maldives is 30 minutes behind India.** A flight that lands at 1 PM in Malé lands at 1:30 PM India time.
- **Land in daylight if you are taking a seaplane.** Seaplanes fly only between sunrise and sunset, says Trans Maldivian Airways. A late landing can mean a night near the airport.
- **Baggage:** IndiGo gives 20 kg to Malé on regular fares (25 kg on UpFront). See our [Maldives packing list](../guides/maldives-packing-list.html#which-bag-to-take) and the [international baggage rules](../baggage/international.html).

## Resort or local island: which one to choose

There are two kinds of stays in the Maldives, and the rules are different.

**A resort island:** one hotel on its own island.

- **Best for a honeymoon** and for a relaxed holiday with privacy: beach villas, overwater villas, restaurants, spas and water sports.
- **Dress is relaxed.** Swimwear is normal on the beach.
- **Alcohol is sold** at resorts and hotels that hold a special licence.
- **It costs more.** Meals, transfers and activities add up, so check what your plan includes: breakfast only, half board, full board or all-inclusive.

**A local island:** a guesthouse or small hotel on an island where Maldivians live.

- **Costs much less,** and you see everyday Maldivian life.
- **Dress modestly** away from the beach. Cover your shoulders and knees in the village. Ask your guesthouse where you may swim in swimwear.
- **No alcohol.** Bringing in alcohol is against Maldivian law, and it's sold only at licensed resorts and hotels.
- **Day trips** to resorts, sandbanks and snorkelling spots are usually booked through the guesthouse.

**Can't decide?** Split the trip: 2 nights on a local island, then 3 nights at a resort.

## Getting to your island: speedboat, seaplane or domestic flight

Your resort or guesthouse usually books the transfer from the airport. Ask which one you'll get, the time it takes, the price and the bag limit before you pay.

- **Speedboat:** for resorts and islands near Malé. You board at the airport jetty.
- **Seaplane:** for resorts in the farther atolls. Trans Maldivian Airways allows 25 kg per person (20 kg check-in and 5 kg hand luggage); extra weight costs US$5 per kg plus taxes. Maldivian's seaplanes allow 15 kg per seat. Seaplanes fly only in daylight.
- **Domestic flight and boat:** for far atolls, a Maldivian domestic flight (20 kg check-in) and then a boat.
- **To local islands:** shared speedboats and public ferries run from Malé to some islands. Times change, so ask your guesthouse to book and confirm.

The full bag limits are in our [Maldives packing list](../guides/maldives-packing-list.html#which-bag-to-take).

## A 5-day Maldives plan

Most trips from India are 4 or 5 nights. This plan works at a resort or a guesthouse.

| Day | Plan | Evening |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Land at Malé and transfer by speedboat or seaplane. Check in and rest | Sunset on the beach |
| 2 | Snorkel the house reef, or take a snorkelling boat trip | Dinner on the beach |
| 3 | A sandbank picnic, or a dolphin-watching cruise | Sunset cruise or night fishing |
| 4 | A spa, a diving try-out, kayaking, or a day trip to a local island or a resort | Special dinner |
| 5 | Half a day in Malé: the fish market, the old Friday Mosque and the waterfront, then fly home | |

- **A 6-day trip?** Add a lazy day, or split your stay between a local island and a resort.
- **Book activities at your resort or guesthouse,** and ask what is included in your plan.
- **Visiting Malé?** Dress modestly: cover your shoulders and knees.
- **Don't touch or stand on coral,** and don't take coral or shells home.

## Maldives honeymoon tips

- **Choose the villa first.** A beach villa opens onto the sand; an overwater villa sits on stilts over the lagoon. Read recent reviews and photos.
- **Check the meal plan.** All-inclusive or full board can work out cheaper than paying for each meal at a resort island.
- **Check the transfer cost.** Seaplane transfers to far resorts can cost a lot; ask for the price per person.
- **Tell the resort it's your honeymoon** when you book, and ask what it offers. Get any extras in writing.
- **Plan one special evening:** a beach dinner, a sunset cruise or a spa for two.
- **For the photos,** pack bright or white outfits. ## Maldives weather in December and the best time to go

Long-term averages from the Maldives Meteorological Service, published by the World Meteorological Organization:

| Month | Day | Night | Rain in Malé |
|---|---|---|---|
| November | About 30 °C | About 25 °C | About 231 mm, on about 13 days |
| December | About 30 °C | About 25 °C | About 217 mm, on about 12 days |
| January | About 30 °C | About 26 °C | About 114 mm, on about 6 days |
| February | About 31 °C | About 26 °C | About 38 mm, on about 3 days, the driest month |
| March | About 31 °C | About 26 °C | About 74 mm, on about 5 days |
| May | About 31 °C | About 26 °C | About 219 mm, on about 15 days |

- **It's hot all year,** about 30–32 °C by day and 24–27 °C at night.
- **February to April is the driest time** around Malé. January is drier than December.
- **Is December a good time?** Yes. It rains on about 12 days in Malé, often in short, heavy showers, and it gets drier towards January. It's also the busiest and costliest time, from Christmas to New Year, so book early.
- **The north is drier and the far south wetter in winter.** In December, Hanimaadhoo in the north averages about 117 mm on 7 days, and Gan in Addu Atoll about 236 mm on 15 days.

## Phone, SIM and eSIM in the Maldives

You have three ways to stay online in the Maldives:

- **Roaming on your own SIM:** the easiest, but often the costliest. Check your mobile company's international pack before you fly.
- **A local SIM card:** sold at the airport and in phone shops. Shops usually ask for your passport to register it.
- **An eSIM:** a data plan you buy online and install on your phone before you go, with no plastic card. Your phone must support eSIM and must not be locked to one network. On an iPhone, look in Settings > Mobile Service (or Cellular). On Android, look in Settings > SIM or Connections.

Install the eSIM at home over Wi-Fi and switch it on when you land. Most travel eSIMs give data only, with no local number, so keep your home SIM on for calls and bank OTP texts (roaming charges may apply).

## Green tax and money

- **Green tax:** tourists pay a green tax for each night, says the Maldives Inland Revenue Authority (MIRA). Since 1 January 2025 it is US$12 a night at resorts, resort hotels, tourist vessels and most hotels, and US$6 a night at guesthouses and hotels on an inhabited island with 50 rooms or fewer. Children under 2 don't pay. Your resort or guesthouse adds it to the bill, so check whether your price includes it.
- **The currency is the Maldivian rufiyaa (MVR).** Resort prices are usually shown in US dollars. Carry a card and some US dollars for tips and small costs.
- **Carrying cash:** RBI lets a bank or money changer sell you up to US$3,000 in foreign currency notes per trip, and you can take up to ₹25,000 in Indian notes. The Maldives asks anyone carrying US$10,000 or more in cash to report it to Maldives Customs. See [how much cash you can carry abroad](../baggage/customs/cash-and-currency.html).

## Food in the Maldives for Indian travellers

- **Maldivian food is mostly fish,** especially tuna, with rice, coconut and roshi (a flatbread).
- **Pork needs a permit** to bring into the Maldives, so don't pack any pork snacks.
- **Resorts usually serve international buffets or menus.** If you are veg or Jain, tell the resort when you book and again when you check in.
- **On local islands, choice is smaller.** Ask your guesthouse in advance for veg meals.
- **Drink bottled or filtered water.**

## Rules to know in the Maldives

The Maldives has strict rules on what you bring in, says Velana International Airport:

- **No idols for worship,** even small ones for your own puja.
- **No alcohol.** Alcohol bought on the flight or at duty-free shops on the way is taken away when you land. Drink only at licensed resorts and hotels.
- **No pornographic material,** and no pork without a permit.
- **No vapes or e-cigarettes.** The Maldives bans them for everyone, says the Ministry of Health.
- **No tobacco if you were born on or after 1 January 2007.**
- **Drones** need permission from the Civil Aviation Authority.
- **Don't take coral, Triton shells or turtle shells home.**

## What to wear in the Maldives

- **At resorts:** shorts, T-shirts, dresses and swimwear.
- **In Malé and on local islands:** clothes that cover your shoulders and knees, and a kaftan or T-shirt over swimwear away from the beach.
- **At night:** light dresses, linen shirts and trousers, and flat sandals for sand and jetties.
- **For snorkelling:** a rash guard or quick-dry T-shirt, and water shoes.
- **Sunscreen,** sunglasses, a hat and a waterproof pouch for your phone.

## Questions

### Do Indians need a visa for the Maldives?

No visa is needed in advance. The Maldives gives a tourist visa on arrival with no pre-approval, says Maldives Immigration. You need a passport valid for at least 1 month, a confirmed return ticket, a paid booking at a registered resort, hotel or guesthouse, enough money for the stay, and the IMUGA declaration.

### Is the Maldives visa free for Indians?

Maldives Immigration's tourist visa page lists no fee, and you don't apply in advance. We couldn't confirm the number of days on that page, so ask the officer if you plan a long stay.

### What is the IMUGA form for the Maldives?

The Traveller Declaration that every traveller, including children, must submit online within 96 hours before arriving in the Maldives, and again before leaving. It's free on imuga.immigration.gov.mv.

### Which cities in India have direct flights to the Maldives?

Bengaluru, Delhi, Mumbai, Kochi and Thiruvananthapuram on IndiGo (and Hyderabad in some seasons), and Kochi and Thiruvananthapuram on Maldivian.

### What is the difference between a resort and a local island in the Maldives?

A resort is one hotel on its own private island, with relaxed dress and alcohol. A local island is where Maldivians live: guesthouses cost much less, but you must dress modestly in the village and there's no alcohol.

### How do you get from Malé airport to a resort?

By speedboat for resorts near Malé, by seaplane for farther atolls, or by a domestic flight and a boat. The resort usually books it. Seaplanes fly only in daylight.

### What is the green tax in the Maldives?

US$12 per person per night at resorts and most hotels, and US$6 at guesthouses and small hotels on inhabited islands, since 1 January 2025, says MIRA. Children under 2 don't pay.

### How many days are enough for the Maldives?

Four or five nights are enough for most couples. Add a night or two if you split your stay between a local island and a resort.

### Is December a good time to visit the Maldives?

Yes. It's about 30 °C, and Malé gets rain on about 12 days, often in short showers. January to April is drier. December, especially Christmas to New Year, is the costliest time.

### Can I take alcohol to the Maldives?

No. Alcohol bought on the way is taken away when you land. You can drink only at licensed resorts and hotels, not on local islands.

### What is the time difference between India and the Maldives?

The Maldives is 30 minutes behind India.
