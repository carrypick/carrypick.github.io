Manali is India's favourite winter hill station. Many people go hoping for snow at Christmas or New Year, but snow isn't guaranteed, roads close when it falls, and the town gets very crowded. This guide helps you plan for both: a snowy trip and a dry, sunny one.

Still choosing? Compare Goa, Manali, Jaipur and Kerala in [where to go in December and January](where-to-go-in-december-january.html). Going in late January? See our [Republic Day long weekend ideas](republic-day-long-weekend-2027.html).

## Will there be snow?

Snow in Manali usually comes between December and February, but some winters are dry:

- **Winter 2025–26 was very dry.** December 2025 was Himachal's sixth-driest December since 1901, with no snow in Manali on New Year's Day. The season's first big snowfall came only around 24 January 2026.
- **Winter 2024–25 was snowy.** Heavy snow just before Christmas 2024 left more than 1,000 vehicles stuck between Solang and the Atal Tunnel.

So watch the weather forecast in the week before you go. If you want snow, keep your dates flexible, or plan for **January and February**. The mountaineering institute at Solang runs its ski courses from mid-January to March, when snow is most dependable there.

## Weather in December and January

- **Very cold nights.** Himachal Tourism says temperatures in December and January can fall below −5 °C.
- **Mild, sunny days** when there's no storm. Incredible India gives ranges of about −3 to 17 °C for December and −6 to 16 °C for January.
- **Sudden changes.** The weather can turn from warm to snowy within minutes, so carry warm layers every day.

## Getting to Manali

- **By air:** Kullu-Manali Airport (KUU) is at Bhuntar, 50 km from Manali. It's small, and flights are often cancelled in bad weather. News reports say there are no direct Delhi flights this winter: until 27 March 2027 the only flights are from Jaipur and Dehradun. Most people fly to **Chandigarh (IXC)** and drive from there.
- **By bus:** Himachal Tourism (HPTDC) and HRTC run overnight Volvo and luxury buses from Delhi. Delhi to Manali is about 550–570 km, and news reports put the bus trip at about 10–12 hours. Book in advance for Christmas and New Year.
- **By train:** the nearest big railway stations are Chandigarh and Kalka, then a long drive. Joginder Nagar, about 160 km away, has only a slow narrow-gauge line.
- **By road from Chandigarh:** about 300 km. The new four-lane road has made it shorter than the official 320 km.

Going by train? See the [Indian Railways luggage rules](../baggage/train-luggage-rules.html). Flying? See the [cabin bag rules for Indian airlines](../baggage/index.html).

## Entry tax and green tax for outside cars

If your car isn't registered in Himachal Pradesh:

- **Manali green tax:** ₹200 for a car, ₹100 for a bike and ₹300 for a passenger vehicle. It's valid for 7 days. Pay online up to 9 days ahead at [rohtangpermits.hp.gov.in](https://rohtangpermits.hp.gov.in/ManaliEntry/InformatoryScreen) and carry a printout.
- **Himachal entry toll:** the state's official 2026–27 toll policy says ₹130 a day for a car or jeep, but news reports say it was later cut to ₹100 for passenger cars. Expect to pay at the state border.

## Rohtang Pass and the Atal Tunnel in winter

- **Rohtang Pass is closed in winter.** Himachal Tourism says heavy snow closes it in late November, and it reopens around May. The road beyond the Gulaba barrier usually closes from mid-November. Don't plan Rohtang for Christmas or New Year.
- **The Atal Tunnel stays open in principle.** The 9 km tunnel starts 25 km from Manali and leads to Sissu in the Lahaul valley. But after snow, police often stop tourist cars at Nehru Kund or Solang, or allow only 4x4 vehicles beyond the tunnel.
- **Expect long queues.** Around Christmas 2023, more than 28,000 vehicles crossed the tunnel in one day, and police named Manali–Sissu a major bottleneck.

## Driving in snow

Himachal Police's advice for snowy areas:

- Fit anti-skid tyres or carry snow chains, and drive a 4x4 if you can. Most cars stuck in past snowfalls were ordinary cars.
- Use low gears downhill, and don't brake or speed up suddenly.
- Check the brakes, battery, fuel and coolant before you leave.
- Carry warm clothes, blankets, food, water, a first-aid kit, a torch and a power bank.
- Never drive on roads that police have closed, and call 112 if you're stuck.

If you aren't used to mountain driving, take a local taxi from an authorised stand instead.

## Things to do in winter

- **Solang Valley:** 13 km from Manali, with a ropeway and ski slopes. Ski lessons are run by the government mountaineering institute (ABVIMAS) from mid-January.
- **Hadimba Devi Temple:** a wooden pagoda-style temple in a cedar forest near Mall Road, built in 1553.
- **Old Manali:** narrow lanes, cafes and the Manu Temple, which gives Manali its name.
- **Vashisht hot springs:** about 3 km away, with separate baths for men and women, open 7 AM to 1 PM and 2 to 9 PM.
- **Jogini Falls:** a forest walk from Vashisht to a waterfall.
- **Mall Road:** for woollens, Himachali shawls and street food.
- **Naggar Castle:** 22 km away, now a heritage hotel and museum run by Himachal Tourism, near the Roerich Art Gallery.
- **Manali Winter Carnival:** Himachal Tourism says it's held every year in the first week of January. The 2027 dates haven't been announced yet.

## Christmas and New Year crowds

- **Hotels fill up.** In January 2026, when snow finally came on a long weekend, hotels were full and traffic jams stretched up to 8 km. Book early, and stay in registered hotels or homestays.
- **Roads jam on New Year's Eve.** In past years, police divided Manali into sectors, added extra staff, and made buses drop passengers in town and park outside it. Walk around town where you can.
- **Carry photo ID.** Hotels and homestays record every guest's ID.

## Local rules

- **Polythene is banned** in Himachal Pradesh. Carry a cloth bag, and take your waste back with you.
- **Littering is fined.** News reports say the fine is ₹1,500, and every vehicle, including taxis, must carry a garbage bin, with a ₹10,000 fine if it doesn't.
- **No alcohol or smoking in public places.** Drugs are punishable under the NDPS Act.
- **Park only in marked lots,** or your car may be towed.

## Health and safety

- **Height:** Manali is about 2,050 m above sea level, below the height where altitude sickness is common. The Atal Tunnel and Sissu are above 3,000 m, so go slowly and come back down if you get a headache, feel dizzy or sick.
- **Cold:** dress in layers and change out of wet clothes quickly. Shivering, confusion and clumsiness are signs of hypothermia.
- **Heaters:** never sleep with a gas, coal, wood or kerosene heater burning in a closed room. They give off carbon monoxide, which can kill without warning.
- **Help:** call **112** for emergencies, or **108** for an ambulance. The Manali Tourist Information Centre on The Mall is on 01902-252175, and the national tourist helpline is **1363**.

## For foreign visitors

- **No permit for Manali itself.** Foreigners need an Inner Line Permit only for certain border areas of Kinnaur and Spiti. The SDM office in Manali is one of the places that issues them.
- **Visa:** apply for the e-Tourist Visa only at [indianvisaonline.gov.in](https://indianvisaonline.gov.in/evisa/tvoa.html), at least 4 days before you travel. Enter India through an approved airport such as Delhi or Chandigarh; Kullu isn't one.

## Questions

### Will it snow in Manali at Christmas?

It might, but it isn't guaranteed. Christmas 2024 had heavy snow, but December 2025 was almost completely dry, and the first big snow came only in late January 2026. Check the forecast before you go, or plan for January and February.

### Is Rohtang Pass open in December?

No. Rohtang Pass closes because of snow, usually from late November, and reopens around May.

### Is the Atal Tunnel open in winter?

It's open in principle all year, but after snowfall police often stop tourist vehicles at Nehru Kund or Solang, or allow only 4x4 vehicles.

### How cold is Manali in December and January?

Very cold at night. Himachal Tourism says it can fall below −5 °C. Sunny days are milder, but the weather changes quickly, so carry warm layers.

### What is the green tax for Manali?

For vehicles not registered in Himachal Pradesh: ₹200 for a car, ₹100 for a bike and ₹300 for a passenger vehicle, valid for 7 days. Pay it online at rohtangpermits.hp.gov.in.

### How do I reach Manali from Delhi?

Most people take an overnight Volvo bus (about 10–12 hours) or drive about 550–570 km. You can also fly to Chandigarh and drive about 300 km. This winter there are no direct Delhi–Kullu flights.
