**Short answer:** plan 3 days in Manali itself, plus a night each way on the bus from Delhi, so 5 days in all. Day trips go to Solang Valley (13 km) and, when police allow it, through the Atal Tunnel to Sissu. For snow, go in January or February: December is often dry, and Rohtang Pass is closed in winter. For mild weather and open passes, go from late May to June.

## Manali itinerary for 3 days

The most searched plan. It counts days in Manali; add an overnight bus each way from Delhi.

| Day | What to do |
|---|---|
| 1 | Arrive in the morning and rest. Hadimba Devi Temple in its cedar forest, then Old Manali and the Manu Temple. Mall Road in the evening |
| 2 | Leave early for Solang Valley, 13 km away, before the traffic: the ropeway, and snow play in winter. If police allow it, go on through the Atal Tunnel (its south portal is about 25 km from Manali) to Sissu |
| 3 | Vashisht hot springs (about 3 km away) and the forest walk to Jogini Falls. Evening bus back to Delhi |

## Best time to visit Manali

| Season | Months | What to expect |
|---|---|---|
| Spring and summer | March to June | Mild: IMD's averages are about 21–26 °C by day from April to June. Rohtang usually reopens around May |
| Monsoon | July to September | The wettest months: about 200–217 mm of rain in each of July and August. Landslides can block roads |
| Autumn | October to November | Dry and clear, with the least rain of the year (about 25–31 mm a month). Cold nights from November |
| Winter | December to February | Freezing nights (about 0 °C on average in December and February, −1 °C in January). Snow is possible, but not every year |

IMD figures are 1991–2020 averages for Manali. Manali town is about 2,050 m above sea level.

### When does it snow in Manali?

| Month | Rain and snow (IMD average) | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| December | About 39 mm, on about 2 days | Often dry. December 2025 had no snow in Manali on New Year's Day |
| January | About 90 mm, on about 6 days | The best odds. Last winter's first big snow came around 23 January 2026 |
| February | About 114 mm, on about 7 days | Snow is likely, and the ski courses at Solang are running |

Snow comes in storms, not on a calendar, so watch [IMD's forecast for Himachal](https://mausam.imd.gov.in/shimla/) in the week before you go, and keep your dates flexible if snow is the point of the trip. Solang and the road to the tunnel are higher than the town and hold old snow longer.

## Getting to Manali

- **By bus:** HPTDC and HRTC run overnight Volvo and luxury buses from Delhi. Book ahead for Christmas, New Year and long weekends.
- **By air:** Kullu-Manali Airport is small, and flights are often cancelled in bad weather. News reports say direct Delhi flights stopped after 24 October 2026, and the winter schedule to 27 March 2027 links Kullu only with Jaipur and Dehradun. Most people fly to **Chandigarh (IXC)** and drive.
- **By train:** there's no railway to Manali. The nearest big stations are Chandigarh and Kalka, then a long drive. See the [train luggage rules](../baggage/train-luggage-rules.html).

### Green tax and entry tax for outside vehicles

- **Manali green tax:** ₹200 for a car, ₹100 for a bike, ₹300 for a passenger vehicle and ₹500 for a goods vehicle, for any vehicle not registered in Himachal Pradesh. It's valid for 7 days. Pay online up to 9 days ahead at [rohtangpermits.hp.gov.in](https://rohtangpermits.hp.gov.in/ManaliEntry/InformatoryScreen), and carry the printout: the barrier won't let you through without it.
- **Himachal entry toll:** paid at the state border. The state's 2026–27 toll policy lists ₹130 a day for a car, but news reports say it was later cut to ₹100. Check at the barrier.

## Rohtang Pass and the Atal Tunnel in winter

- **Rohtang Pass is closed in winter.** It usually closes in November and reopens around May. This year, news reports say Kullu police stopped tourist traffic beyond Gulaba towards Rohtang after snow in mid-November 2026. Don't plan Rohtang for a December or January trip.
- **The Atal Tunnel stays open in principle,** but after snow police often stop tourist cars at Nehru Kund or Solang, or allow only 4x4 vehicles beyond the tunnel.
- **Expect long queues** at Christmas, New Year and on any weekend after fresh snow.

## Driving in snow

Himachal Police's advice for snowy areas:

- Fit anti-skid tyres or carry snow chains, and use a 4x4 if you can.
- Use low gears downhill, and don't brake or speed up suddenly.
- Carry warm clothes, blankets, food, water, a torch and a charged phone.
- Never drive on a road the police have closed. Call 112 if you're stuck.

If you aren't used to mountain driving, take a local taxi from an authorised stand.

## Christmas and New Year crowds

- **Hotels fill up** when snow falls on a holiday weekend, and traffic jams have stretched for kilometres. Book early, and stay in registered hotels or homestays.
- **Carry photo ID.** Hotels and homestays record every guest's ID.
- **Manali Winter Carnival:** Himachal Tourism says it's held in the first week of January. The 2027 dates haven't been announced.

## Local rules, health and safety

- **Polythene is banned** in Himachal Pradesh. Take your waste back with you. News reports say littering is fined ₹1,500.
- **No alcohol or smoking in public places.** Drugs are punishable under the NDPS Act.
- **Height:** Manali (about 2,050 m) is below where altitude sickness is common, but Sissu is above 3,000 m. Come back down if you get a headache or feel dizzy or sick.
- **Heaters:** never sleep with a gas, coal, wood or kerosene heater burning in a closed room. Carbon monoxide can kill without warning.
- **Help:** 112 for emergencies, 108 for an ambulance, and the national tourist helpline 1363.

## For foreign visitors

- **No permit for Manali itself.** Foreigners need an Inner Line Permit only for certain border areas of Kinnaur and Spiti.
- **Visa:** apply for the e-Tourist Visa only at [indianvisaonline.gov.in](https://indianvisaonline.gov.in/evisa/tvoa.html), and enter India through an approved airport such as Delhi; Kullu isn't one. More in our [visiting India guide](visiting-india.html).

## Questions

### How many days are required for a Manali trip?

Three days in Manali, plus a night on the bus each way from Delhi, so about 5 days in all. Add a day or two for Naggar, Sissu or a spare snow day.

### What is a good 3-day Manali itinerary?

Day 1: Hadimba Devi Temple, Old Manali and Mall Road. Day 2: Solang Valley, and the Atal Tunnel to Sissu if police allow it. Day 3: Vashisht hot springs and Jogini Falls, then the bus home.

### What is the best time to visit Manali for snowfall?

January and February. On IMD's averages, January gets more than twice December's rain and snow, and February more still. Snow isn't guaranteed: December 2025 was almost completely dry.

### What is the best time to visit Manali?

March to June for mild, sunny weather, with Rohtang usually open from around May. December to February for a chance of snow and very cold nights. July to September is the monsoon, when landslides can block roads.

### Will it snow in Manali at Christmas?

It might, but it often doesn't: December averages only about 2 days of rain or snow. Check the forecast in the week before you go.

### Is Rohtang Pass open in December?

No. Rohtang closes with the winter snow, usually in November, and reopens around May.

### Is the Atal Tunnel open in winter?

In principle all year, but after snowfall police often stop tourist vehicles at Nehru Kund or Solang, or allow only 4x4s.

### How do I reach Manali from Delhi?

Most people take an overnight Volvo bus (about 10–12 hours) or drive about 550–570 km. You can also fly to Chandigarh and drive about 300 km. There's no train to Manali.

### How much does a Manali trip cost?

We don't publish a cost, because it depends on your dates, transport and hotel. Fixed costs include the green tax if you drive an outside car (₹200 for a car). Add your own amounts in our [trip budget calculator](../tools/trip-budget-calculator.html).

### What is the green tax for Manali?

For vehicles not registered in Himachal Pradesh: ₹200 for a car, ₹100 for a bike and ₹300 for a passenger vehicle, valid for 7 days. Pay it online at rohtangpermits.hp.gov.in.
