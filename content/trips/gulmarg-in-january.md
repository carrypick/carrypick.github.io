January is when Gulmarg becomes a proper ski resort. The meadow is usually under deep snow, the slopes above Kongdori fill with skiers, and the government ski institute runs its courses all month. It's also bitterly cold and stormy at times, and storms close the upper gondola and the road. This guide covers January's weather, how the month changes from the first week to the last, ski courses, the gondola in deep snow, getting up from Srinagar, and a simple 3-day plan.

## Gulmarg weather in January

Long-term averages for Gulmarg from the India Meteorological Department (IMD), 1981–2010:

| Month | Day | Night | Snow and rain |
|---|---|---|---|
| December | About 4.4 °C | About −4.7 °C | About 104 mm, on about 5 days |
| **January** | **About 0.5 °C** | **About −7.7 °C** | **About 158 mm, on about 8 days** |
| February | About 1.3 °C | About −6.6 °C | About 224 mm, on about 11 days |

- **January days barely get above freezing.** On an average January day, the meadow tops out at about 0.5 °C. The top of the gondola is far colder, and the wind makes it feel colder still.
- **Every January night is far below freezing.** In each of the ten Januaries from 2012 to 2021, IMD recorded a low of −10 °C or colder. The record is −19.8 °C, on 4 January 1991.
- **The snow changes a lot from year to year.** IMD recorded about 383 mm of snow and rain in January 2017, but only about 17 mm in January 2018. A dry January is possible, though the higher slopes usually keep the snow from December.
- **More snow is to come:** February and March are even snowier on average, so the ski season runs well past January.

**Gulmarg weather right now:** check [IMD's Gulmarg forecast](https://city.imd.gov.in/citywx/city_weather_test_try_warnings.php?id=42026) in the week before you go.

## Gulmarg in the first week of January

The New Year crowds are still there until about 3 January, and Gulmarg's Winter Carnival has been held around New Year: last winter it ran from 31 December 2025 to 6 January 2026 (news report; no 2027 dates yet). After the first weekend, the resort gets quieter, and the first January ski course starts on 3 January.

## Gulmarg in the middle of January

Usually the quietest stretch of the month, and often the coldest. A good time for a ski course or a few days on the slopes, with shorter gondola queues. Last year, January stayed dry in the valley until a big snowfall on 23 January 2026, so the snow can arrive late.

## Gulmarg in the last week of January

**Republic Day** is on **Tuesday 26 January 2027**, so many people take Monday off for a 4-day trip, and hotels and gondola slots fill up. Chillai Kalan, the coldest 40 days, ends around 30–31 January. Last year both 23 and 27 January brought heavy snow that shut Srinagar's airport and the highway for a time, so keep a spare day.

## Skiing in Gulmarg in January

The government's Indian Institute of Skiing and Mountaineering (IISM) runs 14-day snow skiing courses in Gulmarg for ages 10 to 40. Its 2026–27 schedule (booking opened on 12 November 2026):

| Course | Dates | Levels |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 20 December 2026 to 2 January 2027 | Basic, intermediate and advanced |
| 2 | 3 to 16 January 2027 | Basic, intermediate and advanced |
| 3 | 17 to 30 January 2027 | Basic, intermediate and advanced |
| 4 | 31 January to 13 February 2027 | Skiing and snowboarding |

Later courses run until 13 March. Book only on [iismgulmarg.in](https://iismgulmarg.in/Course_Schedule.html), and check the fees and seats there.

- **Not doing a course?** You can hire skis, boots and a registered instructor for a day in Gulmarg. Ask your hotel to suggest one, and agree what's included first.
- **Where to ski:** beginners stay on the gentle slopes near the base and around Kongdori. The open bowls below Apharwat are for strong skiers with a guide.
- **Khelo India Winter Games:** the 2026 snow events were held in Gulmarg from 23 to 26 February 2026. The 2027 dates hadn't been announced when we checked.

## The Gulmarg Gondola in January

- **Phase 1** runs from Gulmarg up to **Kongdori**, at about 3,100 m. It's the one that runs most days, even in winter.
- **Phase 2** goes on to about **3,980 m on Apharwat**. It's the first to stop in a snowstorm, strong wind or an avalanche warning. Go on your first clear morning.
- **Book online only** at the J&K Cable Car Corporation's official site, [etickets.jammukashmircablecar.com](https://etickets.jammukashmircablecar.com/). The corporation asks tourists not to buy from touts. We don't show fares: the official site has them.
- **At the top,** wear every layer you have, plus gloves, a cap and sunglasses.

## Gulmarg trip plan for January: 3 days

**Day 1: Srinagar to Gulmarg**

1. **Leave Srinagar by mid-morning** for the drive of about 52 km via Tangmarg. Your driver may need to fit snow chains at Tangmarg.
2. **Walk the meadow** and see St Mary's Church and the Maharani temple in the snow.
3. **Sledging** near the gondola base. Agree the fare and the route first.

**Day 2: the gondola and the slopes**

1. **Take Phase 1 early** to Kongdori, before the queues.
2. **If the weather is clear,** go on up Phase 2 to Apharwat for the views.
3. **Afternoon ski lesson** on the beginner slopes, or snow walks towards Khilanmarg if the path is safe.

**Day 3: a spare day**

1. **Use it for Phase 2** if it was closed on day 2, or for more skiing.
2. **Drive back to Srinagar** by early afternoon, before the road ices over.

## Getting to Gulmarg in January

- **By air:** fly to Srinagar (SXR), then drive about 57 km from the airport through Tangmarg. Snow can close the airport for a day, as it did on 23 January 2026.
- **By train:** Vande Bharat trains run from Jammu Tawi and Katra to Srinagar, and Indian Railways says they're built for −20 °C. They kept running on 23 January 2026 but didn't on 27 and 28 January (news reports). Check NTES or IRCTC on the day.
- **The Tangmarg–Gulmarg road:** after snow, usually only 4x4 vehicles or cars with chains fitted by registered vendors are allowed. The road can close for a few hours during heavy snow.
- **Stay, don't day-trip,** in January. The drive, the chains and the short daylight make a day trip from Srinagar tiring, and a night or two gives you a clear morning for Phase 2.

## What to wear in Gulmarg in January

What should I wear? Try the [what-to-wear helper](../tools/what-to-wear.html#kashmir) for Gulmarg and Srinagar temperatures by month.

January in Gulmarg is serious cold. Dress like you're going skiing, even if you're not:

| Layer | What to wear |
|---|---|
| 1. Next to your skin | Thermal top and bottom, plus a spare set |
| 2. Middle | A thick fleece, or a fleece plus a light down jacket |
| 3. Outside | A waterproof, hooded ski or snow jacket, and waterproof trousers |
| 4. Extras | Woollen cap or balaclava, neck warmer, insulated waterproof gloves, two pairs of woollen socks |

- **Boots:** insulated waterproof boots with a deep grip. Ski boots are hired with the skis.
- **Sunglasses or goggles, sunscreen and lip balm.** The sun on fresh snow is strong at this height.
- **Renting:** shops in Tangmarg and Gulmarg rent snow coats and gumboots by the day.

## Safety in the cold

- **Never sleep with a heater, charcoal or a kangri burning in a closed room.** Carbon monoxide can kill without warning.
- **Avalanches:** after heavy snow, the authorities may warn of avalanches on the upper slopes. Don't ski or walk off the marked slopes at Apharwat without a guide, and obey closures.
- **Frostbite:** numb, white fingers or toes are a warning sign. Get warm slowly, and don't rub them.
- **Batteries die fast in the cold.** Carry a power bank and keep your phone in an inside pocket. Prepaid SIMs from other states usually don't work in Jammu and Kashmir.

## For foreign visitors

- **Read your government's advice.** The UK's travel advice (updated 7 October 2026, still current on 24 November) is against all travel to Jammu and Kashmir, naming Gulmarg, Srinagar, Pahalgam, Sonamarg and the Jammu–Srinagar highway. The US advice says "Do Not Travel" to Jammu and Kashmir, except eastern Ladakh and Leh. Travel against this advice can make your travel insurance invalid.

## Questions

### Is January a good time to visit Gulmarg?

Yes, it's one of the best months for snow and skiing. IMD's averages are about 0.5 °C by day and −8 °C at night, with about 158 mm of snow and rain. It's very cold, and storms can close the upper gondola or the road for a time.

### Will there be snow in Gulmarg in January 2027?

Very likely, though the amount changes a lot: IMD recorded about 383 mm in January 2017 but only about 17 mm in January 2018. Check the forecast a week before you go.

### Gulmarg in the first week or the last week of January?

The first week has the New Year crowds until about 3 January and the winter carnival. The last week has the Republic Day long weekend and is busy, but often snowy. The middle of January is the quietest.

### Can I learn skiing in Gulmarg in January?

Yes. IISM's 14-day courses at Gulmarg start on 3 January, 17 January and 31 January 2027. Book on iismgulmarg.in. You can also hire a registered instructor for a day.

### Does the Gulmarg Gondola run in January?

Yes, but Phase 2 to Apharwat often stops in snowstorms, strong wind or avalanche warnings. Book only on the official site, and keep a spare day.

### How cold is Gulmarg in January?

About 0.5 °C by day and −7.7 °C at night on IMD's averages. Each January from 2012 to 2021 reached −10 °C or colder, and the record is −19.8 °C.

### When are the Khelo India Winter Games 2027?

Not announced yet. The 2026 snow events were held in Gulmarg from 23 to 26 February 2026.
