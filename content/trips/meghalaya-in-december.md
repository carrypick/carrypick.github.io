Meghalaya in December is cool, dry and clear. The monsoon that makes Cherrapunji famous is long over, the rivers at Dawki run glass-clear, and Shillong is busy with Christmas lights. Nights are cold, and the days are short. This guide covers the weather, the truth about snowfall, a 4-day and 5-day itinerary, Christmas and New Year, the guest registration rule, getting there and what to wear.

## Meghalaya weather in December and January

Long-term averages from the India Meteorological Department (IMD, 1991–2020):

| Month | Shillong day | Shillong night | Sohra (Cherrapunji) day | Sohra night | Rain in Shillong |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| December | About 16 °C | About 7 °C | About 19 °C | About 8 °C | About 7 mm |
| January | About 15 °C | About 6 °C | About 17 °C | About 7 °C | About 13 mm |
| February | About 17.5 °C | About 7.5 °C | About 19 °C | About 9 °C | About 15 mm |

- **December is the driest month.** Shillong gets rain on less than one day in an average December, and so does Sohra, one of the wettest places on earth in the monsoon.
- **Nights can freeze.** The lowest December reading on record in Shillong is −3.3 °C. In January it is −0.9 °C.
- **The days are short.** In December the sun rises around 6 AM and sets around 4:30–4:40 PM in Shillong. Plan sightseeing for the morning and early afternoon.
- **Waterfalls are thinner.** Nohkalikai and the Seven Sisters falls have less water than after the monsoon, but the views are clearer.
- **Shillong is high.** The city is about 1,500–1,950 m above sea level, and Shillong Peak about 1,966 m. Sohra is about 1,430 m.

## Does it snow in Meghalaya?

- **No, Shillong doesn't get snow.** We found no record of real snowfall in the IMD data or official pages.
- **What people call "snow" is usually hail.** On 22 December 2021, Shillong turned white after a hailstorm, and an IMD scientist said it was small hailstones, not snow.
- **Frosty nights happen.** With record lows below 0 °C, you may see frost on the coldest clear mornings, but it isn't common.
- **Want snow?** Choose [Kashmir](kashmir-in-december.html), [Manali](manali.html) or [Shimla](shimla-in-december.html). In the east, Sikkim's high passes and Sandakphu near [Darjeeling](darjeeling-in-december.html) get snow most winters.

## Meghalaya itinerary: 4 days

Most visitors base themselves in Shillong, with a night in Sohra. Hire a taxi for the trip: shared cabs don't reach most viewpoints. Start every day early, because it gets dark before 5 PM.

**Day 1: Guwahati to Shillong**

1. **Drive up from Guwahati,** about 100 km and 3 hours on NH 40. Stop at Umiam Lake on the way.
2. **Ward's Lake and Police Bazar** in the evening, for Shillong's Christmas lights and food.

**Day 2: Sohra (Cherrapunji)**

1. **Drive to Sohra,** about 53 km from Shillong.
2. **Nohkalikai Falls,** **Seven Sisters Falls** viewpoint and **Mawsmai Cave.**
3. **Stay the night in Sohra** if you plan to walk to the root bridges next morning.

**Day 3: Living root bridges, or Dawki and Mawlynnong**

1. **Dawki,** about 80 km from Shillong, for a boat on the clear Umngot river. Meghalaya Tourism lists visiting hours of 6 AM to 6 PM.
2. **Mawlynnong,** about 76 km from Shillong, a village known for being clean, with a living root bridge nearby at Riwai.
3. **Drive back to Shillong** before dark.

**Day 4: Shillong and back to Guwahati**

1. **Shillong Peak** in the morning for the view, then **Elephant Falls.**
2. **Drive down to Guwahati** for an evening flight or train.

## Meghalaya itinerary: 5 days

Add one day in Sohra for **Nongriat and the double-decker living root bridge.** The walk goes down about 3,000 steps from Tyrna and back up again, so it takes most of a day. Start early, carry water, and wear shoes with a good grip. Meghalaya's living root bridges are on UNESCO's Tentative List for World Heritage (since 2022).

## Christmas and New Year in Shillong

- **Christmas is Shillong's biggest festival.** Churches and streets are lit up, and Police Bazar and the cathedral areas are busy on Christmas Eve.
- **Winter Tales at Ward's Lake** runs from 17 to 19 December 2026, according to Meghalaya Tourism, and starts the Christmas season.
- **Me·Gong Festival in Tura** (Garo Hills) runs on 4 and 5 December 2026.
- **The Cherry Blossom Festival is over** for this year: it was on 20 and 21 November 2026.
- **Book early.** This year Christmas is on a Friday, and so is New Year's Day (1 January 2027), so both are long weekends. Hotels, homestays and taxis in Shillong and Sohra fill up.
- **Expect traffic** on the Guwahati–Shillong road and in Shillong around Christmas and New Year. Leave early.

## Registration and permits

- **Your hotel or homestay registers you.** Since June 2025 the Meghalaya government has told homestays, resorts and landlords to register every visitor through the Meghalaya Tourism app. Stay at registered places, and share your ID when asked.
- **Carry a government photo ID.** Meghalaya Tourism says travellers must carry government-issued ID at entry points.
- **Inner Line Permit:** we found no official page saying Meghalaya needs an ILP, but entry rules have been discussed in recent years. Check [meghalayatourism.in](https://www.meghalayatourism.in/notices/) before you travel.
- **Foreign visitors:** Meghalaya Tourism tells international travellers to register with the authorities if required. Ask your hotel, and check the latest rules with the state before you go.

## Getting to Meghalaya

From Meghalaya Tourism:

- **By air:** Guwahati (GAU) has the most flights and is about 100 km (3 hours) from Shillong. Shillong's own airport at Umroi is 35 km from the city, with only a few flights.
- **By train:** the nearest big railway station is Guwahati. From there, go up by road.
- **By road:** taxis and shared cabs run from Guwahati to Shillong. State and private buses reach the Shillong ISBT.
- **By helicopter:** a state-supported helicopter service links Guwahati and Shillong on most days. Seats are few, so check the latest schedule with Meghalaya Tourism.
- **Distances from Shillong:** Sohra about 53 km, Mawlynnong about 76 km and Dawki about 80 km.

## Things to do in winter

- **Shillong:** Ward's Lake, Police Bazar, Shillong Peak, Elephant Falls and Umiam Lake.
- **Sohra (Cherrapunji):** Nohkalikai Falls, Seven Sisters Falls, Mawsmai Cave and the views over Bangladesh.
- **Living root bridges:** the double-decker at Nongriat and the single bridge at Riwai, near Mawlynnong.
- **Dawki:** a boat on the clear Umngot river, best in the dry months.

## What to wear in Meghalaya in December

What should I wear? Try the [what-to-wear helper](../tools/what-to-wear.html#meghalaya) for day and night temperatures and an outfit list by month.

Days are cool, and nights in Shillong are cold, sometimes near freezing.

- **Layers:** a thermal or full-sleeve top, a sweater or fleece, and a warm jacket for evenings.
- **For early starts and Shillong Peak:** a woollen cap, gloves and a muffler.
- **For ladies:** jeans or leggings with a kurta or top, a warm cardigan or jacket, and a stole. Dress modestly in churches and villages.
- **Shoes:** walking shoes with a good grip for the root-bridge steps, caves and wet rocks at the falls.
- **For Dawki boats and caves:** clothes that can get a little wet, and a small towel.
- **Sunscreen and sunglasses:** the sun is strong on clear days.

## Health and safety

- **Never sleep with a heater or coal fire in a closed room.** It gives off carbon monoxide, which can kill without warning.
- **Don't drive the hill roads after dark** if you can avoid it. Fog can come down quickly in the evening.
- **Take care at the root bridges and falls.** Steps and rocks are slippery, and the climb back from Nongriat is steep.
- **Respect village rules.** Follow local signs, and ask before you photograph people or homes.

## Questions

### What is the weather in Meghalaya in December?

Cool, dry and clear. IMD's long-term averages for Shillong are about 16 °C by day and 7 °C at night, with rain on less than one day in the month. Sohra (Cherrapunji) is a little warmer, at about 19 °C and 8 °C.

### What is the temperature in Meghalaya in December?

About 16 °C in the day and 7 °C at night in Shillong (IMD averages, 1991–2020). The coldest December night on record was −3.3 °C. January is a little colder.

### Does it snow in Meghalaya in December?

No. Shillong doesn't get snow. The white streets in December 2021 were hail, according to IMD. You may see frost on the coldest mornings.

### Is Meghalaya good in December end?

Yes. The weather is dry and clear, Dawki's river is at its clearest, and Shillong celebrates Christmas in a big way. It's also peak season, so book hotels and taxis early, and plan for short days.

### How many days are enough for Meghalaya?

Four days cover Shillong, Sohra (Cherrapunji), and Dawki with Mawlynnong. Add a fifth day for the walk down to the double-decker living root bridge at Nongriat.

### Do I need a permit to visit Meghalaya?

Indian tourists don't need a permit that we could find on official pages, but carry a government photo ID. Your hotel or homestay must register you on the Meghalaya Tourism app. Foreign visitors should check the latest rules with Meghalaya Tourism.

### How do I reach Shillong from Guwahati?

By road, about 100 km and 3 hours on NH 40, by taxi, shared cab or bus. A small helicopter service also links Guwahati and Shillong. Shillong's own airport at Umroi has only a few flights.

### What should I wear in Meghalaya in December?

Layers: a thermal or full-sleeve top, a sweater or fleece, and a warm jacket, plus a cap and gloves for cold mornings. Wear shoes with a good grip for the root bridges and falls.

### Meghalaya or Darjeeling in December?

Both are cool and clear. Darjeeling has Kanchenjunga views and the toy train, and is colder. Meghalaya has the living root bridges, waterfalls, Dawki's clear river and Christmas in Shillong. See [Darjeeling in December](darjeeling-in-december.html).
