Tickets for the Republic Day parade on Kartavya Path, New Delhi, are sold only by the **Ministry of Defence**: on its Aamantran website and app, and at a few ticket counters in Delhi. Republic Day 2027 is on a Tuesday.

**The 2027 ticket sale is not announced yet.** When we checked on 16 November 2026, the Defence Ministry's [Republic Day 2027 page](https://rashtraparv.mod.gov.in/events/republic-day-2027) said its seating plan, route map and rules "will be available soon". Below is how it worked last year, taken from the Ministry's own press releases, so you know what to expect. We'll update this page when the 2027 details come out.

## 2027 dates and prices: not announced yet

| Event | 2027 | Last year (2026) |
|---|---|---|
| Ticket sale | Not announced yet | 5 to 14 January, from 9 AM each day until that day's quota ran out |
| Parade | Not announced yet | 26 January: ₹100 and ₹20 |
| Beating Retreat full dress rehearsal | Not announced yet | 28 January: ₹20 |
| Beating Retreat | Not announced yet | 29 January: ₹100 |
| Parade full dress rehearsal | Not announced yet | 23 January: free passes, booked on 15 and 16 January |

**When to look for the 2027 sale:** the Ministry announced the 2026 sale on 3 January 2026, two days before it began. In 2025, the sale began on 2 January. So keep checking from late December.

## How to book online on Aamantran

1. Open the Defence Ministry's [Aamantran website](https://www.aamantran.mod.gov.in) or the Aamantran app. In 2026 the app was on the iOS App Store and the Gov.in App Store for Android.
2. Try at **9 AM** on a sale day. Each day has a quota, and in 2026 tickets were sold from 9 AM until that day's quota ran out. If you miss out, try again the next morning.
3. Choose the event and the ticket, and complete the booking.
4. Keep the ticket on your phone or print it. The Ministry's advice says either a printed or a digital copy is fine.

The Ministry's press release named only two ways to buy: the Aamantran website and the official counters. Don't pay anyone else for a ticket. When we checked on 16 November 2026, the Aamantran website was not opening (it showed error 503), so check again once the sale is announced.

## Offline ticket counters (last year)

In 2026, you could also buy tickets at **six counters in New Delhi**, from 5 to 14 January. They were open from **10 AM to 1 PM** and from **2 PM to 5 PM**. You had to show an original photo ID.

| Counter | Where exactly |
|---|---|
| Sena Bhawan | Near Gate No. 5, inside the boundary wall |
| Shastri Bhawan | Near Gate No. 3, inside the boundary wall |
| Jantar Mantar | Main gate, inside the boundary wall |
| Parliament House | Reception |
| Rajiv Chowk Metro Station | D Block, near Gates 3 and 4 |
| Kashmere Gate Metro Station | Concourse level, near Gate 8 |

The counters change from year to year. In 2025 there were five, including one at Pragati Maidan. Check the 2027 list when it comes out.

## ID you need

- **To buy at a counter:** an original photo ID card, such as an Aadhaar card, Voter ID, driving licence, PAN card, passport, or an ID card from the central or state government.
- **To get in:** the Ministry says entry is allowed only with a valid government photo ID. If you bought at a counter, carry the same ID you showed there.

## Parade day: gates, timings and getting there

This is how it worked on 26 January 2026:

- **Gates usually open at about 7 AM**, the Ministry's 2026 advice said. Seats are first come, first served. An enclosure closes once it's full, and late arrivals may not be let in.
- **The parade began at 10:30 AM** and lasted about 90 minutes.
- **Enclosures were named after rivers**, such as Ganga, Yamuna, Kaveri and Narmada. The seating plan and an arrival plan with the entry routes were posted on the Ministry's [Rashtraparv site](https://rashtraparv.mod.gov.in).
- **The Delhi Metro started at 3 AM**, and ticket holders rode free by showing their ticket. Free Park & Ride buses ran from JLN Stadium and the Palika Bazaar parking area.
- **There's no parking for visitors near the venue**, so use the metro or other public transport.
- **Keep your phone on silent.** The Ministry asks visitors not to use their phones during the national anthem and other solemn moments.

## Beating Retreat and its rehearsal

Beating Retreat is the evening band ceremony at **Vijay Chowk** that closes the Republic Day celebrations. In 2026 the main ceremony was on 29 January (₹100 tickets) and its full dress rehearsal on 28 January (₹20). Tickets were sold in the same sale, on Aamantran and at the same counters.

- **Gates usually open at about 3 PM**, the 2026 advice said.
- The ID rule and the list of banned items were the same as for the parade.
- Enclosures were named after Indian instruments, such as Sitar, Tabla, Santoor and Veena.

## Parade full dress rehearsal: free passes

In 2026, passes for the parade's full dress rehearsal on Kartavya Path, on **23 January**, were **free**. They were booked on the Aamantran website or app on **15 and 16 January**, from 9 AM until each day's quota ran out. They were announced on 12 January, a week after the main sale began, so look out for them in mid-January 2027.

## What you can't take in

The Defence Ministry's list of banned items for 2026 was long. It was the same for the parade and Beating Retreat:

| Type | Not allowed |
|---|---|
| Bags | Any bag or briefcase |
| Food and drink | Eatables, water bottles, thermos flasks, cans, alcohol |
| Electronics | Power banks, mobile chargers, headphones, iPads, iPods, laptops, cameras, handycams, binoculars, radios, remote car-lock keys |
| Sharp things | Knives, blades, razors, scissors, screwdrivers, swords |
| Smoking and fire | Cigarettes, bidis, matchboxes, lighters, crackers, anything flammable |
| Other things | Coins, paper, umbrellas, perfume, sprays, laser lights, toy guns, wires |

Banners and posters with political or promotional messages aren't allowed either. Umbrellas are banned, so the Ministry suggests a raincoat if rain is forecast.

Since you can't take a bag, go with just your ticket and ID in your pockets. If you drive to a Park & Ride stop, note that remote car-lock keys are on the list.

## What to wear on a January morning

Gates open at about 7 AM and the parade ends around noon, so you'll sit outdoors for hours. The Ministry's advice says January mornings in Delhi can be chilly. It asks you to wear warm, comfortable clothes and shoes you can walk in. You can't carry a bag, so wear your warm layers rather than carrying them.

## Questions

### How do I book Republic Day parade tickets for 2027?

The 2027 sale isn't announced yet (last checked 16 November 2026). Last year, tickets were sold on the Defence Ministry's Aamantran website and app, and at six counters in New Delhi, from 5 to 14 January.

### What is the Republic Day parade ticket price?

The 2027 prices aren't announced yet. In 2026, parade tickets cost ₹100 and ₹20. Beating Retreat cost ₹100, and its full dress rehearsal ₹20.

### When will Republic Day 2027 ticket booking start?

It isn't announced yet. In 2026, the sale ran from 5 to 14 January, and the Ministry announced it on 3 January. In 2025, it began on 2 January.

### Can I buy Republic Day parade tickets offline?

Yes, last year you could. In 2026, six counters sold tickets, including at Sena Bhawan, Jantar Mantar and the Rajiv Chowk and Kashmere Gate metro stations, from 10 AM to 1 PM and 2 PM to 5 PM. You had to show an original photo ID.

### How do I get Beating Retreat tickets?

They're sold in the same sale as the parade tickets, on Aamantran and at the same counters. In 2026, the ceremony on 29 January cost ₹100 and its full dress rehearsal on 28 January cost ₹20.

### Is the parade full dress rehearsal free?

It was in 2026. Free passes for the rehearsal on 23 January were booked on Aamantran on 15 and 16 January.

### Which ID do I need for the Republic Day parade?

An original government photo ID, such as an Aadhaar card, Voter ID, driving licence, PAN card or passport. If you bought at a counter, carry the same ID you showed there.

### What is not allowed at the Republic Day parade?

Bags, food, water bottles, thermos flasks, power banks, chargers, headphones, cameras, umbrellas, coins, paper, perfume, lighters, cigarettes and sharp things, among others. That was the Defence Ministry's 2026 list.

### What time do the gates open for the Republic Day parade?

The Ministry's 2026 advice said gates usually open at about 7 AM, and the 2026 parade began at 10:30 AM. For Beating Retreat, gates usually open at about 3 PM.

### Can I buy a VIP ticket for the Republic Day parade?

The 2026 sale had only ₹100 and ₹20 parade tickets. Other seats went to invited guests: the Ministry invited about 10,000 special guests in 2026.
