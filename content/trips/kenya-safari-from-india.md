Mumbai has direct flights to Nairobi, about 6 hours, and Indians apply for an eTA online. This guide covers the eTA, flights, a 7-day safari plan, park fees, the dry and rainy seasons, health rules, getting around and food for vegetarians.

## Kenya eTA for Indians

Indians need an **Electronic Travel Authorisation (eTA)** before they travel to Kenya. Kenya's Directorate of Immigration Services says all visitors, including infants and children, must have an approved eTA before the start of their journey, and India isn't on its list of exempt countries (read 23 Nov 2026).

- **Apply only on the official site,** [etakenya.go.ke](https://etakenya.go.ke/), or the official "Kenya Travel Authorisation" mobile app. Other websites that offer the eTA aren't the government's.
- **Each traveller needs their own eTA,** children too. For a family or group, choose "Apply as a Group".
- **Fee:** the normal eTA starts at **US$30**, and the urgent (expedited) one costs **US$100 more**, says the eTA site's FAQ.
- **Processing time:** generally within **3 working days (72 hours)**, but it can take longer, says the FAQ. The expedited eTA is processed immediately.
- **Travel only after approval.** Print the approved eTA from your email, or save it on your phone or in the app, and show it when you leave and arrive.

### What documents do you need?

The eTA site's FAQ lists, usually:

- **A passport** valid for at least 6 months, with at least one blank page.
- **A selfie or passport-style photo.**
- **Your email and phone number.**
- **Your travel plan,** with arrival and departure dates.
- **Your hotel or lodge booking confirmation.** On a safari package, ask your operator for the booking letter for each night.
- **A credit or debit card** to pay.

### How long can you stay?

The eTA site's pages we read don't state the length of stay for an Indian passport in plain words, so check the approval you receive. The immigration officer decides on arrival.

**Changing planes in Nairobi?** Passengers in transit who don't leave the airport are exempt from the eTA, says the eTA site.

## Flights from India to Kenya

- **There are direct flights from Mumbai.** Kenya Airways and IndiGo fly from Mumbai to Nairobi (Jomo Kenyatta International Airport), say the airport's airline lists (Wikipedia, read 23 Nov 2026).
- **From other Indian cities,** fly one-stop via Mumbai, or through the Gulf: Dubai (Emirates), Abu Dhabi (Etihad) or Doha.
- **Air India doesn't fly to Nairobi,** so we don't show it below.
- **A direct flight from Mumbai takes about 6 hours.** Nairobi is about 4,500 km from Mumbai in a straight line.
- **Time difference:** India is 2 hours 30 minutes ahead of Kenya all year; Kenya doesn't change its clocks.
- **Into the Masai Mara:** small planes fly from Nairobi's Wilson Airport to airstrips in the reserve, or you can drive, about 5 to 6 hours on a safari tour.
- **Baggage:** small safari planes have low baggage limits and often ask for soft bags. Check with your operator, and see the [international baggage rules](../baggage/international.html).

## A 7-day Kenya safari itinerary: Nairobi, Masai Mara, Lake Nakuru and Amboseli

Most first safaris from India use a week, with a driver-guide in a pop-up-roof safari vehicle. This plan uses 7 days.

| Day | Plan | Evening |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Land in Nairobi. Nairobi National Park: lions and rhinos with the city skyline behind | Nairobi |
| 2 | Drive to the Masai Mara via the Great Rift Valley viewpoint (about 5 to 6 hours) | Afternoon game drive |
| 3 | A full day of game drives in the Masai Mara: the big cats, elephants, and in season the migration at the Mara River | Camp or lodge |
| 4 | An early morning game drive, or a hot-air balloon ride at sunrise, then visit a Maasai village | Camp or lodge |
| 5 | Drive to Lake Nakuru National Park: flamingos, white rhinos and Rothschild's giraffes; or Lake Naivasha for a boat ride | Nakuru or Naivasha |
| 6 | Drive to Amboseli National Park (a long day) | Sunset over Kilimanjaro on a clear day |
| 7 | A morning game drive in Amboseli, then drive back to Nairobi and fly home | |

- **More time?** Add Tsavo East and West, or end with a few beach days at Diani or Mombasa on the coast.
- **Short of time?** Fly Nairobi to the Masai Mara and back and spend 3 nights there.
- **Game drives start at dawn,** when the animals are most active. You'll spend long hours on bumpy roads.

## Park fees: Kenya Wildlife Service and the Masai Mara

Kenya's national parks charge a conservation fee for each person. For non-residents, Kenya Wildlife Service's 2025 conservation fee table (read 23 Nov 2026) lists:

| Park | Non-resident adult | Non-resident child or student |
|---|---|---|
| Amboseli and Lake Nakuru National Parks | US$90 | US$45 |
| Nairobi National Park | US$80 | US$40 |
| Tsavo East and Tsavo West National Parks | US$80 | US$40 |

- **Check the full table** on [KWS's website](https://www.kws.go.ke/parks/) for other parks, vehicle charges and camping.
- **The Masai Mara is not a KWS park.** It's the Maasai Mara National Reserve, run by the county government. We couldn't find its entry fees on an official page, so ask your safari operator to show the official fee, and whether it's included in your package.
- **On a package, park fees are usually included,** but check, because they add up to a big part of a safari.

## Kenya weather by month, and the best time to go

Kenya is on the equator, so it's warm by day all year, with cool nights in the highlands. The seasons are dry and rainy, not summer and winter. Long-term averages from the Kenya Meteorological Department, published by the World Meteorological Organization (Narok is the nearest town to the Masai Mara):

| Month | Nairobi (day / night) | Narok (Masai Mara) | Mombasa (coast) |
|---|---|---|---|
| January | About 25 °C / 12 °C, dry | About 27 °C / 9 °C | About 32 °C / 23 °C |
| April | About 24 °C / 14 °C, the wettest month | About 25 °C / 12 °C, wet | About 31 °C / 24 °C, rain begins |
| July | About 21 °C / 10 °C, cool and dry | About 22 °C / 9 °C, dry | About 28 °C / 20 °C |
| October | About 25 °C / 13 °C | About 26 °C / 10 °C, dry | About 30 °C / 22 °C |
| November | About 23 °C / 13 °C, the short rains | About 25 °C / 10 °C | About 31 °C / 23 °C |
| December | About 23 °C / 13 °C | About 26 °C / 9 °C | About 32 °C / 23 °C |

- **Late June to October is the main safari season:** dry, with short grass that makes animals easy to see. It's also when the wildebeest migration is usually in the Masai Mara, but its timing depends on the rains each year.
- **January and February are dry too,** and a good, quieter time to go.
- **April and May are the long rains:** Nairobi gets its most rain in April. Some camps close and roads get muddy, but prices are often lower.
- **November brings the short rains,** usually showers rather than all-day rain.
- **Nights and early mornings are cold** on game drives at any time of year. Pack a warm layer.

## Getting around Kenya

- **On safari, your operator drives you** in a safari vehicle with a pop-up roof. Self-driving in the parks isn't a good idea on a first trip.
- **Small planes** link Nairobi's Wilson Airport with the Masai Mara, Amboseli and the coast, and save long drives.
- **Between Nairobi and Mombasa,** the Madaraka Express train is a comfortable option.
- **In Nairobi,** use ride-hailing apps or hotel taxis, especially after dark.
- **Kenya drives on the left,** like India.

## Food for Indian travellers: veg and Jain tips

Kenya has a long-established Indian community, so Indian food is easier to find than you may expect:

- **Indian restaurants** are common in Nairobi and Mombasa, and many safari lodges and camps serve Indian dishes or will cook vegetarian food.
- **Tell your operator** when you book if you're vegetarian, vegan or Jain, so each lodge knows in advance.
- **Easy veg choices:** rice, chapati (Kenya has its own chapati), beans, lentils, sukuma wiki (greens), and vegetable curries.
- **Jain travellers:** carry some ready-to-eat food for long drives, and confirm no onion and garlic with each lodge.
- **Drink bottled or filtered water,** and check whether your lodge's tap water is safe.

## Health: yellow fever and malaria

- **Yellow fever card for coming home:** India's official e-Visa site says travellers arriving in India from yellow fever affected countries must carry a yellow fever vaccination card, or may be quarantined for 6 days on arrival. We couldn't open the Health Ministry's current country list on 23 Nov 2026, so ask a government yellow fever vaccination centre whether Kenya needs it, before you go. The vaccine is given at authorised centres in India.
- **Malaria:** ask your doctor about malaria tablets, use insect repellent, and wear long sleeves in the evenings.
- **Health insurance:** buy travel insurance with medical cover, including evacuation from remote parks.

## Phone, SIM and eSIM in Kenya

You have three ways to stay online in Kenya:

- **Roaming on your own SIM:** the easiest, but often the costliest. Check your mobile company's international pack before you fly.
- **A local SIM card:** sold at Nairobi airport and phone shops. You need your passport to register it.
- **An eSIM:** a data plan you buy online and install on your phone before you go. Your phone must support eSIM and must not be locked to one network.

Coverage in the parks is patchy, and many camps have Wi-Fi only in the main area.

Install the eSIM at home over Wi-Fi and switch it on when you land. Most travel eSIMs give data only, with no local number, so keep your home SIM on for calls and bank OTP texts (roaming charges may apply).

## Money and rules to know

- **The currency is the Kenyan shilling (KSh, KES).** Cards work in Nairobi hotels and malls. Mobile money (M-Pesa) is used everywhere by locals.
- **Carry some US dollars** in clean, newer notes for tips on safari, and shillings for small purchases.
- **Tipping your driver-guide** and the camp staff is customary on safari. Ask your operator what's normal.
- **Cash from India:** RBI lets you buy up to US$3,000 in foreign currency notes per trip, and you can carry up to ₹25,000 in Indian notes. Put the rest on a forex card. See [how much cash you can carry abroad](../baggage/customs/cash-and-currency.html).
- **Plastic bags are banned in Kenya.** Don't pack shopping bags in your luggage.
- **Plugs:** Kenya uses 240 V and the three square-pin plug used in the UK, different from India's. Read our [power bank and travel adapter guide](../guides/power-bank-and-travel-adapter-guide.html).
- **Ask before you photograph people,** including the Maasai.

## What to wear on a Kenya safari

- **Neutral colours:** khaki, beige, olive and brown. Avoid bright white, which shows dust, and dark blue and black, which attract tsetse flies.
- **Layers:** a fleece or warm jacket for early-morning game drives, which are cold, and light shirts for the afternoon heat.
- **Long sleeves and long trousers** in the evenings, against mosquitoes.
- **A hat, sunglasses and sunscreen:** the equator sun is strong.
- **Comfortable closed shoes,** and a soft bag if you're taking a small plane.
- **Binoculars** make a big difference on game drives.

## Questions

### Do Indians need a visa for Kenya?

Indians need an eTA (electronic travel authorisation), applied for online before they travel on etakenya.go.ke. All visitors need one, including children, and India isn't on the exempt list.

### What is the Kenya eTA fee for Indians?

The normal eTA starts at US$30, and an urgent one processed immediately costs US$100 more, says Kenya's official eTA site.

### How long does the Kenya eTA take?

Generally within 3 working days (72 hours), says the eTA site, but it can take longer. Apply well before you fly, and travel only after it's approved.

### Is there visa on arrival in Kenya for Indians?

No. Kenya's eTA must be approved before you start your journey. Apply on the official site.

### Are there direct flights from India to Kenya?

Yes. Kenya Airways and IndiGo fly direct from Mumbai to Nairobi, about 6 hours. From other cities, connect in Mumbai or the Gulf.

### What is the best time for a Masai Mara safari from India?

Late June to October, the long dry season, when the wildebeest migration is usually in the Masai Mara. January and February are also dry. Avoid April and May, the long rains, if you can.

### How many days are enough for a Kenya safari?

A week covers Nairobi, the Masai Mara, Lake Nakuru and Amboseli. With 4 or 5 days, focus on the Masai Mara.

### What are the park fees for Kenya safari?

Kenya Wildlife Service's 2025 table lists US$90 per non-resident adult for Amboseli and Lake Nakuru and US$80 for Nairobi and Tsavo. The Masai Mara is run by the county, with its own fees: ask your operator for the official amount.

### What is the time difference between India and Kenya?

India is 2 hours 30 minutes ahead of Kenya all year.
