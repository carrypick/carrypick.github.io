Add Phnom Penh, the capital, for its Royal Palace and riverside, or combine Cambodia with Vietnam or Thailand. ## Cambodia visa for Indians

Indians need a visa for Cambodia; India isn't on its list of visa-exempt countries, which covers Southeast Asian neighbours only. The easiest way is the **Cambodia e-visa** (Visa T, the tourist visa) on the official site of Cambodia's Ministry of Foreign Affairs. The site lists (page last updated 1 Jan 2025, read 23 Nov 2026):

- **Apply only on the official site, [evisa.gov.kh](https://www.evisa.gov.kh/).** Many other websites look official and charge extra.
- **e-Visa fee:** US$30, paid online by Visa or Mastercard credit card.
- **Single entry,** with a stay of up to **1 month**. The visa is valid for 3 months from the date it's issued, so you must enter within that time.
- **Processing time:** 3 business days. The approved e-visa is emailed to you, or you can download it from "Check & Change" on the site. Carry a printout or the file on your phone.
- **Passport:** valid for more than 6 months when you enter.
- **Photo:** a recent passport-size photo in digital format (JPEG or PNG). The site doesn't give a fixed pixel size.

**The e-visa works only at some entry points,** says the official site: the airports of Phnom Penh (Techo International, KTI), Siem Reap (Siem Reap Angkor International, SAI) and Sihanoukville (KOS), and the Bavet (from Vietnam) and Trapeang Kriel (from Laos) land borders. The Cham Yeam and Poipet land borders from Thailand are closed for e-visa entry until further notice. Crossing by road from Thailand? Check the site for the latest list before you go.

### Fill in the e-Arrival form

All travellers, even those who already have a visa, must fill in the **Cambodia e-Arrival** form, says the e-visa site. Submit it online at [arrival.gov.kh](https://www.arrival.gov.kh/) within 7 days before you arrive.

### Visa on arrival in Cambodia for Indians?

Many people search for this. We couldn't open an official page that confirms a visa on arrival for Indians when we checked on 23 Nov 2026. The official e-visa site covers only the e-visa. To be safe, get the e-visa before you fly, or check with the Royal Embassy of Cambodia in New Delhi.

### A warning from the Indian Embassy: don't take jobs on a tourist visa

The Embassy of India in Phnom Penh warns that Indians have been lured to Cambodia with job offers and then trapped by criminal gangs running online fraud. It advises Indians not to seek work on a tourist visa, and to check any employer carefully. Treat any "job in Cambodia" offer that asks you to travel on a tourist visa as a scam.

## Angkor Wat tickets: the Angkor Pass

Angkor Archaeological Park covers about 400 km² of temples and forest, the remains of the capitals of the Khmer Empire from the 9th to the 15th century, says UNESCO. You need an **Angkor Pass** to visit. Buy it only from **Angkor Enterprise**, the official ticket office: on [its website](https://www.angkorenterprise.gov.kh/en/available-tickets), in its mobile app, or at the ticket counter in Siem Reap.

Prices on the official site, read 23 Nov 2026:

| Ticket | Price | Use it within |
|---|---|---|
| Angkor 1-day pass | US$37 | 1 day |
| Angkor 3-day pass | US$62 | 7 days |
| Angkor 7-day pass | US$72 | 30 days |
| Koh Ker temples, 1 day | US$15 | 1 day |
| Beng Mealea, 1 day | US$10 | 1 day |
| Kbal Spean, 1 day | US$5 | 1 day |

- **Opening hours:** the Angkor temples park opens at 5:00 AM (for sunrise) and closes at 6:30 PM, says the official site.
- **Children under 12 don't need a ticket,** says Angkor Enterprise, but carry their passport to show their age. They can't climb Angkor Wat's upper tower, which is open only to visitors aged 12 and over.
- **A free sunset:** buy a 1-day ticket for the next day after 4:45 PM, and you can enter that evening for the sunset as well.
- **3-day and 7-day passes need a photo** for identification; the 1-day pass doesn't.
- **What each pass covers:** a 1-day pass suits the "Small Circuit" (Angkor Wat, the Bayon, Ta Prohm, Phnom Bakheng and more). With 3 or 7 days you can add the "Grand Circuit" (Preah Khan, Neak Pean, Ta Som, East Mebon, Pre Rup and more).

## A 6-day Cambodia itinerary: Siem Reap and Phnom Penh

Most first trips from India spend 3 days at Angkor and 2 in Phnom Penh. Fly into one city and out of the other, or take the bus or a domestic flight between them.

| Day | Plan | Evening |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Land in Siem Reap. Buy your Angkor Pass after 4:45 PM for the next day | Sunset at Phnom Bakheng or Pre Rup |
| 2 | Sunrise at Angkor Wat, then Angkor Thom: the Bayon, the Terrace of the Elephants, then Ta Prohm | Pub Street and the night market |
| 3 | The Grand Circuit: Preah Khan, Neak Pean, Ta Som, East Mebon | An apsara dance show |
| 4 | Banteay Srei and Kbal Spean, or the floating villages of Tonle Sap lake | Fly or take the bus to Phnom Penh |
| 5 | Phnom Penh: the Royal Palace and Silver Pagoda, the National Museum, Wat Phnom | A sunset boat on the Mekong |
| 6 | The Tuol Sleng Genocide Museum and Choeung Ek, then your flight home | |

- **With a few more days,** go south to Kampot and Kep for pepper farms and the coast, or to Koh Rong island from Sihanoukville.
- **Combining Cambodia with Vietnam?** Phnom Penh is close to Ho Chi Minh City by road (cross at Bavet) or a short flight. With Thailand, fly between Bangkok and Siem Reap; check the land border rules first.
- **Start early at Angkor.** The temples are cooler and quieter at sunrise, and midday is very hot from March to May.
- **Temples are sacred:** cover your shoulders and knees, take off hats inside, and don't touch the carvings.

## Cambodia weather by month, and the best time to go

Long-term averages (1982–2024) from Cambodia's Ministry of Water Resources and Meteorology, published by the World Meteorological Organization:

| Month | Siem Reap (day / night) | Phnom Penh (day / night) | Rain in Siem Reap |
|---|---|---|---|
| January | About 31 °C / 20 °C | About 31 °C / 22 °C | About 4 mm, dry |
| February | About 32 °C / 21 °C | About 33 °C / 23 °C | About 4 mm |
| April | About 35 °C / 24 °C | About 35 °C / 25 °C | About 48 mm |
| May | About 35 °C / 25 °C, the hottest | About 34 °C / 25 °C | About 143 mm |
| July | About 33 °C / 24 °C | About 32 °C / 25 °C | About 170 mm |
| September | About 32 °C / 25 °C | About 32 °C / 24 °C | About 305 mm, the wettest |
| November | About 32 °C / 22 °C | About 30 °C / 23 °C | About 62 mm |
| December | About 31 °C / 20 °C | About 30 °C / 21 °C | About 7 mm |

- **November to February is best:** dry, sunny days and the coolest nights, about 20 °C in Siem Reap. December and January are the busiest months at Angkor.
- **March to May is the hottest,** about 35 °C by day. Visit the temples at sunrise and rest at midday.
- **June to October is the rainy season,** heaviest in September and October. Showers usually come in the afternoon, the countryside is green and Angkor is quieter, but some roads get muddy.
- **It's warm all year.** Even in January, the coolest month, days are about 31 °C.

## Getting around Cambodia

- **At Angkor,** hire a tuk-tuk (remork) with a driver for the day, or a car for the Grand Circuit and far temples. Agree the price and the route before you start. You can also rent a scooter or an e-bike, says Angkor Enterprise.
- **Between Siem Reap and Phnom Penh,** fly (under an hour) or take a bus or minivan (about 6 hours by road).
- **In the cities,** ride-hailing apps such as Grab and PassApp work for tuk-tuks and cars, with the fare shown before you ride.
- **Language:** Khmer is the national language; English is common in hotels, restaurants and with guides. "Arkoun" means thank you.

## Food for Indian travellers: veg and Jain tips

Khmer food is lighter and less spicy than Thai food, and many dishes can be made vegetarian:

- **Vegetable amok:** a mild coconut curry, usually made with fish; ask for the tofu or vegetable version.
- **Lok lak and fried rice** can be made with tofu or vegetables.
- **Fresh spring rolls,** noodle soups with vegetable broth, and plenty of tropical fruit.
- **Ask about fish sauce and prahok** (fermented fish paste), which go into many dishes, even vegetable ones. Say "no fish sauce" clearly.
- **Indian restaurants:** Siem Reap and Phnom Penh both have Indian restaurants, many near Pub Street in Siem Reap and the riverside in Phnom Penh.
- **Jain travellers:** most dishes use garlic and onion. Carry ready-to-eat food or khakhra, and check what you can take in our [can I carry it on a flight?](../baggage/can-i-carry.html) list.

## Phone, SIM and eSIM in Cambodia

You have three ways to stay online in Cambodia:

- **Roaming on your own SIM:** the easiest, but often the costliest. Check your mobile company's international pack before you fly.
- **A local SIM card:** sold at the airports and in phone shops. Shops ask for your passport to register it.
- **An eSIM:** a data plan you buy online and install on your phone before you go, with no plastic card. Your phone must support eSIM and must not be locked to one network. On an iPhone, look in Settings > Mobile Service (or Cellular). On Android, look in Settings > SIM or Connections.

Install the eSIM at home over Wi-Fi and switch it on when you land. Most travel eSIMs give data only, with no local number, so keep your home SIM on for calls and bank OTP texts (roaming charges may apply).

## Money and rules to know

- **The currency is the Cambodian riel (KHR), but US dollars are used almost everywhere** in hotels, restaurants and tuk-tuks. Change is often given in riel. Carry clean, untorn dollar notes, as damaged notes may be refused.
- **Cash from India:** RBI lets you buy up to US$3,000 in foreign currency notes per trip, and you can carry up to ₹25,000 in Indian notes. Put the rest on a forex card. See [how much cash you can carry abroad](../baggage/customs/cash-and-currency.html).
- **Cards** work in bigger hotels and restaurants; markets and tuk-tuks want cash.
- **Bargaining is normal** at markets, not in shops with fixed prices.
- **Respect the monks:** women shouldn't touch a monk or hand things to him directly.
- **Ask before photographing people,** and don't take photos inside the Royal Palace's Silver Pagoda, where it's not allowed.

## What to wear in Cambodia

- **For the temples,** cover your shoulders and knees: a T-shirt and long trousers, or a long skirt. Visitors in short shorts or sleeveless tops can be stopped at Angkor Wat's upper level and the Royal Palace.
- **Light cotton or quick-dry clothes,** a sun hat, sunglasses and sunscreen all year.
- **Comfortable shoes with grip** for steep, uneven temple steps.
- **A light rain jacket** from June to October, and a light layer for cold air-conditioning.

## Help in an emergency

- **Emergency numbers in Cambodia:** police 117, ambulance 119, fire 118.
- **Embassy of India, Phnom Penh:** contact details are on its [website](https://embindpp.gov.in/).
- **Keep a photo of your passport, your e-visa** and your insurance papers on your phone, and save your hotel's details.

## Questions

### Do Indians need a visa for Cambodia?

Yes. Indians can apply for a Cambodia e-visa online on the official site, evisa.gov.kh, before they travel. It costs US$30, gives one entry and a stay of up to 1 month, and takes 3 business days.

### What is the Cambodia visa fee for Indians?

US$30 for the tourist e-visa, says the official site (page last updated 1 Jan 2025, read 23 Nov 2026). You pay online by Visa or Mastercard.

### How long does the Cambodia e-visa take?

3 business days, says the official site. Apply at least a week or two before you travel to be safe.

### Can Indians get a visa on arrival in Cambodia?

We couldn't confirm it on an official page when we checked. Get the e-visa before you fly, or check with the Royal Embassy of Cambodia in New Delhi.

### What photo do I need for the Cambodia e-visa?

A recent passport-size photo in digital format, as a JPEG or PNG file, says the official site. It doesn't give a fixed size.

### How much is the Angkor Wat ticket?

On the official Angkor Enterprise site (read 23 Nov 2026), the Angkor Pass costs US$37 for 1 day, US$62 for 3 days and US$72 for 7 days. Children under 12 don't need a ticket.

### Are there direct flights from India to Cambodia?

Yes. Air Cambodia flies direct from Delhi to Phnom Penh. IndiGo has flown Kolkata to Siem Reap; check it's flying on your dates. From other cities, fly one-stop via Bangkok, Singapore or Kuala Lumpur.

### What is the best time to visit Cambodia from India?

November to February, when it's dry and a little cooler: Siem Reap is about 31 °C by day and 20 °C at night in December, on the Ministry of Water Resources and Meteorology's averages.

### How many days are enough for Cambodia?

Five or six days: three at Angkor from Siem Reap and two in Phnom Penh. With more time, add Kampot and Kep, or Koh Rong island.
