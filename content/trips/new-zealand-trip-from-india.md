There are no direct flights from India, so plan on one stop and a long journey, and Indians need a visa, applied for online. ## New Zealand visa for Indians

Indians need a **Visitor Visa** for a holiday in New Zealand. Immigration New Zealand says people travelling on a passport from some countries must apply for one, and India isn't on its list of visa waiver countries (read 23 Nov 2026).

- **Apply online** on Immigration New Zealand's [Visitor Visa page](https://www.immigration.govt.nz/visas/visitor-visa). It has a checklist for visitors from India staying less than 6 months.
- **Cost:** from **NZD 441**, says the Visitor Visa page.
- **Processing time:** the Visitor Visa page shows the current time. We don't quote it here because it changes. Immigration New Zealand says to apply early, and not to book non-refundable travel until your visa is approved.
- **How long you can stay:** up to 6 or 9 months, and at most 9 months in any 18-month period. Immigration New Zealand decides whether to give you a single-entry or a multiple-entry visa.
- **You can't work** on a Visitor Visa, but you can study for up to 3 months.

### What documents and money do you need?

- **Your passport** must be valid for at least 3 months after the date you plan to leave New Zealand, says Immigration New Zealand. You upload a good scan of it, and of the pages with your old visas and stamps.
- **Money to live on:** at least **NZD 1,000 a month**, or **NZD 400 a month** if your accommodation is already paid, says the Visitor Visa page. A sponsor in New Zealand can support you instead.
- **A ticket out of New Zealand,** or bank or card statements showing you can buy one. The money for the ticket must be on top of the money you live on.
- **An acceptable photo,** to Immigration New Zealand's photo rules.
- **Families:** if family members apply on their own separate visas, write a letter with each person's name, date of birth, passport number and application number, and add it to your application.

### Can Indians use the NZeTA?

No. The NZeTA (New Zealand Electronic Travel Authority) is for citizens of visa waiver countries, and India isn't on Immigration New Zealand's list (read 23 Nov 2026). Indians need the Visitor Visa, even with a US, UK or Australian visa.

**Changing planes in Auckland** on the way to another country? India isn't on the transit visa waiver list either, so check Immigration New Zealand's [transit page](https://www.immigration.govt.nz/visit/transiting-through-new-zealand/) to see whether you need a Transit Visa.

### The New Zealand Traveller Declaration

Everyone arriving in New Zealand must complete a **New Zealand Traveller Declaration (NZTD)**, says Immigration New Zealand. It collects your travel, customs, immigration and biosecurity details. Fill it in online before you fly; a paper form is available if you can't.

## Flights from India to New Zealand

- **There are no direct flights from India to New Zealand.** Fly one-stop to Auckland: via Singapore (Singapore Airlines), Kuala Lumpur (Malaysia Airlines), Hong Kong (Cathay Pacific or Air New Zealand), Guangzhou (China Southern), or Dubai (Emirates), say Auckland Airport's airline lists (Wikipedia, read 23 Nov 2026).
- **Via Australia:** fly to Sydney or Melbourne, then on to Auckland, Christchurch or Queenstown. Australia needs its own visa for Indians if you leave the airport: see our [Australia guide](australia-trip-from-india.html).
- **Air India doesn't fly to New Zealand,** so we don't show it below.
- **The journey takes a long day.** Auckland is about 12,500 km from Delhi in a straight line, so plan for about 16 hours or more in the air, plus the stop.
- **Time difference:** New Zealand is 7 hours 30 minutes ahead of India during New Zealand summer time (late September to early April) and 6 hours 30 minutes ahead the rest of the year.
- **Baggage:** check the limits by airline in the [international baggage rules](../baggage/international.html) and the [carry-on size by airline](../guides/carry-on-size-by-airline.html) guide.
- **Fares go up for Christmas, New Year and the New Zealand summer holidays.** Book early, but only after you have your visa.

## A 10-day New Zealand itinerary: North and South Islands

Most first trips fly into Auckland, see a little of the North Island, then fly to Queenstown on the South Island and end in Christchurch. This plan uses 10 days.

| Day | Plan | Evening |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Land in Auckland and rest. The waterfront and the Sky Tower | Viaduct Harbour |
| 2 | Drive or take a tour south to the Hobbiton movie set near Matamata, then on to Rotorua | A Māori cultural evening with a hāngī dinner |
| 3 | Rotorua: geysers and mud pools at Te Puia or Wai-O-Tapu, and the Redwoods forest | Hot pools |
| 4 | Back to Auckland and fly to Queenstown (about 2 hours) | Queenstown lakefront |
| 5 | Queenstown: the Skyline gondola, a jet boat ride, or a bungy jump for the brave | Dinner in town |
| 6 | A long day trip to Milford Sound, with a boat cruise through the fiord | Back in Queenstown |
| 7 | Arrowtown and the drive over the Crown Range to Wanaka | Lake Wanaka |
| 8 | Drive to Aoraki/Mount Cook National Park. Walk the Hooker Valley Track | Stars over Mount Cook |
| 9 | Lake Pukaki and Lake Tekapo, then drive to Christchurch | Christchurch |
| 10 | Christchurch's Botanic Gardens, then fly home | |

- **Two weeks?** Add Franz Josef and Fox glaciers on the West Coast, Wellington, or the Bay of Islands north of Auckland.
- **Distances are long and roads are winding.** Allow more time than the map says, especially in winter.
- **Milford Sound road can close** in snow or heavy rain. Keep a spare day, or fly over it from Queenstown.

## New Zealand weather by month, and the best time to go

The seasons are the opposite of India's. Long-term averages (1981–2010) from New Zealand's national weather service (MetService), published by the World Meteorological Organization:

| Month | Auckland (day / night) | Queenstown | Christchurch |
|---|---|---|---|
| January | About 24 °C / 16 °C, summer | About 22 °C / 10 °C | About 23 °C / 12 °C |
| April | About 20 °C / 13 °C, autumn | About 15 °C / 4 °C | About 17 °C / 7 °C |
| July | About 14 °C / 8 °C, the wettest month | About 8 °C / −2 °C, snow on the mountains | About 11 °C / 1 °C |
| October | About 18 °C / 11 °C, spring | About 15 °C / 4 °C | About 17 °C / 6 °C |
| November | About 20 °C / 13 °C | About 18 °C / 6 °C | About 19 °C / 8 °C |
| December | About 22 °C / 15 °C | About 20 °C / 8 °C | About 21 °C / 11 °C |

- **December to March is summer:** the warmest weather, long evenings and the busiest time. Book hotels early for Christmas and January.
- **March to May (autumn) and October and November (spring)** are quieter and cooler, with autumn colours in Arrowtown and Wanaka in April.
- **June to August is winter:** cold nights in Queenstown, below freezing in July, and the ski season. Mountain roads may need snow chains.
- **The weather changes fast,** even in summer. Carry a rain jacket and a warm layer every day.

## Getting around New Zealand

- **Between the islands, fly.** Domestic flights link Auckland, Wellington, Christchurch and Queenstown.
- **Most visitors rent a car or campervan.** New Zealand drives on the left, like India. Check the New Zealand Transport Agency's rules for driving on an overseas licence before you go, and get an International Driving Permit from your RTO.
- **Roads are narrow and winding,** with one-lane bridges. Drive slowly and stop at viewpoints, not on the road.
- **Coach buses and day tours** run between the main towns and to Milford Sound and Hobbiton if you don't want to drive.

## Food for Indian travellers: veg and Jain tips

- **Indian restaurants** are easy to find in Auckland, and there are some in Queenstown, Christchurch and Rotorua. Auckland also has Indian grocery shops.
- **Vegetarian and vegan options** are on most café menus. Ask whether soups use meat stock.
- **Easy veg choices:** pizza, pasta, veggie burgers, Thai, Chinese and Middle Eastern food, and fish and chips shops often sell chips and battered vegetables.
- **Jain travellers:** most restaurant food uses onion and garlic. Ask for food without them, or carry ready-to-eat food.
- **Carrying food from India? Declare it.** Your New Zealand Traveller Declaration asks about biosecurity items, and New Zealand is strict about food, plants and seeds. Check what you can pack in our [can I carry it on a flight?](../baggage/can-i-carry.html) list.

## Phone, SIM and eSIM in New Zealand

You have three ways to stay online in New Zealand:

- **Roaming on your own SIM:** the easiest, but often the costliest. Check your mobile company's international pack before you fly.
- **A local SIM card:** sold at Auckland and Christchurch airports and at phone shops.
- **An eSIM:** a data plan you buy online and install on your phone before you go, with no plastic card. Your phone must support eSIM and must not be locked to one network.

Mobile coverage is weak on many mountain roads, including the road to Milford Sound. Download offline maps before you drive.

Install the eSIM at home over Wi-Fi and switch it on when you land. Most travel eSIMs give data only, with no local number, so keep your home SIM on for calls and bank OTP texts (roaming charges may apply).

## Money and rules to know

- **The currency is the New Zealand dollar (NZ$, NZD).** Cards, including contactless, work almost everywhere.
- **Prices in shops include GST** (goods and services tax).
- **Tipping is not expected,** but you can leave something for good service.
- **Cash from India:** RBI lets you buy up to US$3,000 in foreign currency notes per trip, and you can carry up to ₹25,000 in Indian notes. Put the rest on a forex card. See [how much cash you can carry abroad](../baggage/customs/cash-and-currency.html).
- **Plugs:** New Zealand uses 230 V and the angled flat-pin plug also used in Australia, different from India's. Read our [power bank and travel adapter guide](../guides/power-bank-and-travel-adapter-guide.html) or the [travel adapter by country](../guides/travel-adapter-by-country.html) list.
- **Health insurance:** buy travel insurance with medical cover before you fly, especially if you plan adventure sports.

## What to wear in New Zealand

- **Summer (December to February):** light clothes, plus a fleece and a rain jacket for cool evenings and mountain weather. The sun is strong: bring sunglasses, a hat and sunscreen.
- **Spring and autumn:** layers, a warm jacket and waterproof walking shoes.
- **Winter (June to August):** a warm, waterproof coat, thermal innerwear, gloves and a woolly cap, especially in Queenstown.

## Questions

### Do Indians need a visa for New Zealand?

Yes. Indians need a New Zealand Visitor Visa, applied for online with Immigration New Zealand. India isn't a visa waiver country, so the NZeTA isn't an option.

### What is the New Zealand visitor visa fee for Indians?

From NZD 441, says Immigration New Zealand's Visitor Visa page (read 23 Nov 2026). Check the page for the exact amount when you apply.

### How long does a New Zealand tourist visa take for Indians?

Immigration New Zealand shows the current processing time on its Visitor Visa page. It changes, so check it there and apply early. Don't book non-refundable travel before the visa is approved.

### Can Indians with a US visa visit New Zealand without a visa?

No. Immigration New Zealand doesn't list a US, UK or Australian visa as a way round the Visitor Visa for Indian passports. Only visa waiver countries can use the NZeTA, and India isn't one.

### How much bank balance do I need for a New Zealand visa?

Immigration New Zealand asks for at least NZD 1,000 for each month of your stay, or NZD 400 a month if your accommodation is already paid, plus a ticket out of New Zealand or the money to buy one.

### Are there direct flights from India to New Zealand?

No. Fly one-stop via Singapore, Kuala Lumpur, Hong Kong, Guangzhou, Dubai or an Australian city to Auckland.

### How many days are enough for New Zealand?

Ten days covers Auckland, Rotorua, Queenstown, Milford Sound and Mount Cook if you fly between the islands. With two weeks, add the West Coast glaciers or Wellington.

### What is the best time to visit New Zealand from India?

December to March for summer, or March to May and October to November for quieter, cooler trips. June to August is the ski season in Queenstown.

### What is the time difference between India and New Zealand?

New Zealand is 7 hours 30 minutes ahead of India from late September to early April (New Zealand summer time) and 6 hours 30 minutes ahead the rest of the year.
