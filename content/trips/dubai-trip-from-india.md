## Dubai visa for Indians

The UAE isn't visa-free for Indians. Its visa on arrival for Indians is only for people who hold certain visas or residence permits (below). Everyone else needs a visit visa before the flight.

- **Who applies:** a normal UAE tourist visa is sponsored by a tourism company, says GDRFA Dubai (Dubai's immigration office). You can't apply for it on your own. In practice, travel agents, tour operators, some hotels and the UAE airlines arrange it for you.
- **Fee:** GDRFA lists AED 200 for a 30-day tourist visa and AED 300 for a 60-day one, plus 5% VAT. The company that applies for you may add its own charges, so ask for the full price first.
- **Documents:** a passport valid for at least 6 months, a return or onward ticket, and medical insurance valid in the UAE, says GDRFA.
- **The airline or travel agent decides the paperwork.** Keep a copy of the visa on your phone and a printout in your cabin bag.
- **Apply only through official or known sellers.** Look-alike visa websites charge extra.

### Visa on arrival for Indians with a US, UK or EU visa

GDRFA Dubai gives Indians with an ordinary passport a visa on arrival for 14 days, which can be extended once for another 14 days, if they hold "a permanent resident card (green card) issued by the United States of America or a residence visa in the United Kingdom or European Union countries". Its list of requirements also mentions "a visa or green card" from the USA. Your passport and the US, UK or EU document must each be valid for at least 6 months.

- **ICP, the UAE's federal immigration authority, words it more widely.** It lists Indians who hold a valid visa, residence permit or US green card from the US, the EU or the UK, or a valid residence permit from Japan, South Korea, Australia, Canada, Singapore or New Zealand. It offers 14 days (extendable once) or 60 days (not extendable).
- **The fee isn't the same on every official page.** ICP lists AED 100 plus a AED 100 smart services fee for 14 days, and AED 250 plus AED 100 for 60 days. GDRFA Dubai's page shows a fee of "253" with no currency. Check the fee shown when you apply.
- **Apply online first,** on the GDRFA smart services portal, says GDRFA. It takes about 48 hours.
- **Only have a US tourist visa (B1/B2)?** ICP's page includes a valid US visa, but GDRFA's main sentence names only the green card. Ask your airline before you fly, or get a normal tourist visa to be safe.
- **Overstaying costs AED 50 a day,** says ICP.

## A simple 5-day Dubai plan with an Abu Dhabi day trip

Most first trips from India spend 4 to 6 days. This plan keeps one area per day and puts the desert and Abu Dhabi in the middle, when you've rested from the flight.

| Day | Plan | Evening |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Land at DXB, check in and rest | Downtown Dubai: the Dubai Mall, the Burj Khalifa from below and the Dubai Fountain |
| 2 | Old Dubai: Al Fahidi, an abra (water taxi) across Dubai Creek, the Gold and Spice souks | A dhow dinner cruise on the creek or at the Marina |
| 3 | A slow morning at the beach (JBR or Kite Beach) | Desert safari: dune drive, camel ride and a camp dinner |
| 4 | Day trip to Abu Dhabi: the Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque, then the Corniche or a theme park on Yas Island | Back in Dubai |
| 5 | Dubai Marina and the Palm Jumeirah, last shopping, then fly home | |

- **Going up the Burj Khalifa?** Book a time slot online, and pick sunset for the views. We couldn't read the official ticket site to confirm its rules.
- **Only 4 days?** Skip the beach morning, or do Abu Dhabi only if the mosque or a theme park matters to you.
- **With kids?** Swap day 5 for a water park or a theme park, and keep afternoons indoors when it's warm.
- **6 or 7 days?** Add a slow day, a second theme park, or Sharjah or Hatta.
- **Book the desert safari with pickup from your hotel,** and ask whether dinner and drinks are included.

### Abu Dhabi day trip from Dubai

- **By bus:** RTA's E100 bus runs from Al Ghubaiba to Abu Dhabi's central bus station, and the E101 from Ibn Battuta. RTA's journey planner showed the E100 at AED 30, taking about 2 hours 10 minutes (read 21 Nov 2026). Tickets can be booked online.
- **By car or tour:** quicker and easier with family. Many Dubai tours include the mosque and a theme park.
- **Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque:** visitors from outside the UAE pay AED 10, says the mosque's FAQ, and must book an online pass before they go. Worshippers enter free.
- **Mosque hours:** the FAQ gives 9 AM to 8:30 PM from Saturday to Thursday, and 9 AM to 11:30 AM and 2:30 PM to 8:30 PM on Friday. Another official page says 9 AM to 9 PM, last entry 8:30 PM. Avoid Friday midday.
- **Mosque dress code:** long, loose clothes down to the ankles for everyone. Women need sleeves to the wrists and must cover their hair. Men should cover their legs and not wear sleeveless tops. Free guided tours of 30 to 45 minutes run every day.
- **BAPS Hindu Mandir:** many Indian visitors add the Hindu temple near Abu Dhabi. Check its visiting hours and booking on its official site before you go; we haven't checked them.

## Dubai weather in December

Long-term averages from the UAE's National Center of Meteorology, published by the World Meteorological Organization:

| Month | Day | Night | Rain |
|---|---|---|---|
| November | About 31 °C | About 18 °C | About 2 mm |
| December | About 26 °C | About 15 °C | About 14 mm, on about 2 days |
| January | About 24 °C | About 14 °C | About 11 mm, on about 2 days |
| February | About 25 °C | About 15 °C | About 36 mm, on about 4 days |
| March | About 28 °C | About 17 °C | About 22 mm, on about 4 days |
| July | About 41 °C | About 29 °C | Almost none |

- **November to March is the best time.** Days are warm, not hot, so outdoor trips are easy.
- **December and January nights are cool,** about 14–15 °C. Take a light jacket for the evenings, the desert and the dhow cruise.
- **Rain is rare,** even in winter.
- **May to September is very hot,** about 37–41 °C by day. Plan outdoor trips for early morning and evening.
- **Malls and the metro are cold** all year because of the AC.

## Getting around Dubai

- **Dubai Metro:** the Red Line stops at DXB Terminals 1 and 3, and runs along Sheikh Zayed Road to the Dubai Mall (Burj Khalifa), the Marina and Ibn Battuta. The Green Line serves Old Dubai and the creek.
- **Metro hours,** says Dubai Airports: 5 AM to midnight from Monday to Thursday and on Saturday, 5 AM to 1 AM on Friday, and 8 AM to midnight on Sunday. You can take up to two pieces of luggage.
- **The nol card pays for the metro, buses, trams and water buses,** says RTA. A Silver card costs AED 25, with AED 19 of credit on it. A Gold card costs the same and lets you ride in the Gold Class cabin. A Red paper ticket costs AED 2 and holds up to 10 trips.
- **Fares by zone:** with a Silver card, AED 3, 5 or 7.50 a trip, depending on how many zones you cross; Gold Class costs double. Children aged 5 and under ride free.
- **Metro rules and fines,** from RTA: AED 100 for eating or drinking, AED 100 for sitting in a cabin reserved for others (there are cabins for women and children only), AED 200 for smoking, and AED 500 for carrying alcohol.
- **Taxis and ride-hailing apps** are easy to find, and help with luggage and late nights. The abra across Dubai Creek is cheap and fun.
- **Walking:** fine in winter, hard in summer. Wear comfortable shoes.

## Food for Indian travellers

- **Indian food is everywhere.** Bur Dubai, Karama and the malls have many Indian restaurants, from South Indian tiffin to North Indian thalis, and Indian chains you know from home.
- **Vegetarian food is easy to find.** For Jain food, ask the restaurant first.
- **Try Emirati and Arabic food too:** shawarma, falafel, hummus, grills, and Arabic sweets like kunafa.
- **Carrying food from home?** Packed sweets and snacks are fine in normal amounts, but paan and poppy seeds (khus khus) are banned. See [food from home](../baggage/customs/uae-dubai.html#food-from-home) in our UAE customs rules.

## Phone, SIM and eSIM in Dubai

You have three ways to stay online in Dubai:

- **Roaming on your own SIM:** the easiest, but often the costliest. Check your mobile company's international pack before you fly.
- **A local SIM card:** sold at the airport and in phone shops. Shops usually ask for your passport to register it.
- **An eSIM:** a data plan you buy online and install on your phone before you go, with no plastic card. Your phone must support eSIM and must not be locked to one network. On an iPhone, look in Settings > Mobile Service (or Cellular). On Android, look in Settings > SIM or Connections.

Install the eSIM at home over Wi-Fi and switch it on when you land. Most travel eSIMs give data only, with no local number, so keep your home SIM on for calls and bank OTP texts (roaming charges may apply).

## Money, shopping and VAT refunds

- **The currency is the UAE dirham (AED).** Cards are accepted almost everywhere. Carry a forex card and a little cash.
- **Declare more than AED 60,000** in cash, cheques, gold or precious stones when you arrive or leave. See [UAE customs rules](../baggage/customs/uae-dubai.html#cash-gold-and-the-aed-60-000-rule), and [how much cash you can carry abroad](../baggage/customs/cash-and-currency.html) for India's own limits.
- **VAT refund for tourists:** you can claim back VAT if you spend at least AED 250 (before VAT) in one bill at a shop in the refund scheme, says the UAE's Federal Tax Authority. You must be on a tourist visa and 18 or older. Validate your bills at a Planet kiosk or desk, usually at the airport, within 90 days of buying, and leave within 6 hours of validating. Planet keeps a fee of 13% of the VAT plus AED 3.60 a bill.
- **Buying gold?** Your personal jewellery is duty free in the UAE. Read what India allows on the way back in our [Indian customs rules](../baggage/customs.html).

## Rules to know in Dubai

- **Dress modestly in malls, souks and heritage areas.** Swimwear is only for the beach and the pool.
- **Don't carry paan, betel leaves or poppy seeds.** They are banned in the UAE.
- **Medicines:** carry them in the original pack with your prescription. Some, like tramadol, pregabalin and codeine, are controlled. See [medicines](../baggage/customs/uae-dubai.html#medicines) in our UAE customs rules.
- **Alcohol, tobacco and vapes** have their own limits. See our UAE customs rules.
- **Don't photograph people,** especially women, without asking.

- **A light jacket, sweater or shawl** for winter evenings, the desert and cold malls.

## Questions

### Do Indians need a visa for Dubai?

Yes, most do. The UAE's visa on arrival for Indians is only for those holding a US green card or visa, a UK or EU residence visa, or a residence permit from a few other countries. Everyone else needs a UAE visit visa before the flight, usually through a tourism company, airline or hotel.

### What is the Dubai visa fee for Indians?

GDRFA Dubai lists AED 200 for a 30-day tourist visa and AED 300 for a 60-day one, plus 5% VAT. The tourism company or airline that applies for you may add its own charges.

### Can Indians get a visa on arrival in Dubai?

Only if they hold an ordinary Indian passport and a US green card or visa, or a UK or EU residence visa, says GDRFA Dubai; ICP also lists residence permits from Japan, South Korea, Australia, Canada, Singapore and New Zealand. It gives 14 days, extendable once. Apply online on the GDRFA portal first.

### Can I get a Dubai visa on arrival with a US visa?

ICP's page includes Indians with a valid US visa. GDRFA's main sentence names only the US green card, though its list of documents mentions a US visa. Check with your airline before you fly, or get a normal tourist visa.

### How many days are enough for Dubai?

Four or five days cover Downtown Dubai, Old Dubai and the souks, a desert safari and the Marina. Add a day for Abu Dhabi, and more for theme parks with kids.

### Is December a good time to visit Dubai?

Yes. December averages about 26 °C by day and 15 °C at night, with rain on only about 2 days. It is also busy and costly around Christmas and New Year, so book early. For New Year's Eve, see our [Dubai New Year 2027 guide](dubai-new-year-2027.html).

### What is the weather in Dubai in December?

About 26 °C by day and 15 °C at night, with about 14 mm of rain on about 2 days, on long-term averages from the UAE's National Center of Meteorology.

### What is the time difference between India and Dubai?

Dubai is 1 hour 30 minutes behind India.

### How do I go from Dubai to Abu Dhabi?

By RTA's E100 bus from Al Ghubaiba (about 2 hours 10 minutes, AED 30 on RTA's journey planner) or the E101 from Ibn Battuta, by taxi or car, or on a day tour.

### How much is the Dubai Metro card?

A nol Silver card costs AED 25, with AED 19 of credit on it, says RTA. Trips cost AED 3, 5 or 7.50 depending on the zones you cross.
