India is huge, so "the best time to visit India" depends on where you go. For a first trip to the classic places, Delhi, Agra, Rajasthan, Varanasi, Kerala and Goa, the simple answer is **October to March**: dry, sunny and not too hot. This guide explains the seasons with the India Meteorological Department's (IMD) dates, and gives a 5-day and 7-day Golden Triangle plan.

New to India? Start with our [visiting India guide](visiting-india.html).

## India's seasons in one table

| Months | North and centre (Delhi, Agra, Rajasthan, Varanasi) | South-west coast (Goa, Kerala) |
|---|---|---|
| October–November | Dry, warm days, cooler nights. A great time | Rain ends; Goa and Kerala dry out |
| December–February | Sunny days, cold nights and mornings. Fog can delay trains and flights | Peak season: dry and warm |
| March | Warming up, still pleasant | Hot and dry |
| April–June | Very hot | Hot and humid; the monsoon reaches Kerala around 1 June |
| July–September | Monsoon rain, green, humid | Heavy monsoon rain |

- **The monsoon:** IMD says the south-west monsoon normally reaches Kerala **around 1 June** and covers the whole country **around 15 July**. It starts to withdraw from the north-west **from 1 September** at the earliest, and leaves the whole country only after 1 October.
- **Goa's rain:** Goa Tourism says the monsoon lasts from June to September. Its rainfall table shows about 890 mm in July, but under 31 mm in every month from November to May.
- **Winter numbers:** IMD's long-term averages for Jaipur are about 24 °C by day and 9 °C at night in December. Varanasi is about 24 °C and 10 °C. Pack a warm layer for mornings in the north.

## The Golden Triangle: Delhi, Agra and Jaipur

The "Golden Triangle" is the classic first trip to India: **Delhi**, the capital; **Agra**, for the Taj Mahal; and **Jaipur**, the Pink City in Rajasthan. Delhi and Jaipur airports both accept e-Visa arrivals, so you can fly into one and out of the other.

### Before you plan: the Taj Mahal's hours

From the Taj Mahal's official website (checked 21 Nov 2026):

- **Closed on Fridays** for general visitors. Don't put Agra on a Friday.
- **Opens 30 minutes before sunrise** and closes 30 minutes before sunset. Ticket counters open an hour before sunrise.
- **Foreign tourists pay ₹1,100**, and an extra ₹200 to go inside the main mausoleum. Buying online takes ₹50 off a foreigner's ticket.
- **Night viewing** is on the full-moon night and two nights before and after, from 8 PM to midnight, in small batches.

### A 5-day Golden Triangle itinerary

1. **Day 1, Delhi:** arrive, rest, and see one sight close to your hotel. Evening walk at India Gate.
2. **Day 2, Delhi:** Old Delhi in the morning, then Humayun's Tomb and Qutub Minar.
3. **Day 3, to Agra:** go by train or road. Agra Fort in the afternoon, and the Taj Mahal from across the river at sunset.
4. **Day 4, Agra to Jaipur:** the Taj Mahal at sunrise, then on to Jaipur (roughly 240 km by road). Fatehpur Sikri is on the way.
5. **Day 5, Jaipur:** Amer Fort in the morning, City Palace, Jantar Mantar and Hawa Mahal in the afternoon. Fly or take the train out.

### A 7-day Golden Triangle itinerary

Do the 5 days above, then add:

- **Day 6, Jaipur:** Nahargarh Fort, a bazaar walk in the old city and a cooking class or food walk.
- **Day 7:** a day trip, or go on to **Udaipur** (lakes and palaces) or **Ranthambore** for a tiger safari. Or fly to **Varanasi** for the Ganga aarti.

Times, tickets and Jaipur's monument fees are in our [Jaipur trip guide](jaipur.html). Going by train? See the [Indian Railways luggage rules](../baggage/train-luggage-rules.html).

## Longer first trips

- **Rajasthan, 10–14 days:** Delhi, Agra, Jaipur, then Udaipur, and if you have time Jodhpur and Jaisalmer in the desert. Best from October to March.
- **Golden Triangle plus Varanasi, 8–9 days:** add 2 nights in Varanasi for the ghats and the evening aarti.
- **Kerala, 6–8 days:** Kochi, the tea hills of Munnar, a houseboat night in Alappuzha and a beach in Varkala. See our [Kerala trip guide](kerala.html).
- **Goa, 4–6 days:** beaches, old Portuguese churches and food. See our [Goa trip guide](goa.html).
- **North and south in one trip:** fly between them. Delhi to Kochi or Goa is a domestic flight, so check the [cabin bag rules for Indian airlines](../baggage/index.html).

## What to pack

Pack for the season and for temples: light clothes that cover shoulders and knees, a warm layer for winter mornings in the north, and comfortable shoes you can slip off. See our full [packing list for India](../guides/what-to-pack-for-india.html).

## Questions

### What is the best month to visit India?

For most first trips (Delhi, Agra, Rajasthan, Varanasi, Goa and Kerala), November to February: dry, sunny and not too hot. October and March are also good.

### When is the monsoon in India?

IMD says the south-west monsoon normally reaches Kerala around 1 June and covers all of India around 15 July. It withdraws from September, and has left the whole country after 1 October.

### What is the best time to visit the Golden Triangle?

October to March. December and January mornings are cold and can be foggy in Delhi and Agra, so leave some time in your plan for delays.

### How many days do you need for the Golden Triangle?

5 days for the highlights (2 in Delhi, 1 in Agra, 2 in Jaipur), or 7 days for a slower trip with a day trip or Udaipur.

### Is the Taj Mahal open every day?

No. It's closed on Fridays for general visitors. Other days, it opens 30 minutes before sunrise and closes 30 minutes before sunset.
