Pondicherry in December is warm, green and relaxed, with the French Quarter's yellow streets, the seafront Promenade and Auroville close by. But December is also the last month of the northeast monsoon on this coast, and the rainiest month of the winter, so keep your plans flexible. This guide covers the weather and rain, cyclone warnings, a simple 3-day plan, how to visit the Matrimandir, Christmas and New Year, getting there, and what to wear.

Puducherry is the official name; most people still say Pondicherry or Pondy.

## Pondicherry weather in December and January

IMD's averages for Pondicherry airport (1991–2020):

| Month | Day | Night | Rain |
|---|---|---|---|
| December | About 30 °C | About 21 °C | About 248 mm, on about 6 days |
| January | About 30 °C | About 21 °C | About 18 mm, on about 1 day |
| February | About 31 °C | About 21 °C | About 17 mm, on about 1 day |

- **It's warm all winter.** The Puducherry government says winter runs from December to February, with days around 30 °C and pleasant, even cool, nights.
- **December is wet.** IMD says the northeast monsoon runs from October to December on this coast, and it's also the main cyclone season. Rain usually comes in a few heavy spells, not every day.
- **Cyclones have hit in early December:** Cyclone Fengal crossed the coast near Puducherry on 30 November 2024, and the town got 49 cm of rain on 1 December. In 2025, Cyclone Ditwah brought heavy rain around 2 December (IMD).
- **January and February are nearly dry.** For a sure beach trip, go after Christmas or in January.
- **Before you go,** check the forecast and warnings from [IMD Chennai](https://mausam.imd.gov.in/chennai/).

## What to see in Pondicherry

- **The French Quarter (White Town):** walk Rue Dumas, Rue Romain Rolland, Rue Suffren and Rue La Bourdonnais, says Incredible India. See the Governor's Palace (Raj Nivas), Bharathi Park, the Church of Our Lady of Angels and the French War Memorial. France handed Pondicherry over in 1954, and it became part of India in 1963.
- **The Promenade (Rock Beach) on Goubert Avenue:** a seafront walk along the rocks. A police page once listed Beach Road as vehicle-free from 4 PM to 7 AM; we couldn't confirm the current hours.
- **Sri Aurobindo Ashram:** the main building with the Samadhi of Sri Aurobindo and the Mother is open from **8 to 11:30 AM and 2 to 6 PM**, says the Ashram. Outside those hours you need a pass from its Bureau Central. Keep quiet and dress modestly.
- **Puducherry Museum:** Tuesday to Sunday, 10 AM to 5 PM, closed on Mondays (Art and Culture Department). It has finds from Arikamedu and Chola bronzes.
- **Basilica of the Sacred Heart of Jesus:** a red-and-white Gothic church with stained glass.
- **Manakula Vinayagar temple:** a Ganesha temple over 500 years old, near the Ashram.
- **Paradise Beach:** reached by boat from the **Chunnambar boat house**, about 8 km south of town, run by the Tourism Department. Book the boat on [its official site](https://pondytourism.py.gov.in/chunnambar/).
- **Ousteri (Oussudu) lake:** a wetland sanctuary, the first protected area of Puducherry.

## Auroville and the Matrimandir

Auroville is about 15 km from Pondicherry, in Tamil Nadu. From Auroville's official pages:

- **Matrimandir Viewing Point:** free. Get the pass yourself at the **Auroville Visitors Centre** after a short video, from 9 AM to about 5 PM. It's about a 15-minute walk, or a free shuttle.
- **The Inner Chamber** (for concentration inside the Matrimandir) needs a booking on the official site, [mmaccess.auroville.org.in](https://mmaccess.auroville.org.in/visitors/new):
  - Bookings open for the **next 5 working days** only, and the form works from **7 AM to 12 noon**, every day except Tuesday. Booking 3 to 7 days ahead gives the best chance.
  - **One booking per person,** with photo ID. No group bookings, and no bookings through others.
  - The Matrimandir is **closed on Sundays and Tuesdays.** Be free from 8 to 11:30 AM on your day.
  - **Children under 10** can't go in. The chamber is silent, with no photos.
  - **If it rains, visits are cancelled** for the day, with an email by 7:30 AM. Have a back-up day in December.
  - On **30 November 2026** the Inner Chamber is closed while the Golden Door is installed. The Viewing Point stays open.

## Pondicherry trip plan: 3 days

**Day 1: the French Quarter and the Promenade**

1. **Sunrise on the Promenade,** then breakfast in White Town.
2. **Walk the French Quarter:** Raj Nivas, Bharathi Park, the French War Memorial and Our Lady of Angels.
3. **Sri Aurobindo Ashram** (2 to 6 PM) and the **Manakula Vinayagar temple**.
4. **Evening on Beach Road.**

**Day 2: Auroville**

1. **Visitors Centre** for the video and the Viewing Point pass, then walk to the **Matrimandir**.
2. **Inner Chamber,** if you booked it (not on Sundays or Tuesdays).
3. **Afternoon at a beach** north of town if the sea is calm.

**Day 3: Paradise Beach and the town**

1. **Boat from Chunnambar to Paradise Beach,** in the morning.
2. **Puducherry Museum** (closed on Mondays) and the **Basilica**.
3. Shopping and cafés before you leave.

If rain is forecast, swap the days: keep Auroville and the beach for a dry day.

## Getting to Pondicherry

From the Puducherry government and district sites:

- **By road from Chennai:** about **135 km from Chennai airport**, about 2½ hours. Buses from Chennai run about every 15 minutes in the day and take about 3½ hours.
- **From Bengaluru:** about 320 km from Bengaluru airport. Buses run daily, including overnight.
- **By train:** Puducherry station is at the end of a branch line from **Villupuram junction**, about 38 km away. Daily trains run to Villupuram, Chennai and Tirupati, and a few weekly trains go further. Check times on [NTES](https://enquiry.indianrail.gov.in/mntes/) or IRCTC.
- **By air:** Puducherry's small airport has daily flights to **Bengaluru and Hyderabad**, says the district site. Most people fly to **Chennai**.

## Pondicherry at Christmas and New Year

- **Christmas** (Friday 25 December) is a public holiday in Puducherry. The French Quarter's churches, including the Basilica, are busy. We couldn't find official mass times.
- **New Year's Eve on Beach Road** draws huge crowds. Last year the police barricaded the road and kept people off the shore, according to news reports. The 2026–27 rules haven't been announced; check [Puducherry Police](https://police.py.gov.in/) in late December.
- **New Year's Day,** Friday 1 January 2027, is a holiday too, so it's a long weekend. Book early.
- **International Yoga Festival:** the Tourism Department usually holds it from **4 to 7 January**. 2027 dates aren't announced yet.
- **Pongal:** Friday 15 January 2027, with Mattu Pongal on Saturday 16 January (Puducherry's 2027 holiday list).

## What to wear in Pondicherry in December

What should I wear? Try the [what-to-wear helper](../tools/what-to-wear.html#pondicherry) for day and night temperatures and an outfit list by month.

- **Light cotton or linen clothes:** it's about 30 °C by day and humid.
- **Cover your shoulders and knees** for the Ashram, temples and churches.
- **A light rain jacket or a small umbrella,** and sandals or quick-dry shoes for December showers.
- **Swimwear only at the beach,** with a cover-up in town.
- **A cap, sunglasses and sunscreen.** You won't need woollens; a thin layer is enough for a breezy evening.

## Health and safety

- **Don't swim at the Promenade:** it's a rocky seawall, not a swimming beach (general advice). Swim only at calm beaches, and never when IMD warns of rough seas or a cyclone.
- **Check IMD warnings** in early December, and don't go to the shore during a storm.
- **Drink bottled or filtered water,** and carry mosquito repellent after rain.
- **Traffic is heavy at Christmas and New Year.** Leave the car and walk in White Town.

## For foreign visitors

- **Auroville is in Tamil Nadu and Pondicherry is a Union Territory,** but there's no border check between them.
- **Visa:** apply for the e-Tourist Visa only at the official site, [indianvisaonline.gov.in](https://indianvisaonline.gov.in/evisa/tvoa.html).

## Questions

### Is December a good time to visit Pondicherry?

It's warm and green, but December is the wettest month of the winter: IMD's average is about 248 mm of rain on about 6 days, and cyclones have hit in early December. Late December and January are better bets; January is nearly dry.

### What is the weather in Pondicherry in December?

About 30 °C by day and 21 °C at night, from IMD's averages. It's humid, with heavy showers on some days.

### Does it rain in Pondicherry in December end?

It can. IMD says the northeast monsoon season runs to the end of December, and in 2025–26 its rains over Puducherry ended on 19 January. Check IMD Chennai's forecast before you go.

### How do I visit the Matrimandir in Auroville?

For the Viewing Point, get a free pass at the Auroville Visitors Centre after a short video. For the Inner Chamber, book on mmaccess.auroville.org.in, up to 5 working days ahead, between 7 AM and noon. It's closed on Sundays and Tuesdays, and children under 10 can't go in.

### How many days are enough for Pondicherry?

Two to three days: one for the French Quarter and the Ashram, one for Auroville, and one for Paradise Beach and the museum.

### How do I reach Pondicherry from Chennai or Bangalore?

From Chennai airport it's about 135 km by road, about 2½ hours, and buses from Chennai run often. From Bengaluru airport it's about 320 km. Trains go via Villupuram, and small flights go from Bengaluru and Hyderabad.

### What to do in Pondicherry on New Year's Eve?

Most people go to Beach Road, which gets very crowded and is usually barricaded. Book a stay early, walk rather than drive, and check police rules in late December.

### What should I wear in Pondicherry in December?

Light cotton clothes that cover your shoulders and knees for the Ashram and temples, a rain jacket or umbrella, sandals, sunscreen and a cap.
