Can I carry pickle in a flight? **Cabin bag: no on IndiGo, and on Air India only a small amount that isn't chilli pickle, 100 ml or less in your liquids bag. Check-in bag: yes, packed so it can't leak or break.** Pack pickle in check-in and it's fine on every airline. Checked on 28 November 2026.

## By airline

| Airline | Cabin bag | Check-in | Checked |
|---|---|---|---|
| IndiGo | No | Yes, "if properly packed" so it can't be damaged | 28 Nov 2026 |
| [Air India](../air-india.html) | Yes, but "Chilly pickle is only allowed in check-in baggage", and the 100 ml liquids rule applies | Yes | 28 Nov 2026 |
| [Akasa Air](../akasa-air.html) | No oily or strong-smelling food on board | Food must be packed so it can't leak or spill | 28 Nov 2026 |
| [SpiceJet](../spicejet.html) | Not named; airport rules apply | Not named | 15 Nov 2026 (SpiceJet's site was down on 28 Nov) |

The simple rule: **pack pickle in your check-in bag.** That works on every airline we checked.

## Pickle counts as a liquid in the cabin

Pickle sits in oil or masala paste. Air India's list of liquids names oils, and anything with a similar texture at room temperature. So even on Air India, pickle in the cabin must be in a container of **100 ml or less**, inside your clear 1-litre liquids bag.

A normal jar of 250 g or 500 g is bigger than that, so it won't pass security in your cabin bag.

## How much pickle in check-in?

None of the airline lists we checked sets a limit for pickle. It counts towards your check-in weight, and a few glass jars get heavy fast. Check your fare's allowance on our [airline baggage rules](../index.html) pages.

## How to pack pickle so it doesn't leak

- Use a plastic jar with a screw lid if you can. IndiGo's list says glass items in check-in must be packed properly so they don't break.
- Wipe the rim, put a piece of cling film under the lid, close it tight and tape the lid all round.
- Put the jar in two zip bags or plastic bags, one inside the other.
- Stand it upright in the middle of the bag, with clothes around it. A hard trolley protects jars better than a soft bag.
- Oil stains, so keep it away from clothes you can't wash easily.

## What airport security (BCAS) says

BCAS doesn't name pickle. Two of its rules catch it in the cabin: the 100 ml liquids rule, because pickle is oily and paste-like, and the ban on all spices "in powder or physical form including chilly" (Kannur Airport security list, based on BCAS rules). That's why chilli pickle in particular is check-in only. In check-in, pickle isn't on any banned list we read.

## Taking pickle on an international flight

**Leaving India,** pickle goes in your check-in bag, as on domestic flights. Air India allows the same on its international flights. **Arriving abroad,** the other country's food rules apply. We read them on 28 November 2026:

| Country | Pickle on arrival | What the official page says |
|---|---|---|
| USA | Vegetable pickle: declare it | Pickle isn't named, but US Customs (CBP) says "condiments, vinegars, oils, packaged spices" are generally admissible, and almost anything with meat isn't. Declare all food. |
| UK | Shop-sealed vegetable pickle: likely fine | GOV.UK doesn't name pickle. It allows processed and packaged plant products from any country. Meat and fish products from India aren't allowed, so leave out prawn, fish or chicken pickle. |
| Singapore | Yes, within 5 kg | The Singapore Food Agency's list names kimchi, a pickle, as processed food. All processed food together can be up to 5 kg per traveller. |
| UAE | Small amounts for yourself | Dubai Customs' 2025 guide lists food products as needing permits, and commercial quantities aren't allowed. We found no kg limit for pickle. See our [Dubai customs guide](../customs/uae-dubai.html). |
| Saudi Arabia | Small amounts for yourself | The GCC customs guide (2025) allows personal belongings and gifts in non-commercial quantities. It sets no food limit. |

Shop-sealed jars with an ingredients label clear customs more easily than homemade pickle in a plain box. Wrap them as below, because a leak in a long international journey ruins a whole bag.

## What to do instead

- Flying IndiGo with only a cabin bag? Leave the pickle at home, or send it with someone who has a check-in bag.
- On Air India, a small sachet of pickle that isn't chilli pickle can go in your liquids bag.
- Carrying ghee too? See our [ghee rules](ghee.html), and for sweets and snacks our [home food rules](sweets-and-home-food.html).

## Questions

### Can I carry pickle in my cabin bag?

Not on IndiGo. Air India allows pickle in the cabin but not chilli pickle, and oily pickle must be in a container of 100 ml or less inside your liquids bag.

### Can I carry pickle in my check-in bag?

Yes. IndiGo and Air India allow pickle in check-in if it's packed so it can't leak or break.

### Can I carry pickle on an IndiGo flight?

Only in your check-in bag. IndiGo's list doesn't allow pickle in the cabin.

### Is there a weight limit for pickle?

No airline list we checked sets one. It counts towards your check-in weight allowance.

### Can I carry pickle in cabin baggage on IndiGo?

No. IndiGo's list marks pickles as not allowed in the cabin, and allowed in check-in if properly packed (checked 28 Nov 2026).

### Is pickle allowed in Air India's cabin baggage?

A small amount, yes, but not chilli pickle: Air India says chilli pickle is only allowed in check-in. Any pickle in the cabin must be 100 ml or less, inside your clear liquids bag.

### Can I carry pickle on an international flight?

Yes, in your check-in bag, packed so it can't leak. The country you land in decides the rest: Singapore allows it within 5 kg of processed food, and the USA asks you to declare all food (checked 28 Nov 2026).

### Can I carry pickle to the USA?

Vegetable pickle like mango or lemon pickle, declared on arrival, is usually fine: CBP says condiments and oils are generally admissible. Leave out pickle with meat or fish.

### Can I carry pickle to Dubai?

Small amounts for your own use, yes, in check-in. Dubai Customs says food in commercial quantities isn't allowed and gives no kg limit.

### Can I carry mango pickle on a flight?

Yes, in your check-in bag, sealed and wrapped. Mango pickle has chilli and oil, so keep it out of the cabin on every airline.
