Can I carry a nail cutter in a flight? **Cabin bag: yes, a plain nail cutter without a fold-out knife or scissors (Air India allows it in both bags). Check-in bag: yes, any nail cutter.** A cutter with a knife, and scissors, go in check-in. Checked on 28 November 2026.

## What Air India's list says

Air India's list allows these in **both the cabin bag and check-in**:

- Nail clippers without a knife
- Cuticle cutters (nail cutters without a knife)
- Nail files
- Tweezers
- Eyelash curlers (manual ones)

IndiGo's list doesn't name nail cutters. It bans knives, scissors and other sharp instruments in the cabin.

## Check your nail cutter for a knife

Some nail cutters have a small fold-out knife, and grooming sets often add a pair of small scissors.

- **No knife, no scissors:** fine in your cabin bag.
- **Fold-out knife:** it counts as a knife, and knives are banned in the cabin. Pack it in check-in.
- **Small scissors in the set:** scissors are banned in the cabin (IndiGo, Air India). Take them out, or pack the whole set in check-in.

If you fly with only a cabin bag, carry a plain nail cutter without a knife.

## Scissors

| Airline | Cabin bag | Check-in |
|---|---|---|
| IndiGo | No | Yes |
| Air India | No | Yes |

The Airports Authority of India's list bans metal scissors with pointed tips in the cabin, and the airport security list (based on BCAS rules) bans scissors in the cabin. In check-in, Air India asks that sharp things are covered or wrapped so they can't hurt baggage staff.

Air India is the only Indian airline that names nail cutters. Airport security applies the same rules to every airline, so a plain cutter is fine whichever one you fly.

## What airport security (BCAS) says

BCAS's list of items banned in the cabin includes knives, except round-bladed butter knives, metal scissors with pointed tips, and razor blades not in a cartridge. It doesn't name nail cutters. The Kannur Airport security list (based on BCAS rules) bans "Scissors" and "Knives with blades of any length" in the cabin. So the knife or scissors in a grooming kit is the problem, not the clipper itself. Security staff make the final call, so a plain, small cutter is the safe choice.

## Flying abroad with a nail cutter

**Leaving India,** the rules above apply. **On the flight home,** these countries' airport rules apply. We read them on 28 November 2026:

| Country | Nail cutter in hand luggage | Scissors in hand luggage | What the official page says |
|---|---|---|---|
| UK | Yes | Only small ones, blades up to 6 cm | GOV.UK allows nail clippers, nail files and tweezers in hand and hold luggage. Scissors with blades over 6 cm go in the hold. |
| USA | Yes | Only under 4 inches (about 10 cm) from the pivot | TSA allows nail clippers in carry-on and checked bags. Wrap sharp objects in checked bags. |
| UAE, Singapore, Saudi Arabia | Pack a plain one | Pack in check-in | The government pages we read don't name nail cutters. Follow your airline's rules; a plain cutter in the cabin and scissors in check-in works everywhere. |

Abroad the UK and the USA allow small scissors in hand luggage, but India doesn't. Flying back to India with a connection? The Indian rule applies at the Indian airport, so move scissors to check-in.

## Tweezers, razors and trimmers

- **Tweezers:** fine in both bags (Air India).
- **Razors:** safety and disposable razors are fine in both bags, loose blades go in check-in. See our [razor rules](razor.html).
- **Trimmers:** one with a built-in battery is fine in the cabin. See our [trimmer rules](trimmer.html).

## Packing a grooming kit

- Keep the nail cutter, tweezers and nail file in the kit in your cabin bag.
- Move the scissors and any knife to your check-in bag, wrapped in a sock or a cloth.
- Creams and liquids in the kit follow the 100 ml rule in the cabin.
- Security staff can still stop any item they think is sharp, so keep the cabin kit simple.

## Questions

### Can I carry a nail cutter in my cabin bag?

Yes, if it has no fold-out knife or scissors. Air India's list allows nail clippers without a knife in both bags.

### Can I carry a nail cutter on IndiGo?

IndiGo's list doesn't name nail cutters, but it bans knives, scissors and other sharp instruments in the cabin. Take a plain cutter without a knife, or pack it in check-in.

### Can I carry a nail cutter in my check-in bag?

Yes, any nail cutter, including one with a knife or scissors.

### Can I carry scissors in my check-in bag?

Yes. IndiGo and Air India allow scissors in check-in. They are banned in the cabin.

### Can I carry tweezers or a nail file?

Yes. Air India's list allows both in your cabin bag and in check-in.

### Is a nail cutter allowed in Air India's cabin baggage?

Yes. Air India's restricted items page allows nail clippers without a knife, cuticle cutters, nail files and tweezers in both the cabin bag and check-in (checked 28 Nov 2026).

### Is a nail cutter allowed on an IndiGo domestic flight?

IndiGo's list doesn't name nail cutters. It bans knives, scissors and other sharp instruments in the cabin. A plain cutter without a knife is treated like Air India's "nail clippers without knife"; airport security makes the final call.

### Can I carry a nail cutter on an international flight?

Yes, a plain one. The UK and the USA allow nail clippers in hand luggage and checked bags (GOV.UK and TSA, checked 28 Nov 2026). Leaving India, a cutter with a knife or scissors goes in check-in.

### Where should I keep a nail cutter on a flight?

A plain one can stay in your cabin bag, in a pouch with your tweezers and nail file. A cutter with a fold-out knife, or a grooming set with scissors, goes in your check-in bag, wrapped in a cloth.
