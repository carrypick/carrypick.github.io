Can I carry a hair dryer in a flight? **Yes, in your cabin bag and in check-in: IndiGo's list allows it in both. A plug-in hair straightener isn't named or banned on any Indian airline list we checked. Hair curlers that run on gas are banned in both bags.** Checked on 28 November 2026.

## The short answer

| Item | Cabin bag | Check-in |
|---|---|---|
| Hair dryer | Yes (IndiGo) | Yes (IndiGo) |
| Plug-in hair straightener | Not named | Not named |
| Hair curler that runs on gas | No | No |
| Cordless straightener with a battery | Ask your airline | Ask your airline |
| Hair spray, hair gel | 100 ml or less, in your liquids bag | Yes |

## Hair dryer

IndiGo's list marks a **hair dryer as allowed in both the cabin bag and check-in**. It counts towards your bag's weight, so a light travel dryer helps if your cabin bag is close to 7 kg.

## Hair straightener

Straighteners aren't named on IndiGo's, Air India's or Akasa Air's lists, or on SpiceJet's pages. None of them bans a plug-in straightener either, and IndiGo allows a hair dryer, another electric hair tool, in both bags.

Because no list names it, we can't give you a firm rule. If you want to be sure, ask your airline before you fly. Security staff make the final decision at the checkpoint.

Let it cool fully before you pack it, and keep it in a heat-proof pouch if it came with one.

## Gas hair curlers: banned

Air India's list bans **hair curlers that contain gas** in any bag. IndiGo's list bans gases such as butane in both bags. So leave gas-powered curlers and their refills at home.

## Cordless straighteners

A cordless straightener has a lithium battery. The Indian lists we checked don't name them, so ask your airline first. If it's allowed, battery rules apply: Akasa Air, for example, allows up to 15 battery devices per person, each battery up to 100 Wh, and in check-in a device must be fully switched off and protected from turning on. Spare batteries go only in your cabin bag.

## Hair spray, gel and serum

These are liquids. IndiGo's list names hair gels and hair sprays. In the cabin, each one must be **100 ml or less**, inside your clear 1-litre liquids bag. In check-in, Air India allows toiletry sprays up to 500 ml a can and 2 litres in total, with the cap on. See our [100 ml rule page](liquids.html).

"Not named" means the airline's list doesn't mention it, and doesn't ban it either. A hair dryer and a plug-in straightener have no battery and no gas, so nothing on the dangerous goods lists covers them.

## What airport security (BCAS) says

BCAS's list of items banned in the cabin doesn't include hair dryers or straighteners. The one hair tool it rules out is the gas kind: the international rules allow one gas hair curler with a safety cover, but IndiGo's dangerous goods table (valid 28 April to 31 December 2026) marks it **"As per BCAS not allowed."** Spare gas cartridges are banned in every bag.

## Flying abroad with a hair dryer or straightener

**Leaving India,** the rules above apply. **On the flight home,** these countries' own airport rules apply. We read them on 28 November 2026:

| Country | Hair dryer | Straightener | What the official page says |
|---|---|---|---|
| UK | Yes, both bags | Yes, both bags | GOV.UK's table allows a "hairdryer or straighteners" in hand and hold luggage. Gas hair curlers are allowed in either bag with the safety cover on. |
| USA | Yes, both bags | Corded: both bags. Cordless: carry-on only | TSA's list allows hair dryers in both bags. Cordless straighteners and curling irons go in carry-on bags with a cover over the heating element; gas refills aren't allowed. |
| UAE, Singapore, Saudi Arabia | No customs limit | No customs limit | Your own hair tools are personal belongings. The government pages we read don't name them, so follow your airline's rules. |

Taking a hair dryer abroad? Check the plug and voltage. The UK, UAE, Singapore and Saudi Arabia use 230 V like India, the USA uses 120 V. Our [power bank and travel adapter guide](../../guides/power-bank-and-travel-adapter-guide.html) lists the plug type for each country.

## What to do instead

- Short trip with only a cabin bag? Your hotel may have a hair dryer, so ask before you pack one.
- Going to a wedding? Pack the straightener in check-in, wrapped in clothes, and keep hair spray under 100 ml in your cabin bag.
- Packing a trimmer too? See our [trimmer rules](trimmer.html).

## Questions

### Can I carry a hair dryer in my cabin bag?

Yes. IndiGo's list allows a hair dryer in both the cabin bag and check-in.

### Can I carry a hair straightener in my cabin bag?

Straighteners aren't named on the airline lists we checked, and none of them bans a plug-in one. Ask your airline if you want to be sure.

### Can I carry a hair straightener in my check-in bag?

The lists don't name it. Let it cool, wrap it in clothes, and ask your airline if you want to be sure.

### Can I carry hair spray on a flight?

Yes. In the cabin, a can of 100 ml or less inside your clear liquids bag. In check-in, up to 500 ml a can and 2 litres in total.

### Can I carry a hair dryer on an IndiGo flight?

Yes. IndiGo's list allows a hair dryer in your cabin bag and your check-in bag (checked 28 Nov 2026). It counts towards your 7 kg cabin weight.

### Is a hair dryer allowed on Air India flights?

Air India's restricted items page doesn't name hair dryers, and doesn't ban them. The only hair tool it bans is a hair curler that contains gas.

### Is a hair straightener allowed on an IndiGo flight?

IndiGo's list doesn't name hair straighteners. It allows a hair dryer, another plug-in hair tool, in both bags, and nothing on its dangerous goods list covers a plug-in straightener. Ask IndiGo if you want a firm answer.

### Can I carry a hair dryer on an international flight?

Yes. The UK and the USA allow hair dryers in both hand and checked luggage (GOV.UK and TSA, checked 28 Nov 2026). Bring a plug adapter, and check the voltage for the USA.

### Can I carry a Dyson hair dryer in my cabin bag?

A plug-in Dyson dryer is a hair dryer, so IndiGo's rule applies: allowed in both bags. It has no battery. The Dyson cordless straightener does have one, so ask your airline about it.

### Can I carry a cordless hair straightener on a flight?

The Indian airline lists we checked don't name cordless straighteners. Abroad, TSA allows them in carry-on bags only, with a cover over the heating element. Keep it in your cabin bag and ask your airline first.
