## What's allowed

| Item | Cabin bag | Check-in |
|---|---|---|
| Safety or disposable razor, blades in a cartridge | Yes | Yes |
| Loose razor blades | No | Yes, wrapped |
| Shaving brush | Yes | Yes |
| Shaving foam or gel | 100 ml or less, in your liquids bag | Yes |
| Trimmer with a built-in battery | Yes | Yes, switched off |

## Safety and disposable razors

Air India's list allows **safety razors, including disposable ones, in both bags**. The Airports Authority of India's list bans razor blades that aren't in a cartridge, but leaves out safety razors. So an ordinary razor with the blades fixed in a cartridge can go in your cabin bag.

## Loose blades

- IndiGo's list marks **blades as not allowed in the cabin**, and allowed in check-in.
- Air India bans razor blades not in a cartridge in the cabin, along with box cutters and utility knives, and allows them in check-in.
- The airport security list (based on BCAS rules) bans razor blades in the cabin.

Keep loose blades in their packet or a blade case in your check-in bag. Air India asks that sharp things in check-in are covered or wrapped, so baggage staff can't get cut.

## Old-style razors with loose blades

Some razors take a loose double-edge blade, and a barber's razor holds an open blade. The lists don't say how they treat a loose blade fitted inside a razor. To be safe, pack these razors and their spare blades in check-in.

## Shaving foam, gel and aftershave

These are liquids. In the cabin, each one must be **100 ml or less**, inside your clear 1-litre liquids bag. Bigger cans go in check-in, where Air India allows toiletry sprays up to 500 ml a can and 2 litres in total, with the cap on. See our [perfume and deo rules](perfume.html) for sprays.

## Electric shavers and trimmers

A rechargeable trimmer or shaver has a built-in battery. It can go in your cabin bag, and in check-in it must be fully switched off. Our [trimmer page](trimmer.html) has the details, including IndiGo's rule for trimmers without a battery.

## What to do instead

- Cabin bag only? Take a disposable or cartridge razor and a travel-size foam of 100 ml or less.
- Leave loose blades at home and buy them at your destination.
- Grooming kit with scissors? See our [nail cutter and scissors rules](nail-cutter.html).

## Questions

### Can I carry a razor in my cabin bag?

Yes, a safety or disposable razor with the blades in a cartridge (Air India, Airports Authority of India). Loose blades aren't allowed in the cabin.

### Can I carry razor blades in my check-in bag?

Yes. Keep them in their packet or a blade case, so nobody gets cut.

### Can I carry a disposable razor on a flight?

Yes. Air India's list allows disposable razors in both the cabin bag and check-in.

### Can I carry shaving foam in my cabin bag?

Only a can of 100 ml or less, inside your clear 1-litre liquids bag. A bigger can goes in check-in.
