Can I carry a laptop in a flight? **Cabin bag: yes, on every airline in India. Take it out of your bag at security. Check-in bag: it depends on the airline. IndiGo allows it, Air India only with the battery taken out, Akasa Air only fully switched off, and SpiceJet says don't.** Checked on 28 November 2026.

## Cabin bag: yes, on every airline

Every airline we checked allows a laptop in your cabin bag. At the security check, take it out of your bag and put it in a tray on its own (airport security list).

Most airlines also let you carry a laptop bag along with your cabin bag:

| Airline | Laptop bag with your cabin bag |
|---|---|
| IndiGo | One personal item up to 3 kg, like a small laptop bag |
| Air India | One small bag up to 3 kg and 40 x 30 x 20 cm |
| Akasa Air | A laptop in a slim bag, up to 3 kg, under the seat |
| SpiceJet | Allowed, but it counts inside the same 7 kg |
| Air India Express | Up to 30 x 40 x 10 cm, inside the same 7 kg |

Check your cabin bag's size with our [cabin bag checker](../checker.html), and see each airline's limits on our [baggage rules](../index.html) pages.

## Check-in bag: the airlines differ

| Airline | Laptop in check-in |
|---|---|
| IndiGo | Allowed, "subject to BCAS regulations" |
| Air India | Only with the battery taken out. Carry the battery in your cabin bag |
| Akasa Air | Only fully switched off, protected from turning on and packed securely |
| SpiceJet | No. Its terms say not to put computers in check-in (checked 15 Nov 2026; SpiceJet's site was down on 28 Nov) |

IndiGo's dangerous goods table (valid 28 April to 31 December 2026) adds that a laptop in checked baggage "must be completely switched off", and that each battery must be 100 Wh or less. Air India and IndiGo were checked on 28 November 2026.

Air India's rule applies to all its flights leaving India. Most new laptops have a battery you can't take out, so on Air India keep the laptop in your cabin bag.

Even where check-in is allowed, the cabin is safer. Check-in bags get thrown around and can be late or lost, and IndiGo says it takes no responsibility for electronics packed in check-in.

## Chargers, power banks and spare batteries

- **Laptop charger:** fine in both bags (IndiGo).
- **Power bank:** cabin bag only, never in check-in. See our [power bank rules](power-bank.html).
- **Spare laptop battery:** cabin bag only, with the ends covered. Air India allows up to 2 spare batteries, each up to 100 Wh.

## Two or more laptops

IndiGo allows more than two laptops in the cabin. Like Akasa Air, it sets a limit of **15 battery devices per person**, each battery up to 100 Wh. That covers laptops, phones, tablets and cameras together.

Watch the weight: two laptops with their chargers can easily use half of a 7 kg cabin allowance.

Bringing a new laptop home from abroad? Indian Customs allows one new laptop duty free per passenger aged 18 or older. See our [customs rules for iPhones and laptops](../customs/iphone-and-laptop.html).

## Big batteries and old MacBooks

- The usual limit is **100 Wh** per battery. Batteries over 100 Wh need your airline's approval, and Akasa Air doesn't allow laptops over 100 Wh.
- Air India and SpiceJet ban some **old 15-inch MacBook Pro laptops sold between September 2015 and February 2017**, because of a battery problem. Check your serial number on Apple's support site.

## What airport security (BCAS) says

BCAS doesn't ban laptops in either bag. Its rule is about screening: "Laptops in hand baggage will need to be removed from your bag and screened separately," as the Kannur Airport security list (based on BCAS rules) puts it. The same list bans spare laptop batteries and power banks in check-in. So a spare battery always travels in your cabin bag, whatever the airline allows for the laptop itself. Akasa Air also says laptops can be used on board only while the plane is cruising.

## Flying abroad with a laptop

**Leaving India,** the rules above apply. Keep the laptop in your cabin bag: on long international trips, check-in bags change planes and are more likely to be lost. **Arriving abroad,** a laptop for your own use is fine. We read each government's page on 28 November 2026:

| Country | Laptop in hand luggage | Laptop in checked bags | Customs |
|---|---|---|---|
| UAE | Yes | Yes, switched off | Personal luggage, gifts and perfume are duty free up to AED 3,000 in value. A new laptop for sale or as a gift counts towards it. |
| UK | Yes | Yes | GOV.UK's table allows laptops in hand and hold luggage. Security may ask you to switch it on, so charge it. |
| USA | Yes | Yes | TSA: remove the laptop from your bag and put it in a separate bin for X-ray. |
| Singapore | Yes | Yes, switched off | Your own used laptop is fine. New goods count towards the GST relief; declare anything above it. |
| Saudi Arabia | Yes | Yes, switched off | Personal belongings and gifts are duty free up to SAR 3,000 in value (GCC customs guide, 2025). |

**Coming back to India** with a new laptop? Indian Customs' Baggage Rules, 2026 allow one new laptop free of duty per passenger aged 18 or older, on top of the ₹75,000 allowance. See our [customs rules for iPhones and laptops](../customs/iphone-and-laptop.html).

## Questions

### Can I carry a laptop in my cabin bag?

Yes, on every airline we checked. Take it out of your bag at the security check and put it in a tray on its own.

### Can I carry a laptop in my check-in bag?

It depends on the airline. IndiGo allows it. Air India wants the battery taken out and carried in the cabin. Akasa Air needs it fully switched off. SpiceJet says not to. The cabin is safer on every airline.

### Can I carry a laptop charger in check-in?

Yes. IndiGo's list allows a laptop charger in both the cabin bag and check-in.

### Can I carry two laptops on a flight?

Yes. IndiGo and Akasa Air allow up to 15 battery devices per person, each battery up to 100 Wh. Keep your cabin bag within its weight limit.

### Does a laptop bag count in the 7 kg cabin limit?

On SpiceJet and Air India Express, yes. IndiGo, Air India and Akasa Air allow a small personal bag of up to 3 kg, such as a laptop bag, with your cabin bag.

### Can I carry a laptop on an IndiGo flight?

Yes, in your cabin bag, and in check-in too. IndiGo's dangerous goods table says a laptop in check-in must be completely switched off, with a battery of 100 Wh or less (checked 28 Nov 2026).

### Is a laptop bag allowed on IndiGo with a cabin bag?

Yes. IndiGo allows one personal item of up to 3 kg, such as a small laptop bag, on top of your 7 kg cabin bag.

### Can I carry a laptop on an international flight?

Yes, in your cabin bag on every airline. The UK and the USA allow laptops in both hand and checked luggage (GOV.UK and TSA, checked 28 Nov 2026). The cabin is safer, because checked bags can be lost on connecting flights.

### Do I need to take my laptop out of the bag at security?

Yes. Laptops in hand baggage are removed from the bag and screened on their own at Indian airports (BCAS rules). TSA asks the same in the USA.

### Can I carry 4 laptops on a flight?

IndiGo and Akasa Air allow up to 15 battery devices per person, each battery up to 100 Wh, so four laptops are within the limit. Four new laptops coming into India are a customs question: only one new laptop is duty free per adult.

### Can I carry a laptop stand on a flight?

The airline lists we checked don't name laptop stands. A folding plastic or aluminium stand with no sharp parts is usually treated as an ordinary item. If it has a sharp edge, pack it in check-in.
