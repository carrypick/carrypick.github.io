## Dry fruits: yes in both bags

IndiGo's list allows **dry fruits in the cabin bag and in check-in**, and dry foods in both bags too. Air India's list doesn't name dry fruits.

IndiGo's food rules are the strictest of the airline pages we checked, so dry fruits that pass its list are a safe choice on any airline. That covers kaju, badam, kishmish, akhrot and pista.

## The one exception: dry coconut

Dry coconut, also called copra, gola or khopra, is **banned in both bags**. IndiGo allows dry fruits in check-in only "without copra", and Air India and the airport security list ban copra completely. Take it out of mixed dry fruit packs before you pack them. See our [coconut rules](coconut.html).

## Honey

Honey is runny, so in the cabin it counts as a liquid. IndiGo's list allows honey **up to 100 ml in the cabin**, and in check-in if it's **sealed and packed properly**. For other liquids, see our [100 ml rule page](liquids.html).

## Gift boxes for weddings and festivals

- A small box of dry fruits can go in your cabin bag, but it counts towards the cabin weight limit, usually 7 kg.
- Big gift boxes are easier in check-in. Put them in a plastic bag so they don't get crushed or wet.
- Dry fruits are heavy. Weigh your bag at home so you don't pay extra at the airport.

## Flying abroad

Other countries have their own rules on food at customs. This page is about flights within India, so check the customs rules of the country you're flying to.

Flying back into India with dry fruits, dates or sweets as gifts? Gifts count towards your duty-free allowance. See our [Indian customs rules](../customs.html).

## What to do instead

- Carry a small pack of dry fruits in your cabin bag as a snack for the journey.
- Keep big gift boxes and honey jars for check-in.
- Packing sweets too? See our [sweets and home food rules](sweets-and-home-food.html).

## Questions

### Can I carry dry fruits in my cabin bag?

Yes. IndiGo's list allows dry fruits in the cabin bag.

### Can I carry dry fruits in my check-in bag?

Yes, but not dry coconut (copra), which is banned in both bags.

### Can I carry honey on a flight?

Yes. Up to 100 ml in your cabin bag, inside the clear liquids bag, and in check-in sealed and packed properly (IndiGo).

### Can I carry dry fruits to another country?

Indian airlines allow them, but the country you land in has its own customs rules for food. Check them before you fly.
