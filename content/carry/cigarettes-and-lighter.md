Can I carry cigarettes in a flight? **Cigarettes: yes, in your cabin bag and check-in (IndiGo). Lighter: no, in either bag, because airport security in India bans it. Matches: no. E-cigarettes and vapes: banned completely.** You can't smoke on board. Checked on 28 November 2026.

## The short answer

| Item | Cabin bag | Check-in |
|---|---|---|
| Cigarettes | Yes (IndiGo) | Yes (IndiGo) |
| Lighter | No | No |
| Matchbox | Rules differ | No |
| E-cigarette or vape | No | No |

## Cigarettes

IndiGo's list marks **cigarettes as allowed in both the cabin bag and check-in**. Air India's and Akasa Air's lists don't name cigarettes, and don't ban them either.

You can't smoke anywhere on the plane. Akasa Air's rules say smoking is strictly banned and the toilets have smoke detectors, and tampering with them is not allowed.

## Lighter: banned in both bags

- Air India's list bans **lighters, gun lighters and lighter fluid** in any bag.
- The airport security list (based on BCAS rules) bans lighters in both the cabin bag and check-in.
- The Airports Authority of India's list bans lighters in the cabin.

IndiGo's own page names lighter fuel, lighter refills and lighters that must be turned upside down to light. Airport security in India follows the BCAS rules for every airline, so leave lighters at home whichever airline you fly.

A rechargeable USB lighter doesn't get round the rule. Akasa Air's list of dangerous goods includes lighters powered by lithium batteries.

If you find a lighter in your bag at the airport, hand it over at security. Air India asks passengers to tell the security staff about such items and leave them as instructed.

## Matches

The rules don't agree here:

- IndiGo's and SpiceJet's pages say matches may be carried **on your person**, not in your bags.
- The airport security list (based on BCAS rules) bans safety matches in **both bags**.

Security can take them, so don't carry matches.

## E-cigarettes and vapes: banned completely

E-cigarettes, vapes, heated tobacco devices and e-hookahs are banned in both bags (IndiGo, Air India, Akasa Air). Akasa Air's rules add that e-cigarettes can't be taken out of India or brought into India, and it can refuse to fly a passenger who carries one.

## What airport security (BCAS) says

IndiGo's dangerous goods table (valid 28 April to 31 December 2026) settles the lighter question. The international rules allow one small lighter or one small packet of safety matches carried on you, but next to that row IndiGo writes **"As per BCAS not allowed."** BCAS's list bans lighters in the cabin, and the Kannur Airport security list (based on BCAS rules) puts lighters, safety matches and e-cigarettes under items banned in both cabin and check-in. So at Indian airports, no lighter and no matches, whatever the airline's own page says.

## How many cigarettes can I bring into India?

Indian Customs' Baggage Rules, 2026 allow **100 cigarettes, or 25 cigars, or 125 g of tobacco** per passenger without duty. A full carton of 200 is double that, and the extra pays the full duty. See our [customs rules for alcohol and cigarettes](../customs/alcohol-and-cigarettes.html).

## Flying abroad with cigarettes

**Leaving India,** cigarettes go in your cabin or check-in bag, but no lighter. **Arriving abroad,** each country sets its own duty-free limit. We read each government's page on 28 November 2026:

| Country | Cigarettes duty free | Lighters and vapes | What the official page says |
|---|---|---|---|
| UAE | 200 cigarettes, or 50 cigars, or 500 g of tobacco | E-cigarettes are restricted: declare them | The UAE government portal (updated 27 Oct 2026) gives 200; some older Dubai Customs pages still say 400. Not allowed for travellers under 18. See our [Dubai customs guide](../customs/uae-dubai.html). |
| UK | 200 cigarettes, or 100 cigarillos, or 50 cigars, or 250 g of tobacco | One lighter, on you, in a clear plastic bag; not in hold luggage. E-cigarettes in hand luggage only | You can split the allowance, for example 100 cigarettes and 25 cigars. No allowance for under-17s. |
| USA | Declare tobacco | TSA allows disposable and Zippo lighters in carry-on bags; in checked bags only without fuel. Torch lighters are banned. E-cigarettes in carry-on only | TSA allows cigarettes in both bags. The CBP pages we read don't give a tobacco number, so declare cigarettes on arrival. |
| Singapore | None | Vapes and e-cigarettes are banned | "There is no duty-free concession or GST import relief for cigarettes and tobacco products." Declare every packet at the Red Channel and pay the duty, or face fines. |
| Saudi Arabia | 200 cigarettes | Not stated on the page we read | The GCC customs guide (2025) says exempt cigarettes "must not exceed 200 cigarettes". |

Coming home to India? The Indian limit of 100 cigarettes applies, even if you were allowed 200 abroad.

## What to do instead

- Keep your cigarettes in their packet in your cabin bag.
- Buy a lighter or matches after you land if you need them.
- Pack nothing that can make a flame. Security checks every bag.

## Questions

### Can I carry cigarettes on a domestic flight in India?

Yes. IndiGo's list allows cigarettes in both the cabin bag and check-in. You can't smoke on board.

### Can I carry a lighter in my check-in bag?

No. Lighters are banned in both bags (Air India, airport security list based on BCAS rules). Lighter fluid and refills are banned too.

### Can I carry a matchbox on a flight?

The rules differ. IndiGo and SpiceJet allow matches only on your person, not in bags, and the airport security list bans them in both bags. Leave them at home.

### Can I carry an e-cigarette or vape on a flight?

No. E-cigarettes and vapes are banned in both bags on flights in India.

### Can I smoke on the plane?

No. Smoking is banned on board, and the toilets have smoke detectors.

### Can I carry cigarettes on an IndiGo flight?

Yes. IndiGo's list allows cigarettes in your cabin bag and your check-in bag. You can't smoke on board, and you can't carry a lighter or matches.

### Can I carry a lighter on an IndiGo flight?

No. IndiGo's dangerous goods table says a small lighter is "As per BCAS not allowed". Airport security in India bans lighters in both bags on every airline.

### Is a cigarette allowed on Air India flights?

Air India's restricted items page doesn't name cigarettes, and doesn't ban them. It does ban lighters, gun lighters, lighter fluid and e-cigarettes in any bag (checked 28 Nov 2026).

### How many cigarettes can I take on an international flight?

The airline doesn't set the limit: the country you land in does. The UAE, the UK and Saudi Arabia allow 200 cigarettes duty free, Singapore allows none, and India allows 100 when you come back (checked 28 Nov 2026).

### Can I carry cigarettes to Dubai from India?

Yes, up to 200 cigarettes, or 50 cigars, or 500 g of tobacco, duty free, if you're 18 or older (UAE government portal, updated 27 Oct 2026). Don't take a lighter on the flight from India.

### Can I carry cigarettes to Singapore?

Yes, but every packet pays duty. Singapore Customs gives no duty-free allowance for cigarettes, so declare them at the Red Channel. E-cigarettes and vapes are banned in Singapore.

### Can I carry cigarettes to Saudi Arabia?

Yes, up to 200 cigarettes duty free, under the GCC customs guide (2025) that Saudi Arabia uses.

### Can I carry cigarettes to the USA?

Yes. TSA allows cigarettes in carry-on and checked bags. Declare tobacco to US Customs when you arrive.
