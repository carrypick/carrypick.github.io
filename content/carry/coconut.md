Can I carry coconut in a flight? **Cabin bag: no, on IndiGo and Air India. Check-in bag: yes on Air India, and small pieces only on IndiGo. Dry coconut (copra, gola, khopra) is banned in both bags on every airline.** Checked on 28 November 2026.

## The short answer

| Item | Cabin bag | Check-in |
|---|---|---|
| Coconut, whole or in pieces | No | Air India: yes. IndiGo: small pieces only |
| Dry coconut (copra, gola, khopra) | No | No |
| Coconut oil | 100 ml or less in your liquids bag, not on IndiGo | Up to 5 litres |
| Coconut water | 100 ml or less in your liquids bag | Not stated |

## Coconut: IndiGo and Air India differ

- **IndiGo:** no coconut in the cabin bag. In check-in, its list allows **small pieces only**, and never dry coconut.
- **Air India:** no coconut in the cabin bag. In check-in, its list allows **coconut**. It also names "coconut crushed (green tissue)" as allowed in both bags, while crushed dry coconut is banned.

So a whole coconut, for example one for a puja, can go in check-in on Air India. On IndiGo, its list allows only small pieces, so break it first or ask IndiGo before you fly.

## Dry coconut: banned in both bags

Dry coconut, also called copra, gola or khopra, is **banned in both the cabin bag and check-in**:

- Air India's list bans copra and crushed dry coconut.
- IndiGo's list says no dry coconut or copra, even in check-in, and its dry fruits row says "without copra". Other [dry fruits](dry-fruits.html), such as kaju, badam and kishmish, are fine in both bags.
- The airport security list (based on BCAS rules) bans copra in both bags.
- Akasa Air lists copra among flammable solids, because dry coconut can catch fire.

Dry grated coconut, often sold as coconut powder, is dry coconut too, so leave it out of your bags.

Air India also allows "coconut crushed (green tissue)", meaning fresh grated coconut, in both bags, while crushed dry coconut is prohibited.

## What airport security (BCAS) says

The Kannur Airport security list, based on BCAS rules, puts **copra** with lighters, safety matches, camphor and e-cigarettes, under items banned in both cabin and check-in. Dry coconut is oily and dry at the same time, so it can heat up and catch fire. That's why it's treated like a flammable solid, and why the ban covers check-in too. Fresh coconut isn't on the BCAS-based list; the cabin ban for it comes from the airlines' own lists, which don't give a reason.

## Coconut sweets and laddu

Coconut laddu and barfi aren't named on any list we checked. IndiGo's list allows dry sweets in both bags. If you're carrying a big box, ask your airline before you fly. See our [sweets and home food rules](sweets-and-home-food.html).

## Coconut oil

- **Check-in:** up to 5 litres or 5 kg per person (Air India, airport security list).
- **Cabin bag:** only in containers of 100 ml or less, inside your clear 1-litre liquids bag. IndiGo's list doesn't allow oily items in the cabin at all.

It follows the same rules as ghee. See our [ghee and coconut oil page](ghee.html).

## Coconut water

Coconut water is a drink, so in the cabin it follows the liquids rule: 100 ml or less, inside your clear 1-litre bag. Drink it before security.

## How to pack coconut in check-in

- Put pieces in a sealed box, then in a zip bag, so water and smell stay in.
- Wrap a whole coconut in a plastic bag and pack it in the middle of the bag, with clothes around it.
- Coconuts are heavy. Weigh your bag at home so you don't pay extra at the airport.

## Taking coconut on an international flight

**Leaving India,** the same airline rules apply: no coconut in the cabin, and no dry coconut in any bag. **Arriving abroad,** each country has its own rules for fruit. We read them on 28 November 2026:

| Country | Coconut on arrival | What the official page says |
|---|---|---|
| UK | Yes | Coconut is on GOV.UK's short list of fruit you can bring from outside the EU without a plant health certificate. |
| USA | Declare it; fresh is risky | US Customs (CBP) says it's best not to bring fresh fruit and vegetables, and undeclared items are taken away. Coconut isn't named. |
| Singapore | A small amount | The Singapore Food Agency allows fruit in "a small, reasonable quantity (i.e. hand-carry size)" for your own use. |
| UAE | Small amounts for yourself | Dubai Customs' 2025 guide lists food products as needing permits, and commercial quantities aren't allowed. See our [Dubai customs guide](../customs/uae-dubai.html). |
| Saudi Arabia | Small amounts for yourself | The GCC customs guide (2025) allows personal belongings and gifts in non-commercial quantities. It sets no food limit. |

Coconut oil is easier to take abroad than coconut: CBP lists oils among foods that are generally admissible in the USA. Keep the bottle in check-in.

## Questions

### Can I carry coconut in my cabin bag?

No. IndiGo and Air India don't allow coconut in the cabin bag.

### Can I carry a coconut in my check-in bag?

On Air India, yes. On IndiGo, its list allows only small pieces in check-in. Dry coconut is banned on every airline.

### Can I carry dry coconut (gola) on a flight?

No. Dry coconut, copra or gola is banned in both the cabin bag and check-in, because it can catch fire.

### Can I carry coconut oil on a flight?

Yes, up to 5 litres in check-in. In the cabin, only 100 ml or less in your liquids bag, and not on IndiGo.

### Can I carry coconut on an IndiGo flight?

Only small pieces, in check-in. IndiGo's list doesn't allow coconut in the cabin, and doesn't allow dry coconut or copra in either bag (checked 28 Nov 2026).

### Is coconut allowed on Air India flights?

In check-in, yes. Air India doesn't allow coconut in the cabin bag, and it bans copra and crushed dry coconut in both bags. Fresh grated coconut is allowed in both.

### Why is dry coconut not allowed on flights?

Because copra can catch fire. Akasa Air lists it among flammable solids, and the BCAS-based airport list bans it in both bags, next to lighters and matches.

### Can I carry a whole coconut on a flight?

On Air India, in check-in, yes. On IndiGo, its list allows only small pieces in check-in, so break it first. No airline here allows a coconut in the cabin.

### Can I carry grated coconut or coconut powder on a flight?

Fresh grated coconut, yes: Air India allows it in both bags. Dry grated coconut or coconut powder is dry coconut, which is banned in both bags.

### Can I carry coconut on an international flight?

Fresh coconut in check-in, on Air India; never dry coconut. On arrival, the UK allows coconut from India without a certificate, Singapore allows a small amount, and the USA advises against fresh fruit (checked 28 Nov 2026).
