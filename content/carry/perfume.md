Can I carry perfume in a flight? **Cabin bag: only bottles of 100 ml or less, inside one clear 1-litre bag, and not at all on IndiGo. Check-in bag: yes, up to 2 litres or 2 kg of perfume and toiletries in total, with no bottle over 500 ml.** These are the rules of IndiGo, Air India, Akasa Air and SpiceJet, checked on 28 November 2026.

## Flying IndiGo? Pack perfume in check-in

Most airlines in India follow the airport liquids rule, so a small bottle of perfume is fine in your cabin bag. **IndiGo is different.** Its own list of things not allowed in flight marks **perfume and deo as not allowed in the cabin bag**, and allowed only in check-in.

So on IndiGo, put every bottle of perfume, attar and deo spray in your check-in bag. If you're flying IndiGo with only a cabin bag, leave the perfume at home or buy some after you land. The same IndiGo list bans lighters in both bags: see [can I carry cigarettes and a lighter?](cigarettes-and-lighter.html)

## Cabin bag on other airlines: the 100 ml rule

- Each bottle must be **100 ml or less**. A 150 ml bottle that is half empty still isn't allowed. The size printed on the bottle counts, not what's left inside (Air India).
- All your liquids, creams and gels go in **one clear, resealable bag of up to 1 litre**. That includes perfume, deo spray, toothpaste, face wash and sunscreen.
- Take the clear bag out of your cabin bag at the security check. See our [100 ml rule page](liquids.html) for what counts as a liquid. Liquid medicine is the exception: a bigger bottle of syrup is allowed if you need it on the journey, see [medicines](medicines.html).

Air India's list of liquids names perfumes, deodorants and other sprays, so a deo spray follows exactly the same rule as perfume. Hair spray and gel do too: see [hair dryer and straightener](hair-dryer-and-straightener.html).

## Attar

Attar (itr) is a perfume oil, so it follows the same rules: small bottles of 100 ml or less in your clear liquids bag, and not in the cabin on IndiGo. Attar usually comes in small bottles, so on other airlines it fits in the liquids bag easily. Bringing attar back from Umrah? See our [Umrah packing list](../../guides/umrah-packing-list.html) for the Zamzam water and baggage rules on the flight home.

## Check-in bag: how much perfume?

IndiGo, Air India and SpiceJet use the same limit for perfume, deo and other toiletries in check-in:

- **2 litres or 2 kg in total** per person
- **No single bottle over 500 ml**
- Sprays need their cap on, so they can't spray inside the bag

That's a lot for one trip. Four 100 ml bottles use only 400 ml of the 2 litres.

## How to pack perfume so it doesn't break or leak

- Wrap the bottle in a sock or a T-shirt and put it in the middle of the bag, between soft clothes, not against the sides.
- Put it in a zip bag in case it leaks. Perfume can stain clothes.
- If the cap is loose, tape it down.
- A hard trolley protects glass better than a soft bag.

## What to do instead

- Pour some of your perfume into a small travel bottle of 10 to 30 ml for the trip, and leave the big bottle at home. On IndiGo, pack even the small one in check-in.
- No check-in bag on IndiGo? Buy a small perfume at your destination.
- Carry wet wipes or a small towel for freshening up on a long journey.

IndiGo's two pages don't quite agree. Its item list marks perfume and deo as not allowed in the cabin. Its dangerous goods table (valid 28 April to 31 December 2026) allows toiletries in carry-on baggage under the 100 ml rule. Staff at the airport may go by either, so on IndiGo the safe choice is check-in.

## What airport security (BCAS) says

BCAS, India's aviation security regulator, sets the liquids rule for every airport in India: each bottle 100 ml or less, all in one clear, resealable bag of up to 1 litre. Air India's list of liquids names perfumes, deodorants and other sprays. Anything bigger "must be packed into the hold luggage", as the Kannur Airport security list puts it. BCAS doesn't ban perfume itself, so the limits above come from that liquids rule and from each airline's own list. Perfume bought in the airport shops after security is the exception: it can stay with you if it's sealed in the shop's tamper-evident bag (Air India).

## Flying abroad with perfume

**Leaving India,** the rules above apply on international flights too: 100 ml bottles in the clear bag in your cabin bag, the rest in check-in. Perfume is a liquid at every airport in the world, so the 100 ml rule follows you on the flight home as well. **Arriving abroad,** perfume is fine for your own use. What changes is how much you can bring without paying duty. We read each government's own page on 28 November 2026:

| Country | Perfume on arrival | What the official page says |
|---|---|---|
| UAE (Dubai, Abu Dhabi) | Yes, within AED 3,000 | Gifts, perfumes and personal luggage are duty free up to AED 3,000 in value. See our [Dubai customs guide](../customs/uae-dubai.html). |
| UK | Yes, within £390 | Liquids over 100 ml can't go through security at most UK airports. Goods you bring in from outside the UK, perfume included, are duty free up to £390. |
| USA | Yes | TSA allows perfume of 100 ml (3.4 oz) or less in carry-on bags and any size in checked bags, up to 2 kg or 2 litres in total per person. |
| Singapore | Yes, declare if over your relief | Perfume isn't on Singapore's banned list. It counts towards the duty-free and GST relief on goods; above it, declare at the Red Channel. |
| Saudi Arabia | Yes, within SAR 3,000 | The GCC customs guide (2025) allows personal belongings and gifts up to SAR 3,000 in value, in non-commercial quantities. |

So one or two bottles for yourself or as gifts are fine everywhere on this list. A suitcase full of the same perfume can look like goods for sale, and customs can charge duty on it.

## Questions

### Can I carry perfume in hand luggage on IndiGo?

No. IndiGo's list of things not allowed in flight marks perfume and deo as not allowed in the cabin. Pack them in your check-in bag.

### Can perfume be carried in hand luggage?

On Air India and Akasa Air, yes, in bottles of 100 ml or less inside your clear 1-litre liquids bag. On IndiGo, no: pack it in check-in.

### Can I carry a 100 ml perfume in my cabin bag?

On Air India and Akasa Air, yes, inside your clear 1-litre liquids bag. 100 ml is the biggest bottle allowed. Not on IndiGo.

### Can I carry a 200 ml perfume on a flight?

Only in your check-in bag. It is over 100 ml, so it can't go in the cabin, even if it's half empty.

### How much perfume can I carry in my check-in bag?

Up to 2 litres or 2 kg of perfume and toiletries in total, with no single bottle over 500 ml (IndiGo, Air India, SpiceJet).

### Can I carry attar on a flight?

Yes. Attar follows the same rules as perfume: small bottles in the cabin liquids bag (not on IndiGo), and up to the 2-litre toiletries limit in check-in.

### Can I carry a deo spray on a flight?

Yes. In the cabin, 100 ml or less in your clear liquids bag, but not on IndiGo. In check-in, up to 500 ml a can within the 2-litre limit, with the cap on.

### Can I carry perfume in an international flight?

Yes. In the cabin, only bottles of 100 ml or less in your clear 1-litre bag (not on IndiGo). In check-in, up to 2 litres of toiletries in total, 500 ml a bottle. On arrival, the UAE allows perfume within AED 3,000 of gifts, the UK within £390, and Saudi Arabia within SAR 3,000 (checked 28 Nov 2026).

### Is perfume allowed in Air India flights?

Yes. In the cabin, bottles of 100 ml or less inside the clear 1-litre bag. In check-in, up to 2 litres or 2 kg of toiletries in total, with no bottle over 500 ml (Air India's restricted items page, checked 28 Nov 2026).

### Where should I keep perfume on an IndiGo flight?

In your check-in bag, wrapped in clothes. IndiGo's list of things not allowed in flight doesn't allow perfume or deo in the cabin bag.

### Can I carry a body spray on an IndiGo flight?

In check-in only. IndiGo's list marks perfume and deo as not allowed in the cabin. In check-in, a can of up to 500 ml is fine, with the cap on.

### Is perfume allowed in a handbag on a flight?

Yes, if the bottle is 100 ml or less and sits in your clear liquids bag, on Air India and Akasa Air. Your handbag goes through security like a cabin bag. On IndiGo, pack it in check-in.

### How much perfume can I take to Dubai?

Leaving India, the normal limits apply: 100 ml bottles in the cabin, up to 2 litres of toiletries in check-in. Arriving in the UAE, gifts, perfumes and personal luggage are duty free up to AED 3,000 in value (UAE government portal, checked 28 Nov 2026).
