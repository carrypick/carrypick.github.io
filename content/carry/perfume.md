## Flying IndiGo? Pack perfume in check-in

Most airlines in India follow the airport liquids rule, so a small bottle of perfume is fine in your cabin bag. **IndiGo is different.** Its own list of things not allowed in flight marks **perfume and deo as not allowed in the cabin bag**, and allowed only in check-in.

So on IndiGo, put every bottle of perfume, attar and deo spray in your check-in bag. If you're flying IndiGo with only a cabin bag, leave the perfume at home or buy some after you land.

## Cabin bag on other airlines: the 100 ml rule

- Each bottle must be **100 ml or less**. A 150 ml bottle that is half empty still isn't allowed. The size printed on the bottle counts, not what's left inside (Air India).
- All your liquids, creams and gels go in **one clear, resealable bag of up to 1 litre**. That includes perfume, deo spray, toothpaste, face wash and sunscreen.
- Take the clear bag out of your cabin bag at the security check.

Air India's list of liquids names perfumes, deodorants and other sprays, so a deo spray follows exactly the same rule as perfume.

## Attar

Attar (itr) is a perfume oil, so it follows the same rules: small bottles of 100 ml or less in your clear liquids bag, and not in the cabin on IndiGo. Attar usually comes in small bottles, so on other airlines it fits in the liquids bag easily.

## Check-in bag: how much perfume?

IndiGo, Air India and SpiceJet use the same limit for perfume, deo and other toiletries in check-in:

- **2 litres or 2 kg in total** per person
- **No single bottle over 500 ml**
- Sprays need their cap on, so they can't spray inside the bag

That's a lot for one trip. Four 100 ml bottles use only 400 ml of the 2 litres.

## How to pack perfume so it doesn't break or leak

- Wrap the bottle in a sock or a T-shirt and put it in the middle of the bag, between soft clothes, not against the sides.
- Put it in a zip bag in case it leaks. Perfume can stain clothes.
- If the cap is loose, tape it down.
- A hard trolley protects glass better than a soft bag.

## What to do instead

- Pour some of your perfume into a small travel bottle of 10 to 30 ml for the trip, and leave the big bottle at home. On IndiGo, pack even the small one in check-in.
- No check-in bag on IndiGo? Buy a small perfume at your destination.
- Carry wet wipes or a small towel for freshening up on a long journey.

## Questions

### Can I carry perfume in my cabin bag on IndiGo?

No. IndiGo's list of things not allowed in flight marks perfume and deo as not allowed in the cabin. Pack them in your check-in bag.

### Can I carry a 100 ml perfume in my cabin bag?

On Air India and Akasa Air, yes, inside your clear 1-litre liquids bag. 100 ml is the biggest bottle allowed. Not on IndiGo.

### Can I carry a 200 ml perfume on a flight?

Only in your check-in bag. It is over 100 ml, so it can't go in the cabin, even if it's half empty.

### How much perfume can I carry in my check-in bag?

Up to 2 litres or 2 kg of perfume and toiletries in total, with no single bottle over 500 ml (IndiGo, Air India, SpiceJet).

### Can I carry attar on a flight?

Yes. Attar follows the same rules as perfume: small bottles in the cabin liquids bag (not on IndiGo), and up to the 2-litre toiletries limit in check-in.

### Can I carry a deo spray on a flight?

Yes. In the cabin, 100 ml or less in your clear liquids bag, but not on IndiGo. In check-in, up to 500 ml a can within the 2-litre limit, with the cap on.
